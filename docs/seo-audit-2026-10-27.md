## Interpretation

The actionable target is sound, crawlable pages and truthful metadata. A zero count across every Ahrefs notice is not appropriate: canonical redirects, deliberate crawler restrictions and detected content changes are valid behavior. The audit's machine-learning page classification can also mistake a directory for an article. No issue checks were disabled to hide these distinctions.

Descriptions target 110–160 characters and entry titles target at most 60 characters including any site suffix. These are editorial budgets, not search-engine ranking guarantees; display widths vary.
