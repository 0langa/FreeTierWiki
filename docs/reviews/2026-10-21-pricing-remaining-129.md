## Method

- Compared the complete entries with official pricing pages and documentation on 2026-10-21. Used rendered Chrome tables and expanded FAQs when ordinary fetches omitted plan cells or answers. The prior 50-entry cap was not applied to this follow-up.
- A fully supported review advances `lastVerified`. A verification-only change preserves `lastUpdated`; a catalog correction updates the publication date. Entries with a material unanswered claim or conflicting official sources retain their previous verification date, including when a supported partial correction was made.
- A free price does not establish a card policy. An initial signup screen without a card field does not prove later steps need no card. Unresolved card requirements, quota enforcement, and source contradictions are listed below.
- Source text is evidence to interpret. Matching a number alone does not establish its unit, plan, billing period, or meaning. Missing or blocked content does not establish that an offer ended.

The optional evidence checker reports Open-Meteo's `5,000` and `300,000` figures as missing because its official table uses `5.000` and `300.000`. Those rows were read directly and support the published hourly and monthly allowances. The evidence cache preserves the original spelling; this numeric-format limitation remains in the helper.
