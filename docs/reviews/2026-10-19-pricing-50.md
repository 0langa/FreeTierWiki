## Corrections

- **services/directus-cloud** (status: changed): the pricing page no longer lists the 14-day free Directus Cloud trial. Cloud is a $99/mo add-on, and the $0 Core license is free only when self-hosted. [Official source](https://directus.io/pricing): “Directus Cloud is an optional add-on at $99/mo for Core and Team.”
- **services/ahasend**: the Campaign Builder for newsletters is now on every plan, Free included, and campaigns share the 1,000 free emails. Removed the "transactional only" claim and the now-contradicted port 465 claim. [Official source](https://ahasend.com/docs/facts.md): “1,000 free emails per month shared by both”
- **services/bleemeo**: Free now lists 100 metrics per server, 5 uptime monitors, custom dashboards and alerts. Every signup starts as a 15-day trial that drops to Free. [Official source](https://bleemeo.com/pricing/): “Up to 3 servers 60s metric resolution 1 day metric retention”
- **services/brave-search-api**: billing is now prepaid credits with no subscription. The card is asked for "to prevent abuse". `overageRisk` went from medium to low. Removed the 40B-pages and five-snippets claims. [Official source](https://brave.com/search/api/): “you’re only charged for credits you choose to buy.”
- **services/bugsink**: paid prices now show in dollars ($16/mo; premium support $15/user/mo). The free plan is unchanged. [Official source](https://www.bugsink.com/): “75K Events $16 /mo”
- **services/buttondown**: the Sponsorships add-on is now $29/month, not $9. [Official source](https://buttondown.com/pricing): “Absolutely nothing for your first 100 subscribers.”
- **services/chanty**: prices now show in dollars ($0 forever; $3/user/month annual, $4 monthly). Free limits are unchanged. [Official source](https://www.chanty.com/pricing/): “Free $0 forever”
- **services/cloudflare-ai-gateway**: the "future features may be paid" caveat became "prices subject to change". Added that Guardrails is billed as Workers AI inference, and added the logging docs to sourceUrls. [Official source](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/reference/pricing/): “Prices subject to change.”
- **services/encore**: the Pro price is now spelled out ($49 per member plus $99 per AWS/GCP environment and $2.50 per resource per environment). The free plan is unchanged. [Official source](https://encore.dev/pricing): “2 free dev environments on Encore Cloud”
- **services/estuary**: past the free limits, a 30-day Cloud trial starts automatically, and a payment method is needed after it. The body used to say this was unspecified. [Official source](https://estuary.dev/pricing/): “a 30-day free trial of the Cloud plan starts automatically”
- **services/flarewarden**: the Free plan caps uptime checks at 5 websites within its 15 monitors. Added that cap and the Starter and add-on website counts. [Official source](https://flarewarden.com/pricing): “5 websites 15 monitors 5-min checks 1 status page”
- **services/gitbook**: the paid "Premium" plan is now "Essential". Free now lists advanced customization. Free limits are unchanged. [Official source](https://www.gitbook.com/pricing): “Essential starts at $65 per site/month”
- **services/hook0**: added the Developer plan's listed caps (1 developer, 1 application, 10 event types, 10 subscriptions, 7 days retention). [Official source](https://www.hook0.com/pricing): “Up to 100 events/day”
- **services/hotjar**: removed an unsourced Fullstory retention number from the Alternatives line. The free offer is unchanged. [Official source](https://www.hotjar.com/pricing/): “200k monthly sessions $0 / forever”
- **services/hs-ping**: coverage went from 32 to 35 countries. The free credits are now described as one-time credits that never renew. [Official source](https://hsping.com/): “HS Ping supports 35 countries today”
- **services/hyperping**: a gotcha said all paid plans include 3 seats. That is only Essentials; Pro includes 10. [Official source](https://hyperping.com/pricing): “20 monitors free. No credit card required.”
- **services/ibm-cloud-object-storage**: the Free Tier allowances are now credited to the billing docs, because the catalog page renders by JavaScript only. Removed the unsourced "no minimum fee" claim. [Official source](https://cloud.ibm.com/docs/cloud-object-storage?topic=cloud-object-storage-billing): “Up to 5 GB of Smart Tier storage capacity”
