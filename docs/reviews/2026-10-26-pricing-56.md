## Scope and method

- Starting content revision: `f66b5b34470ab0eb93573021f9dad39f0ea40943`. Branch: `routine/pricing-review-2026-10-26`.
- The issue body is cut short, so the full ordered queue came from `freshness-review.json` in the run's `pricing-check` artifact.
- Every cited page was fetched with `npm run verify:evidence` on 2026-10-26 and read with `npm run verify:show`. Where the plain fetch was thin, localized, cut short or lost table cells, the official page was read another way and stored with `verify:evidence --add`:
  - WebFetch or the page's own HTML, JSON-LD or script data.
  - Official Markdown copies (`index.md`, `pricing.md`) and public help-center article APIs (Zendesk).
  - A rendered browser view.
- The 13 thin and blocked entries, Brevo, and pages behind 403 or bot checks (GTmetrix, Snyk support) were read in Chrome. No bot check was solved or worked around. One batch probe sent a browser user agent to GTmetrix; its response was discarded.
- Only official vendor pricing pages and docs were used. No accounts, subscriptions or credentials were created.
- Verification-only reviews update `lastVerified` and keep `lastUpdated`. Corrections update both dates and add a `changes` item that describes the catalog correction, not a vendor effective date.
- A changed or noisy hash was treated as a reason to look, not as proof. Most flags came from localized pages (Zoho and Google served in German, French, Hindi or Portuguese), rotating page content, and the scanner's plain text dropping tables.
