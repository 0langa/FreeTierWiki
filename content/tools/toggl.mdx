Toggl is a time tracker with one-click timers, calendar integrations, and light planning with tasks and boards. The pricing page now presents a single Toggl product with time tracking, capacity management, and project planning; existing Toggl Track and Toggl Plan accounts remain available and supported. A developer would pick it to track hours on freelance or client work, or to see how long tasks really take.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0, and Toggl says it is free forever. The pricing FAQ says it is free for teams of up to 3 users, and that the free tier includes manual and real-time timers, calendar integrations, and simple planning with tasks and boards.

The plan card lists time tracking with focus mode, simple planning with tasks, boards, and task estimates, Google and Outlook Calendar integrations, and personal-level insights with reports on individual progress. Toggl describes the free plan as genuinely usable for a solo freelancer rather than a trial in disguise. Sign-up needs no credit card. The Starter plan is listed from $9 per user per month.

## When you hit a limit

The limit is the number of users: the free tier covers up to 3. The pricing page does not say what happens when a fourth person joins, and it lists no usage-based charges for the Free plan.

Paid plans add what larger teams need, which the FAQ lists as approval workflows, billable rates, capacity planning, time off, budgets and alerts, and deeper reporting. Each paid plan has a 30-day trial, and Toggl says the trial has no strings attached.

## Gotchas

- The page says "Free for a limited number of users" on the plan card; the 3-user figure is in the FAQ.
- Reports on Free are personal-level, so a team lead cannot see team-wide reports without upgrading.
- Toggl Track is now part of a combined Toggl product, so older guides may show different plan names.

## Questions people ask

### Is Toggl free?

Yes. Toggl says the Free plan is free forever, and it covers teams of up to 3 users.

### What happened to Toggl Track?

The pricing page now sells one Toggl product that includes time tracking. Existing Toggl Track and Toggl Plan accounts remain available and supported.

### Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. The sign-up form on the pricing page says no credit card is required.
