Plasmic is a visual builder for websites, web apps, and internal tools. You design with components, style tokens, and interactions, then either publish to Plasmic hosting or render the pages inside your own codebase with code components. It suits developers who want designers or marketers to edit pages without touching the code.

## What the free plan gives you

Plasmic's Free plan is free forever, needs no credit card, and has unlimited time on the plan. It includes 3 collaborators, unlimited projects, publishes, workspaces, and cross-project imports, and the headless CMS with unlimited types and entries.

You can use custom domains with Plasmic hosting or deploy to third-party servers. Version history covers 14 days. Plasmic-hosted websites carry a Plasmic badge on Free, and support is through the community forum. The Starter plan costs $39 per month billed yearly and removes the badge.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan's main limit is people: 3 collaborators, and no extra collaborators can be bought on Free. The pricing page does not say what happens if you try to add a fourth, and it lists no usage-based charges for the Free plan, so there is no overage bill. Moving to Pro allows 4 to 10 collaborators, with additional collaborators at $20 each.

## Gotchas

- The Plasmic badge appears on every Plasmic-hosted site on Free; sites you deploy to your own servers do not carry it.
- Version history goes back only 14 days on Free.
- Plasmic AI is in beta and its unlimited access is listed from the Pro plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Plasmic free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever with no credit card, and includes 3 collaborators and unlimited projects.

### Can I use a custom domain on Plasmic's Free plan?

Yes. The Free plan lists custom domains with Plasmic hosting, though hosted sites show a Plasmic badge.

### Can I use Plasmic with my own React app for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes deploying to third-party servers, and the badge applies only to Plasmic-hosted websites.
