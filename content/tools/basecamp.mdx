Basecamp is a project tool where each project holds its own to-do lists, message board, chat room, schedule, and files. A developer would pick it to run a side project or freelance engagement with a few collaborators in one calm place, rather than a chat app plus a separate tracker.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan offers one free project for personal use. It is limited to 1 project, 1 GB of storage space, and 3 users max. The free account includes the project tools and Pings. Its help page excludes public sharing links and paid account add-ons. No credit card is required for the permanent free account.

Paid plans are flat monthly prices rather than per-user fees. Freelancer costs $29 per month for 3 active projects, 10 GB of storage, and 5 users. Pro allows 8 projects, 25 GB, and 10 users for $79 per month. Pro Plus allows 12 projects, 100 GB, and 15 users for $99 per month.

## When you hit a limit

Basecamp states that the free plan is limited to one project and that you can always upgrade for more. For storage, it says you can upgrade if you ever need more file storage. The pricing page does not say what happens if the 1 GB fills up or a fourth user is invited.

On paid plans the project limit counts active projects only: archived or deleted projects do not count against your total. Business accounts start with a 30-day free trial. Pro Unlimited has unlimited projects and users and 1 TB of storage for $199 per month billed annually, or $329 month-to-month.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan is described as for personal use; review the paid packages for a business team.
- One project means you cannot separate clients or products without upgrading.
- The 3-user cap includes everyone you invite to that project.

## Questions people ask

### Is Basecamp free?

Yes, for one project. The Free plan gives one free project for personal use, with 1 GB of storage and up to 3 users.

### How many users can join Basecamp Free?

Up to 3 users, including clients and outside collaborators. Freelancer raises this to 5; unlimited users require Pro Unlimited.

### Does Basecamp have a free trial?

Yes. Business accounts have a 30-day free trial. The permanent free account is a separate option for one personal project.
