Basecamp is a project tool where each project holds its own to-do lists, message board, chat room, schedule, and files. A developer would pick it to run a side project or freelance engagement with a few collaborators in one calm place, rather than a chat app plus a separate tracker.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan offers one free project for personal use. It is limited to 1 project, 1 GB of storage space, and 5 users max. Basecamp says the same core features are included with every package from Free to the Unlimited Edition, so the free project has the same building blocks as paid ones.

Paid plans are flat monthly prices rather than per-user fees. Freelancer costs $25 per month for up to 3 active projects, 5 GB of storage, and 20 users max. Studio allows up to 10 active projects and 25 GB, and Pro allows up to 25 active projects and 100 GB, both with unlimited users.

## When you hit a limit

Basecamp states that the free plan is limited to one project and that you can always upgrade for more. For storage, it says you can upgrade if you ever need more file storage. The pricing page does not say what happens if the 1 GB fills up or a sixth user is invited.

On paid plans the project limit counts active projects only: archived or deleted projects do not count against your total. Freelancer, Studio, and Pro start with a 30-day free trial, and the Unlimited Edition with a 45-day free trial.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan is described as for personal use, so it is not meant to run a business.
- One project means you cannot separate clients or products without upgrading.
- The 5-user cap includes everyone you invite to that project.

## Questions people ask

### Is Basecamp free?

Yes, for one project. The Free plan gives one free project for personal use, with 1 GB of storage and up to 5 users.

### How many users can join Basecamp Free?

Up to 5 users. The Freelancer plan raises this to 20, and Studio and above have no user limit.

### Does Basecamp have a free trial?

Yes. Paid plans start with a 30-day free trial, and the Unlimited Edition has a 45-day free trial.
