You keep your own form markup and point its action at a Formspree endpoint. Submissions land in a dashboard inbox, and plugins can pass them on to other tools.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and is meant for testing and development. It allows 50 submissions a month. You can create unlimited forms and unlimited projects, and the submission archive keeps entries for 30 days.

The plan includes 1 team member, 2 linked emails, and 0 GB of file uploads, so forms cannot take files. You still get private emails, AJAX forms, custom reCAPTCHA, restrict to domain, and basic spam filtering.

Several features start on the Personal plan. These include submission export, search, a custom thank-you page, basic plugins, and multiple target emails. API access and autoresponses start on Professional.

## When you hit a limit

Your forms keep working for visitors. Formspree stops processing new submissions once you reach the monthly limit. It saves them in an Over Limit folder, skips the notification emails and plugins, and emails you when this starts.

You can reprocess those submissions once you have room, by upgrading or after the limit resets. Each reprocessed submission uses one from your monthly limit. The folder shows only short previews, and search and export do not work there.

All plans also share a rate limit of 20 posts per minute per form. AJAX forms receive a 429 error when they pass it.

## Gotchas

- The free plan archive keeps submissions for only 30 days, so read them before they expire.
- Over-limit submissions are saved, but you see only previews until you reprocess them.
- File uploads are off on the free plan. Paid plans accept up to 10 files per submission, each up to 25MB.

## Questions people ask

### Is Formspree free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and allows 50 submissions a month with unlimited forms. Personal starts at $15 a month, or $10 with annual billing.

### What happens when I go over the Formspree submission limit?

Formspree keeps the submissions in an Over Limit folder without sending emails or running plugins. You can reprocess them once you have room in your limit.

### Does Formspree allow file uploads on the free plan?

No. The plans page lists 0 GB of file uploads on the free plan and 1 GB on Personal.
