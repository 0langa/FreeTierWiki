ClickUp is a work app that puts tasks, docs, chat, whiteboards, and dashboards in one Workspace. A developer team would pick it when it wants planning and documentation in a single tool, and its free plan lets every member join at no cost.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan costs nothing. Its key features are unlimited tasks, unlimited free plan members, 60MB of storage, two-factor authentication, and 1 Form. The comparison table lists 5 Spaces on the free plan and unlimited custom views.

Many other features show up on the free plan as Trial rather than as included, including Gantt charts, dashboards, timesheets, sprint points, and goals. That means you can try them, but the plan does not include them on an ongoing basis. The Unlimited plan, at $7 per user per month billed yearly, adds unlimited Spaces, storage, integrations, and native time tracking.

## When you hit a limit

Storage is the limit teams reach first. Once files in the Workspace add up to 60MB, the plan has no room for more; the pricing page does not say exactly what happens when you try to upload past that point.

Features marked Trial work only for a limited trial on the free plan, and the page does not list the number of uses. Upgrading is per Workspace, not per person: the pricing FAQ says you need to upgrade your entire Workspace, which means all members in it.

## Gotchas

- 60MB is the total for the free Workspace, not per file, so a few screenshots and PDFs can fill it.
- Trial features can disappear from your daily workflow once their trial ends.
- Because upgrades cover every member, a large free team becomes expensive the moment you upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is ClickUp free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan has unlimited tasks and unlimited free plan members, with 60MB of storage.

### How much storage does ClickUp Free include?

60MB in total. Unlimited storage starts on the Unlimited plan.

### Do I have to upgrade every member?

Yes. ClickUp says upgrading means upgrading your entire Workspace, including all members.
