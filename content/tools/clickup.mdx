ClickUp is a work app that puts tasks, docs, chat, whiteboards, and dashboards in one Workspace. A developer team would pick it when it wants planning and documentation in a single tool, and its free plan lets every member join at no cost.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan costs nothing. Its key features are unlimited tasks, unlimited free plan members, 60MB of storage, two-factor authentication, and 1 Form. The current comparison table confirms five Spaces on Free.

The current rendered comparison marks Gantt charts, dashboards, goals and time tracking as Trial. Its Free card lists time tracking, so confirm that scope before relying on it. Free Forever no longer includes AI credits: both the current pricing table and AI-credit help article mark them unavailable. The earlier 500-credit one-time offer has been removed, including from the help article's existing-plan section. Core currently displays $9 per user monthly billed yearly, versus a $12 regular price, with unlimited Spaces and storage.

## When you hit a limit

Storage is the limit teams reach first. Once files in the Workspace add up to 60MB, the plan has no room for more; the pricing page does not say exactly what happens when you try to upload past that point.

Trial feature uses are cumulative across the Workspace and do not reset. Features with a separate monthly allowance, such as Automation Actions, renew monthly. At a use limit, existing data remains but creating or editing items with that feature stops until the Workspace upgrades. Upgrading is per Workspace, not per person: the pricing FAQ says you need to upgrade your entire Workspace, which means all members in it.

## Gotchas

- 60MB is the total for the free Workspace, not per file, so a few screenshots and PDFs can fill it.
- Trial feature uses do not reset; the Free card and comparison disagree about time-tracking inclusion. Activity view on Free covers only one day.
- Because upgrades cover every member, a large free team becomes expensive the moment you upgrade. Paid plans also bill limited members and can automatically add a paid member seat when permission-controlled guests exceed the included allowance; view-only guests have different rules.

## Questions people ask

### Is ClickUp free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan has unlimited tasks and unlimited free plan members, with 60MB of storage.

### How much storage does ClickUp Free include?

60MB in total. The currently rendered Core plan adds unlimited storage.

### Do I have to upgrade every member?

Yes. ClickUp says upgrading means upgrading your entire Workspace, including all members.
