ClickUp is a work app that puts tasks, docs, chat, whiteboards, and dashboards in one Workspace. A developer team would pick it when it wants planning and documentation in a single tool, and its free plan lets every member join at no cost.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan costs nothing. Its key features are unlimited tasks, unlimited free plan members, 60MB of storage, two-factor authentication, and 1 Form. The current comparison table confirms five Spaces on Free.

The current rendered comparison marks Gantt charts, dashboards, goals and time tracking as Trial. Its Free card lists time tracking, so confirm that scope before relying on it. Free also lists 500 AI credits per Workspace. Core currently displays $9 per user monthly billed yearly, versus a $12 regular price, with unlimited Spaces and storage.

## When you hit a limit

Storage is the limit teams reach first. Once files in the Workspace add up to 60MB, the plan has no room for more; the pricing page does not say exactly what happens when you try to upload past that point.

Feature uses are cumulative across the Workspace and do not reset. At a use limit, existing data remains but creating or editing items with that feature stops; ClickUp says a grace period may be available. Upgrading is per Workspace, not per person: the pricing FAQ says you need to upgrade your entire Workspace, which means all members in it.

## Gotchas

- 60MB is the total for the free Workspace, not per file, so a few screenshots and PDFs can fill it.
- Trial feature uses do not reset; the Free card and comparison disagree about time-tracking inclusion.
- Because upgrades cover every member, a large free team becomes expensive the moment you upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is ClickUp free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan has unlimited tasks and unlimited free plan members, with 60MB of storage.

### How much storage does ClickUp Free include?

60MB in total. The currently rendered Core plan adds unlimited storage.

### Do I have to upgrade every member?

Yes. ClickUp says upgrading means upgrading your entire Workspace, including all members.
