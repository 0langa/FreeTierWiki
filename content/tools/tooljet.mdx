ToolJet is an open source low-code platform for building internal apps. You generate an app from a prompt or assemble it from 80+ components, connect it to 100+ data sources, and share it with users. You can self-host it or use ToolJet Cloud; this entry covers the cloud Free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

The ToolJet Cloud Free plan costs $0 per builder per month and is meant for small teams evaluating ToolJet. The homepage explicitly says no card and no trial clock. It includes 2 builders, up to 50 end users, and up to 10 apps. You get 100 AI credits a month for AI app generation, code generation, and debugging, with no add-on credits.

ToolJet Database is included with 10 tables and 500 rows, and database users are unlimited. Public apps, predefined user roles, and community support via Slack are part of the plan. Audit logs are not included on Free. Cloud Team is the first plan with audit logs, retained for up to 14 days.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 50 end users, 10 apps, or 500 database rows on the Free plan. It lists no overage charges for Free. Monthly AI credits reset at the start of each billing cycle, and the Free plan cannot buy add-on credits. The paid Basic plan keeps the same user and app limits with more AI credits; billing interval affects its displayed price.

## Gotchas

- Workflows and the agent builder are available on self-hosted ToolJet only, not in the cloud.
- ToolJet Database on Free holds only 500 rows, so store real data in your own database.
- Version control and Git sync are not on Free or Basic.

## Questions people ask

### Is ToolJet free?

Yes. ToolJet Cloud has a Free plan with 2 builders, up to 50 end users, and up to 10 apps, and the open source edition can be self-hosted.

### How many users can use a ToolJet app on the Free plan?

Up to 50 end users, plus the 2 builders.

### Does ToolJet Cloud include workflows?

No. Workflows and the agent builder are available on self-hosted ToolJet only.
