You build Zaps in the Zapier web editor, from a blank Zap, a template, or a plain-language description to Copilot. It suits people who want automations between popular apps without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month, free forever. It includes 100 tasks a month, and the allowance resets each month. You can build unlimited Zaps, Tables, and Forms, but every Zap is limited to 2 steps: a trigger and one action.

Free Tables hold up to 2,500 records per account, and Forms allow up to 10 pages per account. Form file uploads are limited to 5 MB at 3 files max. Polling triggers check for new data every 15 minutes, while paid plans check more often. Premium apps are not included.

A task is used when a Zap successfully moves data or completes an action. Triggers do not count toward the task limit.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan has no pay-per-task billing. When you reach 100 tasks, usage stops until the next monthly cycle, so Zaps pause and nothing is billed. The 100 tasks come back at the start of the next monthly cycle. The pricing page does not say how Zapier warns you before you reach the limit, so check your task usage in your account during the month.

Paid plans differ. Professional starts at $19.99 a month, and Team starts at $69 a month. The pricing page lists pay-per-task billing as not available on Free.

## Gotchas

- Free Zaps have one trigger and one action. Filters, searches, and extra actions make a Zap multi-step, which needs a paid plan.
- Premium apps are only on paid plans or during a free trial.
- The 14-day Professional trial is the only offer the pricing page labels as no credit card required. The page does not say whether the Free plan needs a card.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zapier free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 100 tasks, unlimited two-step Zaps, Tables, and Forms.

### What happens when I use all 100 Zapier tasks?

Usage stops until the next monthly cycle, and your Zaps pause. The Free plan has no pay-per-task option, so nothing is billed.

### Does Zapier need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say. It states no credit card required only for the 14-day Professional trial.
