You open the site, type a URL into the box, and read the result. It suits site owners, students, and agencies who want a quick look at one page.

## What the free plan gives you

SeoTest.me is a free on-page SEO tool. The home page has one form. You enter your URL and the site says it will give you a free on-page SEO test result for that address.

The page we could read shows a single box and an Analyze button. It shows no sign-up step, no plan names, and no credit card step. It checks the one URL you enter, so you run it again for the next page.

The page does not list the checks the test runs. It does not say whether you get a saved report or an account area. Note down your findings yourself.

## When you hit a limit

The site has no pricing page, and the pages we read list no quota, daily cap, or overage fee. We found no paid plan and no card step, so the risk of a surprise bill is listed as none.

The pages also say nothing about an API, saved history, or advanced reports. Do not assume the tool has them. If you need a documented limit, an API, or tracking over time, pick a different tool.

## Gotchas

- The pages do not document an API, so do not plan to script tests or add the tool to a build process.
- The test takes one URL at a time, so checking a whole site means repeating it page by page.
- The pages do not mention saved results, so save any findings you want to compare later.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [Checkbot.io](/services/checkbot-io/): when you want to crawl many pages of a site instead of testing one.
- [GTMetrix.com](/services/gtmetrix-com/): when you care about page speed as well as on-page checks.
- [Microsoft Clarity](/services/microsoft-clarity/): when you want to see how visitors use a page once it is live.

## Questions people ask

### Is SeoTest.me free?

Yes. The site offers a free on-page SEO test for a URL you enter. The home page shows no sign-up step.

### Does SeoTest.me have an API?

The pages we read do not mention an API, so treat it as a manual tool.

### Can SeoTest.me test my whole site?

It tests one URL at a time. For a crawl of many pages, use a crawler such as Checkbot.
