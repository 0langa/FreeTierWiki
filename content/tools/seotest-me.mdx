You open the site, type a URL into the box, and read the result on the page. It suits site owners, students, and agencies who want a quick look at one page.

## What the free plan gives you

SeoTest.me is a free on-page SEO tool. There is one form on the home page. You enter a URL and the site gives you a free on-page SEO test result for that address.

The listed terms are short. The test is free, and you do not need to sign up. It checks one URL at a time. The site does not offer a paid plan that this atlas could find, and it does not ask for a credit card.

There is no account, so there is no dashboard to log in to. You get a result for the page you test, and you run it again for the next page. That keeps the tool simple. It also means you must note down your findings yourself.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page could not be read, because the site has no pricing page. The atlas lists no quota, no daily cap, and no overage fee for SeoTest.me. That is why the risk of a surprise bill is listed as none.

The practical limits are about features, not usage. The tool does not offer an API. It does not track history over time. It does not provide advanced reporting. If you need any of those, you need a different tool, and a paid SEO platform is the usual next step.

## Gotchas

- There is no API, so you cannot script tests or connect the tool to a build process.
- The test covers one URL at a time, so checking a whole site means repeating the test page by page.
- The tool is not built for historical tracking, so save any results you want to compare later.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [Checkbot.io](/services/checkbot-io/): when you want to crawl many pages of a site instead of testing one.
- [GTMetrix.com](/services/gtmetrix-com/): when you care about page speed as well as on-page checks.
- [Microsoft Clarity](/services/microsoft-clarity/): when you want to see how visitors use a page once it is live.

## Questions people ask

### Is SeoTest.me free?

Yes. The site offers a free on-page SEO test for any URL, and it needs no sign-up.

### Does SeoTest.me have an API?

No. The tool has no API, so it fits manual checks only.

### Can SeoTest.me test my whole site?

It tests one URL at a time. For a crawl of many pages, use a crawler such as Checkbot.
