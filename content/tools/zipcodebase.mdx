You use Zipcodebase through an API key from your account. The documentation page fills in code snippets with your own key after you log in. It suits developers who add postal code checks and distance search to forms and small apps.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and is for hobby projects. It gives you 5000 searches a month and 1 concurrent request. The search radius for distance and radius lookups is limited to 10 miles.

The service holds postal code data for more than 200 countries. The documentation covers looking up a postal code, working out the distance between postal codes, finding postal codes within a radius, and listing postal codes by city or state. It can also list the provinces or states of a country.

Only successful responses use your allowance. Invalid requests and errors do not count.

## When you hit a limit

The site sends you an email when your monthly usage reaches 90% and again at 100%, so you have time to react. The pricing page does not say what the API does after 100%. Plan for requests to stop until the next month or until you upgrade.

Paid plans start at $45 a month for 60.000 searches, a 100-mile radius, and the same single concurrent request. Larger plans add more concurrent requests and bigger radii.

## Gotchas

- The free radius is 10 miles. The Small plan raises it to 100 miles.
- You can run 1 request at a time. Parallel calls need a larger plan.
- The larger plans come with unlimited support and a service level agreement. The free plan has neither.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zipcodebase free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 5000 searches, 1 concurrent request, and a 10-mile radius.

### What happens when I use all my Zipcodebase requests?

You get emails at 90% and 100% of your monthly usage. The pricing page does not say what the API does next, so upgrade before you reach the limit.

### Do failed requests count against the Zipcodebase limit?

No. The FAQ says you are only charged for successful responses, and invalid requests do not affect your usage.
