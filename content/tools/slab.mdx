Slab is a knowledge base for teams: you write posts, group them into topics, and search across Slab and the tools you connect. A developer team would pick it for an internal wiki of onboarding guides, runbooks, and design notes, where the main need is that people can find things later.

## What the free plan gives you

Slab says you can use it free forever with no credit card required. The Free plan is $0 per user per month for up to 10 users, and the FAQ says Slab is free for up to 10 users plus guests forever.

Free includes unlimited posts and topics, realtime collaboration, templates, verification, and unified search. Attachments can be up to 10MB, version history goes back 90 days, and usage analytics cover 30 days. You can connect 10 standard integrations. Support is the Basic level. The Startup plan, at $6.67 per user per month billed annually, raises these to unlimited users, 25MB attachments, and 365 days of version history.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan's user limit is up to 10. The pricing page does not say what happens when one more person tries to join; the next step is the Startup plan. Slab bills for the number of users who can log in, and admins can deactivate users at any time.

Version history older than 90 days and analytics older than 30 days are outside the Free plan's window. The page lists no overage charges for Free. On paid plans Slab offers a 30-day money-back guarantee.

## Gotchas

- The table shows guests starting on Startup at 3 per user, even though the FAQ mentions guests on Free; confirm before relying on them.
- The GraphQL API and webhooks are only on Business and Enterprise.
- Slab has no read-only user type, so everyone who can log in counts as a user.

## Questions people ask

### Is Slab free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 for up to 10 users, and Slab says no credit card is required.

### Does Slab Free include an API?

No. The pricing table lists the GraphQL API on the Business and Enterprise plans only.

### Is Slab free for non-profits?

Slab says its Startup plan is free for non-profits and educational use; you register a free account and then request the discount.
