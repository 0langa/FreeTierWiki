You create an Automation account in the Azure portal, then write runbooks in PowerShell, Python, or a graphical editor. One Automation account can manage resources across all regions and subscriptions in a tenant.

## What the free plan gives you

Each month includes free units that refresh every month. Process automation gives you 500 minutes of job run time and 744 hours of watcher time. A job counts for the minutes it runs, and a watcher counts for the hours it runs.

Configuration management is free for Azure nodes and for the first 5 non-Azure nodes. The pricing page also says there is no charge for Update management itself. You only pay for log data stored in Azure Log Analytics. The free units are not available on rate plans with a flat discount or a fixed monthly credit.

## When you hit a limit

Usage past the free units is billed at the standard rates. Process automation bills only the minutes and hours above the free amount. Configuration management bills by the number of nodes, pro-rated hourly, once a node is registered, and the charge stops when you unregister it.

Usage is added up across all the Automation accounts in your subscription. The pricing page does not show the per-minute prices as numbers in the text we read, so check the page for the current rate before you plan a large workload.

## Gotchas

- Free units are shared across all Automation accounts in a subscription, so many accounts do not give many free allowances.
- Configuration management bills non-Azure nodes beyond the first 5, and the billing starts when a node registers.
- The free units do not apply to subscribers on rate plans with a flat discount or fixed monthly credit.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Automation free?

Partly. Each month includes 500 minutes of job run time, 744 watcher hours, and configuration management for the first 5 non-Azure nodes. Usage past that is billed.

### What happens when I go over the Azure Automation free minutes?

You are billed at the standard rate for the minutes above 500. The charge applies to all Automation accounts in the subscription together.

### Does Azure Automation need a credit card?

You need an Azure subscription to create an Automation account, and this atlas marks the plan as requiring a card.
