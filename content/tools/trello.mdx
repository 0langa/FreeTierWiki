Trello is a board tool: each board has lists, and each task is a card you drag from list to list. A developer team would pick it when it wants a lightweight Kanban board for a side project, a launch checklist, or a small backlog, without the setup of a full issue tracker. Power-Ups link boards to other tools.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 USD and is free for up to 10 collaborators per Workspace. It includes unlimited cards, up to 10 boards per Workspace, the Inbox for capturing to-dos, and quick capture from email, Slack, and Teams. Power-Ups are unlimited per board.

Storage is unlimited with a cap of 10MB per file, and each Workspace gets 250 command runs per month for automation. Custom backgrounds and stickers are included. The Standard plan, at $5 per user per month billed annually, adds unlimited boards, 250MB per file, and 1,000 command runs per month.

## When you hit a limit

Above 10 collaborators, boards become view-only and automation stops. At 10, new collaborators cannot be added. Guests and pending invites count too. Automation pools 250 runs and 2,500 operations per Workspace, resetting on the first of each month at midnight GMT.

The cited pages do not establish every quota response, Free card requirements or excess-usage billing. Upgrade the Workspace for higher allowances. Premium trials add boards and views; some Power-Ups charge separately.

## Gotchas

- The 10-collaborator and 10-board limits are per Workspace, so a growing team hits them together.
- Command runs and operations are separate shared quotas, so one busy automation can exhaust either allowance for everyone.
- Calendar, Timeline, Table, Dashboard, and Map views are Premium features, not part of Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Trello free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and is free for up to 10 collaborators per Workspace, with up to 10 boards.

### How many boards do I get on Trello Free?

Up to 10 boards per Workspace. Unlimited boards start on the Standard plan.

### Does Trello offer a free trial of Premium?

Yes. Trello says all users can enroll their Workspace in a free trial of Trello Premium.
