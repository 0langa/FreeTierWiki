You work in the CodeKeep web app, where you paste a snippet, give it labels, and put it in a folder. The VS Code and Google Chrome extensions import and export snippets. It suits developers who are happy to keep their snippets public.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It allows unlimited public code snippets, up to 40 folders, and up to 20 bookmarks for folders of other users. The VS Code extension is included. The pricing table also lists the Screenshot Watermark as "Yes" for Free, so code screenshots you make carry a watermark.

The plan does not include private snippets, private folders, or folder icons. Every snippet you save is public. Pro costs $4.99 per month and lists unlimited snippets, folders, private folders, folder icons, and bookmarks, with no screenshot watermark.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what the app does when you go past the folder or bookmark caps. The table shows them as fixed limits. The page does not describe any bill, because the Free plan costs $0.

If you need private snippets, you must switch to the Pro plan. The pricing page also does not say whether Pro needs a card, so check at checkout. Do not save secrets, tokens, or private code on the Free plan, because the snippets are public.

## Gotchas

- Free snippets are public. Do not paste API keys, passwords, or client code.
- The Free plan allows up to 40 folders and up to 20 bookmarks, and the table shows folder icons as not included.
- Screenshots made on the Free plan carry a watermark.

## Questions people ask

### Is CodeKeep free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and gives you unlimited public snippets, up to 40 folders, and up to 20 bookmarks.

### Can I keep private snippets on CodeKeep?

Not on the Free plan. Private snippets and private folders are listed as "No" for Free and are part of the Pro plan at $4.99 per month.

### Does CodeKeep have a VS Code extension?

Yes. The VS Code extension is included in the Free plan, and the site also names a Google Chrome extension for importing and exporting snippets.
