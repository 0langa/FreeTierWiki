You can run Actors by hand in the Console, call them with the API, or start them from your own code. The command line tool helps when you write your own Actor.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs nothing and needs no credit card. It gives you $5 a month to spend in Apify Store or on your own Actors. Compute costs $0.2 per compute unit, where one unit is 1 GB of RAM for one hour. A single Actor run can use up to 16 GB of RAM, and you can run up to 5 Actors at once.

Support is community only, and Apify Store prices carry no discount. The free plan includes Apify Proxy, but only for use inside the platform. It does not extend to external clients. Paid plans start at $19 a month and lower the compute price.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan has a hard stop. Once your platform usage passes the $5 credit, Apify blocks your access to its services until the next monthly cycle begins. You are not billed, because there is no card on file. Paid users keep running and pay for the extra usage on the next invoice.

Unused credit does not roll over. It expires at the end of the billing cycle, so you cannot save it for a bigger job. Runs cost more when they use proxies, move more data, use more memory, or retry failed requests.

## Gotchas

- Many Store Actors charge per event or per result, so read the pricing section on each Actor's page before you run it.
- Proxy traffic, data transfer, and storage operations all draw from the same $5 credit as compute.
- Extras such as more concurrent runs, more Actor RAM, and datacenter proxies are paid add-ons.

## Questions people ask

### Is Apify free?

Yes, there is a free plan. It gives you $5 of platform credit each month and needs no credit card. When the credit is used up, access is blocked until the next cycle.

### What happens when I run out of Apify free credit?

Apify blocks your access to its services until the beginning of the next monthly cycle. Free accounts are not charged. Paid accounts can keep going and pay for the extra usage.

### Does Apify need a credit card for the free plan?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the free plan.
