You use Cursor as a desktop app, and you can also run its agent from a CLI. It suits developers who want to try an editor that can explain, plan, and change code through a conversation, while keeping the resulting changes available for review.

## What the free plan gives you

The current pricing page calls the entry plan Free and lists it at $0 per month. Its pricing guide includes Agent, Chat, and Tab completions with Grok and Composer under limited usage. The comparison table includes Agent access but excludes cloud agents, automations, code hosting, AI code reviews, and third-party model usage on Free.

The page gives no numeric Free request allowance. Model choice and the work requested affect usage, so a request count would not describe every session equally. Check the remaining allowance in your settings before relying on the editor for a long task.

Paid plans now start with Plus at $8 per month. Plus covers Grok models; Premium at $30 adds capabilities such as cloud agents and access to third-party models billed separately at API list prices. Those charges are outside the advertised subscription price.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ describes weekly usage, while detailed guides explicitly describe paid weekly pools without publishing a numeric Free allowance. The editor reports exhausted usage. The prepaid guide says additional usage stops after the included pool and purchased credit are exhausted when Auto Top-up is off. Auto Top-up starts off; enabling it charges your saved payment method when the balance drops below your chosen threshold.

Hobby is a legacy plan. Its monthly pools differ from current plans. Current public docs do not explicitly state Free signup card requirements or its precise Free-specific exhaustion steps. Check your actual plan and remaining allowance in settings.

## Gotchas

- Free Agent and Chat limits are qualitative, so they do not provide a dependable daily request budget.
- Third-party model charges on eligible paid plans are separate from the subscription and use prepaid credit.
- Legacy Hobby and current Free accounts have different plan descriptions; old monthly rules should not be assumed to describe new signups.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cursor free?

Yes. The current Free plan costs $0 and includes limited Agent and Chat requests. Paid features and additional usage have separate terms.

### How many requests does the Cursor free plan include?

The pricing page does not publish exact numbers. Check your account's remaining usage rather than assuming an older Hobby allowance applies.

### What happens when I go over the Cursor Free limit?

The current guide describes a notification, waiting for the usage reset, adding prepaid credit, or upgrading. It does not give a separate Free-only exhaustion policy.
