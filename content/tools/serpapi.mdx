You send a GET request with your API key and get the search results back as JSON or markdown. It fits developers who build search tools, SEO checks, or research scripts.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and gives you 250 searches per month. The guaranteed throughput is 50 successful searches per hour. The pricing page says to spread searches evenly through the hour and across hours.

Only successful searches count. Cached, errored, and failed searches do not. A response with 100 results counts as one search, and so does an empty result set. SerpApi runs each request in a full browser, solves CAPTCHAs for you, and lets you pick a location so results match what a person in that city would see.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a hard stop for free accounts. It does say what to do when a plan runs out of searches. You can switch on Automatic Early Renewal, which renews the plan once you use all your searches. You can also renew by hand. Either way, your searches are replenished and your renewal date updates.

For free users the practical choice is to wait for the next month or move to a paid plan. The Starter plan costs $25 a month for 1,000 searches. Every plan has a 99.95% SLA.

## Gotchas

- Throughput is capped at 20% of plan volume per hour on plans under 1 million searches a month, which is why the free plan allows 50 an hour.
- Cached results are free and are not counted, but they can be stale. The cache expires after 1h, and you can set `no_cache` to true to force a fresh fetch.
- A refund covers only the first 7 days after you subscribe to a paid plan, and only if you used 20% or less of the searches.

## Questions people ask

### Is SerpApi free?

Yes, there is a free plan. It gives you 250 searches per month and 50 successful searches per hour. The pricing page does not say whether a credit card is needed to register.

### What counts as a search on SerpApi?

Only successful searches count. Cached, errored, and failed searches do not count. A response with many results still counts as one search.

### What happens when I run out of SerpApi searches?

You can renew your plan early, either automatically or by hand, and your searches are replenished. On the free plan, you can also wait for the next monthly reset.
