You sign up, run the setup wizard or add a snippet or library, and read results in the PostHog dashboard. A PostHog API also exists. It suits small teams that want several product tools without paying for each.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan needs no credit card, and PostHog says it is not a trial. Your free allowance renews every month. It is the same whether or not you have a card on file. Free includes unlimited team members, 1 project, and 1-year data retention, plus community support.

Each product has its own monthly allowance. You get 1 million product analytics events and 1M feature-flag requests. You also get 5,000 session replay recordings and 1,500 survey responses. Logs allow 10 GB ingested, data warehouse allows 1M rows, and data pipelines allow 10K events. PostHog AI includes 500 credits.

Experiments are billed with feature flags. PostHog says 97% of companies use it for free.

## When you hit a limit

On the Free plan, usage stops at the free tier limits, so you cannot be charged by surprise. To get more, you add a card and move to pay-as-you-go. It keeps the same free tier every month, and you only pay for what you use above it.

Pay-as-you-go gives you unlimited usage, 6 projects, and 7-year data retention. You can set a billing limit for each product, so a viral week does not create a large bill. After the first 1M events each month, analytics events cost $0.00005 each.

## Gotchas

- Free covers 1 project only. More projects need a card and the pay-as-you-go plan.
- Data is kept for 1 year on Free, and for 7 years after you add a card.
- Product analytics events and feature-flag requests have separate allowances.

## Questions people ask

### Is PostHog free?

Yes. The Free plan has a monthly allowance for every product, including 1 million events and 5,000 session replay recordings. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the PostHog free limit?

Usage stops at the free tier limits, so you are not charged by surprise. Add a card if you need more.

### Does PostHog need a credit card?

No. Only add a card if you need more than the free tier limits, advanced features, or more projects.
