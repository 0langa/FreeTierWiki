You log in with GitHub, add a repository from the dashboard, and read the issues in the project page. DeepScan can also review pull requests and show a grade badge for your README. It suits open-source maintainers and small teams on GitHub.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for open-source projects and costs $0. It includes unlimited public projects, JavaScript and TypeScript analysis, automatic sync with GitHub, a grade system for code quality, and 3 months of historical trends.

DeepScan works on JavaScript projects that use TypeScript, Node.js, React, Vue.js, and Astro. You can add only repositories where you have owner permission. The site also has a demo where you paste code or upload a file for a quick check.

Private projects need a paid plan. Lite costs $9 per seat per month and covers 1 private project. Starter costs $19 per seat per month and covers 5 private projects.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no usage cap for public projects, so the limit is the kind of project, not a quota. When you add a private repository, you can start a free trial. The trial gives you the full paid plan for 14 days with no credit card.

After 14 days the trial expires on its own. You then need to upgrade to keep using DeepScan on private repositories. The pricing page does not say what happens to the results of a private project after the trial ends.

## Gotchas

- The login session expires after 14 days since your last access, so you log in with GitHub again after a long break.
- DeepScan asks to read your e-mail address and your repositories when you authorize it.
- Paid plans bill per seat, and the pricing page says removing seats is not supported.

## Questions people ask

### Is DeepScan free?

Yes, for open-source projects. The Free plan covers unlimited public projects with JavaScript and TypeScript analysis. Private projects need a paid plan.

### Does DeepScan need a credit card?

No. The 14-day trial of the paid plan needs no credit card, and you sign up with your GitHub account.

### What happens after the DeepScan trial ends?

The trial expires after 14 days. You need to upgrade to keep using DeepScan on private repositories.
