You add the editor to a page with one embed line, a React component, or an iframe. Visitors then edit the PDF right in their browser. It suits people who want fill-and-sign without building an editor.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is for individuals who edit PDFs. It includes adding SimplePDF to your site, simple electronic signatures, no ads, and an unlimited number of end users and PDFs. The pricing table shows no monthly quota for the free plan. On the free editor, the document stays in your browser, and SimplePDF never receives or stores the PDF. The free plan is listed as GDPR compliant and HIPAA compliant.

The free plan leaves out a dashboard, email notifications, webhook notifications, custom editor branding, and API access. Paid plans start with Basic at $99 per month, which adds a dashboard with 5 seats and a quota of 2,500 documents and submissions per month.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan has no monthly quota on the pricing page, so there is no limit to hit. The pricing page does not describe a limit or a throttle for it. Paid plans do have a quota. For those, SimplePDF says it notifies you at 80% and 100% of your monthly quota, and it does not block requests or fail submissions in production. SimplePDF bills a flat monthly price and never per document.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has no dashboard, so you cannot see submissions in one place.
- The free plan has no email or webhook notifications when someone submits a PDF.
- The pages we read describe filling, signing, and submitting existing PDFs. They describe no PDF generation from HTML or Markdown.

## Questions people ask

### Is SimplePDF free?

Yes, for individuals. The free plan lets you add the editor to your site with electronic signatures and no ads. It has no dashboard, notifications, or API access.

### Does SimplePDF store my PDF?

Not on the free editor. The document stays in your browser, and SimplePDF never receives or stores the PDF.

### How much does SimplePDF cost after the free plan?

Basic costs $99 per month and includes up to 2,500 documents and submissions per month. Pro and Premium cost more and add more quota and features.
