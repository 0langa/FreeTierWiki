Bytebase is a tool for database change management. Developers submit schema changes, teammates review them, and Bytebase applies them and records the history. It also has a SQL editor with access control, so people can query databases without sharing passwords. You can use it as Bytebase Cloud or run it yourself.

## What the free plan gives you

The Community plan is free forever and aimed at individuals and small teams. It covers up to 20 users and 10 database instances, with database change management and community support. The docs page comparing self-host and Cloud says Bytebase Cloud offers the Community and Pro plans, so the free plan is available hosted as well as self-hosted.

Bytebase Cloud is for databases reachable over the public internet, such as Neon, Supabase, or RDS with a public IP. It sets up instantly, updates automatically, and runs on Google Cloud in the US.

## When you hit a limit

The Community plan's limits are counts of users and database instances, not usage. The pricing page does not say what happens when you need more users or instances than the plan allows; it points to upgrading. Pro costs $20 per user per month, keeps 10 database instances, and adds email support, Google and GitHub SSO, and groups.

Enterprise is quoted by sales and adds custom instance counts, approval flows, data masking, and SSO through OIDC or LDAP. Self-hosted Community instances can start a 14-day Enterprise trial. Pro is cloud-only.

## Gotchas

- Bytebase Cloud cannot reach a database that only exists inside a private network or VPC; that needs self-hosting.
- Pro keeps the same 10 database instances as the free plan, so more instances means Enterprise.
- The pricing page has separate Self-host and Cloud tabs, and the Enterprise plan is not yet on Cloud.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bytebase free?

Yes. The Community plan is free forever for up to 20 users and 10 database instances.

### Is there a free hosted version of Bytebase?

Yes. The docs say Bytebase Cloud offers the Community plan as well as Pro, for databases reachable over the public internet.

### Is there a free trial of Bytebase Enterprise?

Yes, for self-hosted installs. The pricing FAQ says you can try the Enterprise plan for free for 14 days.
