App Distribution is the Firebase tool for getting pre-release builds of iOS and Android apps to testers. You upload a build, choose testers or groups, and they receive an invitation to install it. It suits mobile developers who want beta builds in testers' hands without going through an app store review.

## What the free plan gives you

The Firebase pricing page lists App Distribution as a no-cost product. It costs nothing on the no-cost Spark plan and on the pay-as-you-go Blaze plan, and Spark needs no billing account. The separate troubleshooting guide publishes tester and release quotas.

You manage iOS and Android pre-release distributions from the same place. Testers get fast onboarding, with no SDK to install, and instant app delivery. You can distribute builds from the Firebase console, the Firebase command line tool, fastlane, Gradle for Android, or the REST API, and automate releases from a CI system. You can also collect feedback from testers and notify them about new builds.

## When you hit a limit

There is no bill to reach, since App Distribution has no paid tier. There are technical quotas: the maximum file size for all binaries is 2048 MiB, which is a little over 2 GB. An upload over that limit fails with an error about processing your distribution.

A project supports 500 testers and each group 200; you can request a no-cost increase. Each app keeps at most 1,000 releases, and releases expire after 150 days. Older releases are removed from the dashboard and tester app, though installed copies keep running. Invitations expire after 30 days and can be resent.

## Gotchas

- It distributes test builds only; public releases still go through Google Play or the App Store.
- Ad Hoc iOS builds need each tester's device registered before they can install, which adds a step for every new iPhone.
- Large tester lists are easier to manage with groups, CSV imports, or invite links than one email at a time.

## Questions people ask

### Is Firebase App Distribution free?

Yes. The Firebase pricing page lists App Distribution as no-cost on both the Spark and Blaze plans.

### What is the maximum build size?

The maximum file size limit for all binaries is 2048 MiB, a little over 2 GB.

### Do testers need to install an SDK?

No. Testers get fast onboarding with no SDK to install.
