You manage templates, keys, and usage in a dashboard. Then you generate PDFs with a REST API, a JavaScript SDK, or a Zapier integration. It suits teams, but a single person can use it too.

## What the free plan gives you

The Forever Free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It gives you 250 pages per month and 3 templates. The pricing page counts pages, not documents. A PDF with 10 pages takes 10 pages from your quota. Previews inside the editor do not count. You are only charged for requests through the API, the SDK, or Zapier. The pricing page says the free account never expires.

Paid plans add volume. Startup costs $29 per month for 2,500 pages and 10 templates, plus chat and email support. Growth costs $69 per month for 10,000 pages and 30 templates. Enterprise has unlimited templates and custom terms.

## When you hit a limit

Export SDK sends you an email when you reach 85% of your plan's limit. If you hit the limit, it prompts you to upgrade your plan or buy more pages in packs of 1,000. The pricing page does not say whether API requests fail at that point. On paid plans, extra pages cost $12 per 1,000 on Startup and $6 per 1,000 on Growth.

## Gotchas

- Limits count pages. A long PDF uses up the free allowance much faster than a one-page invoice.
- The free plan includes only 3 templates, so a project with many layouts will need a paid plan.
- By default the request data is not saved. The docs describe an optional setting that saves it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Export SDK free?

Yes. The Forever Free plan gives you 250 pages per month and 3 templates, with no credit card. The pricing page says it never expires.

### How does Export SDK count pages?

It counts pages, not documents. A 10-page PDF uses 10 pages of your quota, and editor previews are free.

### What happens when I run out of Export SDK pages?

You get an email at 85% of your limit. At the limit, you are prompted to upgrade or buy extra pages in packs of 1,000.
