You send plain HTTPS GET requests to `ipapi.co` from any language, and paid plans add a token in the query string. There is no self-serve free sign-up, so you contact the team to ask for a trial. It suits developers who want to test a lookup API before they buy.

## What the free plan gives you

ipapi has no permanent free plan. The pricing table lists a Free Trial row with the note "Please contact us for a trial". The FAQ says the trial gives you up to 1000 IP lookups in a day, about 30K a month, for a limited time.

The trial is for testing and development. The FAQ says it is not meant for production or deployments, and that the system may restrict trial calls further under some conditions. The trial has no usage monitoring and only limited bulk IP lookup. Paid plans start at $15 a month for 60 000 lookups.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ does not describe what happens when you pass the 1000 daily lookups. It only says the trial is limited and may be restricted further. Plan for blocked or failed calls, not a bill.

A paid plan counts every conversion of an address as a lookup, and a repeat query counts again. Paid plans have usage alerts by email before you run out of quota and an API to monitor usage. The trial does not have them.

## Gotchas

- You cannot sign up for the trial on your own. You must contact the team and wait for access.
- The trial is a limited-time offer, not an always-free plan, and it is not meant for production.
- Every request counts as a lookup, even a repeat of the same address. Cache results on your side.

## Questions people ask

### Is ipapi free?

Only as a trial. You must contact the team to request it. The FAQ says it gives up to 1000 IP lookups a day for a limited time.

### Can I use the ipapi free trial in production?

No. The FAQ says the trial is for testing and development and is not meant for production or deployments.

### How much do ipapi paid plans cost?

Monthly billing starts at $15 for 60 000 lookups a month. Annual billing starts at $12 a month, billed as $144 a year.
