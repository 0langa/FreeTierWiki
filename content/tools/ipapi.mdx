You send plain HTTPS GET requests to `ipapi.co` from any language, and paid plans add a token in the query string. The homepage directs trial requests to support, while the FAQ describes basic access without signup. It suits developers who want to test a lookup API before they buy.

## What the free plan gives you

The homepage lists a Free Trial row and asks you to contact the team. It describes up to 1000 IP lookups daily, approximately 30K monthly, for a limited time. It restricts this offer to testing and development, excludes production deployments, and allows further restrictions.

The separate official FAQ describes a free trial/testing plan with no time limit, no card, and basic fields without signup; it directs full-access or higher-limit trials to support. These public descriptions do not establish one universal trial duration or access path. Confirm the scope you need rather than assuming all trial features work anonymously. Paid plans start at $15 monthly for 60,000 lookups.

## When you hit a limit

The API reference documents HTTP 429 with reason RateLimited when quota is exceeded. This establishes a possible quota error, not a guarantee of the exact response or billing treatment for every trial arrangement. The homepage says trial calls may be restricted further; requested-trial duration and card requirements are not specified.

Paid plans count each lookup, including repeat queries, and provide usage monitoring and email alerts. The homepage excludes usage monitoring from its trial. Ask for the granted allowance and cap behavior before depending on it, and handle quota errors rather than repeatedly retrying exhausted access.

## Gotchas

- The homepage requires a trial request, but the FAQ allows basic fields without signup. Ask support which features and limits apply to your intended use.
- Trial expiry is inconsistent across official pages: the homepage says limited time and the FAQ says no time limit. Neither grants production use.
- Every request counts as a lookup, even a repeat of the same address. Cache results on your side.

## Questions people ask

### Is ipapi free?

Free access is described as trial/testing use. The homepage advertises requested limited-time access; the FAQ separately offers no-card basic access without signup or a time limit.

### Can I use the ipapi free trial in production?

No. The homepage says its trial is for testing and development and is not meant for production or deployments.

### How much do ipapi paid plans cost?

Monthly billing starts at $15 for 60 000 lookups a month. Annual billing starts at $12 a month, billed as $144 a year.
