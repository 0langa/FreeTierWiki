You work in the web app: create a project, add cases to suites, and start a run. Developers can also send automated results through the Qase API with a token.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per user per month and is described as free forever. It is meant for students, hobbyists, educators, and non-profits who want to learn the basics of a test management system.

Free includes up to 4 full seats, up to 2 projects, 500 MB of storage, and 30 days of test history. You can have 2 active test runs. Test case management, defects, and shared steps are included. Automated results sent through the API are capped at 5,000 per month, and the API rate limit is 150 requests per minute.

The Teams plan costs $35 per user per month when billed annually, and $42 when billed monthly. It adds unlimited projects, runs, and storage, plus SSO and role-based access control.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists these numbers as the Free plan limits. It does not say what Qase does when you pass them, such as more than 5,000 API results in a month or a third project. Plan on being blocked rather than billed.

Free asks for no card, so there is no bill to run up. If you outgrow the limits, you move to Teams. Teams has a 14 day trial with no credit card and no obligation, so you can test the upgrade first.

## Gotchas

- Free allows up to 4 full seats. Collaborators who only view runs and reports are not included on Free; they are a paid add-on at $10 per seat.
- Test history is kept for 30 days on Free, compared with 2 years on Teams.
- The pricing page lists Jira, GitHub, and GitLab integrations as not included on Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Qase free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and is free forever. It includes up to 4 full seats, 2 projects, and 500 MB of storage.

### Does Qase need a credit card?

No credit card is needed for the Free plan. The Teams trial also says no credit card is needed for its 14 day trial.

### How many users can I have on the Qase free plan?

Free allows up to 4 full seats. Teams starts at 5 seats, and Enterprise has unlimited users.
