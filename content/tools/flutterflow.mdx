FlutterFlow is a visual development environment for Flutter. You lay out screens, wire up logic and state, connect Firebase or Supabase, and FlutterFlow generates a Flutter app for mobile, web, and desktop. It suits developers and founders who want Flutter apps faster than writing every widget by hand.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for building and testing your app and costs $0. It includes the visual development environment, 1,000+ prebuilt templates, API and data integration, and web publishing. You can have up to 2 projects and up to 2 API endpoints, with Firebase and Supabase integration.

AI generation is limited to 5 requests per lifetime, and you get 10 Lite credits. Web deployment uses up to 2 free subdomains. Free has 1 editor, the main branch only, and automated snapshot backups up to 1 hour prior. Code download, APK download, and a custom domain start on Basic at $39 per month.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan's limits are counts, not metered usage: 2 projects, 2 API endpoints, 5 lifetime AI requests, and 10 Lite credits. The pricing page does not say what happens when you use them up, beyond listing larger amounts on paid plans, such as unlimited projects and 50 AI requests per month on Basic. There are no overage charges listed for Free.

## Gotchas

- You cannot download the generated Flutter code or an APK on Free, so the app stays inside FlutterFlow until you pay.
- AI generation on Free is 5 requests for the life of the account, not per month.
- One-click app store deployment and local device testing need Basic or higher.

## Questions people ask

### Is FlutterFlow free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you build and test apps with up to 2 projects and publish web apps on up to 2 free subdomains.

### Can I export my code on FlutterFlow's Free plan?

No. Code download and APK download start on the Basic plan at $39 per month.

### How many AI requests does FlutterFlow Free include?

5 AI generation requests per lifetime, plus 10 Lite credits.
