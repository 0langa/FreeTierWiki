FlutterFlow is a visual development environment for Flutter, now named FlutterFlow Studio. You lay out screens, wire up logic and state, connect Firebase or Supabase, and FlutterFlow generates a Flutter app for mobile, web, and desktop. It suits developers and founders who want Flutter apps faster than writing every widget by hand.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for building and testing your app and costs $0. It includes the visual development environment, 1,000+ prebuilt templates, API and data integration, and web publishing. You can have up to 2 projects and up to 2 API endpoints, with Firebase and Supabase integration.

AI use comes from one allowance shared across FlutterFlow and Studio, and the pricing page lists it for Free only as a free AI allowance, with no number. It is shown as the percentage left. Lite credits are retired. Web deployment uses up to 2 free subdomains. Free has 1 editor, the main branch only, and automated snapshot backups up to 1 hour prior. Code download, APK download, and a custom domain start on Basic at $39 per month.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan counts 2 projects and 2 API endpoints, and its AI allowance is shown as a percentage. The pricing page does not say what happens when you use them up, beyond listing more on paid plans, such as unlimited projects on Basic and refills on Pro and Max. There are currently no overage charges listed for Free. Students and educators at colleges and universities can apply for a free year.

## Gotchas

- You cannot download the generated Flutter code or an APK on Free, so the app stays inside FlutterFlow until you pay.
- The size of the free AI allowance is not published, so do not plan around a fixed number of AI requests.
- One-click app store deployment and the Local Run desktop emulator need Basic or higher.

## Questions people ask

### Is FlutterFlow free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you build and test apps with up to 2 projects and publish web apps on up to 2 free subdomains.

### Can I export my code on FlutterFlow's Free plan?

No. Code download and APK download start on the Basic plan at $39 per month.

### How many AI requests does FlutterFlow Free include?

The pricing page says only a free AI allowance and gives no number. FlutterFlow and Studio share it, it is shown as the percentage left, and Lite credits are retired.
