Sauce Labs is a testing cloud with virtual browsers, emulators and simulators, and real Android and iOS devices. You point Selenium, Appium, Espresso, or XCUITest suites at it, or open a device by hand for live testing. It is for teams that want to test across many browsers and devices without keeping a device lab.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no always-free plan for most developers; there is a trial. The sign-up page says you get a free account with no credit card required, and the free 28-day trial includes instant access to real devices, 60 automated and live testing minutes, local testing with Sauce Connect, error reporting with Backtrace, and 3 virtual machines in parallel.

The pricing page also says Sauce Labs supports open source projects with free accounts for qualifying initiatives. The details of who qualifies are on a separate page that did not load in this check.

## When you hit a limit

The sign-up page lists both a 28-day length and 60 testing minutes, but it does not say what happens when the minutes run out before the days do. To keep testing you buy a plan. The paid plans on the pricing page advertise unlimited users and unlimited testing minutes, priced by plan type.

Monthly credit card plans are billed automatically each month and can be cancelled at any time without penalty. Annual credit card plans are charged up front. The pricing page does not say what happens to your tests or data when a trial ends without a purchase.

## Gotchas

- 60 minutes covers a few runs of a small suite, not a full evaluation of a large one, so plan what you test first.
- The trial clock runs for 28 days from sign-up, even on days you do not test.
- The pricing page lists plan names and features, but some prices and the Enterprise terms need a conversation with sales.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sauce Labs free?

Not permanently. New accounts get a free 28-day trial with 60 automated and live testing minutes and no credit card. Qualifying open source projects can get free accounts.

### Does the Sauce Labs trial need a credit card?

No. The sign-up page says no credit card is required.

### Can I test a site running on my laptop?

Yes. The trial includes local testing with Sauce Connect, which lets the cloud browsers reach an app on your machine or private network.
