You build forms in a web builder and publish them as a live form. Zoho lists many app connections, so answers can flow into the tools you already use.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is for 1 user. It allows 3 forms, unlimited form views, 500 submissions a month, and 200 MB of file storage. Zoho says the free plan is and stays free, and you can use it as long as it meets your needs.

You also get 10 payment submissions through gateways such as Stripe, PayPal, and Square. Custom PDFs can generate up to 50 PDFs. The plan includes pre-built themes, UTM tracking, and webhooks.

Integrations include Zoho apps such as CRM, Desk, Campaigns, and Sheet. They also include Salesforce, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Microsoft Teams, and Zapier. The pricing page lists no custom branding on the free plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 500 submissions in a month or after 200 MB of storage. Its FAQ says you can raise the submission limit or storage with a monthly or yearly add-on, but the add-on table says add-ons apply only to paid plans, so the free plan has no stated way to buy more.

The free plan has no card on file, so no bill can come from it. Basic is the first paid plan. It lists unlimited forms and 10,000 submissions a month, with 500 MB of storage. The pricing page text does not show prices, so check them in your browser.

## Gotchas

- The free plan allows 3 forms and 1 user. Basic raises forms to unlimited but still lists 1 user.
- Only 10 payment submissions are included on the free plan, so it is not for a shop.
- The Powered by Zoho footer stays on your form until you move to a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Forms free?

Yes. The free plan allows 3 forms and 500 submissions a month for 1 user. Zoho says the free plan stays free.

### How many submissions can I get on the Zoho Forms free plan?

The free plan lists 500 submissions a month. The FAQ mentions submission add-ons, but the add-on table says they apply only to paid plans, so moving to a paid plan is the stated way to get more.

### How do I remove the Powered by Zoho footer?

Upgrade to a paid plan. Zoho says the footer is removed automatically once you do.
