You chat in the Cliq web app or in the mobile apps. An admin sets up the organization first, then adds people to channels.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan includes 100 GB of file storage per company and 10,000 searchable messages. Channels can have 100 participants, and audio and video meetings can have up to 100 participants for up to 60 minutes.

You can also build with the platform: the free plan lists 10 extensions, 100 bots, 100 slash commands, 50 databases, and 25 widgets per company. Call recording and external channels are not available. The planner is listed at 50 per company, and the free plan also lists 1,000 database entries per company for your own apps.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page gives a 14-day trial of paid features. After it, you must buy a subscription to keep the paid features. If you do not, Zoho moves the account to the free plan, where you can still communicate and collaborate with colleagues.

The page does not say what happens to messages older than the 10,000 searchable ones, or what happens when storage fills. It also lists no user cap for the free plan. Paid tiers are grouped by user count.

## Gotchas

- External channels, which let you chat with people in other organizations, are not available on the free plan.
- Call recording is not available, and meetings stop at 60 minutes.
- Storage on the free plan is 100 GB per company, while the paid plans list 100 GB per user.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Cliq free?

Yes. The free plan has 100 GB of storage per company, 10,000 searchable messages, and 100 participants per channel.

### Does Zoho Cliq need a credit card?

The pricing page does not mention a credit card. It describes a 14-day trial and a free plan that you fall back to if you do not subscribe.

### Can I have video meetings on the Zoho Cliq free plan?

Yes, for up to 100 participants. Each meeting is limited to 60 minutes, and call recording is not available.
