You start the trial from the IBM site, sign in with an IBMid, and work in API Manager for APIs and products and in the Developer Portal for consumers. There is also a command line toolkit. It suits teams that already evaluate enterprise API management and want a hands-on test.

## What the free plan gives you

IBM offers a 30-day free trial of API Connect. It is a trial, not a permanent free plan. The pricing page shows the trial next to each plan type, and it also offers a free demo on request.

The paid options are pay-as-you-go usage-based pricing hosted on Amazon Web Services, with no upfront costs, and other subscription plans for on-premises, hybrid, and multicloud setups. The pricing page lists no allowance of API calls for the trial, and it does not say whether sign-up asks for a payment card. Check the IBM sign-up form before you start.

## When you hit a limit

The limit is time. After the 30 days, the trial ends and you need a paid plan to continue. The pricing page says you can upgrade the trial to a paid subscription at any time during the 30 days.

If you upgrade with the same IBMid or email that you used for the trial, your trial instance and assets move to the paid subscription automatically. If you want a fresh start, use a different IBMid or email for the upgrade. The pages do not describe a bill during the trial.

## Gotchas

- The pages do not say what happens to trial assets after day 30, so export or recreate your API definitions before then if you do not buy.
- Upgrading with the same IBMid migrates your trial assets. Use another IBMid if you do not want them moved.
- The product is aimed at enterprises. The pages stress hybrid and multicloud gateways, which is more than a small project needs.

## Questions people ask

### Is IBM API Connect free?

Only as a trial. IBM offers a 30-day free trial. After that you need a paid plan, such as pay-as-you-go on AWS.

### What happens when the IBM API Connect trial ends?

The trial stops after the 30 days unless you upgrade. The pricing page says upgrading during the trial with the same IBMid moves your trial instance and assets to the paid subscription.

### Can I upgrade the IBM API Connect trial early?

Yes. The pricing page says you can upgrade the trial to a paid subscription at any time during the 30-day trial period.
