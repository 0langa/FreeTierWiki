WakaTime is a service that records how long you spend coding. Plugins for editors and IDEs send activity to your account, and the dashboard breaks it down by project, language, and editor. Individuals use it to build a coding habit; teams use paid plans for shared dashboards and AI usage reports.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. It includes 1 week of dashboard history, unlimited projects, shareable project charts, weekly coding reports, 1 programming goal, and a place on the public leaderboard. The pricing page says your coding history stays safely stored and exportable on Free, and the FAQ says the Free plan does not delete your code stats.

You can still show and export older stats in several places, such as embeddable charts, badges, and the data export tool. The dashboard itself only shows the last week. Upgrading later reveals your complete history, including stats from before you paid.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan's limit is how far back the dashboard looks, not how much you record, so nothing stops working. Basic is $9 a month and extends the dashboard to 2 weeks, with daily and weekly reports and 3 programming goals. Premium is $14 a month and shows your complete coding history, AI adoption and spend, and lets you export stats from the dashboard.

For companies, Team plans start with a free 14-day trial and no credit card. Each person who shows stats on team dashboards counts as a paid developer; view-only accounts do not need seats.

## Gotchas

- The free dashboard only covers the last week, so monthly or yearly trends need a paid plan.
- AI coding insights, such as model usage and AI-generated versus human code, are Premium features.
- Stats come from editor plugins, so time spent in a tool without a plugin is not counted.

## Questions people ask

### Is WakaTime free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and tracks unlimited projects, with 1 week of dashboard history.

### Does the WakaTime Free plan delete old stats?

No. The FAQ says the Free plan keeps your stats safe, and upgrading shows your full history from when you created the account.

### Is there a free trial for teams?

Yes. Team plans have a free 14-day trial with no credit card required.
