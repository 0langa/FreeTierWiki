BrowserStack is a cloud of real browsers and mobile devices for testing web and mobile apps. Live is for manual testing, Automate runs Selenium and similar suites, and newer products cover test management, accessibility, low code automation, and load testing. Percy, its visual testing product, has its own entry in this atlas; this entry covers the rest.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no single free plan. Instead, the pricing page lists a Free tier for several products. Test Management is free with unlimited test cases and test runs and import by API or CSV. Accessibility is free for testing user journeys across 5 unique pages. Load Testing is free at starter scale: up to 1,000 API virtual users or 10 browser virtual users per run, tests of at most 10 minutes, and up to 500 VUH.

Low Code Automation, App Accessibility, Test Companion, and Testing Toolkit also show free plans. Open source projects can join a program with lifetime access to Live, Automate, and Percy, with 5 users and 5 parallels.

## When you hit a limit

Live and Automate require payment after a trial; pricing does not give a general trial duration. The Live Freelancer plan costs $19 month-to-month, or $12.50 per month billed annually, for 100 minutes and one user.

Enforcement is product-specific. App Accessibility manual Free allows 50 scans per report and 25 reports per user per day. Submissions or scanning stop at the relevant limit; daily counters reset at midnight UTC. Automated Free builds are also constrained by trial capacity. Other Free products do not share one documented exhaustion policy, and public sources do not establish a universal no-card requirement. Review the selected product's allowance rather than assuming every Free tier behaves alike.

## Gotchas

- The open source program is for open source projects, and membership lasts for the duration of your project; the page does not spell out the eligibility rules.
- Many plans on the pricing page are priced per user or per parallel, so a team grows the bill quickly once the free plans stop being enough.
- Percy and BrowserStack share an account, but Percy's free screenshots are a separate allowance described in its own entry.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [Percy.io](/tools/percy-io/): when you only need screenshot comparison in CI, which is BrowserStack's visual testing product with its own free plan.
- [TestingBot](/tools/testingbot-com/): when you want another cloud grid of real browsers and devices.
- [Cypress](/tools/cypress/): when you want to run end-to-end tests locally or in CI with an open source runner.

## Questions people ask

### Is BrowserStack free?

Partly. Test Management, Accessibility, Low Code Automation, and Load Testing have free plans. Live and Automate, the cross-browser products, are paid after a free trial.

### How is BrowserStack different from Percy?

Percy is BrowserStack's visual testing product: it compares screenshots between builds. BrowserStack's Live and Automate let you open or script real browsers and devices. Percy's free plan is in its own entry.

### Can open source projects use BrowserStack for free?

Yes. The open source program gives lifetime access to Live, Automate, and Percy, with 5 users and 5 parallels.
