Qualys Community Edition is a free version of the Qualys Enterprise TruRisk Platform, a commercial vulnerability management service. It finds the IT assets on your network, scans them and your web applications for known vulnerabilities, and shows the results in dashboards and reports. The management console runs in the Qualys cloud; scanning internal infrastructure may require a scanner appliance.

## What the free plan gives you

The Community Edition page calls it a free version of the Enterprise TruRisk Platform designed for the security community, and says plainly that Qualys Community Edition is free. Qualys's free services page says the same: a community edition of the platform is available for free.

The page lists what it does. It discovers and inventories IT assets on premises, at endpoints, and in clouds and containers. It scans IT infrastructure and web applications against the full Qualys Knowledgebase of vulnerabilities, which includes thousands of CVEs and is updated continuously. It gives a single dashboard where you can drill into assets, vulnerabilities, and misconfigurations, build widgets, and generate reports. Unlimited network mapping keeps track of internal and external assets.

## When you hit a limit

Current visible pages do not confirm the asset, IP-address, or web-application allowances or their enforcement. Older guides contain numerical allowances, but those should not be treated as confirmed current terms. Card requirements are also not confirmed. Expect the scope to be small and aimed at individuals and small organisations; if you need more, the same page offers a request for a full trial of the Enterprise TruRisk Platform.

## Gotchas

- Without published limits, you learn the free scope only after signing up, so check it before planning around the tool.
- Only scan systems you own or have written permission to test.
- A vulnerability scanner finds known issues; it does not replace code review or dependency scanning.

## Questions people ask

### Is Qualys Community Edition free?

Yes. Qualys describes Community Edition as free with a cloud management console; internal scanning may require a scanner appliance.

### What can I scan with it?

IT assets on your network and in the cloud, and web applications, against the Qualys Knowledgebase of vulnerabilities.

### How many assets does the free version cover?

Current visible pages do not confirm those allowances. Check the current account terms before relying on older published quotas.
