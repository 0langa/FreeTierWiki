1Password is a password manager for people and teams. Beyond storing logins, it includes developer tools: SSH key signing, Git commit signing, a CLI, SDKs, and integrations with IDEs, CI/CD, and infrastructure-as-code tools. It suits developers who want credentials and SSH keys in one place instead of plain files.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. The business pricing page offers a 14-day free trial of Teams Starter Pack and Business with no credit card required, and the personal pricing page offers the same for Individual and Families.

Business costs $8.99 per user per month billed yearly and includes developer tools for SSH key signing, Git commit signing, the CLI, and SDKs. Teams Starter Pack is $24.95 a month billed annually for 10 members, with up to 10 more seats at per-member pricing. On the personal side, Individual is $2.99 a month and Families $4.49 a month with annual billing during the current promotion, against regular prices of $3.99 and $5.99.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing pages do not say what happens when the 14-day trial ends. You can pay by credit card or ACH direct debit for eligible US bank accounts, so you choose a plan and payment method to keep using 1Password. Teams Starter Pack has a 10-user minimum and goes up to 20 users; past that you need Business, which has a 1 user minimum.

## Gotchas

- Personal prices shown are promotional, and the regular prices are higher.
- Teams Starter Pack is billed for 10 users minimum, even for a smaller team.
- Identity provider integrations such as Okta and Entra ID need Business.

## Questions people ask

### Is 1Password free?

Not permanently. Every plan can be tried free for 14 days, with no credit card required.

### Does 1Password have tools for developers?

Yes. Business includes SSH key signing, Git commit signing, a CLI, SDKs, and integrations with IDEs and CI/CD tools.

### How much is 1Password Business?

$8.99 per user per month when billed annually, or $107.88 per user per year.
