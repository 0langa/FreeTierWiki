Pinggy is a tunneling service that gives a service on your machine a public URL. You start a tunnel with a single SSH command, so there is no binary to download, though apps for Windows, Mac, and Linux, a CLI, Docker image, and Node.js and Python SDKs exist too. Developers use it for webhook testing and quick demos.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is listed at 0 a month and marked free for life. It includes single command tunneling, HTTP(S), TCP, UDP, and TLS tunnels, live header manipulation, request and response inspection with replays, random subdomains, and a 60 minutes tunnel timeout. HTTPS tunnels come with Let's Encrypt certificates.

The docs show the free command connecting to free.pinggy.io, and the URLs it prints are on a pinggy-free.link domain. The Web Debugger for inspecting, modifying, and replaying traffic is built into the Pinggy Dashboard.

## When you hit a limit

A free tunnel times out after 60 minutes. The home page says that when the tunnel reconnects, your public URL will change, and that persistent URLs need Pinggy Pro. So after the timeout you start the tunnel again and share the new address.

Pro adds persistent tunnels and custom subdomains and has a 7 days free trial. Enterprise adds dedicated or on-premise servers, unlimited persistent tunnels and custom subdomains, and an API to monitor and manage tunnels. The free plan has no usage charges on the page.

## Gotchas

- The public URL changes every time the tunnel reconnects, so webhook settings at the provider need updating each time.
- The 60-minute timeout cuts off long debugging sessions; plan to restart the command.
- Free tunnels get random subdomains on a pinggy-free.link domain; choosing your own subdomain needs Pro.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pinggy free?

Yes. The free plan is free for life, with HTTP(S), TCP, UDP, and TLS tunnels and a 60-minute tunnel timeout.

### Do I need to install anything to use Pinggy?

No. You can start a tunnel with an SSH command; apps, a CLI, Docker, and SDKs are optional.

### Does my Pinggy URL stay the same?

Not on the free plan. The URL changes when the tunnel reconnects. Persistent URLs need Pro.
