You set your form's action to a FormKeep endpoint or post JSON to it like a regular API. You can also use the drag-and-drop form builder if you do not want to write HTML.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and lets you create unlimited forms. It includes spam protection, a form designer, and a branded thank-you page. The plan allows 50 submissions a month, and the pricing page says each form has its own monthly limit.

Notifications arrive daily on the free plan, and FormKeep stores submissions for 1 month. Paid plans keep them for longer, from 3 months up to forever. Premium is inconsistent: its card says permanent retention, but the comparison table says 12 months; confirm that paid entitlement before subscribing.

FormKeep shows its branding in notification emails and on hosted forms. On the paid plans you control the thank-you message and can redirect people back to your site. Essential adds attachments, field rules, and webhooks. The Free integration cell is empty; All integrations starts on Professional.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page now says excess submissions are kept waiting, hidden from your dashboard and not delivered. They are released when the monthly count resets or you upgrade. Submissions still waiting when they reach the form's retention period are deleted; Free retention is 1 month. This wording was observed on November 23, 2026; the provider does not give an effective date.

Excess submissions are held rather than billed automatically. Free signup does not require a credit card. Higher allowances require a paid plan. Essential costs $4.99 a month for 1k submissions and includes 1 form. Professional costs $19.50 a month for 10k submissions. Premium costs $59.00 a month for 100k submissions.

## Gotchas

- Submissions over the limit are temporarily hidden and undelivered. Those still waiting after 1 month are deleted on Free.
- Free notifications are daily, while the Professional plan lists realtime notifications.
- File attachments are not part of the free plan, and the Essential plan lists them as an added feature.

## Questions people ask

### Is FormKeep free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and includes unlimited forms with 50 submissions a month. Paid plans start at $4.99 a month.

### What happens when I go over the FormKeep submission limit?

They wait, hidden and undelivered, until the monthly count resets or you upgrade. Any still waiting at the retention deadline are deleted. There is no published automatic overage charge.

### Does FormKeep need a credit card?

No credit card is required for a free account, according to FormKeep's privacy policy.
