You set your form's action to a FormKeep endpoint or post JSON to it like a regular API. You can also use the drag-and-drop form builder if you do not want to write HTML.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and lets you create unlimited forms. It includes spam protection, a form designer, and a branded thank-you page. The plan allows 50 submissions a month, and the pricing page says each form has its own monthly limit.

Notifications arrive daily on the free plan, and FormKeep stores submissions for 1 month. Paid plans keep them for longer, from 3 months up to forever.

FormKeep shows its branding in notification emails and on hosted forms. On the paid plans you control the thank-you message and can redirect people back to your site. The Essential plan adds file attachments and dynamic field rules, and its integrations column reads All instead of Webhook. Webhook is the only integration the table lists for the free plan.

## When you hit a limit

FormKeep ignores submissions that go over the monthly limit. The pricing page says so directly. It does not say that it holds them for you, so treat those entries as lost.

The free plan needs no card, so no bill can come from it. To raise the limit, you move to a paid plan. Essential costs $4.99 a month for 1k submissions and includes 1 form. Professional costs $19.50 a month for 10k submissions. Premium costs $59.00 a month for 100k submissions.

## Gotchas

- Submissions over the limit are ignored, not saved. Watch your count in busy months.
- Free notifications are daily, while the Professional plan lists realtime notifications.
- File attachments are not part of the free plan, and the Essential plan lists them as an added feature.

## Questions people ask

### Is FormKeep free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and includes unlimited forms with 50 submissions a month. Paid plans start at $4.99 a month.

### What happens when I go over the FormKeep submission limit?

FormKeep ignores submissions past the monthly limit. The pricing page names no overage charge and does not say they are held for later.

### Does FormKeep need a credit card?

The pricing page shows no card step for the free plan. You choose Start Free Account to sign up.
