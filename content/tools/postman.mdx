You use Postman through its desktop app or in the browser, and from the command line with the Postman CLI. It suits one developer or tester who wants a full API client without paying.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is for one user. Since the 2026 plan change, it cannot create a team. It includes the API client, collections and environments, mock servers, and the Postman CLI. Mock server use and collection runs are unlimited on all plans. Each month you get 50 AI credits, 1,000 monitoring requests, and 10,000 calls to the Postman API.

The Solo plan costs $9 per month billed annually. It adds more AI credits, data-driven testing, and custom domains. The Team plan costs $19 per user per month billed annually and adds collaboration and basic role-based access control.

## When you hit a limit

When your monitoring requests run out, you get an email and a message in the app. Monitors and scheduled collection runs then stop running on their schedule until the monthly reset. Pay-as-you-go can resume them, and the docs list add-ons and upgrades as the other options. The Free plan has no card on file, so this does not bill you. AI overages on pay-as-you-go are listed for paid plans.

## Gotchas

- Before March 2026, Free plans could invite up to three users. The new Free plan is limited to one user.
- A collection run with several iterations counts each iteration as a run, but runs are unlimited in the 2026 plans.
- Scheduled runs in the Postman Cloud use the monitoring quota, not the manual run quota.

## Questions people ask

### Is Postman free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 for one user. It includes the API client, mock servers, unlimited collection runs, 1,000 monitoring requests, and 50 AI credits per month.

### Can a team use Postman for free?

No. The Free plan is limited to one user and cannot create a team. Team collaboration needs a paid plan.

### What happens when I use up my Postman monitoring requests?

You get an email and an in-app notice. Your monitors and scheduled collection runs stop until the monthly reset, unless you enable pay-as-you-go, buy add-ons, or upgrade.
