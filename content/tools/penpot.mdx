You design in the browser, organize work in teams, projects, and files, and hand developers a view that shows CSS. Penpot also offers self-hosting, so it suits teams that care about open source or data control.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Professional plan at $0 per user per month and describes it as free and fully featured, with no limits and no missing tools. Every plan has the same core features: no file limits within your storage capacity, unlimited teams, and open source code with no vendor lock-in.

You can create and join as many teams as you need. The help center says there are no team size limits when you add stakeholders. Viewers can view, comment on, and inspect files but cannot edit them. Editors can create, import, edit, and manage files and libraries, and admins can invite members and change team settings.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you run out of storage. It describes no file limits within your storage capacity, so storage is the ceiling to watch. Version history is another difference between plans.

The Unlimited plan costs $7 per editor per month, with a cap of $175 per month no matter your team size. It adds extra space, extended history, and early access to features. The Enterprise plan costs $25 per member per month, and Private Server costs $50k a year.

## Gotchas

- The pricing table lists storage caps of 10GB and 25GB, and history windows of 7, 30, and 90 days, across plans. Check the table for the free values before you plan a large library.
- Only one owner exists per team, and the owner must transfer ownership before leaving.
- Deleting a team permanently deletes all its projects and files. Only the team owner can do it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Penpot free?

Yes. The Professional plan costs $0 per user per month and is described as free and fully featured. It includes unlimited teams and no file limits within your storage capacity.

### Is Penpot really open source?

Yes. The pricing page lists open source as a core feature of every plan, with no vendor lock-in. Penpot can also be self-hosted.

### What does paid Penpot add?

The Unlimited plan costs $7 per editor per month, with a cap of $175 per month. It adds extra space, extended history, and early features.
