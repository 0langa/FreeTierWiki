Zeplin sits between design tools and code. Designers publish screens to it, and developers inspect measurements, styles, and assets, follow flows, and see which components a screen uses. It connects to Jira, Storybook, VS Code, Slack, and more, so specs stay linked to the work.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and covers 1 project. The project can hold plenty of screens to start, 100, and has unlimited members who can all edit it. You get 1 styleguide with up to 100 components and 30 days of version history in a personal workspace.

All plans include annotations, flows, and approvals, screen versioning with version compare, screen variants, component versioning, and integrations including Jira Cloud, Azure DevOps, the VS Code extension, Storybook, Slack, Teams, the Zeplin API, and webhooks. On-premises Jira Server integration requires Advanced or higher. The AI features in beta are limited on Free to 3 screens a week of AI Design Review and 3 runs a week of AI Organize.

## When you hit a limit

The limits are counts of projects, screens, and components. The pricing page does not say what happens when the project is full; it points to paid plans. Basic is a pay-per-project plan from $13.75 a month billed annually, or $15 with monthly billing, with a flat rate for 1, 3, 6, 9, or 12 projects, unlimited users, and practically unlimited screens of 1,000 per project.

Advanced is priced per seat at $12 per seat per month, paid annually, with 50 projects, roles, flow boards, and unlimited styleguides, components, and version history.

## Gotchas

- Every member can edit on Free, but only Owners can publish or delete designs. There is no granular read-only role.
- Version history goes back only 30 days, so older iterations are outside the accessible Free history; this does not establish deletion.
- The single project fills up quickly if you publish several variants of each screen.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zeplin free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 1 project with 100 screens and unlimited members.

### How many screens can I upload on Zeplin's free plan?

100 screens in your one project. Paid per-project plans allow 1,000 per project.

### Does the Zeplin free plan include the API and integrations?

Yes. The pricing page lists all integrations, including Jira, Storybook, the Zeplin API, and webhooks, on every plan.
