Backlog is a project management tool from Nulab that combines issue tracking, a Kanban board, and Git and Subversion repositories. A developer team would pick it when it wants bugs, tasks, and code hosting for a project in one product, and its paid plans charge per team rather than per user.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is labeled free forever. It allows 10 users and includes 1 project, 100 MB of storage, Git and Subversion, and the Board view. You track work as issues in that project, and Nulab hosts the service on backlog.com.

Attachments are tight on Free: each issue can hold 1 file, up to 5 MB, while files stored inside the project can be up to 100 MB each. iOS and Android apps are available. The Starter plan costs $35 per month billed monthly and raises the limits to 30 users, 5 projects, and 1 GB of storage, and adds subtasking.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan stops at 1 project and 10 users, and the next plan up, Starter, allows 5 projects and 30 users. The pricing page does not say what happens when storage reaches 100 MB or when you try to add a user past the 10 included.

Backlog's paid plans are flat monthly prices for the team rather than per-user fees; the page headline says project management built for growth with no per-user fees. The page does not list overage charges for the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- One project means one codebase or product per space; a second project needs Starter.
- The 1-file, 5 MB limit per issue makes screenshots and logs hard to attach.
- Gantt charts, burndown charts, and custom fields are on Standard or Premium.

## Questions people ask

### Is Backlog free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, is labeled free forever, and covers 10 users and 1 project.

### Does Backlog Free include Git?

Yes. Git and Subversion are listed on the Free plan.

### How much storage does Backlog Free have?

100 MB in total, with 1 attachment of up to 5 MB per issue.
