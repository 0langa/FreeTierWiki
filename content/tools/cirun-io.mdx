You describe your runners in a `.cirun.yml` file and point your GitHub Actions workflow at them. Cirun starts a machine in your cloud for each job. This suits teams that need GPU or ARM runners and are happy to use their own cloud account.

## What the free plan gives you

The Open Source plan costs $0 and is free forever. You can install Cirun on unlimited public repositories. It allows unlimited runners, including ARM-based runners and GPU runners, on all supported cloud providers.

Cirun is a managed runner platform for GitHub Actions. You bring credentials for AWS, GCP, Azure, Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Oracle, OpenStack, or Vast.ai. You can also register existing Linux, macOS, or Windows machines as a runner pool. Private repositories are not part of the free plan. The Startup plan costs $29 per month for 3 private repositories, and Business costs $79 per month for 10.

## When you hit a limit

The Open Source plan has no usage cap on the pricing page, only the public-repository rule. The pricing page does not say what happens if you install Cirun on a private repository without a paid plan.

The real cost sits in your cloud account. Cirun says you pay only your cloud provider for what you use, and that runners are shut down right after a job ends. A large GPU machine or a long job still costs money at your provider's rates. The pricing page describes no cap on that spending, so set budgets and alerts in your cloud console.

## Gotchas

- Cirun supports GitHub Actions. The docs describe no GitLab or other CI setup.
- You need cloud credentials, or your own machines registered as a pool, before the first job can run.
- The quickstart says the file name is `.cirun.yml`, not `cirun.yml`.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cirun free?

Yes, for public repositories. The Open Source plan costs $0 forever and includes unlimited runners, including ARM and GPU runners. Private repositories need a paid plan.

### Does Cirun pay for the GPU?

No. Cirun starts runners in your own cloud account, and you pay that provider for the machine. Cirun's own fee is $0 on the Open Source plan.

### How much is Cirun for private repositories?

The Startup plan costs $29 per month and covers 3 private repositories. The Business plan costs $79 per month and covers 10.
