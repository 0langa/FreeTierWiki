Lucidchart is a browser-based diagramming app from Lucid. You drag shapes onto a canvas to build flowcharts, architecture and network diagrams, org charts, and UML, and teammates can comment on or present them. Developers use it for design docs and system diagrams.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and the pricing page says no credit card is required. It includes 3 editable Lucidchart documents, 75 shapes per Lucidchart document, 100 templates, basic visual activities, basic data linking, presentation mode, and commenting, plus the learning center and Lucid AI.

The pricing FAQ confirms you do not have to pay to use Lucidchart: the free version gives access to basic features, including three editable documents, up to 75 shapes per document, and 100 professional templates.

## When you hit a limit

The limits are counts of documents and shapes. The pricing page does not describe what happens when you reach 75 shapes or try to edit a fourth document; it points to paid plans, which unlock unlimited shapes, unlimited documents, premium shape libraries, and over 1,000 premium templates. The Individual plan is $9 per month billed annually.

A free trial of the Individual or Team license lasts seven days. The FAQ says you must add payment information at sign-up, you are not charged until the trial ends, and you are billed automatically after the trial unless you cancel.

## Gotchas

- 75 shapes per document is small for a detailed architecture diagram, so plan to split diagrams.
- Only 3 documents are editable on Free, so old diagrams compete with new ones.
- The paid trial asks for payment details and renews into a paid plan unless you cancel within the seven days.

## Questions people ask

### Is Lucidchart free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3 editable documents with up to 75 shapes each and 100 templates, with no credit card required.

### Does the Lucidchart free trial need a credit card?

Yes, for the paid trial. The FAQ says you add payment information at sign-up for the seven-day trial and are billed automatically when it ends unless you cancel.

### How many shapes can I use on the free plan?

Up to 75 shapes per document.
