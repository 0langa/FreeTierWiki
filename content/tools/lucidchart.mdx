Lucidchart is a browser-based diagramming app from Lucid. You drag shapes onto a canvas to build flowcharts, architecture and network diagrams, org charts, and UML, and teammates can comment on or present them. Developers use it for design docs and system diagrams.

## What the free plan gives you

Lucid's pricing page confirms the Free plan costs nothing and requires no credit card. It includes 3 editable Lucidchart documents, 75 shapes per Lucidchart document, 100 templates, basic visual activities, basic data linking, presentation mode, and commenting, plus the learning center and Lucid AI.

The pricing FAQ confirms you do not have to pay to use Lucidchart: the free version gives access to basic features, including three editable documents, up to 75 shapes per document, and 100 professional templates to start a diagram.

## When you hit a limit

The limits are counts of documents and shapes. The pricing page does not explain which document becomes uneditable when you reach the allowance. Lucid's sharing help also says Free users cannot edit shared documents with more than 60 objects. Paid plans unlock unlimited shapes and editable documents. The Individual card shows an annual billing option and excludes VAT. Check the amount displayed for your region.

A free trial of the Individual or Team license lasts seven days. The FAQ says you must add payment information at sign-up, you are not charged until the trial ends, and you are billed automatically after the trial unless you cancel.

## Gotchas

- 75 shapes per document is small for a detailed architecture diagram, so plan to split diagrams.
- You can import and view diagrams on Free. Editing an imported diagram requires a paid plan.
- The paid trial asks for payment details and renews into a paid plan unless you cancel within the seven days.

## Questions people ask

### Is Lucidchart free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3 editable documents with up to 75 shapes each and 100 templates, with no credit card required.

### Does the Lucidchart paid trial need payment details?

Yes, for the paid trial. The FAQ says you add payment information at sign-up for the seven-day trial and are billed automatically when it ends unless you cancel.

### How many shapes can I use on the free plan?

Up to 75 shapes per document.
