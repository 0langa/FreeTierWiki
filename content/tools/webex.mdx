Webex is Cisco's app for video meetings, messaging, and whiteboards. A developer team would pick Webex Free for short stand-ups, pairing sessions, and demos with screen sharing, especially if the company or its clients already use Webex.

## What the free plan gives you

Webex Free costs $0 per user per year. It includes unlimited meetings of up to 40 minutes each, with up to 100 attendees per meeting. Screen sharing with annotation, unlimited whiteboards, local recording, and calendar service integration are included.

The free plan also lists advanced noise cancellation, unlimited messaging, video creation and sharing with Vidcast, advanced security, integration of leading apps, and basic support. Webex Meet, at $144 per user per year, adds the AI Assistant, meetings of up to 24 hours, up to 200 attendees, 10 GB of cloud recording, Slido polling and Q&A, and closed captions.

## When you hit a limit

Each meeting on Webex Free can run up to 40 minutes, and the pricing page frames this as unlimited meetings, up to 40 min per meeting. The page does not say exactly what happens at the 40-minute mark, such as a warning before the meeting ends.

Attendance is capped at up to 100 per meeting on Free. Recording is local only; cloud recording starts with Webex Meet. The pricing FAQ asks whether you need a credit card for a free account, but the answer is not shown on the page.

## Gotchas

- Forty minutes is short for a planning session or retro; split long meetings or upgrade.
- Local recordings stay on your machine, so share them yourself.
- Add-ons such as Call Me and real-time translation are only for Webex Meet or Meet plus Call plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Webex free?

Yes. Webex Free costs $0 and includes unlimited meetings of up to 40 minutes each with up to 100 attendees.

### How long can a free Webex meeting last?

Up to 40 minutes per meeting. Webex Meet allows meetings of up to 24 hours.

### Can I record meetings on Webex Free?

Yes, locally. Cloud recording starts with Webex Meet, which includes 10 GB.
