Plane is a project management tool for software teams, with work items, cycles for timeboxed sprints, modules for breaking work into blocks, and pages for documentation. A developer team would pick it as an open-source alternative to heavier trackers: the pricing page offers both a Cloud version and a self-hosted one.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan on Plane Cloud costs $0 per seat per month and is pitched as no-frills project management for startups and passion projects. The seat limit is 12 users in your workspace.

Free includes projects and work items, comments with mentions, cycles and modules, and project pages for documenting work. Roles are basic, and guest access is limited. The Pro plan, at $6 per seat per month when billed annually, adds work item types and properties, a workspace wiki, integrations, and 500 AI credits per seat each month. New Cloud workspaces automatically start a 14-day Business trial with no payment method required. You can switch once to a Pro trial with the same end date, or cancel early to use Free immediately.

## When you hit a limit

Free permits 12 workspace users, including guests/pending invites. Trial/subscription downgrades above that retain people but stop invitations and restrict everyone to Billing and plans/Members until reduction or payment. Some pre-December 17, 2024 Free workspaces retain larger allowances.

Trials without payment details end on Free without charges, retaining projects, work items, pages and members. Adding payment details enables automatic paid Business conversion. AI-agent usage warnings appear at 80% of monthly allowance, with pools sized by plan/paid seats. Check trial billing settings and membership together before relying on a downgrade to preserve ordinary workspace access.

## Gotchas

- The Free cap includes guests and pending invitations; the general quickstart's guest-seat exemption describes paid seats, not this Free allowance.
- Integrations and the marketplace are Pro features, so Git host links may need a paid plan.
- The self-hosted edition has its own plans; this entry covers the hosted Cloud Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Plane free?

Yes. The Free Cloud plan costs $0 per seat per month for up to 12 users.

### Is Plane open source?

Yes. Plane says it is open-source and can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure, in addition to the hosted Cloud plans.

### Does Plane have a free trial of paid features?

New Cloud workspaces automatically get a 14-day Business trial without a card. You can switch to Pro for the remainder, or cancel early to use Free. Leave payment details unset to avoid automatic paid conversion at the end.
