Plane is a project management tool for software teams, with work items, cycles for timeboxed sprints, modules for breaking work into blocks, and pages for documentation. A developer team would pick it as an open-source alternative to heavier trackers: the pricing page offers both a Cloud version and a self-hosted one.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan on Plane Cloud costs $0 per seat per month and is pitched as no-frills project management for startups and passion projects. The seat limit is 12 users in your workspace.

Free includes projects and work items, comments with mentions, cycles and modules, and project pages for documenting work. Roles are basic, and guest access is limited. The Pro plan, at $6 per seat per month when billed annually, adds work item types and properties, a workspace wiki, integrations, and 500 AI credits per seat each month. Pro and Business each offer a two-week free trial.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan's seat limit is 12 users per workspace. The pricing page describes it as the maximum number of users allowed, but does not say what happens when you try to invite one more person. Growing past it means a paid plan.

The pricing page lists no overage charges for the Free plan. A usage dashboard that warns at 80% of a monthly allowance is listed in the feature table, tied to AI agent runs, which draw from a pool sized by plan and paid seats.

## Gotchas

- The 12-user cap counts everyone in the workspace, so contractors use seats too.
- Integrations and the marketplace are Pro features, so Git host links may need a paid plan.
- The self-hosted edition has its own plans; this entry covers the hosted Cloud Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Plane free?

Yes. The Free Cloud plan costs $0 per seat per month for up to 12 users.

### Is Plane open source?

Yes. Plane says it is open-source and can be self-hosted on your own infrastructure, in addition to the hosted Cloud plans.

### Does Plane have a free trial of paid features?

Yes. Both Pro and Business offer a two-week free trial.
