You work in the browser. You design in Figma Design, hand off to developers through inspect and Dev Mode, and brainstorm in FigJam. The Starter plan suits solo designers and people who mainly review other people's work.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists Starter as free with limited access to Figma products. You get unlimited drafts, UI kits and templates, and 150 AI credits a day, up to 500 credits a month. Auto layout, components, prototypes, and inspect for design properties are included. Community plugins, widgets, and files are available too.

The downgrade guide specifies one team folder, 3 total Figma Design/Sites files, and 3 files each for FigJam and Figma Slides. Starter has 30 days of version history, with unlimited drafts listed separately. Anyone can view, comment, inspect, and export from your files for free, and free viewers can copy CSS, iOS, and Android code.

## When you hit a limit

After downgrading above Starter's shared-file or folder allowance, Figma blocks file editing until you reorganize files, move them to drafts or another team, or upgrade. This is separate from the unlimited draft allowance. Paid seat prices depend on type and billing schedule; Starter's signup card requirement is not explicitly published in the reviewed billing guides.

AI actions stop at exhausted credits. Starter's 150-credit daily cap resets the next day; the 500-credit allowance resets monthly, without rollover. Pricing promotes additional AI credits broadly, while the linked purchase guide is scoped to paid-plan admins. Confirm Starter eligibility before planning to buy extra credits.

## Gotchas

- Team libraries for components, styles, and variables are not part of Starter. Paid plans add them, along with Dev Mode.
- Figma says included AI credits are subject to change, and the pricing page does not list every Starter limit.
- Free viewers can inspect files, but they have view-only access.

## Questions people ask

### Is Figma free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free, with unlimited drafts and 500 AI credits a month. Anyone can view, comment on, and inspect files for free.

### How many files can I have on the free Figma plan?

The downgrade guide lists one team folder with 3 total Figma Design/Sites files and 3 files each for FigJam and Slides. Unlimited drafts are separate.

### Does Figma have a free Dev Mode?

No. Dev Mode needs a paid seat. Free viewers can still inspect designs, copy CSS and platform code, and export assets.
