You work in the browser. You design in Figma Design, hand off to developers through inspect and Dev Mode, and brainstorm in FigJam. The Starter plan suits solo designers and people who mainly review other people's work.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists Starter as free with limited access to Figma products. You get unlimited drafts, UI kits and templates, and 150 AI credits a day, up to 500 credits a month. Auto layout, components, prototypes, and inspect for design properties are included. Community plugins, widgets, and files are available too.

The Figma help center describes the Starter plan as a single team with one folder and 3 files, with 30 days of version history. The plan also lists unlimited drafts. Anyone can view, comment, inspect, and export from your files for free, and free viewers can copy CSS, iOS, and Android code.

## When you hit a limit

The team file allowance is separate from unlimited drafts. Professional adds unlimited files and projects for a single team; paid seat prices depend on the seat type and billing schedule. Keep shared team work within the Starter allowance or review an upgrade before adding more files.

AI actions stop when you run out of credits, with an in-product message. On Starter, reaching the 150-credit daily limit means waiting until the following day; reaching 500 for the month means waiting until the monthly reset. Credits do not roll over. The AI credit management guide describes buying additional credits for paid plans.

## Gotchas

- Team libraries for components, styles, and variables are not part of Starter. Paid plans add them, along with Dev Mode.
- Figma says included AI credits are subject to change, and the pricing page does not list every Starter limit.
- Free viewers can inspect files, but they have view-only access.

## Questions people ask

### Is Figma free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free, with unlimited drafts and 500 AI credits a month. Anyone can view, comment on, and inspect files for free.

### How many files can I have on the free Figma plan?

The Figma help center lists one team with one folder and 3 files on Starter. The plan lists unlimited drafts as a separate item.

### Does Figma have a free Dev Mode?

No. Dev Mode needs a paid seat. Free viewers can still inspect designs, copy CSS and platform code, and export assets.
