Budibase is an open source platform for building internal apps, automations, and AI agents on top of your data. You can self-host it for free or use Budibase Cloud. This entry covers Budibase Cloud, which offers a trial rather than a free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

Cloud has no ongoing Free plan. Sales offers trials without published duration/entitlements. Paid Cloud starts at $299/month: unlimited apps, agents, automations and requests, 2 creators, 20 end users, 250K monthly actions and 50K AI credits. Actions include agent tool calls, automation nodes and row saves/deletes, excluding custom SQL/API requests.

Self-managed Community costs $0 indefinitely with open-source code, unlimited users/apps/agents/automations/requests, one workspace and one-day automation logs. It supports your AI keys but excludes hosted Budibase AI, public API access, workspace backups and audit logs. Infrastructure and external AI costs remain yours, separate from the software license.

## When you hit a limit

Budibase says actions are hard monthly limits based on your selected Cloud allowance. When you reach the limit, you need to increase that allowance, through sales, to continue running agents and automations. Budibase chose hard limits over overage billing so costs stay predictable.

The pricing page does not say the trial duration, what happens when it ends, or whether it asks for a card. Paid Cloud customers can buy extra AI credits as one-off purchases, from 10,000 credits for $20.

## Gotchas

- There is no free hosted plan; after the trial, staying free means self-hosting.
- Cloud starts at $299 per month, so the step up from self-hosting is large.
- Extra end users cost $5 each per month on yearly billing, and extra creators cost $50 each.

## Questions people ask

### Is Budibase free?

The self-hosted open source edition is free to use indefinitely. Budibase Cloud is paid, from $299 per month, with a free trial arranged through sales whose duration is not published.

### Are Budibase actions a hard limit?

Yes. Actions are hard monthly limits, and when you reach yours you need a higher allowance to keep running agents and automations.

### Do API requests count as Budibase actions?

No. Custom SQL queries and API requests are not counted as actions.
