You write a normal Playwright or Puppeteer script and change one connection line to point at BrowserCat. It suits developers who test or scrape from code and do not want to host browsers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan costs $0 per month and includes 1,000 credits each month. Credits count work, not wall-clock hours. A successful call to the utility API costs 1 credit. A websocket session costs 1 credit for every 30 seconds of activity, so long sessions use credits fast.

The plan allows 1,000 concurrent requests and works with Playwright, Puppeteer, and any CDP client. The home page says no credit card is needed and the free tier is free forever. Today your sessions run on Cloudflare browsers, and BrowserCat says it will add more backends behind the same endpoint.

## When you hit a limit

If you pass 1,000 credits, BrowserCat charges $0.0025 for each extra credit. It also keeps a soft limit and a hard limit on your account. On free plans these are $10 and $20.

When you cross the soft limit, BrowserCat emails you and asks you to approve a higher limit. If you do not approve, it rejects all requests once you reach the hard limit. The block lasts until the billing period ends or you approve an increase. The pricing page does not say how a free account without a card pays for extra credits.

## Gotchas

- One credit equals 30 seconds of websocket activity, so long sessions use credits fast.
- BrowserCat says it has no plans to support Selenium or Cypress. It supports Playwright, Puppeteer, and CDP libraries.
- Extra credits are billed at $0.0025 each, and the hard limit of $20 on free plans is the real cap.

## Questions people ask

### Is BrowserCat free?

Yes. The Hobby plan costs $0 per month and includes 1,000 credits. You can pay for extra credits if you go past that.

### Does BrowserCat need a credit card?

The home page says no credit card is needed for the free tier. The pricing page does not describe how extra credits are paid on a free account.

### What happens when I go over the BrowserCat free limit?

Extra credits cost $0.0025 each. BrowserCat emails you at the soft limit and rejects requests at the hard limit until you approve more or the period ends.
