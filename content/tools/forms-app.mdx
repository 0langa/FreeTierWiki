You build forms in a web editor, and you can create one before you sign up. After sign-up, the dashboard keeps your forms and results.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs 0 USD and the pricing page says no credit card is required, ever. You can have up to 5 forms. Responses, views, questions, notifications, and team members are all listed as unlimited.

The free plan includes all 30+ field types, conditional logic, e-signatures, pre-filled forms, auto-scoring, unlimited payments, live support, and AI-powered features. File storage is 10 MB.

Forms.app shows its branding on free forms. The pricing page lists branding removal from the Basic plan up. The help center also says you can set a quota in a form to accept only a set number of responses a month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to make a sixth form or fill the 10 MB of storage. It shows no overage fee, and the free plan has no card on file.

To go past the limits, you move to a paid plan. Basic costs 29 USD a month, or 19 USD with annual billing, and allows 25 forms and 10 GB of file storage. Pro offers unlimited forms and 100 GB of storage. The site also lists a 90-day refund guarantee.

## Gotchas

- Free accounts can hold only 5 forms, even though responses are unlimited.
- File storage on the free plan is 10 MB across your account.
- Your username shows in form URLs on the standard subdomain, so check it at sign-up.

## Questions people ask

### Is Forms.app free?

Yes. The free plan costs 0 USD and allows up to 5 forms with unlimited responses. Paid plans start at 29 USD a month, or 19 USD with annual billing.

### Does Forms.app need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required ever on the free plan.

### Can I use Forms.app without signing up?

You can create your first form without signing up, but you may need an account to save and share it. The help center says forms made before sign-up appear on your dashboard afterward.
