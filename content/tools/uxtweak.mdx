UXtweak is a user research platform. It covers card sorting, tree testing, surveys, preference and five-second tests, first-click tests, prototype and website testing, mobile app testing, and live interviews. It suits developers and designers who want evidence about navigation or a design before they build it.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 a month and is meant for experimenting with UX research tools at no cost. It includes 1 user license, an unlimited number of studies, and 15 responses per month, with limits per tool. The current comparison gives Free access to unmoderated research tools and allows only 1 concurrent active study.

Each tool has its own response limit per study on the Free plan: 10 for card sorting, tree testing, and surveys, and 3 for preference, five-second, first-click, prototype, website, and mobile app tests. Card sorting allows 20 cards, and tree testing allows 2 tasks. After a study is finished, its results can be viewed for 14 days, and the data is stored for 12 months, so you can see it again if you upgrade.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan limits the maximum number of respondents per study and the number of recordable sessions. UXtweak gives an example of what happens at the respondent limit: a card sorting study with 50 respondents closes automatically once the account is on the Free plan, where the limit is 10.

The reset FAQ ties paid-plan periods to the activation date rather than the calendar month; it does not separately establish Free's reset day. The current comparison excludes moderated Live Interviews from Free.

## Gotchas

- Results are visible for only 14 days after a study ends on Free, so review them promptly. The current table does not include video, PDF, or CSV exports on Free.
- The 15 monthly responses are shared across all your studies.
- If a paid subscription is canceled, the Free plan limits apply and larger studies close.

## Questions people ask

### Is UXtweak free?

Yes. The Free plan costs 0 a month and gives 1 user 15 responses per month across unlimited studies.

### How long can I see study results on the Free plan?

For 14 days after a study is finished. The data is stored for 12 months, so it comes back if you upgrade.

### Does UXtweak Free include moderated interviews?

No. The current comparison limits Free to unmoderated research; moderated Live Interviews need a paid plan.
