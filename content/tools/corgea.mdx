Corgea is an application security scanner. It runs static analysis with AI to find vulnerabilities, including logic and authentication flaws, and also checks dependencies, secrets, containers, and infrastructure as code. For findings it can, it suggests a code fix. It connects to your source control and IDE.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. The pricing FAQ says it includes AI SAST, logic and auth scanning, dependency scanning, secrets detection, container scanning, and IaC scanning for up to 2 team members and 10 repositories. The plan comparison table lists 10 PR scans per month and 10 SAST auto fixes on Free.

Developer integrations in the table include the GitHub App, GitLab, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket, IDE extensions, and an MCP server. New accounts also get a 2-week trial of the Growth plan with full access and no credit card required.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists the Free plan's limits but does not say what happens when you use the 10 PR scans in a month or need more repositories; it points to upgrading. Growth is $39 per developer per month, with a minimum of 5 developers, 100 repositories, unlimited PR scans, and 50 auto fixes.

Scale is $49 per developer per month with a minimum of 20 and adds custom and blocking rules, reporting, and APIs and webhooks. Developer seats count contributing developers in the last 90 days, so a paid bill follows who committed recently.

## Gotchas

- Paid plans have minimum team sizes, so the step up from Free is at least 5 seats.
- The Free plan's 10 auto fixes are a small allowance if you have many findings to fix.
- Blocking rules that stop a pull request on a finding are on Scale, not Free or Growth.

## Questions people ask

### Is Corgea free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and covers 2 team members and 10 repositories with SAST, dependency, secrets, container, and IaC scanning.

### Does the Corgea trial need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the 2-week Growth trial gives full access with no credit card required.

### How are paid Corgea seats counted?

Developer seats are contributing developers in the last 90 days, and Growth and Scale have minimum team sizes.
