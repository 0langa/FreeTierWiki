UI Bakery is a platform for building internal tools and apps, either by describing them to its AI App Agent or by dragging components onto pages. Apps connect to SQL databases, HTTP APIs, and other services, and UI Bakery can host a PostgreSQL database for you. This entry covers the cloud Free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per developer. It includes unlimited apps, pages, and data source connections, public and private apps, unlimited public app users, release history, and community support. Monthly AI trial credits come with it for building with the AI App Agent.

Scheduled jobs and webhooks get 1000 executions per month. The UI Bakery Database, a hosted PostgreSQL database with a visual structure and data editor, has 5 GB on Free. Custom JavaScript and Python code, custom components, and modules are included.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say whether scheduled jobs and webhooks stop at 1000 executions on the Free plan. The table lists extra executions at $50 per 5000 next to the Free amount, so check your billing settings before you rely on a hard stop. It also does not say what happens when the database reaches 5 GB.

The Builder plan costs $20 per developer per month billed annually, or $25 monthly.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has no workspace viewer seats, so colleagues who only use apps need a paid plan or a public app.
- Public apps on Free show a Built with UI Bakery badge.
- App and data source environments, app export, and built-in roles start on the Builder plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is UI Bakery free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per developer and includes unlimited apps, data sources, and public app users.

### Does UI Bakery include a database?

Yes. Every plan includes the UI Bakery Database, a hosted PostgreSQL database, with 5 GB on Free.

### Can colleagues sign in to my UI Bakery apps on the Free plan?

The Free plan has no workspace viewer seats; paid plans include up to 50.
