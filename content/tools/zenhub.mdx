Zenhub adds agile project management on top of GitHub. You link a Zenhub org to your GitHub organization, and Zenhub syncs issues, pull requests, repositories, and permissions, then adds boards, sprints, estimates, epics, and roadmaps. A developer team would pick it when it wants planning without moving its issues out of GitHub.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is $0 per user per month and is built for small teams, with essential features and a single workspace. The comparison table sets its limits: 1 team workspace, up to 50 users, 1 connected GitHub organization, up to 2 connected repositories, and up to 250 issues.

Free includes the GitHub integration and the Zenhub extension for GitHub, automated sprint planning, planning poker, estimates, Zenhub projects and epics, real-time roadmaps, basic sprint reports, velocity reports, sprint insights, and the GitHub and Slack integrations. It is cloud hosted, with Google SSO and email and community support. Teams costs $4.99 per user per month billed yearly.

## When you hit a limit

The limits that bite are repositories and issues. Once a workspace has 2 connected repositories or 250 issues, the next step is the Teams plan, which allows up to 50 repositories and up to 5000 issues. Billing docs say an organization exceeding any Free limits cannot revert to Free without creating a new organization within them. They do not specify ongoing Free issue or sync enforcement.

When you create a free account you automatically receive a 14-day trial of the Teams plan; at the end you choose to upgrade or continue on Free if the organization fits its limits. An Enterprise trial of 60 days is available through sales.

## Gotchas

- The 250-issue cap counts issues across the workspace, which an active repository can pass quickly.
- Features you use during the 14-day Teams trial, such as AI labels and sprint review, go away when you drop to Free.
- Non-GitHub users viewing GitHub issues is not included in Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zenhub free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 per user per month for 1 workspace, up to 2 connected repositories, and up to 250 issues.

### How many repositories can I connect for free?

Up to 2, from 1 connected GitHub organization. Teams raises this to up to 50.

### Is there a free trial of Zenhub Teams?

Yes. A new free account automatically gets a 14-day trial of the Teams plan, then can continue on Free within its limits.
