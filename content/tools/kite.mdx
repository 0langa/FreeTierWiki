You design in the browser or the Mac and Windows apps, and AI agents work in the same file through Kite's hosted MCP server. Agents draw real layers such as frames, text, components and auto layout, so teammates can edit what an agent made instead of starting over.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Free plan at $0 with unlimited editors and viewers, one workspace with 3 files and 3 design systems, 100 agent actions a day, and everything in the editor. You can connect any agent, including Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex.

Kite does not sell AI credits. The pricing FAQ says Kite runs no model to design, so your agent runs on the plan you already pay for, and Kite charges only for the people who edit your files.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan allows 100 agent actions a day. The pricing page does not say what happens when an agent reaches that limit, so plan on the agent being unable to make more changes until the next day. You can keep 3 files in your free workspace.

Pro costs $20 per editor per month, or $16 per editor per month billed yearly. It adds unlimited files and workspaces, unlimited agent actions and version history. Team costs $35 per editor per month, or $28 billed yearly, and adds SSO and SAML, admin controls and dedicated support.

## Gotchas

- Free has no version history; it is listed as a Pro feature.
- On Pro each editor is a paid seat, while viewers stay free. On Free you can invite as many editors as you like.
- The 3 design systems limit on Free applies alongside the 3 file limit.

## Questions people ask

### Is Kite free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes unlimited editors and viewers, one workspace with 3 files and 3 design systems, and 100 agent actions a day.

### Do I pay for AI credits in Kite?

No. Kite runs no model itself. Your agent, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, runs on the plan you already pay for.

### What does Kite Pro add?

Pro costs $20 per editor per month, or $16 billed yearly. It adds unlimited files and workspaces, unlimited agent actions and version history.
