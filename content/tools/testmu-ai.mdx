TestMu AI is the new name of LambdaTest, a cloud for running browser and mobile tests. You run Selenium, Playwright, Puppeteer, or Cypress suites on its grid, test pages by hand in remote browsers, and compare screenshots between builds. The old lambdatest.com pricing page now redirects to TestMu AI.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free plan for each product, marked Free Forever, and its FAQ says the plan has no trial time limit. Automation gets 100 minutes with 2 parallel sessions, valid for lifetime. Live testing gets 5 sessions a month of up to 2 minutes each on 200+ desktop browsers and real devices.

HyperExecute, the test orchestration product, gets 300 minutes with up to 2 concurrent sessions on Windows or Linux VMs. Visual regression testing gets 2000 screenshots valid for lifetime, with 1 month of build history. Each organisation also gets 100 accessibility tests, and the FAQ says signing up needs no credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The automation allowance is lifetime, not monthly. The FAQ says that once those minutes run out, continued execution requires a paid plan, so tests stop rather than being billed. Live sessions renew each month.

Paid automation is priced by parallel session: desktop web automation starts at $79 a month billed annually, or $99 billed monthly. The FAQ describes the free plan as suited to evaluation and a first suite, not continuous integration. The free JMeter load testing tier runs 100 concurrent users, 100 VUH per month, and a 40-minute test cap.

## Gotchas

- The 100 automation minutes never reset, so one long CI suite can use them up in a few runs.
- Live sessions end after 2 minutes, which is enough for a quick look but not for a debugging session.
- Docs, blog posts, and SDKs may still say LambdaTest, while the site and dashboard now say TestMu AI.

## Questions people ask

### Is LambdaTest still free?

Yes, under the TestMu AI name. The free plan has no time limit and includes 100 lifetime automation minutes and 5 short live sessions a month.

### Do the free automation minutes reset each month?

No. The FAQ says the free plan gives 100 lifetime minutes, not monthly, with 2 parallel sessions.

### Do I need a credit card?

No. The FAQ says signing up for TestMu AI needs no credit card.
