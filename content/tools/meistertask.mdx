MeisterTask is a task manager built around Kanban project boards, with notes, templates, and native mobile and desktop apps. A developer team would pick it for a small set of shared boards that many people can use; its Free plan also lists a beta MCP connector that lets Claude create and update tasks.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is €0 forever and is aimed at individuals starting to manage daily work. It includes unlimited tasks with checklists, comments, and time tracking; up to 3 projects per team; up to 5 notes per team; 5 Content Manager seats; and unlimited team members.

Content Managers are the people who can create, edit, share, and manage the settings of projects and notes. You also get import from Asana and Trello, templates, the mobile and desktop apps, and 20 Meister AI credits, with 1 prompt per credit. AI project statuses and the MeisterTask MCP connector are both listed as Beta.

## When you hit a limit

Free lists 3 projects and 5 notes per team. The pricing page does not explain whether archived projects count or exactly how extra creation is blocked. The AI help article calls the free tier Basic: its 20 prompts are shared by the team, refill at the start of each month, and stop until reset or upgrade when exhausted.

Free signup card requirements are not stated explicitly. The separate 14-day Business trial needs no payment information and returns to the previous plan without an upgrade. No Free overage price is published; paid prices exclude VAT and vary by region.

## Gotchas

- Unlimited team members does not mean unlimited editors: only 5 Content Manager seats can create and manage projects and notes.
- The 20 monthly AI prompts are shared across the team, including Ask Meister AI and the Writing Assistant.
- Beta features such as the MCP connector may change before they are final.

## Questions people ask

### Is MeisterTask free?

Yes. The Free plan is €0 forever, with unlimited tasks and team members and up to 3 projects per team.

### How many projects can I create on MeisterTask Free?

Up to 3 projects per team, and up to 5 notes per team.

### Can I import from Trello or Asana?

Yes. Import from Asana and Trello is included in the Free plan.
