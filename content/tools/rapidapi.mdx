You use RapidAPI in the browser: find an API in the Hub, subscribe to a plan, and test its endpoints on the API page. Then you call the API from your own code with your key, and track plans and usage in the Developer Dashboard. It suits developers who want to try many APIs with one account.

## What the free plan gives you

RapidAPI sorts APIs into four types: Free, Pay Per Use, Freemium, and Paid. Only the Free type works without a credit card. RapidAPI says it hosts thousands of Free APIs, and they are clearly marked.

Free API plans are limited to 1000 requests per hour and 500K requests per month. Some Free APIs also ask you to get a key or token from the provider's own website.

A Freemium API has a Basic plan with a free allowance. The provider sets that allowance. The docs show an example with a monthly quota, and each plan lists its own limits. Quotas can be daily, monthly, or both, and they can count queries or other items, such as emails. Read the plan before you subscribe.

## When you hit a limit

On a Free API, going over the 1000 per hour or 500K per month limit gives you a 429 Too Many Requests error. Nothing is billed.

On a Freemium API, you keep working past the quota and pay an overage fee for each extra request. RapidAPI sends email alerts at 85 percent and at 100 percent of the quota. It says an email is no guarantee, because a fast burst can pass the limit before the alert fires. The docs say you must watch your own usage. RapidAPI cannot give refunds without the provider's permission.

A daily quota resets 24 hours after you subscribed, not at midnight.

## Gotchas

- Freemium APIs need a credit card even on the free Basic plan. The card exists so RapidAPI can charge overages.
- Quotas and overage prices differ by API. Check the pricing tab of each API before you subscribe.
- RapidAPI or the API provider may block your access for abuse or suspicious activity.

## Questions people ask

### Is RapidAPI free?

Signing up and calling Free APIs costs nothing. Free APIs need no card. Freemium APIs have a free Basic plan, but they need a card on file.

### What happens when I go over the RapidAPI limit?

On a Free API you get a 429 error. On a Freemium API, you pay an overage fee for each extra request, and the fee is set by the API provider.

### Does RapidAPI need a credit card?

Only for Pay Per Use, Freemium, and Paid APIs. Free APIs say they do not need a card or subscription.
