TeleportHQ is a visual builder for websites, web apps, and online stores. You design pages in its editor or import from Figma, add logic, data sources, and a CMS, and either host on TeleportHQ or export code for frameworks such as React, Next.js, Vue, and Angular. This entry covers its free trial.

## What the free plan gives you

TeleportHQ starts with a 7-day free trial that needs no credit card. The trial covers 1 website and 1 web app project, up to 200 pages per project, automatic backups, and 200 MB of runtime storage per interactive project. It includes 30,000 AI tokens once for the AI website and web app builders.

For hosting, the trial includes TeleportHQ hosting with 1 custom domain, 1 GB of asset storage per project, and 5 GB of monthly bandwidth. You get 10 forms and 2,000 form submissions per month. Every plan includes free subdomain hosting, responsive templates, Google Fonts, and Figma import. Pro costs $14 per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 7-day trial ends, or when a trial project uses its bandwidth or form submissions. No credit card is required to start, so there is no card on file to bill. The paid plans are Pro at $14 per month for presentational websites and Premium at $28 per month for web apps. Add-ons such as extra seats or AI token packs are only available after upgrading.

## Gotchas

- There is no always-free plan on the current pricing page, only the 7-day trial.
- The 30,000 AI tokens are a one-time amount for the trial, not a monthly allowance.
- The trial allows just 1 website and 1 web app project.
