TeleportHQ is a visual builder for websites, web apps, and online stores. You design pages in its editor or import from Figma, add logic, data sources, and a CMS, and host on TeleportHQ. Paid plans export static projects for frameworks such as React, Next.js, Vue, and Angular; the trial has code preview only. This entry covers that trial.

## What the free plan gives you

TeleportHQ starts with a 7-day free trial that needs no credit card. The trial covers 1 website and 1 web app project, up to 200 pages per project, automatic backups, and 200 MB of runtime storage per interactive project. It includes 30,000 AI tokens once for the AI website and web app builders.

For hosting, the trial includes TeleportHQ hosting with 1 custom domain, 1 GB of asset storage per project, and 5 GB of monthly bandwidth. You get 10 forms and 2,000 form submissions per month. Every plan includes free subdomain hosting, responsive templates, Google Fonts, and Figma import. Code export is excluded from the trial despite the generic All plans include description.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 7-day trial ends, or when a trial project uses its bandwidth or form submissions. No-card signup does not establish trial-expiry billing policy. Pro and Premium are $14 and $28 per month billed yearly, or $20.99 and $39.99 billed monthly. Add-ons such as extra seats or AI token packs are only available after upgrading.

## Gotchas

- There is no always-free plan on the current pricing page, only the 7-day trial.
- The 30,000 AI tokens are a one-time amount for the trial, not a monthly allowance.
- Code export is unavailable in the trial; paid export is limited to static projects. The generic All plans include copy conflicts with the comparison table.

## Questions people ask

### Is TeleportHQ free?

The current comparison offers a 7-day no-card trial and no always-free tier. Trial-expiry handling is unpublished, and older FAQ copy still mentions a Free Plan.

### Do I need a credit card for the TeleportHQ trial?

No. The pricing page says the 7-day free trial needs no credit card.

### What does the TeleportHQ trial include?

1 website and 1 web app project, 200 pages per project, 30,000 AI tokens once, and hosting with 5 GB of monthly bandwidth.
