Mendix is a low-code platform aimed at business and enterprise apps. You model data, pages, and logic in Studio Pro and deploy to Mendix Cloud or other targets. Its Free plan lets you build and deploy apps without paying, on a limited Free App environment.

## What the free plan gives you

Mendix says the Free plan is free, with no hidden fees, no commitment, and no credit card required. It includes what you need to design, build, and deploy demos, prototypes, or small applications, with a deployment environment for each app and unlimited users.

Free Apps run on Mendix Cloud, a public multi-tenant cloud. They get 0.5 GiB of database storage and 1 GiB of file storage. Agile project management, collaborative development, and the free online Academy are included. Support, the ops dashboard, scaling, and an uptime guarantee start on the Standard plan, from $1,090 per month.

## When you hit a limit

A Free App goes into sleep mode after about an hour of inactivity and resumes automatically when someone opens it; all data is kept while it sleeps. If a Free App stays in sleep mode for three months or longer, it may be archived, and the technical contact is told two weeks beforehand.

The pages do not say what happens when a Free App reaches its 0.5 GiB database or 1 GiB file storage. There are no usage charges on Free.

## Gotchas

- Mendix says Free Apps do not support complex or large applications and provide limited operational information.
- Free Apps cannot be scaled; avoiding sleep mode requires a licensed app on a Mendix Cloud node.
- Deleting a Free App removes the running app, its database, files, and backups, though the project and repository stay.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mendix free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with no credit card and lets you deploy small apps to Free App environments on Mendix Cloud.

### Why does my Mendix Free App go to sleep?

Free Apps enter sleep mode after about an hour of inactivity and wake when accessed. A licensed app on a Mendix Cloud node has no sleep mode.

### How much storage does a Mendix Free App get?

0.5 GiB of database storage and 1 GiB of file storage.
