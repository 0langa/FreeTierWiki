You write the form yourself in HTML and set its action to your Form.taxi endpoint. A management panel then shows settings and the submission archive.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs €0. It allows 3 forms and 40 submissions. The plans page presents this as a monthly quota.

You get email notifications to up to 5 recipient addresses, a Spam Shield filter, a custom success page, and a monthly submission archive. Webhooks and no-code automation tools are listed as free features too. The FAQ says you can use the free plan for commercial projects if you follow the terms.

The docs also set technical limits for every plan. One submission can carry at most 500 form fields, and one field can hold at most 10,000 characters. Email headers are not supported in submissions.

## When you hit a limit

When you reach the monthly quota, Form.taxi keeps accepting new submissions and stores them securely. You can read their contents only after you upgrade to a plan with a higher limit. So you do not lose entries, but you lose access to them until you pay.

Paid plans are billed through Paddle. Standard costs €15 a month, or €12 with annual billing. Premium costs €58 a month, or €47 with annual billing. You can cancel at any time in the online panel.

## Gotchas

- Entries past the free quota are stored but hidden until you upgrade, so a busy month can lock you out of your own data.
- File uploads, auto response mail, WhatsApp notifications, and API access are listed as paid features.
- You need a user account before the form works, and you must register each form there to get its endpoint.

## Questions people ask

### Is Form.taxi free?

Yes. The free plan costs €0 and includes 3 forms and 40 submissions. Paid plans add more forms, uploads, and a longer archive.

### What happens when I exceed the Form.taxi submission limit?

Form.taxi still accepts and stores new submissions. You can read them only after you upgrade to a plan with a higher limit.

### Does Form.taxi need a credit card?

The plans page asks you to register for the free plan and shows no card step. Only the paid plans go through Paddle for payment.
