Forminit handles the data after a visitor sends a form you designed. This is useful on a static site, or when you want to keep form processing independent of the rest of your app. You can use the endpoint with HTML or the provider’s JavaScript integration.

## What the free plan gives you

The no-card Free plan includes a single form and up to 100 submissions a month. It supports file uploads with 100 MB of file storage. In addition to the inbox, the provider lists email notifications, spam protection, validation and an automation workflow. Test the form with the fields a visitor will actually see before adding it to the live site. Check that invalid inputs behave as expected and that the right person gets notified. If you accept attachments, also test a realistic file to see how it uses the account’s storage allowance.

## When you hit a limit

At the monthly submission limit, forms pause and reject new entries until the allowance renews or you upgrade. Rejected entries are not stored or queued and cannot be recovered. When file storage is full, submissions without files still work, but submissions with attachments are rejected until you free space or upgrade. Keep another reliable contact method available during busy periods.

## Gotchas

- The provider used to be called Getform.io. Older instructions may use that name.
- Uploads draw on file storage. A handful of large files can be the first limit you reach. Normal submissions remain until you delete them; spam is deleted after 30 days.
- Free includes workflow automation, but webhooks, REST API access, CSV export and autoresponders start on paid plans.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [BootForm](/tools/bootform/) for a text-oriented form endpoint with a larger free submission quota.
- [Formspree.io](/tools/formspree-io/) for another hosted form inbox and static site integration.

## Questions people ask

### Is Forminit free?

Yes. One form and 100 submissions each month are included, and the provider says a card is unnecessary.

### Can people attach a file?

File upload is listed on Free. The account’s free file storage is limited to 100 MB.

### Can I use it from plain HTML?

Yes. Point the form at the endpoint Forminit issues and use the documented request method.
