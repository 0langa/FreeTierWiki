You sign in with GitHub and point your requests at CurlHub. CurlHub then lets you inspect the API traffic it receives. It suits one developer or a small team that debugs HTTP calls now and then. The FAQ recommends development and testing, and says its proxy servers are in the United States.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan includes 10,000 API requests per month. That is the only limit the pricing page shows for the free plan. The home page repeats the same number.

CurlHub lists the plans by monthly request volume. The next step up is 20,000 requests for $5 per month. After that come 100,000 requests for $10 per month and 1,000,000 requests for $20 per month. A yearly plan costs half as much per month on the smaller tiers. The pricing page also says the free tier will get more requests as more people sponsor the project on GitHub.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 10,000 requests. It does not mention a hard stop, a throttle, or a bill. The pages show no overage charge, and they also do not confirm a hard cap.

The public sign-up page says to sign up for a free account with GitHub. It does not establish whether a later step requests a card. If you need more room, pick a paid plan. If you only debug now and then, the free volume is probably enough.

## Gotchas

- The sign-up page says to sign up with GitHub. It names no other way in.
- The pages we read link to no documentation, so the exact steps to create an endpoint are not confirmed here.
- The FAQ says traffic passes through a US proxy and requests are saved in browser local storage. Secure your device or clear saved requests after handling sensitive data.

## Questions people ask

### Is CurlHub free?

Yes. The free plan includes 10,000 API requests per month. Paid plans start at $5 per month for 20,000 requests.

### Does CurlHub need a credit card?

The public sign-up page says to use GitHub for a free account. It does not publish a card policy for the complete signup process.

### What happens when I go over the CurlHub free tier?

The pricing page does not say. It lists the 10,000 request limit and the paid plans, and it describes no overage charge or hard stop.
