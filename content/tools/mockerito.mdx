You call the free sample APIs with plain HTTP requests from `fetch` or any client. For custom mocks you sign in, create endpoints in the dashboard, and call the API URL Mockerito gives you. It suits front-end developers and teachers.

## What the free plan gives you

There are two separate things. The first is a catalog of free sample APIs: 200+ endpoints across 9 domains, such as a fake store with products, carts, and users. They need no signup, no API key, and no credit card. They support create, read, update, and delete, with filtering, search, and pagination.

The second is the custom mock builder, which offers a 7-day free trial of its Developer plan. The plan includes 20 projects and 50,000 API calls per month. After the trial it costs $10 per month, and the page says you cancel any time. The page says you will not pay before the 7 days end.

## When you hit a limit

The public-API terms allow unlimited requests, including commercial use, while prohibiting overload and offering no uptime guarantee. Those terms still describe premium features as future offerings, so they do not establish the current custom builder’s billing policy.

For custom mocks, the pricing card lists 50,000 calls per month and $10 monthly after the trial. It does not explain quota enforcement, post-trial data access, or how payment becomes active. No card is required to start, but that does not establish every later billing condition.

## Gotchas

- Custom mock APIs are a paid product. Only the sample APIs stay free after 7 days.
- Sample endpoints have a predefined schema even though CRUD operations are supported; use the paid builder for your own response format.
- The pricing page shows one paid plan for custom mock APIs, and shows both $15 and $10; the trial line says $10/month after 7 days.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mockerito free?

Partly. The sample APIs are free with no signup. Custom mock APIs have a 7-day free trial, then cost $10 per month.

### Does Mockerito need a credit card?

No. The sample APIs need no card, and the page says the trial needs no card either.

### What happens after the Mockerito free trial?

The Developer plan costs $10 per month. The free sample APIs stay available without an account.
