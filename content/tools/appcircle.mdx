You work in the Appcircle web dashboard, which has separate modules for Build, Testing Distribution, Publish, and CodePush. There is also an API and a command line tool, and you can run builds on your own machines with runner scripts.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is listed as always free. It allows 1 concurrent build, 20 builds per month, and a maximum of 30 minutes per build. You can use an unlimited number of apps.

The other modules have their own monthly caps. CodePush allows 1000 updates per month. Testing Distribution allows 100 downloads per month, and so does the Enterprise App Store. You can publish to the stores 5 times per month. Reporting and activity logs are marked limited on this plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach a monthly cap or the 30 minute build limit. It says limits renew every month and that you can track what is left in the Billing section of the dashboard, which shows builds, publishes, and other module usage.

To raise a limit, you move to the Corporate plan. That plan is tailored, so you contact the Appcircle team for it. The page lists no price.

## Gotchas

- The 20 builds per month cap and the 30 minute build cap are the first limits most teams reach.
- Reporting and activity logs are limited on Starter, in every module.
- Corporate is the only upgrade path on the page, and it has no listed price.

## Questions people ask

### Is Appcircle free?

Yes. The Starter plan is always free and includes 20 builds per month with 1 concurrent build. Each build can run for up to 30 minutes.

### Does Appcircle build iOS apps on the free plan?

The docs describe building iOS and Android apps in the cloud with build profiles. The pricing page lists no platform restriction for the Starter plan.

### What happens when I go over the Appcircle free limits?

The pricing page does not say. Limits renew each month, and you can check your remaining usage in the Billing section of the dashboard.
