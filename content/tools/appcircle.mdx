You work in the Appcircle web dashboard, which has separate modules for Build, Testing Distribution, Publish, and CodePush. There is also an API and a command line tool, and you can run builds on your own machines with runner scripts.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is listed as always free. It allows 1 concurrent build, 20 builds per month, and a maximum of 30 minutes per build. You can use an unlimited number of apps.

The other modules have their own monthly caps. CodePush allows 1000 updates per month. Testing Distribution allows 100 downloads per month, and so does the Enterprise App Store. You get 5 publishes per month for TestFlight, Google Play, or Huawei AppGallery. Direct App Store and Microsoft Intune publishing are excluded from Starter. Reporting and activity logs are marked limited on this plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not explain the exact response when a monthly allowance or build-time limit is reached. Limits renew monthly, and the dashboard Billing section shows remaining usage. Starter's card requirement is also not explicitly stated in these public sources.

The terms reserve cancellation or requests for additional charges for excessive resource use beyond plan limits. That is not a published per-build overage tariff or proof of automatic billing, but it prevents treating Starter as guaranteed zero-overage. Higher allowances require discussing a tailored Corporate plan with Appcircle; pricing gives no standard Corporate fee.

## Gotchas

- The 20 builds per month cap and the 30 minute build cap are the first limits most teams reach.
- Reporting and activity logs are limited on Starter, in every module.
- Corporate is the only upgrade path on the page, and it has no listed price.

## Questions people ask

### Is Appcircle free?

Yes. The Starter plan is always free and includes 20 builds per month with 1 concurrent build. Each build can run for up to 30 minutes.

### Does Appcircle build iOS apps on the free plan?

The docs describe building iOS and Android apps in the cloud with build profiles. The pricing page lists no platform restriction for the Starter plan.

### What happens when I go over the Appcircle free limits?

The exact response is unpublished. Limits renew monthly; fair-use terms allow cancellation or requests for additional charges for excess resource use. Check your Billing dashboard and confirm any exceptional usage terms with Appcircle.
