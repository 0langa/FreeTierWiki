You design a template in the browser, then send a template ID and JSON data to a REST endpoint. A Postman collection and a Zapier connection are also available. It suits developers and no-code users.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan needs no credit card. It gives you 100 PDFs per month, 1 user, and 1 template, with API and Zapier integration. The docs describe two editors, a WYSIWYG editor and an HTML editor, and an endpoint that merges templates into one PDF. Paid plans add more room. Basic is for freelancers and small businesses, and Pro adds team access and unlimited templates. Paid plans come with a 7-day free trial. The docs set a default rate limit of 60 requests per minute for the API. Some endpoints may differ.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says you get an email at 80% of your monthly PDF limit. If you go over, you cannot generate more PDFs until the first day of the next month. You can upgrade at any time to raise the limit. The API docs add detail. When you reach an endpoint quota, further requests get a 403 Forbidden error until the new period starts. If you go over the rate limit, you get a 429 Too Many Requests error, and you should wait for the counter to reset.

## Gotchas

- The API key is shown only once. If you lose it, you must create a new one.
- The free plan has 1 template, so a second layout needs a paid plan.
- Digital signature is listed as coming soon on the Pro plan, so do not plan around it.

## Questions people ask

### Is PDF-API.io free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 100 PDFs per month, 1 user, and 1 template. It needs no credit card.

### What happens if I go over the PDF-API.io limit?

You get an email at 80% of your limit. After that you cannot make more PDFs until the first day of the next month, unless you upgrade.

### Is there a PDF-API.io free trial for paid plans?

Yes. Every paid plan has a 7-day free trial, and you are not charged until the trial ends.
