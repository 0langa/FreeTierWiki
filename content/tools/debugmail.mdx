DebugMail is a hosted test inbox for developers. Your app sends mail to DebugMail's SMTP host with a project's login, and the messages are collected in that project instead of reaching real people.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. It includes 1 project, 2 team members, unlimited test emails per day, 1 forwarding email per day, and a mailbox storage limit of 10MB per team. You do not need a real SMTP server: the site says you can start a project without one.

Captured emails can be viewed as HTML or plain text, grouped by project, shared with colleagues, and shared publicly through a secret link. The setup guide shows SMTP settings for several languages, all pointing at app.debugmail.io with the project's port, login, and password. DebugMail also says it provides the Silver plan free for open-source projects if you contact it.

## When you hit a limit

The main limit is storage: 10MB per team on the free plan. The page lists the limit but does not say whether older emails are removed or new ones refused when the mailbox is full.

Forwarding a captured email to a real address is limited to 1 per day, and forwarding rules are not on the free plan. Silver raises this to 10 projects, 100 forwards a day, and 1GB per team after a 2 weeks trial period, at $2 per user per month; Gold is $5 per user per month.

## Gotchas

- 10MB fills quickly if your test emails carry images or attachments, so clear the project often.
- Only 1 project is free, so staging and development of two apps share one inbox.
- The REST API, IMAP, and spam rating are not on the free plan; some are marked as coming soon.

## Questions people ask

### Is DebugMail free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with 1 project, 2 team members, and unlimited test emails per day.

### Do I need my own SMTP server to use DebugMail?

No. You point your app at app.debugmail.io with the project's credentials, and the site says no real SMTP server is required.

### Is DebugMail free for open-source projects?

DebugMail says it provides the Silver plan free for open-source projects; you contact it to connect.
