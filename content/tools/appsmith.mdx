Appsmith is an open source low-code platform for internal tools. You build admin panels, dashboards, and CRUD front ends from widgets, connect them to databases and APIs, and write JavaScript where you need logic. You can self-host it or use Appsmith's cloud; this entry covers the cloud Free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for individual developers and small teams and costs $0. On cloud it covers up to 5 users. Apps are unlimited, and you get 5 workspaces.

Version control with Git is included for 3 repos. Sign-in uses Google and GitHub OAuth, and access control uses 3 standard pre-defined roles. You can build public apps and embed them in other sites. Support is through the community on Discord. Apps show Powered by Appsmith branding. The Business plan costs $15 per user per month and adds workflows, reusable packages, custom roles, and audit logs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a sixth user joins a Free cloud workspace, or when you try to add a fourth Git repo. It lists no metered charges for the Free plan, so there is no usage bill to run up. Moving past 5 users means the Business plan, which is billed per user and allows up to 99 users.

## Gotchas

- Business billing counts any member added to a workspace during the billing cycle for the full month.
- Only public apps can be embedded on Free; private embedding with shared logins is an Enterprise feature.
- The free self-hosted Community edition is a separate option with its own terms; the numbers here are for cloud.

## Questions people ask

### Is Appsmith free?

Yes. Appsmith Cloud has a Free plan for up to 5 users with unlimited apps, and the open source edition can be self-hosted.

### Does the Appsmith Free plan include Git?

Yes. Version control with Git is included for 3 repos on the Free plan.

### Can I remove the Appsmith branding for free?

No. Removing the Powered by Appsmith branding starts on the Business plan.
