You point a plain HTML form at a SmartForms endpoint, or call it with Ajax, Axios, or Fetch. You manage settings and alerts in a web panel.

## What the free plan gives you

The Generous plan is forever free. It allows 50 submissions and 250MB of bandwidth a month. The plan lists a custom response page that shows your own logo and colors, Telegram and Slack bots, and spam protection.

SmartForms forwards files that arrive with a form. The site says it processes the files and sends them to you, and it stores nothing. A form can have several file inputs. Spam messages do not count toward your monthly submissions.

Spam checks use an AI-based method and a malicious IP database. If a message looks suspicious, the sender must solve a reCAPTCHA challenge. You can turn this off and use your own reCAPTCHA.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 50 submissions or 250MB in a month. It also shows no overage fee, and the free plan has no card on file.

To get more room you move up a plan. Starting costs $6.00 a month and allows 1000 submissions and 2GB of bandwidth. Business costs $14.00 a month and allows 10000 submissions and 10GB of bandwidth. You can cancel at any time, and upgrades are prorated.

## Gotchas

- SmartForms does not store submissions. If an alert fails to reach you, the site holds no copy for you to read later.
- Every input needs a name attribute, or its data is never sent.
- The pricing cards list webhooks, multiple e-mail notifications, and white-label after the free plan's list, so confirm which plan includes each before you rely on it.

## Questions people ask

### Is SmartForms free?

Yes. The Generous plan is forever free and allows 50 submissions and 250MB of bandwidth a month. Paid plans start at $6.00 a month.

### Does SmartForms store my submissions?

No. The FAQ says it does not hold any submission data. It receives each submission, processes it, and sends it to you.

### Can I use my own reCAPTCHA with SmartForms?

Yes. The FAQ says you can disable the built-in anti-spam system and use reCAPTCHA or another tool on your side.
