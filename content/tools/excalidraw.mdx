Excalidraw is a whiteboard with a hand-drawn look, used by developers for architecture sketches, flow diagrams, and explanations. The free app runs in the browser, and the editor is also open-source software you can embed. Excalidraw+ is the paid hosted product that adds cloud storage, teams, and sharing controls on top.

## What the free plan gives you

The Excalidraw+ pricing page lists a Free solo plan that is free forever. It includes the full editor functions, 1 infinite scene, unlimited collaborators, export to PNG, SVG, or a saved file, integrations such as Notion and Obsidian, libraries, and end-to-end encryption locally in your browser.

The comparison table adds 10 AI requests a day, unlimited Mermaid to Excalidraw conversions, invite-by-link collaboration, sharing with guests for free, and a personal library stored in the browser. Image uploads are compressed on Free. The plan's Draw now button links to the free app at excalidraw.com.

## When you hit a limit

The AI allowance is daily: after 10 AI requests you wait for the next day or move to Plus, which allows 100 a day. The free plan has no usage charges, so nothing is billed.

The other limits are about storage. Free has 1 infinite scene and does not sync scenes to a server, so to keep more drawings you save each to a file. Excalidraw+ Plus costs $6 a month per user and adds unlimited scenes and folders, cloud saving, access management, view-only links, comments, and presentations, with a 14-day free trial. The FAQ says payment is by credit card only.

## Gotchas

- Free scenes are not synced to a server, so save a file if you want the drawing on another computer or as a backup.
- There is only 1 scene on Free; starting a new drawing means saving or exporting the current one first.
- The pricing page sits on the Excalidraw+ site, but the Free plan's Draw now button links to the regular excalidraw.com app.

## Questions people ask

### Is Excalidraw free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever with the full editor, 1 infinite scene, unlimited collaborators, and exports.

### What is the difference between Excalidraw and Excalidraw+?

Excalidraw is the free app. Excalidraw+ is the paid hosted product at $6 a month per user, with unlimited cloud-saved scenes, teams, access management, and comments.

### Does Excalidraw have AI features for free?

Yes, within limits. Free accounts get 10 AI requests a day, and Plus gets 100.
