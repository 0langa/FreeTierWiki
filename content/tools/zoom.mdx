Zoom Workplace is Zoom's app for video meetings, team chat, whiteboards, notes, and clips. A developer team would pick the free Basic plan for short stand-ups, pairing calls, and demos, often because clients already use Zoom. This entry is the meeting app; to build video into your own product, see the separate Zoom Video SDK entry.

## What the free plan gives you

Workplace Basic is free and covers 1 license. Meetings run for up to 40 minutes with up to 100 participants per meeting, and recording is local. Instant messaging, VoIP calling, Gmail and Outlook connections, Google and Microsoft calendar sync, tasks, and the Hub for Zoom files are included.

The free AI features are small monthly allowances: meeting summaries for 3 hosted meetings per month, 20 AI queries per month, and AI note-taking for 3 uses per month. You also get 3 editable whiteboards and 5 two-minute clips. Workplace Pro, at $14.16 per user per month billed annually, raises meetings to 30 hours and adds 10 GB of cloud storage per user.

## When you hit a limit

Meetings on Basic are limited to 40 minutes each; the comparison table lists a meeting duration of 40 mins for Basic against 30 hours on paid plans. The pricing page does not describe what you see when a meeting reaches the limit.

Participant capacity is 100 per meeting on both Basic and Pro; larger meetings need the Large Meeting add-on on a paid plan. When the monthly AI allowances run out, the page does not say whether they reset on a calendar month or the account's cycle.

## Gotchas

- Forty minutes is the cap per meeting, so long planning sessions get cut short.
- Basic is one license; a team that wants shared admin needs paid seats for each user.
- Paid plans are billed annually as a single upfront charge, and the monthly price shown is only for comparison.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoom free?

Yes. Zoom Workplace Basic is free, with meetings of up to 40 minutes and up to 100 participants.

### How long can a free Zoom meeting last?

Up to 40 minutes. Paid plans allow meetings of up to 30 hours.

### Is this the same as the Zoom Video SDK?

No. This is the meeting app; the Video SDK is a separate developer product for adding video to your own app.
