You run the ngrok agent CLI next to your app, and it prints a public URL that forwards to your local port. It suits developers who need a quick tunnel for testing, and the agent also has SDKs for Python, Go, Node, and Rust.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and includes 1 GB of outbound transfer, 20,000 HTTP requests and 5,000 TCP connections per month. These quotas reset each monthly billing cycle. A separate $5 one-time usage credit expires after a year, works only on Free and cannot pay for outbound transfer.

You get up to three online endpoints, three concurrent agents, one team member and one assigned development domain that stays the same when the agent restarts. Endpoints have no session timeout, but remain subject to quotas. Rate limits are 4,000 HTTP requests and 100 TCP connections per minute.

HTML browser traffic sees an interstitial; programmatic API traffic is unaffected. TCP endpoints require credit-card verification, which ngrok says it does not charge.

## When you hit a limit

Free does not allow extra usage beyond its credit or limits and does not bill overages. Monthly traffic quotas reset each billing cycle; the separate $5 credit does not replenish. Check the dashboard to distinguish quota exhaustion from exhausted credit. Upgrade if you need more capacity.

Paid plans differ. On Hobbyist, which costs $10 a month and includes $10 of monthly usage, endpoints stop until the next billing cycle when the credit runs out. On Pay-as-you-go, endpoints keep working and ngrok bills the extra usage at the end of the cycle. Unused credit does not roll over.

## Gotchas

- The $5 credit is one-time; it is separate from the monthly traffic quotas.
- A development domain does not use up endpoint hours, but the free plan caps online endpoints at 3.
- Bringing your own domain is not on Free or Hobbyist. It starts on Pay-as-you-go.

## Questions people ask

### Is ngrok free?

Yes. Free includes 1 GB transfer and 20,000 HTTP requests monthly, up to three online endpoints, and a separate $5 one-time credit valid for a year.

### What happens when ngrok free credit runs out?

The pricing page says there is no usage beyond the credit on the Free plan. Hobbyist endpoints stop until the credit resets, and Pay-as-you-go keeps running and bills extra usage.

### Does ngrok need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan in general. TCP endpoints on Free need credit card verification.
