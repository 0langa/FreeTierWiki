You run the ngrok agent CLI next to your app, and it prints a public URL that forwards to your local port. It suits developers who need a quick tunnel for testing, and the agent also has SDKs for Python, Go, Node, and Rust.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and comes with $5 of one-time included usage. The pricing page says the credit lasts a year, only works while you are on the Free plan, and cannot pay for data transfer out. There is no usage beyond the credit.

The plan allows up to 3 online endpoints, up to 1 GB of data transfer, and up to 20k HTTP/S requests. You get one team member and an assigned dev domain for public endpoints. HTTP/S endpoints show an interstitial page to visitors. Rate limits are 4k HTTP requests a minute and 100 TCP connections a minute.

TCP endpoints on Free need credit card verification, and the pricing page lists randomly assigned TCP addresses only with that verification.

## When you hit a limit

On the free plan, usage past the credit is not allowed, so your endpoints stop. You are not billed for extra. The pricing page does not describe the Free case in more detail.

Paid plans differ. On Hobbyist, which costs $10 a month and includes $10 of monthly usage, endpoints stop until the next billing cycle when the credit runs out. On Pay-as-you-go, endpoints keep working and ngrok bills the extra usage at the end of the cycle. Unused credit does not roll over.

## Gotchas

- The $5 credit is one-time, so the free plan is not an ongoing monthly allowance.
- A development domain does not use up endpoint hours, but the free plan caps online endpoints at 3.
- Bringing your own domain is not on Free or Hobbyist. It starts on Pay-as-you-go.

## Questions people ask

### Is ngrok free?

Yes, with limits. The Free plan gives a one-time credit of $5 that lasts a year, with up to 3 online endpoints. It does not give an ongoing monthly allowance.

### What happens when ngrok free credit runs out?

The pricing page says there is no usage beyond the credit on the Free plan. Hobbyist endpoints stop until the credit resets, and Pay-as-you-go keeps running and bills extra usage.

### Does ngrok need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan in general. TCP endpoints on Free need credit card verification.
