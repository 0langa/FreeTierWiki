You install the open-source Cypress App with npm, open the Launchpad, and write tests in your editor. Cypress Cloud is an optional web service that stores the results of your CI runs. This entry suits web developers and small teams that test in a browser.

## What the free plan gives you

The Cypress App is free and open source under the MIT license. It runs your tests on your own machine with no limit.

The free Cypress Cloud plan, called Starter, needs no credit card. It includes 10 users, 500 test results per month, and 100 prompt executions per month. A test result is one run of a single `it()` block that you record to Cloud with the `--record` flag. Local runs do not count toward recorded test results. Each cy.prompt call does count toward the separate AI allowance, including local open/run modes and CI. A test that retries counts once, and skipped tests do not count.

The pricing page also offers a free 30-day trial of the premium features. Non-commercial open-source projects can apply for an OSS plan with 100k test results per month.

## When you hit a limit

After 500 results, Free's unavailable on-demand option means --record still works but parallelization stops and new dashboard results hide until reset/upgrade. Team on-demand costs $6/1000 results.

At the user cap, remove someone or upgrade before adding another. Local runs remain unlimited. Exhausting 100 monthly prompt executions disables new prompt creation and AI self-healing until cycle renewal. Existing prompt commands/steps still execute, but AI-dependent operations fail. Distinguish ordinary local tests from Cloud-recorded results and AI features: their limits do not all stop test execution in the same way.

## Gotchas

- Only runs recorded with `--record` count toward the monthly results. A big CI suite that records every build can use up 500 results fast.
- The Free plan lists on-demand test results as not available, so extra volume means a paid plan.
- The pricing page says organizations you create start on the Free plan, and you add payment details only when you upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cypress free?

Yes. The Cypress App is free and open source, and Cypress Cloud has a free Starter plan with 10 users and 500 test results per month.

### Does Cypress need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says you do not need a credit card to create an account. You add payment details only when you upgrade.

### What happens when I go over the Cypress Cloud free limit?

Recording continues, but parallelization is disabled and new results are hidden from the dashboard until you upgrade or the billing cycle resets. Local test runs remain available.
