You install the open-source Cypress App with npm, open the Launchpad, and write tests in your editor. Cypress Cloud is an optional web service that stores the results of your CI runs. This entry suits web developers and small teams that test in a browser.

## What the free plan gives you

The Cypress App is free and open source under the MIT license. It runs your tests on your own machine with no limit.

The free Cypress Cloud plan, called Starter, needs no credit card. It includes 10 users, 500 test results per month, and 100 prompt executions per month. A test result is one run of a single `it()` block that you record to Cloud with the `--record` flag. Local runs do not count. A test that retries counts once, and skipped tests do not count.

The pricing page also offers a free 30-day trial of the premium features. Non-commercial open-source projects can apply for an OSS plan with 100k test results per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what Cloud does when you pass 500 test results. It lists on-demand test results as not available on the Free plan. On-demand test results cost $6 per 1000 on the Team plan, so you would need to upgrade to buy more.

For users, the page is clear. When you reach the limit, you cannot add another user until you remove one or upgrade. Your local test runs do not depend on any of these limits.

## Gotchas

- Only runs recorded with `--record` count toward the monthly results. A big CI suite that records every build can use up 500 results fast.
- The Free plan lists on-demand test results as not available, so extra volume means a paid plan.
- The pricing page says organizations you create start on the Free plan, and you add payment details only when you upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cypress free?

Yes. The Cypress App is free and open source, and Cypress Cloud has a free Starter plan with 10 users and 500 test results per month.

### Does Cypress need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says you do not need a credit card to create an account. You add payment details only when you upgrade.

### What happens when I go over the Cypress Cloud free limit?

The pricing page does not say. It shows no on-demand results on the Free plan, and it points you to upgrade for more.
