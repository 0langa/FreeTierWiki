You call a REST API with a Bearer token and get JSON or CSV back, or you use the Google Sheets add-on with formulas. It suits students, spreadsheet users, and hobby developers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan costs $0 and is described as the best option for students or small side projects. You get 100 API credits per day and 100 Sheets formulas per day. It uses standard API endpoints, and stock quotes and option chains are 24 hours delayed.

Each successful call normally costs 1 credit. If a response includes several symbols, the call costs 1 credit per symbol. Only status 200 and 203 responses use credits.

The credit counter resets at 9:30 AM Eastern Time, the NYSE opening bell. Every plan allows up to 50 concurrent requests.

New accounts can also take a 30-day free trial of a paid plan with no credit card. Paid plans start at $12 per month with annual billing, or $30 per month when paid monthly.

## When you hit a limit

You can make any single request as long as you have at least 1 credit left, even if it costs more than your balance. Your balance can then go negative. Further requests return 429 responses until the window resets at 9:30 AM Eastern Time.

A request over the 50 concurrent request limit also returns a 429. There is no bill on the Free plan. Large option chains can drain 100 credits fast, so use the filters to ask only for the strikes and expirations you need.

## Gotchas

- An entire option chain returns every contract, and each one costs a credit. One unfiltered call can use your whole day.
- Free data is delayed by 24 hours. Real-time options data starts on the Trader plan.
- Your token is delivered by email after you sign in to the dashboard. Keep it secret, and send it in the Authorization header rather than the URL.

## Questions people ask

### Is the Market Data API free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan gives 100 API credits and 100 Sheets formulas per day. A separate 30-day trial of a paid plan needs no credit card.

### When do Market Data API credits reset?

The daily counter resets at 9:30 AM Eastern Time, at the NYSE opening bell.

### Is Market Data API data real time on the free plan?

No. Stock quotes and option chains are 24 hours delayed on Free. Real-time options data starts on the Trader plan.
