You call the ipstack endpoint over HTTPS with your access key and an IP address. The free plan is a small test allowance, so it fits people who want to try the API before paying.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and includes 100 requests a month. It has the Location module, which returns continent, country, region, and city for an IP address.

The pricing table marks several things as not included on Free: standard support, commercial use, the Time Zone, Connection, and Currency modules, the Bulk endpoint, and the Security module. The FAQ describes the Free plan as a way to test the API on a non-production website or application.

Paid plans start with Basic at $12.99 a month for 50,000 requests. Basic adds commercial use, standard support, and the Time Zone, Connection, and Currency modules. The Bulk endpoint and Security module are not on Basic.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ lists notifications at 75%, 90%, and 100% of the monthly allowance. Its overage examples cover Basic, Professional, and Professional Plus; it does not publish a Free overage rate or explain whether Free can continue beyond 100 requests.

Official API error guidance also documents usage_limit_reached when monthly quota is exhausted, with waiting for reset or upgrading as remedies. These general descriptions do not settle Free-specific handling. Do not assume either uninterrupted paid overages or a guaranteed hard stop for Free. Check the applicable subscription settings and handle quota errors; repeated retries do not restore exhausted allowance.

## Gotchas

- The free allowance is 100 requests a month, not a day. A single test page can use it up.
- Commercial use is not allowed on the Free plan. You need a paid plan, starting with Basic, for a real product.
- Free responses hold the Location module only, so time zone, currency, and connection fields are not part of what you get.

## Questions people ask

### Is ipstack free?

Yes, there is a Free plan with 100 requests a month and no credit card. It has the Location module only and does not allow commercial use.

### What happens when I go over the ipstack limit?

Notifications are listed at 75%, 90%, and 100%. Official sources describe paid overages and quota errors, but do not settle which Free-specific policy applies.

### Can I use the ipstack free plan in production?

No. The FAQ describes the Free plan as a way to test on a non-production website or application, and the pricing table lists commercial use as not included.
