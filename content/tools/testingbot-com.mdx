You run tests from your own machine or CI, pointed at the TestingBot grid. A web dashboard shows each test with video and logs.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says there is a 14-day free plan, and no credit card is needed to register. It is a trial that lets you try the service without paying. You can upgrade later by credit card or wire transfer.

Open source projects can apply for a separate free plan. It offers unlimited automated tests with no monthly cap and 2 parallel sessions, on 6100+ browsers and devices, including real iOS and Android devices. To qualify, the project must be public, non-commercial, actively maintained, and under an OSI-approved license. You create a free account first, then submit the repository URL, and approval usually takes a couple of business days.

Paid plans start with Live at €20 per month billed annually, or €30 month to month. Automated costs €50 per month billed annually, and Automated Pro costs €90.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the trial ends, beyond needing to upgrade. For paid plans, the FAQ is clear. Once you reach your monthly testing time you can no longer run tests until your next billing month, or you can upgrade to a larger plan.

You can cancel at any time, but if you cancel mid-month you are charged for that month. Pay-as-you-go packs let you buy testing minutes once, for example 1,000 minutes for €60, with no subscription.

## Gotchas

- The pages disagree about the free offer. The pricing page lists a 14-day plan with no card, and the home page FAQ describes a free plan plus a 7-day trial that asks for a card. Check the sign-up screen.
- The open source plan is not automatic. Your project is reviewed, and it must be actively maintained with recent commits.
- A parallel test is a test running at the same time. The open source plan lists 2 parallel sessions, and the Automated plan lists 1 parallel test.

## Questions people ask

### Is TestingBot free?

It has a 14-day free trial with no credit card, and a free plan for qualifying open source projects. Other use needs a paid plan.

### Does TestingBot have a free plan for open source?

Yes. Open source projects can apply for unlimited automated testing with 2 parallel sessions. The project must be public, non-commercial, and under an OSI-approved license.

### What happens if I use all my monthly testing time?

You can no longer run tests until your next billing month, or you can upgrade to a larger plan.
