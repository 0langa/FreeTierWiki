You connect your repository, add a configuration file, and read the reports in the Scrutinizer web app. The docs have guides for PHP, Python, Ruby, Java, NodeJS, and Go.

## What the free plan gives you

Scrutinizer does not charge for open-source repositories. The pricing FAQ says you can add as many as you like. There is no separate free tier name or usage meter. The line is open source or private.

All plans have unlimited repositories and users. Private repositories start with a 14 day trial. The trial asks for no credit card, and you subscribe in your profile if you want to continue.

The paid plans are Basic at EUR 49 per month, Professional at EUR 99 per month, and Unlimited at EUR 199 per month. Basic and Professional include 1 container, and Unlimited includes 2. Extra containers cost EUR 50 per month each.

## When you hit a limit

A container is the environment where an analysis runs. With one container, inspections run one at a time. If you push often, the next inspection waits in line. That is a speed limit, not a bill.

For open-source repositories the pricing page names no cap and no bill. For private code, the trial ends after 14 days. After that you need a paid plan, which is billed monthly in advance, with no minimum contract period.

## Gotchas

- The free offer is for open-source repositories. A private repository needs a paid plan once the trial ends.
- Code Intelligence, the add-on for reading and reviewing code like in an IDE, costs an extra EUR 14.90 per seat on every plan.
- The sign-up page lists GitHub, Bitbucket, and email. It does not list GitLab.

## Questions people ask

### Is Scrutinizer free?

For open-source projects, yes. The pricing FAQ says it does not charge for open-source repositories, and you can add as many as you like.

### Does Scrutinizer need a credit card?

No. The 14 day trial for private repositories asks for no credit card details.

### How much does Scrutinizer cost for private repositories?

Plans start at EUR 49 per month for Basic. Professional is EUR 99 and Unlimited is EUR 199. Extra containers cost EUR 50 each.
