You build a template in the browser editor, then send a template ID and a JSON payload to one REST endpoint. Zapier, Make.com, n8n, and Bubble.io call the same endpoint. You need no HTML or CSS to design the layout.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and gives you 50 PDFs or images per month and 3 templates. It includes the drag-and-drop editor, the REST API, and the Zapier, Make.com, and Bubble.io integrations. You can build and test a real workflow before paying. One PDF costs 1 credit, no matter how many pages it has. Other operations, such as adding a watermark or merging PDFs, cost 0.5 credit per request.

Paid plans start at Lite for $29 per month, with 1,200 credits. A Data Processing Agreement comes from Lite upward. Teams, Bring Your Own Storage, and PDF passwords start on the Professional plan. The free plan has community-level support only. Generation takes about 1.5 seconds to 30 seconds.

## When you hit a limit

CraftMyPDF emails you when your usage reaches 80% of your monthly credits, and again at 100%. It never upgrades your plan automatically. To move up, log in, open the Subscription page, and pick a plan. Credits reset at the start of each month, and unused credits do not roll over. The pricing page does not say exactly what the API returns after the free credits run out. Since the free plan takes no card, it cannot create a bill.

## Gotchas

- Free credits do not roll over. They reset at the start of each month.
- E-signature requests cost 10 credits each, so they use up a small allowance fast.
- The free plan lacks a Data Processing Agreement, teams, Bring Your Own Storage, and PDF passwords.

## Questions people ask

### Is CraftMyPDF free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 50 PDFs or images per month and 3 templates, with no credit card. You get the editor, the REST API, and the Zapier, Make.com, and Bubble.io integrations.

### What happens when I run out of CraftMyPDF credits?

You get an email at 80% and again at 100% of your monthly credits. Plans are never upgraded for you, so you upgrade yourself on the Subscription page.

### Do unused CraftMyPDF credits roll over?

No. The allowance resets at the start of each month, and unused credits are not carried over.
