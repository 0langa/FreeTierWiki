You install CodeRabbit on your Git platform and it comments on each new pull request. You can also use it in VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf, or from a command line. It suits maintainers who want a quick AI review.

## What the free plan gives you

CodeRabbit's open-source plan is free forever. You sign up with GitHub or GitLab, install CodeRabbit on a public repository, and get reviews. The pricing FAQ says no extra setup is needed. Private repositories are not part of this free plan.

The paid plans add private repositories. Essentials costs $24 per developer per month billed annually, and Team costs $48. Advanced costs $72 and adds continuous security monitoring. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card. The docs say you can sign up with GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket Cloud, or Bitbucket Data Center.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not list limits for the free open-source plan. It does list review limits for paid plans: 5 pull request reviews per developer per hour on Essentials and 12 on Team, subject to a Fair Usage Policy. The page says there is no total cap on pull requests or repositories.

On paid plans, usage-based reviews let reviews continue after the hourly allowance. They cost $0.25 per reviewed file, and admins can pick Automatic, On demand, or Off and set a monthly spending cap. The page bills only developers who create pull requests.

## Gotchas

- Free reviews apply to public repositories only. A private repository needs a paid plan or a trial.
- The paid prices on the page assume annual billing. Essentials costs $30 per developer per month when billed monthly.
- CodeRabbit Agent for cloud coding tasks, Slack, and automations is separate and costs $0.40 per agent minute after trial and free minutes.

## Questions people ask

### Is CodeRabbit free?

Yes, for public repositories. Sign up with GitHub or GitLab and install CodeRabbit on a public repository for free reviews forever. Private repositories need a paid plan.

### How much does CodeRabbit cost for private repositories?

Essentials costs $24 per developer per month billed annually. Team costs $48 and Advanced costs $72, and every plan has a 14-day free trial.

### Does CodeRabbit need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says all plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.
