You use the Gemini API through an API key from Google AI Studio, with the Python or JavaScript SDK or plain REST. It suits developers who prototype AI features and do not want to run models themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

New accounts start on the Free Tier. It allows access to certain models in the Gemini API and AI Studio, with free input and output tokens, up to each model's rate limits. The pricing page lists "Limited access to certain models" and "Free of charge" prices for the Standard tier. Batch and Flex pricing show as not available on the free tier.

Rate limits are measured in requests per minute, tokens per minute, and requests per day. They apply per project, not per API key. Limits are stricter for experimental and preview models. The pricing page does not list exact numbers. You read your active limits in Google AI Studio.

## When you hit a limit

If you go over any single limit, the API returns a rate limit error. Daily request quotas reset at midnight Pacific time. Billing starts only after you link a billing account. To get higher limits and keep your prompts out of Google's training, you link a billing account and prepay a minimum of $5 to move to the Paid Tier. The Paid Tier also unlocks 5,000 free grounding requests per month for Search, then $14 per 1,000 requests.

## Gotchas

- On the free tier, Google uses your content to improve its products. The Paid Tier removes this.
- Rate limits are stricter for experimental and preview models.
- Grounding with Google Search and Maps shows as not available on the free tier for Gemini 3.x models.

## Questions people ask

### Is the Gemini API free?

Yes, on the Free Tier. You get free input and output tokens on supported models, with rate limits. The pricing page also lists free access to Google AI Studio.

### Does the Gemini API need a credit card?

No. New accounts start on the Free Tier. You link a billing account only to move to the Paid Tier.

### Is my data used to train Google models on the Gemini API free tier?

The pricing page says content on the free tier is used to improve Google's products. The billing page says moving to the Paid Tier keeps your prompts and responses out of that use.
