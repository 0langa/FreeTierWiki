Gemini API provides developers with access to Google's generative AI models for building applications that require text, code, or multimodal understanding. The free tier gives free input/output tokens on supported models, though exact rate limits are not published and vary by usage tier. Integration is straightforward via REST or SDK, and the service is ideal for teams looking to quickly add AI capabilities without managing infrastructure.
