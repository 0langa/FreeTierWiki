You paste a script, a stylesheet, and a form action into your HTML, then read the entries in the Pageclip dashboard. There is also a REST API and a webhook for developers who want the data elsewhere.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs nothing and includes one site, one form, and 1,000 submissions. The pricing page says prices are per month, and the submission count is the main cap.

Pageclip saves every input, textarea, or select that has a name attribute. It can send you an email for each submission. If you name a field `email`, the address becomes the reply-to of that email. A field named `subject` sets the email subject. You can also set a webhook so Pageclip posts each submission to your own server, and you can use the REST API to fetch data, including CSV output.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after the 1,000 submissions on the Free plan. It lists the next plans as Basic, with one site, unlimited forms, and 20,000 submissions, and Pro, with unlimited sites and 100,000 submissions.

Nothing on the page suggests a bill on the Free plan, and the Free plan needs no payment details, so no bill is expected. For a busy form, plan to move to a paid plan or add a second service.

## Gotchas

- File inputs are ignored, and Pageclip rejects multipart requests.
- A site accepts data only from the domains you list for it. Data from other domains is rejected.
- With more than one form on your website, you must create a named form in Pageclip for each one, and the free plan has one form.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pageclip free?

Yes. The Free plan includes one site, one form, and 1,000 submissions per month. Paid plans start at $9 per month.

### What happens when I go over the Pageclip free limit?

The pricing page does not say. The next plan up, Basic, allows 20,000 submissions on one site with unlimited forms.

### Does Pageclip work with a static site?

Yes. The docs say it works anywhere you can put an HTML form, including Jekyll, Hugo, Gatsby, and GitHub Pages.
