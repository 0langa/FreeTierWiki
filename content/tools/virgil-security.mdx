You use Virgil by adding its SDKs to your app and keeping an application in its dashboard. Your backend issues access tokens, and your client code registers users and encrypts data. It suits developers who add encryption to their own product.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan covers up to 250 registered users at no monthly cost. It includes unlimited secure communication between users, including group chat. You also get unlimited sharing and backup of sensitive data between devices, and unlimited registered user devices with authentication. Support comes through the Slack community.

The toolkits are E3Kit for end-to-end encrypted communication, and PureKit for protecting passwords and stored data. Users can be deleted from your application by its developers. The pricing page says the plans are designed to scale as your user or device count grows, and the Pro plan adds dev, staging, and production environments for teams that move past the free basics.

## When you hit a limit

Free stops at 250 registered users. After that, the pricing page lists 251 to 5K users at $99 per month, then $0.019 per user per month from 5K to 100K users. Above 100K users the price is custom. The home page says "$0.02/month per user beyond 250 users," which does not match the tiers, so ask Virgil before you plan a budget. The docs page does not describe any other effect of passing 250 users.

## Gotchas

- Free support is the Slack community only. The guaranteed SLA, 24/7 support, and separate dev, staging, and production environments are on the Pro plan.
- On-premise hosting and country-specific data location are Enterprise features.
- The page footers show 2020 and 2021 copyright dates. Check that Virgil is still actively maintained before you make it a core part of your product.

## Questions people ask

### Is Virgil Security free?

Yes, up to 250 registered users. The Free plan includes unlimited secure communication, group chat, and registered devices.

### What does Virgil Security cost after 250 users?

The pricing page lists $99 per month for 251 to 5K users, then $0.019 per user per month up to 100K users. The home page quotes a different per-user price, so confirm before you budget.

### Does Virgil Security need a credit card?

The pricing page lists a free sign-up with no upfront costs and shows no card step.
