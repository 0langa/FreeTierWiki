You build forms in the web editor and share them by link, embed, or QR code. Answers go to your dashboard and to the tools you connect.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan has no limit on forms, responses, or questions per form. You can add images, set custom colors, build logic, use hidden fields, and score answers. Embedding works on the free plan too.

Integrations include Google Sheets, Slack, Zapier, Calendly, Cal.com, SavvyCal, and webhooks. You also get signature collection and an email notification to yourself.

File uploads are allowed up to 10MB, and form analytics are basic. The home page lists more than 500 templates. The site says you can connect FabForm to over 6,000 tools through Zapier and similar links.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page shows no cap on forms or responses, so there is no hard stop to hit. It does not say what happens if a form gets very heavy traffic.

You meet the plan edge when you need a paid feature instead. The free plan does not offer custom domains, team members, payment collection, a brand logo, or removal of FabForm branding. The one-time Lifetime Deal is the only paid plan shown, at $99. The pricing page names no monthly fee and no overage charge.

## Gotchas

- FabForm is a form builder with its own form pages. It does not give you an endpoint to post your own HTML form to.
- File uploads stop at 10MB on the free plan. The Lifetime Deal lists uploads as unlimited under a fair use policy.
- Free forms keep FabForm branding, cannot redirect to a URL, and have no partial submissions or refill links.

## Questions people ask

### Is FabForm free?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited forms, responses, and questions per form. A $99 Lifetime Deal adds extras such as custom domains and team members.

### Does FabForm need a credit card?

The pricing page shows no card step for the free plan. It asks you to choose Get Started Free.

### Can I use FabForm for a static site contact form?

You can embed a FabForm form on your site. FabForm does not offer a plain form endpoint for your own HTML form, so a form backend service fits that job better.
