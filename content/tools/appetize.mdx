You upload a build in the browser or through a CI tool or the REST API, then run it from the Apps dashboard. You can share the app by link or embed it in a web page.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It allows a maximum of 2 active devices and gives you 30 minutes, refilled monthly. The support page says you can sign up and start without entering a credit card or any other details, and it describes the plan as a limited account for testing Appetize with your own app.

For comparison, the Starter plan costs $59 per month on monthly billing. It allows 3 active devices and includes 500 minutes, then charges $0.06 per minute.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when your 30 free minutes run out. The page says the minutes are refilled monthly, but it gives no detail about a stop, a slowdown, or a bill when they run out. Appetize also states that you do not need a credit card for the Free plan. Check the plan comparison page or ask support before you rely on the free minutes for a demo.

Paid plans bill extra minutes at $0.06 per minute after the included 500 minutes.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has only 30 minutes in total each month, so long test sessions use it up.
- Only 2 active devices can run at the same time on the free plan.
- The free plan is described as limited, so check the plan comparison before you promise a client a long demo.

Yes, there is a Free plan. It has a maximum of 2 active devices and 30 minutes refilled monthly. The support page says no credit card is needed.

### Does Appetize need a credit card?

No for the Free plan. Appetize says you can sign up and start without entering a credit card or any other details.

### What happens when I go over the Appetize free minutes?

The pricing page does not say. Paid plans charge $0.06 per minute after their included minutes.
