FormSubmit turns any HTML form into an email. You point the form's action at a FormSubmit URL with your address, and each submission arrives in your inbox. There is no account and no backend code.

## What the free plan gives you

FormSubmit is free and needs no registration. You can create unlimited forms for one email address and receive unlimited submissions. Every form comes with reCAPTCHA spam protection, which you can turn off per form.

Special hidden fields add features: a custom subject, a reply-to address, a CC list, a thank-you page, an auto-response to the sender, and a choice of 3 email templates. A webhook can fire on each new submission. File uploads work too, as long as all files in one submission add up to no more than 10MB. If you miss an email, you can read your submissions through the archive API up to 5 times a day. Submissions stay in the archive for 30 days.

## When you hit a limit

You will not get a bill. Setup is free, and the site shows no paid plan for the form endpoint. It lists no quota on forms or submissions, and it does not say what happens if a form sends a very large volume.

The site names only a few hard limits. Files in one submission must add up to no more than 10MB, and archive API calls are capped at 5 a day. FormSubmit also says it imposes technical limitations from time to time to filter out bots, spam, and other malicious programs. It recommends keeping reCAPTCHA on so your forms avoid them.

## Gotchas

- Nothing is delivered until you confirm your email address. The first submission to a new address triggers a confirmation email instead.
- The auto-response does not work on forms that turn off reCAPTCHA or that submit through AJAX.
- The archive keeps submissions for only 30 days, and uploaded files are not kept or available through the API. Save what you need from the emails.

## Questions people ask

### Is FormSubmit really free?

Yes. The site shows no paid plan for the form endpoint, and it allows unlimited forms and submissions. You only confirm your email address once.

### How do I hide my email address in a FormSubmit form?

Confirm your address first. FormSubmit then sends you a random string to use in the form's action URL instead of your email address, so spam bots cannot read it.

### Does FormSubmit store my form submissions?

Yes, for 30 days. You can read them through the archive API up to 5 times a day. Uploaded files are not stored.
