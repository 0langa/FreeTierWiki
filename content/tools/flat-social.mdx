You use Flat.social in the browser with nothing to install. You create a space, share the link, and guests walk their avatars around to join conversations.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 forever. It allows up to 5 people at once, with unlimited session length and spatial audio and video rooms. The pricing page says everyone joins free and that you pay only for how many people fit in your flat at once, with no named seats and no per-guest surprises.

The site says no credit card is needed, and you can try a live demo without signing up. The home page describes custom maps, branding, and room layouts, whiteboards, games and icebreakers, and webinars with screen sharing and Q and A, but it does not say which of them the free plan includes.

## When you hit a limit

The cap is on how many people are in your flat at the same time: 5 on Free. The pricing page does not say what a sixth person sees. To fit more, upgrade from your flat's settings. The Monthly plan costs $6 per seat per month for 10 to 100 seats, and you can resize whenever, prorated. A Day Pass costs $2 per seat per day with a 10 seat minimum and covers up to 100 people for a one-off event. You can cancel a Day Pass for free until 24 hours before.

## Gotchas

- The limit counts people in the space at the same time, not total invited guests or session length.
- Paid plans start at 10 seats, so there is no small step between 5 and 10 people.
- Priority support is listed with the Monthly plan, not the free one.

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever, with up to 5 people at once, unlimited session length, and spatial audio and video rooms.

### How much does Flat.social cost for more people?

The Monthly plan is $6 per seat per month for 10 to 100 seats. A Day Pass is $2 per seat per day with a 10 seat minimum.

No. The home page says no credit card is required, and you can try a live demo without signing up.
