Framagit is Framasoft's hosted GitLab Community Edition service. It combines Git repositories with project collaboration, and Framasoft documents continuous integration and static Pages hosting. The published allowance is 42 projects per person, including public and private projects.

## What the free plan gives you

Framasoft's current Framagit guide says the forge is open to everyone within the 42-project allowance. The live help page identifies the installation as GitLab Community Edition. Framasoft describes its hosted services as free and supported by donations; that does not establish every signup or resource policy.

The official tutorials cover account creation, profile settings, Git workstation setup, SSH keys, project activity and team communication. The current sign-in page also offers GitHub, GitLab.com and Bitbucket sign-in. Separate documentation explains publishing static sites with GitLab Pages, while the service overview links GitLab CI documentation. These are documented capabilities, not a promise of unlimited build minutes, storage or artifact retention. Public and private projects both count toward the stated personal project allowance.

## When you hit a limit

The readable guides do not explain the response when someone reaches the project allowance. They also do not publish a storage allowance, CI-minute budget or overage price. The sign-in page says projects without automatic cleanup of old pipelines receive a one-year cleanup setting to limit logs and artifacts. A project count alone does not prove that every resource has an enforced spending stop.

Keep a local copy of your repository and the build outputs you need. Before moving a large project or depending on hosted pipelines, confirm the current resource and account policies with Framasoft. No additional paid capacity or automatic upgrade is established here.

## Gotchas

- New accounts require moderation. The sign-in page estimates 1 to 5 days before activation and says Tor exit nodes are blocked.
- An older registration tutorial describes an earlier interface; use it as orientation rather than a guarantee of today's exact controls or approval process.
- IPTV link sharing, copyrighted non-free uploads, advertising-only profiles, huge repository mirrors and using the service as file storage can lead to bans without further warning.

## Questions people ask

### How many projects does Framagit allow?

Framasoft documents 42 projects per person. They may be public or private.

### Is Framagit the same as GitLab.com?

No. Framagit is Framasoft's own GitLab Community Edition installation, so GitLab.com's hosted plan quotas are not its published allowance.

### Can I assume immediate account access?

No. New accounts are moderated, and the current sign-in page estimates 1 to 5 days before activation.
