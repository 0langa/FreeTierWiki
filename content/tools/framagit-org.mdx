You use Framagit like any GitLab server: in the web interface, or with Git over SSH or HTTPS. The service is run by Framasoft, a non-profit. It suits free-software projects and small personal repositories.

## What the free plan gives you

Framagit is a GitLab Community Edition instance that Framasoft offers for free. It has no price tiers. The sign-in page lists a single limit: 42 projects per account.

You can register on the site, or sign in with a GitHub, GitLab.com, or Bitbucket account. Because it is GitLab, you get repositories, issues, merge requests, and CI pipelines through a `.gitlab-ci.yml` file.

To limit the space that logs and pipeline artifacts take up, any project without an automatic cleanup setting gets a one-year cleanup of old pipelines.

## When you hit a limit

The page does not say what happens when you try to create more projects. It states that you can only have 42 projects per account.

The page is also clear on abuse. Accounts that break the rules are banned without further warning. Framagit is not for IPTV link sharing, copyrighted non-free material, pure advertisement profiles, mirroring huge repositories, or storing files as if it were a drive.

## Gotchas

- Account creation is moderated because of spam. A new account only becomes active once the moderation team authorizes it, which takes between 1 and 5 days.
- Tor exit nodes are blocked.
- Old pipelines are cleaned up after one year when a project has no cleanup setting of its own, so do not keep artifacts there as an archive.

## Questions people ask

### Is Framagit free?

Yes. Framasoft offers this GitLab Community Edition server for free. Each account can have up to 42 projects.

### Why is my Framagit account not active?

New accounts are moderated because of spam. The moderation team authorizes them, which takes between 1 and 5 days.

### Can I mirror a big repository to Framagit?

No. The sign-in page lists mirroring huge repositories as something Framagit is not for. Accounts that do so are banned without further warning.
