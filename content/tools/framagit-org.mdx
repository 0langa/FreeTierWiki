Framagit is Framasoft's hosted GitLab Community Edition service. It combines Git repositories with project collaboration, and Framasoft documents continuous integration and static Pages hosting. The published allowance is 42 projects per person, including public and private projects.

## What the free plan gives you

Framasoft's current Framagit guide says the forge is open to everyone within the 42-project allowance. The live help page identifies the installation as GitLab Community Edition. Framasoft describes its hosted services as free and supported by donations; that does not establish every signup or resource policy.

The official tutorials cover account creation, profile settings, Git workstation setup, SSH keys, project activity and team communication. Separate documentation explains publishing static sites with GitLab Pages, while the service overview links GitLab CI documentation. These are documented capabilities, not a promise of unlimited build minutes, storage or artifact retention. Public and private projects both count toward the stated personal project allowance.

## When you hit a limit

The readable guides do not explain the response when someone reaches the project allowance. They also do not publish a storage allowance, CI-minute budget, overage price or current retention guarantee. A project count alone does not prove that every resource has an enforced spending stop.

Keep a local copy of your repository and the build outputs you need. Before moving a large project or depending on hosted pipelines, confirm the current resource and account policies with Framasoft. No additional paid capacity or automatic upgrade is established here.

## Gotchas

- The sign-in page presented an anti-bot screen during this review. Current activation timing and third-party sign-in options were not independently confirmed.
- An older registration tutorial describes an earlier interface; use it as orientation rather than a guarantee of today's exact controls or approval process.
- Earlier sign-in claims about Tor access, pipeline cleanup and abuse enforcement could not be reconfirmed, so this entry does not present them as current terms.

## Questions people ask

### How many projects does Framagit allow?

Framasoft documents 42 projects per person. They may be public or private.

### Is Framagit the same as GitLab.com?

No. Framagit is Framasoft's own GitLab Community Edition installation, so GitLab.com's hosted plan quotas are not its published allowance.

### Can I assume immediate account access?

No activation deadline is confirmed here. The sign-in page presented an anti-bot screen during the current review, and the older registration tutorial cannot establish today's approval timing.
