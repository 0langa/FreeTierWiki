Jitsi Meet is open-source video meeting software, and meet.jit.si is the free hosted instance run by 8x8. The room creator signs in; subsequent participants can join without creating an account. That distinction matters when inviting contributors or clients. For meetings inside a product, compare the separate Jitsi as a Service developer offering or a deployment you host yourself.

## What the free plan gives you

The hosted service provides video meetings with screen sharing and integrated chat. The Jitsi Meet overview also describes custom meeting URLs, presentations, Etherpad document editing, and HD Opus audio. Features and availability can change on the public instance.

The older overview says no account is ever needed, but the specific authentication announcement and current security page supersede that claim for meet.jit.si: since August 24, 2023, creating a room requires an account. Guests do not need an account, and supplying their name or email is optional.

Jitsi encrypts traffic in transit by default. End-to-end encryption is a separate option on supported clients, with limitations; the security page says it covers audio, video, and screen sharing rather than chat or polls. Do not interpret the overview's broad encryption language as a promise that every feature is end-to-end encrypted.

## When you hit a limit

The October 9, 2026 announcement limits meet.jit.si meetings to 95 minutes. It also says more changes are coming, without explaining exactly what happens at the time boundary.

The hosted terms separately limit free use to 25 monthly active endpoints across your accounts. An endpoint can be a browser tab, laptop, or mobile device used in a meeting with another endpoint. Above that allowance, 8x8 may limit use or require a paid service. This is separate from meeting duration.

## Gotchas

- The room creator must authenticate, even though the older general overview still advertises anonymous use.
- The service is provided as-is without support or an uptime commitment. The operator can suspend, change, or limit features without notice.
- The terms say the service is not intended for backup or long-term storage. Check the current site before scheduling recurring meetings.

## Questions people ask

### Is Jitsi Meet free?

The public meet.jit.si service is free within its published limits. Its room creator needs an account; invited guests do not.

### Is there a time limit on meet.jit.si?

Yes. The October 9, 2026 announcement sets a 95-minute meeting limit.

### Can I use Jitsi for meetings inside my app?

Compare Jitsi as a Service or self-host the open-source software. The public hosted service has its own usage terms and availability limitations.
