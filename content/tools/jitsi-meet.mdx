Jitsi Meet is an open-source video meeting app, and meet.jit.si is the free hosted instance run by 8x8. You open the site, start a meeting, and share the URL; nobody needs an account. A developer team would pick it for quick calls with contributors or clients, or for open-source community calls where asking people to sign up is a barrier. To put meetings inside your own app with branding and backend events, 8x8 offers Jitsi as a Service, a separate developer product.

## What the free plan gives you

The Jitsi Meet page describes a fully encrypted, 100% open source video conferencing solution that you can use for free with no account needed. On meet.jit.si you can start a meeting right away or book a meeting URL in advance where you are the only moderator.

In a meeting you can share your desktop and presentations, invite people with a simple custom URL, edit documents together using Etherpad, and trade messages in the integrated chat. You can connect your calendar to see and start meetings, and mobile apps are available. Jitsi says audio uses HD Opus and that conversations are encrypted by default.

## When you hit a limit

On October 9, 2026, the Jitsi team announced that meetings on meet.jit.si are limited to 95 minutes, starting that day, and that more changes are coming. The meet.jit.si page itself now shows a notice that new meeting limits apply. The announcement does not say what happens at the 95-minute mark.

The terms of service say the service is provided as-is and without support, with no commitment on availability, and that 8x8 may suspend, limit, change, or downgrade the service at any time, with or without notice.

## Gotchas

- The older Jitsi Meet page still says you can use it all day, every day; the newer 95-minute limit applies to meet.jit.si.
- More changes to meet.jit.si were announced as coming, so check the site before relying on it for a recurring meeting.
- The terms say the service is not meant for back-up or long-term storage of data.

## Questions people ask

### Is Jitsi Meet free?

Yes. The Jitsi Meet page says you can use meet.jit.si for free with no account needed.

### Is there a time limit on meet.jit.si?

Yes. Since October 9, 2026, meetings on meet.jit.si are limited to 95 minutes.

### What is the difference between Jitsi Meet and Jitsi as a Service?

Jitsi Meet on meet.jit.si is the free meeting app you use in a browser. Jitsi as a Service is 8x8's developer product for adding meetings to your own website or app.
