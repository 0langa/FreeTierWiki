You use the HostedScan web dashboard to add targets, run scans, and read reports. There is also a developer API with webhooks on the Premium plan and above. It suits small IT teams who want several scanners in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

HostedScan has no permanent free plan. Instead, you get a 14 day free trial of its paid plans. The home page says the 14 days are free and no credit card is required. The pricing page puts a "Try 14 days for free" button on the Basic and Premium plans.

The paid plans each include 5 targets and unlimited scanning of those targets. Basic costs $39 per month and covers full-power scans, scheduled scans, custom reporting, and inviting your team. Premium costs $109 per month and adds authenticated scans, internal network scanning, and a developer API. Professional costs $189 per month and adds SAML SSO and white-label reports.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the trial ends or what the trial limits are. A paid plan is needed to keep scanning in the way the trial allowed. The FAQ says that with any paying account you can create unlimited targets, but you can only scan the number you pay for each month.

That rule keeps your historical risk, scan, and target data in one place without ongoing cost. Extra targets cost more, and a Flex plan starts at $3,500 per year for high-volume scanning.

## Gotchas

- The Professional plan shows a Request a trial button, not an instant 14 day trial.
- Prices on the pricing page differ between monthly and yearly billing, and annual billing saves up to 34 percent.
- Scan only systems you own or have permission to test. Authenticated scans and internal network scanning are Premium features.

## Questions people ask

### Is HostedScan free?

Not permanently. HostedScan offers a 14 day free trial of its paid plans, with no credit card required according to its home page. The cheapest paid plan is Basic at $39 per month.

### What does HostedScan include in each plan?

Every plan includes 5 targets with unlimited scans of them, and the OWASP ZAP, OpenVAS, Nuclei, SSLyze, and Nmap scanners. Higher plans add authenticated scans, a developer API, and SSO.

### What counts toward the HostedScan target limit?

The FAQ says you can create unlimited targets on a paying account, but you can only scan the number of targets that you pay for each month.
