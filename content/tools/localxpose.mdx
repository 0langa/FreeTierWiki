LocalXpose is a tunneling tool: you run its client on your machine, and it gives a local service a public URL through LocalXpose's reverse proxy, with automatic SSL. Developers, testers, and operators use it to share local work, test webhooks, and reach devices remotely.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and is described as ideal for testing and personal use. It allows 2 active HTTP or HTTPS tunnels, with SSL/TLS encryption and unique subdomains, and the client runs on your machine using any build from the download page.

An active tunnel is any tunnel currently running on your account, so two connected at once count as two. The page lists features that every plan includes: basic and key authentication, rate limiting, IP whitelisting, editing request and response headers, and a built-in file server. You can run the client on as many machines as you like within your plan limits.

## When you hit a limit

The Starter plan lists two drawbacks: time limits and an interstitial warning page. The pricing page does not say how long a free tunnel lasts or what the warning page shows. A third tunnel cannot be active at the same time as two others on Starter.

Pro is $8 a month billed annually at $96 a year. It includes 10 tunnels, HTTP, HTTPS, TCP, TLS, and UDP tunneling, port forwarding, custom subdomains and domains, wildcard tunnels, 24/7 availability, and unlimited bandwidth subject to an acceptable use policy.

## Gotchas

- Visitors to a free tunnel see an interstitial warning page first, which may get in the way of webhooks or automated clients that expect your app's response.
- Free tunnels have time limits, and the pricing page does not give the length.
- Only HTTP and HTTPS are free; TCP, TLS, and UDP tunnels need Pro.

## Questions people ask

### Is LocalXpose free?

Yes. The Starter plan costs $0 with no credit card and allows 2 active HTTP or HTTPS tunnels.

### Does the free plan show a warning page?

Yes. The pricing page lists an interstitial warning page as a limitation of the Starter plan.

### Can I use TCP or UDP tunnels for free?

No. TCP, TLS, and UDP tunneling are listed under Pro.
