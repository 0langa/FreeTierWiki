You design forms in a drag-and-drop editor, or describe a form to the AI builder. You then share it by link or embed it, and read answers in the dashboard.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0, lasts forever, and needs no credit card. It has no limit on the number of forms, monthly responses, or fields per form.

File uploads can be up to 100 MB. The plan includes $0.25 a month of AI credits, which gives you 2 AI form generations a day. Spam protection on the free plan is basic.

The free plan also lists card-style forms, quiz auto-scoring, drop-off analysis, custom email notifications, and payment collection. It allows 1 webhook and 1 Google Sheets connection. Conditional logic, redirect on completion, and email verification start on the Personal plan, and appointment scheduling is not on the free or Personal columns.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page shows no cap on forms or responses, so there is nothing to hit there. The AI credits are the limit you can meet. The page does not say what happens when the daily AI form generations or the monthly AI credits run out.

Paid plans start with Personal at $13 a month, or $12 with annual billing. Business costs $49 a month, or $45 with annual billing. The free plan has no card on file, so no bill can arrive from it.

## Gotchas

- The free plan keeps Formester branding on your forms. Removing it needs a paid plan.
- Conditional logic, custom domains, custom URL slugs, and extra team seats are not part of the free plan.
- AI credits on the free plan are small, at $0.25 a month, so heavy AI use needs a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Formester free?

Yes. The free plan is free forever and includes unlimited forms and unlimited responses. The home page says no credit card is required.

### Does Formester limit responses on the free plan?

No. The pricing table lists unlimited monthly responses and unlimited fields per form on the free plan.

### Can I remove Formester branding for free?

No. The pricing page lists branding removal as a paid feature.
