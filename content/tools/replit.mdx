Replit is a browser-based workspace for writing, running, and publishing apps. Its main feature today is Replit Agent, which builds an app from a description, and the workspace also lets you edit code yourself. It is aimed at learners, makers, and developers who want a prototype without local setup.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists only the paid Core and Pro plans; the free tier is the Starter plan, described in Replit's billing docs. Starter users get daily credits for Agent usage, up to a monthly cap, and the credits reset daily. They also get monthly credits for production databases, object storage, and autoscale or static publishing.

Starter includes Lite build, a fast and focused build mode for quick edits with Agent, and you can create designs on the Design Canvas. You get one free published app, from either Lite build or Design Canvas. The docs do not state the credit amounts as numbers.

## When you hit a limit

The docs say the daily Agent credits reset each day and the cloud credits reset each month, and they do not describe a charge on Starter when credits run out. More credits come with Core, which is $20 a month, or $18 a month billed annually, and includes $20 towards more powerful models.

The free published app goes down automatically after 30 days and shows a Made with Replit badge with your referral link. Additional published apps, Full build, Plan Mode, third-party connectors such as Google and Stripe, and Replit AI Integrations all require Core or Pro.

## Gotchas

- The free published link stops working after 30 days, so it suits demos, not a site people rely on.
- Starter only has Lite build; the more autonomous Full build needs a paid plan.
- External connectors require Core, so a free app cannot call services like Stripe through Replit's integrations.

## Questions people ask

### Is Replit free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes daily Agent credits, monthly cloud credits, and one free published app.

### How long does a free published Replit app stay online?

The docs say the free published link goes down automatically after 30 days.

### Can I remove the Made with Replit badge for free?

No. Removing the badge from published apps requires Core.
