OutSystems is an enterprise low-code platform for building apps and AI agents, with AI app generation, full-stack development, and built-in DevSecOps. Companies buy it on custom quotes. For individuals, OutSystems offers Personal Edition, which is free.

## What the free plan gives you

OutSystems Personal Edition is for exploring and learning the platform on your own, at no cost. On the pricing page's comparison, it includes a development runtime, deployment on OutSystems Cloud, and up to 100 internal users for the apps you build.

Personal Edition has no uptime guarantee and no compliance options. The paid Agentic Systems Platform adds non-production and production runtimes, a 99.5% uptime guarantee with an upgrade to 99.95%, and starts with 100 internal users. Paid pricing depends on Application Objects, which are the screens, database tables, and API methods across your apps; OutSystems puts a typical medium-sized app at about 150 AOs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Personal Edition app passes 100 internal users, or list any other usage limits for it. Personal Edition has no billing attached, so there is no overage charge. To go beyond it you contact OutSystems for an estimate on the Agentic Systems Platform, since paid pricing is quoted rather than listed.

## Gotchas

- Personal Edition has only a development runtime, so there is no separate production environment.
- The 100-user limit is for internal users; external end users are counted separately on paid plans.
- Paid pricing is quote-based, so the cost of moving off Personal Edition is not on the page.

## Questions people ask

### Is OutSystems free?

OutSystems Personal Edition is free for exploring and learning the platform on your own. Business use runs on paid, quoted subscriptions.

### How many users can use apps on OutSystems Personal Edition?

Up to 100 internal users, according to the pricing page.

### Does OutSystems Personal Edition have a production environment?

No. The pricing page lists a development runtime only for Personal Edition.
