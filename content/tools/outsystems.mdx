OutSystems is an enterprise low-code platform for building apps and AI agents, with AI app generation, full-stack development, and built-in DevSecOps. Companies buy it on custom quotes. For individuals, OutSystems offers Personal Edition, which is free.

## What the free plan gives you

OutSystems Personal Edition is free and requires no credit card. The OutSystems Developer Cloud environment includes one development stage, two API clients, 1 GB of database storage, up to eight active components, and up to 100 internal users. Components include apps, workflows, agents, and timers. You can invite teammates in the OutSystems Developer Cloud Portal.

Personal Edition includes Mentor, Agent Workbench, and Private Gateway. Mentor provides 11.5 million daily tokens shared by Mentor, Mentor Studio, and OutSystems MCP, with up to 20 app generations a day and two a minute. The resource table also lists 10 minutes a day of custom code execution, 30 minutes a day of timers, 10 minutes a day of external libraries, and 50 application logs per minute.

## When you hit a limit

New Personal Editions are active for 15 days. Using the environment in the last five days extends it by seven days. After 72 hours idle it hibernates and keeps your work. A developer action such as signing in to Studio, or waking the organization in the portal, restores it.

OutSystems emails you three days before deletion if inactivity continues through the end of the active period. Export apps and agents before expiry. You can import them into a new environment, but configuration and application data do not transfer. To add capacity or deploy to production, contact OutSystems for a paid quote based on application objects, end users, and add-ons.

## Gotchas

- Personal Edition provides a development stage with best-effort support and no uptime guarantee.
- Use the subscription console to monitor resource consumption. OutSystems does not document the exact response when you reach each resource allowance.
- A connected AI coding tool may charge separately for its own model usage, even when you have free Mentor tokens.

## Questions people ask

### Is OutSystems Personal Edition free?

Yes. It requires no credit card and stays free while active. Use it to learn OutSystems or prototype apps, workflows, and agents.

### How many users can use apps on OutSystems Personal Edition?

Up to 100 internal users. You can invite teammates from the OutSystems Developer Cloud Portal.

### What happens if I stop using OutSystems Personal Edition?

It hibernates after 72 hours of inactivity. If you let the active period expire, OutSystems deletes the environment after emailing you three days beforehand.
