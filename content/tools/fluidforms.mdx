You build forms in a web editor and mix standard fields with AI sections. Answers go to your dashboard, email, Slack, or other tools through webhooks and no-code connectors.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and the pricing page calls it free forever. It includes unlimited forms and 100 responses a month. Each response keeps for 30 days.

You also get a starter pack of 10 AI actions. The pack is one-time and does not renew. An AI interview action covers up to 10 messages, and extra messages use more actions. An AI autofill section reads uploaded files and fills fields for you.

Each response can hold 1 file of up to 5 MB. The free plan includes the AI interview, autofill, and document intelligence features. FluidForms branding may appear prominently on your forms.

## When you hit a limit

When AI actions run out, AI interview sections turn into standard form sections. AI autofill sections get skipped. The site says it keeps collecting responses without interruption, and it warns you by email and in the app as you near the limit.

The pricing page does not say what happens after 100 responses in a month. It also names no charge for the free plan. The paid Pro plan costs $49 a month, and the site says you can change or cancel plans at any time.

## Gotchas

- The 10 AI actions are a lifetime pack. They do not come back each month.
- Responses are kept for only 30 days on the free plan, so export what you need.
- The site says unlimited features are subject to fair use, and it may contact you if your usage stays far above the usual pattern for your plan.

AI interview sections turn into standard form sections, and AI autofill sections are skipped. The site says responses keep coming in.
