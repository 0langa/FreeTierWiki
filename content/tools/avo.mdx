Avo is a tool for planning and governing analytics events. You design events and properties in a shared tracking plan, generate code to send them, and watch live data for mistakes. It suits developers and product teams who send events to tools such as Mixpanel, Amplitude, or Segment and want them to stay consistent.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is aimed at startups and small teams who want to move from spreadsheets to a single source of truth. It includes 2 editors, unlimited comment seats, 5 stakeholder domains, and 100k events observed in Inspector. Codegen is included for editors.

Schema sync covers 1 downstream destination, such as Mixpanel Lexicon, Amplitude Govern, Segment Protocols, or your own webhook. You can build the tracking plan from your live tracking, keep a shared event and property library with naming conventions, and get a standard audit that flags inconsistent casing, duplicate events and properties, and missing descriptions.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when Inspector observes more than 100k events, or whether that count resets. Nothing on the page describes an automatic charge on the Free plan.

The Team plan is the next step. It is $250 a month billed annually, or $300 a month billed monthly, with 5 editors included; extra editors cost $50 a month each on annual billing or $60 on monthly billing. Team adds approval workflows and branch audits, unlimited stakeholder teams, and schema sync to up to 5 registries.

## Gotchas

- Only editors get Codegen, so count who writes tracking code when you plan seats.
- Schema sync on Free reaches 1 destination, which matters if you send events to several tools.
- Branch approvals, which stop bad tracking from being merged, are not part of Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Avo free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 2 editors and 100k events observed in Inspector.

### Which tools can Avo sync a tracking plan to?

Mixpanel Lexicon, Amplitude Govern, Segment Protocols, or your own webhook. The Free plan syncs to 1 of them.

### Who can comment on the tracking plan for free?

Anyone with a comment seat; comment seats are unlimited on the Free plan.
