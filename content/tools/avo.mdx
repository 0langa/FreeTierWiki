Avo is a tool for planning and governing analytics events. You design events and properties in a shared tracking plan, generate code to send them, and watch live data for mistakes. It suits developers and product teams who send events to tools such as Mixpanel, Amplitude, or Segment and want them to stay consistent.

## What the free plan gives you

On November 15, Avo's [pricing page](https://www.avo.app/pricing) offered a demo and sandbox without a plan comparison. Its [Open Source page](https://www.avo.app/open-source) still mentions Free, and an [older plan article](https://www.avo.app/blog/team-vs-enterprise-whats-the-right-avo-plan-for-your-organization) gives different Inspector and schema-sync allowances. Free availability for new accounts and current quotas are unconfirmed.

The last-verified $0 plan included 2 editors, unlimited comment seats, 5 stakeholder domains and 100k events observed in Inspector, with Codegen for editors. That comparison listed 1 downstream schema-sync destination, such as Mixpanel Lexicon, Amplitude Govern, Segment Protocols, or your own webhook.

## When you hit a limit

Current signup, card and Inspector overage rules are unconfirmed. Avo's [terms](https://www.avo.app/l/terms) describe how Free works when offered, without publishing current quotas or overage rates.

The last-verified Team rates were $250 a month billed annually or $300 monthly, with 5 editors. Extra editors were $50 each per month on annual billing or $60 on monthly billing. The plan also listed approval workflows, branch audits, unlimited stakeholder teams and sync to 5 registries.

## Gotchas

- The last-verified Free plan included Codegen for editors.
- Free schema sync reached 1 destination in the last-verified plan.
- The last-verified comparison listed branch approvals on Team.
