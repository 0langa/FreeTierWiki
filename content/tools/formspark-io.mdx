You bring your own HTML form and point it at a Formspark action URL. The dashboard shows submissions, and the docs also describe a REST API and guides for many frameworks.

## What the free plan gives you

Every account starts with one free workspace. It holds 250 submissions, 10 forms, and 5 team members, and the plan needs no credit card. The 250 submissions are a one-time allowance, not a monthly one.

Spam filtering is on for every submission. Webhooks, Slack, Zapier, and exports are included in the free plan. The submission archive is kept forever. Support on the free plan comes from the community.

Each accepted submission spends one from the workspace total. Submissions rejected as spam do not count. The docs say the average user gets 5 submissions a day, so 250 can last a while on a small site.

## When you hit a limit

Formspark does not cut you off at exactly zero. The docs describe a small buffer past the end of your submissions. After that, recent submissions are held back and not discarded. A new bundle releases the held submissions into your inbox.

You then buy a bundle of 50,000 submissions as a one-time payment. It costs $50, and the pricing page shows $25 with a 50% discount. There are no recurring charges. Bundles do not expire, and you buy another when one runs out.

## Gotchas

- The 250 submissions are a one-time allowance for the workspace, so they do not renew each month.
- Extra workspaces you create start with 0 submissions, and upgrades apply to one workspace only.
- Formspark is not a form builder. Fields without a name attribute never reach the dashboard, and input names that start with an underscore are hidden.

## Questions people ask

### Is Formspark free?

Yes, to start. Every account gets one workspace with 250 free submissions, 10 forms, and 5 team members. The allowance is one-time.

### What happens when I run out of Formspark submissions?

Formspark gives a small buffer, then holds back new submissions instead of discarding them. A new bundle of 50,000 releases them to your inbox.

### Does Formspark need a credit card?

No. The site says no credit card is required, and upgrades are a one-time payment with no subscription.
