You run the Percy CLI or an SDK in your tests or CI, and it uploads screenshots as a build. You then review the differences in the Percy dashboard. It suits teams that want a shared visual review step.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and includes 5,000 screenshots per month, unlimited users, and unlimited projects. A screenshot is one page or component rendered in one browser at one width. Two pages in two browsers at three widths make twelve screenshots, although the Percy dashboard shows them as two snapshots.

The free plan includes all desktop browsers, but you cannot choose browsers for each project. Build history is short: builds on the free plan expire after 30 days, while paid plans keep 1 year. The free plan also lists support by email.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says your plan comes with included monthly screenshots, and you may keep using Percy after you reach the limit. You are then charged for the extra screenshots. The dedicated billing documentation scopes overage billing to paid plans. The reviewed sources do not establish the exact Free exhaustion response or Free signup card requirement. On the paid Desktop plan, each extra screenshot costs $0.036. You can turn on usage notifications to get an alert when you cross a threshold you choose.

## Gotchas

- Screenshots multiply fast. Every browser and width in your project counts as a separate screenshot.
- Build history on the free plan lasts 30 days, so older builds disappear.
- Percy is now sold under BrowserStack, and its pricing and docs live on the BrowserStack site.

## Questions people ask

### Is Percy free?

Yes. The free plan includes 5,000 screenshots per month, unlimited users, and unlimited projects.

### What happens when I go over the Percy free screenshot limit?

Paid plans allow billed overages, but the reviewed sources do not establish what happens at the Free quota. Set usage notifications and confirm the policy before depending on uninterrupted builds.

### How long does Percy keep builds on the free plan?

Builds on the free plan expire after 30 days. Paid plans keep 1 year of build history.
