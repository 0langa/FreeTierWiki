You call the API from your own code with an API key, or through the Node.js, Python, or PHP SDK. You can also use automation tools such as Make, Zapier, and n8n. A dashboard holds your templates, keys, and request logs.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan gives you 100 PDF documents per month and needs no credit card. You can send 20 requests per minute, with 1 request at a time. Each PDF can be up to 2MB, and each conversion counts as 1 document. You also get 1 template and 1 AI template generation. Only successful conversions count, so a failed request leaves your quota unchanged.

Paid plans remove the file size cap. On them, each 5MB portion counts as 1 document, so a 7MB PDF uses 2 documents. Basic costs $19 per month for 2 000 documents. Growth costs $79 per month and Enterprise costs $249 per month. Documents are processed on EU-based servers.

## When you hit a limit

By default, overage is disabled on every plan. Requests past 100% of your quota are blocked with a 403 error. Your quota resets each billing cycle, and unused documents do not roll over. You can check what is left in the Dashboard or with the usage endpoint. Overage pricing is opt-in and only for paid plans, at $0.01 per extra PDF on Basic. Because the free plan has no overage charge and no card, the free plan cannot run up a bill.

## Gotchas

- AI template generation needs a card for verification. PDFBolt says it does not charge you.
- The free plan caps each PDF at 2MB, and larger files are not allowed on it.
- Storage on PDFBolt's side is short: if you use their storage, documents are deleted after 24 hours.

## Questions people ask

### Is PDFBolt free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 100 PDF documents per month, with no credit card for PDF generation. It also includes 1 AI template generation.

### What happens when I go over the PDFBolt limit?

Overage is off by default, so requests past 100% of your quota get a 403 error. Paid plans can opt in to overage pricing.

### Does PDFBolt need a credit card?

Not for the free plan. Only the AI template generation asks for a card for verification, and PDFBolt says you will not be charged.
