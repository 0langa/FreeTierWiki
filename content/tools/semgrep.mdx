You sign in with GitHub or GitLab, then scan with the Semgrep command-line tool, in CI, or through Semgrep infrastructure. Results appear in the Semgrep platform and in pull requests. It suits small teams adding a first scanner.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Edition is free for up to 10 contributors and up to 10 private repositories. Public repositories are unlimited. It includes Semgrep Code (static analysis) at $0 per contributor and Supply Chain (dependency scanning) at $0 per contributor.

You get Pro rules with cross-file analysis, AI-powered detection, triage, and remediation, and 60 monthly AI credits. The pricing table lists support for 35+ languages. CI/CD runs through Semgrep infrastructure. Support is community-based, and single sign-on works only through GitHub or GitLab.

## When you hit a limit

The usage docs say the first Free contributor-limit overage automatically starts a Teams trial. Excess contributors otherwise stop scans until licenses are purchased or limits reset; the eleventh-private-repository response and Free card requirement remain unpublished. A contributor is anyone who made at least one commit to a scanned private repository in the past 90 days, so the count changes over time. The next plan, Teams, starts at $30 per contributor per month for Code or for Supply Chain and allows up to 500 private repositories. Secrets detection costs $15 per contributor per month on Teams.

## Gotchas

- Secrets detection is not part of the Free plan. The page lists it under Teams and Enterprise.
- Contributor limits apply across all organizations owned by a company, using the rolling last 90 days. At AI exhaustion, autotriage continues with a warning, while detection, autofixes, and Agentic Workflows stop.
- If Semgrep runs locally or fully in your CI, the pricing FAQ says your source code never leaves your machine or CI. Opting in to AI features or managed scans changes that.

## Questions people ask

### Is Semgrep free?

Yes. The Free Edition covers up to 10 contributors and up to 10 private repositories, with Code and Supply Chain scanning.

### How does Semgrep count contributors?

A contributor is someone who made at least one commit to a private repository scanned by Semgrep in the past 90 days.

### Does Semgrep send my source code to the cloud?

Not if Semgrep runs locally or fully in your CI. Only metadata about the runs is sent. AI features and managed scans do send code, as the pricing FAQ explains.
