Katalon is a test automation platform built around Katalon Studio, a desktop tool for web, API, and mobile tests. You can record and play back tests or write scripts, then group them into suites. The paid platform adds test management, cloud browsers, and runs in CI.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is a Katalon Studio download for authoring and running tests locally. It includes web, API, and mobile testing, basic record and playback for web and mobile, data-driven testing from basic sources, automated test executions, and a local execution environment. You can keep up to 40 static test suites.

Every new sign-up starts a 30-day trial with no credit card. The trial gives 5 seats of Katalon Studio with all Professional features, TestOps test management and AI test authoring, 5 Katalon Runtime Engine licenses for headless and CI/CD runs, 3 TestCloud cross browser sessions, and 5 admin seats.

## When you hit a limit

When the 30 days end, the pricing FAQ says your account continues on the Free plan unless you buy a paid plan. Your projects, tests, users, and run history are kept, and paid capabilities become limited to the Free plan's entitlements. Nothing is billed automatically.

On the Free plan itself, the limits are features rather than usage. Headless and CI/CD execution requires Katalon Runtime Engine add-ons, and parallel execution, desktop testing, and AI script generation are not included. The Professional plan is listed from $84 per seat per month, billed annually.

## Gotchas

- Free runs happen on your own machine, so a nightly CI run is not part of the Free plan.
- The trial's cloud browsers and Runtime Engine licenses disappear after 30 days, so tests built around them stop running in CI.
- Dynamic test suites that add tests by search query are paid; Free suites are static and capped at 40.

## Questions people ask

### Is Katalon Studio free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you author and run web, API, and mobile tests locally, with up to 40 static test suites.

### Does the Katalon trial need a credit card?

No. The pricing FAQ says no credit card is needed and every new sign-up starts a 30-day trial.

### What happens when the Katalon trial ends?

Your account continues on the Free plan. Projects, tests, users, and run history are kept, but paid features are limited to the Free plan's entitlements.
