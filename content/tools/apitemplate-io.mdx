You design a template in the web console, then call a REST endpoint with a template ID, an API key, and JSON data. You can also wire it to Zapier or Integromat. It suits developers and no-code users alike.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and gives you 50 images or PDFs per month and 3 templates. The pricing page says the free plan has everything you need to generate images or PDFs, and that you do not need a credit card to sign up. Paid plans start with PDF Basic at $19 per month when billed annually. It gives you 3000 PDFs and 20 templates. If you also need images, the Starter plan gives you 1500 images or PDFs and 15 templates. Downloads are unlimited once a file exists. Generation takes about 1.2 seconds to 15 seconds, depending on the content.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says you get emails when you reach 80% and 100% of your monthly credit. It also says APITemplate.io does not upgrade your plan for you. To get more, you log in, open Subscription, and upgrade yourself. The free plan needs no card to sign up. The page does not say what happens to API calls after 100%. Pay-as-you-go exists only on enterprise plans, and it is off by default.

## Gotchas

- Files made on a free account are purged after one year. Paid accounts keep their files for as long as the subscription lasts.
- The free plan stops at 3 templates, so a project with many layouts will need a paid plan.
- Prices on the page differ by billing period. PDF Basic is $19 per month billed annually and $24 per month billed monthly.

## Questions people ask

### Is APITemplate.io free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 50 images or PDFs per month and 3 templates. You do not need a credit card.

### What happens when I run out of credit on APITemplate.io?

You get emails at 80% and 100% of your monthly credit. The service does not upgrade your plan automatically, so you upgrade it yourself under Subscription.

### How long do APITemplate.io files stay online?

On a free account, images are purged after one year. On a paid account, files keep working for as long as you pay for a subscription.
