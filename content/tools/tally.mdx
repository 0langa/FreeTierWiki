You build forms in a document-style editor, share them by link or embed, and read submissions in a dashboard. The site also lists an API and an MCP server.

## What the free plan gives you

Tally says its free plan has unlimited forms and unlimited submissions, as long as you stay within the fair usage guidelines. The pricing page lists payments, signatures, file uploads, a custom Thank You page, and self email notifications as free features.

You also get redirect on completion, conditional logic and calculations, duplicate prevention, password protection, closing forms on a limit or date, and answer piping. Integrations include Google Sheets, Notion, Airtable, Zapier, Make, and webhooks. The forms support 45+ languages.

File uploads on the free plan are capped at 10 MB per file. Pro removes that limit.

## When you hit a limit

No form or submission cap exists on the free plan, so you will not hit a hard stop. The fair use policy is the limit to know. If your usage strains the infrastructure, Tally says it will contact you about a custom plan.

The policy gives examples of extensive usage. These are consistently collecting around 50,000 submissions a month, 100 GB of uploads a month or 500 GB in total, and sending 50,000 email notifications a month. A custom plan sits on top of your subscription and is billed each quarter. Pro costs €20 a month and Business costs €65 a month.

## Gotchas

- Free forms show Tally branding. Hiding it needs Pro.
- Custom domains, partial submissions, team workspaces, and email customization are Pro features.
- Fair use looks at months of high volume. If it continues for 3 to 4 months in a row, Tally may reach out about a custom plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tally really free?

Yes. Tally gives unlimited forms and submissions for free within its fair use guidelines. Advanced features such as conditional logic, signatures, and file uploads are free too.

### Do I need an account to use Tally?

No. The pricing page says you can create your first form right away without signing up. You can also sign up for an account.

### What counts as extensive usage on Tally?

The fair use policy gives examples such as 50,000 submissions a month, 100 GB of uploads a month, or 50,000 email notifications a month. Tally contacts you to set up a custom plan.
