Softr is an AI app builder for business operations. You build internal tools and client portals on top of Softr Databases or connected data, automate steps with Softr Workflows, and share the apps with signed-in users. It suits small teams that want a working portal without writing a front end.

## What the free plan gives you

Softr says its Free plan is free forever. It includes unlimited apps, databases, workflows, agents, and forms, the Softr API and MCP. Integration counts conflict: the Free card says +38 integrations while the comparison table says 42 apps; some API endpoints require higher plans. You get 5 AI credits per month.

Up to 3 builders can work in the workspace. Apps can have 5 app users, counted once across all your apps. Softr Databases hold 5K records in total, and Softr Workflows can run 500 actions per month; triggers and flow controls do not count. Builders can be added as app users without counting toward the limit. Basic costs $25 monthly or $19 monthly billed yearly, adding a custom domain and 50K records.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says workflows pause when the monthly execution limit is reached, until the limit renews or the workspace is upgraded. It does not say what happens when you reach 5 app users or 5K records. Included AI credits reset monthly without rollover, and the editor remains usable without them. Optional AI credit add-ons are sold even on Free; unused add-ons roll over for one month. The no-card statement covers the trial, not an explicit ongoing Free policy. New accounts also start with a free trial of the paid features, with no credit card required, which can be extended once by choosing a plan to trial again.

## Gotchas

- App users who sign up with free email domains such as Gmail count as client users, not team users, on paid plans.
- There is no custom domain on Free; it starts on Basic.
- Removing Softr branding starts on the Pro plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Softr free?

Yes. Softr's Free plan is free forever with unlimited apps, up to 3 builders, 5 app users, and 5K database records.

### How many users can log in to a Softr app for free?

5 app users on the Free plan, counted once across all your apps. Builders can be added as app users without counting toward the limit.

### Does Softr have a free trial?

Yes. Everyone gets a free trial of the paid features at sign-up, with no credit card required.
