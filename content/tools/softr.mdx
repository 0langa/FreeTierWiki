Softr is an AI app builder for business operations. You build internal tools and client portals on top of Softr Databases or connected data, automate steps with Softr Workflows, and share the apps with signed-in users. It suits small teams that want a working portal without writing a front end.

## What the free plan gives you

Softr says its Free plan is free forever. It includes unlimited apps, databases, workflows, agents, and forms, the Softr API and MCP, and 38 integrations. You get 5 AI credits per month.

Up to 3 builders can work in the workspace. Apps can have 5 app users, counted once across all your apps. Softr Databases hold 5K records in total, and Softr Workflows can run 500 actions per month; triggers and flow controls do not count. Builders can be added as app users without counting toward the limit. The Basic plan costs $19 per month and adds a custom domain and 50K records.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 5 app users, 5K records, or 500 workflow actions in a month. The Free plan has no listed overage charges, and Softr accepts credit card payment only for its paid plans, so there is nothing to bill on Free. New accounts also start with a free trial of the paid features, with no credit card required, which can be extended once by choosing a plan to trial again.

## Gotchas

- App users who sign up with free email domains such as Gmail count as client users, not team users, on paid plans.
- There is no custom domain on Free; it starts on Basic.
- Removing Softr branding starts on the Pro plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Softr free?

Yes. Softr's Free plan is free forever with unlimited apps, up to 3 builders, 5 app users, and 5K database records.

### How many users can log in to a Softr app for free?

5 app users on the Free plan, counted once across all your apps. Builders can be added as app users without counting toward the limit.

### Does Softr have a free trial?

Yes. Everyone gets a free trial of the paid features at sign-up, with no credit card required.
