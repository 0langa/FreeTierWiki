Visual Debug provides a simple way to collect visual bug reports and feedback directly on your website, making collaboration between developers, designers, and testers more efficient. The free "Freelancer" plan is suitable for solo developers, with 1 team member, unlimited reporters, and 5 GB storage, but integrations and more seats require a paid plan. It's best for web-based projects in early-stage or light production environments.
