You install a browser extension, add your site as a project, and then you or your visitors mark up the live page. There is no code to embed on your site. It suits freelancers, agencies, and indie makers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Freelancer plan is free. It includes 1 team member, unlimited reporters, and 5GB of storage. Reporters are the people who leave feedback, and they can report a bug without an account if they have the extension. The FAQ says you can manage multiple projects without limits.

Reporters can select an element, draw on the website, or send a text-only comment. Visual Debug blurs content outside the selection, and an eraser tool hides more. The FAQ says the file upload limit is 10MB. Paid plans add team members, storage, and all integrations.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 5GB or need more than 1 team member. Plan on upgrading. The Small team plan costs $29 per month for 5 team members and 25GB of storage. Regular team costs $79 per month, and Large team costs $129 per month.

You can start a free 14-day trial of a paid plan without a credit card. Adding a payment method is not required to start the trial. You can cancel any time, and refunds are available within 14 days of payment.

## Gotchas

- The FAQ says teams are free, but the pricing page lists paid team plans. Trust the pricing page.
- Verifying your domain is needed to close bug reports, respond to feedback, or hide a project from public feedback.
- The integrations page lists tools such as Jira, Asana, ClickUp, Slack, and GitHub. The pricing page lists all integrations on the paid plans only.

## Questions people ask

### Is Visual Debug free?

Yes. The Freelancer plan is free with 1 team member, unlimited reporters, and 5GB of storage.

### Do people need an account to report a bug with Visual Debug?

No. The FAQ says you do not need an account to report a bug. You only need the browser extension.

### Does Visual Debug have a free trial for paid plans?

Yes. You can start a free 14-day trial of a paid plan, and no credit card is required.
