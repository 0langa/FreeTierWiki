Retool is a low-code platform for building internal tools. You drag components onto a canvas, connect them to databases and APIs with queries, and add workflows and AI agents. It suits developers who need an admin panel or a back-office app without writing a whole front end.

## What the free plan gives you

Retool's Free plan is free for everyone and runs on Retool cloud for up to 5 users. It includes 250 pooled AI credits per month for app building and AI Actions; they renew monthly and do not roll over.

Workflows get 500 runs per month, with unlimited steps, schedule triggers, and webhook triggers. Retool Database stores up to 5 GB of data, with a database editor and connection strings for outside access. Agents get up to 20 free hours per month. You can connect to all databases and APIs. The Free plan has a production environment only.

## When you hit a limit

For agents, Retool says execution pauses when you use all your free agent hours, and you can upgrade to continue. For AI credits, more monthly capacity means a higher tier, since extra credit packs are for paid plans.

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 500 workflow runs or 5 GB on the Free plan. Paid plans list extra workflow runs at $75 per 5,000. The Team plan costs $10 per builder per month billed annually.

## Gotchas

- External users and embedded apps are not included on Free or Team; they start on Business.
- The Free plan has no staging environment, so changes go straight to production.
- AI credits are pooled across the whole account and do not roll over, and Free cannot buy extra packs.

## Questions people ask

### Is Retool free?

Yes. The Free plan is free for everyone with up to 5 users on Retool cloud, 500 workflow runs a month, and a Retool Database with up to 5 GB.

### Can external customers use a Retool app on the Free plan?

No. External users and embedded apps are not included on the Free plan.

### What happens when I run out of free agent hours?

Agent execution pauses. You can upgrade to a paid plan to continue.
