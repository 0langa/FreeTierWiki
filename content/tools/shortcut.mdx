Shortcut is a project management tool made for software teams. Work lives in stories and epics, you plan with Kanban views, and Docs sit next to the work. A developer team would pick it for an issue tracker that links to GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket without the weight of a large enterprise tool.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Plan costs $0 and is described as best for teams getting started. It lets you plan work with Kanban views, work together with Docs, and connect GitHub, Slack, and Figma. The comparison table lists 1 team for the Free Plan, and Shortcut's integration list across plans includes GitLab, Bitbucket, Zendesk, and Zapier.

The pricing FAQ sets the user limit: if you have 10 or fewer users when the trial ends, you can use Shortcut for free. Every new workspace starts with a free 14-day trial with full access and no credit card required. Free includes basic Epic and Iteration reports. Team costs $8.50 per user per month billed yearly, or $10 monthly, and adds the Metrics Dashboard.

## When you hit a limit

The user count is the rule that matters. If you have more than 10 users when the 14-day trial ends and want to keep using Shortcut, you must subscribe to a paid plan. The pricing page does not say what happens if a free workspace later grows past 10 users.

During the trial you get everything, including an Organization with unlimited Workspaces, unlimited Observer users, the API, and outgoing webhooks. The current rendered comparison explicitly includes API and webhook access on Free, along with GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket integrations. Observer seats and extra workspaces have separate paid entitlements.

## Gotchas

- Features you set up during the 14-day trial may not all be on the Free Plan afterward.
- Basic Epic and Iteration reports are Free. Velocity and the full Metrics Dashboard need Team; additional charts need Business.
- Shortcut does not offer pro-rated refunds for cancelled subscriptions.

## Questions people ask

### Is Shortcut free?

Yes. The Free Plan costs $0, and a workspace with 10 or fewer users can keep using it after the trial.

### Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. Shortcut offers a free 14-day trial with full access and says no credit card is required.

### Does Shortcut Free work with GitHub?

Yes. The Free Plan lists GitHub, Slack, and Figma integrations.
