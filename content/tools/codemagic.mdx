You add your repository in the Codemagic web app and describe the build in a `codemagic.yaml` file, or use the Flutter workflow editor. It suits a solo mobile developer who wants hosted Mac builds.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for individuals. It gives you a personal account with unlimited applications and 500 free minutes per month on macOS M2 machines. The minutes reset on the 1st of each month. You get 1 parallel build and a build duration limit of 120 minutes. You cannot invite collaborators to an individual plan.

Free minutes are not available if you use a team. Builds on Linux and Windows have no free minutes. They cost $0.045 per minute. The pricing page says teachers, students, and non-profits can ask for free accounts. The plan is meant for hobby projects, indie apps, and proofs of concept.

## When you hit a limit

If you do not enable billing, the free minutes are your ceiling. To use more, you enable billing on your account and enter a credit card. Codemagic then charges for usage monthly, on the first day of the next month. Even then, your first 500 macOS M2 minutes stay free.

After the 500, macOS M2 minutes cost $0.095 per minute and macOS M4 minutes cost $0.114 per minute. A build that times out or fails from a Codemagic service error is not counted toward billing. Builds that fail for any other reason count. The pricing page does not say exactly what happens in the free plan if billing is off and the 500 minutes are gone.

## Gotchas

- Free minutes work only on a personal account. Put the app in a team and the free allowance is gone.
- Linux and Windows machines have no free minutes, so a Flutter Android build on Linux is paid once billing is on.
- Build time is counted separately for your personal account and for each team.

## Questions people ask

### Is Codemagic free?

Partly. The individual plan gives you 500 free macOS M2 minutes per month, reset on the 1st, with 1 parallel build. Linux and Windows minutes are paid.

### Does Codemagic need a credit card?

Not for the free minutes. You need a card only when you enable billing to pay for extra minutes.

### How much does Codemagic cost after the free minutes?

macOS M2 minutes cost $0.095 per minute, and Linux X2 and Windows minutes cost $0.045 per minute. Billing is monthly, on the first day of the next month.
