You open the EveryStep Web Recorder, click through your site in a browser, and it records each step as a script you can replay. It suits testers and site owners who want to automate checks of a web flow without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is the EveryStep Web Recorder. The site says EveryStep Browser Automation is free, and that it records every step of a web transaction and replays the script in a real browser. You can use the scripts on a local machine to automate QA testing of web applications.

The scripts also work for monitoring and load testing in Dotcom-Monitor, which is the paid side. Premium plans start at $38.99 per month and let you schedule monitoring from dozens of global testing locations. The site also links a free 30 day trial for those premium features. Enterprise plans are priced by quote.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not list any limit on the free recorder, such as a script count or a run cap. It also does not describe a bill for the free part.

The wall you meet is the feature gap. Scheduled monitoring from several locations, alerting, and load testing need a Dotcom-Monitor account. The getting started page says an account is necessary to reach the premium features.

## Gotchas

- Free means the recorder and local replay. Hosted monitoring from multiple locations costs money.
- The site says scripts can run from any Windows-based device, so check your platform first.
- The site names no supported browsers, only compatibility with 40+ desktop and mobile devices.

## Questions people ask

### Is EveryStep free?

Yes, the EveryStep Web Recorder is free. It records browser scripts and replays them locally. Hosted monitoring in Dotcom-Monitor is paid.

### How much does EveryStep monitoring cost?

Premium plans start at $38.99 per month. Enterprise plans need a quote from the company.

### Does EveryStep need an account?

An account with Dotcom-Monitor is necessary for the premium features. The page does not say whether the free recorder needs one.
