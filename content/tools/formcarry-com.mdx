You keep your own form and point its action at a Formcarry endpoint. The docs also cover framework guides, site builders, a REST API, and an MCP server.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called Baby and costs $0 a month. It includes 1 form, 50 submissions a month, and 1 team member.

Every submission triggers an instant email notification, and the site says it filters spam. Formcarry stores submissions forever, according to the home page. The docs say any form that can send an HTTP POST works, including plain HTML, React, Webflow, Framer, and WordPress. You can send an Accept header for JSON to get a JSON reply from JavaScript.

File uploads are a paid feature. The home page also lists over 3000 integrations, but the pricing page shows integration call counts only on paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

Formcarry does not drop extra submissions. The site says it saves them in a secure place until the first day of next month. You cannot see them unless you upgrade your plan, and upgrading lets you recover them.

So a busy month hides your data instead of billing you. The free plan has no card on file. The paid plans start at $6 a month for Starter, or $5 with yearly billing, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard at any time.

## Gotchas

- Only 1 form is allowed on the free plan. Paid plans list unlimited forms.
- Fields without a name attribute are never sent by the browser, so they never reach Formcarry.
- Entries past 50 in a month stay hidden until you upgrade. The site says Formcarry saves them only until the first day of next month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Formcarry free?

Yes. The free Baby plan costs $0 and includes 1 form and 50 submissions a month. Paid plans start at $6 a month.

### What happens when I exceed the Formcarry submission limit?

Formcarry saves the extra submissions until the first day of next month. You can see them only after you upgrade, and then you recover them.

### Does Formcarry support file uploads on the free plan?

No. The site says file uploads are available on paid plans.
