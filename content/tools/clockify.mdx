Clockify is a time tracker: you start a timer or enter time by hand, organize it by project, client, task, and tag, and review it in reports. A developer would pick it to track hours on freelance or client work, and teams can push and pull data through the Clockify API.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan lets you track time without limits. Clockify says you can track as much time as you want and invite up to 4 people for free. Projects are unlimited, and you can organize time by project, client, task, and tag.

Reports show who worked on what and how much you earn, but the data range on the Free plan is 1 month. Clockify offers support to everyone regardless of plan. You can try all PRO features for free for 7 days with no credit card required; the trial starts automatically when you sign up. Paid plans are billed per seat, starting with Basic.

## When you hit a limit

The two limits are people and report history. Past 4 invited people, you need a paid plan, where each active and invited user requires a paid seat. The pricing page does not say what happens if you try to invite a fifth person on Free.

For history, reports on the Free plan cover a 1-month data range. If you cancel a paid plan, Clockify says you are downgraded to the free version with no further charges, and you won't lose any data; canceling only affects access to the extra features.

## Gotchas

- The 1-month report range makes Free awkward for quarterly or yearly reviews.
- Adding time for other people is a paid feature, so a manager cannot fill in a teammate's hours on Free.
- Paid seats are counted per active and invited user, so deactivate people you no longer need.

## Questions people ask

### Is Clockify free?

Yes. The Free plan offers unlimited time tracking and unlimited projects, and you can invite up to 4 people for free.

### Can I try Clockify's paid features?

Yes. All PRO features can be tried free for 7 days, with no credit card required.

### Do I lose data if I cancel a paid plan?

No. Clockify says you are downgraded to the free version and won't lose any data.
