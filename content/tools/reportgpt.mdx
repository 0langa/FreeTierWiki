You work in the browser. You describe your task, edit the suggested outline, fill in a short form for each chapter, and export the result. It fits people who write one long document at a time. This page covers the free demo.


## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is one demo document, once, for $0. The pricing card says it lets you generate a document structure and run an AI plagiarism check. The page does not make clear how much of a full document the demo covers, so test it with a small task.

Paid reports are one-time purchases, not a subscription. One report costs $5. Three reports cost $10, shown as a discount from $15. Ten reports cost $25, shown as a discount from $50. Every paid pack lists access to GPT-5, unlimited document structures, unlimited chapters, and unlimited plagiarism checks.

You can export to Word, LaTeX, or Markdown.

## When you hit a limit

The free demo is a single use. After that, you buy a report pack. Nothing renews by itself and nothing bills you on its own, since the plans are one-time.

The FAQ says you can also use your own OpenAI API key on the platform at no extra cost from ReportGPT. A fair use policy applies, and the site points to its Terms of Service for details. The pages do not mention a credit card for the demo.

## Gotchas

- The home page also talks about a free trial with full access and a subscription tier, while the pricing card shows only one free demo document and one-time packs. Treat the pricing card as the offer.
- The packs of 3 and 10 show two prices each, a lower one and a higher one. The lower price may be a limited discount, so check it before you buy.
- The pages describe writing documents from your prompts and forms. They do not mention building reports from your data files.

Only for one demo document. After that, a report costs $5, or you can buy 3 for $10 or 10 for $25.

### Does ReportGPT have a subscription?

No. The site says no subscription is required, and every pack is a one-time payment.

### Can I use my own OpenAI key with ReportGPT?

Yes. The FAQ says you can use your own OpenAI API key on the platform without any additional cost from ReportGPT.
