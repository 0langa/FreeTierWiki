You use Codeberg through Git and a web interface based on Forgejo. It also runs Pages, a Weblate translation service, and Woodpecker CI. It suits maintainers of free and open-source projects who want a non-profit home for their code.

## What the free plan gives you

Codeberg charges nothing. The service is for free and open-source projects, and the site runs on donations and membership fees. You get Git hosting, issues, pull requests, a wiki, Pages, and organizations. The FAQ says there is no quota for valid use cases.

You may keep up to 100 MB of private content if you contribute to free software. Use it for notes or a side project. Codeberg asks you to request resources first if you plan to use more than 750 MiB of Git storage, more than 1.5 GiB of packages, LFS, and attachments, or large CI resources. There is a limit of 100 repositories per user or organization. You can ask for a higher limit.

## When you hit a limit

No bill exists, because Codeberg does not charge for hosting. Limits work through review and requests. Codeberg reviews larger projects and can deny service if your resource use looks unreasonable or hurts other users.

If you need more private storage, a higher repository limit, or CI access, open a request in the public Codeberg e.V. requests repository. If you use more than 100 MB of private storage for a valid case, send a request too. If you do not contribute to any free software, the FAQ says Codeberg is not the right place for you.

## Gotchas

- Codeberg does not offer private hosting services. The FAQ points commercial private projects to self-hosting Forgejo.
- The CI needs a manual access request, and a volunteer reviews it. CI access is provided as-is and can break at any time.
- The Woodpecker CI instance builds for linux/amd64 only.

## Questions people ask

### Is Codeberg free?

Yes. Codeberg charges nothing for free and open-source projects. It runs on donations and has no quota for valid use cases.

### Can I have private repositories on Codeberg?

A little. If you contribute to free software, Codeberg allows up to 100 MB of private content. It does not offer private hosting for commercial projects.

### Does Codeberg have CI?

Yes, through Woodpecker CI at ci.codeberg.org. You must request access first, and a volunteer reviews your project.
