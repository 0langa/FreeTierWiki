You describe your jobs in a `.circleci/config.yml` file in your repository, and CircleCI runs them on its own machines. You manage projects and read logs in the web app. It suits developers who want hosted CI without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and includes 30,000 credits each month. Credits pay for compute by the second. At 10 credits per minute on Linux Medium, 30,000 credits equal 3,000 minutes. Larger machines cost more per minute.

The plan allows 5 active users and 30 concurrent job runs on Docker, Linux VM, Arm, and Windows. macOS allows 1. Self-hosted runners can run 5 concurrent tasks. You also get 1 GB of network transfer and 2 GB-months of storage; use beyond that draws from your credits. Flaky test detection covers up to 5 tests. Open source organizations on the Free plan can use up to 400,000 credits per month for Linux open source builds if the repository is public.

## When you hit a limit

When your credits reach 0, personal builds are blocked until the credits refill at the start of the next month. Jobs fail to run in the meantime. Open source builds are not subject to this block and keep running even when the balance is empty.

The Performance plan starts at $15 per month and adds credits at $15 for every 25,000 credits. If you queue more jobs than your concurrency allows, the extra jobs wait until a slot opens.

## Gotchas

- Free credits expire after a month and do not roll over.
- The Free plan includes only 5 active users. On the Performance plan, each extra user costs 25,000 credits per month.
- A minute on a bigger machine uses more credits than a minute on a small one, so the 3,000 minutes is a Linux Medium estimate.

## Questions people ask

### Is CircleCI free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and gives you 30,000 credits per month, 5 active users, and 30 concurrent jobs. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I run out of CircleCI credits?

On the Free plan, your personal builds are blocked until the credits refill at the start of the next month. Open source builds keep running.

### Is CircleCI free for open source?

Yes, within limits. Organizations on the Free plan with public repositories can use up to 400,000 credits per month for Linux open source builds.
