You build forms in a drag-and-drop editor on a dashboard. You then share each form by link, embed, popup, or QR code.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. The pricing page says you will never hit a limit on forms, questions, or responses, within fair use guidelines.

All input types are included, such as file uploads, dates, ratings, and picture choice. File uploads can be up to 10 MB per file. You also get full design options, embeds, popups and share links, hidden fields, answer piping, conditional logic, calculators, webhooks and integrations, and WhatsApp sharing.

Email notifications fire for every submission from a default template. Free workspaces have one user. Free forms show a small Powered by Formware badge, which the site calls the only catch.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page names no usage cap on the free plan, so there is no hard stop or overage bill to expect. The paid plan exists for other reasons than higher limits.

Pro costs $29.99 a month, or $299 a year. It removes the branding and the upload size limit, and adds custom domains, partial submissions, respondent emails, redirects, and unlimited team members. The pricing page does not say what happens if usage goes beyond fair use.

## Gotchas

- The free plan workspace allows a single user. Team members and roles need Pro.
- Partial submissions, which save answers from people who drop off, are a Pro feature. They cannot trigger email notifications or integrations even there.
- You cannot customize the respondent email or redirect people to your own page after submission on the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Formware really free?

Yes. The free plan allows unlimited forms, questions, and responses within fair use. Free forms show a small Powered by Formware badge.

### Does Formware support file uploads?

Yes. The free plan allows uploads up to 10 MB per file, and Pro removes the size limit.

### Can I use my own domain with Formware?

Yes, but only on Pro. The pricing page lists custom domains with SSL as a Pro feature.
