You use the PagerTree web app to connect your monitoring tools, set a schedule, and invite your team. Alerts then page the person on call, with the mobile app as one way to receive them. It suits small teams that want a lighter option than a large incident platform.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan costs $0 per user per month and is built for up to 5 users. It includes up to 2 teams, unlimited integrations, and 3 months of data retention. The comparison table also lists an iPhone and Android app for Basic.

The paid plans start at $15 per user per month for up to 25 users, according to the pricing page. SSO starts on Elite. Each team has an escalation policy with configurable timeouts: unacknowledged or rejected alerts move to the next layer. Basic includes push, email and Slack; SMS, voice and WhatsApp start on paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens if you add a sixth user to the Basic plan. It lists "Up to 5 Users" as the plan size. Notifications have a separate budget. The notification limits page says you can use up to 50% of your subscription price in notifications, and that you stop receiving SMS, voice, and WhatsApp alerts at the limit. Push, email, and Slack notifications are still attempted. This budget is not a Basic phone-alert allowance: the plan card places those channels on paid plans.

## Gotchas

- The 14-day trial on the pricing page needs no credit card, but the pages do not describe how trial features differ from Basic.
- Basic includes push, email and Slack. Upgrade for SMS, voice or WhatsApp, then review the separate paid notification budget.
- Auto-recharge for notification credit is opt-in and charges the card on file. The pricing page also says PagerTree may charge overage fees for abnormal or unreasonable SMS and voice usage.

## Questions people ask

### Is PagerTree free?

Yes, there is a Basic plan at $0 per user per month for up to 5 users, with 2 teams and unlimited integrations.

### Does PagerTree need a credit card?

The pricing page says the 14-day free trial needs no credit card. Basic is listed at $0.

### What happens when I go over the PagerTree free limits?

The pricing page does not say. The notification limits page says SMS, voice, and WhatsApp alerts stop at your notification limit, while push, email, and Slack are still tried.
