You call Antideo over HTTP or HTTPS with an API key, and you manage your plan in the account section. There is also a WordPress plugin for email validation. It suits small sites that want a basic spam check.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and lasts as long as you want. The pricing page says the only restriction is the hourly rate limit of 10 requests per hour. You can use the email, IP, and phone endpoints, over HTTP or HTTPS, and you get email support. No credit card is required.

The email check looks for disposable, free-provider, generic, and spam addresses. The IP check covers reputation, proxies, and location. The phone check returns the country, carrier, and time zone. Paid plans start at $5 per month for 50 requests per hour.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan has strict limits. The pricing page does not say what the API returns once you pass 10 requests in an hour. It describes the soft limit on paid plans: an extra 10 percent buffer before a 429 "Too many requests" response. That implies free accounts have no buffer.

You cannot be billed on the free plan, since no payment is attached. To get more requests, subscribe to a paid plan, which goes up to $80 per month for 2000 requests per hour.

## Gotchas

- The email check detects disposable, free, and spam addresses, but the site says it does not verify that an address exists.
- The FAQ says a paid plan's API key is emailed to the address you enter, so use a mailbox you can open. It does not describe key delivery for free accounts.
- Paid plans come with a 30 day money back guarantee. The home page says the free account needs no credit card.

## Questions people ask

### Is Antideo free?

Yes. The free account costs $0 and has no time limit. It allows 10 requests per hour across the email, IP, and phone endpoints, and it needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Antideo rate limit?

The pricing page does not say what the free plan returns. It says paid plans get a 10 percent buffer before a 429 error, and the free plan has strict limits.

### Does Antideo check that an email address exists?

No. The email page says it detects disposable, free, and spam or scam addresses. It says verification that a mailbox exists is still planned.
