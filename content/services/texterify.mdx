You use Texterify in the browser, with a command line tool called `texterify download`, or on your own server. It suits solo developers and small teams that want a tool they can also host themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The free cloud plan costs 0 euros. It has a single user, 1 project, and 2 languages. Keys, translations, and words are unlimited. It includes the collaborative online editor and white-label support, and it exports in all formats. The sign-up pages say no credit card is required.

The paid cloud plans are priced per user per month. Basic costs 9 euros and adds multiple users and unlimited projects/languages. Team costs 19 euros with QA features. Basic’s card promises machine translation suggestions, but the comparison table starts them at Team: confirm before buying. Business costs 39 euros with automatic machine translation.

Texterify also offers on-premise plans. Those let you host it in your own infrastructure, where you set up and manage the instance yourself.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to go past one user, one project, or two languages. The free plan lists these as its limits, and the paid plans remove them.

The pages publish no Free usage-based rate or precise exhaustion policy, so the limits alone do not prove automatic blocking. Upgrading is done by credit card. If you have no card, the FAQ says to email support and Texterify will find a solution.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page says all plans come with unlimited languages and projects. The plan table shows 2 languages and 1 project on Free, so trust the table.
- Free excludes machine translation. Basic’s card includes suggestions, while the comparison row excludes them until Team.
- Paid plans bill per user per month, so the cost of a team grows with each person you add.

## Questions people ask

### Is Texterify free?

Yes. The free cloud plan has a single user, 1 project, and 2 languages. Keys and translations are unlimited, and no credit card is needed.

### Can I self-host Texterify?

Yes. The docs say you can host Texterify on your own infrastructure, and the pricing page sells on-premise plans for that.

### Does the Texterify free plan have machine translation?

No. Free excludes it; Basic’s card and comparison table disagree about suggestions. Business lists automatic machine translation.
