Fivetran is a managed data integration service. It runs connectors that copy data from SaaS apps, databases, and files into a warehouse, runs transformations on it, and, through Activations, sends warehouse data back out to business tools. It suits developers and data engineers who want pipelines without writing and maintaining them.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Plan gives you access to Standard Plan features, free up to 500,000 monthly active rows (MAR) for connections, 3,500 MAR for activations, and 5,000 monthly model runs (MMR) for transformations. The pricing page lists 700+ fully managed connectors, 200+ activation destinations, and integration for dbt Core. The Free Plan page lists unlimited users.

The Free Plan has no time limit and needs no credit card. Fivetran also offers a 14-day trial on its Business Critical Plan, and you can select the Free Plan during the trial or later, as long as your usage is below the limits. The $5 base charge on standard connections does not apply to accounts on the Free plan.

## When you hit a limit

Fivetran notifies you when connections exceed 350,000 MAR and again at 500,000 MAR, and you get a one-time grace period to lower usage or upgrade. If usage exceeds 500,000 MAR for the second time, Fivetran will freeze your connections, transformation jobs, and your account, and you must upgrade to a paid plan by the end of the month to continue. The same pattern applies to the activation and model run limits.

## Gotchas

- MAR counts the active rows Fivetran processes in a calendar month, so a busy table can pass 500,000 quickly.
- A second time over any limit freezes the whole account, not just one connection.
- You can only pick the Free Plan if your trial usage estimate is below the limits.

## Questions people ask

### Is Fivetran free?

Yes, for low volumes. The Free Plan covers 500,000 MAR for connections, 3,500 MAR for activations, and 5,000 model runs a month, with no time limit.

### Do I need a credit card?

No. Fivetran says you can sign up for a trial and enroll in the Free Plan without a credit card.

### What happens if I exceed the free MAR?

You get a one-time grace period. The second time you exceed 500,000 MAR, Fivetran freezes your connections and account until you upgrade.
