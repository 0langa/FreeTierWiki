Mux is a video API. You upload a file or a URL, Mux encodes it into a streaming format, stores it, and delivers it to viewers through a CDN. It also ships a video player, viewer analytics called Mux Data, and AI workflows called Mux Robots.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan needs no credit card to get started. It stores up to 10 videos, on-demand only, and each month includes 100K free delivery minutes and 100K free Mux Robots units for AI workflows such as captions or moderation.

Basic quality is the default for new accounts and has free input, so encoding a video at that level costs nothing. Mux Player and Mux Data analytics are free, on-demand captions are free, and smart signed URLs with domain and referrer restriction are free. Mux prices by minutes of video, not file size, and does not round up to the nearest minute.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free account reaches 10 videos or 100K delivery minutes. To store more videos or run live streams, you move to Pay as you go, which has no storage limit, includes a $20 monthly usage credit, and bills usage past it.

On paid plans, storage starts at $0.0024 per minute and delivery past the free 100k minutes starts at $0.0008 per minute. Automatic cold storage gives up to a 60% discount on assets that are not being watched.

## Gotchas

- The 10-video limit counts stored videos, so delete test uploads you no longer need.
- Live streaming requires plus or premium quality, and the Free plan is on-demand only.
- Higher resolutions cost more: 1080p is 1.25 times the base price, so check the resolution you deliver.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mux free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan stores up to 10 videos and includes 100K delivery minutes each month without a credit card.

### Can I live stream on the Mux Free plan?

No. The Free plan lists on-demand videos only, and live video appears on Pay as you go.

### Does Mux charge to encode video?

Not at basic quality, which is the default for new accounts and has free input. Plus and premium quality charge per minute of input.
