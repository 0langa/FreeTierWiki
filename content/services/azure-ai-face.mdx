You call it through the REST API, a client SDK, or the Foundry portal, after Microsoft approves your access. It suits teams with a clear photo or identity use case.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free tier gives you 30,000 transactions per month. The pricing page lists a rate of 20 transactions per minute for the free web endpoint. The listed features include Face Detection and Similar Face Search.

The paid tier charges per 1,000 transactions, and it also bills face storage per 1,000 faces per month. Face Liveness and Face Liveness plus Verification are billed per 1,000 transactions too. The price fields on the page are blank dashes, so use the Azure pricing calculator for real prices.

Photos must be JPEG, PNG, WEBP, or BMP, and no larger than 6 MB.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 30,000 free transactions. Paid usage is a separate tier, so you must choose it on purpose.

The harder limit is access. The docs say Face service access is limited by eligibility and usage criteria, and it is only available to Microsoft managed customers and partners. You apply through the Face Recognition intake form. The quickstart tells you to request the features you need before you follow its steps.

## Gotchas

- You need approval before you can use the Face service. A normal Azure sign-up is not enough.
- Face liveness SDKs are gated. You must request access, and your Azure subscription must be granted it before you can download the SDK.
- Microsoft retired emotion and gender detection. The service no longer offers them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Face free?

There is a Free tier with 30,000 transactions per month and 20 transactions per minute. Access to the service is limited, so you must apply first.

### Can anyone use the Azure Face API?

Not by default. The docs say access is limited by eligibility and usage criteria. You apply with the Face Recognition intake form.

### Does Azure AI Face detect emotions?

No. Microsoft retired the emotion and gender capabilities.
