You build scenarios in Make's visual editor by dragging modules onto a canvas and mapping data between them. It suits people who want to connect apps without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and shows up to 1,000 credits a month. The pricing page says there is no time limit on it. It includes the visual workflow builder, more than 3,500 apps, routers and filters, and customer support.

The comparison table lists these Free limits: 2 active scenarios, a 15-minute minimum interval between runs, a 5-minute maximum run time for a scenario, a 5MB maximum file size, 512MB of data transfer, and 7 days of execution logs. Paid plans allow a 1-minute interval.

Most apps use 1 credit for each operation. Some AI and content modules use more or use a dynamic amount based on tokens, file size, pages, or run time. The help center lists modules that use 2 or 10 credits per operation.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says that when credits run out, your scenarios stop running until credits are added again. On the Free plan this means a stop, not a charge. Make notifies you when you approach 75% and again at 90% of your credits. Incoming webhooks are queued up to your webhook queue storage limit.

The help center says you can upgrade, buy extra credits, or turn on automatic extra credit purchases when you run out. Paid plans such as Core start at $9 a month for 10k credits.

## Gotchas

- A credit is not always one operation. AI apps and some advanced modules use more, and the cost can depend on the amount of data.
- Each scenario has a size limit of 2MB, and each team can create up to 100 scenarios per day.
- The pricing page does not say whether a credit card is needed for the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Make free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, has no time limit, and includes up to 1,000 credits a month, 2 active scenarios, and a 15-minute minimum interval between runs.

### What happens when I run out of Make credits on the free plan?

Your scenarios stop running until credits are added again. Make warns you at 75% and 90% of your credits.

### Does Make need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say whether the Free plan needs a card. This entry lists none as required.
