Everything runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install. You write issues in a rich text or markdown editor, and programmers can also use a REST API and a command line tool. It suits writers who want a simple newsletter tool.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says Buttondown costs absolutely nothing for your first 100 subscribers. That price includes the rich text or markdown editor, sending from a custom domain, hosted newsletter archives, and a privacy-first setup.

Other features are paid add-ons. Tagging and segmentation, analytics, paid subscriptions, comments and surveys, RSS-to-email, sponsorships, and email attachments cost $9 per month each. Automations, multiple newsletters, and archives on a custom domain cost $29 per month each. Whitelabeling and teams cost $79 per month each.

## When you hit a limit

The page does not list a hard stop at 100 subscribers. Pricing is set by a slider that depends on your subscriber count, and the pricing page text does not show the price above 100. You pay for active subscribers only, not for all contacts.

The prices also assume that you send at most one email a day to your entire subscriber base. If you send more often, the FAQ says to contact Buttondown to talk about usage-based pricing. Registered non-profits can get a 50% discount on their plan.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page text does not show what you pay above the free 100 subscribers. Check the slider before you grow past 100.
- Unless you set up a custom domain, subscribers see a Buttondown address at buttondown.email, not the email address you signed up with.
- Add-ons are billed on top of the base price, so tagging and analytics add $9 per month each.

## Questions people ask

### Is Buttondown free?

Yes, for your first 100 subscribers. The free price includes the editor, custom domain sending, and hosted archives. No card is required.

### Does Buttondown charge for all contacts?

No. The FAQ says it charges for active subscribers, so you pay only for the people who actively receive your emails.

### Is there a limit to the emails I can send on Buttondown?

The prices assume at most one email a day to your whole subscriber base. If you send more than that, Buttondown asks you to contact them about usage-based pricing.
