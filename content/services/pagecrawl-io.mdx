You use PageCrawl in the browser: add a URL, choose what to track, and read the diff when it changes. It also reads PDF, Word, Excel, and CSV files by link. It suits one person with a short watch list.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan lets you track up to 6 pages. It allows up to 220 checks per month, with checks as often as every 60 minutes. You get 1 user and 90 days of history.

The pricing FAQ says document monitoring works on every plan, including Free. You can paste a link to a published PDF, Word, Excel, or CSV file and PageCrawl compares the contents on each check. The paid Standard plan raises the page and check limits and keeps history for 12 months.

## When you hit a limit

If you exceed your plan limits, PageCrawl pauses your checks. It shows a notice when you near the monthly limit, so you can act before it happens. The pricing page does not describe any charge for going over; you only get the pause.

On the free plan, 220 checks go fast if you check hourly. Checking pages less often stretches the quota across the month.

## Gotchas

- Technical support is not offered on the Free plan. Paid plans have response targets.
- Some sites block monitoring tools and return errors or empty pages. The pricing page says the Enterprise and Ultimate plans include residential proxies to unblock most of them.
- The Visual tracking type is marked beta in the help center.

## Questions people ask

### Is PageCrawl free?

Yes, within limits. The Free Forever plan tracks up to 6 pages with up to 220 checks per month and 90 days of history.

### What happens when I run out of PageCrawl checks?

Your checks pause. PageCrawl shows a notice before you reach the monthly limit so you can upgrade or wait for the next month.

### Can PageCrawl monitor a PDF?

Yes, on every plan including Free. Paste a link to a published PDF, Word, Excel, or CSV file and it compares the contents on each check.
