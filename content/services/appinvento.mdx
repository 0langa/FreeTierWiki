You work in a web dashboard. You create a project, add roles, collections, fields, and routes, and connect add-ons. The docs call it a backend builder, so it suits people who want an API and data model more than a visual front-end editor.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free column with 3 projects and 5 tables. It also lists unlimited API and unlimited routes, and 1 add-on, which is the Google add-on. The page shows 0 commits on the Free plan. The pricing page also says there is a 14 day unlimited free trial, with no contract and no credit card required.

Paid plans add more. The Pro plan costs $99 per month and includes 10 projects, unlimited tables, unlimited commits, and unlimited add-ons. Add-ons that the Free column does not include are Stripe, Facebook, Apple, notification, file upload, captcha, rate limit, and GitHub. Yearly billing is shown with 20% off. Enterprise pricing is custom.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the project or table counts on the Free plan. It only shows the counts for each plan. The page does not describe overage charges for the Free plan, and it says no credit card is required.

The docs show an Upgrade Project step during project creation. It asks for card details so you can use unlimited add-ons, routes, and API. That step is for upgrading, so expect to move to Pro, or ask the team, when you need more projects, more tables, or more add-ons.

## Gotchas

- The Free column lists 0 commits, while the docs describe a Commit Your Changes step. Test that flow on a free project before you plan around it.
- Only 1 add-on, Google, is included on the Free plan. Login, file upload, and payment add-ons need a paid plan.
- The docs home page says it was last updated 3 years ago, so screens in the guides may differ from the live app.

## Questions people ask

### Is Appinvento free?

Yes, there is a Free plan with 3 projects and 5 tables. The pricing page also offers a 14 day free trial with no credit card.

### What does Appinvento cost after the free plan?

The Pro plan costs $99 per month and includes 10 projects. Yearly billing is shown with 20% off.

### Does Appinvento need a credit card?

The pricing page says no credit card is required for the free trial. The upgrade step in the docs does ask for card details.
