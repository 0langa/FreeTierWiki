You use Calendly in the browser, with a browser extension and a mobile app alongside it. Developers can use the REST API, webhooks, embeds, or the MCP server from the developer docs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is marked always free. You get one event type, one calendar connection, unlimited one-on-one meetings, and a customizable booking page. The comparison table also lists unlimited hosted meetings, availability rules, meeting polls and one-off meeting links, a mobile app, and the browser extension.

You can sync one calendar so Calendly checks for conflicts before someone books. The Standard plan at $10 per seat per month adds unlimited event types, more calendars, reminders, and payments. The Teams plan at $16 per seat per month adds round-robin and team scheduling.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan does not run out. Its limits are about features: one event type and one calendar. If you need a second event type or calendar, the page points you to the Standard plan. Invitees never need a seat or an account, so your guests are not counted. The pricing page does not describe any monthly booking cap, and no card is needed to sign up.

## Gotchas

- You get only one event type, so you cannot offer a short call and a long call side by side.
- Group and multi-person meeting types, round-robin, automated reminders, and payments are not in the free column of the comparison table.
- Notetaker and the Callie email assistant are add-ons for Standard and Teams, not part of the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Calendly free?

Yes. The Free plan is always free, with one event type, one calendar connection, and one-on-one scheduling. Paid plans start at $10 per seat per month.

### Does Calendly need a credit card?

No. The home page says no credit card is required, and you can sign up with Google, Microsoft, or email.

### Do my invitees need a Calendly account?

No. The pricing page says invitees do not need an account or a seat. Only the people who connect calendars and host meetings need a seat.
