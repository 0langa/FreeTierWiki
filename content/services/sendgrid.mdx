You send with the Web API v3, SMTP, or an official library, and you manage keys and sender identities in the console. The free offer is a trial, so it suits testing more than a long-lived small project.

## What the free plan gives you

SendGrid does not have a permanent free plan on its pricing page. The free trial costs $0 for 60 days and lets you send 100 emails a day with no credit card. The trial gives you SMTP and API access, analytics, deliverability features, and the dynamic template editor.

The pricing table shows 60 content testing credits per month on the trial. Free validations of email addresses are listed for paid tiers. After the trial, the paid plans are Essentials from $19.95 per month, Pro from $89.95 per month, and custom Premier pricing.

## When you hit a limit

The trial has a daily cap of 100 emails. The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass it, or what happens to the account when the 60 days end. Treat a paid plan as required to keep sending.

On paid plans, the FAQ says that sending past what your plan includes may add overage charges on top of your monthly rate. Upgrading to the next tier usually costs less than paying overages.

## Gotchas

- This is a 60-day trial, not a free-forever plan. Do not build a long-term project on it without a paid plan in mind.
- Twilio requires you to authenticate or verify your sender identity, either as a single sender or for a domain, to protect sending reputation.
- The total size of a message, including headers and attachments, cannot exceed 20MB.

## Questions people ask

### Is SendGrid free?

Only as a trial. The free trial costs $0 for 60 days and lets you send 100 emails a day, with no credit card required.

### Does SendGrid have a permanent free plan?

No. The pricing page lists a free trial, then paid plans starting at $19.95 per month.

### What happens when the SendGrid free trial ends?

The pricing page does not say what happens when the trial ends. Plan to move to a paid plan to keep sending.
