You create the database in the Azure portal and query it with the portal query editor, SQL Server Management Studio, VS Code, or `sqlcmd`. It suits people who want a free SQL Server to learn or prototype on.

## What the free plan gives you

The free offer lasts for the lifetime of your subscription. Each database gets 100,000 vCore seconds of serverless compute, 32 GB of data storage, and 32 GB of backup storage every month. You can create up to 10 General Purpose databases per subscription.

The databases run on the same managed platform as paid ones, with patching, backups, and monitoring handled for you. The maximum is 4 vCores and 32 GB of data per database. Your allowance renews at the start of each calendar month.

## When you hit a limit

You choose what happens in the Behavior when free limit reached setting. The default option auto-pauses the database until the start of the next calendar month. The other option keeps the database online, and Azure charges the extra vCore use and storage to your billing method at standard serverless rates.

You are not charged unless you pick that second option and go over. Once you pick it, you cannot switch back to auto-pause. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- Open connections from tools like SQL Server Management Studio can keep using credits by stopping auto-pause, so disconnect when you finish.
- Point-in-time restore keeps seven days, and long-term backup retention is not available with auto-pause on.
- You cannot copy the free database, put it in an elastic pool or failover group, or convert an existing database to the free offer.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure SQL Database free?

Yes, within a monthly allowance. Each of up to 10 databases per subscription gets 100,000 vCore seconds, 32 GB of data, and 32 GB of backup storage free every month.

### What happens when the free limit runs out?

By default the database auto-pauses until the next calendar month. You can instead choose to keep it online and pay standard serverless rates for the extra use.

### Can I use the Azure SQL free offer without an Azure free account?

Yes. The offer works with any Azure subscription type, but you do need an Azure account and subscription.
