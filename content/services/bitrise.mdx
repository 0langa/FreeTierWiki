You use Bitrise in the web dashboard and describe builds as workflows, with an API and a CLI around it. The docs build the first project from a wizard that scans your repository.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free and the pricing page calls it free forever. The FAQ says it includes 500 build minutes a month on Linux or macOS, 1 private app, and unlimited public apps. It also includes Pipelines, unlimited workflow steps, 1 free on-prem runner, and unlimited team members. Builds on Hobby time out at 90 minutes.

To keep usage fair, Bitrise says you need to verify a credit card for Hobby, and that you will not be charged unless you turn on Pay-As-You-Go.

## When you hit a limit

A build that reaches the 90-minute timeout is stopped. On Starter and Pro the timeout is 210 minutes.

When the 500 minutes are gone, the FAQ says you can keep building by turning on Pay-As-You-Go. On Hobby it charges 1.5x per minute up to a $15 monthly cap, and after the cap you cannot start new builds until your next billing cycle. Pay-As-You-Go charges under $5 are not billed. The Starter plan costs $99 per month on monthly billing and has unlimited usage with up to 4 concurrent builds. The docs say Bitrise does not sign you up for a paid plan when the trial ends.

## Gotchas

- The docs say a new account cannot start CI builds until you start the 30-day trial or buy a Pro plan.
- The trial needs a credit card. The card is charged $0.01, and the charge is voided right away.
- The pricing FAQ says Hobby also needs a verified credit card, and you are charged only if you turn on Pay-As-You-Go.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bitrise free?

Yes, through the Hobby plan: 1 private app, unlimited public apps, 500 build minutes a month, and a 90-minute build timeout, with unlimited team members.

### Does Bitrise need a credit card?

Yes. The pricing FAQ says you need to verify a credit card for Hobby, and the docs say the 30-day trial asks for a card with a $0.01 charge that is voided. You are only charged if you turn on Pay-As-You-Go.

### What happens when my Bitrise trial ends?

You must choose a plan to continue. You can pick the free Hobby plan with 500 build minutes per month, and Bitrise does not move you to a paid plan on its own.
