You use Bitrise in the web dashboard and describe builds as workflows, with an API and a CLI around it. The docs build the first project from a wizard that scans your repository.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free and the pricing page calls it free forever. The FAQ says it includes 1 private app and 5 concurrent builds, with a 90-minute build timeout and 300 credits per month. The plan is access for a team of one, with support from the Bitrise Community on Slack or the Discuss forum.

The pricing page says the credits are enough to build a small project a few times per month. The pricing page also lists build timeout and concurrent builds as plan limits, so check them before you rely on the plan.

## When you hit a limit

A build that reaches the 90-minute timeout is stopped. The FAQ says that on the Free plan the build times out at 90 minutes, while Starter and Pro allow longer runs.

When the monthly credits are gone, the pricing FAQ for the trial says you can request a trial extension or get going with a paid plan. The Starter plan costs $99 per month on monthly billing and has unlimited build minutes with up to 4 concurrent builds. The docs say Bitrise does not sign you up for a paid plan when the trial ends.

## Gotchas

- The docs say a new account cannot start CI builds until you start the 30-day trial or buy a Pro plan.
- The trial needs a credit card. The card is charged $0.01, and the charge is voided right away.
- The pricing FAQ says Hobby needs no credit card, and it is offered at the end of the trial. Plan to enter a card once.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bitrise free?

Yes, through the Hobby plan: 1 private app, 5 concurrent builds, a 90-minute build timeout, and 300 credits per month. It is for a team of one.

### Does Bitrise need a credit card?

The pricing FAQ says Hobby needs no credit card. The docs say you must start the 30-day trial to run builds, and the trial asks for a card with a $0.01 charge that is voided.

### What happens when my Bitrise trial ends?

You must choose a plan to continue. You can pick the free Hobby plan with 300 credits per month, and Bitrise does not move you to a paid plan on its own.
