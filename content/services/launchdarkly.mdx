You create flags in the LaunchDarkly dashboard and read them in your app through one of its SDKs. It suits small teams that want gradual rollouts and a kill switch.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is $0 per month, forever, with no credit card. It has unlimited seats and unlimited feature flags, and the pricing page says to invite your whole team because pricing is based on usage. It also includes 100K experimentation MAU, 10M logs and 10M traces, and 5K session replays and 5K errors each month.

The cap on delivery is 5 service connections and 1K client-side MAU per month. A service connection is one server-side SDK connected to one environment for a month, and each unique client-side user or device is one MAU. The plan also includes 5K AI runs a month, with 14 days of data retention.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says AI run ingestion stops at 5K on the Developer plan. For service connections and client-side users it says only that the free tier is limited, not what happens past the limit.

Foundation is the pay-as-you-go step up, with a 14-day trial. It bills $10 per additional service connection per month and $8.33 per 1k client-side MAU. It adds unlimited projects and environments. Past 5K AI runs, Foundation charges $5 per additional 1k runs.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is capped at 1 project and 3 environments in the plan table, while Foundation has unlimited.
- Server-side SDK usage is counted per connection, and the page notes serverless and short-lived processes bill fractionally by connection time on paid plans.
- Special rates exist for Kubernetes, serverless, Python, and Ruby setups, because they create many server-side connections.

## Questions people ask

### Is LaunchDarkly free?

Yes. The Developer plan is $0 per month, forever, with unlimited seats and unlimited feature flags. It limits you to 5 service connections and 1K client-side MAU a month.

### Does LaunchDarkly need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the Developer plan.

### What happens when the LaunchDarkly free trial ends?

The getting started guide says that after 14 days your trial converts to a developer tier plan. That plan is free.
