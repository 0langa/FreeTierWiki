MojoAuth is a hosted login service built around passwordless sign-in. Your users log in with a magic link, a one-time code, or a social account instead of a password, and MojoAuth hosts the login and registration pages. It suits apps that want to drop passwords without building email and OAuth flows themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free plan at $0 a month with core passwordless features for up to 25,000 MAU and no credit card required. MAU means monthly active users: unique users who authenticate at least once during a calendar month. The plan has unlimited total users, so only active users in a month count.

The FAQ lists what the permanent Free plan includes: magic links, email OTP, and Google and Facebook login. The plan card also lists the hosted login page. Passkeys, phone OTP, MFA, and One Tap Login are on Business Pro, which you can try in a 30-day free trial from the dashboard.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free plan goes past 25,000 MAU. It does say that Business Pro is priced by MAU tier, starting at $150 a month for 2,500 MAUs, and that usage above your tier costs $0.06 per MAU. Above 100,000 MAUs you talk to sales about Enterprise.

The Free plan needs no credit card to start. Watch your monthly active user count as you grow, and plan the move to a paid tier before you get close to the free amount.

## Gotchas

- MAU counts unique users per calendar month, so a launch spike counts in full for that month.
- Phone OTP and passkeys are not on the Free plan, so mobile-first sign-in by SMS means a paid tier.
- Separate production and test environments are listed as a paid feature; on the free plan, test carefully.

## Questions people ask

### Is MojoAuth free?

Yes. The Free plan is permanent and covers up to 25,000 MAUs with no credit card required.

### Does the free plan include passkeys?

No. Passkeys are on Business Pro, which has a 30-day free trial.

### How are monthly active users counted?

A monthly active user is a unique user who authenticates at least once during a calendar month.
