You order the gateway on the OVHcloud site and manage it later in the Control Panel under Sunrise. It suits people who run their own server and want OVHcloud to handle the certificate.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free SSL Gateway is for low-traffic sites such as blogs, associations, and forums. It covers your main domain, one www subdomain, and one more subdomain of your choice. Only domains up to level 3 are allowed, so `www.example.org` works and `blog.france.example.org` does not.

You get the default Let's Encrypt DV certificate, which OVHcloud renews for you. The Free offer also includes OVHcloud Anti-DDoS Infrastructure and one month of metrics.

The paid Advanced offer costs €20 excluding VAT per month. It allows up to 1000 domains and subdomains, level-4 domains and higher, load balancing, a dedicated IP, and an optional EV certificate. The service runs in 2 locations, Roubaix and Beauharnois.

## When you hit a limit

The pages describe no traffic limit on the Free offer. Its limits are about what you can protect: the number of domains and subdomains, and the domain level. If you need more, you need the Advanced offer.

You must point your DNS at the gateway. If you do not change the A record within 3 days of the order, the order is cancelled. DNS changes can take up to 24 hours to work. The pages do not say whether the Free order needs a payment method, only that you follow the steps until you pay for the order.

## Gotchas

- SSL Gateway is not compatible with OVHcloud web hosting plans.
- You can pick only one IP address when ordering, even if your domain has several. Extra IPs are an Advanced feature.
- The Free offer cannot load balance or give you a dedicated IP, and your domain must be up to level 3.

## Questions people ask

### Is OVH SSL Gateway free?

Yes, there is a Free offer. It covers a main domain, a www subdomain, and one more subdomain, with a Let's Encrypt certificate.

### Can OVH SSL Gateway Free protect a level-4 domain?

No. The Free offer only allows domains up to level 3. Level-4 domains and higher need the Advanced offer.

### Does OVH SSL Gateway work with OVHcloud web hosting?

No. The docs say SSL Gateways are not compatible with OVHcloud web hosting offers.
