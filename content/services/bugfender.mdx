You add the Bugfender SDK to a mobile or web app, and logs from your users' devices show up in the Bugfender web dashboard. It suits solo developers and small teams who need to see what happened on a device they cannot touch.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is meant for trying Bugfender in small projects, and the home page says no credit card is required. It includes 100K log lines per day and 2,000 devices. Logs are kept for 24 hours.

Apps, user accounts, and team members are unlimited. You also get a daily summary email. All plans include unlimited app installs, unlimited app users and sessions, and enterprise-grade encryption.

Crash reporting and user feedback collection start at the Basic plan, not on Free. Basic costs from 39 per month, with 10M monthly log lines, 7-day log retention, and unlimited devices.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens on the Free plan when you pass 100K log lines in a day or 2,000 devices. It asks for no card, so a bill is unlikely. Check the dashboard before you rely on it at that scale.

The paid plans work differently: the page says you start with reserved monthly logs and scale with pay-as-you-go pricing when you need more.

## Gotchas

- Free logs vanish after 24 hours, so a bug a user reports the next day may already be gone.
- Crash reporting and user feedback are not in the Free plan list.
- The 2,000 device limit counts customer devices, and the daily 100K log lines reset every day.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bugfender free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 100K log lines per day, 2,000 devices, and unlimited apps and team members, with no credit card required.

### How long does Bugfender keep logs on the free plan?

Free logs are kept for 24 hours. Basic keeps them 7 days, and Pro keeps them 30 days.

### Does Bugfender Free include crash reporting?

No. The pricing page lists crash reporting under the Basic plan and above.
