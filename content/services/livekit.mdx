LiveKit Cloud is the hosted version of LiveKit, an open-source WebRTC media server. Your app connects users to rooms over WebRTC, and LiveKit routes the audio and video. It also hosts voice AI agents and can bridge phone calls into rooms.

## What the free plan gives you

The Build plan costs $0 a month, and the pricing page says no credit card is required. Each month it includes 5,000 WebRTC minutes, which is the time end users spend connected, with up to 100 concurrent connections and 50GB of downstream data transfer.

For agents, Build includes 1,000 agent session minutes, 5 concurrent agent sessions, one agent deployment, and $2.50 in LiveKit Inference credits, which the page estimates at about 50 minutes. It also gives you 1 free US local phone number with 50 inbound minutes, 1,000 third-party SIP minutes, and 60 transcode minutes shared between stream import and recording.

## When you hit a limit

LiveKit's quota docs say that on the free Build plan the included allowance acts as a hard cap: after you exceed it, new requests fail rather than incurring overage charges. Allowances reset on the first day of each calendar month and do not roll over.

Concurrency limits work the same way at any moment: once 100 users are connected, new connections fail until someone leaves. The Ship plan costs $50 a month, includes 150,000 WebRTC minutes, and bills $0.0005 per minute past that.

## Gotchas

- All of your free projects share one set of allowances, so creating more projects does not add minutes.
- Self-hosted agents that connect to LiveKit Cloud count against WebRTC participant minutes.
- Build has community support only; email support starts on Ship.

## Questions people ask

### Is LiveKit Cloud free?

Yes, within limits. The Build plan costs $0 a month and includes 5,000 WebRTC minutes and 1,000 agent session minutes each month.

### Will LiveKit charge me if I go over the free minutes?

No. On the Build plan the allowance is a hard cap, so new requests fail instead of creating overage charges.

### Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The pricing page says the Build plan needs no credit card.
