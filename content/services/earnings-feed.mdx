You call Earnings Feed over plain HTTP with a Bearer API key and get JSON back. No SDK is required. It suits developers and researchers who want SEC data without parsing EDGAR themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan allows 15 requests per minute and 5,000 requests per UTC calendar month. It costs $0 and needs no credit card or trial period. Every plan includes every endpoint, so Free can read filings, single filing details, insider transactions, 13F holdings, and company profiles.

The data covers 10-K and 10-Q reports, 8-K events, Form 3, 4, and 5 insider trades, 13F holdings, and 13D and 13G ownership stakes. The site checks EDGAR every minute. Its own figures say half of filings appear within two minutes of SEC acceptance, and 9 in 10 within five.

The quota is shared across API keys, interactive docs, and MCP. Interactive docs tokens are fixed at 10 requests per minute.

## When you hit a limit

Monthly limits are hard caps on every plan, and they reset at the start of each UTC calendar month. When you reach the monthly cap, the docs say not to retry in a loop. Wait for the UTC reset, upgrade, or contact support.

The per-minute limit works differently. A minute-scoped 429 response includes a Retry-After header. Wait that long, add some jitter, and cap your retries. Responses include headers that show your minute and monthly usage, so you can track it in code. Pro is $10 per month billed annually and allows 60 requests per minute and 250,000 per month.

## Gotchas

- The monthly cap is account-wide. Testing in the interactive docs or through MCP uses the same 5,000 requests as your app.
- The pricing table and the FAQ show Pro both as $15 per month and as $10 per month billed annually. The lower price needs an annual plan.
- The site says it is not affiliated with the SEC, and nothing there is investment advice.
