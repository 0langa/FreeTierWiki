You add the LangSmith SDK to your Python or TypeScript code, mark the functions you want traced, and read the traces in the web app. It suits a solo developer who builds agents or LLM apps.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan costs $0 per seat per month and is meant for solo users getting started. It includes 1 seat, community support, and up to 5k base traces per month. A trace is one execution of your app, such as an agent run, an evaluator, or a playground session.

Base traces have a short retention of 14 days. Extended traces last 180 days, and you can upgrade a base trace to an extended trace for an extra fee. The Plus plan costs $39 per seat per month, adds unlimited seats, and includes 10k base traces.

## When you hit a limit

The billing docs say personal organizations are limited to 5,000 traces per month until a credit card is added. After you add a card, you are no longer rate limited at 5,000 traces, and you are charged for any excess traces at the pricing page rates.

So you do not get a surprise bill unless you add the card. You can also set a spend limit for a workspace under Usage limits in the billing settings.

## Gotchas

- The 5,000 trace limit is lifted by adding a credit card, and extra traces then cost money.
- Base traces vanish after 14 days. Extended traces cost more.
- Developer has 1 seat. Adding teammates needs the Plus plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is LangSmith free?

Yes, for one person. The Developer plan costs $0 per seat per month and includes up to 5k base traces per month and community support.

### What happens when I go over the LangSmith free trace limit?

Personal organizations are limited to 5,000 traces per month until a credit card is added. After that, excess traces are charged.

### Does LangSmith need a credit card?

Not to sign up. The docs say you can sign up without one. You add a card only to go past 5,000 traces per month.
