You use Transifex in the web app, through the `tx` command line client, or through its API and repository integrations. It suits open source maintainers and teams that automate localization.

## What the free plan gives you

Transifex is free forever for eligible open source projects. An eligible project has public source code under an OSI-approved license. It must also have no funding, revenue, or commercialization model. Transifex began as an open source project in 2007. It says it has always provided the platform free to open source projects.

Commercial use is paid. The pricing page now shows one plan, with no tiers or feature gates. It includes unlimited collaborators, hosted content, and projects. You pay for AI-translated words, at a rate that falls as your volume grows. Human review and post-editing do not count toward usage.

## When you hit a limit

The open source offer publishes neither usage caps, AI allowances nor a card requirement. An unspecified quota is not an unlimited-use promise. The main risk is losing eligibility. If your project gains funding, revenue, or a commercial model, it no longer matches the rules, and you should expect to move to the paid plan.

On the paid plan, usage is the number of source words that XTM IQ translates for you. The pricing page does not state a numerical price or advertise a self-serve trial. It does not explain the billing transition if an open-source project loses eligibility. It also says third-party machine translation is not part of that plan.

## Gotchas

- Free use is only for projects that meet the open source rules. A project with revenue or funding does not qualify.
- The paid plan does not include third-party machine translation. Every translation goes through XTM IQ.
- The pricing page text shows no price. It offers a word range selector and a Contact Sales button, so ask for a quote before you plan a budget.

## Questions people ask

### Is Transifex free?

Transifex is free forever for eligible open source projects. Commercial use needs a paid plan, and the pricing page shows no free trial.

### Who can use the Transifex open source plan?

Projects with public source code under an OSI-approved license, and with no funding, revenue, or commercialization model.

### How does Transifex charge for paid use?

The pricing page says you pay for AI-translated words at one rate that improves with volume. Seats, projects, and hosted content do not change the price.
