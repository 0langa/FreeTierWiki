You keep your own HTML form and set its action to a HeroTofu endpoint. The site also has guides for React, Next.js, Vue, Jekyll, Hugo, and Gatsby.

## What the free plan gives you

The free Basic plan costs $0. It allows unlimited form endpoints and 25 submissions a month. The submission archive keeps entries for 30 days.

The plan includes form spam protection and email notifications. The home page says spam tools include honeypots, Akismet, and Google reCAPTCHA v3. Popups and banners are unlimited, with 100 impressions a day.

Every signup first gets 14 days of the Business plan, and no credit card is needed. If you do not subscribe, you move to the free plan on its own. The Pro plan adds 1500 submissions a month, Zapier and Slack integration, and email support.

## When you hit a limit

When you reach the monthly submission limit, new submissions are paused until the next billing cycle. The site says you get a notification before you hit the limit, so you can upgrade if you need to.

The pricing page does not say if paused submissions are kept for you. The free plan has no card on file, so no bill can come from it. Pro costs $14.99 a month, or $8.33 a month billed yearly. After the trial, the site says it pauses API access and some premium features.

## Gotchas

- The free plan allows only 25 submissions a month, which a busy contact form can use up fast.
- After the 14-day trial, your account downgrades, and API access plus some premium features pause.
- The pricing page lists Zapier and Slack integration under Pro, not Basic.

## Questions people ask

### Is HeroTofu free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever with unlimited form endpoints and 25 submissions a month. Paid plans start at $8.33 a month on yearly billing.

### What happens when I exceed the HeroTofu submission limit?

New submissions are paused until the next billing cycle. HeroTofu sends a notification before you reach the limit.

### Does HeroTofu need a credit card?

No. The site says no credit card is needed to start the 14-day trial or the free plan.
