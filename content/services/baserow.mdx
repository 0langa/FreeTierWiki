Baserow is a no-code database you can self-host or use as a hosted cloud service. It gives you typed tables, forms, an application builder for portals, automations, and a REST API. This entry covers the hosted cloud Free plan, which Baserow runs for you.

## What the free plan gives you

The cloud Free plan is for individuals and small teams and costs $0. It includes unlimited databases, with 3,000 rows and 2 GB of storage per workspace. You get 2,000 automation credits per workspace each month and up to 500 external app users for applications you build.

Views on the Free plan are Grid, Form, and Gallery. Row change history covers 14 days. The plan includes a library of 65+ templates. Kanban, calendar, timeline, and survey views, personal views, and AI features start on Premium, which costs $10 per user per month billed yearly or $12 billed monthly.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a workspace reaches 3,000 rows or 2 GB of storage, or when the monthly automation credits run out. Keep an eye on your row count as it grows, and plan an upgrade or a cleanup before you need the space.

There is no metered overage listed for the Free plan. The next tier, Premium, raises the limits to 50,000 rows and 20 GB of storage per workspace and 100,000 automation credits.

## Gotchas

- The row and storage limits are per workspace, not per table, so every database in the workspace shares them.
- Published applications on the Free plan carry a Made with Baserow badge; logo removal is a paid feature.
- Self-hosting Baserow is a different offer with its own limits; the numbers here are for the hosted cloud.

## Questions people ask

### Is Baserow free?

Yes. The hosted Free plan costs $0 and includes 3,000 rows and 2 GB of storage per workspace, with unlimited databases.

### How many rows can I store on Baserow's Free plan?

3,000 rows per workspace in the hosted cloud, shared across all databases in that workspace.

### Can I build apps on top of Baserow for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes the application builder and up to 500 external app users, with a Made with Baserow badge.
