You create flags in the Rollgate dashboard and read them in your app through an SDK. The docs list SDKs for frontend, backend, and mobile.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs €0 per month and is labeled free forever. It is meant for side projects and trying things out. It allows 500K requests per month and unlimited flags, with 3 environments, 3 projects, and 3 seats. Support is through Discord.

Rollgate prices by requests, not by seats or monthly active users. The paid Starter plan costs €39 per month billed annually and raises the volume to 1M requests per month, and it adds scheduled changes. Pro costs €99 per month billed annually with 3M requests, A/B testing, RBAC, and 15 seats.

Data is hosted in the EU, in Germany, and the site says it is GDPR compliant by default.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say whether requests stop, are throttled, or incur charges after the Free allowance of 500K requests per month. Do not treat the quota as a published billing safeguard.

Starter raises the allowance to 1M requests and adds scheduled changes and rollback. Pro includes 3M requests, A/B testing, role-based access control, and 15 seats. The displayed annual prices are €39 and €99 per month respectively, billed yearly. Choose the billing period before comparing paid prices.

## Gotchas

- Free includes 3 seats; Starter includes 5, and Pro includes 15.
- Server keys must stay out of frontend code. The docs tell you to use client keys for React apps.
- The docs list a self-hosted option as coming soon. Today the service is hosted.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rollgate free?

Yes. The free plan costs €0 and includes 500K requests per month, unlimited flags, 3 environments, 3 projects, and 3 seats.

### Does Rollgate need a credit card?

No. The site says to start free with no card required.

### Where does Rollgate host my data?

In the EU, specifically in Germany. The FAQ says no data leaves the region and there are no US sub-processors in the request path.
