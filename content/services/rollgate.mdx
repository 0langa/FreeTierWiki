You create flags in the Rollgate dashboard and read them in your app through an SDK. The docs list SDKs for frontend, backend, and mobile.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs €0 per month and is labeled free forever. It is meant for side projects and trying things out. It allows 500K requests per month and unlimited flags, with 3 environments and 3 projects. Support is through Discord.

Rollgate prices by requests, not by seats or monthly active users. The paid Starter plan costs €45 per month and raises the volume to 1M requests per month, and it adds scheduled changes. Pro costs €119 per month with 3M requests, A/B testing, RBAC, and 15 seats.

Data is hosted in the EU, in Germany, and the site says it is GDPR compliant by default.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 500K requests in a month. The pricing model is built on request volume, so that count is the number to watch.

The free plan needs no card, so Rollgate cannot bill you by surprise. If you outgrow it, you pick a plan by request volume. The site says flag evaluation happens locally in your app, which keeps your request count down compared with a call for every check.

## Gotchas

- Seat counts are tied to plans. The pricing page lists 15 seats for Pro and 50 for Growth, but it does not list a seat number for Free.
- Server keys must stay out of frontend code. The docs tell you to use client keys for React apps.
- The docs list a self-hosted option as coming soon. Today the service is hosted.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rollgate free?

Yes. The free plan costs €0 and includes 500K requests per month, unlimited flags, 3 environments, and 3 projects.

### Does Rollgate need a credit card?

No. The site says to start free with no card required.

### Where does Rollgate host my data?

In the EU, specifically in Germany. The FAQ says no data leaves the region and there are no US sub-processors in the request path.
