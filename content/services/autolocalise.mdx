You add an SDK to your code and mark the text to translate. You then review the AI translations in the dashboard. It suits developers who build with React, Next.js, React Native, Expo, or Python.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 per month. It gives you 10,000 characters of translation per month and 1 project. It covers more than 100 languages, the same list as the paid plan. The home page calls the free plan a fit for side projects and testing, and a Start Free button starts it. That makes it a good place to try the AI translation quality on one real app before you decide to pay.

The paid Pro plan costs 9 USD per month. It adds up to 250,000 characters per month, unlimited private projects, and priority support. Pro also includes 1 editor member per project, and each extra member costs $2 per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use all 10,000 free characters. It names only the Pro overage rule: $1 for each extra 30,000 characters. That rule applies to the paid plan, not to the free plan.

The free plan asks for no card, so a bill on the free plan looks unlikely. Check the dashboard for your character use before you ship a large app. If you need more than 1 project or more characters, the next step is the Pro plan.

## Gotchas

- The free plan allows 1 project. Unlimited private projects need Pro.
- Translation runs through the SDK with your API key. The docs say you do not manage translation files, so you have no locale files of your own in the repository.
- The docs list React, Next.js, React Native, Expo, and Python. The home page marks Vue.js and Flutter as planned.

## Alternatives in this atlas

## Questions people ask

### Is AutoLocalise free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 per month and includes 10,000 characters of translation per month for 1 project. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the AutoLocalise free limit?

The pricing page does not say. It only lists a price of $1 per extra 30,000 characters for the Pro plan.

### Which frameworks does AutoLocalise support?

The docs list React, Next.js, React Native, Expo, and Python with Django, FastAPI, or Flask. The home page shows Vue.js and Flutter as planned.
