You use the Cerbos Hub web app to manage policy stores and deployments, and the Cerbos CLI or SDKs to automate them. Your application asks a Cerbos policy decision point for each access check through an SDK or API. It suits teams that want authorization rules kept separate from code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Proof of Concept plan costs $0 per month for up to 100 monthly active principals. A principal is a user or a service. You get 1 workspace and 2 developers, 2 Playgrounds, and 2 simultaneous policy decision points. You can also have up to 5 custom tenants and 5 policy builds per week.

Hub keeps 1 week of unified audit logs. The plan also lists a managed CI/CD pipeline, in-browser and serverless authorization, and community support. The open-source Cerbos PDP is a separate option. It is free forever and runs anywhere, on premises or in the cloud, without the Hub features.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 100 monthly active principals or the build and PDP limits. It says pricing is based on the number of monthly active principals, counted as unique principals authorized in one calendar month.

The next plan, Development, starts from $25 per month with the first 100 principals included. It comes with a 3 months free trial. It raises the limits to 3 workspaces, 5 developers, and 100 policy builds per week. The FAQ says you can cancel at any time.

## Gotchas

- Cerbos Hub runs in the cloud, but the policy decision points run in your environment. They need outbound internet access to fetch bundle updates.
- The audit log retention on the free plan is only 1 week, and the Development plan raises it to 3 months.
- The FAQ says you can return to the open-source PDP from Hub. First host your own policy files, then change the PDP settings to point at them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cerbos Hub free?

Yes, within limits. The Proof of Concept plan costs $0 per month for up to 100 monthly active principals, 1 workspace, and 2 developers. The open-source Cerbos PDP is also free forever.

### What is a monthly active principal in Cerbos?

It is a unique user or service that needs an authorization decision during one calendar month. If your app has 3000 monthly active users and 15 non-human identities, you have 3015 principals.

### Does Cerbos Hub need a credit card?

The pricing page lists the Proof of Concept plan at $0 and does not mention a card for it. It only says the Development plan has a 3 months free trial.
