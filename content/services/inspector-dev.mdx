You add the Inspector package for your framework, give it an ingestion key, and read traces and errors in the web dashboard. It suits PHP developers on Laravel, Symfony, CodeIgniter, Slim, or plain PHP.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever and has no deadline. It includes 30K transactions per month, unlimited events, unlimited hosts and apps, and up to 3 team members. It keeps 3 days of data and includes 1 notification channel. AI Bug Fix is part of every plan, including Free. You get unlimited data for the first 7 days, then the 30K monthly quota applies.

A transaction is one execution cycle of your app, such as an HTTP endpoint call, a scheduled task, or a background job. Only transactions count toward the data volume. Single events inside a transaction are unlimited. The quota renews every month. Paid plans start with Beginner at 300k transactions.

## When you hit a limit

When your account reaches the transactions included in its plan, Inspector drops the data your app sends until the next monthly cycle. It does not bill you unless usage-based billing is on.

On Free, plan to wait for the next cycle or upgrade. The pricing FAQ says usage-based billing keeps monitoring on past the quota, but the pricing table lists no extra-slot rate for Free, while the billing docs mention a fee from €3 for the free tier. Check the billing section before you count on it.

## Gotchas

- Data is dropped, not queued, after you reach the limit. You lose traces until the cycle resets.
- The pricing table and FAQ both limit Free to 3 team members, 3 days of retention, and 1 notification channel.
- Free has no REST API access. Check the current integration guide for your framework before relying on an SDK.

## Questions people ask

### Is Inspector.dev free?

Yes. The free forever plan includes 30K transactions per month with unlimited events, hosts, and apps. The pricing page says no credit card or payment is required to register.

### What happens when I reach the Inspector usage limit?

Inspector drops the data your app sends until the next monthly cycle. You can wait or upgrade. Usage-based billing on Free is not confirmed.

### Which languages does Inspector.dev support?

The docs list guides for Laravel, Symfony, CodeIgniter, Slim, Neuron AI, and plain PHP.
