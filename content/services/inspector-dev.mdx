You add the Inspector package for your framework, give it an ingestion key, and read traces and errors in the web dashboard. It suits PHP developers on Laravel, Symfony, CodeIgniter, Slim, or plain PHP.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever and has no deadline. It includes 30K transactions per month, unlimited events, unlimited hosts and apps, and up to 3 team members. The AI Bug Fixer is also listed on the free plan.

A transaction is one execution cycle of your app, such as an HTTP endpoint call, a scheduled task, or a background job. Only transactions count toward the data volume. Single events inside a transaction are unlimited. The quota renews every month. Paid plans start with Beginner at 300k transactions.

## When you hit a limit

When your account reaches the transactions included in its plan, Inspector drops the data your app sends until the next monthly cycle. It does not bill you unless you turn on usage-based billing.

You then have three options. Wait for the next cycle, upgrade to a higher plan, or enable usage-based billing, which keeps monitoring on for a small fee beyond the quota. You can turn usage-based billing on or off in the billing section.

## Gotchas

- Data is dropped, not queued, after you reach the limit. You lose traces until the cycle resets.
- The pricing table says the free plan has up to 3 team members, while the FAQ says you can invite as many as you want, so check your account.
- The home page describes the product as visibility into PHP production environments, so other stacks may not fit.

## Questions people ask

### Is Inspector.dev free?

Yes. The free forever plan includes 30K transactions per month with unlimited events, hosts, and apps. The pricing page says no credit card or payment is required to register.

### What happens when I reach the Inspector usage limit?

Inspector drops the data your app sends until the next monthly cycle. You can wait, upgrade, or enable usage-based billing.

### Which languages does Inspector.dev support?

The docs list guides for Laravel, Symfony, CodeIgniter, Slim, Neuron AI, and plain PHP.
