Simple Analytics is a web analytics service built around privacy. It collects no personal data, uses no cookies, and does not fingerprint visitors, and stores website data in the EU, primarily in the Netherlands. Optional features such as the AI assistant can send data outside the EU when enabled. It suits developers who want a one-page dashboard of site traffic without a consent banner.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is meant for hobby sites. It covers 1 user and 5 websites, keeps the latest month of analytics data in your dashboard, and requires a badge on your site. Simple Analytics calls it free forever.

Every account starts the same way: when you sign up, a 14-day free trial begins with no credit card required, and you can use the advanced features without restrictions. After the 14 days, you choose a plan to keep using the service: one of the paid plans or a downgrade to the Free plan. The paid plan is priced by estimated monthly pageviews, for example €20 a month at 100k pageviews, and it includes 1 user, with each extra user at €20 a month.

## When you hit a limit

Free explicitly offers unlimited pageviews, subject to fair use. Simple Analytics says it emails you when traffic exceeds fair-use limits, but does not publish the threshold or subsequent enforcement. Only the latest month of Free history remains available; older data is deleted.

After the 14-day trial, choose a paid plan or downgrade to Free to continue. Paid plans need payment details and can adjust charges with traffic. That paid billing rule does not establish automatic Free overage billing. The separate 30-day assisted proof of concept is another evaluation offer.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan requires a badge on your site, which some projects will not want.
- Only the latest month of data shows on Free, so longer trends need a paid plan.
- The trial shows advanced features that the Free plan does not keep.

## Questions people ask

### Is Simple Analytics free?

Yes. The Free plan for hobby sites covers 1 user and 5 websites with 1 month of history, and it needs a badge on your site.

### Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The 14-day trial starts at signup with no credit card required.

### What happens after the trial?

You choose a plan: one of the paid plans, or a downgrade to the Free plan to keep using Simple Analytics free.
