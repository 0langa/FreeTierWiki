You use UptimeRobot through a web dashboard, a mobile app, and an API that is included on every plan. The help center has a quick setup guide for your first monitor. It suits personal and commercial sites that need many cheap checks.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euro per month and needs no credit card. It includes 50 monitors with a 5-minute monitoring interval. It has 1 status page and keeps 3 months of data. The API is included, with a limit of 10 requests per minute on Free.

The setup guide covers types across plans; its full menu is not proof that all types are available on Free. The Free card marks API, UDP, DNS, SSL and domain-expiry monitoring with exclusion icons, matching the comparison and FAQ, and the machine-readable pricing also lists heartbeat monitoring as paid. Free integrations are basic, and the list includes Google Chat, Discord, Pushover, Pushbullet, and Splunk.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 50 monitors, and it does not describe a bill on the Free plan. Paid plans raise the monitor tier and shorten the interval.

SMS and voice call credits are not included on Free. They are sold separately; card-paying accounts can optionally enable automatic credit refills. The monitor allowance is separate from those purchases. Solo starts at EUR 9 per month billed annually for only 10 monitors, with 60-second checks and 3 custom-domain status pages. Keeping 50 monitors on Solo costs EUR 19 per month billed annually, or EUR 22 monthly. These are the amounts UptimeRobot serves to euro regions, so your currency may differ.

## Gotchas

- Commercial projects are now explicitly included in the Free plan description; advanced features still require a paid plan.
- Free checks run every 5 minutes, so a short outage can pass between two checks.
- SSL and domain-expiry monitoring need a paid plan. Own-domain status pages are listed on Solo; Free has one basic status page.

## Questions people ask

### Is UptimeRobot free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 50 monitors with 5-minute checks, 1 status page, and 3 months of data. It is described as good for personal and commercial projects.

### Does UptimeRobot need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the Free plan.

### How often does UptimeRobot check my site on the free plan?

Every 5 minutes. The Solo plan checks every 60 seconds, and higher plans go down to 30 or 15 seconds.
