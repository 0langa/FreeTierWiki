You use UptimeRobot through a web dashboard, a mobile app, and an API that is included on every plan. The help center has a quick setup guide for your first monitor. It suits hobby and non-profit sites that need many cheap checks.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euro per month and needs no credit card. It includes 50 monitors with a 5-minute monitoring interval. It has 1 status page and keeps 3 months of data. The API is included, with a limit of 10 requests per minute on Free.

Monitor types in the setup guide include HTTP(s), Keyword, Ping, Port, Heartbeat, DNS, API, and UDP. The pricing page says SSL monitoring is included only in paid plans, yet the Free card also lists SSL and domain expiry monitoring, so check what the Free plan covers. Free integrations are basic, and the list includes Google Chat, Discord, Pushover, Pushbullet, and Splunk.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 50 monitors, and it does not describe a bill on the Free plan. Paid plans raise the monitor tier and shorten the interval.

SMS and voice call credits are not included on Free. Both free and paid users can buy credits separately, as one-time purchases. The Solo plan costs 9 euro per month with annual billing and gives you 60-second checks and 3 status pages with your own domain.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan is described as good for hobby and non-profit projects. A commercial site may not fit its intent.
- Free checks run every 5 minutes, so a short outage can pass between two checks.
- The pricing page says SSL monitoring is paid-only, but its Free card lists SSL and domain expiry monitoring. Own-domain status pages are listed on the Solo plan, and Free status pages are basic.

## Questions people ask

### Is UptimeRobot free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 50 monitors with 5-minute checks, 1 status page, and 3 months of data. It is described as good for hobby and non-profit projects.

### Does UptimeRobot need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the Free plan.

### How often does UptimeRobot check my site on the free plan?

Every 5 minutes. The Solo plan checks every 60 seconds, and higher plans go down to 30 or 15 seconds.
