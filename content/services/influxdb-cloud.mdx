InfluxDB Cloud Serverless is the hosted, multi-tenant version of InfluxDB, a database built for time series such as metrics, events, and sensor readings. You write points with Telegraf, the CLI, or client libraries, and query them with SQL or InfluxQL. The Free Plan suits small IoT projects and learning, with tight rate limits and short retention.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says Cloud Serverless has a free tier with limited data ingestion rates and 30-day retention, and you start without a credit card. The service quotas page lists the Free Plan in detail.

Writes are limited to a rate of 5 MB per 5 minutes, an average of 17 kb/s of uncompressed line protocol. Query responses are limited to 300 MB per 5 minutes, an average of 1000 kb/s. You get 2 buckets, not counting the system buckets, and 30 days of data retention. Storage-level limits apply to every plan: at most 500 tables per database and 200 columns per table, and a query may touch at most 500 partitions and 1000 Parquet files.

## When you hit a limit

The Free Plan is a hard cap. All write and query rates are counted in a fixed five-minute window, and once a rate is exceeded, an error response is returned until the current five-minute window resets.

The usage-based plan lifts these limits and bills data in at $0.0025 per MB, queries at $0.012 per 100 executions, storage at $0.002 per GB-hour, and data out at $0.09 per GB. New paid accounts receive $250 in credits applied to the first 30 days.

## Gotchas

- Data older than 30 days is dropped on the Free Plan, so it does not suit long-term history.
- Bursty writers can hit the 5 MB per 5 minutes limit even when their daily total is small, and the excess writes are rejected.
- Only 2 buckets are available, so separate projects or environments share them.
