You add pages in the Wachete web app, with browser extensions, or with iOS, Android, and Windows apps. There is also a REST API, a webhook, Zapier, and an MCP server for AI assistants. It suits researchers, shoppers, and site owners who want change alerts.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page says Wachete is free to start with no credit card needed. It does not publish an exact page limit for the free plan, and the pages we read show no pricing table. What the site does list is the feature set: whole pages or parts of a page, pages behind a login, text inside PDF, Word, and Excel files, and alerts by email, phone, or chat.

It keeps up to 12 months of history with charts and spreadsheet downloads. Number charts go back up to 5 years. The features page does not say which of these are limited on the free plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pages we read do not say what happens when you pass the free allowance. They also publish no plan prices, so check the dashboard for the exact limit before you add many pages.

The FAQ says Wachete does not cap checks per month. Once you pick a plan, each page is checked at the plan's interval around the clock, and you pay only for the number of pages and how often they are checked. The FAQ says crawling goes down to the 3rd level of sub-pages and may find several hundred pages, so you will probably need a paid plan for it.

## Gotchas

- The official site publishes no free page limit, so confirm it in your account before relying on it.
- Pages whose content is built by JavaScript should be monitored as dynamic pages. If you can see the content in the preview, a static monitor works.
- You can cancel any paid subscription at any time, according to the FAQ.

## Questions people ask

### Is Wachete free?

It is free to start, with no credit card needed. The official site does not publish an exact limit on pages for the free plan.

### Does Wachete limit the number of checks per month?

No. The FAQ says it has no monthly check cap. You pay for the number of pages and how often they are checked.

### Can Wachete watch a PDF or a password-protected page?

Yes. The FAQ says Wachete extracts text from PDF, Word, and Excel files and watches it for changes, and the monitor setup offers a password-protected page option.
