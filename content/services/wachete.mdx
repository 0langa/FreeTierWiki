You add pages in the Wachete web app, with browser extensions, or with iOS, Android, and Windows apps. There is also a REST API, a webhook, Zapier, and an MCP server for AI assistants. It suits researchers, shoppers, and site owners who want change alerts.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page says Wachete is free to start with no credit card needed. The Free plan costs $0 and monitors 5 static pages and 1 dynamic page, checking every 24 hours, with no limit on the number of checks. You can watch a whole page or one part of it, and the site lists alerts by email, phone, or chat.

The pricing cards mark four things as not included on Free: selecting multiple parts of a page, 12 months of data history, pages behind a login, and text in PDF, Word, and Excel files. The first two start on Starter, pages behind a login start on Standard, and document text starts on Premium. The features page does not say which alert options or charts each plan gets.

## When you hit a limit

The pages we read do not say what happens when you add a sixth static page or a second dynamic page. Paid plans raise the limits and offer a 7-day free trial. Starter costs $5.40 a month for 50 static pages, 1 dynamic page, and checks every 60 minutes.

The FAQ says Wachete does not cap checks per month. Once you pick a plan, each page is checked at the plan's interval around the clock, and you pay only for the number of pages and how often they are checked. The FAQ says crawling goes down to the 3rd level of sub-pages and may find several hundred pages, so you will probably need a paid plan for it.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan checks every 24 hours, so it will not catch a change within the day. Starter shortens that to 60 minutes.
- Pages whose content is built by JavaScript should be monitored as dynamic pages, and Free allows only 1. If you can see the content in the preview, a static monitor works.
- Free does not include 12 months of data history, pages behind a login, or text in PDF, Word, and Excel files.

## Questions people ask

### Is Wachete free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 with no credit card and monitors 5 static pages and 1 dynamic page, checked every 24 hours.

### Does Wachete limit the number of checks per month?

No. The FAQ says it has no monthly check cap. You pay for the number of pages and how often they are checked.

### Can Wachete watch a PDF or a password-protected page?

Not on the Free plan. The pricing cards mark both as not included on Free. Pages behind a login start on Standard, and text in PDF, Word, and Excel files starts on Premium.
