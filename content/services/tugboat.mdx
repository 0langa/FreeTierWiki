Tugboat lets teams and developers create on-demand preview environments for web applications, making it easy to review changes before deployment. The Free tier is $0, no card required, for validating Tugboat as a workflow tool, with unlimited previews but limited storage. Integration with popular version control systems streamlines the workflow, but larger storage and premium features require a paid plan.
