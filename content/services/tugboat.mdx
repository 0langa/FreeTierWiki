You work in the Tugboat dashboard, the command line tool, or the API. Each preview is a copy of your site built from the config file in your repository.

## What the free plan gives you

The Tugboat blog says the Free tier costs $0 and has 5GB of storage. All plans include unlimited users and unlimited previews. Previews are limited by how much storage they take, not by count.

Pricing works per project, not per account or user. When you create a project, you pick a plan for your preview storage and build performance. You can then link as many repositories and add as many users as you want under that plan. Project storage is the total size of all previews in the project, including base previews.

Paid plans from the blog are Intro at $39 per month for 15GB, Plus at $119 per month for 60GB, and Premium from $599 per month for 300GB and more.

## When you hit a limit

On the Free tier, the terms say Tugboat deletes previews that have not been accessed or changed for 30 days, to reclaim disk space. No data in your repository is removed.

The terms also set a soft bandwidth limit of 10GB per month for previews. If your site passes usage quotas, Tugboat may not be able to serve it, or support may email you suggestions. Tugboat bills in advance monthly and upgrades from the free plan are billed immediately. If you cancel a paid plan, your account stays active on the Free tier.

## Gotchas

- Preview size is the size of the whole container after the build, so large databases and images count, not only your code.
- Do not use health data, card data, or other regulated data in previews. The terms list these as data that should not go into non-production environments.
- Data imported into a preview is not backed up. When a preview is deleted or rebuilt, that data is destroyed.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tugboat free?

Yes, there is a Free tier. It costs $0 and has 5GB of preview storage, unlimited users, and unlimited previews within that storage.

### What happens to old previews on the Tugboat free tier?

Tugboat deletes previews that have not been accessed or modified for 30 days, to reclaim disk space. Your repository data stays.

### Does Tugboat work with GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket?

Yes. It connects to all three, and it also supports a generic git repository that you link manually.
