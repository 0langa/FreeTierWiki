You work in the Tugboat dashboard. The pricing page lists a command-line tool and an API from the Intro plan. Each preview is a copy of your site built from the config file in your repository.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no ongoing Free plan for new users. The [archived pricing page](https://web.archive.org/web/20260824061509/https://www.tugboatqa.com/pricing) still showed Free at $0 on 24 August 2026. The [October change tracker](https://stacksfree.com/blog/free-tier-change-tracker-2026-10-18/) records its removal by 16 October. These observations bound the pricing-page change; they do not establish an exact shutdown day for existing accounts.

Pricing is per project. Project storage includes all previews, including base previews. The chosen plan covers its linked repositories and users.

Current pricing starts at Intro for $39 per month, with Plus at $119 and Premium from $599. Terms and docs still mention Free for existing accounts that downgrade. Those legacy references do not restore a Free signup offer.

## When you hit a limit

New projects need a paid plan, so there is no current Free allowance to size a new deployment against. The pricing page ties preview capacity to the storage purchased for the project. All previews, including base previews, share that storage; unlimited previews does not mean unlimited resources.

For an existing account, read its actual plan and downgrade terms before cancelling. The published terms retain a Free downgrade path, but that is distinct from the plans offered to new users. Do not assume an older account's entitlement transfers to a new project.

## Gotchas

- Preview size is the size of the whole container after the build, so large databases and images count, not only your code.
- Do not use health data, card data, or other regulated data in previews. The terms list these as data that should not go into non-production environments.
- Data imported into a preview is not backed up. When a preview is deleted or rebuilt, that data is destroyed.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tugboat still free for new users?

No ongoing Free plan is offered to new users. The cheapest published plan is Intro at $39 per month per project.

### Why do Tugboat's terms and docs still mention Free?

They retain references to existing accounts and downgrading from a paid plan. Check your account's actual entitlement before cancelling; those references are not a new-user Free offer.

### Does Tugboat work with GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket?

Yes. It connects to all three, and it also supports a generic git repository that you link manually.
