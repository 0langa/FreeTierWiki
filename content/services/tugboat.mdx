You work in the Tugboat dashboard. The pricing page lists a command-line tool and an API from the Intro plan. Each preview is a copy of your site built from the config file in your repository.

## What the free plan gives you

The current pricing page lists no Free plan. The [archived pricing page](https://web.archive.org/web/20260824061509/https://www.tugboatqa.com/pricing) still showed Free at $0 on 24 August 2026, so it was removed after that; Tugboat publishes no date. The site's buttons still say Try it FREE and lead to a sign-up page that names no plan, so the published pages do not confirm whether a new account can stay free.

Pricing is per project. Project storage includes all previews, including base previews. The chosen plan covers its linked repositories and users.

Current pricing starts at Intro for $39 per month, with Plus at $119 and Premium from $599. The terms still describe a Free tier: a cancelled paid subscription stays active on it. The pricing page does not offer it.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no Free allowance to size a new deployment against. It ties preview capacity to the storage purchased for the project. All previews, including base previews, share that storage; unlimited previews does not mean unlimited resources.

For an existing account, read its actual plan and downgrade terms before cancelling. The published terms retain a Free downgrade path, and Free accounts lose previews not accessed or modified for 30 days. Do not assume an older account's entitlement transfers to a new project.

## Gotchas

- Preview size is the size of the whole container after the build, so large databases and images count, not only your code.
- Do not use health data, card data, or other regulated data in previews. The terms list these as data that should not go into non-production environments.
- Data imported into a preview is not backed up. When a preview is deleted or rebuilt, that data is destroyed.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tugboat still free for new users?

The pricing page no longer lists a Free plan. The cheapest published plan is Intro at $39 per month per project, and the published pages do not confirm whether a new account can stay free.

### Why do Tugboat's terms still mention Free?

They say a cancelled paid subscription stays active on the Free tier. Check your account's actual entitlement before cancelling; the pricing page lists no Free offer.

### Does Tugboat work with GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket?

Yes. It connects to all three, and it also supports a generic git repository that you link manually.
