You create a Language resource in the Azure portal, then call it from the REST API, a client library, or the Foundry portal. It suits developers who add text analysis to an app without training their own models.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free (F0) tier includes 5,000 text records per month. One text record is up to 1,000 characters. A document with 7,500 characters counts as 8 records. The pricing page says summarization, sentiment analysis, key phrase extraction, language detection, question answering, named entity recognition, and conversational language understanding all share this one pool of free records.

Summarization counts the input text and the summary together. The other features count only the input text.

Custom features have their own free amounts. Custom text classification and custom NER give up to 1 hour of free training. Custom NER and classification allow up to 1 model hosted for free. Conversational language understanding training is free at the standard level.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page FAQ is direct. Usage is throttled when you reach the transaction limit on the Free tier, and customers cannot build up overages on the free tier. The page does not say how long the throttling lasts.

Paid tiers bill per 1,000 text records, but the price fields on the page are blank. Check the Azure pricing calculator before you upgrade. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- Question answering needs an Azure AI Search resource. Azure deploys one in your subscription when you choose it.
- The synchronous API accepts documents up to 5,120 characters. Longer files need the asynchronous API, which allows 125,000 characters across up to 25 documents.
- Azure Translator is a separate service with its own pricing.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Language free?

Yes, within limits. The F0 tier includes 5,000 text records per month, shared across the main features.

### What counts as a text record in Azure AI Language?

A text record is up to 1,000 characters. A longer document counts as one record for each 1,000 characters.

### What happens when I go over the Azure AI Language free limit?

Usage is throttled, and you cannot build up overages on the free tier.
