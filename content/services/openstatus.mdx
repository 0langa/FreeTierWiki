You work in a web dashboard to create monitors and a status page. There is also a CLI, an API, a Terraform provider, and a self-hosting guide. It suits one developer or a small team with a single service to watch.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free and needs no credit card. It includes one monitor, one status page with three components, and a minimum check interval of 10 minutes. Every new workspace also starts with a 14-day trial of the Starter plan, which needs no card either.

The paid Starter plan costs $30 per month. It adds 20 monitors, 1-minute checks, a custom domain, and subscribers. You can also run openstatus yourself, because it is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

## When you hit a limit

When the 14-day trial ends and you have not added a card, the workspace returns to the free Hobby plan. You are not billed.

For plan limits, the pricing page says openstatus will never stop your monitors or status pages without notice. If you approach or exceed a limit, the team reaches out to help you pick the right plan. Extra status pages and monitor packs are paid add-ons, billed per unit as listed on the pricing page.

## Gotchas

- Hobby has one monitor and one status page, so a second service needs a paid plan or an add-on.
- The 10-minute minimum check interval is slow for fast outage alerts. The Starter plan moves to 1-minute checks.
- The home page lists a custom domain under Starter, not under Hobby. New status pages get a subdomain on openstatus.dev from the slug you choose.

## Questions people ask

### Is openstatus free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free with one monitor, one status page, and 10-minute checks. No credit card is required, and you can also self-host the open source code.

### Does openstatus need a credit card?

No. The Hobby plan and the 14-day Starter trial both start without a card. If you add no card by the end of the trial, the workspace goes back to Hobby.

### What happens when I go over the openstatus free limits?

The pricing page says openstatus will not stop your monitors or status pages without notice. It will contact you to choose a plan that fits.
