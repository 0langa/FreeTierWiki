You add the Braintrust SDK for Python, TypeScript, Go, Java, Ruby, or C# to your app, and traces show up in the Braintrust web app. It suits AI teams that want to log, score, and compare model outputs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan has a $0 platform fee and needs no credit card. Each month it includes 1 GB of processed data, 10,000 scores, and $10 in model credits. Retention is 14 days. You get unlimited users, projects, datasets, playgrounds, and experiments.

Model credits pay for built-in models that you use without your own API key, and for AI features such as Topics. They do not roll over from month to month. On Starter, your organization needs an owner with a work email address, or a payment method on file, to use built-in models.

Starter has one human review score per project. Custom dashboards, environments, and dataset snapshots need the Pro plan.

## When you hit a limit

Above the included amounts, on-demand rates apply: $4 per extra GB of processed data and $2.50 per extra 1,000 scores. On Starter, if you use up the model credits and have not turned on on-demand usage, Topics and built-in model use pause until the next monthly cycle or until you add a card.

Pro costs $249 per month. It includes 5 GB of processed data, 50,000 scores, 30-day retention, and $100 in model credits. The pricing page also lists 6 to 12 months of free Pro for qualifying startups.

## Gotchas

- Starter keeps data for 14 days. Longer retention comes with Pro.
- Calling built-in models directly through the gateway needs a Pro or Enterprise plan.
- Legacy plans: if you signed up before March 16, 2026, you may keep access to features you already created.

## Questions people ask

### Is Braintrust free?

Yes. The Starter plan has a $0 platform fee, needs no credit card, and includes 1 GB of processed data, 10,000 scores, and $10 in model credits each month.

### What happens when I go over the Braintrust free limits?

On-demand rates apply: $4 per extra GB and $2.50 per extra 1,000 scores. Built-in model use pauses if you run out of credits and on-demand usage is off.

### How long does Braintrust keep my data on the free plan?

Starter keeps data for 14 days. Pro keeps it for 30 days.
