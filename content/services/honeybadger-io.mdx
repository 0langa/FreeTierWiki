You add a client library to your app, such as the Ruby gem or the JavaScript package, and view errors, logs, and uptime results in the web dashboard. It suits solo developers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free forever and is built for solo developers. It includes 5,000 errors per month, 50 MB per day of logging and performance data, and 1 uptime monitor. You can create unlimited projects, but the plan has 1 user.

Error data is kept for 15 days on Developer, while the Team plan keeps it for 90 days. The pricing page also describes uptime monitoring that covers your tasks and cron jobs, and a status page feature. Both appear in the plan comparison, so check the table before you plan around them on Developer.

## When you hit a limit

When you pass your monthly error limit, Honeybadger keeps processing up to 125% of the limit. It then stops processing until the end of the month. It sends emails along the way, and you can set your own traffic limits for each project.

Logging quotas work differently. The quota is daily, and Honeybadger does not stop processing data when you go over it. It prompts you to upgrade if you keep exceeding it. Overage billing is an option you switch on for Team and Business plans.

## Gotchas

- New accounts start with a 30-day trial that has unlimited users. If you choose no plan when it ends, monitoring pauses until you log in and pick one.
- The free plan has 1 user, so collaboration needs the Team plan at $26 per month.
- Error data is kept for only 15 days, against 90 days on Team.

## Questions people ask

### Is Honeybadger free?

Yes. The Developer plan costs $0 and is free forever. It has 5,000 errors per month, 50 MB per day of logging, and 1 uptime monitor for one user.

### What happens when I go over my Honeybadger error limit?

Honeybadger processes errors up to 125% of your limit and then stops until the end of the month. It emails you along the way.

### Does Honeybadger need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the 30-day trial needs no credit card. You can switch to the free Developer plan at any time.
