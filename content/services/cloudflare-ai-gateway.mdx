You send your model requests to an AI Gateway endpoint instead of the provider, using a REST call or your Workers code. It suits teams that call several model providers and want caching, limits, and logs in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

AI Gateway is available on all plans. The pricing page says the core features are free: dashboard analytics, caching, and rate limiting. All you need is a Cloudflare account. The Free plan allows 10 gateways per account. Data Loss Prevention scanning is free too, with two predefined profiles: Financial Information and national identifier numbers.

Logs depend on when you made your first gateway. New customers whose first gateway is dated on or after September 24, 2026 follow Workers Logs rules. On Workers Free that means 200,000 log events per day with 3 days of retention. Older customers use Legacy Logs, which store 100,000 logs in total across all gateways on Workers Free.

You still pay each model provider for the calls.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a hard stop for the core features. For logs, the rules differ by customer type. With Legacy Logs, once you reach the storage limit, the service can stop saving logs or delete the oldest ones, if you turn on automatic deletion.

Cloudflare says some new features may be part of a premium plan. The Unified Billing rate limit is 200 requests per 60 seconds per gateway, and a 429 error comes back above it. That limit does not apply when you bring your own provider keys.

## Gotchas

- Cloudflare says some future features may be paid, and it will announce them.
- Workers Logs will move to Cloudflare Observability pricing starting December 1, 2026.
- Logs are on by default for each gateway. You can turn them off in settings or per request with a header.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare AI Gateway free?

Yes. The core features, dashboard analytics, caching, and rate limiting, are free on all plans. You still pay your model provider.

### How many logs does Cloudflare AI Gateway store for free?

New customers follow Workers Logs, with 200,000 log events per day and 3 days of retention on Workers Free. Older customers store 100,000 logs in total.

### Does Cloudflare AI Gateway need a credit card?

No card is listed. The pricing page says all it takes is a Cloudflare account and one line of code.
