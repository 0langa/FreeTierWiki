Sitelemetry gathers evidence about a website’s security posture and presents it in an audit. You can run checks on a project you control and inspect what the service observed. Its free offer covers a subset of the security modules, which is useful for an initial review of one site.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free tier lists 10 security scans each month, 10 of the provider’s 27 security modules, one project and one seat. The remote MCP server can run the security work within that allowance. You can use the account dashboard to review the results as well. Free workspaces also start with one audit credit in each of six areas: security, technical SEO, AI visibility, accessibility, performance and integrations. This is a starter allocation, separate from the recurring security scans; its expiry is not stated. Saved-project capacity does not prevent on-demand audits of additional verified sites within available allowances. Use the available checks to examine a particular concern, then review the underlying evidence against what your application actually serves.

## When you hit a limit

The official page lists a monthly scan allocation without detailing the exact response when you try another scan after using it up. Paid plans offer additional modules and scheduled monitoring. Free workspaces can instead buy one-time audit-credit packs. Each completed area uses one credit; credits cannot transfer between areas, failed areas are not charged, and up to three repeats of the same area/address within 15 minutes of a charged audit are free. Purchased credits expire after 12 months and use the earliest expiry first; security credits require a verified site. Before repeating a scan, make the intended site change; compare results with deployment history because reports reflect the target at scan time.

## Gotchas

- Active probes require verification and authorization. Use a target you control or have explicit permission to test. Recurring Free scans cover limited security checks; the other areas have starter credits. Full Audit orchestration, Search Console, scheduled monitoring and JSON/PDF exports require paid plans.
- A public promotion gives new paid subscriptions and Free upgrades 50% off the first three monthly payments or first annual year, and 50% off audit-credit packs, through November 30, 2026 at 23:59 UTC+3. Existing paid plan changes are excluded; subscriptions then renew at regular prices, and taxes are extra.
- Keep a record of a reported issue before making changes if you need to compare the evidence later.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sitelemetry free?

Its Free plan covers one site project and includes 10 security scans per month.

### Can I use an assistant to run those checks?

Remote MCP security access is included. Requests still use the Free plan’s scan allowance.

### Are SEO and accessibility audits included?

Free includes one starter audit credit in each of technical SEO and accessibility, alongside the other four audit areas. Further audits need available plan allowance or separately purchased credits.
