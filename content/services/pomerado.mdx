Pomerado exposes a website workflow as a hosted tool or API. A developer describes the task, checks the resulting integration, and calls it from an application or agent. That can fit a small workflow on an authorized site where the action is otherwise performed through the browser.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes 100 tool calls per day, ten new integrations per month, and one account per website. The homepage lists sign-in support, including handling two-factor steps. Test the intended workflow first with data you can safely use. An integration that reads a record is a suitable place to start because its output is easy to check. Once you understand what the call does, connect it to the application that needs the result. Keep separate counts for the integrations you create and the calls you make to existing ones. Saving credentials is optional: they can instead accompany a request. Pomerado retains hashes of sign-in identifiers to enforce the one-account-per-website limit. Its FAQ says task results are erased after delivery, or within sixty minutes if unread, so keep any output you need.

## When you hit a limit

A busy workflow can use up the daily calls while you still have room to create new integrations that month. The pricing page presents larger budgets on paid plans and does not describe the exact treatment of an excess free call. If a site changes in a way that affects a workflow, review the integration before invoking it repeatedly to diagnose a failure. Keeping sample inputs lets you retest the corrected workflow before reconnecting it to a schedule.

## Gotchas

- Check account permissions for the target website before configuring the integration.
- An account-per-site allowance matters if you have to separate activity for different teams or customers.
- Test workflows after changes to sign-in steps or navigation, particularly before approving actions that modify data.

## Questions people ask

### Does Pomerado have an ongoing free plan?

The homepage lists a recurring free plan with separate daily and monthly allowances.

### Can I use multiple accounts on the same site?

Free allows one account per website. The business offer lists support for more.

### What counts against the monthly allowance?

It counts new integrations. Calling a hosted tool uses the daily call allowance instead.
