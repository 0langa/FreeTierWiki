incident.io is an incident management tool that works inside Slack or Microsoft Teams. You declare an incident in chat, it opens a channel and tracks the response, and it adds on-call paging and status pages. It suits developer teams who already coordinate in chat and want structure when something breaks.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan is listed as Free and Forever, "everything you need to get started with incident management": Slack or Microsoft Teams native incident response, single team on-call, a status page, and essential incident automation.

The comparison table gives the numbers. Basic includes 1 on-call schedule, 1 custom field, 1 workflow, and 2 integrations. On status pages it includes 1 public page with unlimited page subscribers. Post-mortems use the basic editor, and the service catalog uses basic types. Slack and Microsoft Teams support is marked on every plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach a Basic limit; the limits are counts of schedules, workflows, and integrations rather than metered usage, so nothing is billed until you choose a paid plan. The Team plan is $15 per user per month, with on-call an extra $10 per user per month, and raises the limits to 2 schedules, 3 workflows, and unlimited integrations.

## Gotchas

- Only 2 integrations are included, so connecting several monitoring tools needs a paid plan.
- On-call is a per-user add-on on the paid plans, so the price per person rises when you add it.
- Multi-team on-call and alerting start on the Team plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is incident.io free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever and includes chat-based incident response, single-team on-call, and a status page.

### How many on-call schedules does Basic include?

One. The comparison table lists 1 schedule for Basic and 2 for the Team plan.

### Do status page subscribers have a limit on Basic?

No. The table lists unlimited page subscribers on every plan, with 1 public page on Basic.
