You create a Container Apps environment and a container app in the Azure portal, or with the Azure CLI, and point it at a container image. It suits teams that already package code as containers and want Azure to run them without managing Kubernetes. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## What the free plan gives you

The free grant belongs to the Consumption plan. Each Azure subscription gets the first 180,000 vCPU-seconds, the first 360,000 GiB-seconds, and the first 2 million HTTP requests free every calendar month. A vCPU-second is one second of one CPU core. A GiB-second is one second of one GiB of memory.

Free usage does not show on your bill. You only pay once your use passes these monthly grants. The pricing page says an app can scale to zero replicas when there are no requests or events, and no usage charges apply while it is scaled to zero. Container Apps jobs also cost nothing while no execution is running.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops your apps at the free amounts. After the grants are used, Azure bills you per second for the vCPU and memory your replicas have, plus the requests they handle. The pricing page and the billing docs describe no hard spending cap.

You can keep a minimum number of replicas always running. A replica that sits idle is charged at a reduced rate, and a replica is charged at the active rate while it starts, handles requests, or uses more than a small amount of CPU or network. Scaling to zero is the way to stop all usage charges.

## Gotchas

- The free grants cover the Consumption plan. The Dedicated plan bills for the instances in each workload profile.
- Your own virtual network or other Azure resources your apps use may add charges. Private endpoints and planned maintenance carry a Dedicated Plan Management charge, even on the Consumption plan.
- A minimum replica count above zero stops scale to zero, so idle charges can start even when nobody uses the app.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Container Apps free?

Partly. The Consumption plan includes 180,000 vCPU-seconds, 360,000 GiB-seconds, and 2 million requests free each month per subscription. You pay for use above that.

### Does Azure Container Apps scale to zero?

Yes, you can configure an app to scale to zero replicas when there are no requests or events. No usage charges apply while it is scaled to zero.

### What happens when I go over the Azure Container Apps free grant?

Azure keeps your apps running and bills the extra usage per second. The pricing page shows no hard cap.
