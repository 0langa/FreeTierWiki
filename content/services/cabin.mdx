Cabin is a privacy-first web analytics service. It uses no cookies and no fingerprinting, and it offers MCP access on every plan so an AI client can query your stats. It suits developers with personal projects, hobby sites, and portfolios.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is aimed at personal projects, hobby sites, and portfolios. It covers 1 domain with 10,000 pageviews a month, keeps data for 30 days, and includes data export and full MCP access. You do not need a credit card: the FAQ says you sign up and start tracking in under a minute, and add payment details only if you upgrade.

Cabin says it is cookieless and GDPR, CCPA, and ePrivacy compliant by design, so no consent banner is required. MCP access works with clients such as Claude, Cursor, and ChatGPT. Every plan can share a public dashboard link that anyone can open without an account, and data is stored in the EU.

## When you hit a limit

Each plan includes a monthly pageview allowance: 10k on Free, 100k on Plus, and 5M on Scale. Cabin's FAQ says you can upgrade at any time as you grow, and if you go over, it will never stop recording your traffic.

The page does not say whether going over leads to an upgrade request, a locked dashboard, or anything else. With no card on the Free plan, there is nothing to charge automatically. Plus costs $14 a month for up to 10 domains, 100,000 pageviews, and 12 months of retention.

## Gotchas

- Free data retention is 30 days, so month-over-month comparisons need exports.
- The Free plan covers a single domain; a second site means Plus.
- Sharing a site with other Cabin users as viewers or managers starts on Plus; Free gets the public link only.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cabin free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 for 1 domain and 10,000 pageviews a month.

### Do I need a credit card for Cabin?

No. Cabin says the Free plan needs no credit card.

### What happens if I go over 10,000 pageviews?

Cabin says it will never stop recording your traffic, and you can upgrade whenever you need to.
