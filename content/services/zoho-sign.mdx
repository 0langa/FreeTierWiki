You use Zoho Sign in a web app, plus mobile and desktop apps. You upload a document, add fields and signers, and send it. Signers get an email link and do not need an account.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euros and is for a single user. It includes 5 envelopes per month. An envelope is one signing request. It can hold several documents and go to several recipients. The free plan includes signature workflows, reminders and notifications, audit trails, import and export with cloud storage, and mobile and desktop apps. You can sign with Zoho DSC.

Standard costs 10 euros per user per month when billed annually. It gives you 25 envelopes per user and month, approval workflows, and SMS delivery. Annual billing saves 20%. New accounts get a 14-day trial of the Enterprise plan with no card needed. The trial allows up to 10 users and 50 signed documents, and documents signed during it carry a watermark.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe what happens after your fifth free envelope. The plan limits you to 5 envelopes per month, so plan to wait or upgrade. After the 14-day trial ends without an upgrade, your account moves to the free plan and keeps all your data. The pages do not mention a card for the free plan, and the trial needs none.

## Gotchas

- Envelopes sent through the API or third-party apps always use Zoho Sign credits, whatever your plan. Credits are a paid add-on.
- The free plan is for one user only, so you cannot add teammates.
- The pricing page we read was the German version, so check the plan names and prices on your own region's page.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Sign free?

Yes. The Free plan gives one user 5 envelopes per month with signature workflows, reminders, and audit trails.

### Do my signers need a Zoho account?

No. Signers do not need an account or a subscription. They get an email with a signing link.

### What happens after the Zoho Sign 14-day trial?

If you do not upgrade, your account switches to the free plan and keeps all your data. The trial needs no credit card.
