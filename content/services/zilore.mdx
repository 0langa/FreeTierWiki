Zilore is a managed DNS provider. You host your domains' zones on its authoritative servers and manage records in a web interface or through its API, with extras such as snapshots, templates, and web and mail forwarding on paid plans.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and covers 5 domains. In the plan table, the Free column lists resource records as "1 000/domain" and DNS queries as "10 000 000", which reads as one thousand records per domain and ten million queries. The table does not say whether the query figure is per month.

The DNS page describes instant updates across Zilore's authoritative servers, ALIAS records that act like a CNAME at the apex of a domain, support for national and new top-level domains, and statistics on DNS requests. Free is the only plan you get with the GET button; the paid plans start with a trial.

## When you hit a limit

The plan table gives caps but the page does not say what happens when you reach them. It also contradicts itself: the feature text says all plans include an unlimited number of DNS records and requests, while the Free column shows fixed numbers. Plan around the lower figures.

To grow, the Personal plan costs $5 a month or $50 a year, and extra domains are priced for paid plans only. Personal and Professional have a 14-day free trial that needs an active debit or credit card, and payment is taken automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel.

## Gotchas

- The Free column and the feature text disagree on record and query limits, so check your usage in the statistics.
- Starting a paid trial puts your card on file and bills automatically at the end of the 14 days.
- Paid subscriptions are not refunded for the current period when you cancel.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zilore DNS free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and covers 5 domains.

### Does the Zilore free trial need a credit card?

The trials of the Personal and Professional plans need an active debit or credit card. The Free plan has no trial; you choose GET.

### Does Zilore support ALIAS records?

Yes. Zilore offers an ALIAS record that lets you use a CNAME-like record at the apex of a domain.
