You add sites to a dashboard in the MonitorMonk web app and read the results there or share them. It suits solo developers and small teams with a handful of sites.

## What the free plan gives you

The Forever Free plan costs 0 euro per month and needs no credit card. It allows up to 2 dashboards and up to 10 websites. The checks include SSL checks, keyword checks, HTTP status checks, and response time checks. The page says every plan, free or paid, includes all features.

MonitorMonk displays site status on status pages and lets you share dashboards with clients or team members by link or invitation. The page footer says you can get 10 monitors with 1-minute checks on a visual dashboard for free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you go past 2 dashboards or 10 websites. It lists higher limits only on paid plans.

Premium small costs 15 euro per month with up to 10 dashboards, up to 80 websites, and a service-level agreement. Premium large costs 100 euro per month with unlimited dashboards, up to 1000 websites, and premium support. You can upgrade or cancel at any time.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has no SLA, so you cannot rely on MonitorMonk to promise uptime for itself.
- The pricing page does not name the alert channels, such as email or chat, so check the app after you sign up.
- There is no separate docs site on the pricing page, so setup help comes from the app.
