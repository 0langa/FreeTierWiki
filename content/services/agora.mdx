Agora sells SDKs and a global network for real-time voice, video, and live streaming, which it groups as Agora RTC. It also offers signaling, chat, recording, and conversational AI products, each with its own price.

## What the free plan gives you

The first 10,000 combined RTC minutes are free every month, and the Agora RTC pricing page says no credit card is required. The allowance is shared across video calling, voice calling, and live streaming. The overview page also lists recording, AI noise suppression, and 3D spatial audio as included in the same 10,000 free minutes.

Other products have separate monthly free amounts. Agora Signaling gives the first 1 million messages free every month, Agora Chat covers the first 500 monthly users, and real-time speech to text and translation share the first 300 language services minutes free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing pages do not say that calls stop at 10,000 minutes. Past the free block, minutes are billed per 1,000 participant minutes: the RTC page lists $0.99 for voice, $3.99 for Video HD, and $8.99 for Video Full HD.

Volume discounts apply as monthly usage grows, starting at 10% for the 100K to 500K band. Prepaid monthly plans with discounted rates are also offered. The page does not describe a spending cap, so check usage in the console while you test.

## Gotchas

- Minutes are charged per participant, so a four-person video call uses four minutes of allowance for every minute it runs.
- Video price depends on resolution, so the free minutes stretch further for voice than for HD video.
- The Cloud Proxy feature, which forces traffic over UDP or TCP 443, carries a minimum monthly base fee that starts at $500.

## Questions people ask

### How many free minutes does Agora give?

The first 10,000 combined RTC minutes are free every month. They are shared across voice, video, and live streaming.

### Do I need a credit card for Agora's free minutes?

No. The Agora RTC pricing page says the 10,000 free minutes every month need no credit card.

### Is HD video priced differently?

Yes. Past the free minutes, Video HD is $3.99 and Video Full HD is $8.99 per 1,000 participant minutes, while voice is $0.99.
