You use Webvizio in a web app and a Chrome extension. You paste a page URL, then you or your clients pin feedback on the live page. It suits web agencies, QA teams, and freelancers who collect feedback from clients.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. The site offers a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. The pricing page says the trial gives you access to the Advanced plan, which supports up to 100 projects and 20 users.

The pricing page lists four paid plans, and it says a subscription is required to continue after the trial. Solo costs $8 per month for a personal workspace. Starter costs $28 per month for a team workspace and includes up to 10 projects and 5 users. Advanced supports up to 100 projects and 20 users, and Enterprise has unlimited projects. Guests and viewers are free and do not need a seat, on Starter and above.

## When you hit a limit

The trial lasts 7 days. The pricing page does not say what happens to your projects when it ends, only that a subscription is required to continue. Export or note what you need before day 7.

After you subscribe, adding users beyond your plan costs a small prorated fee for the rest of the billing period. If you need more projects, you upgrade your plan. On Advanced, you can archive finished projects to free up active slots. Nothing is charged during the trial.

## Gotchas

- The free offer is a trial, not a free plan. Webvizio's own tools, such as the AI prompt generator, are free but are not the product plan.
- Guest and Viewer roles are available only on the Starter plan and above.
- Only Owner, Project Manager, Assignee, and Client roles need a paid seat.

## Questions people ask

### Is Webvizio free?

Only as a trial. You get a 7-day free trial with no credit card. After that, plans start at $8 per month.

### Does Webvizio need a credit card for the trial?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required until you decide to upgrade.

### Are guests free in Webvizio?

Yes. Guests and viewers do not count toward your seats. These roles are available on Starter and above.
