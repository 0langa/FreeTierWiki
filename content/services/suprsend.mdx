You build notification workflows in a web dashboard and trigger them from your code through an HTTPS API or a backend SDK. A client SDK covers the frontend. It suits developers and product teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and asks for no credit card. It includes 10,000 notifications per month, unlimited channels, unlimited team members, lists and broadcasts, and branching and conditions in workflows. The comparison table shows 30 days of log retention.

The in-app notification feed carries SuprSend branding on this plan. The paid plans remove it.

New accounts unlock all features for a 30 day trial. After that, the account moves to the Free plan.

Billing counts notifications, not API requests. A bulk request that reaches many users counts each user separately, while a digest counts as one.

## When you hit a limit

SuprSend does not stop sending when you pass the free limit. The FAQ says it alerts and contacts you if you go over. You can then upgrade or ask for custom pricing.

The Essentials plan costs $100 per month billed annually and includes 50k notifications. Extra notifications on it cost $2 per 1,000. The pricing page does not say how long the free plan keeps sending after you pass the limit, so watch your usage in the dashboard.

## Gotchas

- SuprSend does not send the mail or SMS itself. You bring your own provider accounts, such as Twilio, Sendgrid, or Mailjet, and pay them directly.
- Group alerts that arrive in a batch or digest count as one notification, but a bulk send to many users counts each user.
- A workflow request is accepted when the API call is correct. Check the Requests tab under Logs in the dashboard to confirm delivery.

## Questions people ask

### Is SuprSend free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month, includes 10,000 notifications per month, and needs no credit card.

### What happens if I go over the SuprSend free limit?

SuprSend keeps processing and sending your notifications. It alerts you, and you can upgrade or discuss custom pricing.

### Do I still pay for Twilio or Mailjet with SuprSend?

Yes. You bring your own provider accounts and pay those providers directly for what you use.
