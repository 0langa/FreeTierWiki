Emailvalidation.io is an API that tells you whether an email address is valid before you send to it. It also offers an email finder, bulk processing, and an MCP server so AI agents can check addresses.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and is described as for testing and personal projects. It includes 100 credits every month with no credit card, 10 requests per minute, and 1 API key. The FAQ says the free plan includes the email validator, the email finder, the LinkedIn email finder, and bulk processing.

Credits are spent per task. An email validation uses 1 credit, so the free plan covers 100 checks a month. An email search and a LinkedIn email search each use 10 credits, which means a free account can run only 10 searches a month if it does nothing else.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page counts credits per month but does not say what the API returns when your 100 credits are used up. It also caps the free plan at 10 requests per minute, while the paid plans list no per-minute limit.

The next plan up is Small at $9.99 a month for 5,000 credits. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time, and yearly billing gives 12 months of credits for the price of 10. Prices exclude VAT.

## Gotchas

- Searches cost 10 credits each, so the email finder uses up the free amount ten times faster than validation.
- The 10 requests per minute cap can slow a bulk check of a list, even when you have credits left.
- Keep the API key on your server; with only 1 key on the free plan, a leaked key means rotating the one key your app uses.
