Emailvalidation.io is an API that tells you whether an email address is valid before you send to it. It also offers an email finder, bulk processing, and an MCP server so AI agents can check addresses.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and is described as for testing and personal projects. It includes 100 credits every month with no credit card, 10 requests per minute, and unlimited API keys. The FAQ says the free plan includes the email validator, the email finder, the LinkedIn email finder, and bulk processing.

Credits are spent per task. A definitive validation uses 1 credit, so the grant covers 100 deliverable or undeliverable results monthly. Invalid-format results and some unknown results are free under fair-use rules. An email search and a LinkedIn email search each use 10 credits, which means a free account can run only 10 searches a month if it does nothing else.

## When you hit a limit

Monthly quota exhaustion returns HTTP 429 until the end of the month or upgrade. Free has no overage. Minute-limit exhaustion also returns 429 with Retry-After; wait until the next minute. A separate five-validation concurrency cap returns concurrency_limit_exceeded without consuming quota. Paid plans allow 25 concurrent validations and have no minute limit.

The next plan up is Small at $9.99 a month for 5,000 credits. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time, and yearly billing gives 12 months of credits for the price of 10. Prices exclude VAT.

## Gotchas

- Searches cost 10 credits each, so the email finder uses up the free amount ten times faster than validation.
- Unknown-result retries within an hour consume quota. Other unknown results have fair-use allowances; inspect X-Cost rather than assuming every unsuccessful validation is free.
- Keep the API key on your server. Every plan has unlimited API keys, so you can create a new key if one leaks.

## Questions people ask

### Is Emailvalidation.io free?

Yes, within limits. The free plan includes 100 credits every month, and one validation uses 1 credit.

### Do I need a credit card for the free plan?

No. The pricing FAQ says the free plan needs no credit card.

### How many requests per minute does the free plan allow?

The free plan allows 10 requests per minute. The paid plans list no per-minute limit.
