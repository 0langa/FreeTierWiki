You work in the Supabase dashboard with the Table Editor and SQL Editor, call the database through the auto-generated API and client libraries, or connect with any Postgres tool. It suits builders who want a full Postgres database without running a server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month. Each project gets a 500 MB database on shared CPU with 500 MB of RAM. The plan also includes 1 GB of file storage, 5 GB of egress, 50,000 monthly active users, and unlimited API requests. Support comes from the community.

You can have 2 active free projects. Paused projects do not count toward that quota, and Supabase does not charge for them. The billing FAQ adds that the free project count includes projects owned by organization members with the Owner or Admin role.

## When you hit a limit

Supabase notifies you when you exceed the Free quota. If you keep exceeding it, the Fair Use Policy applies service restrictions, such as read-only databases or a 402 status on API requests. You get a grace period first, and restrictions lift when the quota refills at the start of the next billing cycle.

Inactivity is a separate limit. Free projects are paused after 1 week of inactivity. The Pro plan starts at $25 a month and adds daily backups kept for 7 days.

## Gotchas

- A free project pauses after 1 week without activity, so a demo you share later may be asleep.
- Free projects have no daily backups. Those start on the Pro plan, so export your own dumps.
- The 2 project limit counts all active free projects across organizations where you are an Owner or Admin.

## Questions people ask

### Is Supabase database free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes a 500 MB Postgres database per project, with up to 2 active projects. It also includes 1 GB of file storage and 50,000 monthly active users.

### Does the Supabase free tier pause?

Yes. Supabase pauses free projects after 1 week of inactivity. Paused projects do not count toward the 2 active project limit.

### What happens when I exceed the Supabase free quota?

Supabase notifies you first. If you keep going over, service restrictions apply, such as read-only databases. The restrictions lift when your quota refills.
