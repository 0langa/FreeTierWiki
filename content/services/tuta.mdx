You can use Tuta in a browser, in a desktop client, or in the phone apps. The free plan suits people who want one private address, without custom domains or aliases.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan gives you 1 GB of storage, 1 calendar, and 3 labels. The pricing table lists extra email addresses and custom domains as not included. It also lists unlimited search, calendar sharing, and event invites as paid features.

Tuta stores all data on its own servers in ISO 27001 certified data centers in Germany. The address book and the calendar are fully encrypted on every plan. The Revolutionary plan raises storage to 20 GB and adds 30 extra email addresses, while the Legend plan gives 500 GB. Revolutionary costs €3 per month and Legend costs €8 per month, according to Tuta's own setup guide.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 1 GB mailbox fills up. The Free plan has fixed limits and no overage price, so the next step is a paid plan.

Inactivity is a bigger limit. Tuta deletes free accounts if you do not log in for more than six months. The address is then blocked for new registrations, and you can re-use it only with a paid account and the correct credentials.

## Gotchas

- Log in at least once every six months, or Tuta deletes the free account and keeps its address blocked.
- Email import into Tuta is limited to the Legend and Unlimited plans, so moving a large old mailbox needs a paid plan.
- If you forget the password and lose the recovery code, Tuta cannot reset it for you. The support page says only you can reset your password.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tuta free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you an encrypted mailbox with 1 GB of storage, 1 calendar, and 3 labels. The plan costs nothing.

### Does Tuta delete inactive free accounts?

Yes. Free accounts are deleted if you do not log in for more than six months. Tuta does not recycle the address.

### Can I use my own domain with free Tuta?

No. The pricing table shows custom domains as not included on the Free plan. Revolutionary and Legend plans support them.
