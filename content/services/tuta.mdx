You can use Tuta in a browser, in a desktop client, or in the phone apps. The free plan suits people who want one private address, without custom domains or aliases.

## What the free plan gives you

Free includes 1 GB storage, 1 calendar and 3 labels. Extra addresses/custom domains, unlimited search, calendar sharing and invitations require paid service. All plans encrypt address books/calendars on Tuta-owned servers in ISO 27001-certified German data centers.

Revolutionary includes 20 GB and 15 extra addresses; Legend includes 500 GB. Setup guidance lists regular annual-equivalent prices of €3/month and €8/month respectively. On November 23, pricing and Black Friday pages advertised 24 months for the price of 12 for new yearly Revolutionary/Legend subscriptions via desktop/web. The promotion leaves Free unchanged and publishes no end date.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 1 GB mailbox fills up. The Free plan has fixed limits and no published overage price; the exact exhaustion response and signup card requirement are not established.

Inactivity is a bigger limit. Tuta’s terms allow termination after at least six months of inactivity. Its support page describes deletion after more than six months without a login. The address is then blocked for new registrations, and you can re-use it only with a paid account and the correct credentials. Deleted mailbox data cannot be restored; accounts that had two-factor authentication also need the recovery code to reclaim the address.

## Gotchas

- Free is offered for private users, with one free account per person. Log in before six months have passed. Tuta keeps deleted addresses blocked.
- Email import into Tuta is limited to the Legend and Unlimited plans, so moving a large old mailbox needs a paid plan.
- Tuta's support page says its password reset feature lets only you reset your password, and Tuta has no influence on that process.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tuta free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you an encrypted mailbox with 1 GB of storage, 1 calendar, and 3 labels. The plan costs nothing.

### Does Tuta delete inactive free accounts?

Yes. The terms allow termination after at least six months of inactivity, and Tuta does not recycle the address.

### Can I use my own domain with free Tuta?

No. The pricing table shows custom domains as not included on the Free plan. Revolutionary and Legend plans support them.
