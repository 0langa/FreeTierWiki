Sitesure is a monitoring service for websites, API endpoints, TCP services, and background jobs. It checks endpoints from around the world and listens for check-ins from scheduled jobs, alerting you when a response is wrong or a job goes quiet. It suits developers with a small number of things to watch.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free plan with 2 monitors and all features. The home page says your first two monitors are free, no strings attached.

All features on the paid plans include uptime monitoring, IPv4 and IPv6 monitoring, scheduled task monitoring, heartbeat monitoring, SSL certificate monitoring, DNS monitoring, and domain monitoring, plus unlimited users, configurable notifications, email reports, and 1 year of history. Uptime checks run through the whole network stack from the DNS query through SSL negotiation to reading the response. Status pages are offered too.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to add a third monitor on the Free plan. Paid plans are priced by included monitors: Petite costs $20 a month for 5 monitors, with additional monitors at $2 a month each. Every paid plan starts with a 30 day, no-limits free trial. Larger plans lower the price of each extra monitor, down to $0.75 a month on Prime, which includes 150 monitors.

## Gotchas

- Two monitors run out quickly if you watch both a site and its API.
- The pricing page does not list check intervals or alert channels for the Free plan.
- The jump from 2 free monitors to the first paid plan is $20 a month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sitesure free?

Yes, for 2 monitors. The Free plan includes all features.

### What can a Sitesure monitor watch?

Websites, API endpoints, TCP services, cron jobs, and heartbeats, plus SSL certificates, DNS, and domains.

### Is there a trial of the paid plans?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 30-day, no-limits free trial.
