TomTom's developer platform offers map display, routing, places search, geocoding, and traffic APIs, plus SDKs. Pricing is usage-based per API, and each API starts with a free monthly allowance. Developers use it for maps, routes with live traffic, and address search without a contract.

## What the free plan gives you

TomTom gives free monthly requests per API with no credit card needed. Map Display vector and raster tiles include 200K requests a month, and traffic flow and incident tiles include 200K. The Routing API includes 20K a month, Matrix Routing 2.5K, and Snap to Roads 2.5K. Geocoding and Reverse Geocoding include 20K each, Places Search Suggest 10K, and Places Search Details 5K. TomTom says the free tier has no time limit, and the allowance resets at the start of every billing cycle. It is shared across your whole account, so multiple API keys draw from the same pool.

## When you hit a limit

On the free tier, once you have used your monthly allowance for an API, further requests are blocked until your next billing cycle begins. If you add prepaid credit, requests keep flowing and the credit is charged at the applicable rate. When the credit runs out, requests above the free tier stop until you top up. With recurring payments enabled, your balance tops up automatically when it runs low, so turn that off in your dashboard if you want a hard limit on spending.

## Gotchas

- Allowances differ a lot between APIs, from 200K tile requests to 2.5K matrix routing requests.
- Some products have no free monthly tier; they may be evaluated for up to 30 days, but commercial use needs a custom contract.
- Recurring payments on prepaid credit remove the hard stop until you switch them off.

## Questions people ask

### Is TomTom's API free?

Yes, within monthly allowances per API, such as 20K routing requests, with no credit card needed.

### What happens when I hit TomTom's monthly limit?

On the free tier, requests to that API are blocked until your next billing cycle begins, so there are no surprise charges.

### Does the TomTom free tier expire?

No. TomTom says free means free, with no time limit and no credit card required.
