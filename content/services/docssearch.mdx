You apply through the Algolia dashboard, let a crawler index your site, and then drop a front-end package or a framework integration into your pages. It suits open source maintainers and documentation teams who want search without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

DocSearch is a free program from Algolia for public technical documentation and technical blogs. Once your site is approved, you get a managed Algolia Crawler that indexes your docs on a schedule, plus DocSearch v5 packages that show keyword search or Ask AI in your front end. The site says more than 9,000 projects use it.

The plan terms allow 50M search requests and 5M crawled resources per month, plus 5M records, 25GB per application/index, 100KB per record, 20 indices and one application. The one rule you act on straight away is domain ownership: you must verify it within 7 days of approval to keep using the crawler. You also keep the "Search by Algolia" link on your site.

## When you hit a limit

The plan terms permit Algolia to degrade or suspend an application that reaches its usage or service limits until usage is reduced. Approved sites receive up to 50M search requests and 5M crawled resources per month, plus 5M records. Paid add-ons require a separate agreement and payment information.

The plan has no support commitment. Algolia may remove data after more than 30 days of account inactivity. You must maintain eligibility, use domains you control, verify ownership within 7 days and keep the required visible Algolia attribution.

## Gotchas

- Only public technical documentation and technical blogs qualify; sites with non-technical content or that are not production ready are usually turned down. The pages do not say how private docs are treated.
- The automated check comes first, and a manual review adds one to two business days when the check is unclear.
- The crawler and the front end are separate parts, so you pick your front-end package or framework integration after your content is indexed.

## Questions people ask

### Is DocSearch free?

Yes, for eligible sites. DocSearch is free for public technical documentation and technical blogs that Algolia approves. You also keep the Search by Algolia link on your site.

### How long does DocSearch approval take?

The application starts with an automated check. If it cannot decide, a person reviews your site, and that takes one to two business days.

### Does DocSearch need a credit card?

The public DocSearch signup page explicitly says no credit card is required. Paid add-ons require separate agreement and payment information.
