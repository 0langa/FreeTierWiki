DB Designer offers a free, browser-based environment for visually creating and sharing database schemas, ideal for learning and prototyping. The free Starter plan is capped at 2 database models and 10 tables per model, but includes every feature the paid plans have — students, non-profits, and open-source contributors can get unlimited models for free.
