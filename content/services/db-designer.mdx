You design in the browser by adding tables and fields to a canvas, then export SQL, PNG, or PDF. It suits learners and small teams who want to plan a schema before they build.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free. It includes 2 database models with 10 tables per model. The pricing page says all plans, including Free, come with all features. That covers inviting teammates by email, who get Owner, Editor, or Viewer access, and exporting your work.

Paid plans only raise the size limits. Basic costs $9 per month for 5 models and 25 tables per model. Unlimited costs $17 per month. Academic, non-profit, and open-source contributor plans are free with unlimited models and tables.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say how the editor behaves when you pass 2 models or 10 tables in a model. You upgrade to Basic or Unlimited to raise the limits, or ask for one of the free plans if you are a student, educator, non-profit, or open-source contributor.

If you cancel a paid plan, you will not lose your projects or data. You fall back to the Free plan limits on your next billing date.

## Gotchas

- Free users can run only 2 models, so a third project needs an upgrade or deleting an older model.
- Each model on the free plan can hold at most 10 tables.
- The free academic, non-profit, and open-source plans need you to belong to those groups.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [DrawDB](/services/drawdb/): when you want a free, open-source editor that runs in the browser without an account.
- [EverSQL](/services/eversql/): when you want to tune SQL queries instead of designing a schema.
- [Cloudflare D1](/services/cloudflare-d1/): when you want a small SQL database to put your schema into.

## Questions people ask

### Is DB Designer free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes 2 database models with 10 tables per model. It has the same features as the paid plans.

### Are students and open-source contributors charged for DB Designer?

No. Academic, non-profit, and open-source contributor plans are free, with unlimited models and tables.

### Can I invite my team on the DB Designer free plan?

Yes. All plans have full access to all features, so you can add teammates by email and set their access level.
