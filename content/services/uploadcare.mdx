You add the File Uploader web component to your page with npm or a CDN script, or use the React uploader package. Uploadcare then handles uploads, storage, processing, and delivery. It suits apps that take user files.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is built for small-scale projects. It includes 1,000 operations per month, 5 GB of traffic per month, and 1 GB of storage.

Operations are actions tied to your files and API use. Uploading a file, making requests, and asking for image transformations all count as operations, and different actions cost different amounts. Traffic means data moving from the Uploadcare CDN to your users, and between the CDN and Uploadcare's servers.

Storage is the total size of the original files in your account. It is cumulative, so the counter does not reset at the start of a new billing cycle.

## When you hit a limit

Uploadcare says exceeding the Free plan limits results in a temporary suspension until the start of the next month. The service does not bill you on Free.

Paid plans raise the limits and let you go past them, with extra usage billed. Pro costs $66 per month with 5,000 operations. The Business plan costs $166 per month with 50,000 operations. Uploadcare calls its paid limits soft, so overage fees apply automatically.

## Gotchas

- Video encoding costs many operations. A short video in several sizes can use up a large part of the 1,000 free operations.
- Storage does not reset each month. Delete files you no longer need, or you may hit the 1 GB ceiling.
- Uploadcare accepts major credit and debit cards on paid plans, but not PayPal or Bitcoin.

## Questions people ask

### Is Uploadcare free?

Yes, for small projects. The Free plan includes 1,000 operations per month, 5 GB of traffic per month, and 1 GB of storage.

### What happens when I reach the Uploadcare free limit?

Uploadcare temporarily suspends the account until the start of the next month. Paid plans let you keep going and bill the extra usage.

### Does Uploadcare need a credit card?

The entry records that the Free plan needs no card. Paid plans accept major credit and debit cards.
