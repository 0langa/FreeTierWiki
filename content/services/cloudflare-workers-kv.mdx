You use Workers KV from a Worker through a binding, from the Wrangler CLI, from the dashboard, or over the REST API. It suits Cloudflare developers who read small values often.

## What the free plan gives you

Workers KV is included in the Workers Free plan. Each day you get 100,000 key reads, 1,000 key writes, 1,000 key deletes, and 1,000 list requests. Storage is 1 GB per account and per namespace. You can create 1,000 namespaces per account.

A key can be up to 512 bytes, and a value can be up to 25 MiB. A Worker invocation can make up to 1000 operations. All daily limits reset at 00:00 UTC. KV does not charge for data transfer.

## When you hit a limit

If you pass any one of the daily limits, further operations of that type fail with an error. The Free plan does not bill you, so a traffic spike causes errors rather than a charge. Your Worker should handle a failed read or write without crashing. Limits reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Moving to the Workers Paid plan removes the daily limits and charges per operation beyond the included amount.

## Gotchas

- Operations from the dashboard or Wrangler count as usage, including updating, deleting, and listing keys.
- Reads of keys that do not exist still count, even when they return a null or a 404.
- Writes to the same key are limited to 1 per second, and data is eventually consistent.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Workers KV free?

Yes, on the Workers Free plan. You get 1 GB of storage, 100,000 reads per day, and 1,000 writes, deletes, and list requests per day.

### What happens when I hit the Workers KV free limits?

Further operations of that type fail with an error until the daily reset at 00:00 UTC. You are not billed on the Free plan.

### Is Workers KV strongly consistent?

No. It is eventually consistent, and writes to the same key are limited to 1 per second.
