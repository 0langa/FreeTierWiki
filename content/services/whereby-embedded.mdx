Whereby Embedded is the developer product from Whereby, a browser video meeting service. Instead of building call UI yourself, you create rooms through its API and embed them in your own website or app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Explore plan costs $0, and Whereby labels it as a way to test the API. It includes up to 2,000 participant minutes a month. A participant minute is one minute for each person in a room, so a three-person call uses three minutes of allowance for every minute it runs.

The free plan has no premium features, no support SLA, and limited white labelling, so the rooms still carry some Whereby branding. It is enough to build an integration, run demos, and host a modest number of real calls each month.

## When you hit a limit

The Explore plan lists no additional participant minutes, so it does not bill past 2,000 a month; the pricing page does not say exactly what users see once the minutes are gone. To keep going you move to the Build plan.

Build costs $10.99 per month, includes 2,000 participant minutes a month, and charges for extra participant minutes on top. It adds custom branding, session transcriptions from $0.0065 per unmuted participant minute, and a HIPAA compliance option at $16.99 a month.

## Gotchas

- Participant minutes multiply by the number of people in the room, so group calls use the free allowance quickly.
- Limited white labelling on Explore means you cannot fully brand the rooms as your own.
- The pricing page shows two different per-minute overage figures for Build, so confirm the current rate in the dashboard before relying on it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Whereby Embedded free?

Yes, on the Explore plan, which includes up to 2,000 participant minutes a month for testing the API.

### Can I buy more minutes on the free plan?

No. Explore lists no additional participant minutes; you need the Build plan at $10.99 per month for more.

### Can I remove Whereby branding for free?

No. The free plan has limited white labelling, and custom branding comes with the Build plan.
