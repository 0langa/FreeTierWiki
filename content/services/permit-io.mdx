You set up roles, resources, and users in the Permit dashboard, or through the API and Terraform. Your backend then calls an SDK function such as `permit.check()` to ask if a user may do an action. It suits developers who want to stop hand-writing permission code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Community plan is labeled free forever. It allows 1,000 monthly active users and 20 tenants. You can define 25 roles, 50 resource types, and 5 condition sets, with 2,000 role assignments, 2,000 resource instances, and 2,000 relationship tuples. You also get 20 groups and 3 environments.

Authorization queries, projects, and PDP instances have no limit. The PDP is the service that answers permission checks, and you can run it yourself. The plan supports RBAC, ABAC, ReBAC, and PBAC through the UI, API, SDK, or infrastructure-as-code. Audit logs are kept for 14 days.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass a Community limit, such as 1,000 monthly active users. It lists the Enterprise plan as the step above, with no limits on active users and tenants. Pricing for that plan is by contact. The pricing page labels Community as free forever and shows no payment step for it. Cloud uptime and the support SLA are listed as best effort.

## Gotchas

- Many limits count objects you create, not only users. Role assignments, resource instances, and relationship tuples each stop at 2,000.
- Only a single deployment region is listed for the Community plan.
- Compliance support, such as the SOC 2 report and HIPAA BAA, and on-prem or multi-cloud deployment are listed under Enterprise. The page does not say which plans include SSO.

## Questions people ask

### Is Permit.io free?

Yes. The Community plan is free forever and covers up to 1,000 monthly active users and 20 tenants.

### Does Permit.io need a credit card?

The pricing page shows no card step for the Community plan. You sign up at app.permit.io with a Google, GitHub, or Microsoft account.

### What happens if I go over the Permit.io free limits?

The pricing page does not say. It lists Enterprise as the plan with no limits on monthly active users and tenants.
