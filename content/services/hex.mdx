You work in the Hex web app. You create a project, connect data, build cells with SQL or Python, and publish an app. It suits individual analysts and small teams, and larger teams can step up to paid plans.

## What the free plan gives you

The Community plan is free and meant for hobbyists finishing small projects and showcasing work. You can connect any data source and use the compute profiles listed as Small. Version history is kept for 7 days, and projects are auto-archived after 30 days.

AI use is limited. The plan lists limited credits and a Hex agent trial. Sharing is also capped. You can have up to 3 shared collections and up to 3 shared components. The pricing page lists up to 5 published apps for the individual plans.

Projects are private by default. You can share with your whole workspace or with specific users. Sharing with user groups needs the Team plan or above. Medium compute is included only on paid plans. The Professional plan costs $36 per editor per month, and Team costs $75 per editor per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists the Community caps but does not say what happens when you reach them. Features past the free caps, such as more apps or longer history, need a paid plan.

Hex offers a 14-day free trial of the Team plan with no payment card. If you do nothing at the end, you are downgraded to the free Community plan and lose access to premium features. Students and educators at universities and bootcamps can get the Professional plan for free if they contact support.

## Gotchas

- Version history is 7 days on Community, compared with 30 days on Professional and unlimited on Team.
- Scheduled runs and alerts are not part of Community. They start on the Team plan.
- Publishing an app does not give anyone access to your project. You must share the project too.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hex free?

Yes. The Community plan is free and meant for hobbyists and small projects. It includes small compute, 7-day version history, and limited credits.

### Does Hex have a free trial of paid features?

Yes. You can try the Team plan free for 14 days without a payment card. After that you drop to the free Community plan unless you pay.

### Is Hex free for students?

Students and educators at universities and bootcamps can get the Professional plan for free. You contact support@hex.tech for details.
