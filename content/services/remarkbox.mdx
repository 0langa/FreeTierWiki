Remarkbox is a hosted comment service that embeds under your pages. Its founder built it after moving a WordPress blog to a static site, and it is open source with the code on PyPI. It focuses on fast page loads, no ads, and no tracking, and it supports Markdown with syntax-highlighted code blocks.

## What the free plan gives you

Remarkbox has one plan, Production Yearly, priced as pay what you can, from $0 a month. It covers one moderator on a few domains, with complete control over behaviour and appearance. The page says Remarkbox does not set a price and asks you to contribute what you feel the software and service is worth. Setup is described as risk free with no credit card required.

Readers can comment without an account. When someone leaves a comment, Remarkbox collects their email address and sends a verification email to log them in. Comments support Markdown with real-time previews and deeply nested replies. Remarkbox also follows resilient web design, so it works when JavaScript does not and adds features when it does.

## When you hit a limit

The page does not publish usage numbers. The only limits it states are one moderator and a few domains. It does not say what happens if your site grows large or how many comments or page views the $0 option covers.

If you need more moderators or a stand-alone forum, the page suggests contacting the founder, Russell, directly. Ask before you depend on it for a large site.

## Gotchas

- "A few domains" is not a number; ask before you plan to run many sites on one account.
- The pay-what-you-can model depends on reciprocity, so treat it as a small service and budget something if you rely on it.
- Email verification for commenters means some readers will not finish posting.

## Questions people ask

### Is Remarkbox free?

You can pay $0. The plan is pay what you can, and Remarkbox asks you to contribute what you think it is worth.

### Do readers need an account to comment?

No. A commenter gives an email address and confirms it through a verification email.

### Does Remarkbox show ads?

No. The site says Remarkbox has no ads and no tracking.
