You work in the Capella web console, then connect with Couchbase SDKs, SQL++ queries, or App Services for mobile sync. It suits developers who want to try a JSON database with built-in search.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier has no fixed subscription expiry while it remains active, but inactive clusters are subject to deletion. The pricing page lists a small cluster of about 1 node and about 8 GB, with forum support. You get SQL++ and key-value access, search (vector, full-text, and geo), Mobile App Services, role-based access control, and scopes and collections.

Only 1 free tier operational cluster is available per organization. You pick AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure and a region. No credit card is needed. Paid accounts can run a free cluster next to their paid ones.

## When you hit a limit

The free configuration is fixed. After more than 72 hours without activity, Couchbase turns off the cluster and linked App Services while preserving data and state. After 30 days of inactivity, it deletes the cluster and linked App Services. Warnings follow the shutdown; sleep is not permanent storage.

Before the deletion threshold, you can turn it back on from Operational Home. The docs also say upgrading the free tier to a paid support plan deletes the existing free cluster. Back up and transfer its data with the documented cbbackupmgr process first. Paid Basic starts at $0.15 per hour per node; another free cluster can coexist with paid clusters.

## Gotchas

- The free tier is available only in Capella Operational, not Capella Operational Insights (formerly Analytics).
- Only 1 free cluster is allowed per organization.
- The Health Advisor dashboard is not available on free tier clusters.

## Questions people ask

### Is Couchbase Capella free?

Yes. Capella has a forever free tier operational cluster of about 1 node and about 8 GB. You do not need a credit card.

### Does the Couchbase free tier expire?

There is no fixed expiry for active free use. Inactivity turns the cluster off after 72 hours and deletes it, with linked App Services, after 30 days.

### Does the Capella free cluster turn off?

Yes. After 72 hours it sleeps with data preserved and can be turned back on. After 30 days of inactivity the cluster and linked App Services are deleted.
