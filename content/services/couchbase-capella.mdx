You work in the Capella web console, then connect with Couchbase SDKs, SQL++ queries, or App Services for mobile sync. It suits developers who want to try a JSON database with built-in search.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier is a forever free operational cluster with no time limit. The pricing page lists a small cluster of about 1 node and about 8 GB, with forum support. You get SQL++ and key-value access, search (vector, full-text, and geo), Mobile App Services, role-based access control, and scopes and collections.

Only 1 free tier operational cluster is available per organization. You pick AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure and a region. No credit card is needed. Paid accounts can run a free cluster next to their paid ones.

## When you hit a limit

The docs do not list a data cap for the cluster. The limit you will notice is inactivity. If there is no activity for more than 72 hours, Couchbase turns off the cluster and any linked App Services. Your data and state are kept.

To turn the cluster back on, go to Operational Home and click Turn On Cluster. To use Capella beyond the free cluster, you upgrade to a paid plan, which starts at $0.15 per hour per node.

## Gotchas

- The free tier is available only in Capella Operational, not Capella Analytics.
- Only 1 free cluster is allowed per organization.
- The Health Advisor dashboard is not available on free tier clusters.

## Questions people ask

### Is Couchbase Capella free?

Yes. Capella has a forever free tier operational cluster of about 1 node and about 8 GB. You do not need a credit card.

### Does the Couchbase free tier expire?

The docs say there is no time constraint on the free tier. A free trial of 30 days converts to the free tier when it ends.

### Does the Capella free cluster turn off?

Yes. It turns off after more than 72 hours without activity. Your data and state are preserved, and you turn it back on from Operational Home.
