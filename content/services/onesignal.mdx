You set up an app in the OneSignal dashboard, add its SDK to your mobile app or website, and then send from the dashboard or the API. It fits small teams that want push, email, and in-app messages without managing providers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. The pricing page lists unlimited mobile push notifications for organizations with up to 1,000 monthly active users, and unlimited in-app messaging that comes with mobile push. Email is free up to 10,000 sends per month.

Web push on the Free plan allows a maximum of 10,000 subscribers per send. The free plan allows 6 audience segments and 6 data tags, and more of both come with paid plans. On Free, OneSignal keeps subscription records that were active in the past 18 months.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what OneSignal does when you pass 1,000 monthly active users on the Free plan. A monthly active user is a mobile subscription record that was active in your app in the last 30 days.

Paid mobile push is charged per monthly active user, and the Growth plan starts at $19 per month. You can upgrade in the dashboard under Billing. Email beyond the free allowance is billed per 1,000 sends on paid plans.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page lists no free SMS or RCS allowance. Its cost calculator says to talk to sales, and Professional and Enterprise list SMS and RCS as purchasable with volume discounts.
- Web push, mobile push, and email are priced differently. Web push is charged by subscriber, mobile push by monthly active user, and email by sends.
- You need platform credentials first, such as Firebase for Android or an APNs p8 token for iOS, before the SDK can deliver mobile push.

## Questions people ask

### Is OneSignal free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes mobile push for organizations with up to 1,000 monthly active users, plus 10,000 email sends per month.

### Does OneSignal charge for push notifications?

On paid plans, mobile push is charged per monthly active user at $0.012 each, and web push per subscriber. Sends themselves are unlimited.

### Does OneSignal need a credit card?

The pricing page lists the Free plan at $0 per month and does not mention a card for it. You upgrade to Growth in the dashboard under Billing.
