Email Delivery sends email from your applications through Oracle's mail transfer agents over SMTP. You create an approved sender for your From address, generate SMTP credentials, and point your app at a regional endpoint. It suits developers whose apps run on Oracle Cloud and need transactional email.

## What the free plan gives you

Oracle's Always Free resources page says you can send 3000 emails for free per month with Email Delivery. The service includes bounce collection, a suppression list that stops sends to addresses with permanent failures or complaints, list-unsubscribe handling, and SPF authentication.

There is a catch. The Limits by Service table lists 0 emails sent in the last 24 hours for an Always Free tenancy, with 10 emails per minute and 10 approved senders. Trial accounts can send 200 emails in the last 24 hours, and Pay As You Go accounts 50,000. In practice, the free monthly emails are usable on a Trial or Pay As You Go account.

## When you hit a limit

On a Pay As You Go account, emails past the free monthly amount are billed. The daily sending limit caps your volume, and to raise it you open a limit increase request. Increases need SPF and DKIM set up for each sending domain, and the always-free limits for a tenancy cannot be increased.

The default message size limit is 2 MB on every account type. An email is counted per recipient in the To, CC, or BCC fields, or per 2 MB chunk, so a single request to 10 recipients counts as 10 emails.

## Gotchas

- Approved senders exist only in the region where you create them, so sending from another region needs another approved sender.
- An Always Free tenancy that was never upgraded cannot send any email under the published limits.
- Tenancies cannot send through outbound TCP port 25 to the internet by default, so use the Email Delivery SMTP endpoint.

## Questions people ask

### Is OCI Email Delivery free?

The Always Free resources include 3000 emails per month, but the limits table sets an Always Free tenancy to 0 emails a day. Sending needs a Trial or Pay As You Go account.

### How many emails can a trial account send?

Trial accounts can send 200 emails in the last 24 hours, at 10 emails per minute.

### How does Oracle count emails?

Each recipient in the To, CC, or BCC fields counts as one email, and so does each 2 MB chunk of a message.
