You use SimpleLogin from its website, from browser extensions for Chrome, Firefox, and Safari, or from the iOS and Android apps. It suits people who want to keep their real address private and do not mind managing a short list of aliases.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. It includes 10 aliases, 1 mailbox, and unlimited bandwidth. You can reply from an alias, so the other side sees the alias and not your real address. You also get the browser extensions, the mobile apps, two-factor sign-in with TOTP or WebAuthn, and Sign in with SimpleLogin.

The maximum email size is 25MB. When you delete an alias, the default is to move it to the global trash after 30 days, so you can restore it in the meantime. The service is open source, and the FAQ points to a GitHub repository.

## When you hit a limit

Free has a fixed cap of 10 aliases. The pricing page does not say what the app does when you try to create an eleventh. Premium removes the cap. It costs $36 per year, or $4 billed monthly, and adds unlimited aliases, custom domains, a catch-all domain, 5 subdomains, 50 directories, unlimited mailboxes, and PGP encryption. New accounts can try Premium free for 7 days with no credit card, and then drop to Free. Aliases made during the trial stay, but PGP stops.

## Gotchas

- You cannot start a new email from an alias on Free. That reverse-alias feature is listed under Premium.
- Custom domains, catch-all, subdomains, and directories all need Premium.
- A deleted alias made on a SimpleLogin domain cannot be reused. Aliases on your own domain can always be restored.

## Questions people ask

### Is SimpleLogin free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 10 aliases and 1 mailbox, with unlimited bandwidth and replies from an alias.

### Can I use SimpleLogin aliases for important accounts such as my bank?

The FAQ says yes. The SimpleLogin team uses aliases for its own business, taxes, and bank. If a message cannot be delivered, SimpleLogin notifies you.

### What is the difference between SimpleLogin Free and Premium?

Premium adds unlimited aliases, custom domains, catch-all, subdomains, directories, unlimited mailboxes, and PGP encryption. It costs $36 per year.
