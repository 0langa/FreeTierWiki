You work with Lockitbot inside Slack by mentioning `@Lockit`. A script can also call its HTTP API with an API key. It suits teams that share a few environments or devices.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and covers up to 2 resources. A resource is anything your team shares, such as a staging server or a test phone. The plan lists unlimited members, unlimited users, and unlimited locks, so the only cap is the number of resources.

In Slack, you create a resource with `@Lockit new`, list resources with `@Lockit list`, and lock or release one by name. You can also add a team to a resource so people get an alert when it becomes free. The Starter plan costs $17 a month and covers up to 100 resources. The page also offers a custom plan through the contact form.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to add a third resource on the Free plan. It names 2 resources as the limit and lists Starter as the next step. Nothing on the page suggests a bill for going over, and a card is not mentioned for the Free plan. Any plan can be tried free for 15 days, so you can test the 100-resource limit before you pay.

## Gotchas

- Lockitbot locks and releases shared resources. The official site does not describe channel access control or compliance workflows.
- The pricing page lists API, priority support, and a white-labeled bot as plan features. It does not say which plans include the API, so check before you build a script on it.
- The refund policy says there are no refunds on paid plans, but you can cancel at any time.

## Questions people ask

### Is Lockitbot free?

Yes, there is a Free plan for up to 2 resources with unlimited members and locks. Paid plans start at $17 a month.

### What happens when I go over 2 resources on Lockitbot?

The pricing page does not say. It lists the Starter plan, which raises the limit to 100 resources, as the next step.

### How do I lock a resource in Lockitbot?

Mention `@Lockit lock` with the resource name in Slack. To free it, use `@Lockit release` with the name.
