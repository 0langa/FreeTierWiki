You use Mailinator in a browser by typing an inbox name, or from code through its HTTP API with the Verified Pro plan. It suits one developer or tester who needs a quick inbox to check email flows.

## What the free plan gives you

The public system needs no signup. You type any inbox name, up to 50 characters, and read what arrives. Anyone can read a public inbox, and storage is temporary.

The Verified Pro plan costs $0 but needs a verification step. It gives you unlimited private inboxes on 1 private domain, API access, and 10MB of permanent storage. The limits fit one person. You get 1K emails per month, 60 emails per day, and 60 reads per day, whether through the API or the browser. The API read rate is 10 messages per second, and daily limits reset at 00:00 UTC.

## When you hit a limit

On Verified Pro, going over the limits will result in suspension, according to the plan page. Repeated suspensions can end your place in the program for good. The page also says false information on the application can lead to suspension. Nothing here bills you, because no card is needed. The public system is meant for personal and occasional use, and its docs say usage limits apply there too.

## Gotchas

- Public inbox mail is visible to anyone who guesses the inbox name, so never send secrets to it.
- Addresses such as info@ or admin@ will not be approved for Verified Pro.
- The Verified Pro plan is for single users. Team seats start on the paid plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mailinator free?

Yes. Public inboxes are free and need no signup. The Verified Pro plan also costs $0 and adds a private domain, but you must pass verification.

### Does Mailinator need a credit card?

No. The Verified Pro plan asks for verification, not payment.

### What happens when I go over the Mailinator Verified Pro limits?

The plan page says exceeding the limits will result in suspension. Repeated suspensions may end your access to the program.
