You make a network in the ZeroTier Central web console, install the client on each device, and join with the network ID. It suits hobbyists who want their own devices on one private network without a lot of setup.

## What the free plan gives you

The Personal plan is free. It is for personal, non-commercial use and covers 10 devices on one network with one network admin. You get the CLI, the UI, and webhooks, and support comes from the community.

The pricing page says new accounts, created after Nov. 5, 2025, use New Central, while older accounts use Legacy Central. The Personal plan is aimed at hobbyists, smart home users, and gamers.

Paid plans start with Essential, which adds a commercial license, 10 networks, five admins, OIDC single sign-on, and 30-day audit logs. Essential includes 10 devices, and more cost $2 each.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists the device count as included, with extra devices priced on paid plans. For the Personal plan it shows no per-device price. To go past the device count, plan on upgrading to a paid plan.

Commercial use is the bigger line. The Personal plan is for non-commercial use only, and the commercial license starts on the Essential plan at $18 a month. The pages do not describe what ZeroTier does if you pass the free device count.

## Gotchas

- The quickstart docs say Central is free for personal and small business use up to 25 devices. The pricing page lists 10 devices and non-commercial use, so trust the pricing page.
- A device that joins a network shows Access Denied until you tick Auth for it in the Members panel.
- Windows blocks ping by default, but ZeroTier enables ping automatically, so you can test with it.

## Questions people ask

### Is ZeroTier free?

Yes, for personal use. The Personal plan covers 10 devices on one network with one admin. It is for non-commercial use only.

### Can I use ZeroTier free for business?

No. The Personal plan is non-commercial. The Essential plan adds a commercial license and starts at $18 a month.

### Does ZeroTier need a credit card?

The quickstart docs say no credit card is required for Central. The pricing page shows the Personal plan as free with a Start Now button.
