GetUpdraft is an over-the-air (OTA) distribution platform for mobile app beta testing — an alternative to Diawi, DeployGate, and Applivery. The free Starter plan gives 1 app project, 3 versions, 500 MB storage, and 100 installs a month, with no credit card required and no trial period.
