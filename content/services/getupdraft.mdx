You work in the Updraft dashboard, and you can add upload plugins or call the API from your build system. Testers install from a link or QR code. It suits indie developers and small teams with one app to test.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free. The pricing page says there is no trial period: you sign up and use all features for free, within the plan limits. Starter includes 1 app project, 3 app versions, 500MB of storage, and 100 app installations per month.

Employees, contributors, and testers are unlimited. You get public link installation, QR code installation, and a testers dashboard. You can upload by drag and drop, with a plugin, or through the API with cURL. Supported files are .ipa, .apk, .aab, and Windows files.

The Pay as you go plan costs $5 per month plus $5 per GB of storage. It has unlimited projects and versions and 1000 installations per month. Professional costs $49 per month and adds re-signing of .ipa and .aab files.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach a free limit, such as 100 installations or 500MB of storage. It names the paid plans as the way to get more.

The FAQ says that to upload a file bigger than 500MB you must move to the paid plan. It also says there is no maximum upload size and no install limit. That FAQ page was last updated years ago, and the pricing page lists 100 installs per month for Starter. Trust the pricing page.

## Gotchas

- Starter keeps only 3 builds. Plan to delete old versions as you upload new ones.
- Paid storage is billed to your credit card at the end of the month. The free plan needs no card.
- Re-signing of .ipa and .aab files is only on the Professional plan, not on Starter.

## Questions people ask

### Is GetUpdraft free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes 1 app project, 3 app versions, 500MB of storage, and 100 app installations per month.

### Does GetUpdraft have a trial period?

No. The pricing page says there is no trial period. You sign up and use all features for free within the plan limits.

### Can I install old builds from GetUpdraft?

Yes. The FAQ says you can select a previous version in the build history and install or share it again. The free plan keeps only 3 versions.
