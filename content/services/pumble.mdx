You chat in Pumble's web app or its apps, in channels and direct messages. It suits teams that want a Slack-style tool without a message history cap.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan has an unlimited number of users and unlimited message history, and you can search all of it. You can create unlimited channels. The pricing page also lists voice and video messages, and a way to import your messages from Slack.

You get 10 GB of file storage per workspace, shared by everyone. You can connect up to 3 apps and integrations, and the free Clockify and Plaky integrations do not count toward that limit. Paid plans add group meetings, guests, user groups, and storage per seat.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the workspace passes 10 GB of storage or when you try to add a fourth app. Plan on deleting old files or upgrading to keep adding files. The FAQ says the Free plan does not have any limitations, yet the pricing page lists the storage and app caps, so trust the pricing page. Users and message history stay unlimited.

You can try the paid features free for 7 days, with no credit card required. The Free plan costs $0, and the pricing page does not describe any automatic charge.

## Gotchas

- The 10 GB of storage is shared by the whole workspace on Free. On Pro, storage is 10 GB per seat.
- Group meetings, user groups, and guest users appear as paid features on the pricing page.
- Paid plans bill per seat, and every active or invited member, including multi-channel guests, takes one seat.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pumble free?

Yes. The Free plan has an unlimited number of users and unlimited message history. The pricing page shows it at $0.

### How much storage does Pumble give for free?

The Free plan includes 10 GB of file storage per workspace. Pro raises this to 10 GB per seat.

### Can I try Pumble paid features for free?

Yes. The FAQ says you can try all features free for 7 days, with no credit card. You turn on the trial on the Upgrade page.
