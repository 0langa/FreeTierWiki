You use Seafile through its web app, desktop sync client, drive client, and mobile apps for Android and iOS. The cloud Plus Edition is offered as a trial, and the on-premises Pro Edition is free for a few users.

## What the free plan gives you

The cloud service has no permanent free plan. The Plus Edition trial plan is free for at most 3 users and gives you 1GB of storage. It includes upload and download, cross-platform sync, version history, sharing, public links, full-text search, and SeaDoc for shared documents.

The free option that lasts is the on-premises Pro Edition. The pricing page says you do not need a license file to run it for 3 users, and the docker image is on DockerHub. The Community Edition is free and open source too.

For more users, you can email sales for a trial license that lasts 3 weeks or more.

## When you hit a limit

The cloud trial ends by time, not by usage. The pricing page says the account is inactivated after the 30 days of trial and then deleted within 90 days. Export your files before the trial ends.

To keep using the cloud, you move to the Team plan at $8 per user per month. It starts at 3 users and includes 500GB of storage that you can expand, with 3 months of file version retention. The self-hosted Pro Edition for 3 users has no time limit, but you run the server yourself.

## Gotchas

- The cloud trial is not a free plan. Your data is inactivated after 30 days and then removed.
- The free Pro Edition is for 3 users on your own server. It is not cloud storage, and you handle backups and updates.
- The pricing page lists a 9-user special offer for small businesses, and a 25 percent discount for public institutions. These are paid.

## Questions people ask

### Is Seafile free?

Not as a cloud service. The cloud Plus Edition has a free trial for at most 3 users. The self-hosted Pro Edition is free for 3 users.

### How long is the Seafile cloud trial?

The trial lasts 30 days. Seafile then inactivates the account and deletes it within 90 days.

### How much storage does the Seafile trial give?

The trial plan has 1GB of storage. The paid Team plan has 500GB that you can expand.
