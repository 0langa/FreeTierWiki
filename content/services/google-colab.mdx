You use Colab in the browser. You sign in with a Google account, open or create a notebook, and run Python in cells on a virtual machine that Google hosts. It suits students, teachers, and ML beginners who want a free GPU or TPU without any setup.

## What the free plan gives you

The FAQ says Colab is free of charge and gives access to computing resources, including GPUs and TPUs. There is nothing to install. Notebooks are stored in Google Drive or loaded from GitHub, and you can share them like a Google Doc. You can download any notebook as an .ipynb file.

The free tier has no published numbers. Google says Colab resources are not guaranteed and not unlimited, and usage limits sometimes fluctuate. Colab gives priority to people who are actively programming in a notebook. Your code runs in a virtual machine private to your account.

## When you hit a limit

Virtual machines are deleted when idle for a while, and the service enforces a maximum lifetime. Google does not publish the exact times. The free tier can also terminate runtimes at any time without warning if they do things like remote control with SSH shells or remote desktops, run a web UI instead of the notebook, or run distributed workers. Paid plans, a positive compute unit balance, Colab Enterprise, or a local runtime remove these restrictions. Colab also limits how often you can reset your virtual machine.

## Gotchas

- Free runtimes can end without warning. Save work to Google Drive and keep install cells in your notebook, because custom files and libraries on the virtual machine are not shared.
- Interacting mainly through a web UI instead of working in notebook cells is one of the activities that can end a free runtime.
- Mounting Google Drive can time out if a folder holds thousands of items, so keep your top-level My Drive folder small.

## Questions people ask

### Is Google Colab free?

Yes. The FAQ says Colab is free of charge to use, including GPUs and TPUs. Resources are not guaranteed, and limits sometimes change.

### Does Google Colab need a credit card?

The FAQ describes the service as free of charge and mentions a Google account sign-in. It does not mention a card for the free tier.

### Why does my Colab runtime keep disconnecting?

Colab deletes idle virtual machines and sets a maximum lifetime. Free runtimes are also ended when they run things like web UIs or SSH shells.
