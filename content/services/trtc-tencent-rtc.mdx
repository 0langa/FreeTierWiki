You create an application in a web console, then add a client SDK to your app. Your server creates the user signatures that the SDK needs. It suits developers building call or live features.

## What the free plan gives you

Every account gets a monthly quota of 10,000 free minutes. The free minutes document says it can be used across Call, Conference, Live, RTC Engine, and Conversational AI products. The pricing page says no credit card is required.

Chat starts at $0 per month with 1,000 MAU. Push starts at $0 with unlimited MAU. Call, Room, Live, and Beauty AR have timed free trials, of up to 70 days, up to 140 days, and up to 28 days, depending on the product.

Unused free minutes do not carry over. They expire on the last day of the cycle. Free minutes are deducted at set ratios by usage type, so check the free minutes document for your product.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that without a monthly subscription, service is suspended once the free usage is used. The free trial does not support pay-as-you-go.

With a paid monthly plan, usage beyond the plan is billed pay-as-you-go and service is not interrupted. Audio costs $0.99 per 1,000 minutes, and HD video costs $3.99 per 1,000 minutes. Make sure your account balance is enough to cover it.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page and the docs disagree. The FAQ says the RTC Engine trial is forever free, while the free minutes document says the package is granted each month within a year.
- The secret key must stay on your server. The docs say a key put into client code can be decompiled and abused.
- Which usage types the free minutes can offset depends on when your account was registered. Accounts registered on or after July 25, 2024 can offset more types, such as cloud recording.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tencent RTC free?

Partly. Each account gets 10,000 free minutes per month, and Chat starts at $0 for 1,000 MAU. Call, Room, Live, and Beauty AR have timed free trials.

### What happens when I use all the free minutes?

Without a paid monthly plan, service is suspended. With a plan, extra usage is billed pay-as-you-go.

### Does Tencent RTC need a credit card?

The site says no credit card is required to start the free trial.
