You set up a Data Flow in the Coupler.io web app: pick a source, optionally transform the data, and choose a destination. It suits reporting work where people want app data in a spreadsheet.

## What the free plan gives you

New accounts start with 7 free days of full access. After that, you can pick a paid plan or stay on the Free plan, which costs $0 forever.

The Free plan gives you 1 account, 1 data destination, and manual data refresh only. Each run is limited to 100 rows. The pricing page says all sources are available on every plan, and it lists 440 of them. Paid plans lift the limits. The Starter plan costs $32 a month, or $24 a month billed annually, and it lists 5000 rows per run.

You cannot set a schedule on Free. You run each import yourself when you want fresh data.

## When you hit a limit

The row limit works as a cut, not a charge. A run on the Free plan carries 100 rows, and the help center has a troubleshooting article about imports that finish successfully but whose data was truncated. Check that article if rows seem to be missing.

The Free plan has no scheduled refresh to run out of, so there is no overage and no bill. To get more rows or automatic refresh, you must move to a paid plan. The pricing page does not say whether a card is needed to sign up.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan exports at most 100 rows per data flow run, and the pricing FAQ says only one of your data flows (the active one) works on Free.
- Refresh is manual on Free. The pricing page shows automated refresh only on paid plans.
- The help center lists a known problem that the Claude connector tools do not load on the Free and Pro plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Coupler.io free?

Yes, there is a Free plan that costs $0 forever. It allows 1 account, 1 data destination, manual refresh, and 100 rows per run.

### Does Coupler.io have a free trial?

Yes. New accounts get 7 free days with full access, then they can pick a paid plan or stay on Free.

### Can Coupler.io refresh data automatically on the free plan?

No. The Free plan refreshes only when you run it by hand. Paid plans add scheduled refresh.
