You install the Netdata Agent on each machine and open Netdata Cloud to see all of them in one place. The agent stores the data on your own systems.

## What the free plan gives you

The Community plan is free forever and allows a maximum of 5 active connected nodes. It allows 1 active custom dashboard per room, and infrastructure-wide dashboards are limited to 5 nodes. The pricing page marks the plan as non-commercial.

Your data stays on-prem, and you reach it from anywhere through the cloud. The page gives no retention figure for Community. When you sign up, you get a 14-day trial of Netdata Business for as many nodes as you want, with no limits.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens if you connect more than 5 nodes on Community. It states the limit of 5 active connected nodes, so plan on upgrading beyond that.

Business costs $4.50 per node per month, with unlimited data retention and unlimited custom dashboards. A billable node is each running agent. Offline nodes are not billed. The Homelab plan costs $90 per year with unlimited nodes, under a fair usage policy.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page marks Community as non-commercial, so check the terms before using it for a business.
- Only 1 custom dashboard fits in each room on the free plan.
- The installer asks whether to connect to Netdata Cloud, and the default is disabled, so you must opt in.

## Questions people ask

### Is Netdata Cloud free?

Yes, within limits. The Community plan is free forever for up to 5 connected nodes, and the pricing page marks it non-commercial.

### How many nodes does the free Netdata plan allow?

A maximum of 5 active connected nodes, plus 1 active custom dashboard per room. Business adds more nodes at $4.50 per node per month.

### Do I need a credit card for Netdata Cloud?

The pricing page does not mention a credit card for the free plan, and it offers a 14-day Business trial when you sign up.
