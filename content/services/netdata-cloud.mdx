You install the Netdata Agent on each machine and open Netdata Cloud to see all of them in one place. The agent stores the data on your own systems.

## What the free plan gives you

The Community plan is free forever and allows a maximum of 5 active connected nodes. It allows 1 active custom dashboard per room. The pricing comparison includes metrics and system logs, while application logs and distributed traces require Business. The non-commercial restriction applies to the separate paid Homelab plan.

Your data stays on-prem, and you reach it from anywhere through the cloud. The page gives no retention figure for Community. New accounts get a 14-day trial of Netdata Business. The pricing page says the trial covers every Business feature on as many nodes as you need, and it states no AI-credit allowance for the trial.

## When you hit a limit

The Fair Usage Policy describes a gradual response to excessive Community connections: contact the user, ask them to reduce usage or choose a suitable paid plan, then potentially throttle or suspend unresponsive accounts. This is not a documented automatic overage charge or an immediate strict stop at the fifth node.

Business Infrastructure starts at $7 per node per month with annual billing or $8.75 monthly; APM starts at $15 annually or $18.75 monthly. Metrics and system logs are unlimited, while application logs and traces have pooled quotas and overage fees. Retention depends on your own storage, with no Netdata retention fee. The Homelab plan costs $90 per year with unlimited nodes, under a fair usage policy.

## Gotchas

- The $90/year Homelab plan requires non-professional, non-commercial use. The Fair Usage Policy distinguishes this from Community resource limits.
- Only 1 custom dashboard fits in each room on the free plan.
- The installer asks whether to connect the node to Netdata Cloud, so answer yes or pass a claim token to the script.

## Questions people ask

### Is Netdata Cloud free?

Yes, within limits. The Community plan is free forever for up to 5 connected nodes, subject to its Fair Usage Policy.

### How many nodes does the free Netdata plan allow?

A maximum of 5 active connected nodes, plus 1 active custom dashboard per room. Business adds more nodes at $7 per node per month with annual billing.

### Do I need a credit card for Netdata Cloud?

The pricing page does not mention a credit card for the free plan, and it offers a 14-day Business trial when you sign up.
