You work in a web dashboard, with an API available for developers. It suits people who want simple campaigns and a first chatbot without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

Each SendPulse tool has separate plans and free allowances; signup needs no card. Email Free includes 15,000 monthly emails, 500 subscribers, 140+ editable templates, 3 sender addresses and 250 MB images. Basic automation allows 5 flows, 50 elements/flow and 1 event.

Chatbot Basic includes 3 bots, 500 subscribers and 10,000 monthly messages across bots, plus 10 variables, 10 triggers, unlimited flows, templates, deal editing and a limited API.

Web push Basic provides unlimited notifications for 10,000 subscribers, RSS, automated series and personalization. API, segmentation, A/B testing, Automation 360 integration and branding removal require Pro. Do not combine one tool's allowance with another's.

## When you hit a limit

Free email caps at 500 subscribers/15,000 monthly emails, but campaign/bot exhaustion handling is unspecified. Do not assume blocking or automatic upgrades.

Paid email renews automatically; pay-as-you-go packs last a year. Chatbot Standard removes published message/bot caps and bills by subscribers, including 3 AI agents/1 GB files. Pro adds monthly AI credits, unlimited agents and active-subscriber billing for TikTok, Instagram and Live Chat.

WhatsApp uses separate Meta pricing. From October 1, 2026, service replies within the 24-hour window are chargeable, with 1,000 free service messages/bot/calendar month; rates follow destination-country utility templates.

## Gotchas

- Free chat support lasts 30 days from registration; email and help desk support remain available. A paid plan for any tool unlocks account-wide chat support. The free tiers are per tool. Your email limit of 500 subscribers does not share a pool with your chatbot limit of 500 subscribers.
- The chatbot free plan limits you to 10 variables and 10 triggers, which is easy to outgrow with a busy bot.
- The WhatsApp Business API is listed separately on the chatbot pricing page, and that page says Meta charges for some messages. Read it before you rely on the free chatbot plan for WhatsApp.

## Questions people ask

### Is SendPulse free?

Yes. SendPulse has a free plan for each tool, with no time limit and no credit card. The email plan allows 15,000 emails per month to up to 500 subscribers.

### How many chatbots can I build on the free SendPulse plan?

You can build 3 chatbots. They share up to 500 subscribers and up to 10,000 messages per month.

### Does SendPulse need a credit card?

No. The site says you can create a free account without a credit card.
