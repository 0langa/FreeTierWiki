You work in a web dashboard, with an API available for developers. It suits people who want simple campaigns and a first chatbot without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

SendPulse sells each tool with its own plan, and each tool has a free tier. No credit card is needed to sign up.

The free email plan sends up to 15,000 emails per month. It allows up to 500 subscribers and includes 140+ editable email templates and 250 MB of image storage.

The free chatbot plan (called Basic) gives you 3 chatbots, up to 500 subscribers, and up to 10,000 messages per month across all your bots. It also includes 10 variables, 10 chatbot triggers, unlimited flows, premade flow templates, deal editing in flows, and a limited API.

The web push pricing page says the Basic plan sends web push notifications to 10,000 subscribers every month for free.

## When you hit a limit

Free email allows 500 subscribers and 15,000 emails per month. The public pages direct larger lists to paid plans but do not fully explain what happens to a campaign or bot when its free quota runs out. Do not assume a precise blocking or automatic-upgrade rule.

Paid email subscriptions renew automatically after activation; pay-as-you-go email packs remain valid for a year. Chatbot Pro removes the published message and bot caps, with price based on subscribers. WhatsApp service and template messages have separate Meta pricing, so the chatbot allowance is not a blanket free WhatsApp allowance.

## Gotchas

- The free tiers are per tool. Your email limit of 500 subscribers does not share a pool with your chatbot limit of 500 subscribers.
- The chatbot free plan limits you to 10 variables and 10 triggers, which is easy to outgrow with a busy bot.
- The WhatsApp Business API is listed separately on the chatbot pricing page, and that page says Meta charges for some messages. Read it before you rely on the free chatbot plan for WhatsApp.

## Questions people ask

### Is SendPulse free?

Yes. SendPulse has a free plan for each tool, with no time limit and no credit card. The email plan allows 15,000 emails per month to up to 500 subscribers.

### How many chatbots can I build on the free SendPulse plan?

You can build 3 chatbots. They share up to 500 subscribers and up to 10,000 messages per month.

### Does SendPulse need a credit card?

No. The site says you can create a free account without a credit card.
