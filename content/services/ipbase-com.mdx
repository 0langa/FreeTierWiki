You register for an API key in the dashboard, then call the HTTP API with `curl` or one of the SDKs, or use the MCP server. It suits developers who need a few IP lookups a month and want location, time zone, and network data in one JSON call.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and gives you 150 requests a month with location data. It allows 10 requests per minute, includes 1 API key, and supports batch lookups of up to 100 addresses. No credit card is required.

A batch call counts one request per address. The paid plans remove the per-minute limit and add volume. The first paid plan costs $9.99 a month for 65,000 requests, and the next costs $39.99 for 650,000. Time zone, currency, and security data such as VPN, proxy, and Tor flags need paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

Past the monthly quota, the API returns HTTP 429. You then upgrade or wait until the end of the month. On the free plan, going over 10 requests per minute also returns 429, and you wait until the minute ends.

Only successful calls count against your quota. Errors on the provider side and validation errors do not count. The response headers show how many requests remain in the month and the minute.

## Gotchas

- A batch lookup of 100 addresses uses 100 of your 150 monthly requests. Use it sparingly on the free plan.
- Time zone, currency, and security flags are not part of the free plan, even though the home page shows them in the sample response.
- A 403 status means the endpoint is not allowed on your plan, so check the plan before you build on an endpoint.

## Questions people ask

### Is ipbase free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan gives you 150 requests a month with location data, at up to 10 requests per minute, and needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the ipbase free limit?

The API returns HTTP 429 once you pass the monthly quota or the per-minute rate. You wait for the reset or upgrade.

### Does ipbase support batch lookups on the free plan?

Yes. The pricing page lists batch lookups of up to 100 addresses on the Free plan, and each address counts as one request.
