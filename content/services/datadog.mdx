You install the Datadog Agent on your hosts, and it sends metrics and events to the Datadog web app, where you build dashboards and alerts. It suits small teams that want to watch a handful of servers before paying.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Infrastructure Monitoring plan covers up to 5 hosts. It includes core collection and visualization features, standard events and metrics, and out-of-the-box dashboards. Metrics are kept for 1 day, while the paid plans list 15 months of full-resolution retention.

A host is any physical or virtual OS instance that Datadog monitors, such as a server, a VM, or a Kubernetes node. The free plan does not include log management or APM. Those products are on the pricing page with paid rates.

Separately, the free trial lasts 14 days and covers the whole platform. It needs no credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you add a sixth host to the Free plan. It asks for no card, so a surprise bill is unlikely, but check your host count in Datadog.

When the 14-day trial ends, the trial-only products stop unless you upgrade. The Free plan itself does not expire. Paid plans bill per host, and logs are priced by gigabytes of data ingested.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan keeps metrics for only 1 day, so you cannot look back at last week.
- Log management, APM, and most integrations need a paid plan or the trial.
- The page also lists AI credits as a separate paid product that you buy by the credit.

## Questions people ask

### Is Datadog free?

Yes, in a limited form. The Free plan covers up to 5 hosts with 1 day of metric retention. There is also a 14-day trial of the full platform with no credit card.

### How many hosts can I monitor on the Datadog free plan?

Up to 5 hosts. A host is any physical or virtual OS instance you monitor, such as a server, VM, or Kubernetes node.

### Does Datadog need a credit card for the trial?

No. The trial sign-up page says no credit card is required and starts a 14-day trial.
