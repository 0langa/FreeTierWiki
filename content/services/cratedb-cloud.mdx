CrateDB Cloud runs CrateDB, a distributed SQL database built for real-time analytics on large volumes of data, as a managed service. CrateDB handles deployment, scaling, security, and day-to-day operations. Its free plan gives each user one small cluster to test and evaluate the database.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a free plan with 2 vCPUs, 2 GB of RAM, and 8 GB of storage, with no credit card required. The plans reference in the docs calls it CRFREE: a shared single-node cluster with up to 2 vCPUs, 2 GiB of RAM, and 8 GiB of storage.

The docs say the plan is aimed at new users who want to test and evaluate CrateDB Cloud and is perpetually free to use. Every user can deploy one free tier cluster in their organization without adding a payment method.

## When you hit a limit

A free cluster cannot be scaled or changed, so it stays at its size rather than growing into a bill. The pricing page does not say what happens when the 8 GB of storage fills up.

Inactivity is the limit to watch: free clusters are suspended if they are not used for 4 days, and deleted after 10 more days of inactivity. Paid shared clusters start from $0.073 an hour and scale to up to 8 shared vCPUs, 12 GB of RAM, and 1 TB of storage.

## Gotchas

- An idle free cluster is suspended after 4 days and deleted after 10 more, so keep your own copy of the data.
- Each organization can run only one free tier cluster at a time.
- The free cluster cannot be scaled; moving to a bigger size means a paid cluster.

## Questions people ask

### Is CrateDB Cloud free?

Yes. Every user can deploy one free cluster with 2 vCPUs, 2 GB of RAM, and 8 GB of storage, without a credit card.

### Will my free CrateDB cluster be deleted?

If it is not used for 4 days it is suspended, and after 10 more days of inactivity it is deleted.

### Can I scale the free cluster?

No. Free tier clusters cannot be scaled or changed.
