Val Town hosted small TypeScript programs with HTTP, cron, and email triggers. It ran them on Deno and provided version history, a browser editor, and public endpoints. The company is winding down its hosted service.

## What the free plan gives you

Existing Free accounts can keep running their apps during the wind-down. The published Free limits are 100,000 runs per day, 1 minute per run, and a minimum 15-minute cron interval. Logs are available for 3 days. Pricing advertises unlimited public vals, while the limits page lists 2,500 total vals. Free accounts have no private vals or custom domains and can only use built-in email to send to themselves.

Val Town stopped accepting signups and plan upgrades on November 10, 2026. It also paused billing and says existing subscriptions will continue at no charge during the wind-down.

## When you hit a limit

Existing accounts remain subject to their documented runtime and plan limits. The detailed limits page says some limits are enforced flexibly and does not say how it handles a day over 100,000 runs. With upgrades closed, move work that needs more capacity to the replacement host.

Hosted code becomes read-only on December 10, 2026. The announcement excludes SQLite and blob storage from that read-only step. Existing apps continue to run until January 10, 2027, when hosted services stop.

## Gotchas

- Move code you still need to edit before December 10, 2026, and transfer app data before shutdown.
- If you migrate a webhook, update the sender to the new endpoint. Some webhook callers do not follow redirects.
- Val Town plans to release its source code on February 1, 2027.

## Questions people ask

### Can I sign up for Val Town for free?

New signups and plan upgrades ended on November 10, 2026. Current users can still access their existing plan during the shutdown period.

### When will Val Town stop running existing apps?

Existing apps run until January 10, 2027. Code on the service becomes read-only on December 10, 2026.

### Will Val Town keep billing existing customers?

Val Town paused billing on November 10, 2026. It says existing subscriptions will continue free during the wind-down and customers who prepaid annually will receive a partial refund.
