You write TypeScript in the Val Town editor, add an HTTP, cron, or email trigger, and save. Your code runs on the Deno runtime, and each file change is versioned. You can also use the CLI or an AI agent through the MCP server. It suits people who want small scripts and webhooks online without setting up hosting.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. It includes 100,000 runs per day and unlimited public vals. Each run can last 1 minute of wall-clock time. Cron triggers can run as often as every 15 minutes, with up to 10 crons per val. Logs are kept for 3 days, with 100,000 log lines per day.

The Free plan allows up to 2,500 vals, but 0 private vals and 0 custom domains. You can email yourself from a val, but not other people. The limits page also says the API allows 1,000 requests per minute for authenticated calls, and a worker can use up to 4 GiB of memory.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing and limits pages do not say what happens after 100,000 runs in a day. The limits page says Val Town enforces these limits, that they may change, and that some are soft-enforced. It asks you to contact them if a limit blocks a legitimate use.

A run that goes past the 1 minute execution timeout stops. Pro raises the timeout to 10 min, the cron interval to 1 minute, and the daily runs to 1,000,000.

## Gotchas

- Free vals are public. Private vals need the Pro plan.
- A cron cannot run more often than every 15 minutes on Free. Crons run in UTC.
- Free logs last only 3 days, so debug failures soon after they happen.

## Questions people ask

### Is Val Town free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 100,000 runs per day, unlimited public vals, and 1 minute of wall-clock time per run.

### Can Val Town run cron jobs for free?

Yes, but a cron on the Free plan can run at most once every 15 minutes. The Pro plan allows once a minute.

### Can I keep my Val Town code private for free?

No. The Free plan allows 0 private vals. Pro allows 2,000.
