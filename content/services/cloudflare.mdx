You set Cloudflare up in the dashboard by adding your domain and changing its nameservers. After that, traffic to your site goes through Cloudflare. It suits people with a personal or hobby site who want a free CDN and security layer.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month. It includes DNS, a CDN, unmetered DDoS protection, the Free Managed Ruleset, a WAF, and a Universal SSL certificate that Cloudflare issues and renews for you.

Rules have set sizes. You get 5 custom WAF rules and 1 rate limiting rule, and custom rules on Free do not support regex. Cloudflare Rules allow 70 rules plus 10K Bulk Redirects. Bot mitigation covers simple bots, and API Schema Validation allows up to 5 schemas.

Support is community forums and docs. Cloudflare describes the plan as meant for personal or hobby projects that are not business-critical.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan does not bill you for extra traffic. You hit the rule limits instead: a sixth custom WAF rule needs the Pro plan, which allows 20 rules and costs $25 a month billed monthly.

Paid add-ons are separate and optional. Load Balancing and Smart Shield with Argo start at $5 a month, and Advanced Certificate Manager is $10 a month. The plans page does not describe any bandwidth cap for the CDN on Free.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has no uptime SLA. That starts on the Business plan, which costs $250 a month billed monthly.
- Support on Free is community forums and docs only. Tickets start on the Pro plan.
- Universal SSL covers your root domain and first-level subdomains on a full setup. Deeper subdomains need Total TLS or an advanced certificate.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes DNS, a CDN, unmetered DDoS protection, and a free SSL certificate. Paid plans add more rules, better support, and an SLA.

### Does Cloudflare need a credit card for the free plan?

No. You sign up with an email address and password, and then verify your email. The Free plan has no payment step.

### How many WAF rules does the Cloudflare free plan have?

You get 5 custom rules and 1 rate limiting rule. The Pro plan raises custom rules to 20.
