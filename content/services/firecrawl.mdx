You use Firecrawl through a REST API, Python and Node SDKs, a command-line tool, or an MCP server for AI tools. It suits developers who feed web content into LLM apps, RAG pipelines, and agents.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and gives you 1,000 credits every month, refreshed monthly, with no credit card required. Credits are shared across all endpoints, so searches and scraped pages draw on one balance. The pricing page says that equals 500 searches or 1,000 pages scraped.

A basic scrape, crawl, or map costs 1 credit per page. A search costs 2 credits per 10 results. The JSON, Question, and Highlight formats add 4 credits per page on scrape and crawl. The Free plan allows 2 concurrent requests, and the pricing page calls its rate limits low: 10 requests a minute on scrape, map, and search, and 2 a minute on crawl and agent. Agent is in preview with 5 free daily runs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page describes no overage charge on the Free plan, and it needs no card, so you cannot be billed. Credits refresh monthly, and unused ones do not roll over. When the 1,000 credits are gone, you wait for the refresh or move to a paid plan. Pay-as-you-go top-ups, which add credits in 5 USD increments, are for paid plans only. The Hobby plan costs $16 per month billed yearly, or $19 billed monthly, and includes 5,000 credits.

## Gotchas

- A page that returns an error such as 403 or 404 is still returned and still costs 1 credit. A scrape that returns no result is not charged.
- Heavier formats cost more. JSON, Question, and Highlight add 4 credits per page, so your 1,000 credits cover fewer pages.
- Interact sessions cost 2 credits per browser minute, which uses up a small balance fast.

## Questions people ask

### Is Firecrawl free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 1,000 credits every month, which covers about 1,000 pages. Paid plans start with Hobby at $16 per month billed yearly.

### Does Firecrawl need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the Free tier needs no credit card.

### What happens when I run out of Firecrawl credits?

The pricing page describes no overage charge on Free. You wait for the monthly refresh or move to a paid plan. Pay-as-you-go top-ups need a paid plan.
