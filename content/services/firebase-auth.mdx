You turn on sign-in methods in the Firebase console and call the Firebase client SDKs from your web, iOS, or Android app. It suits developers who already use Firebase or want sign-in without writing a backend.

## What the free plan gives you

The free Spark plan covers email and password, anonymous, and social sign-in at no cost. Registered user accounts are unlimited, and the docs cap anonymous accounts at 100 million. Phone number verification is not on Spark. It needs the pay-as-you-go Blaze plan, and each SMS is billed.

Authentication with Identity Platform on Spark has daily limits: 3000 tier 1 daily active users and 2 tier 2 daily active users, which cover SAML and OIDC. On the Blaze plan, the allowances are monthly: 50K tier 1 monthly active users and 50 SAML or OIDC users at no cost. Email sending is also capped on Spark. Spark allows 150 password reset emails per day and 1000 address verification emails per day.

## When you hit a limit

Spark limits are daily, and they refresh at midnight. Firebase says no-cost limits are enforced daily. The pages do not say exactly what happens when you pass them. Plan to upgrade to Blaze if you expect to. The email link sign-in limit is very low on Spark, at 5 emails per day. The docs say you must add a billing instrument to go beyond it.

Moving to Blaze means a billing account is attached. After that, usage above the monthly no-cost amounts is billed by volume, and SMS messages are billed per message. Set a budget alert before you turn on phone sign-in.

## Gotchas

- Phone and SMS sign-in is not free. It needs Blaze, and each SMS costs money.
- Spark has a daily active user cap, while Blaze has a monthly one. Do not mix the two when you plan for growth.
- Email link sign-in is limited to 5 emails per day on Spark, so it cannot carry real traffic without a billing account.

## Questions people ask

### Is Firebase Authentication free?

Partly. Email and password, anonymous, and social sign-in are free on the Spark plan, within daily limits. Phone sign-in needs the paid Blaze plan.

### Does Firebase Auth need a credit card?

Not on the Spark plan. Phone and SMS sign-in need Blaze, which needs a billing account.

### How many users can Firebase Auth have for free?

Registered user accounts are unlimited. On Spark, the Identity Platform limit is 3000 tier 1 daily active users. On Blaze, 50K monthly active users are free.
