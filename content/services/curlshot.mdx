CurlShot is an HTTP API that renders a web page in a browser and returns an image or a PDF. Send a URL with a GET request, or POST HTML or Markdown as JSON to the same screenshot endpoint. It suits apps that need link previews, social images, or page captures without running a headless browser.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free plan at $0 with 100 screenshots a month, "for as long as you like", and says no card is needed. The plan allows 10 requests per minute and 1 render at a time. Full-page captures go up to 10,000 px tall at 1x pixel density, and videos up to 10 seconds.

The page says all plans include PNG, JPEG, and WebP images, PDF output, video capture, async renders with webhooks, bulk requests, a response cache, and ad and cookie-banner blocking. A failed render and a cache hit are not counted. Paid plans start at $19 a month for 3,000 screenshots.

## When you hit a limit

The quota counts successful renders per monthly period, starting from the day you signed up. Any bonus screenshots, such as referral rewards, are used after the plan allowance. Once both are exhausted, the API answers quota_exceeded with status 402 until the next period or until you move to a larger plan. Overage is only offered on paid plans, is off by default, and stops at a cap you set.

Going past 10 requests a minute returns rate_limited, and a second render while one is running returns concurrency_limit, both with status 429 and a Retry-After header. A free GET /usage call shows what is left.

## Gotchas

- A key works only after you confirm the email address of the account; until then requests answer email_not_verified.
- All keys of one account share the same quota, rate limit, and concurrency, so extra keys do not add capacity.
- Full-page captures taller than 10,000 px are not refused on the free plan; they are cut at that height.

## Questions people ask

### Is CurlShot free?

Yes, for 100 screenshots a month. The pricing page says the free plan needs no card and has no end date.

### What happens when I use all 100 screenshots?

Once the allowance and any bonus screenshots are exhausted, the API returns quota_exceeded with status 402 until your monthly period resets or you upgrade. The free plan has no overage, so there is no bill.

### Can I make PDFs on the free plan?

Yes. The pricing page lists PDF output on all plans, and a PDF counts as one screenshot.
