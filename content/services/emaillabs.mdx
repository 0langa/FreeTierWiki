EmailLabs is an email delivery service run by Vercom S.A. Apps send through its Email RESTful API or an SMTP relay, and the dashboard shows delivery statistics.

## What the free plan gives you

The Startup plan costs 0 EUR a month. The pricing page lists it as up to 9 000 emails per month, which means nine thousand, with a daily limit of 300 emails. It includes the Email RESTful API and SMTP relay, email templates, basic email analytics, and 1 day of log retention.

It also lists email address validation against EmailLabs' internal list of addresses that do not exist, and sending adjusted to local provider requirements. Mail goes out from a shared IP address, and you register for the Startup plan from the pricing page. The pricing page does not say whether the Startup plan needs a card.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that on the Startup and Essential plans you can only send the messages the plan offers, so sending stops at the plan's volume instead of running up a bill. Overage fees per thousand messages start with the PRO plan.

EmailLabs sends email notifications about the upcoming limit at 33%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of the volume. The next plan up, Essential 30, raises the volume and removes the daily limit, at 23 EUR a month for the first 3 months for new customers and 29 EUR after that.

## Gotchas

- The daily limit of 300 emails can stop a burst of sign-ups well before the monthly amount runs out.
- Logs are kept for 1 day on the Startup plan, so you need to check failures quickly.
- The FAQ also describes a free trial that sales must approve, which allows only test addresses and no production sends; that trial is a different thing from the Startup plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is EmailLabs free?

Yes, on the Startup plan. It costs 0 EUR a month for up to nine thousand emails a month, with 300 a day.

### What happens when I reach the EmailLabs Startup limit?

You can only send the messages the plan includes. EmailLabs emails you as you approach the limit.

### Does the Startup plan include an API?

Yes. The Startup plan lists the Email RESTful API and SMTP relay.
