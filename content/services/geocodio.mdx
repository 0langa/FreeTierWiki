Geocodio’s free Pay-as-you-go offer turns addresses into coordinates and back for the US, Canada, Mexico, Australia, and New Zealand, and can append data such as congressional districts, census identifiers, and time zones. It has a pay-as-you-go option, Flex subscriptions, and an Unlimited plan. The daily free lookups suit developers and analysts who geocode lists now and then.

## What the free plan gives you

Pay-as-you-go starts free: the first 2,500 lookups a day are free, and no credit card is required until you exceed the free tier. All Pay-as-you-go and Self-Service Flex accounts get the first 2,500 credits per day for free. One address or coordinate lookup is 1 credit, and each additional data append is 1 credit. The Pay-as-you-go option includes 1 user, single, batch, and list geocoding, spreadsheet uploads, data appends, distance APIs, and maps, with a rate limit of 1,000 lookups a minute. Free credits are use it or lose it and do not roll over. The pricing card lists 5 API keys, but its detail panel still lists 3; confirm the available key count in your dashboard.

## When you hit a limit

Lookups past the free tier cost $1 per 1,000, and Geocodio charges you on the 1st of the following month for the previous month's usage above the free daily allotment. To prevent runaway bills, you can set a daily usage limit of up to 50,000 lookups, and API requests return 403 Forbidden when that limit is reached; the daily counter resets at midnight EST. You can also buy credits upfront and set your account to limit to the free tier after your credits run out. Without a usage limit, regular Pay-as-you-go billing is the default. If you exceed the free tier with no payment method on file, the API returns a 403 error.

## Gotchas

- Data appends multiply usage: timezone data for 1,000 addresses counts as 2,000 lookups.
- Free Pay-as-you-go coverage is limited to the US, Canada, Mexico, Australia, and New Zealand. The current pricing page offers UK coverage through paid options.
- Free daily credits do not roll over, so a quiet day does not add to a busy one.

## Questions people ask

### Is Geocodio free?

Partly. The first 2,500 lookups a day are free, and lookups past that cost $1 per 1,000.

### Do I need a credit card for Geocodio?

No, not until you exceed the free tier. The pricing page says no credit card is required until then.

### How do I avoid a surprise Geocodio bill?

Set a daily usage limit in your account. Requests fail above that limit instead of being billed.
