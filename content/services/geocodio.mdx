Geocodio turns addresses into coordinates and back for the US, Canada, and Mexico, and can append data such as congressional districts, census identifiers, and time zones. It has a pay-as-you-go option, Flex subscriptions, and an Unlimited plan. The daily free lookups suit developers and analysts who geocode lists now and then.

## What the free plan gives you

Pay-as-you-go starts free: the first 2,500 lookups a day are free, and no credit card is required until you exceed the free tier. All Pay-as-you-go and Flex accounts get the first 2,500 credits per day for free. One address or coordinate lookup is 1 credit, and each additional data append is 1 credit. The Pay-as-you-go option includes 1 user, 3 API keys, single, batch, and list geocoding, spreadsheet uploads, data appends, distance APIs, and maps, with a rate limit of 1,000 lookups a minute. Free credits are use it or lose it and do not roll over.

## When you hit a limit

Lookups past the free tier cost $1 per 1,000, and Geocodio charges you on the 1st of the following month for the previous month's usage above the free daily allotment. To prevent runaway bills, you can set a daily usage limit of up to 50,000 lookups, and requests fail above that limit. You can also buy credits upfront and set your account to limit to the free tier after your credits run out. Unless you remove your payment information or stop usage beyond the free tier, you keep being billed under pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- Data appends multiply usage: timezone data for 1,000 addresses counts as 2,000 lookups.
- Coverage is limited to the US, Canada, and Mexico.
- Free daily credits do not roll over, so a quiet day does not add to a busy one.

## Questions people ask

### Is Geocodio free?

Partly. The first 2,500 lookups a day are free, and lookups past that cost $1 per 1,000.

### Do I need a credit card for Geocodio?

No, not until you exceed the free tier. The pricing page says no credit card is required until then.

### How do I avoid a surprise Geocodio bill?

Set a daily usage limit in your account. Requests fail above that limit instead of being billed.
