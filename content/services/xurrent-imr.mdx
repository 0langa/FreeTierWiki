Xurrent IMR is an incident management and response service: on-call schedules, escalation policies, alert routing, and incident workflows. It used to be Zenduty; the old Zenduty pricing page is now titled "Xurrent IMR (Formerly Zenduty) Pricing Plans". It suits teams that want an on-call tool priced per user.

## What the free plan gives you

The main pricing table lists a trial and paid plans; its FAQ also refers to a Free plan that is absent from the table. The pricing page offers a free 14-day trial with no credit card required, and says every feature is on every plan. You start the trial from any plan.

After the trial, Starter costs $5 per user a month billed yearly, or $6 monthly, for up to 5 users, 1 team, and up to 5 integrations. Growth costs $14 per user a month billed yearly, or $16 monthly, for up to 50 users, 5 teams, and up to 25 integrations, with AI correlation, playbooks, workflows, and SSO. Enterprise is a custom proposal with unlimited users, teams, and integrations.

## When you hit a limit

The current page describes the trial as 14 days of the full platform with no credit card, and says you can cancel by doing nothing. It does not explain exactly which account features remain available afterward.

The FAQ describes automatic paid upgrades above 5 Free users or 10 Starter users and paid Starter SMS/phone overages. That conflicts with the table's five-user Starter allowance and omission of Free. Do not rely on those thresholds as universal current terms. Confirm your account's offered plan and notification charges before adding users.

## Gotchas

- Automatic plan upgrades mean adding one teammate too many can raise your bill without a separate approval step.
- Status pages are available on Growth, not Starter.
- The FAQ and table disagree about Free availability and Starter user limits; confirm the account-specific plan before relying on either.

## Questions people ask

### Is Xurrent IMR free?

It has a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. The plan table lists no free plan after that.

### Is Xurrent IMR the same as Zenduty?

Yes. Zenduty's pricing page now reads "Xurrent IMR (Formerly Zenduty)".

### What happens if I add more users than my plan allows?

The FAQ describes automatic upgrades, but its Starter threshold conflicts with the plan table. Confirm the current limit and upgrade behavior for your account.
