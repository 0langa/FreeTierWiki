Xurrent IMR is an incident management and response service: on-call schedules, escalation policies, alert routing, and incident workflows. It used to be Zenduty; the old Zenduty pricing page is now titled "Xurrent IMR (Formerly Zenduty) Pricing Plans". It suits teams that want an on-call tool priced per user.

## What the free plan gives you

There is a trial, not a free plan. The pricing page offers a free 14-day trial with no credit card required, and says every feature is on every plan. You start the trial from any plan.

After the trial, Starter costs $5 per user a month billed yearly, or $6 monthly, for up to 5 users, 1 team, and up to 5 integrations. Growth costs $14 per user a month billed yearly, or $16 monthly, for up to 50 users, 5 teams, and up to 25 integrations, with AI correlation, playbooks, workflows, and SSO. Enterprise is a custom proposal with unlimited users, teams, and integrations.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 14-day trial ends.

On paid plans, the FAQ says that crossing the Starter user limit automatically upgrades you to the Growth Monthly Plan, and that going over Starter's SMS and phone limits is charged on your next invoice, depending on the country of each user's contact methods. The same FAQ still describes a Free plan of up to 5 users that asks for a credit card, but the plan table no longer lists one.

## Gotchas

- Automatic plan upgrades mean adding one teammate too many can raise your bill without a separate approval step.
- Status pages are available on Growth, not Starter.
- The FAQ and the plan table disagree about a free plan; trust what your account shows after sign-up.

## Questions people ask

### Is Xurrent IMR free?

It has a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. The plan table lists no free plan after that.

### Is Xurrent IMR the same as Zenduty?

Yes. Zenduty's pricing page now reads "Xurrent IMR (Formerly Zenduty)".

### What happens if I add more users than my plan allows?

The FAQ says Xurrent IMR moves you to the next plan automatically.
