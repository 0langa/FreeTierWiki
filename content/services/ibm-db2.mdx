You provision Db2 SaaS from the IBM Cloud catalog, manage it in a web console, and connect with SQL clients or JDBC. It suits learners and prototypers who want a real Db2 database.

## What the free plan gives you

The Lite plan is the free tier of Db2 SaaS and has no time limit. IBM says it is meant for developing or learning about Db2 without charge. You get 200 MB of storage and one database schema on a shared multi-tenant system. The pricing page lists 5 connections, while the docs list 15 connections per user.

The plan is available in the Dallas and London regions. Only community support is available. The paid dedicated plan starts at USD 630 per month. IBM also offers a free Db2 Community Edition to download, with limits of 4 CPU cores and 16 GB of memory.

## When you hit a limit

The docs list the Lite limits as restrictions but do not say what error you see at 200 MB. The limit that matters most is time. You must re-extend the Lite plan every 30 days. If you do not reactivate it, IBM deletes the service 60 days later.

Because the system is shared, performance can change with other users' workloads. IBM recommends an enterprise-level plan for mission-critical or performance-sensitive work.

## Gotchas

- Reactivation is required every 30 days, or the Lite service is deleted after a further 60 days.
- You do not get administrative authority, and Federation and Oracle compatibility are not supported.
- The pricing page and the docs give different connection numbers, so test your connection count early.

## Questions people ask

### Is IBM Db2 free?

Yes. The Lite plan of Db2 SaaS is free with 200 MB of storage and no time limit. You must re-extend it every 30 days.

### What happens if I do not renew the Db2 Lite plan?

If the Lite plan is not reactivated, the service is deleted 60 days later. Reactivation is required every 30 days.

### Can I run IBM Db2 for free on my own machine?

Yes. IBM lists a free Db2 Community Edition on the pricing page, with limits of 4 CPU cores and 16 GB of memory.
