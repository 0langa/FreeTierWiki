You create a store in the Azure portal or with the Azure CLI, then read it from your app through a client library or the REST API. The portal also has a page for managing feature flags.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free tier gives each configuration store 10 MB of regular storage and 10 MB of snapshot storage. Each store can handle 1,000 requests per day. A subscription can have 3 free stores per region.

Revision history is kept for seven days on the Free and Developer tiers. A single key-value can be at most 10 KB, including its label, content type, and tags. The FAQ describes the Free tier as a way to evaluate the service in non-production environments.

## When you hit a limit

When a Free tier store reaches 1,000 requests in a day, it returns HTTP status code 429 for all requests until midnight UTC. Your app then gets errors instead of settings until the next day. The pricing page shows the same rule, with the quota ending at the end of the day.

To get more room, move to a paid tier. The Developer tier allows 6,000 requests per hour per store and 500 MB of storage. The Standard tier allows 30,000 requests per hour and 1 GB of storage. Paid tiers bill a charge per store per day plus an overage charge per 10,000 requests.

## Gotchas

- The Free tier is limited to 3 stores per region per subscription. Paid tiers have no limit on stores.
- The Free tier has no SLA.
- The Free tier does not include features such as geo-replication and soft-delete, which come with other tiers.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure App Configuration free?

Yes, there is a Free tier. Each store gets 1,000 requests per day and 10 MB of storage, and you can have 3 free stores per region per subscription.

### What happens when I go over the Azure App Configuration free quota?

The store returns HTTP status 429 for all requests until midnight UTC. The pricing page lists the Free tier as free, with no overage charge.

### Does Azure App Configuration need an Azure subscription?

Yes. The quickstart asks for an Azure account with an active subscription, and this atlas lists the plan as needing a card.
