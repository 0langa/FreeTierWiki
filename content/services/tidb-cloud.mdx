You work in the TiDB Cloud console, where an AI-assisted SQL Editor runs queries in the browser, or you connect with any MySQL tool, driver, or ORM. It suits developers who want MySQL compatibility with room to grow and optional vector search.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists Starter from $0 a month. Each organization gets 25 GiB of row storage, 25 GiB of column storage, and 250M Request Units for free every month. The pricing details page splits this per instance: each of your first 5 instances gets 5 GiB of row storage, 5 GiB of column storage, and 50 million Request Units a month.

Starter scales down to zero, and you pay only for storage and capacity you use beyond the free amount. An organization also includes capacity for up to 100 active instances and branches at no extra charge. Starter is generally available on AWS.

## When you hit a limit

A free instance that uses up its quota is throttled. Once it reaches the quota, it denies new connection attempts until you raise the quota or the usage resets at the start of a new month. Existing connections stay open but are throttled. For example, a free instance with more than 5 GiB of row storage restricts new connections.

You can raise the quota by setting a monthly spending limit. This needs a credit card. Extra storage then costs $0.20 per GiB and extra usage costs $0.10 per 1M Request Units. The instance is not throttled until it reaches the spending limit.

## Gotchas

- The free quota covers only the first 5 instances in an organization. A sixth needs a credit card and a spending limit, unless you delete an earlier instance first.
- Reaching the quota blocks new connections, not only slows them. An app that opens a connection per request can fail until the month resets.
- Starter runs on AWS, and the Alibaba Cloud version is in public preview.

## Questions people ask

### Is TiDB Cloud Starter free?

Yes, up to a quota. Each organization gets 25 GiB of row storage, 25 GiB of column storage, and 250M Request Units free per month.

### What happens when I go over the TiDB Cloud Starter free quota?

A free instance that reaches its quota denies new connections and throttles existing ones until the month resets or you raise the quota. Raising it needs a credit card and a spending limit.

### Does TiDB Cloud Starter need a credit card?

Not for the free quota. You need a card to create more than 5 instances or to set a spending limit that raises the quota.
