Sirv stores your images and serves them through a CDN, resizing, cropping, and optimizing them on the fly through URL options it calls dynamic imaging. It also provides viewers for image zoom and 360 product spins.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month, and the pricing page says no credit card is required. It includes 500 MB of storage and 2 GB of data transfer a month, with unlimited uploads, unlimited image transformations, and unlimited domains served. Up to 3 users can share the account.

Features on Free include 360 spin, image zoom, dynamic imaging, CDN delivery, converting a spin to GIF, FTP and S3 access, and basic email support. Transformed images and files in Trash do not count toward storage, and uploads do not count toward data transfer.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free account passes 500 MB of storage or 2 GB of transfer. Its plan comparison lists flexible storage and data transfer limits, explained as: your account continues working and you upgrade to the next plan within 30 days. The text does not show which plans that row covers.

The Business plan costs $19 a month or $209 a year, starts at 5 GB of storage and 20 GB of monthly transfer, removes Sirv branding, and comes with a 30-day trial.

## Gotchas

- Sirv branding shows on spins and zooms on the Free plan.
- Data transfer counts every optimized file served to visitors, so 2 GB is small for a public site.
- Using your own domain is a Business feature, not part of Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sirv free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month with 500 MB of storage and 2 GB of monthly data transfer.

### Do transformed images count toward storage?

No. The pricing page says transformed images and files in Trash do not count toward the storage limit.

### Can I remove Sirv branding for free?

No. The Free plan shows Sirv branding on spins and zooms; Business removes it.
