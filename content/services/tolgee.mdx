You use Tolgee as a web platform, with SDKs in your app, with a CLI, or through a REST API. It suits developers who want translators to edit text inside the running app.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page offers Tolgee for free with 30,000 hosted words, 10,000 MT credits, and 3 seats. The pricing page adds unlimited projects. You get the Free plan automatically when you sign up, and it asks for no credit card.

Hosted words are the pricing metric. Keys are not, so you can make as many keys as you need. One MT credit is about one translated character for providers such as Google Translate, AWS, and DeepL. For the AI translator, credits follow LLM token use.

You can also self-host Tolgee. The free self-hosted version includes core features, up to 10 seats, and machine translation with your own API keys. SSO and granular permissions need a licensed plan.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ says word-based plans come in tiers, and auto-upgrade is on by default. If you add or import content past the word limit, Tolgee lets the change through. About a day later it emails you that your subscription will move to the higher tier. The move happens at the end of the billing period, based on your word count then.

If you delete content before then and get back under the limit, nothing changes. The word limits have no pay-as-you-go overage. The pricing page does not say whether the Free plan follows this auto-upgrade rule, so check your word count before large imports.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has 3 seats. The paid cloud plans list unlimited seats.
- Going over the limit can move you to a paid tier. The word count is checked at the end of the billing period.
- Self-hosting needs a machine with at least 2 CPU cores and 4 GB of RAM, and the docs recommend more.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tolgee free?

Yes. The cloud Free plan has 30,000 hosted words, 10,000 MT credits, 3 seats, and unlimited projects. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I exceed the Tolgee word limit?

For word-based plans, Tolgee lets the change through. It emails you about a day later and moves you to a higher tier at the end of the billing period, unless you drop under the limit first.

### Can I self-host Tolgee for free?

Yes. The free self-hosted version includes core localization features and up to 10 seats. Advanced features such as SSO need a licensed plan.
