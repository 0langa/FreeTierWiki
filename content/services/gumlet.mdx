Gumlet is a media platform with two products: video hosting and streaming, and an image CDN that resizes, compresses, and converts images on the fly. Each product has its own plans on the pricing page.

## What the free plan gives you

The video Free plan is listed as Free Forever with 1 seat, 60 storage minutes, 100 GB of bandwidth a month, and 60 minutes of live streaming a month. It includes 4K and HDR10 support, an ad free player, password protection and geo-blocking, and unlimited AI generated subtitles.

The image Free plan, also Free Forever, includes 30 GB of image delivery bandwidth a month, unlimited image processing requests, 2 image sources, detailed usage analytics, and GIF and PDF support. Images can be up to 6000x6000 px.

## When you hit a limit

The image plan lists bandwidth overage at $0.5 per GB on Free, so the pricing page implies image traffic past 30 GB is billed rather than stopped. It does not say how that is charged on an account with no payment method.

For video, the FAQ says you can upgrade when you are close to your limits, that streaming overages range from $0.5 to $0.06 per GB depending on the plan, and that on Free you need to upgrade to add more storage. The Creator plan costs $6 a month billed annually.

## Gotchas

- API and MCP access appear among the Creator plan's additions, so the free video plan is meant for the dashboard and player.
- Free video keeps Gumlet branding; removing it is a Creator feature.
- Storage is counted in minutes of video, not gigabytes, so 60 storage minutes is about an hour of uploads in total.

## Questions people ask

### Is Gumlet free?

Yes. Both the video and image products have Free plans listed as Free Forever, with 100 GB and 30 GB of monthly bandwidth respectively.

### Does the free plan include the video API?

The pricing page lists API and MCP access among the Creator plan's features, not the Free plan's.

### What happens if I go over the free image bandwidth?

The image plan lists overage at $0.5 per GB on the Free plan. The page does not describe a hard stop.
