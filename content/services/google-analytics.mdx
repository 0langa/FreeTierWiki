Google Analytics measures how people find and use your website or app, from traffic sources to events and conversions. Google says it gives you these tools free of charge. It suits developers and marketers who want detailed reports, and its BigQuery export lets you run your own SQL over raw events.

## What the free plan gives you

Standard Google Analytics properties are free; Google Analytics 360 is the paid edition, upgraded under a 360 contract. Standard properties allow 25 parameters per event, 25 user properties, 50 event-scoped custom dimensions and 50 custom metrics, and 30 key events. Explorations sample at 10M events per query.

Event-level data retention can be set to 2 or 14 months; 360 adds 26, 38, and 50 months. BigQuery export is included, with a daily export of 1 M events and unlimited streaming export. Data import allows 120 manual uploads per day per property and 10 GB of storage per property. There is no limit on distinctly named events for web data streams.

## When you hit a limit

There is no bill for a standard property, but the limits bite in other ways. When a standard property becomes Large, its event-level retention is automatically cut to 2 months, and event-level data older than that becomes inaccessible and is permanently deleted. Analytics emails property administrators when a property is on pace to reach the limit, and again when retention is reduced.

The retention setting affects explorations and funnel reports, not standard aggregated reports. If an exploration's date range is longer than the retention period, the extra days show no data.

## Gotchas

- Retention settings matter: set it to 14 months early if you want long explorations, since only 2 or 14 months are offered.
- Age, gender, and interest data is always kept for 2 months, whatever your setting.
- Daily BigQuery export is limited to 1 M events, while streaming export is unlimited, so busier properties lean on streaming.

## Questions people ask

### Is Google Analytics free?

Yes. Standard properties are free of charge; Google Analytics 360 is the paid edition with higher limits.

### How long does Google Analytics keep my data?

Event-level data in a standard property can be kept for 2 or 14 months. Standard aggregated reports are not affected by this setting.

### Can I export Google Analytics data to BigQuery for free?

Standard properties include BigQuery export, with a daily export of 1 M events and unlimited streaming export.
