You use the page in a browser, or the `whcli` command to forward traffic to your machine. There is also an API and a docs site for paid workflow features.

## What the free plan gives you

Visiting Webhook.site gives you a free, unique, random URL and email address, and everything sent to them shows up at once. The free version works without a login.

A free URL accepts at most 100 requests or emails. It expires after 7 days, and the data is deleted then. The body of a request must be below 10 megabytes, or Webhook.site answers with a 413 error. The free version also has no login, so anyone who knows the URL ID can see the data.

Paid plans start at $7.5 per month. A paid account keeps URLs forever, accepts unlimited requests and emails, and keeps a long history of recent ones. It also adds Custom Actions workflows.

## When you hit a limit

When a free URL reaches 100 requests or emails, it stops accepting new ones. Senders then get a 429 Too Many Requests response. The docs say the limit is lifted when the URL is upgraded and linked to a Webhook.site account.

When a free URL passes 7 days, it returns a 404 and its data is gone. To go on testing, you can open the page again for a new random URL.

## Gotchas

- Copy the URL shown on the page, not the app address in your browser bar. Using the wrong one causes a 405 Method Not Allowed.
- Calls from another website in the browser need CORS. Click Edit and check the Add CORS headers option.
- Paid URLs are protected with login by default. Free URLs are not, so keep real customer data out of them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Webhook.site free?

Yes. A free URL needs no login, accepts up to 100 requests or emails, and expires after 7 days. Paid plans start at $7.5 per month.

### Why do I get a 429 error from Webhook.site?

A free URL has reached its limit of 100 requests or emails. Upgrade and link the URL to an account to remove the limit, or open a new URL.

### How long does a free Webhook.site URL last?

7 days. After that the URL returns a 404 and the data is deleted.
