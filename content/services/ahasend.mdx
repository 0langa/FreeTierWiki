You can send through the REST API, the SMTP relay, a command line tool, or the official Node.js and Go SDKs. It suits developers who need reliable app email and own a domain they can verify.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes 1,000 emails per month. The pricing page says no credit card is required. You can add 3 sending domains, and the plan includes the REST API, the SMTP relay, sandbox mode, webhooks, and inbound routing. Campaign Builder for newsletters and campaigns is also on every plan, and the docs say the 1,000 free emails are shared by transactional mail and campaigns.

AhaSend keeps message data and metadata for 1 to 7 days on this plan, and new accounts start at 7 days. All message data is stored in the EU. Support on the Free plan is community-only, with best-effort replies. The Free plan has no dedicated IP.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after the 1,000 monthly emails. The docs say AhaSend plans have an included monthly volume with usage-based overage, but they do not describe it for the Free plan. The quickstart lists the monthly allowance as one thing to check when mail does not arrive.

Hourly sending limits are separate and apply to transactional mail. They start low for new accounts and grow with account age, plan, and sending reputation. The docs say support can raise them. Marketing mail uses a separate daily marketing limit. API requests also have their own limit of 100 per second.

## Gotchas

- You cannot send until you verify a sending domain with DNS records. The quickstart says verification keeps deliverability good and stops spoofing.
- Campaigns count against the same 1,000 monthly emails as your app mail, so a newsletter can use up the allowance your sign-up emails need.
- One SMTP message can have up to 50 recipients and 20 MiB in total size, including attachments.

## Questions people ask

### Is AhaSend free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 1,000 emails per month, 3 sending domains, and full API access. The pricing page says no credit card is required.

### Does AhaSend support SMTP?

Yes. You create an SMTP credential in the dashboard and connect to `send.ahasend.com`. The docs list ports 25, 587, and 2525 with STARTTLS.

### Can I send marketing email with AhaSend?

Yes. Campaign Builder is included on every plan, with contact lists, templates, and campaign reports. Marketing mail runs on separate infrastructure with its own daily limit, and it shares the 1,000 free monthly emails with your transactional mail.
