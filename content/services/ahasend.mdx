You can send through the REST API, the SMTP relay, a command line tool, or the official Node.js and Go SDKs. It suits developers who need reliable app email and own a domain they can verify.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes 1,000 emails per month. The pricing page says no credit card is required. You can add 3 sending domains, and the plan includes the REST API, the SMTP relay, sandbox mode, webhooks, and inbound routing.

AhaSend keeps message data and metadata for 1 to 7 days on this plan, and new accounts start at 7 days. All message data is stored in the EU. Support on the Free plan is community-only, with best-effort replies. The Free plan has no dedicated IP.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after the 1,000 monthly emails. The docs say AhaSend plans have an included monthly volume with usage-based overage, but they do not describe it for the Free plan. The quickstart lists the monthly allowance as one thing to check when mail does not arrive.

Hourly sending limits are separate. They start low for new accounts and grow with account age, plan, and sending reputation. The docs say support can raise them. API requests also have their own limit of 100 per second.

## Gotchas

- You cannot send until you verify a sending domain with DNS records. The quickstart says verification keeps deliverability good and stops spoofing.
- AhaSend sends transactional email only. Its free email API page says it avoids marketing email to keep delivery fast.
- One SMTP message can have up to 50 recipients and 20 MiB in total size, including attachments.

## Questions people ask

### Is AhaSend free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 1,000 emails per month, 3 sending domains, and full API access. The pricing page says no credit card is required.

### Does AhaSend support SMTP?

Yes. You create an SMTP credential in the dashboard and connect to `send.ahasend.com` on port 25, 587, or 2525 with STARTTLS. Port 465 is not supported.

### Can I send marketing email with AhaSend?

AhaSend says it sends only transactional email, to keep delivery fast. Use a different service for newsletters and campaigns.
