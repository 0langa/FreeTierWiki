You build workflows in the dashboard, show the Inbox component in your front end, and trigger sends from your server with an SDK. Novu is open source, so you can also run it on your own servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes 10,000 workflow runs a month across email, in-app, SMS, chat, and push. A workflow run is one execution of a workflow for one subscriber. Runs from every environment count toward the same monthly total.

The limits page lists 20 workflows, 20 steps per workflow, and 2 environments for Free. Delay and digest steps can last up to 1 day, and the inbox feed keeps messages for 30 days. The activity feed keeps 1 day of history on Free.

## When you hit a limit

The billing docs say Novu does not stop or throttle your notifications when you go over your included workflow runs. Extra usage rolls into on-demand pricing on paid plans, and the docs do not describe the free plan in that passage.

If you pass the default workflow or step limit, the limits page says you may get restricted functionality or errors. Novu suggests upgrading, and support can set custom limits.

## Gotchas

- Novu does not send email or SMS itself. You connect providers such as SendGrid or Twilio, and you pay them.
- Activity feed retention is 1 day on Free, so debug a failed send soon after it happens.
- Workflow runs count per subscriber, so one workflow sent to 100 people uses 100 runs.

## Questions people ask

### Is Novu free?

Yes. Novu Cloud has a Free plan with 10,000 workflow runs per month and no credit card. The software is also open source, so you can self-host it.

### What happens if I go over the Novu free limit?

The docs say Novu does not stop or throttle notifications when you pass your included runs. Overage pricing is described for paid plans.

### Does Novu send my emails?

Novu routes the message to an email provider you connect, such as SendGrid. You pay that provider for delivery.
