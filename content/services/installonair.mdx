You drag a build into the web uploader and send testers the install link. It suits developers who need a quick over-the-air install for a test round.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says the service is free, with no card and no trial. You can upload IPA and APK builds, share install links and QR codes, install over the air, and email builds to testers. A UDID finder for iOS devices is included.

Builds up to 200MB upload for free. Guest links stay active for 3 days. With a free account they stay active for 7 days, and you can manage every build from your App List. The FAQ says there is no limit on how many builds you upload. The site says these core features will stay free.

## When you hit a limit

When a link expires, testers see a message that the build has expired and they can no longer install it. You can upload the build again for a new free link, or extend the expiry date before it runs out to keep the same link and QR code.

Extending costs one-time payments: $1.89 for 1 month, $4.99 for 6 months, or $9.99 for 1 year, per build. A build larger than 200MB, up to 1GB, needs a $2 upload credit. One credit covers one large upload. Payments go through Razorpay.

## Gotchas

- Expiry is short on the free plan: 3 days for guests and 7 days with an account.
- A build over 200MB needs a paid upload credit, and you must log in to buy it.
- iOS installs still follow your provisioning profile and device UDID rules, so the UDID finder helps but does not replace them.

## Questions people ask

### Is InstallOnAir free?

Yes. The pricing page says uploading, sharing and installing builds are free, with no trial and no card. Only longer links and very large builds cost extra.

### How long does an InstallOnAir link last?

Guest links last 3 days. Links from a free account last 7 days. You can extend any build with a one-time payment.

### What is the InstallOnAir file size limit?

Builds up to 200MB are free. Larger builds, up to 1GB, need a $2 upload credit.
