You manage aliases in the web dashboard. You can also use the mobile apps, a browser extension, or an API key from your account settings. It suits people who only need to receive mail on throwaway addresses.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 per month. It allows unlimited standard aliases under your username and 10 active aliases on the shared domains. You can pick from 2 shared alias domains. Mail forwards to 1 recipient, which is your real email address.

Forwarded mail counts against a bandwidth limit of 10MB per month. The FAQ says that is roughly 140 average emails. The counter resets to zero at the start of each calendar month, and blocked mail does not count. You can create 10 new aliases per hour, and the server throttles forwarding to 200 emails per hour.

Custom domains, extra usernames, and extra recipients are not on the free plan.

## When you hit a limit

Free accounts get an email warning when they pass 80 percent of the bandwidth limit. After the limit, the server rejects delivery attempts to your aliases with a "User over quota" message. That lasts until the next calendar month, or until you upgrade to Lite or Pro. There is no bill on the free plan, so nothing is charged.

If you create more than 10 aliases in an hour, the extra emails are deferred until you are back under the limit.

## Gotchas

- You cannot reply to or send from an alias on the free plan. The message is not delivered, and you get an email saying the feature needs a paid plan.
- Deactivated aliases silently discard mail. The sender does not see an error.
- The FAQ says having multiple free accounts is not acceptable use, and such accounts may be disabled.

## Questions people ask

### Is addy.io free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 per month and needs no credit card. It allows unlimited standard aliases, 10 active shared-domain aliases, and 10MB of bandwidth per month.

### What happens when I go over the addy.io bandwidth limit?

The server rejects mail to your aliases until the next calendar month, or until you upgrade. It emails you a warning once you pass 80 percent of the limit.

### Can I send email from an addy.io alias on the free plan?

No. Replying and sending from an alias are paid features. On a free account the message is not delivered, and addy.io sends you a notice.
