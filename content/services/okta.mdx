You work in the Okta admin console, plus the Okta APIs, SDKs, and the guides on the developer site. This plan suits developers who build or test an integration, not teams that need production sign-in.

## What the free plan gives you

The Okta Integrator Free Plan lets you explore the platform in a non-production environment, for up to 10 active users. It is free, and it has a default rate limit of 100 authentications per minute. Paid plans start with a default of 600 authentications per minute.

The signup page describes it as a place to build, test, and manage integrations, and to test your code and apps. A separate free trial of the Okta platform gives access for 30 days. Okta can also change the plan's limits and features at any time, according to the terms. Okta has no duty to give support for this plan, and any support it gives is at its own choice.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say what Okta does when you pass 10 active users or the rate limit. The terms say Okta may end the agreement if you put an excessive burden on its systems. They also say the plan is free only for the number of active users and features Okta sets.

Inactivity is the clearer limit. The signup page says access expires after 90 consecutive days of inactivity, and the terms say inactive orgs are deactivated after 90 days unless you submit an app to the Okta Integration Network. The pricing page says 180 days, so use the shorter time.

## Gotchas

- The plan is for non-production use only. Do not point real customer traffic at it.
- You must accept the Integrator Free Plan agreement when you sign up. The marketing consent on the form is marked optional. Okta may also name you as a sign-up on its site.
- Existing Okta Developer Edition orgs were set to be deactivated from July 18, 2025. Those users must sign up for a new Integrator org.

## Questions people ask

### Is Okta free?

Partly. The Okta Integrator Free Plan is free for up to 10 active users in a non-production org. A separate trial runs for 30 days.

### Can I use the Okta free plan in production?

No. The pricing page describes it as a non-production environment, and the terms say it is for building and testing integrations.

### Does the Okta free org expire?

Yes, if you do not use it. The signup page says access expires after 90 consecutive days of inactivity.
