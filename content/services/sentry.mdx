You add an SDK to your app, initialize it with a project DSN, and read the results in the Sentry web app. SDKs exist for plain JavaScript and many frameworks and languages. It suits a solo developer who wants errors and traces in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free and limited to one user. It includes 5,000 errors per month, 5 GB of logs and metrics, 5M spans, and 50 replays. It also has 1 uptime monitor and 1 cron monitor, and unlimited projects.

New users also get 5,000 free replays per month for their first 3 months. Every new Sentry account starts with a 14-day free trial of paid features, and the docs say you revert to the Developer plan if you do not upgrade. The Team plan costs $26 per month when billed annually and adds unlimited users plus API and third-party integrations.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing docs say data sent after you have used your reserved volume and your pay-as-you-go budget is dropped, and you are not charged for it. That means you lose monitoring for the rest of the billing cycle.

Extra cron and uptime monitors can only be bought with a pay-as-you-go budget. The docs describe quota management settings, such as spike protection, for controlling which events count. The pricing page does not say how the free plan alerts you when it is close to the limit.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is limited to one user. Unlimited users start on the Team plan.
- API access and third-party integrations start on the Team plan.
- Data over the quota is dropped, so you will not see errors that happen after you run out.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sentry free?

Yes. The Developer plan is free for one user and includes 5,000 errors, 5M spans, 1 uptime monitor, and 1 cron monitor per month.

### What happens when I go over the Sentry free limit?

Data sent after you run out of volume is dropped and not charged, so you lose monitoring until the billing cycle ends.

### Can a team use the Sentry free plan?

No. The Developer plan is limited to one user. The Team plan adds unlimited users and starts at $26 per month on annual billing.
