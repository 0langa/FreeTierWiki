Sentry offers a free Developer tier (5,000 errors, 5 GB logs and metrics, 5M spans per month, 1 uptime monitor, 1 cron monitor) for error and performance monitoring, making it ideal for solo developers on small projects. Teams needing more than one user or higher data volumes will need to upgrade to a paid plan.
