You write tasks and workflows in Python, TypeScript, or Go, and run workers on your own machines that connect to Hatchet. The Hatchet CLI sets up a quickstart project and lets you trigger runs from the terminal. It suits teams that want a queue with retries, schedules, and a dashboard, without running one themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

Hatchet Cloud has a pay-as-you-go plan that starts at $0, in two forms. Without a card on file, you get 2,000 task runs and 2,000 events per day. You also get 10 workers, 2,000 worker slots, 1 tenant, 3 users, 500 MB of active storage, and 1 day of run history.

With a card on file, the plan includes 1M task runs and 1M events per month, and 100 workers, 10 tenants, and 7 days of retention. A task that spawns another task counts as two runs. Failed runs count, but retries do not.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says you get alerts by email or Slack as you approach a limit. If you exceed it, tasks get dropped and you see errors when you try to create new tasks.

With a card on file, you pay nothing until you pass the included million runs and million events. After that you pay $30 per million runs and $5 per million events, billed at the end of each billing cycle of 30 or 31 days. Storage and logs above the included amounts are billed by the GB-hour.

## Gotchas

- The no-card limits are per day: 2,000 task runs and 2,000 events. They are far below the 1M a month you get with a card on file.
- Without a card, the queue backlog is 10,000 tasks. When the backlog is full, new tasks are rejected until it drains.
- Your workers run on your own infrastructure. Hatchet orchestrates tasks but does not run your code for you.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hatchet free?

Yes, to start. Without a card you get 2,000 task runs and 2,000 events per day. With a card on file, the first 1M runs and 1M events each month are included.

### Does Hatchet need a credit card?

No, not to get started. The pricing page says no card is needed, but the larger 1M monthly allowance applies to accounts with a card on file.

### What happens when I hit the Hatchet free limit?

Tasks get dropped, and creating new tasks returns errors. Hatchet alerts you by email or Slack as you approach the limit.
