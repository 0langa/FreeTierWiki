You manage accounts and users in a web dashboard and connect with the NATS command line tool or a NATS client library. It suits developers who already know NATS or want to learn it on a managed system.

## What the free plan gives you

The Personal plan costs $0 per month and asks for no credit card. The marketing page lists these resources: 2 accounts, 10 connections, 2 leaf nodes, a 1 MiB maximum message size, 10 GiB of network data, and 5 GiB of storage.

An account is a space where you allocate resources. You can create several and share streams and services between them. You split the plan's total across accounts, so a plan upgrade is not needed just to shift usage around.

The FAQ says a free account lasts as long as you use it. Synadia may delete inactive free accounts.

## When you hit a limit

On the Personal plan, a network data overage drops active and new client connections. They stay dropped until the next billing cycle, or until you upgrade. Nothing is billed.

The FAQ also says some other limits exist, such as subscriptions per connection and JetStream assets. They protect the system, and Synadia says it can often raise them for free if you contact it.

The Starter plan costs $49 per month and the Pro plan costs $199 per month. Upgrade from the Billing tab on the Team Settings page.

## Gotchas

- The FAQ says GDPR compliance is not guaranteed with a free account.
- The pricing docs recommend an annual Enterprise contract with a support SLA for production deployments.
- Lost private credentials cannot be recovered, because Synadia never stores them. You would create a new account.

## Questions people ask

### Is Synadia Cloud free?

Yes. The Personal plan costs $0 per month, needs no credit card, and gives you 10 connections, 5 GiB of storage, and 10 GiB of network data.

### What happens when I go over the Synadia free plan limits?

If you go over the network data limit, Synadia drops active and new client connections until the next billing cycle or an upgrade. There is no bill on the Personal plan.

### Can Synadia delete my free account?

It can. The FAQ says a free account lasts as long as you use it, and Synadia reserves the right to delete inactive free accounts.
