You use Teamplify in the browser after you connect your tools, and a self-hosted version is available. It suits small engineering teams and their managers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free for up to 5 users. It includes 30 days of historical data analysis and unlimited data integrations. The integrations page lists Asana, Bitbucket, GitHub, GitLab, Google, Jira, Linear, Mattermost, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Teams, Okta, Slack, Trello, YouTrack, and Zoom, and says Teamplify Cloud supports them on all plans.

The product also covers time tracking, time off management, and Smart Standups, which are written async standups. The paid Team plan costs $2.5 per user billed yearly. It adds 1 year of historical analysis and beta API access. Business costs $3 per user billed yearly and adds nested teams.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan stops at 5 users. The pricing page does not say what happens when you add a sixth user, so assume you need a paid plan or a trial first. Larger teams can use an unlimited 14-day trial, with no credit card required.

The pricing FAQ says a paid plan renews until you cancel, and it stays active until the end of the current billing period. Free has no billing, so nothing is charged.

## Gotchas

- Free covers 30 days of history. A year of history needs the Team plan or higher.
- API access is in beta and starts on the Team plan.
- Paid seats count only active members. When you remove one mid-cycle, the unused part becomes Teamplify Credits.

## Questions people ask

### Is Teamplify free?

Yes, for up to 5 users. The Free plan includes 30 days of historical data analysis and unlimited data integrations.

### Does Teamplify need a credit card?

No. The pricing FAQ says no credit card is required to start a trial. You can sign up and start right away.

### How much history can Teamplify analyze for free?

The Free plan covers 30 days of history. The Team plan covers 1 year.
