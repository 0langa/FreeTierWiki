Video Indexer runs a set of AI models over video and audio to pull out transcripts, translations, detected objects, and summaries. You upload media on the Video Indexer website or call its API, and browse the insights per video. It suits developers who want to try video analysis on their own files before building it into an app.

## What the free plan gives you

Video Indexer offers a free trial account that does not need an Azure subscription. Microsoft's overview docs say the trial provides up to 2,400 minutes of free indexing through the Video Indexer website. The pricing page describes the trial differently, as up to 10 hours of free indexing for website users and up to 40 hours for API users.

Trial accounts apply only to the cloud-based service, not the Azure Arc edge version, and they are not available in Azure Government. You can upload up to 10 files at a time, from your computer or from a URL, and choose the spoken language for each file.

## When you hit a limit

The trial is a fixed amount of indexing, not a monthly allowance. Because the trial account is not connected to an Azure subscription, it cannot bill you; once the minutes are used, you need a paid account to keep indexing.

A paid unlimited account is a Video Indexer resource connected to a paid Azure subscription. Paid pricing is based on the duration of the input file, with audio analysis, video analysis, or both, in bundles at different price points.

## Gotchas

- The docs and the pricing page state the trial amount in different ways, so plan around the smaller figure that applies to you.
- If you don't use your trial account for 12 months, it can be deleted; sign in at least once every 12 months to keep it.
- The trial is meant for evaluation; production deployments need a Video Indexer resource in your Azure subscription.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Video Indexer free?

There is a free trial account with no Azure subscription. The docs list up to 2,400 minutes of indexing on the website, and the pricing page lists 10 hours on the website and 40 hours through the API.

### Do I need an Azure subscription for the trial?

No. Microsoft says you can try a free trial Video Indexer account with your content without an Azure subscription.

### Will my trial account be deleted?

It can be, if you don't use it for 12 months. Signing in at least once every 12 months keeps it.
