You manage flags and experiments in the GrowthBook web app, then read them in your code through an SDK. It suits product and engineering teams that want one tool for flags and tests.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan on GrowthBook Cloud is free and needs no credit card. It allows up to 3 users and 1 project. You get unlimited feature flags, unlimited experiments, and unlimited traffic.

Caching and delivery have an allowance: 1M CDN requests and 5GB of CDN bandwidth per month. The pricing page says a seat equals a user, and every user needs their own login. The self-hosted plan is free too and has unlimited users. The docs say the same open-source code runs the Cloud platform and your own servers.

## When you hit a limit

If you go over the CDN allowance, the app shows a "CDN usage exceeded" warning. The FAQ says GrowthBook will work with you on a fix, such as raising limits, cutting your usage, or moving to a custom plan.

The FAQ also lists overage prices that apply after you exceed included usage: $10 per extra million CDN requests and $1 per extra GB of CDN bandwidth. The pricing page does not say whether free accounts are billed these amounts. No card is on file for a free account.

## Gotchas

- The free cloud plan stops at 3 users and 1 project. The Pro plan raises that to 30 users and 3 projects.
- Sequential testing, CUPED variance reduction, sticky bucketing, and URL split tests are listed under the Pro plan.
- SSO and SCIM belong to the Enterprise plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is GrowthBook free?

Yes. The Starter plan on the cloud is free for up to 3 users with unlimited feature flags and experiments. The self-hosted plan is also free and has unlimited users.

### Does GrowthBook need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says "No credit card required" for the free cloud plan.

### What happens when I go over the GrowthBook free limits?

You see a "CDN usage exceeded" warning in the app, and GrowthBook works with you on a solution. Paid overage prices exist, but the page does not say they are billed to free accounts.
