Squidex is a headless CMS: you define content schemas, editors fill them in, and your site or app reads the content through an API and CDN. It is open source under the MIT license and can be self-hosted, and Squidex Cloud is the hosted service in Frankfurt, Germany. A developer would pick it for a hosted CMS with a free plan and the option to move to their own servers later.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan on Squidex Cloud is free. It includes twenty thousand API calls (the page writes 20.000), 2 GB of traffic, 500 MB of storage, and 2 contributors, plus a contents CDN and an assets CDN.

There is one condition: the free plan needs the Squidex label on each website page or in your mobile applications. The Basic plan raises the limits to one hundred thousand API calls, 20 GB of traffic, 5 GB of storage, and 10 contributors. All plans are also available yearly, with 2 months free per year.

## When you hit a limit

API calls and traffic behave differently. For API calls, Squidex says the limit for the free plan is twenty thousand, and when you reach the limit your API calls get blocked; it says it will inform you when it can foresee that you will reach your contingent.

For traffic, the FAQ says that if you reach your limit, your app stays active, but additional traffic is charged at 0,13€ per GB, passed on from Google Cloud with a small surcharge. The page does not say how this applies to a free account.

## Gotchas

- The required Squidex label on every page may not suit a client or commercial site.
- Blocked API calls at the limit mean your site can stop loading content; the FAQ settles extra calls on paid plans at the end of the month.
- The pricing page writes numbers in European style, so 20.000 means twenty thousand.

## Questions people ask

### Is Squidex free?

Yes. The Starter plan on Squidex Cloud is free, and the software is open source under the MIT license.

### What happens when I run out of API calls on Squidex Starter?

Squidex says that when you reach the limit, your API calls get blocked. The free plan's limit is twenty thousand calls.

### Do I have to show a Squidex label?

Yes, on the free plan. Starter needs the Squidex label on each website page or in your mobile applications.
