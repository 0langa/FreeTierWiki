You manage FlareWarden in a web dashboard. A Smart Setup step finds content and third-party services on your site and creates monitors for them. It suits freelancers and small sites that want uptime checks and a status page without a card.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and does not expire. It includes 15 total monitors, 5 minute checks, 1 status page, 2 team members, and 2 webhooks. Monitors can be uptime, content, cron job, heartbeat, or third-party service checks, and they all share one limit of 15.

Check history goes back up to 90 days on Free. Alerts reach you by email by default, and Slack and Discord are built in through webhooks. The pricing page says every monitoring feature is available on every tier, even Free, and that paid plans mainly add scale and faster checks.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to add a sixteenth monitor on the Free plan. The monitors share one limit, and the page offers upgrades instead.

Paid plans give more room: Starter costs $10 per month for 30 monitors and 1 minute checks. You can add 10 extra monitors for $5 per month on any paid plan. Paid plans start with a 7 day free trial that needs a card. The Free plan needs none.

## Gotchas

- SMS alerts are not available yet. The pricing page lists SMS credits as coming soon, as a paid add-on.
- Email verification is required before the account is active.
- Free check intervals are 5 minutes, while Business and higher plans list 30 second checks.

## Questions people ask

### Is FlareWarden free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 15 monitors, 5 minute checks, and 1 status page. It needs no credit card and has no expiry.

### Does FlareWarden need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan. Paid plans start with a 7 day trial that requires a card.

### Does FlareWarden send SMS alerts?

Not yet. The pricing page lists SMS credits as coming soon, as a paid add-on. Free alerts go by email, Slack, Discord, or webhook.
