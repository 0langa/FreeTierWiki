Huly is an open-source "everything app" for teams, combining project management, team planner, chat, virtual office, and documents. Huly Cloud's Common plan is free forever with unlimited users and no credit card, up to 10 GB of storage and 10 GB of audio/video traffic per workspace; teams needing more can self-host the open-source platform instead.
