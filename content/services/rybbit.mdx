Rybbit is a web analytics tool that runs without cookies and is open source. It covers pageviews, sessions, custom events, funnels, journeys, retention, web vitals, and error tracking, with session replay on its higher plan. It suits developers who want a privacy-friendly replacement for Google Analytics.

## What the free plan gives you

Rybbit offers a free trial rather than a permanent free plan. You start a 7-day free trial on a paid plan, and the pricing page says there are no credit card charges until the trial ends.

During the trial you use the plan you picked. Standard, from $13 a month billed annually, covers up to 5 websites and up to 3 team members with custom events, funnels, advanced filtering, bot filtering, journeys, retention, and email reports, and keeps data for 3 years. Pro, from $26 a month billed annually, adds unlimited websites and team members, session replays, 5 year data retention, a higher API rate limit, and priority support.

## When you hit a limit

The trial lasts 7 days. The wording on the pricing page, no credit card charges until the trial ends, suggests that billing can begin once the trial is over, so cancel before then if you do not plan to keep it. The page does not describe what happens to your data if you cancel.

On paid plans, price is set by monthly pageviews, from 100,000 up to 50M and more, and you pick the tier that fits your traffic. The page does not say what happens if a site goes over the pageviews of its tier.

## Gotchas

- There is no free plan to fall back to after the 7 days on the current pricing page.
- Session replay is a Pro feature, not part of Standard.
- Annual billing is what the headline prices assume; monthly billing costs more.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rybbit free?

Not permanently on the hosted service. Paid plans start with a 7-day free trial.

### Will I be charged after the Rybbit trial?

The pricing page says there are no credit card charges until the trial ends, so expect billing to start after the 7 days unless you cancel.

### Does Rybbit include session replay?

Yes, on the Pro plan. Standard does not list session replays.
