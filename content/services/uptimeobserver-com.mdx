You use UptimeObserver in a web dashboard, and the home page says setup takes about 2 minutes. Checks run from several regions. It suits indie makers and small teams, and the Free plan's chat alerts go through Telegram.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan lists 20 monitors with a 5-minute monitoring interval and 1 status page. It includes 1 email alert recipient and 2 webhook alerts. Free monitor types are HTTPS, DNS, and port checks; the plan card marks SSL and domain name monitoring as not included. Alerts can go out by email, Telegram, Pushover, or webhook, and Slack and Discord alerts need a paid plan.

New paid subscribers can try Indie or Pro for 14 days. The trial requires a credit card and charges when it ends unless you cancel. Indie costs €9 per month and has 50 monitors, 1-minute checks, 5 status pages, 3 email recipients, and 20 webhook alerts. Pro costs €29 per month with 200 monitors and 30-second checks. The vendor says existing paid subscribers keep their price and monitor allowance.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan lists a 20-monitor allowance. See the paid plans if you need more monitors. UptimeObserver gives no details on free limit enforcement.

Free signup needs no credit card. The pricing page gives no SMS allowance, while the integrations page advertises Twilio SMS; its plan eligibility and external messaging costs are not established here. You can use Telegram or push services such as Pushover for urgent alerts instead.

## Gotchas

- Free has 1 email alert recipient and 2 webhook alerts, so a team that wants many recipients needs a paid plan.
- The free interval is 5 minutes, and the Free card marks Slack alerts, Discord alerts, SSL monitoring, and domain name monitoring as not included.
- No SMS alerts are listed on any plan. The integrations page lists Twilio SMS as an integration, so check how it applies to your plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is UptimeObserver free?

Yes. The Free plan has 20 monitors, 5-minute checks, 1 status page, and alerts by email, Telegram, Pushover, and webhook.

### Does UptimeObserver have a free trial?

Indie and Pro each have a 14-day trial. You enter a credit card and get charged when the trial ends unless you cancel beforehand. The ongoing Free plan needs no card.

### Does UptimeObserver send SMS alerts?

The pricing page lists no SMS alerts on any plan. The integrations page lists Twilio SMS as an integration.
