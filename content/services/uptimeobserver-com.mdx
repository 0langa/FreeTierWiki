You use UptimeObserver in a web dashboard, and the home page says setup takes about 2 minutes. Checks run from several regions. It suits indie makers and small teams that live in Slack, Discord, or Telegram.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan lists 20 monitors with a 5-minute monitoring interval and 1 status page. It includes 1 email alert recipient and 2 webhook alerts. Monitor types include HTTPS, SSL, domain name, DNS, and port checks. Alerts can go out by email, Telegram, Pushover, Slack, or Discord.

The pricing page also advertises a 14-day free trial. The paid Indie plan costs 5 EUR per month and has 50 monitors, 1-minute checks, 5 status pages, 3 email recipients, and 20 webhook alerts. The Pro plan costs 20 EUR per month with 200 monitors and 30-second checks.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach a free limit, and it has no FAQ section. It lists fixed counts per plan, so the safe reading is that extra monitors need a paid plan.

The free plan shows no price and no card step on the pricing page. The page also lists no SMS alerts on any plan, so SMS is not an upgrade path here. You can use chat apps or push services such as Pushover for urgent alerts instead.

## Gotchas

- Free has 1 email alert recipient and 2 webhook alerts, so a team that wants many recipients needs a paid plan.
- The free interval is 5 minutes, and the 30-second interval starts on Pro.
- No SMS alerts are listed on any plan. The integrations page lists Twilio SMS as an integration, so check how it applies to your plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is UptimeObserver free?

Yes. The Free plan has 20 monitors, 5-minute checks, 1 status page, and alerts by email, Telegram, Pushover, Slack, and Discord.

### Does UptimeObserver have a free trial?

The pricing page says there is a 14-day free trial, and you can cancel or change plans at any time. The Free plan stays available.

### Does UptimeObserver send SMS alerts?

The pricing page lists no SMS alerts on any plan. The integrations page lists Twilio SMS as an integration.
