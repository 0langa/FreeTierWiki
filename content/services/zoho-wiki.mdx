You write in a collaborative editor in the browser. Content lives in spaces, which hold manuals, and courses are shared separately with your hub.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan has 5 users, 3 spaces, 5 manuals, 5 courses, and 1 GB of storage. It also includes 1 question bank with 20 questions for quizzes. Manuals hold your written knowledge, and courses turn it into lessons that learners work through.

Features include knowledge manuals, course creation with text, video, and document lessons plus a quiz, a timeline view of activities, universal search, and encryption at rest. Zoho says it does not ask for a credit card during the trial.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 5 manuals or 1 GB. It lists higher limits on paid plans instead. The Express plan lists 25 courses and 1 GB per user, and a higher plan lists unlimited courses and 5 GB per user.

When a trial ends, your account is downgraded to the Free plan, or you can buy a monthly or yearly subscription. Your trial account becomes a permanent account, and all data and customizations are kept.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page header says the trial is 7 days, while its FAQ says 30 days with full Professional access. Check the sign-up screen for the real length.
- Spaces are capped at 3 on the free plan, and manuals live inside spaces or stay private, so plan your structure first.
- The free question bank holds 20 questions, so keep quizzes short on the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Learn free?

Yes, for up to 5 users. The free plan has 3 spaces, 5 manuals, 5 courses, and 1 GB of storage.

### Does Zoho Learn need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is needed for the trial, and your account falls back to the free plan if the trial ends.

### Can I build courses on the Zoho Learn free plan?

Yes, up to 5 courses. Lessons can use text, video, or documents, and a course can end with a quiz.
