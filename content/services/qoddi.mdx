Qoddi is a cloud platform that hosts static sites, web apps, APIs, workers, and datastores, and also sells GPU and AI inference capacity. You deploy from a git repository, and Qoddi builds and serves the result. Its free offer covers static sites only, which suits a portfolio, documentation, or a landing page.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists static sites as free for your first 3 static sites. Each includes git deployments, unlimited bandwidth, and unlimited team members. The page describes static sites as a home for your portfolio, docs, or next big idea.

Preview environments created after a git push are included at no additional charge. They are deleted automatically after promotion to production or after 48 hours. Teams and team members carry no separate seat fee.

App hosting for web apps, APIs, workers, and datastores is not free: it starts at $6 a month for 1 GB of RAM and 1 vCPU on the XS size.

## When you hit a limit

The free amount is a count of sites, not a usage meter. Additional static sites are $3 a month per app, so a fourth site starts a charge on the card you verified with.

Paid app usage is prorated by the second and capped at the displayed monthly price for each size, and the pricing page says monthly estimates assume continuous use over a 31-day month. The page does not describe any stop for static sites, since bandwidth on them is unlimited.

## Gotchas

- A credit or debit card is required to verify your account even if you host only free static sites, and prepaid cards are not accepted.
- Adding a fourth static site is billed at $3 a month, so remove old sites if you want to stay at zero.
- Docker and marketplace apps include 50 GB of persistent storage, and extra storage is billed per GB per day, which matters once you move past static sites.

## Questions people ask

### Is Qoddi free?

Partly. Your first 3 static sites are free; app hosting starts at $6 a month.

### Do I need a card for a free Qoddi site?

Yes. Qoddi says a credit or debit card is required to verify your account, even when you host only free static sites.

### How much does an extra static site cost?

Additional static sites are $3 a month per app.
