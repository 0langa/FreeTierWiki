You create gates and experiments in the Statsig console and call them from an SDK in your app. It suits developers and small product teams who want flags and experiments without paying per seat.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer tier is free and needs no credit card. When you sign up, you start on it automatically. It gives you free access to gates, configs, experimentation, and analytics. You get 2 million events per month at no charge, unlimited flag and config checks, 50,000 session replays per month, and unlimited seats.

Statsig bills by metered events, which are the events and exposures your app sends. Experiment checks with no allocation, and flags that are fully shipped or disabled, do not generate metered events. Statsig also dedupes repeat exposure events for the same user. By default, one event dimension with each log event is free.

The Developer tier keeps 1 year of analytics data. The Pro tier, at $150 per month, includes 5M events and unlimited analytics retention.

## When you hit a limit

If you go over the 2 million event limit, Statsig shows you a prompt to enter a credit card and upgrade to Pro. If you stay on Developer, gate and config targeting keep working. Metric Lift calculations stop, and you get several warning notifications before that happens.

Pro includes 5M events a month. Past that, you pay $0.05 per additional 1K events. The overage appears on the next bill, charged to the card on file. The Pro base fee of $150 is charged at the start of each period.

## Gotchas

- Going over 2M events does not turn off your flags. It stops Metric Lift results, which are the numbers behind your experiments.
- Statsig calls feature flags feature gates in its console and SDKs. Search for both terms in the docs.
- New gates return false until you add a targeting rule, so a gate with no rule will look broken.

## Questions people ask

### Is Statsig free?

Yes. The Developer tier is free, with 2 million events per month, unlimited flag and config checks, and unlimited seats. It needs no credit card.

### What happens if I exceed the Statsig free event limit?

You are prompted to add a credit card to upgrade to Pro. If you stay on Developer, targeting keeps working and Metric Lift computations stop.

### How much does Statsig Pro cost?

Pro is $150 per month and includes 5M events. Additional events cost $0.05 per 1K.
