You define models in the Schema builder, editors write entries in the Content editor, and your app reads them over GraphQL. It suits small teams where developers and editors share one content source.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan costs $0 and is free forever. It includes 1,000 content entries and 500,000 API calls per month. Assets count against the entry limit. You get 3 seats with 2 standard roles, 2 locales, 10 components, and 1 environment.

Asset storage is unlimited, with a 50 MB maximum per upload. The plan table lists asset traffic at 100 GB. Uncached requests are limited to 5 per second, while cached requests have no rate limit. Hobby also allows 5 webhooks and 3 API tokens.

## When you hit a limit

On Hobby, Hygraph blocks usage until the next billing period. It does not charge you. You can watch your usage on the project dashboard.

The paid Growth plan behaves differently: it charges extra usage to your credit card at the end of the billing cycle. Hobby has no such charge, so a surprise bill is not a risk on the free plan.

## Gotchas

- New projects keep public API access off. You need a Permanent Auth Token, or you must turn on public permissions yourself.
- Do not expose a Permanent Auth Token in front-end code when you mutate data. Keep it on the server.
- Hobby has 2 standard roles and no custom roles, so fine-grained editor permissions need a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hygraph free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free forever and includes 1,000 content entries, 500,000 API calls per month, and 3 seats.

### What happens when I go over the Hygraph free limits?

Hygraph blocks usage until the next billing period. It does not bill you on Hobby.

### Does Hygraph need a credit card?

No. You can sign up for Hobby with an email address or a GitHub, Facebook, or Google profile. The 30-day Enterprise trial does not need a card either.
