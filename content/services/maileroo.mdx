Maileroo is an email delivery service for developers. You send through its SMTP relay or REST API, route inbound mail to your app, and catch test emails in SMTP sinks, all from one account.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and sends up to 3,000 outbound emails a month. It also receives up to 1,000 inbound emails a month and includes 2 sending domains. The pricing page says you can start without a credit card.

The SMTP relay, the RESTful email API, suppression handling, resending from logs, and catch-all, wildcard, and regex matching for inbound mail are listed as included on every plan. For testing, the free column lists 100 test emails a month, 3 SMTP sinks, and 5 inboxes, so you can point a staging app at a sink instead of real recipients.

## When you hit a limit

Maileroo's home-page FAQ says additional outbound emails are not sent after the monthly quota is exhausted, until the quota resets or you upgrade. The 12% quota buffer is for paying users. Hourly sending limits also apply as an anti-abuse measure; you can request a higher hourly limit through the dashboard.

The next plan up is Basic at $10 a month, which raises outbound and inbound mail to 25,000 a month each and removes the cap on sending domains. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time, according to the page.

## Gotchas

- Only 2 sending domains are included on the free plan, so a third project needs a paid plan or a shared domain.
- Inbound mail has its own monthly count of 1,000, separate from the 3,000 outbound emails.
- Maileroo Verify, the one-time code product, is not part of the free plan: it costs $0.03 plus a channel fee per successful verification from a prepaid balance.

## Questions people ask

### Is Maileroo free?

Yes, within limits. The free plan sends up to 3,000 emails a month and receives up to 1,000.

### Do I need a credit card for the Maileroo free plan?

No. The pricing page says the free plan needs no credit card.

### Does the free plan include SMTP?

Yes. The pricing page lists the SMTP relay and the REST API as included on every plan, the free plan among them.
