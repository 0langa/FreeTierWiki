You work in a web app. You make a mailbox, send it emails or files, and Parseur extracts the fields you need. You then export them or connect them to other tools. It suits people with no coding background.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan includes up to 20 pages per month and costs 0 euros. It needs no credit card. You get unlimited mailboxes and unlimited extracted fields, and both the AI parsing engine and the template engine. Pages are credits, and 1 page equals 1 credit. For a PDF, every page counts. An email or a spreadsheet counts as a single page, however long it is. A three-page PDF costs 3 credits. The free plan has 1 user and keeps documents for 90 days. It connects to Excel, CSV, JSON, Google Sheets, Zapier, Power Automate, Make.com, webhooks, and the API. Chat and email support is not on the free plan.

## When you hit a limit

At 90% usage, Parseur sends you an email so you can upgrade or pause parsing. At 100%, new documents stop being processed and are marked as Quota exceeded. You can upgrade, buy top up credits, turn on early auto-renewal, or wait for the next renewal. Nothing is lost. Once you have credits again, you select your documents in the mailbox and reprocess them. Unused credits expire at the end of each billing period.

## Gotchas

- Free credits do not carry over. Unused credits expire at the end of each period.
- Documents stay for 90 days on the free plan. The base paid tier keeps them for 1 year.
- The free plan has 1 user. Multi-user accounts for more people start on the Scale tier, which begins at 10,000 credits a month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Parseur free?

Yes. The free plan includes up to 20 pages every month, with unlimited mailboxes and fields. No credit card is needed.

### What happens when I reach my Parseur credit limit?

At 90% you get an email. At 100%, new documents are marked Quota exceeded and stop being processed. You can reprocess them once you have credits again.

### How does Parseur count pages?

Each PDF page costs 1 credit. An email or a spreadsheet counts as a single page, even a CSV file with 100 lines.
