You use Helicone through its dashboard and an OpenAI-compatible gateway: you change the base URL of your SDK and your calls show up as logged requests. It suits solo developers who want to see what their LLM app sends.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free. It includes 10,000 requests per month, 1 GB of storage, and 1 seat in 1 organization. The pricing page lists data retention of 7 days for Hobby. The docs describe the AI Gateway as one OpenAI-compatible API with access to 100+ models, so you can log calls to several providers in one place.

Paid plans start at $79 per month for Pro, which adds unlimited seats, alerts and reports, and a query language. The Pro and Team plans carry a 7-day free trial, and Pro and above charge usage-based pricing above the included 10,000 requests and 1 GB.

## When you hit a limit

The expanded pricing FAQ says Free continues logging requests in the background beyond its 10,000 requests and 1 GB allowance. Accessing those requests requires upgrading to Pro. The comparison table also limits Hobby ingestion to 10 logs per minute and API access to 10 calls per minute.

Pro and Team automatically bill extra usage at tiered rates. The expanded free-plan FAQ explicitly says no credit card is needed; it does not apply those paid-plan charges to Hobby.

## Gotchas

- The current pricing banner says Helicone is no longer part of Mintlify and that details will follow. It does not establish a new maintenance commitment or shutdown date.
- Data is kept for 7 days on Hobby, so older requests are gone.
- Hobby has 1 seat and 1 organization, so it does not suit a team.

## Questions people ask

### Is Helicone free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free with 10,000 requests per month, 1 GB of storage, and 1 seat. The expanded free-plan FAQ says no credit card is required.

### How long does Helicone keep my requests on the free plan?

The pricing page lists 7 days of retention for Hobby. Pro keeps data for 1 month.

### Is Helicone still maintained?

The pricing page still offers Hobby and paid plans, but its banner says Helicone is no longer part of Mintlify, with details pending. The earlier acquisition announcement does not establish its current maintenance policy.
