Meilisearch Cloud is the managed version of Meilisearch, an open source search engine for full-text, vector, and hybrid search. Meilisearch runs the infrastructure, applies upgrades, and provides email support. It suits developers who want fast, typo-tolerant search in an app without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free Cloud plan. The pricing page offers a 14-day free trial for your project, with no credit card required. After that, Cloud starts at $20 a month and includes fully managed infrastructure and email support, with usage-based or resource-based billing.

The cost estimator shows the two models. Usage-based billing starts with a base plan of 100K documents and 50K searches for $30 a month. Resource-based billing prices an instance plus disk, for example an XS instance with 0.5 vCPU and 1 GB of RAM at $18 a month plus $5 for disk, $23 in total.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 14-day trial ends or when usage passes the base plan's documents and searches. The estimator suggests sizing your plan from monthly documents, monthly searches, and average document size, and you can pick resource-based billing if you prefer to pay for a fixed instance instead of usage. Server sizes and regions are listed on a separate page.

## Gotchas

- The trial is the only free hosted option; the free option on the page is self-hosting.
- Documents with embeddings count as a larger average size in the estimator, which raises the price.
- Enterprise features such as SSO SAML and sharding need a sales conversation.

## Questions people ask

### Is Meilisearch Cloud free?

Not permanently. It has a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, then plans start at $20 a month.

### Can I run Meilisearch for free?

Yes, by self-hosting the open source engine, which the page says is free but leaves updates, backups, and scaling to you.

### Which billing model should I choose?

The page offers usage-based billing by documents and searches, or resource-based billing by instance size; the estimator compares them for your workload.
