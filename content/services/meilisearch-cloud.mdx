Meilisearch Cloud is the managed version of Meilisearch, an open source search engine for full-text, vector, and hybrid search. Meilisearch runs the infrastructure and applies upgrades. It suits developers who want fast, typo-tolerant search in an app without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free Cloud plan. The pricing page offers a 14-day free trial for your project, with no credit card required. After that, Cloud starts at $20 a month and includes fully managed infrastructure, with usage-based or resource-based billing.

The cost estimator shows the two models. Usage-based billing starts with a base plan of 100K documents and 50K searches for $30 a month. Resource-based billing prices an instance plus disk, for example an XS instance with 0.5 vCPU and 1 GB of RAM at $18 a month plus $5 for disk, $23 in total.

## When you hit a limit

The public pricing and billing guides do not specify trial-end suspension, deletion, retention, or automatic continuation. Export test data you need and confirm those terms before relying on the trial for a live application. On the paid usage-based Build plan, searches above 50K per month cost $0.40 per 1,000 and documents above 100K cost $0.30 per 1,000. The base fee is charged upfront and extra usage at the end of the cycle. Resource-based projects instead charge for provisioned compute and disk, plus bandwidth; rates can vary by region.

## Gotchas

- The trial is the only free hosted option; the free option on the page is self-hosting.
- Documents with embeddings count as a larger average size in the estimator, which raises the price.
- Enterprise features such as SSO SAML and sharding need a sales conversation.

## Questions people ask

### Is Meilisearch Cloud free?

Not permanently. It has a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, then plans start at $20 a month.

### Can I run Meilisearch for free?

Yes, by self-hosting the open source engine, which the page says is free but leaves updates, backups, and scaling to you.

### Which billing model should I choose?

The page offers usage-based billing by documents and searches, or resource-based billing by instance size; the estimator compares them for your workload.
