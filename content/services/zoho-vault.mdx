You use Zoho Vault through browser extensions, mobile apps, or the web vault. The Free plan works as a personal password manager, and the paid plans add sharing and admin tools. It suits individuals and people who already use Zoho apps.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page labels the Free plan as permanently free. It includes unlimited passwords, notes, and storage on unlimited devices. You can save and autofill passwords, generate new ones, and see a security dashboard. You get data backup, offline access, mobile apps, browser extensions, and audit trails.

Passwordless sign-in works with Zoho OneAuth, Windows Hello, Touch ID, a passkey, or a YubiKey. You can attach files of up to 2 MB each. Support on Free is basic and by email only.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan has no storage or device cap, so you will not run out of room. The wall you hit is sharing. Secure sharing with team members, one-time shares, and user roles start on the Standard plan. Folder sharing and user groups start on Professional. Paid plans come with a 15-day free trial and no credit card. During the trial you get Enterprise features but can add up to 5 users. Without an upgrade, the account moves back to the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page does not state a user limit for the Free plan. It does list user invites and password sharing as paid features.
- Support on Free is basic and by email only.
- File attachments are capped at 2 MB each, even though the number of attachments is unlimited.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Vault free?

Yes. The Free plan is permanently free and includes unlimited passwords, notes, storage, and devices.

### Can I share passwords with Zoho Vault for free?

No. Secure password sharing with team members is a paid feature. It starts on the Standard plan.

### Does Zoho Vault need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the 15-day free trial of paid plans needs no credit card. After the trial you switch to the Free plan.
