Hightouch moves data out of your warehouse and into the tools your business runs on, an approach called reverse ETL. It has grown into a customer data and marketing platform, but its free tier covers the core sync. It suits developers and data engineers who already keep customer data in a warehouse.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic Reverse ETL tier is described as being for teams with limited use cases who want to get started with the free tier of the Reverse ETL platform. It allows up to 2 active syncs, with an unlimited destination count and unlimited user seats.

A source is wherever your business data is stored, most often a data warehouse such as Snowflake or Google BigQuery, but it can also be a database, CSV files, SFTP, or a BI tool. A destination is any of the 300 or more integrations Hightouch can sync data to, and several instances of the same destination count once. A sync defines how data gets from a source to a destination.

## When you hit a limit

The limit on the free tier is the number of active syncs, not rows or users. The pricing page does not say what happens if you try to create a third active sync, or whether a sync must be paused to make room for another.

Paid plans are usage-based. Hightouch says it charges based on actual usage, with no limits on monthly tracked users and no caps on sources, destinations, or user seats, but prices for the Customer Data Platform and AI Decisioning plans come through a demo rather than a public list.

## Gotchas

- Reverse ETL assumes a warehouse or database that already holds clean data; Hightouch does not replace one.
- With only 2 active syncs, pick the pipelines that matter most and pause the rest.
- Observability features such as the live debugger and custom alerting are listed for the paid platform plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hightouch free?

Yes, on the Basic Reverse ETL tier, which allows up to 2 active syncs with unlimited destinations and seats.

### What counts as a destination in Hightouch?

Any of the 300 or more integrations Hightouch syncs to. Several instances of the same destination count once.

### Do I need a data warehouse to use Hightouch?

Most sources are warehouses such as Snowflake or BigQuery, but sources can also be databases, CSV files, SFTP, or BI tools.
