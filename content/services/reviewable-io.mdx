You review inside the Reviewable web app, which sits on top of your GitHub pull requests and posts your comments back to GitHub. You sign in with GitHub and connect repositories in the Admin Center.

## What the free plan gives you

The Open Source and Personal plan is free forever. It gives you unlimited reviews on all public repositories and on private repositories owned by an individual account. File uploads are limited to 10MB, and support is by email.

The pricing FAQ says a repository qualifies when it is public or attached to a person instead of an organization. The pricing page does not state a card rule for the free plan.

The Team plan costs $8 per contributor per month, billed annually. It adds unlimited reviews on all repositories, a removable Reviewable badge in pull requests, and chat support. The Business plan costs $16 per contributor per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page shows no usage numbers for the free plan. The line it draws is repository ownership. Private repositories owned by an organization are not part of the free plan.

For those repositories, the pricing page points you to the Team plan. A trial can start with no credit card, and anyone in the organization can then enter a card to begin a subscription. You can change plans or cancel at any time, but the FAQ says there are no refunds.

## Gotchas

- A contributor is anyone in your GitHub organization who creates a pull request. Admins, reviewers, and people who only use reports do not count.
- Reviewable needs GitHub authorizations. The docs list each scope and why it is needed, including reading private repository data and posting comments for you.
- The docs have a section on OAuth app access restrictions. Read it if your GitHub organization limits which apps can connect.

## Questions people ask

### Is Reviewable free?

Yes, for public repositories and for private repositories owned by a personal account. The Open Source and Personal plan is free forever.

### Does Reviewable work with private organization repositories for free?

No. Repositories attached to an organization are not part of the free plan. The Team plan costs $8 per contributor per month, billed annually.

### Does Reviewable need a credit card?

The pricing FAQ says no credit card is required to start a trial. It does not state a card rule for the free plan.
