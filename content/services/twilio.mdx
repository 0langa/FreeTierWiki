Twilio is a communications platform with APIs for SMS, WhatsApp, RCS, voice calls, SIP trunking, verification, and email. You call its APIs from your app, and it bills per message, minute, or request. It suits developers adding texts, calls, or one-time codes to an app.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. The pricing page says to "start for free, then pay as you go," with a free trial that needs no credit card. The trial docs say the trial includes product-specific free units, such as 100 SMS messages, 100 WhatsApp messages, 3,000 emails, and 75 voice minutes, instead of a shared dollar balance.

You track consumption in the Free units tracker in the Twilio Console, which shows your current usage and the percentage of units remaining. Trial accounts expire after 30 days. During the trial you can call or message only verified recipients, up to 5 per account, and your sign-up number is verified automatically.

## When you hit a limit

The trial ends 30 days after sign-up, and the docs say to upgrade before then to continue building. Upgrading means creating a profile, adding your address, loading a starting balance, and adding payment details. Until you upgrade, trial restrictions apply: SMS and voice work only in your sign-up country, email goes only to your sign-up address, and custom message bodies are not available.

## Gotchas

- You must use Twilio-provided templates during the trial; custom message bodies, email content, and WhatsApp templates are not available.
- A2P 10DLC registration, needed to text US recipients from 10-digit numbers, requires a paid account.
- Trial phone numbers are provided by Twilio and can vary by product and recipient.

## Questions people ask

### Is Twilio free?

Not permanently. The free trial needs no credit card, includes free units such as 100 SMS messages and 75 voice minutes, and lasts 30 days.

### Can I text anyone during the trial?

No. Trial accounts can call or message only verified phone numbers, up to 5 per account.

### What happens after 30 days?

The trial expires; the docs say to upgrade before then to keep building.
