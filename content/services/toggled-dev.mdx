You manage projects, environments, and toggles in the web dashboard. Your app asks the client API for the active toggles, or uses the JavaScript SDK.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page calls it an endless free plan, with the complete set of Toggled.dev features. No credit card is required. The free plan covers a single project with up to 10 feature toggles, up to 2 environments per project, and 2 included seats. Unlimited clients and unlimited monthly active users are included.

The pricing page permits one Free project. The current SDK documentation requires a client key created in the project and a regional endpoint matching your account.

The PRO plan costs $49 per month. It includes everything in Free, up to 3 projects, 10 seats included, unlimited toggles per project, and helpdesk replies within 1 business day. Enterprise adds unlimited projects and seats, and a dedicated environment, optionally in your private AWS account.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says to subscribe to PRO plans to increase service quotas. It does not say what happens if you try to create an eleventh toggle or a second project. The limits are listed only as the shape of the free plan.

The pricing page says the free plan needs no card. MAU are not a limit on any plan listed. To stay within 10 toggles, archive a toggle when its feature is removed from your code.

## Gotchas

- One project means one app or service. If you have a frontend and a backend as two projects, you need PRO.
- The JavaScript SDK caches toggles in localStorage and synchronizes in the background, so account for cached configuration.
- Session stickiness is included. The SDK stores the session identifier returned by the service and reuses it on subsequent requests.

Yes. The free plan has a single project and up to 10 feature toggles per project. It also has unlimited clients and unlimited monthly active users.

### Does Toggled.dev need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the free plan needs no credit card.

### How much is Toggled.dev PRO?

PRO costs $49 per month. It includes up to 3 projects, 10 seats, and unlimited feature toggles per project.
