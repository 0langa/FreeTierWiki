You use Courier through its web app for templates and journeys, and through a REST API, SDKs for several languages, a CLI, and an MCP server. It suits product teams that want to change messages without shipping a release.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free for 10,000 sends a month. It includes journeys, broadcasts, the MCP server, the CLI, and the SDKs, with single sign-on through Google and GitHub, and email and chat support. Logs are kept for 7 days on this plan.

One send is one template sent to one recipient, so a multi-channel template counts as one send. A send to a list of 100 users counts as 100. Courier Inbox, the in-app channel, has no fee of its own. The providers you connect bill you separately under their own pricing.

## When you hit a limit

Sending more than 10,000 a month takes the Business plan, which you get by adding a credit card. Business charges $0.005 for every send, including the first 10,000. For example, 50,000 sends a month costs $250.

The docs do not describe what Courier does if a workspace without a card goes past 10,000. A send counts whether or not the provider delivers it. Messages blocked by preferences and sends made with a Test key count too. AI journey nodes and AI translation credits are not available on the Developer plan.

## Gotchas

- Adding a card moves the whole workspace to Business, and the first 10,000 sends then cost money.
- Test sends count the same as production sends, and so do bounced messages.
- Courier sends through your own provider accounts, so SMS and email fees from those providers are extra.

## Questions people ask

### Is Courier free?

Yes. The Developer plan is free for 10,000 sends a month and needs no credit card. It includes journeys, broadcasts, the CLI, SDKs, and the MCP server.

### What happens when I go over 10,000 sends on Courier?

You add a credit card, which moves the workspace to the Business plan. Business charges $0.005 for every send, including the first 10,000.

### Does a multi-channel message count as one send?

Yes. One template sent to one recipient is one send, even when it goes out over email, SMS, and push.
