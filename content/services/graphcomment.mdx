GraphComment is a hosted comment system from SemioLogic. You add the widget or the WordPress plugin to your site, readers discuss under each article, and you moderate from one admin with optional AI help. It is aimed at publishers, from blogs to large media portals, and is ad-free.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Basic plan as free for up to 1,000,000 data loads a month. It includes customization, advanced moderation tools, unlimited moderator seats, easy installation, the online help center, and no ads. AI Moderation Assist is free for your first 100 AI analyses each month; it reads the article before judging a comment and suggests a decision with its reasoning, which a moderator confirms.

SEO inline is included in every plan and on by default for new sites. For crawlers that run JavaScript, the widget injects your comments as HTML with structured data, and pages served to crawlers this way do not count toward your monthly data loads. Paid plans start with Starter at 29$ a month, or 25$ a month billed yearly.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ says overages apply if you go significantly over the plan limit. If you do, someone from GraphComment contacts you to work out whether it is cheaper to upgrade, buy a page view pack, or pay monthly overages. The page does not say that comments stop loading at the limit.

The AI moderation allowance is separate. Only the first 100 AI analyses each month are free, and the page does not say what happens to AI suggestions after that, so plan on moderating the rest by hand.

## Gotchas

- Outside WordPress, the free plan loads the widget in an iframe, so comments are not associated with your page for search engines. The WordPress plugin in SEO mode serves them as indexable HTML.
- AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, so they never load the widget and never see comments unless you use the WordPress plugin or the documented server-side integration.
- A data load is not the same as a page view in every case, so watch the counter on a high-traffic launch.

## Questions people ask

### Is GraphComment free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free for up to 1,000,000 data loads a month.

### What happens if I go over the free limit?

Overages apply if you go significantly over. GraphComment says someone will contact you to choose between upgrading, a page view pack, or monthly overages.

### Do bot visits count toward my data loads?

AI crawlers never load the widget, so they do not count. Pages served to search crawlers through SEO inline are not counted either.
