You use Servervana through its web dashboard. Pages update live, so each monitor page works as a dashboard. It suits a solo developer or a small team that wants uptime, DNS, and SSL checks without paying.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan page lists advanced uptime monitoring for 10 URLs, SSL validation, custom HTTP requests, and email notifications. The HTTP monitor can run redirect checks and keyword checks, and it keeps historical data. A DNS monitor for one hostname is also included. It checks DNS records on all nameservers for that hostname. Checks run every 3 minutes. The plan also includes one full-featured status page that you can customize and share by link.

The page says no card is required. Paid plans start as low as $3 per month. The Flexible plan costs $4 per month and adds more monitor types.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page text that could be read does not describe what happens when you pass the free limits. It only covers plan changes, cancellation, and discounts. The free plan page says you scale to a paid plan when you are ready, so plan on upgrading if you need more than 10 URLs.

The pricing page says you can cancel a subscription and get a full refund for the current billing period if you ask within 7 days of the period start.

## Gotchas

- The free plan checks every 3 minutes. The free plan page lists 1-minute intervals, browser monitoring, domain monitoring, and SSL expiration monitoring as not included.
- The free plan page lists email notifications only. Slack and webhook integrations, multiple alert policies, and team members are listed as not included.
- Status pages show outage and warning information for the last 30 days and simple performance data for the past 24 hours.

## Questions people ask

### Is Servervana free?

Yes. The free plan covers advanced uptime monitoring for 10 URLs, SSL validation, custom HTTP requests, a DNS monitor, email notifications, and one status page.

### Does Servervana need a credit card?

No. The free plan page says no card is required to start.

### How much do the paid Servervana plans cost?

The site says plans start as low as $3 per month. The Flexible plan is $4 per month, with an extra 10% discount for annual billing.
