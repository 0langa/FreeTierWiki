You use Pub/Sub in the Google Cloud console, with the gcloud CLI, or with client libraries. You create a topic, add subscriptions, and publish. It suits teams that build event-driven systems or data pipelines on Google Cloud.

## What the free plan gives you

Every calendar month, the first 10 GiB of throughput billed as the Message Delivery Basic SKU is free for a billing account. Throughput is the total bytes written to a topic plus the bytes read from a subscription. After the free amount the price is $40 per TiB in all Google Cloud regions.

A minimum of 1 KB is assessed for each request, no matter how small the messages are. For messages under 1 KB it is cheaper to batch several messages in one request. The free amount does not cover BigQuery subscriptions, Cloud Storage subscriptions, or import topics.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no hard stop at the free amount. Usage past 10 GiB is billed at $40 per TiB, and some kinds of subscriptions and topics cost more. BigQuery and Cloud Storage subscriptions cost $50 per TiB. Import topics cost $50 or $80 per TiB depending on the source.

Storage is a separate cost. Pub/Sub charges $0.27 per GiB-month when a topic retains messages, a subscription retains acknowledged messages, or you take a snapshot. Data transfer that crosses a zone or region boundary has its own charge. Pub/Sub has no hard cap, so watch your usage in the console.

## Gotchas

- The 10 GiB is per billing account, not per project, so projects on the same billing account share it.
- A subscription whose backlog sits unacknowledged for more than 1 day can start to incur storage charges.
- The console quickstart says one pull can return zero messages for a low message volume. Click Pull a few times.

## Questions people ask

### Is Google Cloud Pub/Sub free?

Partly. The first 10 GiB of throughput each month is free for a billing account. After that it costs $40 per TiB.

### Does Pub/Sub need a credit card?

You need a Google Cloud project with billing enabled. The quickstart tells you to verify that billing is enabled for the project.

### How is Pub/Sub throughput counted?

Throughput is the total bytes written to a topic and read from a subscription. Each request is assessed at a minimum of 1 KB.
