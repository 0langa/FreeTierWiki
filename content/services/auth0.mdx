You register an app in the Auth0 Dashboard, then add one of the Auth0 SDKs or follow a quickstart for your stack. It fits developers who want login without running their own auth server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and says no credit card is needed to sign up. It covers up to 25,000 monthly active users. You get passwordless authentication, unlimited social connections (subject to system limitations), 5 organizations, and SCIM support.

New accounts first get a trial of paid features. The trial lasts 22 days. After that, the Free plan activates on its own, unless you pick a paid plan. The paid Essentials and Professional plans add higher limits and extra features such as multi-factor authentication. The pricing page lists a 99.99% SLA and enterprise support on the Enterprise plan, not on the Free plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that if you pass 25,000 users you need to select a paid plan. It does not say what Auth0 does to an account that stays on the Free plan past that mark. The page shows a user slider that runs past 30,000 and points larger teams to paid plans and to sales. Plan for a move to a paid plan before you reach the cap.

The paid plans list higher authentication, API, and feature limits than the Free plan. Custom domains need a credit card check even on the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page says paid features run for a 22-day trial, then the Free plan activates. Check what you rely on is in the Free plan before the trial ends.
- A custom domain needs credit card verification, even though sign-up itself needs no card.
- The Free plan lists no SLA. The pricing page lists a 99.99% SLA on the Enterprise plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Auth0 free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan includes up to 25,000 monthly active users and needs no credit card to sign up.

### Does Auth0 need a credit card?

Not to sign up for the Free plan. Custom domains are the exception, because they require credit card verification.

### What happens after the Auth0 trial ends?

The trial lasts 22 days. After that, the Free plan activates automatically unless you choose a paid plan.
