You create and watch pipelines in the AWS console, and the AWS CLI works too. Each pipeline is made of stages, and each stage holds actions such as a source, a CodeBuild build, or a CodeDeploy deploy.

## What the free plan gives you

CodePipeline has two pipeline types, V1 and V2, and the free tier depends on which one you use. For V1-type pipelines, you get one free active pipeline per month. For V2-type pipelines, you get 100 free action execution minutes per month.

The free V2 minutes are shared by all V2 pipelines in the account. They reset at the start of each calendar month, and unused minutes do not carry over. The page says new and existing customers get the free tier. A pipeline is V1 if the type is not specified, but the docs say V2 is the only type you can choose in the console.

## When you hit a limit

Past the free tier, you pay. A V1 pipeline costs $1.00 per active month, where active means it is more than 30 days old and had at least one code change run through it. A pipeline with no new code changes in the month costs nothing.

V2 pipelines cost $0.002 per action execution minute, rounded up to the nearest minute. You are charged for all action types except manual approval and custom actions. The pricing page works through an example where a V2 pipeline uses 8 action minutes in a month and pays $0.00.

## Gotchas

- Pipelines are free for the first 30 days after creation, and a V1 pipeline is billed for the full month once it is active, with no proration.
- Artifact storage in Amazon S3 and triggers from other services are billed separately, as the atlas notes in the caveats.
- The pricing calculator on the page works only for V1 pipelines.

## Questions people ask

### Is AWS CodePipeline free?

Partly. The free tier gives you one free active V1 pipeline per month, or 100 free action execution minutes per month on V2 pipelines.

### What does a CodePipeline V2 action minute cost?

After the free 100 minutes, V2 pipelines cost $0.002 per action execution minute. Manual approval and custom actions are not charged.

### Do I pay for a CodePipeline V1 pipeline that I do not use?

A V1 pipeline that has no new code changes running through it during the month costs nothing. An active V1 pipeline costs $1.00 per month.
