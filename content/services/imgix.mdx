imgix processes and delivers images, and now video, through URLs. You connect it to the storage that holds your originals, then add parameters to an image URL to resize, crop, compress, or convert it, and imgix serves the result from its CDN.

## What the free plan gives you

imgix has a free trial rather than a free plan: 30 days and 100 credits to transform, optimize, and deliver visuals, with full access to the platform and no credit card required. Credits are the unit of all usage.

The pricing page shows how credits are spent. Image cache storage and metadata cost 2 credits per GB per month, and delivery, meaning bandwidth and cache reads, costs 1 credit per GB. Transformations also consume credits at rates listed in the docs. For scale, the Starter bundle's 100 credits a month covers up to 50GB of media.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 30 days end or the 100 trial credits are used up. To keep using imgix you choose a bundle: Starter includes 100 credits a month at $0.25 per credit, and larger bundles lower the price per credit.

On monthly self-serve bundles, credits refresh each month; on annual contract bundles they refresh once per year.

## Gotchas

- The trial lasts 30 days, so imgix suits evaluation rather than a long-running free project.
- Credits pool storage, delivery, and transformations, so heavy video or AI transformations can use credits faster than images.
- Web Proxy sources, which proxy any asset URL, are only for Enterprise plans; others connect a storage bucket or web folder.

## Questions people ask

### Does imgix have a free plan?

No. It has a free trial of 30 days and 100 credits, and then you choose a paid bundle.

### Do I need a credit card for the imgix trial?

No. The pricing page says the trial needs no credit card.

### How are imgix credits used?

Delivery uses 1 credit per GB and cache storage 2 credits per GB per month, and transformations use credits too.
