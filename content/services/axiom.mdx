You send events to Axiom with an HTTP request or one of its integrations, then explore them in the web console with queries and dashboards. It suits individual developers and small projects that need log search.

## What the free plan gives you

The Personal plan costs $0 per month, is described as permanent, and needs no credit card. It includes 500 GB per month of data loading compute and 10 GB-hours per month of query compute. Storage is 25 GB, and data is kept for 30 days. Support is through the community.

The paid Axiom Cloud plan starts at $25 per month plus usage. It has a larger allowance of 1 TB of loading, 100 GB-hours of queries, and 100 GB of storage. SSO, role-based access control, directory sync, and extended audit logs are paid add-ons on that plan, not on Personal.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens on the Personal plan when you pass an allowance. It does not list an overage price for Personal, and it asks for no card.

For the paid Axiom Cloud plan, the page says extra usage is billed at normal rates, with no separate overage rate. Data older than 30 days drops out of the Personal plan, so move anything you need to keep.

## Gotchas

- Personal has no SSO or role-based access control, and those add-ons exist only on the paid Cloud plan.
- The pricing page lists soft limits on datasets, fields per dataset, and query concurrency. It says they can be lifted on request.
- Query compute (10 GB-hours a month) is a separate allowance from data loading compute (500 GB a month).

## Questions people ask

### Is Axiom free?

Yes. The Personal plan costs $0 per month, needs no credit card, and includes 500 GB per month of data loading compute and 25 GB of storage.

### How long does Axiom keep data on the free plan?

The Personal plan keeps data for 30 days. The pricing page lists this as the plan's retention.

### What happens when I go over the Axiom free limits?

The pricing page does not say for the Personal plan. On the paid Cloud plan, extra usage is billed at normal rates.
