You create the cluster in the Qdrant Cloud Console and talk to it through official clients for Python, Rust, Node.js, Java, .NET, and Go, or the REST and gRPC APIs. It suits developers who want a hosted vector store for an AI feature.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier is a single node with 0.5 vCPU, 1 GB of RAM, and 4 GB of disk. You do not need a credit card to join. Qdrant says this setup serves about 1 M vectors of 768 dimensions. Cloud inference with selected models is free, so you can embed text without a separate embedding service.

Standard support, basic monitoring, basic log access, and basic alerting come with it. The choice of cloud providers and regions is limited. The cluster has no dedicated resources.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says that when your data grows past 1 GB of RAM and 4 GB of disk, you upgrade to a Standard cluster. It does not describe a bill for the free tier, because the free cluster is a fixed size. Standard clusters are priced by resources, so a bigger cluster costs more.

Idle time is the limit you can hit sooner. Qdrant suspends an unused free cluster after 1 week and deletes it after 4 weeks of inactivity if you do not reactivate it.

## Gotchas

- An idle free cluster is suspended after 1 week and deleted after 4 weeks. Reactivate it before then, or keep your source data so you can rebuild.
- The free tier has no high availability. Version upgrades happen with downtime.
- Only manual snapshots and restores through the API work on the free tier. Backup and disaster recovery start with Standard clusters.

## Questions people ask

### Is Qdrant free?

Yes. Qdrant Cloud has a free tier with one single-node cluster of 0.5 vCPU, 1 GB of RAM, and 4 GB of disk. You do not need a credit card to join.

### What happens when I outgrow the Qdrant free tier?

The pricing page says to upgrade to a Standard cluster once your data passes 1 GB of RAM or 4 GB of disk. Standard clusters are dedicated and priced by resource use.

### Does the Qdrant free cluster pause or get deleted?

Yes. Qdrant suspends an unused free cluster after 1 week and deletes it after 4 weeks of inactivity, unless you reactivate it.
