iubenda helps websites meet privacy laws. It generates privacy and cookie policies from ready-made clauses and runs a consent banner that collects visitors' choices. It suits developers who need a policy page and a cookie banner for a small site without writing the legal text themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is meant for websites with under 5,000 page views per month. It includes a Privacy and Cookie Policy Generator with thousands of ready-made clauses, each with its mapping of cookies and trackers, and a consent notice. Google Consent Mode is supported even on the Free plan.

The Free plan has clear limits. Terms and Conditions are not included, you cannot add custom text to the policy, and your documents and notices show full iubenda branding, including a clickable created by iubenda button. The consent notice is in one language only, and you cannot add your own logo or change its colors, text, or behavior. Mobile app integration, geolocation consent, detailed analytics, the Consent Database, and the Register of Data Processing Activities are not included.

## When you hit a limit

The consent banner covers up to 5,000 page views per month. Extra usage is not available on the Free plan, so the banner is deactivated for the month whenever that threshold is reached.

That means no surprise bill, but also no consent collection for the rest of the month, which can matter for compliance. Paid plans add a page view quota and charge for overage instead: Essentials covers up to 25,000 pageviews a month and then charges €0.05 per 1,000 pageviews.

## Gotchas

- When the banner switches off at the limit, scripts that need consent are left without a working consent flow until the month resets.
- The iubenda branding on the Free plan cannot be removed.
- Paid plans bill overage per 1,000 pageviews, so upgrading changes the risk from a stop to a bill.

## Questions people ask

### Is iubenda free?

Yes. The Free plan covers a consent banner for up to 5,000 page views per month and a privacy and cookie policy generator.

### What happens when I pass 5,000 page views on the free plan?

Extra usage is not available, so the banner is deactivated for the rest of the month.

### Does the free plan include Terms and Conditions?

No. Terms and Conditions are not included in the Free plan.
