You send through the REST API, SMTP, or an official library, and you read delivery results in the dashboard. It suits developers who want a dependable email path for app messages.

## What the free plan gives you

Every account starts on the free Developer plan, which includes 100 emails per month. The pricing page says it never expires or runs out. It is meant for testing your integration and for side projects with low volume.

The plan allows up to 6 users and keeps activity data for 45 days. The Developer docs list limits per email: 10MB in total and 50 recipients across To, CC, and BCC. A sandbox mode lets you test without sending real mail.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says no overages are allowed on the free plan. After 100 emails you cannot send more that month until the allowance resets or you upgrade, so you cannot be billed by accident.

Paid plans work differently. Extra emails are counted at the end of the billing cycle and added to your next month's cost. The first paid tier is 10,000 emails a month, starting at $15 per month, and there is no tier between 100 and 10,000.

## Gotchas

- Unused emails do not roll over to the next month.
- There is no plan between the 100 free emails and the paid 10,000, so a small project that needs a few hundred emails must pay for 10,000.
- You must verify the address you send from before your first send.

## Questions people ask

### Is Postmark free?

Yes, within limits. The Developer plan includes 100 emails per month, never expires, and needs no credit card.

### What happens if I go over the Postmark free limit?

You cannot send past 100 emails, because the plan allows no overages. You upgrade to a paid plan to send more.

### Does Postmark have a plan between 100 and 10,000 emails?

No. The pricing page FAQ says Postmark does not offer a tier between the free 100 emails a month and 10,000 emails a month.
