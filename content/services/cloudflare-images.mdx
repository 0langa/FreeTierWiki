You write a URL or a Worker call that names the size and format you want. Cloudflare fetches the original, changes it at the edge, and caches the result. It suits developers who already run a site on Cloudflare.

## What the free plan gives you

Everyone starts on the Images Free plan. It includes the transformations feature, which optimizes images stored outside Images, such as files in R2 or on your own server.

On the Free plan you can request up to 5,000 unique transformations each month. A unique transformation is one original image with one set of options. The same thumbnail at 100x100 and at 200x200 counts as two. Repeat requests for the same transformation in the same month count once. The format parameter counts as one transformation, even if some users get AVIF and others get WebP.

Storing images inside Cloudflare Images is not part of the Free plan. That needs the Paid plan.

## When you hit a limit

Cloudflare says you will not be charged for going over the Free plan limits. Once you pass 5,000 unique transformations, new transformations return a 9422 error. If the source image comes from the same domain that serves the transformation, you can use the onerror parameter to send visitors to the original image instead.

To go past 5,000, you buy the Images Paid plan. It includes the first 5,000 and then charges $0.50 per 1,000 unique transformations each month.

## Gotchas

- The free limit counts unique combinations of image and options. Many sizes of many images add up fast.
- Hosted storage, with its delivery domain and variants, belongs to the Paid plan. On Free you keep the originals somewhere else.
- Transformations run on a Cloudflare zone, so your domain needs to be on Cloudflare.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Images free?

Partly. The Free plan lets you request up to 5,000 unique transformations each month on images stored outside Cloudflare Images. Hosted storage needs the Paid plan.

### What happens when I go over the Cloudflare Images free limit?

Cloudflare does not charge you. New transformations return a 9422 error until the next month, unless you buy the Paid plan.

### Does Cloudflare Images need a credit card?

The entry records that the Free plan needs no card. Cloudflare says you are not charged for going over the Free plan limits.
