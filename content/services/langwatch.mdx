You add the LangWatch SDK or an OpenTelemetry exporter to your app, then view traces, evals, and simulations in the web app. It suits developers who want to test agents as well as watch them.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free forever and needs no credit card. It includes 50k events per month, 14-day data access, and 2 users. It also lists 3 scenarios, 3 simulations, and 3 custom evals, with community support on GitHub and Discord.

An event is the work your AI app does in LangWatch. Every LLM call, tool call, retrieval, evaluation, or simulation step counts as one. One user interaction can produce several events. Growth costs 29 euro per core-seat per month and includes 200k events and 30-day retention.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens on Developer after the 50k events. It says that Growth charges 5 euro per 100k events beyond its 200k allowance, and 3 euro per GB for data kept beyond the included 30 days.

The pricing page says you can see usage, events, storage, and cost live in the dashboard, with anomaly alerts. Check it often if your agent makes many tool calls, because each one counts.

## Gotchas

- Events count LLM calls, tool calls, retrievals, evals, and simulation steps, so one chat can use many events.
- Data access is 14 days on Developer, against 30 days on Growth.
- The free plan caps scenarios, simulations, and custom evals at 3 each.

## Questions people ask

### Is LangWatch free?

Yes. The Developer plan is free forever with 50k events per month, 14-day data access, and 2 users. No credit card is required.

### Can I self-host LangWatch?

Yes. The pricing page says LangWatch runs fully self-hosted with docker compose on your own ClickHouse. The docs call it open core.

### Does LangWatch need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says you can sign up and send events with no card required on the Developer plan.
