You use Zoho Notebook in the apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, and Windows, or in the browser. Each note is a card in a notebook, and the site says its tools are free of ads.

## What the free plan gives you

Personal Essential includes 500 MB cloud storage, 10 MB file uploads, unlimited notes and device sync. Homepage FAQ instead advertises 2 GB, conflicting with pricing/billing's 500 MB. Features include document scanning, markup, reminders, smart cards and Face ID/Touch ID locking.

A separate notecard cap is 3 MB, with 10 versions/card and read-only card/notebook sharing. Audio recordings permit 30 minutes, video 30 seconds, nested collections one level and Noteboard four lists. Community/email support is included. Seven card types cover text, checklists, audio, photos, smart cards, sketches and files. Treat file-upload and notecard sizes as distinct restrictions.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says that when you pass the storage limit of your plan, you can choose an add-on for extra storage. It does not say what happens to new uploads before you buy one.

The Pro plan lifts the limits to 100 GB of storage and uploads up to 1 GB. Pro also adds editing and co-owner access for shared notes. The Business plan gives 1 TB for up to 10 users, plus 100 GB for each added user.

## Gotchas

- Sharing is read-only on the free plan. Shared editing needs the Pro plan. The pricing page does not state a device count or card requirement for Essential. Essential does not include Notebook AI, advanced search, recurring reminders or Email-In. The separate Student plan requires an educational email and advertises 5 GB plus Pro and AI features; those are not the standard personal Free allowance.
- The 10 MB upload limit applies to each file, so large PDFs and recordings may not fit.
- Cancel store-bought subscriptions in the relevant Apple or Google store. The pricing FAQ says App Store purchases do not support plan downgrades.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Notebook free?

Yes. The free personal Essential plan has 500 MB of cloud storage and a 10 MB limit per uploaded file. It syncs across devices.

### What happens when I run out of Zoho Notebook storage?

Zoho says you can choose an add-on for extra storage. You can also move to the Pro plan, which lists 100 GB.

### Can I share Zoho Notebook notes for free?

Yes, with read-only access. The Pro plan adds editing and co-owner access.
