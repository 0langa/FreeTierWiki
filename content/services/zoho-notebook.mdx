You use Zoho Notebook in the apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, and Windows, or in the browser. Each note is a card in a notebook, and the site says its tools are free of ads.

## What the free plan gives you

The free Essential plan gives you 500 MB of cloud storage and file uploads up to 10 MB each. Notes sync across your devices. The plan also covers scanning documents, markups and reminders, smart cards, and locking notes with Face ID or Touch ID.

You can keep up to 10 versions of a card. You can share cards and notebooks with read-only access. Support comes from community forums and email. Seven card types cover text, checklists, audio, photos, smart cards, sketches, and files.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says that when you pass the storage limit of your plan, you can choose an add-on for extra storage. It does not say what happens to new uploads before you buy one.

The Pro plan lifts the limits to 100 GB of storage and uploads up to 1 GB. Pro also adds editing and co-owner access for shared notes. The Business plan gives 1 TB for up to 10 users, plus 100 GB for each added user.

## Gotchas

- Sharing is read-only on the free plan. Shared editing needs the Pro plan.
- The 10 MB upload limit applies to each file, so large PDFs and recordings may not fit.
- The pricing page does not state a limit on notes or devices, and it does not mention a credit card.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Notebook free?

Yes. The free Essential plan has 500 MB of cloud storage and a 10 MB limit per uploaded file. It syncs across devices.

### What happens when I run out of Zoho Notebook storage?

Zoho says you can choose an add-on for extra storage. You can also move to the Pro plan, which lists 100 GB.

### Can I share Zoho Notebook notes for free?

Yes, with read-only access. The Pro plan adds editing and co-owner access.
