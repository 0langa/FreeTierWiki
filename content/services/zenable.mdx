You install the Zenable CLI to add checks to your IDE, and you connect GitHub or GitLab for pull request reviews. A web console at zenable.app holds your requirements. It suits individual developers who want AI review on their pull requests.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes 2-click setup, automated PR summaries, documentation, and API access. It gives 5,000 Zenable Credits per seat per week. Credits pay for AI reviews of pull requests, agent code reviews through the CLI, MCP server, or API, and chat tools.

An AI review costs 10 credits per file with the standard model and 25 credits with the premium model. Guardrails and static checks cost no credits and are unlimited on every plan. A pull request can have up to 150 files, and the largest reviewable file is 300,000 characters.

## When you hit a limit

When you use up the weekly credits, AI-powered reviews pause until the next reset. Guardrails and static checks keep running. If a pull request would cost more than the author's remaining credits, Zenable leaves a comment that the limit was reached and charges nothing for the withheld review. Credits reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC. No bill follows, because the Free plan needs no card. Professional gives 200,000 credits per seat per week and costs $99 per user per month, billed annually.

## Gotchas

- On the Free plan, reviews do not run until you link your GitHub or GitLab account to your Zenable user.
- A closed and reopened pull request, or a review re-requested with a comment, is charged again for the files it reviews.
- Custom guardrails for AI IDEs and PR guardrails with custom requirements are listed under Professional.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zenable free?

Yes. The Free plan gives 5,000 Zenable Credits per seat per week and needs no credit card.

### What happens when I run out of Zenable credits?

AI reviews pause until the next weekly reset on Monday at 00:00 UTC. Guardrails and static checks keep running.

### Does Zenable train on my code?

No. The pricing FAQ says Zenable does not train or fine-tune using users' code, on free or paid plans.
