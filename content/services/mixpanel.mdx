You install an SDK, call init with your project token, and send events. Then you build reports in the Mixpanel web app. It suits product teams and startups that want analytics on how people use an app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever and needs no credit card. It includes up to 1M events per month and unlimited seats. It also includes 10K session replays per month, up to 1k MEU per month for experiments, and up to 10 active feature flags.

Free is usage-based. The docs say you can use it for as long as you want with no trial. They encourage you to start there and upgrade once you know your volume. After about a week, the Plan Details and Billing page shows an estimate of your volume.

Free has report limits. You get a maximum of 5 saved reports per user account. You can create cohorts but cannot save them. Features such as lookup tables, custom properties, and permissions are not included.

## When you hit a limit

On the Free plan, Mixpanel does not charge overages. It limits your platform access once you reach 1M monthly events. You are blocked from viewing your reports until you upgrade to a Growth plan that supports your usage, or the allowance resets the next month.

On a Growth plan, extra events are billed at your rate at the start of the next month. You cannot set a billing limit at this time. On paid accounts, owners and billing admins get alert emails at 85% of plan volume and at higher levels.

## Gotchas

- Hitting 1M events locks you out of your reports. It does not drop your data. The block lasts until the next month or until you upgrade.
- Hiding an event in Lexicon does not stop its ingestion. Dropping an event only blocks it from that moment on.
- Downgrading to Free does not take effect at once. It starts on your next billing date.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mixpanel free?

Yes. The Free plan gives up to 1M events per month and unlimited seats. It is free forever and needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over 1M events on the Mixpanel free plan?

Mixpanel does not charge overages on Free. It limits your platform access, so you cannot view reports until you upgrade or the next month starts.

### Does Mixpanel have a startup program?

Yes. Early-stage companies founded less than 5 years ago, with up to $8M in total funding, get their first year free on the Startup Plan.
