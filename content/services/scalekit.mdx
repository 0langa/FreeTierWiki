You set up Scalekit in its dashboard and add one of its SDKs to your backend, which sends users to a hosted login page. It suits small B2B teams that need enterprise login features early.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and has no commitment. It includes 25,000 monthly active users, 25 organizations, 1 SSO connection, and 1 SCIM connection. Support comes through the community and email.

Scalekit branding appears on the auth widgets and emails. The Growth plan costs $99 per month and drops the branding. It includes 100,000 monthly active users, 100 organizations, audit logs, and priority Slack and email support. A SCIM connection automates user provisioning from an external directory such as Azure AD or Okta. You are billed only for connections that are enabled.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan is a hard cap with no overages. The pricing page says that when you hit 25,000 monthly active users or 25 organizations, or need more than 1 SSO connection, you upgrade to Growth. It does not say what your app does at the cap, so plan the upgrade before you get there.

On Growth, usage past the included limits is billed at published per-unit rates, so there are no sudden plan jumps. Extra users cost $0.05 each, and extra SSO or SCIM connections start at $60 per connection per month.

## Gotchas

- The free plan includes exactly 1 SSO connection and 1 SCIM connection. A second customer with SSO means Growth.
- Scalekit branding appears on auth widgets and emails on the free plan.
- The quickstart examples use different redirect paths, `/callback` in some languages and `/auth/callback` in others. Register the exact URL your code uses.

## Questions people ask

### Is Scalekit free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month for 25,000 monthly active users, 25 organizations, and one SSO and one SCIM connection.

### What happens when I reach the Scalekit free limit?

The Free plan is a hard cap with no overages. You upgrade to the Growth plan, which costs $99 per month, to go past it.

### Does Scalekit charge for SSO?

The first SSO connection is free. After that, pricing applies per connection per month, with volume discounts that apply on their own.
