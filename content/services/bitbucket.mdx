You use Bitbucket in the browser, or from the command line with Git. Pipelines is configured by a YAML file in your repository.

## What the free plan gives you

Bitbucket is free for individuals and small teams with up to 5 users. You get unlimited public and private repositories. The plan includes 1 GB of Git LFS file storage and 50 build minutes per month for Pipelines. The pricing table separately lists a 1 GB total workspace storage limit on Free.

Build minutes and storage are shared by everyone in the workspace. A step on a larger runner counts more: the page gives the example of a 5-minute step on a 2x runner counting as 10 build minutes. The Pipelines docs say a pipeline can have up to 100 steps and that up to 10 steps can run at once.

## When you hit a limit

Free includes 50 monthly Pipelines minutes; pricing leaves extra Free minutes blank and does not explain exhaustion. Standard/Premium overages are $10 per 1,000 minutes and $10 per 100 GB LFS storage.

Paid monthly organization pooling began rolling out October 26, 2026. Annual usage billing is scheduled for December 3, 2026; annual customers are not charged these overages before rollout. Neither policy establishes automatic Free charges.

Pipelines do not run for pushes involving more than five tags, bookmarks or branches, protecting against accidental minute consumption. Check both billing eligibility and build behavior before relying on excess capacity.

## Gotchas

- Build minutes are shared across all users in the workspace, so one busy repository can use the whole 50.
- The Standard plan costs $3.65 per user per month, so going past 5 users means paying per user.
- Community and academic subscriptions get more: 5 GB of LFS storage and 500 build minutes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bitbucket free?

Yes, for up to 5 users. The Free plan includes unlimited public and private repositories, 1 GB of LFS storage, and 50 build minutes.

### How many build minutes does Bitbucket give for free?

The Pipelines docs list 50 build minutes per month on the Free plan. Paid overage of $10 per 1,000 minutes applies to Standard and Premium.

### Does Bitbucket need a credit card?

The pricing page says the Free plan is free forever for up to 5 users. It accepts credit cards at the end of signup for paid plans.
