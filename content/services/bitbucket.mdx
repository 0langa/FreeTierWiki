You use Bitbucket in the browser, or from the command line with Git. Pipelines is configured by a YAML file in your repository.

## What the free plan gives you

Bitbucket is free for individuals and small teams with up to 5 users. You get unlimited public and private repositories. The plan includes 1 GB of file storage for LFS, in addition to 1 GB of storage per repository, and 50 build minutes to get started with Pipelines.

Build minutes and storage are shared by everyone in the workspace. A step on a larger runner counts more: the page gives the example of a 5-minute step on a 2x runner counting as 10 build minutes. The Pipelines docs say a pipeline can have up to 100 steps and that up to 10 steps can run at once.

## When you hit a limit

The Pipelines docs list the Free plan at 50 build minutes per month and show overage protection as not included. The pricing page says you can buy more if your plan runs short. Extra allowances come as 1,000 minutes or 100 GB for $10 a month.

The pages do not spell out what a pipeline does when the free minutes are gone. The docs also say Bitbucket does not run pipelines for a push that involves more than five tags, bookmarks, or branches, to keep a mistake from using up your minutes.

## Gotchas

- Build minutes are shared across all users in the workspace, so one busy repository can use the whole 50.
- The Standard plan costs $3.65 per user per month, so going past 5 users means paying per user.
- Community and academic subscriptions get more: 5 GB of LFS storage and 500 build minutes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bitbucket free?

Yes, for up to 5 users. The Free plan includes unlimited public and private repositories, 1 GB of LFS storage, and 50 build minutes.

### How many build minutes does Bitbucket give for free?

The Pipelines docs list 50 build minutes per month on the Free plan. You can buy 1,000 more minutes for $10 a month.

### Does Bitbucket need a credit card?

The pricing page says the Free plan is free forever for up to 5 users. It accepts credit cards at the end of signup for paid plans.
