You use Pipedream Connect through its CLI, a TypeScript SDK, an API, and an MCP server. It suits developers who add integrations or agent tools to their own apps. Pipedream Workflows and String are shutting down.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says the Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and is free to build on. It includes 10,000+ prebuilt tools and triggers, unlimited team members, and community support. Usage on Free is for development only: you get full API, SDK, and MCP access to build and test.

For Workflows, the docs say free workspaces have a daily limit of free credits that cannot be exceeded. They also limit connected accounts, such as Slack, Google Sheets, and GitHub, and the public docs give no number. Credits are not charged for workflows during development or testing.

The Startup plan runs integrations in production. It costs $99 a month billed annually, or $150 billed monthly. It includes 100 users and 10,000 usage credits.

## When you hit a limit

Free workspaces cannot go over their daily credit limit, so the Free plan stops rather than bills. The docs say to lift the limit, add features, or use Connect in production, you upgrade to a paid plan.

On Startup, nothing breaks past the included amounts. Each extra user costs $2 a month, and each extra credit costs $0.012. A credit is one per 30 seconds of compute for actions, tool calls, proxy requests, and deployed trigger emits.

## Gotchas

- Workflows and String shut down on March 31, 2027. Connect is not affected.
- Serving real external users in production needs the Startup plan. Free is development only.
- The `.env` file that the CLI creates holds your OAuth client credentials, so do not commit it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pipedream free?

Yes, to build and test. The Free plan costs $0 with no credit card and unlimited team members. Production use needs the Startup plan.

### Is Pipedream shutting down?

Pipedream Workflows and String are shutting down on March 31, 2027. Pipedream Connect is not affected and remains supported.

### Does Pipedream need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan needs no credit card and has no paid plan until you move to production.
