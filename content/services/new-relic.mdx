You install a New Relic agent through the guided install and use the web platform to explore metrics, traces, and logs. It suits developers who want APM and infrastructure monitoring together.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier includes 100 GB of data ingest per month and costs nothing. You get one full platform user with access to all 50+ platform capabilities, such as APM with distributed tracing, infrastructure monitoring, error tracking, and log management. You also get unlimited basic users, who can use data ingest, querying, dashboards, and alerts.

Default data retention is at least 8 days, and the free tier includes 500 synthetic checks. Logs are obfuscated by default, which masks known credit-card and Social-Security-number patterns.

## When you hit a limit

When your account is within 85% of the monthly 100 GB, you get an email and an on-screen alert asking you to upgrade. If you pass 100 GB, data ingest stops and you lose access to the platform until you upgrade to a paid edition or the first day of the next month.

You cannot be billed by accident. Upgrading to Standard or Pro means adding a credit card, billing information, and accepting terms. Beyond the free 100 GB, those editions charge $0.40 per GB.

## Gotchas

- Passing 100 GB cuts off both ingest and access to the platform until the month resets.
- Only one full platform user is free. Extra full platform users on paid editions start at $10 per user.
- Default retention is short at 8 days, and the FAQ says extended data retention is an add-on.

## Questions people ask

### Is New Relic free?

Yes. The free tier includes 100 GB of data ingest per month, unlimited basic users, and one full platform user. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I go over New Relic's 100 GB free limit?

Data ingest stops and you lose access to the platform until you upgrade or the first day of the next month. You get a warning at 85% of the limit.

### Does New Relic need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says to start now with no credit card required. A card is only needed if you upgrade to a paid edition.
