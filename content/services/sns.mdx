You use SNS through the AWS console or an AWS SDK. You create a topic, add subscriptions, and publish. It suits developers who already run on AWS and need to push one event to several places.

## What the free plan gives you

SNS has no upfront fees. Each month the first 1 million SNS requests are free. Standard topic requests include publishes, batch publishes, and the topic and subscription owner operations.

Deliveries have their own free amounts: 1 million mobile push notifications, 100,000 HTTP/S notifications, and 1,000 email or email-JSON notifications. Deliveries to SQS queues and Lambda functions have no per-message charge. Message filtering on attributes is free. Free usage is counted each month across all regions and does not carry over.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no hard stop at the free amounts. You pay as you go for usage past them, and the charge appears on your bill at the end of the month. The pricing page shows the rates in a calculator, so check the live page for the amount.

SMS has no free allowance at all. Text messages are billed by destination country. Payload size also matters. Each 64KB chunk counts as one request, so a publish with a 1 MiB payload counts as 16 requests. Data transfer charges apply between SNS and SQS or Lambda.

## Gotchas

- A topic's type and name cannot change after you create it. Pick Standard or FIFO with care.
- Free usage resets each month and is calculated across all regions, so topics in several regions share one free allowance.
- Payload-based message filtering is billed by the data scanned, while attribute-based filtering is free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amazon SNS free?

Partly. The first 1 million requests each month are free, plus free monthly deliveries for mobile push, HTTP/S, and email. Past those amounts you pay as you go.

### Is SMS free on Amazon SNS?

No. SMS has no free allowance. It is billed by destination country, and some countries need dedicated origination identities.

### What happens when I go over the SNS free tier?

SNS pricing is pay as you go, so you are charged for the extra usage at the end of the month. The pricing page lists the per-unit rates for the extra requests and deliveries.
