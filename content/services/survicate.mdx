Survicate is a customer feedback tool. You build a survey once and show it through a link, email, a website widget, or inside your product, then read the answers in one dashboard. Product teams use it for NPS, onboarding, and feature feedback.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing FAQ separates the free trial from the Free plan. The Free plan has no time limit and provides 25 responses per month with one active survey. It includes basic CRM integrations, a 30-day data retention period, and collaboration with up to three team members.

The trial comes first. It is a 10-day preview of all features that collects up to 25 responses in total, with unlimited active surveys, CRM and advanced integrations, unlimited team members, and unlimited data retention for those ten days. The pricing page says no card is required for the trial.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ explains what happens at the response limit for paid plans: on Starter you can enable overages and keep collecting for an extra cost, while Growth, Pro, and Enterprise surveys stop collecting until you upgrade or buy more responses. The pricing page does not say what happens when the Free plan reaches its monthly responses.

Because the Free plan keeps data for 30 days, older responses are not a long-term archive. Export anything you want to keep.

## Gotchas

- One active survey means you cannot run, for example, an NPS survey and an onboarding survey at the same time for free.
- The trial's generous features end after ten days, so check which integrations you rely on before it expires.
- Twenty-five responses a month is small for a public website widget with real traffic.

## Questions people ask

### Is there a free version of Survicate?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and provides 25 responses per month with one active survey.

### What is the difference between the free trial and the Free plan?

The trial lasts 10 days with all features and up to 25 responses in total. The Free plan continues indefinitely with limited features and 25 responses a month.

### Do I need a card to start?

The pricing page says the trial needs no card.
