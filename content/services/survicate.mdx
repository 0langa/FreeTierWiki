Survicate is a customer feedback tool. You build a survey once and show it through a link, email, a website widget, or inside your product, then read the answers in one dashboard. Product teams use it for NPS, onboarding, and feature feedback.

## What the free plan gives you

Free includes 25 responses monthly and three team members, with no time limit. The pricing FAQ lists one active survey, basic CRM integrations and 30-day data retention. Free-account help lists storage of 300 responses per survey and 1,000 across unarchived surveys. A separate official plan guide instead describes unlimited active surveys with 300 responses collectively, so those limits are not consistent across sources.

New accounts begin with a 10-day, no-card trial capped at 25 total responses. When it expires without an upgrade, the account becomes Free and existing trial surveys are disabled; you can restart an eligible survey. Free data export is unavailable.

## When you hit a limit

The Free-account help article explicitly says surveys stop collecting responses when the response allowance is exhausted. The trial also stops at 25 responses, even before its ten days end. Upgrading is the documented way to continue collecting beyond the applicable allowance.

Do not depend on exporting older responses: the Free-account documentation expressly says data export is unavailable. The pricing FAQ lists 30-day retention, so Free is unsuitable as a long-term archive. Check the dashboard's usage and renewal information, and confirm active-survey and storage limits before relying on conflicting published figures.

## Gotchas

- The pricing FAQ lists one active survey, while a separate official plan guide says unlimited. Check the allowance displayed in your account.
- The trial's generous features end after ten days, so check which integrations you rely on before it expires.
- Twenty-five responses a month is small for a public website widget with real traffic.

## Questions people ask

### Is there a free version of Survicate?

Yes. Free has no time limit and provides 25 responses monthly. Official pages disagree about the active-survey allowance.

### What is the difference between the free trial and the Free plan?

The trial lasts 10 days with all features and up to 25 responses in total. The Free plan continues indefinitely with limited features and 25 responses a month.

### Do I need a card to start?

The pricing page says the trial needs no card.
