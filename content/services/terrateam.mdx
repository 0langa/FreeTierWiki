You work from pull requests and comments, plus a console for runs and logs. Plans and applies execute on your own CI runners, so cloud credentials stay with you.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan on Stategraph Cloud costs $0 forever and needs no credit card. It supports GitHub and GitLab with Terraform, OpenTofu, Terragrunt, Pulumi, CDKTF, or any command line tool. It allows 3 users, 50 runs a month, and 1 concurrent worker. Audit logs are kept for 30 days, and support comes from the community.

A run is one plan or one apply, counted per calendar month. The docs say Free and Pro include every Orchestration feature and differ only by these limits.

Pro costs $12,000 per year and lifts the limits to unlimited users, runs, and workers. It also has 365 days of audit retention and an onboarding session.

You can also self-host the open source edition under the MPL-2.0 license. It allows up to 3 active users a month per installation and unlimited runs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens on Cloud Free after run 50. It does say how the self-hosted open source edition behaves at its limit. After the first 3 active users in a month, other users' plans and applies do not run. A comment on the pull request explains why.

New accounts start on Pro for 30 days with no credit card, then move to Free. You stay on Free unless you add a card to keep Pro. Nobody is billed unless they choose Pro.

## Gotchas

- Repositories show up in the console only after their first pull request event, so the list starts empty.
- On GitLab, you need a group, because personal namespaces are not supported. You also need an access token with the api scope.
- The terrateam.io address now shows the Stategraph site. Old guides may still use Terrateam names.

## Questions people ask

### Is Terrateam still a product?

Terrateam is now Stategraph Orchestration. The old terrateam.io address shows the Stategraph site, and the free plan is on Stategraph Cloud.

### Is Stategraph Orchestration free?

Yes, for small teams. The Free cloud plan allows 3 users, 50 runs a month, and 1 concurrent worker. The open source edition is free to self-host.

### Does it need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is needed to start, and the 30 day Pro trial also asks for none.
