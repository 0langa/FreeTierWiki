You add the Hexclave SDK to your app and manage users, teams, and settings in the Hexclave dashboard. It suits developers who want auth they can run locally, host in the cloud, or self-host.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 forever. It includes up to 10,000 auth users and 1 dashboard admin. You get email, OAuth, and magic link sign-in, plus 1,000 emails per month and community support.

The plan also covers 100K analytics events per month and 2,500 session replays per month, with a 10 second analytics timeout. An auth user is any account in your project. Inactive users still count, but you can delete accounts you no longer need. The Team plan costs $49 per month, with 50,000 auth users, 4 dashboard admins, and 25,000 emails per month. Hexclave is open source, so you can also self-host it for free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page promises no surprise bills but does not describe what happens when you pass 10,000 auth users, 1,000 emails, or the analytics limits. Plans change any time from the dashboard, and changes are prorated to the day.

The paid tiers are the way past the limits. The Team plan raises auth users to 50,000 and emails to 25,000, and the Growth plan, at $299 per month, has unlimited auth users. Extra dashboard admins cost $29 per month each on those paid plans. Check your usage in the dashboard before you launch.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has 1 dashboard admin. A second person who manages the project needs a paid plan.
- Inactive users count as auth users. Delete accounts you no longer need to stay under the limit.
- The server code is under AGPLv3 and the client SDKs are under MIT. Ask about a commercial license if AGPL does not fit.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hexclave (Stack Auth) free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever and covers up to 10,000 auth users and 1 dashboard admin. The pricing page does not mention a credit card.

### Can I self-host Hexclave?

Yes. Hexclave is open source, and you can run the whole stack on your own infrastructure for free. Commercial licenses are available if AGPL does not fit.

### Can I migrate users from another auth service to Hexclave?

Yes. The pricing FAQ says Hexclave imports from Clerk, Auth0, Firebase Auth, and Supabase Auth without forcing users to reset their passwords.
