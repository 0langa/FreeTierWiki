Hotjar shows how visitors use your website through session replays, heatmaps, and funnels, and lets you ask them questions with surveys. Its pricing page now sits within Contentsquare, which includes the Hotjar tools. It suits developers, designers, and product people working on a site's experience.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. It includes up to 200k monthly sessions and 1 project, with replays, heatmaps, and funnels, error and performance monitoring, basic surveys, and a connection to LLMs through MCP with up to 300 tool calls a month. Analytics data access on the Free plan is 1 month, against 13 months on paid plans, and the analytics collection rate is 100%.

For Voice of Customer, the Free plan gives 100 responses a month plus 3 surveys and feedback widgets. A session is a visitor's full journey on your site, and it ends after 30 minutes of inactivity.

## When you hit a limit

Each plan comes with a set number of sessions and responses that resets each month. If you reach the session limit on the Free plan, you will not get any new heatmap or funnel data until next month's reset date.

If you reach the response limit, your surveys and widgets are no longer sent, so you get no new responses until the reset. To avoid lost data, you can upgrade by adding more sessions or responses or moving to a new plan. Session and response allowances apply to your whole account.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has 1 project and you cannot add more; several domains in that project share one session allowance.
- With 1 month of data access, compare periods inside the month or export what you need.
- Every new account starts with a 15-day trial of the Growth plans, so features can disappear when it ends.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hotjar still free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes up to 200k monthly sessions on 1 project.

### What happens when I run out of sessions?

You stop getting new heatmap or funnel data until the monthly reset, unless you upgrade.

### Is there a Hotjar trial?

Yes. Every new account starts with a 15-day trial of the Growth plans, with no payment details needed and no auto-charges.
