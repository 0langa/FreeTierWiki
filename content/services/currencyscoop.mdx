You sign up on the website, copy an API key from the dashboard, and send HTTPS GET requests to the v1 endpoints. The same account also works through an MCP server for AI clients. It suits developers who need exchange rates for a checkout, a report, or a prototype.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer sandbox plan costs $0 a month and needs no credit card to start. It gives you 5,000 API requests per month with hourly updates. You can use the latest, historical, and conversion endpoints. The historical data goes back to January 1, 1996.

REST calls and MCP tool calls share the same quota. Each successful MCP tool call counts as one request. The time-series endpoint is for the Startup and Professional plans only. Paid plans start at $10 a month for 50,000 requests with 15 minute updates.

## When you hit a limit

The docs say a 429 error means you exceeded your request volume limit, and they tell you to upgrade your plan or slow down. The pricing page lists no overage fee and says there are no hidden fees.

The pages do not say outright what happens to a free account at the quota, but they list no overage fee. Because MCP calls share the quota, an AI agent that loops can use up the 5,000 requests faster than a normal app.

## Gotchas

- The sandbox plan updates rates hourly. Faster updates need a paid plan: 15 minutes on Basic, 1 minute on Startup, and real time on Professional.
- Do not put the API key in front-end code or a public repository. The docs recommend sending it as a Bearer token in the Authorization header.
- The time-series endpoint is not part of the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Currency Beacon free?

Yes, within limits. The Developer sandbox plan gives you 5,000 requests a month with hourly rate updates and needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Currency Beacon free limit?

The docs say a 429 error means you exceeded your request volume limit, and they tell you to upgrade or slow down. The pricing page lists no overage fee.

### Can I use Currency Beacon with an AI agent?

Yes. The site says MCP clients such as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, and Codex can use the same account and quota. Each successful tool call counts as one request.
