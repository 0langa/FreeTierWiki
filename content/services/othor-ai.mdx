You work in a web app. You connect your data, then read the dashboards or ask questions in a chat, so there is no query language to learn. It fits owners and small teams without a data analyst. This page covers the Free plan.


## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and the pricing page calls it free forever. It includes 2 analytical agents, 2 data sources, up to 2 team members, and 2 dashboards. The 2 data sources are capped at 2 tables in total.

You can also chat with your data in plain language. Usage is capped at 20 requests per day and 50,000 tokens per month.

The Premium plan costs $200 per month, shown as a limited-time price against a list price of $499. It raises the limits to 10 agents, 10 data sources, 10 team members, and 10 dashboards. It also allows 500 requests per day and 2,000,000 tokens per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the daily request cap or the monthly token cap. It lists no overage price and no pay-as-you-go option for the Free plan. The only listed upgrade is a fixed monthly Premium plan, plus a Contact Sales tier with no caps.

Plan for the chat to stop working until the daily or monthly window resets, and test this early with your own data before you rely on it.

## Gotchas

- The 2 data sources share a cap of 2 tables in total. A large database with many tables will not fit.
- The Premium price of $200 per month is marked as a limited-time offer against $499, so the upgrade price may change.
- Decision briefs, PDF export, email notifications, and the model selector appear only in the Premium list, not in the Free list.

## Questions people ask

### Is Othor AI free?

Yes, there is a Free plan that costs $0. It includes 2 agents, 2 data sources, and 2 dashboards, with 20 requests per day and 50,000 tokens per month.

### How many requests does the Othor AI free plan allow?

The Free plan allows 20 requests per day and 50,000 tokens per month. Premium allows 500 requests per day and 2,000,000 tokens per month.

### Does Othor AI need a credit card?

The pricing page does not mention a card for the Free plan. It shows the plan as $0 forever with a Get Started button.
