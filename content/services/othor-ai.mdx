Othor AI reads connected business data, computes metrics and delivers scheduled briefs. Its current pricing and public signup form advertise a 14-day hosted trial without a credit card. This replaces the invitation-only presentation reviewed previously, although the terms dated November 11 still say access is by invitation. The form was inspected without submitting an account.

## What the free plan gives you

Hosted service is a trial without a published continuing Free plan or numerical usage allowance. Enterprise Hosted adds advanced intelligence, backups, monitoring, upgrades and email support at contact-based pricing.

The Apache-2.0 open-source edition runs on your Linux/Docker server with a domain, including business use. Each install supports one company; another needs another install. It includes nightly detectors, Morning Brief, action plans, Sweep, Summary and Business Model. Hosting costs remain yours.

Core calculations/briefs need no AI-provider key. Adding one enables AI-assisted setup/wording with separate provider costs. Dedicated Forecasts, Discover, Look back, Weekly Digest, External Signals and MCP enterprise features are not all included in the free core.

## When you hit a limit

Public pages do not state the hosted trial's usage quotas, exact behavior after fourteen days, or how former Free accounts are handled. Do not assume the old hosted allowance returns after the trial. Confirm those terms before connecting an important business workflow.

The current legal page describes agreed order-form or invoice fees, with payment by bank transfer. It also says data is deleted thirty days after service ends and backups roll off within a further thirty-five days; users can export results before then. That termination policy does not explain exactly when a trial ends service.

## Gotchas

- Current signup and pricing offer a public trial, while older terms retain invitation-only wording. Eligibility beyond the initial form was not tested.
- Self-hosting supplies the software, not a free managed server or free external model usage.
- PostgreSQL, MySQL and Google Sheets connections use read-only access. Confirm proposed metrics and inspect the preview run before relying on a brief.

## Questions people ask

### Is Othor AI free?

The hosted service offers a cardless fourteen-day trial. Its open-source core is free to self-host, with your own infrastructure costs.

### Does the hosted trial become a free plan?

No continuing hosted Free plan is published. Trial expiry behavior and legacy Free entitlements remain unspecified.

### Do I need an AI key to self-host?

No. The open-source page says the calculations and briefs work without one. An optional key adds AI-assisted setup and wording and may cost money through your provider.
