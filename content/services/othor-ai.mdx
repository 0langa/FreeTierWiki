Othor AI reads connected business data and summarizes the findings in a brief. Its November 11, 2026 release introduced Othor AI 2.0 with invitation-only signup. The earlier offer was a self-serve plan with limited access to its analytics tools. The current site directs prospective users to request access.

## What the free plan gives you

New readers cannot sign up for the former Free plan through the public site. Othor AI has replaced the signup route with an invitation request while it works with its first customers on the new product. It does not publish a Free quota or terms that carry forward the old request, token, and workspace limits.

The current product connects to PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Google Sheets through a read-only login. During setup, you confirm the metrics it should track and inspect a preview run. The service tests the data and can deliver a brief by email or share it through a read-only link. Each finding links to its calculation and the test behind it.

## When you hit a limit

Othor AI says it bills by invoice. Current public pages give neither a price nor a metered usage policy for new invitations. Ask for the invoice terms and the amount of data and reporting included before you connect a production data source.

If you already have an account on the former Free plan, check the terms for that account before relying on its old allowance. The new release describes how invited users sign in and connect data but does not say how legacy Free accounts are handled.

## Gotchas

- Requesting access starts an invitation process. It does not activate the former Free plan.
- Supported sources include PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Google Sheets. During onboarding you connect with read-only permissions and confirm how your metrics are defined.
- The current site states that billing is by invoice. The old public price page no longer loads.

## Questions people ask

### Is Othor AI free?

New users cannot open the former self-serve Free plan. The current service requires an invitation and does not publicly list a Free plan.

### What does the current product connect to?

The release notes list PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Google Sheets. You connect with a source account that has read-only access.

### Can existing Free customers keep their allowance?

Othor AI has not published how its current invitation-only product affects existing Free account terms. Check the terms shown in your account.
