Cartesia’s hosted APIs can generate speech from text and transcribe recorded audio. The provider also runs voice agents with a distinct allowance. A developer can try either kind of feature, then check which meter moved after a request.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists 20,000 credits each month on Free for text-to-speech and speech-to-text. It also lists a separate $1 of prepaid voice-agent usage per month. Do not include that dollar figure when estimating how many speech API requests the credits can fund. Check the pricing table for how the model and operation you choose draw from the relevant allowance. For a first demonstration, try short sample content. Compare the result with what your application needs and check the amount used before moving to longer input or more frequent requests.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says new speech requests are blocked when credits run out with overages disabled; paid plans can enable overages. New agent calls stop at a zero balance, and agent overages are unavailable on Free. Signup card requirements are still not confirmed. Speech and agent balances are separate, as are their concurrency pools: Free allows two TTS requests, eight STT requests, and eight agent calls concurrently.

## Gotchas

- Sonic TTS uses one credit per character, roughly 750–800 per audio minute; Ink STT uses three credits per audio second. A break tag counts as one TTS credit.
- The $1 agent allowance covers about 33 minutes at $0.03/minute before telephony and LLM costs. Cartesia-provided phone numbers add $0.014/minute; LLM usage is billed separately.
- The commercial-use license and instant voice cloning start at Pro ($5/month). Professional voice-clone slots start at Startup; Free and Pro have none.

## Questions people ask

### Can I try speech synthesis on Free?

Yes. The Free plan lists text-to-speech alongside speech-to-text.

### Does Free include hosted agent usage?

The plan lists a separate $1 prepaid monthly allowance for agents.

### How many requests fit in the credit allowance?

TTS uses one credit per character and STT three per audio second. The Free table estimates about 27 TTS minutes or 1 hour 51 minutes of STT if the whole balance goes to one operation.
