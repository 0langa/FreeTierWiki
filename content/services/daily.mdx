Daily sells video and audio calling infrastructure. You embed calls in your app with its SDKs or its prebuilt call UI, and Daily runs the WebRTC servers. It also runs Pipecat Cloud for voice AI agents, which is priced separately.

## What the free plan gives you

Daily says it provides 10,000 free participant minutes and support to every customer, each month. A participant minute is one minute for each participant on a call, so three people talking for ten minutes use thirty participant minutes. The same free block covers video calls and audio-only calls.

Free developer support runs over email and Discord. HD video costs nothing extra, and Daily does not price by resolution. Regional media zones and IP allowlists are listed as free add-ons. Metrics and logs are stored for 1 day for unpaid users and 3 days once you add a card on file.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that after your free 10,000 participant minutes for the month, each additional participant minute is billed at $0.004. It also says there is no hard cap that stops your calls: Daily Video is pay-as-you-go, and you track usage in the dashboard.

Audio-only calls past the free block start at $0.00099 per minute, and prices drop in tiers as volume grows. A spend limit setting exists for Pipecat Cloud, but the page does not offer one for Daily Video.

## Gotchas

- Participant minutes multiply by headcount, so a large group call uses the free block fast.
- Recording is billed per recorded minute and its storage adds $0.003 per minute, separate from call minutes.
- The number of concurrent calls depends on your rooms, and the page says up to 100,000 simultaneous calls apply with a card on file.

## Questions people ask

### How many free minutes does Daily give?

Daily gives 10,000 free participant minutes every month. A participant minute counts each person on a call separately.

### What happens after the free minutes run out?

Each extra participant minute is billed at $0.004. Daily says there is no hard cap that stops your calls.

### Is recording included in the free minutes?

No. Recording is billed per recorded minute, not per participant, and is separate from call minutes.
