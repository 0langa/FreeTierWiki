You use Miro in the browser or the apps, on a canvas that holds sticky notes, diagrams, tables, docs, Kanban, and slides. It suits teams and individuals who think visually and want a shared board.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 with unlimited team members and 3 editable boards. The pricing card says you can create and share unlimited boards and that the three most recent ones remain editable. Miro's Free help article agrees: only the three most recently created boards in a team are active, and the rest are locked in view-only mode.

You get 7000+ templates, Docs, Tables, Timelines, Kanban, Slides, 250+ integrations, and 100 MCP calls per day. AI is limited: the pricing table calls it a trial, while its FAQ lists 10 credits monthly. Members collaborate on shared boards; Free has no private board sharing. Visitors are view-only, and guests are unavailable.

## When you hit a limit

Miro does not block you from creating a fourth board. The help article says the team sees an "over the active boards limit" message naming the board that will be locked, and the three most recently created boards are always the active ones. To edit an older board, delete other active boards, ask teammates to delete theirs, copy the inactive board, or upgrade.

Starter costs $8 per member monthly when billed yearly and adds unlimited private boards, visitor editing, and high-resolution exports. Miro's mind-map page explicitly says the Free plan needs no credit card. AI credits reset monthly without rollover; additional credit packages are listed for paid plans.

## Gotchas

- You can create unlimited boards on Free, but only the three most recently created are editable. You cannot choose which boards stay active.
- Free has no private board sharing, and visitors are view only.
- High-resolution JPG and PDF export is a Starter feature.

## Questions people ask

### Is Miro free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 3 editable boards, 7000+ templates, and 250+ integrations.

### How many boards can I have on Miro Free?

You can create unlimited boards, but only the three most recently created are editable. The rest are locked view-only until you delete active boards, copy them or upgrade.

### Does Miro need a credit card?

No. Miro's official mind-map page explicitly says its Free plan needs no credit card.
