You use Miro in the browser or the apps, on a canvas that holds sticky notes, diagrams, tables, docs, Kanban, and slides. It suits teams and individuals who think visually and want a shared board.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is for small teams getting started with Miro. The key limit is 3 editable boards. You can create and share unlimited boards, but only the three most recent ones stay editable. You get 7000+ Miro and community-made templates, and structured formats such as Docs, Tables, Timelines, Kanban, and Slides on the canvas. There are 250+ integrations, Live Embed, and an MCP server limited to 100 calls per day. AI credits are a limited trial.

Members can collaborate, and visitor editing is listed as a Starter feature. Private board sharing, spaces, and blueprints are not included.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says unlimited boards stay shareable, but only the three most recent ones remain editable. The page does not say how older boards behave beyond that, so check before you rely on an old board. The Starter plan costs $8 per member per month, billed yearly, and gives unlimited and private boards, visitor editing, and high-resolution exports. The pricing page does not mention a card for the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- Only three boards are editable at a time, so a new board can lock an older one.
- Free has no private board sharing, and visitors are view only.
- High-resolution JPG and PDF export is a Starter feature.
