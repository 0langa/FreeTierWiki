Most people use the website: pick a converter, upload a file, and download the result. Developers can call the same service through a REST API or the official Node.js, Python, and PHP libraries.

## What the free plan gives you

You can convert without an account. Unregistered users get 5 conversions per day, up to 50 per month, with 10 MB per file. If you sign up and activate your account through the confirmation email, you get 10 conversions per day, up to 100 per month, and 20 MB per file. Converted files stay on the server for 24 hours, and uploaded source files are deleted right after conversion.

The API has its own free plan: 10 API calls per day and 100 per month, 20 MB per file. Web and API use share one quota. Sandbox mode is unlimited. Add sandbox: true to a request and you get mock data back in 2 to 5 seconds, without using quota. Registered users also get 100 AI credits per month.

## When you hit a limit

The limits are hard stops, and the free plans need no credit card. On the API, a request over your quota returns a 429 rate limit error. The error includes details about your quota and when it resets. You can upgrade from the dashboard, and the change takes effect at once. Daily and monthly limits both apply and reset at the start of each period, so you can reach the monthly limit before the month ends. Only successful conversions count toward the API quota. Failed ones are not charged.

## Gotchas

- Sandbox requests return mock data. They do not convert your real file.
- A signed-up account that you have not activated by email still counts as unregistered, with the lower limits.
- The free plan has no uptime guarantee. The uptime SLAs start on higher paid plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Conversion Tools free?

Yes, with limits. You can convert without an account, and a free registered account gets 10 conversions per day and 100 per month. No credit card is needed.

### What is the file size limit on Conversion Tools?

It is 10 MB per file for unregistered users and 20 MB per file for registered free users. Paid plans raise the limit.

### What happens when I go over my Conversion Tools limit?

Web conversions stop at the daily or monthly limit. API requests return a 429 error until your quota resets or you upgrade.
