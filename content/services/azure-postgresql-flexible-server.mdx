You create and manage the server in the Azure portal, then connect with any PostgreSQL client or driver. It suits developers who are new to Azure and want a managed PostgreSQL to learn or test on.

## What the free plan gives you

New Azure customers get free monthly amounts of 20 or more popular services for 12 months. For Azure Database for PostgreSQL, that is 750 hours per month of a Flexible Server Burstable B1MS instance, plus 32 GB of storage and 32 GB of backup storage. The amounts reset every month.

You can get this through a pay-as-you-go account or through the Azure free account. The free account adds a $200 credit that you must use within 30 days. The Azure page says your card is not charged on the free account, though a temporary $1 authorization may appear during sign-up.

## When you hit a limit

The free amount is a monthly allowance, not a hard cap. Hours, storage, and backup storage above the allowance are billed at pay-as-you-go rates. The same happens when the 12 months end. Run one Burstable B1MS server to stay inside the 750 hours.

Check your usage in the Azure portal so a second server or larger disk does not create a charge.

## Gotchas

- The 12-month free amount is only for new Azure customers.
- The Development workload type uses Burstable sizes, and Production uses General Purpose or Memory Optimized, which fall outside the free offer.
- Turning on high availability adds a standby server, which is not part of the free amount.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Database for PostgreSQL flexible server free?

It is free for new Azure customers for 12 months, within 750 hours of a B1MS Burstable instance, 32 GB of storage, and 32 GB of backup storage each month.

### What happens after the 12 months?

Standard pay-as-you-go prices apply to the server. The same goes for any usage above the monthly free amounts during the 12 months.

### Does Azure PostgreSQL free tier need a credit card?

The Azure free account asks for card verification, and a temporary $1 authorization may appear and then be reversed. The page says the card is not charged on the free account.
