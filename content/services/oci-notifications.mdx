Notifications is the Oracle Cloud Infrastructure service for delivering messages to subscribers to a topic. An OCI alarm or an app can publish a message, and subscribers receive it through the channel you configured. A team can use this to send an incident to both an email address and a webhook handler.

## What the free plan gives you

Oracle lists 1 million HTTPS notifications per month and 1,000 email notifications per month under Always Free. Those allowances are tied to delivery channels. Start with a topic and a subscription, then confirm that the destination accepts messages. A subscription can remain pending until the recipient or endpoint completes confirmation. Use a test alert to check delivery before putting a monitoring rule in service. A single published event may fan out to more than one subscriber, so budget deliveries based on how many destinations will receive it.

## When you hit a limit

Oracle publishes paid rates by delivery type above the included amount. Track email and HTTPS separately to see which type is approaching its limit. On a Free Tier account, check the available service limit in the Console. Oracle says a signup card can receive a temporary verification hold and that it charges for cloud usage only if you elect to upgrade the account.

## Gotchas

- One active topic can generate a lot of deliveries when several subscribers listen to it. Email notifications are processed and delivered through Oracle resources in US regions. The email allowance is for Notifications topic deliveries. OCI Email Delivery has its own meter and use case.
- Confirm every required subscription and test the endpoint that receives the message. Delivery is at least once, so receivers should tolerate duplicates.
- Unacknowledged messages retry with exponential backoff for two hours by default, then are discarded; the retry duration is configurable.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [Amazon SNS](/services/sns/) for AWS topic-based notifications.
- [OCI Email Delivery](/services/oracle-email-delivery/) for application email through Oracle’s separate sending service.

## Questions people ask

### Is OCI Notifications free to use?

Oracle includes monthly email and HTTPS delivery allowances under Always Free. Costs depend on channel, usage and the tenancy’s billing status.

### Can it call my API?

Yes. Configure an HTTPS subscription and confirm it according to the Notifications documentation.

### Is the email allowance the same as OCI Email Delivery?

They are distinct OCI products. Notifications sends from a topic to subscribers, while Email Delivery serves as an application mail transport.
