You write in the GitBook web editor or sync a Git repository, then publish with one click. There is also a CLI, an API, and an MCP server for working from AI tools.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for individuals and costs $0 per site per month. It includes 1 user and unlimited page views; GitBook says it never limits traffic to published docs. You also get the block-based editor, change requests, commenting, version history, Git Sync, importing, integrations, full text search, page view analytics, basic customization, an API playground, and email support. Docs are optimized for LLMs with llms.txt files, and GitBook creates an MCP server for your published docs.

GitBook Agent, the AI writing helper, is limited to 10 messages per week on Free.

## When you hit a limit

The main limit is people: the plan includes 1 user, so a second editor means a paid plan. Paid plans add users at $12 per user per month. The Premium plan starts at $65 per site per month when billed annually and comes with a 14 day free trial. It adds a custom domain, real-time collaboration, private share links, and more. AI features on paid plans use credits and pause when credits run out. The Free plan needs no card, and the pricing page does not describe a bill for it.

## Gotchas

- Your site gets a gitbook.io address; a custom domain is not included in the Free plan.
- Real-time collaboration, review and approve edits, merge rules, and reusable content are not in the Free plan.
- GitBook Agent allows 10 messages per week on Free, so heavy AI drafting will hit that quickly.

## Questions people ask

### Is GitBook free?

Yes. GitBook has a Free plan for individuals, with 1 user and unlimited page views. Paid plans start at $65 per site per month when billed annually.

### Can I use a custom domain on GitBook Free?

No. The pricing page lists custom domain as not included in the Free plan. It is part of Premium, Ultimate, and Enterprise.

### Can I add teammates on GitBook Free?

The Free plan includes 1 user. To add teammates, you move to a paid plan, where each added user costs $12 per month.
