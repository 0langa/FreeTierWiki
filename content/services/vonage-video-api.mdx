Vonage Video API is a hosted WebRTC platform for embedding video in apps. Core features include video, voice, signaling and chat, TURN relay, and SDKs, with recording, broadcast, and captions as advanced features.

## What the free plan gives you

New Vonage Video API customers get a free trial of 100,000 minutes. That is 75,000 participant minutes for video sessions plus 25,000 test minutes for advanced features, split as 5,000 minutes each of HD composed archive, individual stream archive, Experience Composer, Audio Connector, and live captions.

The trial minutes are valid for 12 months from the date of your first API call, and they do not renew. The trial page notes that while the minutes are free, an active account with a positive cash balance is required to use them. Sessions support up to 15,000 participants and 25 active publishers.

## When you hit a limit

The trial page says that once you have used all minutes for any trial feature, your account begins to be charged through your current payment method. New prepaid customers need to add a payment method and funds to keep using the Video API, and can turn on balance notifications or auto reload.

After the trial, video sessions cost $0.00410 per participant per minute. The pricing page lists no bandwidth limitations, and recording and broadcast are billed per archive or session minute.

## Gotchas

- Each feature has its own pool of trial minutes, so using up the live captions minutes starts billing for captions while video minutes remain.
- A participant is a connection, so one person joining from two devices counts as two participants.
- The free minutes are for new customers only and expire 12 months after your first API call.

## Questions people ask

### Does Vonage Video API have a free tier?

It has a one-time free trial for new customers: 75,000 video participant minutes plus 25,000 minutes for advanced features, valid for 12 months.

### Do the free minutes renew each month?

No. They are a single trial allowance that is valid for 12 months from your first API call.

### What happens when the trial minutes run out?

Your account is charged through your payment method. Prepaid customers need to add funds to keep using the Video API.
