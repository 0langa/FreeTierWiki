You sign up, add your domain in the Zoho Mail admin console, create the mailboxes, and point your domain's MX records at Zoho. After that, people read mail in the web app or the mobile app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Mail Free plan is permanently free and needs no credit card, according to the pricing page. It allows up to 5 users and gives each user 5 GB of email storage. It hosts a custom email address for one domain.

The plan includes Mail, Calendar, ToDo, and Cliq for team chat. It has a mobile app. Zoho states that it never shows ads and never sells your data to third parties. The plan lists file storage as not available and says it is available only in selected data centers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a user fills the 5 GB of storage or when you want a sixth user. The paid plans in the comparison table offer more storage per user, such as 10 GB, and more features.

The free plan is also the one that leaves out IMAP, POP, and ActiveSync. If you need a desktop mail client, you must move to a paid plan, because the free plan does not include those protocols.

## Gotchas

- The free plan does not include IMAP, POP, or ActiveSync, so you read mail in webmail or the Zoho mobile app.
- You must add and verify your domain first. If you registered the domain through Zoho, you can skip the verification step.
- After you create the mailboxes, you must change the domain's MX records. Until you do, mail does not arrive in Zoho Mail.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Mail free?

Yes. The Mail Free plan is permanently free for up to 5 users, with 5 GB of email storage per user and email for one custom domain. No credit card is required.

### Does the Zoho Mail free plan have IMAP?

No. The pricing page says IMAP, POP, and ActiveSync are not included. You can use webmail and the mobile app.

### Can I use my own domain with free Zoho Mail?

Yes, for one domain. You add the domain, verify it, create the users, and change the MX records.
