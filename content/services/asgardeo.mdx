You register applications in the Asgardeo console, then add an SDK such as the React one or use standard OAuth2, OIDC, or SAML endpoints. It suits developers who want hosted sign-in and SSO with a small free tier.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It has no card requirement and no time limit. You get up to 15,000 monthly active users, 5 applications, 1 B2B organization, and 1 social login. It also includes 5,000 M2M tokens and 2,500 AgentID tokens.

Free caps sessions at one day and keeps audit logs for seven days. Users who never sign in are not counted, and stored users cost nothing. The plan includes a 30-day free trial of Growth. After a downgrade, you become eligible for a new trial 60 days later. Growth starts at $25 per month with 3,500 monthly active users and 2 enterprise connections.

## When you hit a limit

On the Free plan, usage above the quotas is deactivated rather than removed. The pricing page says your data and resources are kept and nothing is deleted. So the Free plan acts as a hard cap and does not bill you.

To go further, you move to the Growth plan. For paid plans, the pricing FAQ says nothing stops working when you go over a limit. Extra usage is billed per unit on your next invoice, and Growth bills are capped at $2,000 a month. Check your M2M token use, since only token issuance counts.

## Gotchas

- The product is now called WSO2 Identity Platform. Docs, console pages, and package names can show either name.
- Free allows 5 applications and 1 B2B organization. A multi-tenant app can reach that limit fast.
- Free sessions last one day at most, and audit logs are kept for seven days.

## Questions people ask

### Is Asgardeo free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and has no time limit. It includes up to 15,000 monthly active users and 5 applications.

### Does Asgardeo need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no card is needed for the Free plan, and the Growth trial is also cardless.

### What happens when I go over the Asgardeo free limits?

Usage above the Free quotas is deactivated, not deleted. Your data and resources are kept, and you can move to Growth for more.
