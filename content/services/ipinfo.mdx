You sign up, copy a token from the dashboard, and call `api.ipinfo.io/lite` over HTTPS with the token as a query parameter, Basic auth, or a Bearer header. It suits developers who only need the country and network of a visitor and want no request quota.

## What the free plan gives you

IPinfo Lite is free forever and needs no credit card. It gives you unlimited API requests per month for country-level geolocation, plus ASN, ASN name, and ASN domain. The pricing page also lists 50 web lookups in the dashboard.

Nothing is limited per day, per hour, or per second on the free plan, and the docs say IPinfo does not cap parallel requests. City-level data starts on IPinfo Core at $26 a month. Privacy detection for VPNs, proxies, and Tor is on paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

The free Lite plan has no request quota, so there is no limit to hit. The docs say paid plans return HTTP 429 when you pass the monthly limit, unless metered billing is turned on, in which case overages keep requests flowing.

On Core, IPinfo bills overages at a set price per request. IPinfo also emails you as you near the plan limit and again when you reach it. The free plan does not bill you.

## Gotchas

- Lite gives country, not city. If you need a city or coordinates, you must pay for Core or higher.
- Full ASN details and privacy detection are not part of Lite.
- Your token is in every request, so keep it out of public client-side code unless you accept that exposure.

## Questions people ask

### Is IPinfo free?

Yes. IPinfo Lite is free forever, has no request quota, and needs no credit card. It returns country-level location and ASN data.

### Does the IPinfo free plan include city?

No. The free Lite plan is country level only. City-level geolocation starts on the Core plan.

### What happens when I go over the IPinfo limit?

Lite has no limit. On paid plans the API returns HTTP 429 past the monthly quota, or it bills overages if metered billing is on.
