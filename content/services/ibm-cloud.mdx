You create an account, open the catalog in the web console, and provision services one by one. The free tier suits people who want to test a broad set of IBM services, not host one app long term.

## What the free plan gives you

Your account is a Pay-as-you-go account with a card on file. It includes access to more than 40 products that have an always-free Lite plan. IBM says these plans never expire and cannot be charged. You can provision one instance of any service that has a Lite plan.

New accounts also get a $200 credit for the first $200 of apps and services. The credit is available for 30 days and works on any IBM Cloud product. Lite plan quotas are based on usage, and they renew on a monthly basis or on a one-off usage basis.

## When you hit a limit

Reaching a quota on a Lite plan suspends the service for that month. Quota limits are per org, not per instance, and a new instance in the same org shows the usage of earlier ones. The quotas reset on the first day of every month.

Inactivity is a second limit. After 10 days of no development activity, your apps go to sleep. After 30 days of no development activity, your Lite service instances are deleted. Paid plans are billed monthly once you pass the free threshold, and you can set spending alerts.

## Gotchas

- IBM asks for payment details before you create the account. It puts a hold of about $1 on the card to check it.
- Lite service instances are deleted after 30 days of no development activity, so unattended test setups can disappear.
- Using a non-Lite plan on the free tier needs an upgrade to a Pay-as-you-go account, and the usage beyond the free tier is billed.

## Questions people ask

### Is IBM Cloud free?

Partly. More than 40 products have an always-free Lite plan, and new accounts get a $200 credit for 30 days. You need a credit card to create the account.

### Does IBM Cloud need a credit card for the free tier?

Yes. IBM asks for payment details up front and places a small hold, typically around $1, to check the card. IBM says Lite plans never incur charges; you are billed only when you go beyond the free tier of a non-Lite plan.

### What happens when my IBM Cloud Lite plan runs out?

The service is suspended for that month, and the quota resets on the first of the month. Lite instances are also deleted after 30 days of no development activity.
