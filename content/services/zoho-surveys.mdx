You build surveys in a web editor and send them by email, link, embed, or pop-up.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan allows unlimited surveys, but only 3 can be active at a time. Each survey can have up to 10 questions and 100 responses.

You get over 250 survey templates and a survey builder in 26 languages. Sharing options include email surveys, pop-up surveys on your website, embeds, and social media links. You see responses in real time and can export full surveys or reports as a PDF.

Security features on the free plan include HTTPS encryption, password protection, and hash URLs that encrypt survey links with unique codes. Students get the Plus plan for free, and teachers get 50 percent off all plans.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a survey passes 100 responses or when you try to activate a fourth survey. It shows no overage fee, and the free plan asks for no card.

To go further you move to a paid plan. Basic gives 12000 responses a year and unlimited questions. Basic also adds skip logic, a logo, Slack and Shopify integrations, spam protection, and exports to XLS and CSV. Plus adds unlimited surveys. The pricing page text shows no price, so check it in your browser.

## Gotchas

- The limit of 100 responses applies to each survey, and only 3 surveys can be active at once.
- Surveys longer than 10 questions need the Basic plan, which lists unlimited questions.
- The pricing page shows no price in its text, and it offers a 7-day Enterprise trial with no card instead.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Surveys free?

Yes. The free plan allows unlimited surveys with 3 active at a time. Each survey can have 10 questions and 100 responses.

### How many responses can I collect for free on Zoho Surveys?

You can collect 100 responses per survey on the free plan. The Basic plan raises this to 12000 responses a year.

### Do students get Zoho Surveys for free?

Students get the Plus plan for free, according to the pricing page. Teachers get 50 percent off all plans.
