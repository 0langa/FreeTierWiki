You use Quidlo Timesheets in the browser, or with its free desktop and mobile apps. It suits small teams that want to replace a spreadsheet with a simple timer.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan costs $0 per month, needs no credit card, and covers up to 10 users. It includes unlimited projects and an unlimited timer. You can start the tracker with one button or add time by hand and edit entries later.

The time tracker lets you add notes or tags to tasks. The reports page makes custom reports that you can share with colleagues or clients. The FAQ says the desktop apps for Windows and macOS and the mobile apps for iOS and Android are free. The Pro plan costs $29 per month when billed annually, or $39 billed monthly, for a flat fee for the whole workspace.

## When you hit a limit

The cap is 10 users. If your team grows past 10 users on the Starter plan, the workspace is frozen and no one can track time. To unfreeze it, reduce the number of active users to 10. The pricing page lists Pro with unlimited users.

Nothing here bills you automatically. All new users get a 30-day Pro trial with no billing information, and you lose Pro features when it ends. You then continue on Starter.

## Gotchas

- A workspace above 10 users stops tracking time on Starter. This also happens when you cancel Pro with a larger team.
- Only registered companies can upgrade to Pro, and the upgrade form asks for a Tax ID.
- The pricing page lists Pro as the plan with additional features, but it does not spell out which ones Starter lacks.

## Questions people ask

### Is Quidlo Timesheets free?

Yes. The Starter plan costs $0 per month, needs no credit card, and allows up to 10 users with unlimited projects.

### What happens if I go over 10 users on Quidlo Timesheets?

The workspace is frozen and no one can track time. You unfreeze it by reducing the active users to 10, as the pricing FAQ says.

### Does Quidlo Timesheets have a mobile app?

Yes. The FAQ lists mobile apps for iOS and Android and desktop apps for Windows and macOS. All of them are free to download and use.
