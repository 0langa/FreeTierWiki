You set up a project in the web dashboard and describe the build in an `appveyor.yml` file or in the dashboard UI. Self-hosted AppVeyor and self-hosted build jobs are also on offer.

## What the free plan gives you

The Open-source plan is free. It covers unlimited public projects with 1 concurrent job and 5 self-hosted jobs, and it comes with community support. Private projects are not part of it.

All plans have a quota of 60 minutes per build job. If a build needs longer, the pricing page tells you to contact AppVeyor. The FAQ explains that every push starts a build with one or more jobs. With only 1 concurrent job, a job that arrives while another is running waits in a queue until the first one finishes.

## When you hit a limit

On the free plan, extra jobs do not fail. They queue behind the running job. The pricing page lists extra concurrent jobs at $50 per month, or $25 per month for open-source plans, and extra self-hosted jobs at $10 per month in packs of 5, or $5 per month for open-source plans.

The page does not describe what happens to a job that runs past 60 minutes. It says only that every plan has that quota. It also offers a 50% discount on all plans for students, educational organizations, and open-source projects that want more compute or concurrent jobs.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is for public repositories only. Private projects start with the Basic plan at $29 per month.
- Builds run one at a time, so a busy repository will queue jobs.
- If you keep an `appveyor.yml` in the repository, it overrides settings you made in the UI unless you tell AppVeyor to ignore it.

## Questions people ask

### Is AppVeyor free?

Yes, for public projects. The Open-source plan has unlimited public projects, 1 concurrent job, and 5 self-hosted jobs. Private projects need a paid plan that starts at $29 per month.

### Does AppVeyor need a credit card?

The pricing page says the 14-day trial of the paid plans needs no credit card. It does not mention a card for the free Open-source plan.

### What happens when I run more than one AppVeyor job at a time?

On the free plan, extra jobs wait in a queue until the running job finishes. More concurrent jobs cost extra.
