ArcGIS Location Platform is Esri's developer offering for location services: basemaps, places, geocoding, routing, elevation, spatial analysis, and hosting for your own data. Pricing is per service, and each service lists a free monthly amount before charges apply. It suits developers who want GIS tools and data hosting next to standard map APIs.

## What the free plan gives you

Every listed service has its own free tier. Basemap tiles include 2M free tiles, or 1K basemap sessions on the session model, and static maps include 1K. Geocodes that are not stored include 20K, routes include 20K, and service areas 5K. Nearby and extent place searches include 500, and place attribute requests 100 each. Elevation includes 50K points. Data hosting includes 250 MB of feature storage, 125 MB each of feature query and feature edit bandwidth, and 25 GB of vector tile bandwidth. Free tiers refresh at the start of your billing cycle.

## When you hit a limit

Pay-as-you-go is turned off by default for new accounts. Without it, services where you have exceeded the free tier are disabled, and for bandwidth or tile generation overages access is restored at the start of your next billing cycle. If you exceed hosted data storage, reduce storage below the free threshold and wait for the next cycle. A payment method, such as a credit card, is required to enable pay-as-you-go and keep using services beyond the free tier; then usage is billed, for example $0.5 per 1,000 geocodes.

## Gotchas

- Stored geocodes and batch geocodes have no free tier, so saving results always costs money.
- If you turn pay-as-you-go off after exceeding the storage free tier, storage keeps accruing costs until you delete hosted services.
- Some services, such as stop-optimized routes, start at a price with no free amount.

## Questions people ask

### Is ArcGIS Location Platform free?

Yes, within each service's free tier, such as 20K geocodes and 20K routes a month.

### Will I be charged if I go over the free tier?

Not by default. Pay-as-you-go is off for new accounts, so services past their free tier are disabled until the next billing cycle.

### Do I need a credit card?

Only to enable pay-as-you-go. A payment method is required to use services beyond the free tier.
