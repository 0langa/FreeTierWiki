Bubble is a no-code platform where you design pages, define a database, and wire up logic with visual workflows, then host the app on Bubble. One project can hold a web app and native mobile apps that share a backend. It suits founders and developers who want to build a working app without writing front-end and back-end code.

## What the free plan gives you

Bubble's Free plan is for projects under construction and costs $0 per month. Each month it includes 50K workload units, the single metric Bubble uses for server work such as database operations, workflows, and web interactions, and 500 AI credits for features like the AI agent and page generation. The AI credit allowance refreshes on the first of each month and does not roll over.

You get the API connector, the web debugger, and a development version of your web app. You can build native mobile apps on Free too. A live website, a custom domain, and server logs start on Starter at $29 per month billed annually.

## When you hit a limit

Apps on the Free plan cannot subscribe to workload tiers and cannot enable overages, because Bubble designs the plan for development. So there is no workload bill on Free. The pricing page does not say exactly what the app does when it uses all 50K workload units in a month.

Once AI credits run out, access to AI-powered features is limited, and only paid plans can buy more.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has no live version; you need Starter or higher to put the app on the web.
- Testing a native mobile app in Apple TestFlight or Google Play, or publishing it to a store, needs a paid plan.
- Web and mobile apps in a project share one workload allowance, so usage from both counts together.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bubble free?

Yes, for building. The Free plan includes 50K workload units and 500 AI credits a month and a development version of your app, but a live app needs a paid plan.

### Can I launch a Bubble app on the Free plan?

No. A live website and a custom domain start on the Starter plan, and mobile store publishing also needs a paid plan.

### Will Bubble charge me for workload overages on the Free plan?

No. Free plan apps cannot enable overages or buy workload tiers.
