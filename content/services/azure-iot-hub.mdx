You work with IoT Hub in the Azure portal, with the Azure CLI and its `azure-iot` extension, or with device SDKs. It suits developers who want to test device connectivity on Azure before they pay for a production hub. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free edition is meant for proof of concept projects. It allows up to 8,000 messages per day and up to 500 device identities. Azure counts each message as 0.5 KB for the daily quota on a free hub, while paid tiers use 4 KB.

Each Azure subscription can have one hub in the free tier. The free tier has the capabilities of the Standard tier, but with small allowances. A free hub can have 5 message routing queries, 2 message enrichments, and one extra endpoint. Message routing and Event Grid integration are available on it.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the 8,000 messages per day. The daily quota is a fixed number for the hub, and you can see it in the Azure portal.

Separate throttles limit the rate of calls. If you hit one, IoT Hub answers with error code 429, and your client should back off and retry. IoT Hub accepts short bursts above a throttle, then queues requests, and finally rejects them. You cannot upgrade a free hub to Basic or Standard. You create a new paid hub instead.

## Gotchas

- Only one free hub is allowed per subscription, so a second free hub needs another subscription.
- The name of a free hub can be visible to other free-tier users, and hub names are public DNS names. Do not put private data in the name.
- Deleting a hub deletes its device identity registry, so keep a record of your devices before you remove it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure IoT Hub free?

There is a Free edition. It allows 8,000 messages per day and 500 device identities, with one free hub per Azure subscription. It is meant for testing and evaluation.

### Can I upgrade a free IoT hub?

No. The docs say a free hub cannot be upgraded to Basic or Standard. You create a new hub in a paid tier.

### What happens when I send too many messages to IoT Hub?

The pricing page does not say what happens at the daily quota. For call rates, IoT Hub throttles and answers with error code 429, and your client should back off and retry.
