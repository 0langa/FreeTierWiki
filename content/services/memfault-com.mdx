You add the Memfault SDK to your firmware, then follow devices and crashes in the Memfault web app. It suits embedded teams, especially those on Nordic chips.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free per month. The pricing page describes it as exclusive pricing for teams building on Nordic low-power wireless solutions. It includes no charge for up to 10 development devices. The Get started button leads to nRF Cloud, so sign-up runs through Nordic's cloud pricing page. Memfault also offers an interactive sandbox on its docs site if you want to look around first.

The pricing page does not define a development device, and it does not state data retention for the Developer plan. For comparison, the Growth plan costs $3,495 per month and keeps data for 30 days.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no hard cap. Beyond 10 devices, Memfault uses pay-as-you-go pricing for development and production devices. The pricing page does not list the rate, so ask before you add devices.

The page does not say whether a credit card is required. Count your devices before you ship a pilot fleet, because production devices also count past the free 10.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is aimed at Nordic-based teams, so other hardware may not qualify.
- Production devices count toward the pay-as-you-go line, not only development devices.
- The MCU docs assume you already have a Memfault account, and tell new users to reach out to Memfault.

## Questions people ask

### Is Memfault free?

Partly. The Developer plan has no charge for up to 10 development devices, but the pricing is exclusive to teams building on Nordic low-power wireless solutions.

### What happens when I go over 10 devices on Memfault?

The pricing page says pay-as-you-go pricing applies beyond 10 development and production devices. It does not list the rate.

### How do I send data from my device to Memfault?

The nRF Connect SDK guide says devices push data chunks to the Memfault cloud. nRF91 and nRF70 devices can upload over HTTPS, and Bluetooth LE devices use a phone app.
