You work with Mintlify in a web editor, or in a Git repository with the `mint` CLI. It suits developers who want a docs site that deploys when they push.

## What the free plan gives you

Free costs $0 without a card: 5 editors, web editor, Git sync, search, API playground, MCP, custom domain, SEO/agent optimizations and components. A no-card Pro trial ends after 14 days or 5,000 credits; afterward upgrade or remain Free.

Free excludes AI credits, Assistant, writing agent and automations. New Starter costs $150/month annually or $180 monthly: 10 editors, 5,000 monthly credits, writing agent, Autopilot and previews. Pro adds unlimited editors, 10,000 credits, Assistant and analytics; automations require Enterprise. Writing-agent use consumes no credits, but Assistant/Autopilot do. Credits documentation still names only Pro/Enterprise.

Non-commercial open-source projects can apply for free Pro: recognized license, no venture/revenue funding, and no for-profit ownership or primary maintenance.

## When you hit a limit

Free/Starter/Pro allow 5/10/unlimited editors; sixth-editor enforcement on Free is unspecified. Pro overages cost $0.01/credit if enabled. They default off and are configured per project, though balances pool organization-wide.

A dashboard spending limit can cap overages. With overages off, credit-powered features pause until balance reset; reaching a spending limit pauses them until next billing cycle. Free cannot buy credits. Up to 50% of unused credits roll over, with balance capped at 1.5 times the monthly allowance; add-on changes reset rollovers. Check each project's overage setting rather than relying on the pooled balance.

## Gotchas

- The AI Assistant, writing agent, and automations are not on Free.
- Preview deployments, user feedback, webhooks, and platform analytics show as not included on Free.
- White labeling is listed only for Enterprise.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mintlify free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 per month with 5 editor seats and no credit card. AI credits are not included on it.

### Can I use Mintlify AI features on the free plan?

Not the credit-based ones. The pricing table marks credits, the Assistant, and the writing agent as not included on Free.

### Does Mintlify need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required to get started.
