You work with Mintlify in a web editor, or in a Git repository with the `mint` CLI. It suits developers who want a docs site that deploys when they push.

## What the free plan gives you

Starter costs $0 with no card: 5 editor seats, web editor, Git sync, search, API playground, MCP server, custom domain, SEO/agent optimizations, and custom components.

New accounts get a no-card Pro trial ending after 14 days or 5,000 AI credits, whichever comes first. Then upgrade or keep docs live on Starter. Starter excludes AI credits, Assistant, writing agent, and automations. Pro includes unlimited editors and 10,000 monthly credits; its writing agent uses no credits, while Assistant answers and automation updates consume them.

Eligible non-commercial open-source projects can apply for Pro free forever. They need a recognized open-source license, cannot be venture-backed or revenue-funded, and cannot be owned or primarily maintained by a for-profit company.

## When you hit a limit

The seat limit is the main cap: Starter has 5 editor seats, and Pro has unlimited editor seats. The pricing page does not say what happens if you invite a sixth editor. For AI features, Pro bills extra credits at $0.01 per credit, and its dashboard lets you disable overages or set a hard spending limit. With overages off, credit-powered features stop until the balance resets. With a spending limit, they pause until the next billing cycle. The Starter plan does not list credits, so there is no overage to pay there.

## Gotchas

- The AI Assistant, writing agent, and automations are not on Starter.
- Preview deployments, user feedback, webhooks, and platform analytics show as not included on Starter.
- White labeling is listed only for Enterprise.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mintlify free?

Yes. The Starter plan is $0 per month with 5 editor seats and no credit card. AI credits are not included on it.

### Can I use Mintlify AI features on the free plan?

Not the credit-based ones. The pricing table marks credits, the Assistant, and the writing agent as not included on Starter.

### Does Mintlify need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required to get started.
