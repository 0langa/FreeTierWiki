You work with Mintlify in a web editor, or in a Git repository with the `mint` CLI. It suits developers who want a docs site that deploys when they push.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan costs $0 per month and needs no credit card. The pricing page says to get started for free with your first month of credits on us. Starter includes 5 editor seats, the web editor, Git sync, search, an API playground, and an MCP server. Custom domain, SEO and agent optimizations, and custom components are in the feature list without a Starter restriction. Starter is for individuals and small teams.

The AI features in the table, such as Credits, the Assistant, the writing agent, and automations, are marked as not included on Starter. Pro lists unlimited editor seats and 10,000 credits per month.

## When you hit a limit

The seat limit is the main cap: Starter has 5 editor seats, and Pro has unlimited editor seats. The pricing page does not say what happens if you invite a sixth editor. For AI features, Pro bills extra credits at $0.01 per credit, and a FAQ asks whether you can set a hard limit on AI credits. The Starter plan does not list credits, so there is no overage to pay there.

## Gotchas

- The AI Assistant, writing agent, and automations are not on Starter.
- Preview deployments, user feedback, webhooks, and platform analytics show as not included on Starter.
- White labeling is listed only for Enterprise.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mintlify free?

Yes. The Starter plan is $0 per month with 5 editor seats and no credit card. AI credits are not included on it.

### Can I use Mintlify AI features on the free plan?

Not the credit-based ones. The pricing table marks credits, the Assistant, and the writing agent as not included on Starter.

### Does Mintlify need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required to get started.
