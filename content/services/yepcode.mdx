YepCode is a platform for running code-based integrations and automations. You write a process, and YepCode runs it in a managed sandbox on demand, from a webhook, on a schedule, or from a form. Usage is measured in Yeps, where one Yep is one second of execution time. It suits developers who prefer writing code over dragging boxes in a no-code builder.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free with no credit card required and is aimed at individual developers. It includes 50K Yeps of execution time, a maximum of 300 executions a day, sandboxes with limited CPU and memory, a 30-second maximum execution time, and 1 concurrent execution.

The plan has 1 team member, 3 days of execution history, and up to 5 storage files of 5Mb each at most. Every run consumes one Yep per second, rounded up, so a run that takes 0.5 seconds still uses 1 Yep. The platform shows consumed and available Yeps for the current cycle.

## When you hit a limit

YepCode stops new work on the free plan. Its FAQ says that on the Developer plan, when your team runs out of Yeps you cannot start new executions. You can upgrade, or wait until the next day for daily limits or the first day of the next month for monthly limits.

Yeps do not accumulate from month to month. Only paid plans keep running past the included Yeps, with the extra invoiced at a fixed price. Moving to a paid plan asks you for a credit card.

## Gotchas

- Each execution is rounded up to the next second, so many very short runs use Yeps faster than their real runtime suggests.
- A run is capped at 30 seconds on the free plan; the Starter plan raises the maximum to 1 hour.
- The free plan allows only 1 concurrent execution, so two processes cannot run at the same time.

## Questions people ask

### Is YepCode free?

Yes. The Developer plan is free, needs no credit card, and includes 50K Yeps of execution time.

### What is a Yep?

A Yep is one second of execution time for a process, rounded up to the next second.

### What happens when I use all my free Yeps?

You cannot start new executions until the limit resets, the next day for daily limits or the next month for monthly limits, unless you upgrade.
