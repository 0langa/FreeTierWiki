You work in the Assertible web dashboard, and you can send deployment events from CI with a small HTTP call. It also integrates with GitHub pull requests as a status check.

## What the free plan gives you

The Personal plan is $0 per month and the page says you can use it forever, as an individual or an organization. It includes 2 web services, 10 tests per service, and result retention of 1,000 results. The member count is listed as 1-2.

Schedules on this plan are on the Standard frequency, which the plans table labels as 2 monitors. Assertible does not limit the number of requests you make. It limits how many results you can see, how many tests you can create, and how often you can run schedules.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page does not describe a hard stop or a bill at the free limits. It says that if you need to test more web services or add more frequent health checks, you should look at the paid plans.

The Standard plan costs $25 per month. It has 25 web services, 500 tests per service, and 5,000 results, and it comes with a 1 month free trial. Each account pays one monthly charge, with no per-user fees. Paid plans accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover cards.

## Gotchas

- The free plan keeps 1,000 results, and the page counts results rather than requests.
- Each plan has a limited number of members, and the free plan has only 1-2.
- The free schedule frequency is the Standard one with 2 monitors, so frequent health checks need a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Assertible free?

Yes. The Personal plan is $0 per month, forever. It includes 2 web services, 10 tests per service, and 1,000 results retained.

### Does Assertible limit the number of requests?

No. The plans page says Assertible limits the results you can see, the tests you can create, and how often you run schedules. It does not cap requests.

### Do I need a credit card for Assertible?

The plans page lists no card for the free plan. Paid plans come with a 1 month free trial.
