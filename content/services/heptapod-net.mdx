You use foss.heptapod.net in the browser, like GitLab, and push over Mercurial or Git. It suits open source maintainers who want a free forge, with Mercurial supported.

## What the free plan gives you

Heptapod FOSS is a public software forge that is free of charge for Free and Open Source Software. The pricing page says you can host your public Git or Mercurial projects there for free. It is community managed.

Heptapod adds Mercurial support to GitLab. In Git mode, it gives you the feature set of GitLab Core, and the product page lists merge requests, an issue tracker, milestones, boards, and GitLab Pages. Clever Cloud and Octobus provide the service. The pages show no storage or user caps for the free service.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say that foss.heptapod.net charges you or sets usage caps. The price rows on the pricing page belong to other products: Heptapod Cloud at 7 euros per month per active user, and runners from 1.85 euros per 1000 minutes. Those are separate paid services.

Access is the real gate. You need a hosting request to be handled before you can host there. The project page is the place to submit it and to report bugs specific to this instance.

## Gotchas

- Hosting is by request. You submit a hosting request issue on the instance tracking project and wait for it to be handled.
- The service is for FOSS projects. Private or commercial work belongs on the paid Heptapod Cloud.
- Support for foss.heptapod.net comes from Clever Cloud, and the pages list no support contract for the free service.

## Questions people ask

### Is Heptapod free?

The self-hosted software is free and open source. The public foss.heptapod.net service is free of charge for Free and Open Source Software projects.

### Does Heptapod support Git as well as Mercurial?

Yes. The site says Heptapod hosts Git and Mercurial projects, and the free service hosts both.

### Does Heptapod FOSS need a credit card?

No payment is described for the free FOSS service. You submit a hosting request instead, and the Heptapod team handles it.
