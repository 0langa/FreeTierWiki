You use Exa through its REST API, the Python and JavaScript SDKs, or an MCP server that plugs into agents such as Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, and Cursor. It suits developers who build AI agents and research tools and want search results as clean text.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free tier gives you $10 in credits on the day you sign up. Your free balance resets to $10 on the first of every month, with no payment method needed. Your first team also earns a one-time $10 onboarding bonus when you finish onboarding in the dashboard, and that bonus does not expire. The docs say $10 covers up to 2,500 Instant searches.

Free credits spend at the normal pay-as-you-go rates. Search starts at $4 per 1k requests. Contents costs $1 per 1k pages. The Free tier has a rate limit of 10 queries per second and allows 50 active Agent runs.

## When you hit a limit

When your team uses up its credits, requests return a 402 Payment Required error. An API key that reaches its own budget also returns 402. You can add credits in the billing dashboard, or wait for the free balance to reset on the first of the next month. Going over the rate limit is a separate limit, and the docs say limits are subject to change. Pay-as-you-go has no subscription and no minimum spend, so you only pay for credits you load.

## Gotchas

- The monthly grant does not stack. Unused free credits do not carry into the next month, so the balance never holds more than $10 from the grant.
- Only a user's first team gets the monthly grant.
- Extras cost more. Each search result above 10 adds $1 per 1k results, and AI page summaries add $1 per 1k pages.

## Questions people ask

### Is Exa free?

Yes, within limits. The Free tier gives you $10 in credits every month, plus a one-time $10 onboarding bonus. You do not need a payment method.

### Does Exa need a credit card?

No. The Free tier needs no payment method. You add credits later only if you want to go past the free balance.

### What happens when I run out of Exa credits?

Requests return a 402 Payment Required error until you add credits. The free balance resets to $10 on the first of the next month.
