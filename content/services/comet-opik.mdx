You use Opik through the Python or TypeScript SDK and a web dashboard, either on Comet's hosted cloud or on a server you run yourself. It suits developers who already call LLM APIs and want to see every step their app or agent takes.

## What the free plan gives you

There are two free routes. The open-source version has no span or retention limits on the pricing page, and you run it yourself. You download the code from GitHub and follow the README.

The hosted Free Cloud plan costs $0. It allows up to 10 team members, 25k spans per month, and 60 days of data retention. A span is one tracked step, such as a model call or a tool call. The home page says you do not need a credit card to sign up. Free Cloud also includes the Agent Playground and a trial of the Ollie coding harness.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what Free Cloud does when you pass 25k spans in a month. It only shows what the paid Pro Cloud plan does. Pro costs $19 per month, includes 100k spans, and sells extra spans at $5 per 100k. Longer retention is also a paid add-on: it extends storage from 60 days to 400 days at $29 per 100k spans. If you need unlimited spans and retention without paying, self-host the open-source version.

## Gotchas

- The 60-day retention on Free Cloud means old traces disappear. Pro sells longer retention as an add-on.
- The page does not say what happens to Free Cloud traces after 25k spans, so watch your monthly count.
- Self-hosting means you download the code and run the service yourself.

## Questions people ask

### Is Opik free?

Yes. The open-source version is free to self-host, and the hosted Free Cloud plan costs $0 with 25k spans per month.

### Does Comet Opik need a credit card?

No. The Opik page says you do not need a credit card to sign up, and the free tier lasts as long as you like.

### What happens when I go over the Opik free span limit?

The pricing page does not say. On the paid Pro plan, extra spans cost $5 per 100k. To avoid limits, self-host the open-source version.
