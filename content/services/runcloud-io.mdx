You use the RunCloud web dashboard to connect servers and install web apps. It suits freelancers and agencies who rent their own cloud servers and want a graphical panel in place of the command line.

## What the free plan gives you

RunCloud has no permanent free plan. When you sign up, you get a 7-day free trial of the package you choose. You must add your card details to start the trial.

The home page lists the providers you can build on: Vultr, Linode, UpCloud, Hetzner, AWS, Google Cloud, and DigitalOcean. RunCloud does not provide the server. You rent it from one of those providers and RunCloud manages it. The docs say you can connect a provider through an API token so RunCloud creates the server for you, or connect a server you already have. The pricing page needs JavaScript, so this entry does not list plan prices.

## When you hit a limit

The trial ends after 7 days. If you do not cancel before it ends, RunCloud charges your card on the 8th day. You can cancel any time before the end of the trial without being charged.

If you need more time, you can request a 3-day trial extension. After a charge, you have a 14-day window to ask for a refund. If a later payment fails, RunCloud retries for 10 days, then locks the account for 90 days, and then deletes it.

## Gotchas

- A card is needed to start the trial, and the card is charged on day 8 unless you cancel.
- RunCloud does not include the server. Your cloud provider bills you separately for it.
- If a payment fails and the account is locked, the web server on your connected servers is disabled, automated backups pause, and SSL certificates are revoked.

## Questions people ask

### Does RunCloud have a free plan?

No. RunCloud offers a 7-day free trial of a paid package. After the trial it charges your card unless you cancel first.

### Does RunCloud need a credit card for the free trial?

Yes. The docs say you must add your card details when you sign up. RunCloud charges you on the 8th day unless you cancel before the trial ends.

### Can I get a refund after the RunCloud trial?

The docs say you have a 14-day window after a charge to request a refund if you are not satisfied. You can also ask for a 3-day extension of the trial.
