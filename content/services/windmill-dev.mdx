You write scripts in TypeScript, Python, Go, Bash, SQL, and other languages in a web editor or locally with the CLI. You chain them into flows and optionally build apps on top. It suits small teams who want an open source automation and internal tool platform and can run Postgres and a few containers.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is the open source Community Edition, which you host yourself. The pricing page lists unlimited executions per month, 3 workspaces, up to 50 users, and 10 GiB of workspace object storage. Groups, folders, and permissions are capped at 4 groups. Scripts, flows, apps, variables, and resources are unlimited.

SSO is limited to a maximum of 10 users. The plan also includes 100 free app guests per 30 days. They sign in through your identity provider and take no seat. Support is by community on Discord. Concurrency limits, content search, and script-specific workers are not part of the free edition.

## When you hit a limit

Because you run the software, nothing bills you and executions are not metered. Your own server decides how much work it can do, so you must plan workers and a Postgres database that fit your load. The pricing page shows no usage-based charge on the free edition.

The caps that do apply are structural. You can have 3 workspaces and 50 users. Past that, or for SSO beyond 10 users, an SLA, or audit logs, you need the Enterprise plan, which starts at $120 per month on the pricing page.

## Gotchas

- You host Windmill yourself, so server, database, upgrades, and backups are your job. The docs recommend Docker Compose for small setups and a Helm chart on Kubernetes for larger ones.
- Windmill needs a Postgres database that holds the whole job queue and state, and one or more worker containers.
- The docs mention a hosted Cloud app with no credit card to start, but the free limits listed here are for the self-hosted edition.

## Questions people ask

### Is Windmill free?

Yes, the Community Edition is free and open source. It has unlimited executions, 3 workspaces, and up to 50 users, and you host it yourself.

### Do I need a credit card for Windmill?

Not for the self-hosted Community Edition. The docs also say you can start on the Windmill Cloud app with no credit card.

### What are the Windmill free plan limits?

The free edition caps you at 3 workspaces, 50 users, 4 groups, and 10 GiB of workspace object storage. SSO is limited to 10 users.
