You manage Azure Cosmos DB in the Azure portal and reach it from SDKs, the Azure CLI, or PowerShell. It suits teams building on Azure who want one account to grow with them.

## What the free plan gives you

The lifetime free tier gives you the first 1000 RU/s of throughput and 25 GB of storage in your account, for as long as the account exists. It works with the NoSQL, MongoDB, Cassandra, Gremlin, and Table APIs on accounts with provisioned or autoscale throughput. You get one free tier account per Azure subscription.

A shared throughput database can use up to 1000 RU/s for free, with a maximum of 25 containers. If you also use the Azure free account, you add 400 RU/s and 25 GB for the first 12 months. That combined amount shrinks back to 1000 RU/s and 25 GB after those months.

## When you hit a limit

Cosmos DB does not stop at the free limit. Throughput and storage above the free amount are billed at regular price. The account keeps all regular features, including high availability and global distribution, so extra regions or RU/s add cost.

To stay free, keep total provisioned throughput at or under 1000 RU/s and storage under 25 GB. The discount is applied at the account level, so each extra region is billed for its own throughput and storage beyond one free allowance. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- You must opt in when you create the account. You cannot turn free tier on afterward.
- Free tier is not available for serverless accounts.
- If the free tier option is missing or disabled, another account in the subscription already uses it. If you delete a free tier account, you can apply free tier to a new one.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Cosmos DB free?

Yes, within limits. The lifetime free tier covers the first 1000 RU/s and 25 GB of storage in one account per subscription. Anything above that is billed.

### What happens if I go over the Cosmos DB free tier?

You are billed at the regular price for the extra throughput and storage. The account keeps running.

### How do I turn on the Cosmos DB free tier?

Set Apply Free Tier Discount to Apply when you create the account in the Azure portal, or pass `--enable-free-tier true` in the Azure CLI. You cannot add it to an existing account.
