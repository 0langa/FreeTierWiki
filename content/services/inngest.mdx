You add the Inngest SDK to your app, write functions with steps, and serve them from an endpoint such as /api/inngest. Inngest then calls your code on events, schedules, or retries. It suits developers on TypeScript stacks like Next.js who need background jobs on their own hosting.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It includes 50,000 executions per month, 500,000 events ingested per month, 5 seats, 5 concurrent steps, and 3 workers. Serverless endpoints are unlimited, and the queue depth is 100,000.

An execution is one function run or one step. A run with five steps uses six executions. Free also gives you 24 hours of trace and log history, 500 MB of span data, and 10,000 scores per month. A function run can last up to 30 days in total, including sleeps. Inngest calls your code on your host, so your hosting limits also apply.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that on Free there is no metered overage. Execution pauses once you hit the free quota, and you must upgrade to resume. That makes the free plan a hard cap, so it cannot send you a surprise bill.

On the Pro plan, which starts at $99 per month, extra executions are metered at tiered rates. Other limits such as concurrency, seats, workers, span data, and events have their own separate limits.

## Gotchas

- Count steps, not just runs. Inngest says a multi-step AI agent with tool calls, retries, or sub-agent handoffs often uses 10 or more executions per run.
- Your hosting provider can impose separate request and runtime limits on the code you serve.
- Free allows 5 concurrent steps. Concurrency is its own limit, separate from executions, so check it before you run many steps in parallel.

## Questions people ask

### Is Inngest free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 50,000 executions and 500,000 events per month for $0, and no credit card is required.

### What happens when I go over the Inngest free limit?

Execution pauses once you hit the free quota. You need to upgrade to resume, and Free has no metered overage.

### Does Inngest run my code?

Inngest calls functions that run on your own host, through an endpoint such as /api/inngest. You pay your hosting provider for that.
