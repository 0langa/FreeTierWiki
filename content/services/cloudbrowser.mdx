Cloudbrowser runs Chrome remotely and gives you a live view or a Chrome DevTools Protocol connection. A tool such as Playwright can attach to the browser to run a task. The profile can keep cookies and other saved state between runs so you can return to a site without setting everything up again.

## What the free plan gives you

Free covers one browser-hour per UTC calendar month and allows one running browser at a time. It includes a persistent Chrome profile, CDP access, and the live viewer. You can create a browser after verifying your email and use the console to issue connection links for your automation tool or live view.

Running time counts while Chrome is active, even if you close the viewer. Pausing stops the runtime meter and retains the profile. An ordinary resume starts Chrome again, so do not count on an unsaved form or other in-memory state surviving.

## When you hit a limit

The service automatically pauses running browsers once the free hour is gone. You can wait until the next UTC calendar month or choose a paid plan. The Free plan has no overage charge. Under a paid plan, extra running time can be billed after the included hours. Look at Usage in the console to see how much free time a task used.

## Gotchas

- Closing a client connection alone does not stop the runtime meter. Check the console or explicitly pause the browser when the job is done.
- A saved browser profile does not guarantee a website will keep you signed in. The site can expire its session.
- The plan covers Chrome runtime. Your automation or agent software handles its own model usage.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudbrowser free without a card?

Yes. Free includes one hour per UTC month without a payment card.

### What happens when the free browser-hour runs out?

The running browser pauses automatically. Wait for the monthly reset or upgrade to resume.

### Does closing the browser viewer preserve my free minutes?

Closing the viewer does not stop a running browser. Pause it or check that the service has paused it before leaving the session.
