You create and manage services in the Aiven Console, and you can also use the CLI, API, or Terraform provider. It suits learners and small projects that want a real managed database.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and has no time limit. You get a free service for PostgreSQL, Kafka, OpenSearch, or Valkey. You can run one free service per type on each account. The MySQL Free tier is retired, so new MySQL services start on the Developer plan at $5 a month.

The PostgreSQL Free plan has 1 CPU, 1 GB of RAM, and 1 GB of total storage. Kafka allows up to 250 kb/s of data in and out, with retention up to 3 days. OpenSearch gets 20 GB of storage, 4 GB of RAM, and up to 20 shards. For PostgreSQL Free, you cannot pick the cloud or region, and the pricing table lists a single backup for disaster recovery. The 30-day trial is separate and uses credits for paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

Free services run on a fixed setup, so you cannot scale them in place. Throughput and connections are capped. To get more, you add a payment method to the billing group and upgrade to a paid plan. The upgrade keeps your data and connection details.

The bigger risk is idle services. Aiven can power off a free service that shows no initial usage in its first few hours, or one whose activity stops, after a notice. You can power it back on at any time. Aiven can also shut services down if they break its acceptable use policy.

## Gotchas

- Free services are single node and have no high availability, so treat them as learning and prototype tools.
- PostgreSQL Free does not let you choose the cloud or region, and has no integrations or connection pooling; check the product-specific plan before assuming those restrictions apply to another engine.
- Deleting a free service removes its data, though you can create a new free service later.

## Questions people ask

### Is Aiven free?

Yes. Aiven has always-free plans for PostgreSQL, Kafka, OpenSearch, and Valkey, with no trial period and no expiry date. The MySQL Free tier is retired.

### Does the Aiven free tier need a credit card?

No. You can sign up and create a free service with no payment details. You are not charged automatically.

### Will Aiven shut down my free database?

It can. Aiven reserves the right to power off free services with no initial usage in their first few hours, and services with no continued activity after a notification. You can power the service back on at any time.
