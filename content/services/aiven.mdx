You create and manage services in the Aiven Console, and you can also use the CLI, API, or Terraform provider. It suits learners and small projects that want a real managed database.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and has no time limit. You get a free service for PostgreSQL, MySQL, Kafka, OpenSearch, or Valkey. You can run one free service per type on each account.

The PostgreSQL Free plan has 1 CPU, 1 GB of RAM, and 1 GB of total storage. MySQL also gets 1 GB of storage and 1 GB of RAM. Kafka allows up to 250 kb/s of data in and out, with retention up to 3 days. OpenSearch gets 20 GB of storage, 4 GB of RAM, and up to 20 shards. You cannot pick the cloud or region. Backups are limited to a single copy for disaster recovery. The 30-day trial is separate and uses credits for paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

Free services run on a fixed setup, so you cannot scale them in place. Throughput and connections are capped. To get more, you add a payment method to the billing group and upgrade to a paid plan. The upgrade keeps your data and connection details.

The bigger risk is idle services. Aiven can power off a free service that shows no activity, and it sends a notice first. You can power it back on at any time. Aiven can also shut services down if they break its acceptable use policy.

## Gotchas

- Free services are single node and have no high availability, so treat them as learning and prototype tools.
- You cannot choose the cloud or region, and the Free plan has no integrations or connection pooling.
- Deleting a free service removes its data, though you can create a new free service later.

## Questions people ask

### Is Aiven free?

Yes. Aiven has always-free plans for PostgreSQL, MySQL, Kafka, OpenSearch, and Valkey, with no trial period and no expiry date.

### Does the Aiven free tier need a credit card?

No. You can sign up and create a free service with no payment details. You are not charged automatically.

### Will Aiven shut down my free database?

It can. Aiven reserves the right to power off free services that see no activity, and it sends a notification first. You can power the service back on at any time.
