Aiven offers always-free plans for PostgreSQL, Kafka, MySQL, OpenSearch, and Valkey with no credit card required. The PostgreSQL plan has 1 CPU, 1 GB RAM, and 1 GB storage. A separate 30-day trial with credits is also available for larger plans, but is not required for ongoing free use.
