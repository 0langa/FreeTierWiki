You manage the page in the PingPong web app, and there is an API for components, incidents, and maintenance. It suits small teams that want a clean status page without running their own.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan gives you a public status page on your own subdomain, with SSL included. You can create unlimited components, component groups, and templates. The pricing page blurb also says unlimited incidents, but the comparison table tooltips list 20 incidents and 5 scheduled maintenances per month for Free. Treat the lower numbers as the safe plan.

The free plan has no monitoring metrics and no team members. Metrics, the 60-second checks, and a custom domain start on the paid Solo plan at $29 per month. Solo includes 5 metrics and 100 subscribers. A 14-day trial of paid plans needs no credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the monthly incident or maintenance count on Free. It publishes no Free overage tariff or enforcement details; no-card signup alone does not establish quota behavior.

The comparison assigns additional features to paid plans or their trial, without documenting the exact Free upgrade prompt. If you need uptime metrics or subscribers, plan on moving to Solo. Team members start at Pro, which adds 20 metrics and 10 team members, and Business adds 40 metrics and 25 team members.

## Gotchas

- The page text and the table disagree on incident limits, so check the dashboard before you rely on unlimited incidents.
- Free has no monitoring, so the page changes only when you post an incident yourself.
- Free has no custom domain and no team members. A custom domain starts on the Solo plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is PingPong free?

Yes, there is a free plan. It gives you a public status page on a subdomain with SSL, with no credit card. Monitoring, subscribers, and team members need a paid plan.

### Does PingPong include uptime monitoring for free?

No. The comparison table shows no metrics on the free plan. Solo includes 5 metrics and starts at $29 per month.

### Can I use my own domain on the PingPong free plan?

No. The table lists a custom domain from the Solo plan up. Free pages use a subdomain.
