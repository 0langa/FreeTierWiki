You install an SDK, create a client with your credentials, and call one `send` function for email or SMS. It suits developers who want to ship notifications without running separate providers for each channel.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing selector describes a shared monthly budget equivalent to 100 US SMS or 3,000 emails. Its FAQ and reference instead say both allowances are included, and the homepage says 3,000 emails plus 100 SMS and Calls. Do not assume both maximums combine until confirmed. No credit card is required.

Free lists unlimited sending domains and webhooks, 30-day logs, no Pingram branding, and US/EU/CA regions. The reference says one local number is included, while the feature table leaves Free phone numbers blank. Pricing marks Voice and WhatsApp as coming, although the homepage and technical reference already describe Voice; a separate 100-minute quota is not established.

## When you hit a limit

The balance refills each billing cycle, and unused credit does not roll over. SMS messages deduct the rate for their destination country, so a message to a costly country uses more of the budget than a US message.

The pricing page does not say whether Free stops, queues, or otherwise handles sends after the budget runs out. It says to upgrade for a larger balance, so a hard spending stop is unconfirmed. The first paid budget costs $20 per month. On a paid plan you can turn on Auto upgrade, which moves you up when spend reaches 90% of the budget.

## Gotchas

- Managed A2P 10DLC registration is testing-only on the Free plan, so real US SMS traffic at scale needs a paid plan.
- There is no dedicated IP on Free. It comes with paid plans.
- Number pricing lists $0.50 monthly for US/CA local numbers and says one is free, but the Free feature table omits numbers. Confirm eligibility before relying on one.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pingram free?

Yes, with no credit card. The selector says 100 US SMS or 3,000 emails from one budget, while the FAQ says both. The combined allowance is not consistently documented.

### Can I send SMS on the Pingram free plan?

It advertises up to 100 US SMS monthly, with managed A2P 10DLC limited to testing. Free number inclusion differs between its table and reference; confirm account eligibility.

### Does unused Pingram budget roll over?

No. Your balance renews to your tier amount each billing cycle, so use it within the month.
