You install an SDK, create a client with your credentials, and call one `send` function for email or SMS. It suits developers who want to ship notifications without running separate providers for each channel.

## What the free plan gives you

Pingram uses one monthly budget for email and SMS. The Free plan includes 100 SMS or 3,000 emails each month, and you can mix them from the same pool. It also includes 100 call minutes, one local phone number, and unlimited sending domains.

The platform table shows 30 days of log retention, unlimited webhooks, and no Pingram branding on the Free plan. It supports the US, EU, and CA regions. Voice and WhatsApp are not launched yet and will draw from the same budget.

## When you hit a limit

The balance refills each billing cycle, and unused credit does not roll over. SMS messages deduct the rate for their destination country, so a message to a costly country uses more of the budget than a US message.

The pricing page does not say what happens when the free budget runs out, and it says to upgrade for a larger balance. The first paid budget costs $20 per month. On a paid plan you can turn on Auto upgrade, which moves you up when spend reaches 90% of the budget.

## Gotchas

- Managed A2P 10DLC registration is testing-only on the Free plan, so real US SMS traffic at scale needs a paid plan.
- There is no dedicated IP on Free. It comes with paid plans.
- Phone numbers beyond the first one cost $0.50 per month each.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pingram free?

Yes. Every account includes 100 SMS and 3,000 emails per month at no cost. You do not need a credit card.

### Can I send SMS on the Pingram free plan?

Yes, up to 100 US SMS a month from the shared budget, with one local phone number included. Managed A2P 10DLC is testing-only on Free.

### Does unused Pingram budget roll over?

No. Your balance renews to your tier amount each billing cycle, so use it within the month.
