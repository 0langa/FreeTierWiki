You work in a web dashboard, a CLI, or an API. You group services, jobs, and add-ons in projects, and Northflank builds each service from a Dockerfile or a buildpack. It suits developers who already think in containers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Sandbox plan, which the docs call Developer Sandbox, is free. The pricing page lists 2 free services, 2 free cron jobs, and 1 free database within up to $20 a month of resources on Northflank Cloud, and says compute is always on with no sleeping. The pricing comparison lists 2 projects, while the setup guide says one; confirm that discrepancy before designing your setup. The plan also allows 1 bring-your-own-cloud cluster, which the pricing page puts at up to 12 vCPU and 48 GB.

Teams are included at no extra cost, and the plan includes free TLS and custom domains, plus logs and metrics kept for a month. Vertical scaling is limited on the free plan, and the docs describe it as a good way to explore the platform and spin up hobby projects.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach the sandbox limits. The docs say you can upgrade at any time and keep your existing resources, and the pay-as-you-go plan starts without restrictions.

Northflank says billing alerts and thresholds help you avoid surprise bills. If you bring your own cloud, your cloud provider still charges you for the infrastructure, so that cost is outside the free plan.

## Gotchas

- The card requirement is unclear. The pricing page says the Sandbox needs no card, but the billing docs say every user must add a payment method before creating resources, to verify identity and stop abuse.
- The docs say the free tier should not be used for production applications.
- Project allowance conflicts between the pricing comparison (2) and setup guide (1); check the current console before relying on a second project.

## Questions people ask

### Is Northflank free?

Yes, within the Sandbox plan. It allows 2 services, 2 jobs, and 1 database add-on, within up to $20 a month of resources, and Northflank says it is not for production.

### Does Northflank need a credit card?

It is not clear. The pricing page says no card is needed for the Sandbox, but the billing docs say all users must add a payment method before creating resources, whatever plan they choose, to verify identity and prevent abuse. Check the sign-up flow before you rely on either.

### Does the Northflank free tier sleep?

No. The pricing page says compute is always on and does not sleep.
