You work in the browser, with Chrome officially supported. The flow is connect data, create a dataset, create charts, build a dashboard, and share it. It suits small teams who want Superset without running it.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free forever for up to 5 users. The pricing page describes it as a way to explore Superset in the cloud for small teams. It includes unlimited dashboards and charts, and more than 40 visualization types.

Preset builds its dashboards on datasets. The company says a dataset-centric approach lets anyone create dashboards fast, because datasets contain only the relevant columns. You can also move your charts and dashboards to open-source Apache Superset later, so there is no lock-in.

Professional costs $20 per user per month billed annually, or $25 per user per month billed monthly. It adds unlimited users, 3 workspaces, and standard customer support. A 14-day trial is available.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page gives the 5 user limit but does not say what happens if you invite a sixth person. Plan to move to Professional if your team grows. You pay nothing on Starter, so there is no bill.

Embedded dashboards cost extra and are not on Starter. They are an add-on for Professional and Enterprise. Viewer licenses start at $500 per month for 50 viewers. During the 14-day Professional trial, admins can embed dashboards in any app without buying licenses.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is capped at 5 users, while Professional has unlimited users.
- Embedded dashboards need Professional or Enterprise plus viewer licenses.
- Preset supports Chrome officially. Other browsers may run it, but Safari and Firefox are not comprehensively tested, so some features may not work.

## Questions people ask

### Is Preset Cloud free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for up to 5 users, with unlimited dashboards and charts.

### How much does Preset Cloud cost after the free plan?

Professional costs $20 per user per month billed annually, or $25 per user per month billed monthly. A 14-day trial is available.

### Can I embed Preset dashboards on the free plan?

No. Embedded dashboards are an add-on for Professional and Enterprise plans. Viewer licenses start at $500 per month for 50 viewers.
