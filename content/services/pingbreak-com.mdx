Pingbreak.com provides free, simple uptime monitoring for websites and APIs, sending instant alerts via Twitter DM, Slack, Discord, Mattermost, email, or webhook if your service goes down. It is an open beta, ideal for individuals and small projects needing basic, no-cost monitoring.
