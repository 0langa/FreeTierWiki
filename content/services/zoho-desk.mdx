You work in the Zoho Desk web app, where an admin sets up departments, the email channel, and agent roles first. Agents then handle tickets from a shared queue.

## What the free plan gives you

Zoho describes the free plan as a simple email ticketing system that is ready to use right away. It gives you 3 user licenses, so you can start small and grow your support team.

The three users have limited features. The pricing page does not list the free plan feature by feature. Its FAQ says light users and billing are not available, and that automation, support for multiple departments, SLAs, AI features, and custom roles and profiles need a paid plan.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan stops at its limits instead of billing you. A fourth agent needs a paid plan, and the Standard plan raises the user limit to 5.

When you move to a paid plan, the 3 free users are no longer included. Zoho offers a 15-day trial of any plan with no credit card. When it ends, your account drops to the free plan automatically unless you pick a paid plan.

## Gotchas

- The admin guide lists SLAs, workflow rules, round robin, and multiple departments among its setup steps. The pricing FAQ says the free edition does not offer SLAs or multiple departments, so skip those steps.
- The admin guide says to define roles and profiles before you onboard agents. Custom roles and profiles are listed as not in the free edition.
- Email is the first channel to set up. The admin guide says community, chat, web form, and social media channels come later, and the pricing page does not say if they are free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Desk free?

Yes. The free plan gives 3 users a simple email ticketing system with limited features. It has no time limit.

### Does Zoho Desk need a credit card?

No. The 15-day trial needs no credit card, and the free plan does not either.

### What happens when the Zoho Desk trial ends?

Your account is downgraded to the free plan automatically. To keep the paid features, you pick a monthly or yearly plan.
