Packagecloud hosts software packages so users can install them with apt, yum, npm, pip, Maven, gem, or Helm. Its documentation remains available for people maintaining an existing repository. The Free signup link in its pricing FAQ and the registration link on its plan cards now open a notice saying Packagecloud is part of Buildkite Package Registries.

## What the free plan gives you

The original Packagecloud Free plan is closed to new signups. The old pricing page still lists 10 GB of bandwidth and 2 GB of storage, but its signup links lead to Buildkite. If you already use Packagecloud, check the plan listed in your account to determine which terms apply to your repositories.

Buildkite publishes its own $0 Free plan. Its pricing comparison lists private registries and a combined allowance of 1 GB indexed plus distributed per month. Signup needs no credit card and starts with a 30-day all-access trial. Check which features are in Buildkite’s Free column before using a feature you first see during the trial.

## When you hit a limit

The original Packagecloud pricing FAQ explains storage and bandwidth measurement for customers on its plans. Downloads count toward bandwidth, and storage is measured at its high point during the billing period. For example, removing an older package after a large upload can reduce future storage use while leaving the current billing period’s high point unchanged.

The legacy FAQ describes billing alerts and paid overage terms. It does not establish the overage policy for a legacy Free subscriber. Buildkite indexes and distributes packages against its own registry allocation. Check your actual dashboard and pricing terms before moving an active repository or changing a subscription.

## Gotchas

- Packagecloud’s two public registration links now advertise Buildkite, although the old pricing page still displays a Free card.
- Buildkite’s private package registry usage is separate from the CI artifact allowances shown in its pricing comparison.
- The signup notice does not explain which legacy Packagecloud entitlements remain active for existing customers.

## Questions people ask

### Is Packagecloud still free for new users?

Packagecloud’s old Free signup now goes to Buildkite Package Registries. Buildkite offers its own Free plan with no card for signup.

### Can I host private packages on Buildkite’s Free plan?

Yes. Buildkite’s published Free comparison includes private registries and 1 GB indexed plus distributed per month.

### What happens to my existing Packagecloud repository?

The public signup notice does not state how it affects existing accounts. Check your Packagecloud plan and billing page for your active repository’s terms.
