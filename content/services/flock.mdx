You use Flock in the browser or in apps for Windows, Mac, Linux, and Chrome, plus mobile apps. It looks like a standard team chat tool, with channels, direct messages, and productivity add-ons.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is for teams with 1 to 20 members. It includes unlimited one-to-one and group messages. Search covers 10,000 messages, you can have 10 public channels, and the team shares 5 GB of storage. Other limits are one admin, one approved domain, one smart channel, one announcement channel, 3 process automations with 5 runs per template, and unlimited third-party apps and integrations. Polls, reminders, and notes are listed as unlimited.

Guests are free for 90 days from the date of invitation. After that, Flock charges for a guest at the same rate as other team members.

## When you hit a limit

Search is the clearest cap. Flock searches 10,000 messages on the free plan, and the pricing page does not say what happens to older ones. The Pro plan, for teams of 20 to 100 members, offers unlimited searchable messages, unlimited channels, and multiple admins. It comes with a 30 day free trial and no credit card. The free plan itself has no card step.

## Gotchas

- Free guests become billable after 90 days, at the same rate as team members.
- Message search is limited to 10,000 messages, even though the number of messages you can send is unlimited.
- Private channels are not listed for the free plan; the Pro plan lists unlimited private and public channels.

## Questions people ask

### Is Flock free?

Yes. The free plan covers teams of 1 to 20 members with unlimited messages, 10,000 searchable messages, and 5 GB of team storage.

### Does Flock need a credit card?

Not for the free plan, and the 30 day Pro trial also says no credit card is required.

### What happens to guests on Flock?

Guests are free for 90 days from the date of invitation. After 90 days, Flock bills them at the same rate as other team members.
