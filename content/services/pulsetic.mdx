You work in the Pulsetic dashboard, and there is also an API and webhooks. The setup guide covers monitors, heartbeat monitors for cron jobs, status pages, and badges. It suits one person or a small team with a handful of sites.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and the sign-up flow asks only for an email and password. The comparison table lists 10 monitors, heartbeats, or domains, with a 5-minute check interval and checks from 3 regions. You get 3 status pages, 1 dependency monitor, and 1 webhook per monitor.

History is short: uptime logs are kept for 3 months and error logs for 30 days. The free plan also includes 1,000 real user monitoring page views a month. Alerts go out by email and webhook. The pricing FAQ says the free version has limited functionality, and in many cases is enough to monitor a website.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass a free limit. Paid plans let you buy more capacity as add-ons, such as $0.20 per extra monitor, but the page says that applies on any paid plan, not on Free. Free stays at its fixed limits unless you upgrade.

The Solo plan costs $9 per month and has 60-second checks, 5 regions, and unlimited status pages.

## Gotchas

- The free table shows no status page incident updates and no scheduled maintenance, so the free status pages mostly show monitor status.
- Calls, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat tools like Slack start on the $9 Solo plan. Free has email and webhooks only.
- No teammates or status badges on Free. The help center also says Free accounts must log in at least once every 90 days to stay active.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pulsetic free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 per month and covers 10 monitors with 5-minute checks and 3 status pages. Email alerts and webhooks come with it.

### Does Pulsetic need a credit card?

The help center sign-up steps ask for an email and password, or Google sign-in, plus email verification. No step asks for a card.

### Does Pulsetic send SMS alerts on the free plan?

No. SMS, call, and WhatsApp alerts are not included on Free. Calls and SMS start on the Solo plan.
