You create an instance in the ZITADEL Customer Portal and manage projects and applications in its Management Console. Your apps connect over standard OIDC or SAML. It suits developers who want open-source roots with a hosted option.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs US$ 0 per month and lets you try out all features. It includes all security features and unlimited stored users. You also get unlimited organizations and custom workflows. The pricing table also lists service users and agents. The 3 included identity providers are listed with the Pro plan.

The limit is 100 daily active users. Data residency covers the EU, US, Switzerland, and Australia. An authentication event is a login or a token refresh, and each unique user counts once per day. The monthly total is the sum of every day's count. The Pro plan costs US$ 100 per month. It starts with a much larger daily active user allowance included per month, a custom domain, and a 99.5% uptime guarantee.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the Free plan passes 100 daily active users. The help article on how daily active users are counted does not cover the Free plan limit either. Ask ZITADEL before you plan a launch that could pass it.

The Pro plan is the step up. It includes a much larger daily active user allowance per month and a response SLO of 1h. Enterprise has unlimited daily active users, a custom support SLA, and a 99.99% uptime guarantee, with custom pricing. Stored users are unlimited on every plan.

## Gotchas

- The limit counts daily active users, not monthly ones. A busy day with many unique sign-ins can pass 100 even if you have few total users.
- The pricing page lists no uptime guarantee for the Free plan. Guarantees start with the Pro plan.
- Most developers use separate instances for development, test, and production, and each instance is a fully isolated environment.

## Questions people ask

### Is ZITADEL Cloud free?

Yes. The Free plan costs US$ 0 per month and includes all security features, unlimited stored users, unlimited organizations, and 100 daily active users.

### What counts as a ZITADEL daily active user?

A user who logs in or refreshes a token. Each unique user counts once per day, and the monthly total sums the daily counts.

### Does ZITADEL Cloud need a credit card?

The pricing page shows a Start for free button and does not mention a card.
