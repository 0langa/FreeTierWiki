You create an index in the Upstash Console and use it through a REST API or SDKs for Python, TypeScript, Go, and PHP. It suits developers who want a vector store for AI search without running any infrastructure.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is for prototypes and hobby projects. It includes 1 database with room for 200 million vectors times dimensions. Each vector can have up to 1,536 dimensions. Total data and metadata is capped at 1 GB.

You can use 100 namespaces and filter on metadata. Each vector can carry up to 48 KB of metadata and 1 MB of data. Free indexes can run in N. Virginia or Ireland on AWS, or Iowa on GCP. Support comes from the community.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan caps queries and updates at 10K per day. The pages do not say what happens when you reach it, but the limit is daily, so it resets the next day. To go past it you add a credit card, and the index becomes a pay as you go index with no daily cap.

Pay as you go costs $0.4 per 100K requests, plus $0.25 per GB of storage. The Fixed plan costs $60 a month and allows 1M queries and updates a day.

## Gotchas

- The 10K daily limit counts queries and updates together, and queries and upserts cost the same on paid plans.
- The default topk limit is 1000, so you cannot ask for more than that many matches per query.
- The plan table shows 1 database on Free, but the FAQ says up to 10 indexes are free. Check the console for what your account allows.

## Questions people ask

### Is Upstash Vector free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 1 database, 1 GB of data, and 10K queries and updates per day. Upgrading to pay as you go needs a credit card.

### What are the Upstash Vector free tier limits?

The index holds up to 200 million vectors times dimensions, with at most 1,536 dimensions per vector and 100 namespaces. Queries and updates are limited to 10K per day.

### How much does Upstash Vector cost after the free tier?

Pay as you go costs $0.4 per 100K requests and $0.25 per GB of storage. The Fixed plan costs $60 a month.
