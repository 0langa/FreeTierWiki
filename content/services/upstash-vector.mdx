You create an index in the Upstash Console and use it through a REST API or SDKs for Python, TypeScript, Go, and PHP. It suits developers who want a vector store for AI search without running any infrastructure.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is for prototypes and hobby projects. The comparison table labels it as one Free database. The published capacity is 200 million vectors times dimensions. Each vector can have up to 1,536 dimensions. Total data and metadata is capped at 1 GB.

You can use 100 namespaces and filter on metadata. Each vector can carry up to 48 KB of metadata and 1 MB of data. Free indexes can run in N. Virginia or Ireland on AWS, or Iowa on GCP. Support comes from the community.

## When you hit a limit

Free allows 10K daily queries/updates; exhaustion handling is unspecified, with allowance resetting next day. Adding a card converts the index to pay-as-you-go without a daily cap.

Pay-as-you-go costs $0.4/100K requests plus $0.25/GB storage. Fixed costs $60/month plus $0.25/GB, permitting 1M daily queries/updates. Paid storage bills monthly daily-average size even without traffic. Both paid options include 200 GB monthly bandwidth then list $0.03/GB, although FAQ warns of regional/provider variation. Include persistent storage and transfer when comparing paid costs, not only request volume.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page shows one 10K daily query/update limit, but the quickstart docs say 10,000 queries and 10,000 updates daily. Check the console to see how your account counts them. Queries and upserts cost the same on paid plans.
- The FAQ gives 1000 as the default limit for topk. New or updated vectors may not immediately appear in queries because indexes are eventually consistent.
- The FAQ says the first 10 indexes have no separate index fee. This is separate from the comparison table’s one-Free-database allowance.

## Questions people ask

### Is Upstash Vector free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and lists 1 GB of data and 10K queries and updates per day. Upgrading to pay as you go needs a credit card.

### What are the Upstash Vector free tier limits?

The index holds up to 200 million vectors times dimensions, with at most 1,536 dimensions per vector and 100 namespaces. Queries and updates are limited to 10K per day.

### How much does Upstash Vector cost after the free tier?

Pay as you go costs $0.4 per 100K requests and $0.25 per GB of storage. The Fixed plan costs $60 a month plus $0.25 per GB of storage.
