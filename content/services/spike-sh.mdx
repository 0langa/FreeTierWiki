Spike.sh is an on-call and alerting service. Monitoring tools send it alerts, and it creates incidents, escalates them, and notifies the person on call by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, or Microsoft Teams. It also has a status page. It suits small teams that want straightforward on-call without a large platform.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. The Standard and Business plans each have a Start 14 day trial button. Standard, for small teams setting up a first rotation, costs $7 per user per month billed monthly and includes 100 alerts a month shared across phone and SMS, unlimited WhatsApp and Telegram alerts, the Slack app and Microsoft Teams bot, unlimited integrations, 1 on-call schedule for 1 person, 1 alert rule, and a status page.

Business costs $14 per user per month and adds unlimited phone and SMS alerts, unlimited on-call schedules, multiple teams, and task management integrations such as JIRA, ClickUp, and Linear.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 14-day trial ends or when Standard's 100 phone and SMS alerts are used up in a month. Standard also limits incoming calls to the on-call member through live call routing to 5 a month, and API requests to the number of users times 1000 a month. Business lists these as unlimited.

## Gotchas

- Standard's on-call schedule is listed as 1 schedule for 1 person, so a real rotation needs Business.
- Phone calls and SMS share one pool of 100 a month on Standard.
- Playbooks are listed as available for a limited time, even on Business.

## Questions people ask

### Is Spike.sh free?

Not permanently. Paid plans have a 14-day trial, and Standard starts at $7 per user per month.

### How many phone and SMS alerts does Standard include?

100 a month, shared between phone calls and SMS. Business lists them as unlimited.

### Does Spike.sh include a status page?

Yes. A status page is included on Standard and Business, with private status pages on Business.
