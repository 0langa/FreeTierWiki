You build campaigns and automation workflows in the Sender dashboard, and developers can use a REST API and an SMTP option for transactional email. The Help Center offers guided paths for email marketing, transactional email, SMS, and lead capture.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers and up to 15,000 emails a month. The comparison table explicitly lists no daily email limit and one user seat. It includes the drag-and-drop builder, a free and responsive templates library, popups and forms, and transactional emails. The plan also comes with 24/7 support according to the home page.

The home page says the free plan includes automation and segmentation workflows. Every new account also gets a 14-day Professional trial, and after it ends you stay on the Free Forever plan.

## When you hit a limit

Free has three limits: 2,500 active subscribers, 2,500 unique subscribers emailed per billing cycle, and 15,000 emails per cycle. Campaign and workflow emails both count. Sending campaigns is blocked above the active-subscriber limit, even if the intended audience is smaller. Reduce active subscribers or upgrade to resume.

Exhausting the email or unique-recipient allowance blocks further sending until the billing cycle resets or you upgrade. Deleting contacts does not reduce unique recipients already emailed that cycle. Scheduled campaigns reserve both email credits and recipient capacity; canceling them releases those reservations.

Free forms, popups and newsletters carry Sender branding; paid plans remove it. The email allowance does not include a free SMS quota; SMS has separate pricing.

## Gotchas

- Free newsletters carry Sender branding. Removing it needs a paid plan or the credits system.
- The free plan uses a shared IP address. Dedicated IP eligibility starts on Professional plans with 20,000 subscribers or more; setup is on request.
- The 14-day Professional trial ends on its own. You stay on Free Forever, and the trial features switch off.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sender free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers and 15,000 emails a month. It never expires and needs no credit card.

### Does Sender put its logo on free emails?

Yes. Sender adds its branding to the bottom of newsletters, forms, and popups on the free plan. You remove it by moving to a paid plan.

### Can I send SMS with Sender for free?

The Free Forever email allowance does not include a free SMS quota. Check separate SMS pricing before sending text messages.
