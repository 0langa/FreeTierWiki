You build campaigns and automation workflows in the Sender dashboard, and developers can use a REST API and an SMTP option for transactional email. The Help Center offers guided paths for email marketing, transactional email, SMS, and lead capture.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan costs $0 and covers up to 2,500 subscribers and up to 15,000 emails a month. The pricing table shows no daily email limits. It includes the drag-and-drop builder, a free and responsive templates library, popups and forms, and transactional emails. The plan also comes with 24/7 support according to the home page.

The home page says the free plan includes automation and segmentation workflows. Every new account also gets a 14-day Professional trial, and after it ends you stay on the Free Forever plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 15,000 emails in a month or 2,500 subscribers. It lists larger paid plans and says you can upgrade at any time.

Sender adds its branding to the bottom of subscription forms, popups, and newsletters on the free plan. The branding goes away on a paid plan. SMS is not part of the free plan and is bought by credits or on a paid plan.

## Gotchas

- Free emails carry Sender branding. Removing it needs a paid plan or the credits system.
- The free plan uses a shared IP address. A dedicated IP is set up for Professional plan accounts, or for larger plans, on request.
- The 14-day Professional trial ends on its own. You stay on Free Forever, and the trial features switch off.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sender free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan covers up to 2,500 subscribers and 15,000 emails a month. It never expires and needs no credit card.

### Does Sender put its logo on free emails?

Yes. Sender adds its branding to the bottom of newsletters, forms, and popups on the free plan. You remove it by moving to a paid plan.

### Can I send SMS with Sender for free?

No. SMS messaging is a paid feature. The Standard plan includes it, and you can also buy SMS credits.
