You call the REST API or an SDK for your language, and you read logs and metrics in the dashboard. It suits developers who want a short path from sign-up to a first sent email.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and includes 3,000 emails a month, with a daily limit of 100. The docs say the quota counts both sent and received emails, and each To, CC, or BCC recipient counts as one email. The daily quota resets at midnight UTC.

Free accounts can verify up to 3 domains and send marketing emails to up to 1,000 contacts. The plan includes 1 webhook endpoint and 30 days of data retention. Open and click tracking is available on every plan, including Free.

## When you hit a limit

Free accounts have a daily and a monthly email quota. The pricing page does not describe the exact error you see after 100 emails in a day, but it notes the daily quota resets at midnight UTC, not on a rolling window.

Pay-as-you-go overages are an option on paid plans only. The Pro plan costs $20 per month for 50,000 emails. The API rate limit starts at 10 requests per second per team on every plan, and extra requests get a 429 response.

## Gotchas

- Received emails count toward your quota, so inbound mail uses up the same 100 a day.
- A bounce rate above 4% or a spam rate above 0.08% can lead to a temporary pause in sending.
- Marketing broadcasts do not count against the transactional quota, but they are limited by your contact count.

## Questions people ask

### Is Resend free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3,000 emails a month with a limit of 100 a day. The pricing page says there is no free trial, only a free-forever plan.

### How many emails can I send per day with Resend for free?

You can send 100 emails per day, counted on a UTC calendar day. Sent and received emails both count, and each recipient counts separately.

### Does Resend support custom domains on the free plan?

Yes. A free account can verify up to 3 domains. The Pro plan raises that to 10.
