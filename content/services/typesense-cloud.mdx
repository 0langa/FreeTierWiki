Typesense Cloud is the hosted version of Typesense, an open source search engine with typo tolerance and fast queries. You get a dedicated cluster with no limits on records or operations and pay by memory, CPU, and hours. It suits developers who want site or app search without running the server themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

Typesense Cloud offers a one-time allowance of 720 cluster hours and 10 GB of outbound bandwidth for eligible configurations. It does not replenish monthly, and no card is needed to start. The observed Zurich calculator configuration uses 0.5 GB of memory and 2 vCPUs with a 1 hour daily burst. After the allowance, it costs $0.03 an hour, or $21.60 a month, plus $0.09 per GB of outbound bandwidth. Rates vary by configuration and region.

Critical production support is listed as free. You choose the memory size, vCPUs, high availability, and region when you create the cluster.

## When you hit a limit

Once the allowance is exhausted, running clusters begin incurring charges. Runtime fees accrue even when a cluster receives no traffic or data, so terminate an unwanted cluster. Typesense charges payment methods on file weekly by default. The free-tier documentation recommends adding a payment method to avoid interruptions, but does not specify exactly when a cluster without payment is interrupted. Higher-capacity and multi-region configurations cost more.

## Gotchas

- Only select configurations qualify for the free allowance. Check eligibility before launching a larger cluster.
- The free hours and bandwidth are a one-time account allowance, not a recurring monthly tier.
- Burst vCPU plans allow only a limited burst each day.

## Questions people ask

### Is Typesense Cloud free?

It starts free for eligible configurations, with 720 cluster hours and 10 GB of bandwidth once per account. The allowance does not reset monthly.

### How much does the smallest cluster cost after that?

$0.03 an hour, or $21.60 a month, for the observed smallest Zurich configuration. Bandwidth and other configurations or regions can add costs.

### Is Typesense itself free?

Typesense is open source, so you can run it yourself; Typesense Cloud charges for the hosted cluster.
