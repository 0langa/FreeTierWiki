Typesense Cloud is the hosted version of Typesense, an open source search engine with typo tolerance and fast queries. You get a dedicated cluster with no limits on records or operations and pay by memory, CPU, and hours. It suits developers who want site or app search without running the server themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page says Typesense Cloud gives you free credits and you do not need a credit card. On the pricing page, the default cluster of 0.5 GB of memory with 2 vCPUs and a 1 hour burst per day costs $0.03 an hour, which works out to $21.60 a month, and the cost breakdown lists the first 720 hours free. Bandwidth out costs $0.09 per GB, with the first 10 GB free.

Critical production support is listed as free. You choose the memory size, vCPUs, high availability, and region when you create the cluster.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the free hours or free bandwidth are used up, or whether they reset each month. Past the free amounts, the listed rates apply: $0.03 an hour for the smallest cluster and $0.09 per GB of bandwidth out. Turning on high availability or a search delivery network across 3 or 5 regions raises the hourly price.

## Gotchas

- Larger memory sizes and more vCPUs cost more per hour, so the free hours cover less of a bigger cluster.
- The page does not say if the 720 free hours are a one-time credit.
- Burst vCPU plans allow only a limited burst each day.

## Questions people ask

### Is Typesense Cloud free?

It starts free. The home page mentions free credits with no credit card, and the pricing page lists the first 720 hours and first 10 GB of bandwidth free.

### How much does the smallest cluster cost after that?

$0.03 an hour, which the page says works out to $21.60 a month.

### Is Typesense itself free?

Typesense is open source, so you can run it yourself; Typesense Cloud charges for the hosted cluster.
