APIVerve bundles 300+ small APIs behind one key and one bill: lookups such as IP, airport, and VIN; data feeds such as weather and air quality; validators; converters; and generators. Every call costs credits, and plans differ by monthly credits, rate limit, and license. The Free plan is aimed at development and testing.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and includes 200 credits a month with access to all 300+ APIs. It is rate limited to 5 requests a minute, requires attribution, allows non-commercial use only, and comes with community support. Each API has a credit cost: many simple tools cost 2 credits, IP Lookup, Weather, and Phone Number Validator cost 10 credits, Reverse Geocode costs 6, and heavier tools such as Website Screenshot cost 40. Failed requests never count. The Starter plan, at $29.99 a month, adds 200,000 credits, a commercial license, and 60 requests a minute.

## When you hit a limit

APIVerve sends email alerts at 80% and 95% usage. Once your credits are depleted, requests return a 429 status. You can upgrade instantly to restore access, or wait for the next cycle's reset. Credits reset each billing cycle and unused credits do not roll over. The Free plan lists no overage allowance, so there is nothing to bill when you run out.

## Gotchas

- 200 credits is 20 calls to a 10-credit API such as IP Lookup or Weather.
- The 5 requests a minute limit applies even when you still have credits left.
- Commercial use needs a paid plan, and the Free plan requires attribution.

## Questions people ask

### Is APIVerve free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 200 credits a month across all its APIs, with no credit card.

### What happens when I run out of APIVerve credits?

Requests return a 429 status until you upgrade or the next cycle resets your credits.

### Can I use APIVerve's Free plan commercially?

No. The Free plan is for non-commercial use with attribution; paid plans add a commercial license.
