You use Verimail.io through a web control panel for bulk lists and through a REST API for real-time checks. The API accepts POST with Bearer authentication or a GET request and returns JSON, and the docs show code examples. It suits developers and teams that want clean email data at sign-up or before a send.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and gives you 100 verifications a month with email support. The home page says it is free forever and needs no credit card.

Verification generally costs one credit per address; unknown results, duplicates, and syntax errors are not billed. The response includes a billed field. The service also detects catch-all domains and suggests a fix for common typos in free email providers.

Paid plans start at $15 a month for 10 000 verifications. You can also buy pay-as-you-go credit packs that never expire. If you have both a plan and credits, the plan is used first.

## When you hit a limit

When you reach the monthly limit, the API responds with a 402 status code. The pricing page says you can buy pay-as-you-go credits to keep going. An inactive API key returns 403, which the current docs associate with account issues such as an overdue payment rather than the normal credit-exhaustion response.

The free plan needs no credit card, so you pay only if you choose to buy credits or a plan.

## Gotchas

- Do not share your API key in public code or client-side code. Unauthorized use can spend your verification credits.
- An inactive key returns 403; inspect account status separately from a 402 credit-exhaustion error.
- Free and Starter cards list email support; do not assume Starter includes live chat.

## Questions people ask

### Is Verimail free?

Yes, up to a point. The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 100 verifications. It is free forever, and it needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Verimail free limit?

The API answers with a 402 status code. You can buy pay-as-you-go credits or move to a paid plan to continue.

### Do Verimail pay-as-you-go credits expire?

No. The pricing page says the credits never expire. A monthly plan is always used up before your credits.
