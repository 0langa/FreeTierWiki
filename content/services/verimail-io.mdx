You use Verimail.io through a web control panel for bulk lists and through a REST API for real-time checks. The API takes a GET request with your key and returns JSON, and the docs show code examples. It suits developers and teams that want clean email data at sign-up or before a send.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and gives you 100 verifications a month with email support. The home page says it is free forever and needs no credit card.

Each API request with your key deducts 1 credit from your balance. Syntax checks and duplicate removal are listed as free steps on the home page. The service also detects catch-all domains and suggests a fix for common typos in free email providers.

Paid plans start at $15 a month for 10 000 verifications. You can also buy pay-as-you-go credit packs that never expire. If you have both a plan and credits, the plan is used first.

## When you hit a limit

When you reach the monthly limit, the API responds with a 402 status code. The pricing page says you can buy pay-as-you-go credits to keep going. A key that goes over its quota is marked inactive and requests with it are refused.

Nothing renews on its own to charge you. You decide when to buy credits. The free plan has no card on file, so no bill can follow.

## Gotchas

- Do not share your API key in public code or client-side code. Every request that carries it spends a credit.
- A key can become inactive when the usage quota is exceeded or a payment is overdue.
- Free support is by email only. Live chat starts on the Starter plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Verimail free?

Yes, up to a point. The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 100 verifications. It is free forever, and it needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Verimail free limit?

The API answers with a 402 status code. You can buy pay-as-you-go credits or move to a paid plan to continue.

### Do Verimail pay-as-you-go credits expire?

No. The pricing page says the credits never expire. A monthly plan is always used up before your credits.
