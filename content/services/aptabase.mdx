You add a small SDK to your app, call a track function for each event, and read the counts in the Aptabase dashboard. It fits app developers who want simple usage numbers without consent banners.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan covers up to 20,000 events per month and needs no credit card. Every call to the SDK's track function counts as one event. Nothing is tracked automatically, so you decide how many events your app sends. Plan the events you send, because a few busy screens can use the budget quickly.

Every plan, including Free, has unlimited apps. You can also pick EU or US data residency when you create your account. The free plan has no listed time limit, and the page names no trial period.

Paid plans start at $10 per month for 200,000 events. Bigger tiers run up to $450 per month for 50,000,000 events. You pick the tier by how many events you send each month, and you can cancel any time.

## When you hit a limit

Aptabase does not bill overage fees. It sends you an email when you get close to your monthly limit. If you go over, it temporarily pauses your analytics until the start of the next month.

That means a busy month does not cost you money, but it does cost you data. Events sent after the pause starts are not counted. To keep recording, move to a paid tier. Check the pricing table to see which tier fits your volume.

## Gotchas

- Aptabase has no unique user identifier by design, so user-level metrics such as monthly active users and retention are not available.
- Going over the limit pauses analytics for the rest of the month. You lose that data instead of paying a bill.
- Nothing is tracked automatically. You must add a track call for each event you care about.

## Questions people ask

### Is Aptabase free?

Yes, up to 20,000 events per month, with no credit card. Paid plans start at $10 per month.

### What happens when I go over the Aptabase free limit?

You pay no overage fee. Aptabase emails you near the limit and then pauses analytics until the start of the next month.

### Does Aptabase show monthly active users?

No. It collects anonymous data points without a unique user identifier, so user-level metrics such as monthly active users and retention are not possible.
