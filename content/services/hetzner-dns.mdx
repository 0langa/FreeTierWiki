Hetzner DNS is the DNS management built into the Hetzner Console. You list, add, change, and remove zones and records in the console or through the Hetzner API, and Hetzner's name servers answer queries for your domains.

## What the free plan gives you

Hetzner says DNS management in the Hetzner Console is completely free, and its docs say it does not charge for DNS zones. New customers can create a free account, and existing Robot customers can use their account.

By default you can add up to 25 zones across all projects, with up to 500 records per zone and up to 50 records per RRset. The console supports A, AAAA, CAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, SRV, HTTPS, SVCB, TLSA, and more, and you can import BIND files. Every console feature is also in the REST API, and the hcloud command-line tool can create zones. Secondary zones can pull from up to 4 primary name servers.

## When you hit a limit

When you need more than 25 zones, Hetzner asks you to write a support ticket explaining why, and it can raise the limit. The docs say the zone and per-zone record limits can be increased if there is a justified demand.

Some limits are fixed per record: a value can be up to 4096 characters, and the TTL must be at least 60 seconds. Because the service is free, there is no charge for going past a limit; you ask support instead.

## Gotchas

- Hetzner Console does not support DNSSEC, according to its own FAQ.
- You need to own the domain; Hetzner DNS does not give out free subdomains.
- The minimum TTL of 60 seconds rules out very short TTLs for fast failover tricks.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hetzner DNS free?

Yes. Hetzner says DNS management in the Hetzner Console is completely free.

### How many domains can I host on Hetzner DNS?

By default up to 25 zones across all projects. You can ask support to raise the limit.

### Does Hetzner DNS support DNSSEC?

No. Hetzner's FAQ says the Hetzner Console does not currently support DNSSEC.
