You manage flags on the ConfigCat dashboard, and your app reads them through an SDK. It suits developers who want a small, hosted flag service with unlimited team members.

## What the free plan gives you

The Forever Free plan costs $0 and has the same feature set as the paid plans. Limits are 2 products, 2 environments per product, and 10 feature flags (settings) per product. You also get 2 segments per product and 1 permission group per product. Each month you get 5 million config JSON downloads and 20 GB of network traffic.

Team members are unlimited, and so are monthly active users, service connections, and feature flag reads. The audit log is kept for 7 days. ConfigCat says the free plan includes SSO, SAML, multi-factor authentication, and the EU-only data option. SDKs are MIT licensed and open source on GitHub, for languages such as JavaScript, Python, Java, Go, and .NET.

## When you hit a limit

The product, environment, and flag counts are plan limits. The docs say values marked with a star are technical limits that support can raise. The free plan limits themselves are not starred.

For network traffic, the docs say that if you hit the limit, ConfigCat keeps your application up and running. They also say to expect them to contact you about meeting your needs. The pricing page does not describe an automatic charge on the free plan. The Pro plan costs $110 per month on the page and has 3 products, 100 GB of traffic, and 25 million downloads.

## Gotchas

- The limit is 10 feature flags per product, not per account. Two products give you up to 20 flags.
- Audit log retention is 7 days on Free, against 35 days on Pro.
- Free plan support goes through Slack or email. Premium support starts at the Enterprise plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is ConfigCat free?

Yes. The Forever Free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It has 10 feature flags per product, 2 products, and unlimited team members and users.

### How many feature flags does ConfigCat give for free?

You get 10 feature flags per product and 2 products. Each product also has 2 environments.

### What happens if I go over the ConfigCat free network traffic limit?

ConfigCat says it keeps your application running if you hit the traffic limit. It also says it will contact you to discuss your needs.
