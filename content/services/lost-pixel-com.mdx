You run Lost Pixel as a GitHub Action in your CI pipeline, with a `lostpixel.config.ts` file in your repository. The platform then shows each new screenshot next to the old one so you can approve or reject it.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan includes 7,000 screenshots, which Lost Pixel calls shots, per month. It has no credit card requirement. The pricing table lists unlimited admins and members, so your whole team can review changes. It also lists unlimited repos and projects, and the free plan includes monorepo support.

The pricing page says Lost Pixel is free for open source projects and for small projects. The paid plans start at $100 per month for 40,000 shots. Those paid plans charge a price per shot above their quota. The Hobby column shows no such price.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use all 7,000 shots on the Hobby plan. The Hobby column shows a dash where the paid plans list a price per shot above the quota, and the page asks for no card. So the page gives no sign of a charge on the free plan. If you need more shots, the Startup plan adds a price of $0.006 per shot above its quota.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page says the plan is for open source and small projects but does not define small.
- The platform works through the GitHub Action and a `LOST_PIXEL_API_KEY` secret, so plan for GitHub Actions.
- The core tool is open source and can run alone in a mode that only generates and compares screenshots. The review interface and approval flow belong to the platform.

## Questions people ask

### Is Lost Pixel free?

Yes, the Hobby plan is free. It includes 7,000 shots per month and unlimited admins, members, repos, and projects.

### Does Lost Pixel need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required to get started.

### What happens when I go over the Lost Pixel free limit?

The pricing page does not say. It lists a price per shot above the quota only for the paid plans, not for the free one.
