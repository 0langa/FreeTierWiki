You work in the Catalyst console or use the Catalyst CLI to create a project, add functions, and deploy. Functions can be written in Java, Node.js, or Python. Catalyst also offers a data store, file storage, hosting for front ends, and AI services in the same project. It suits small teams that already use Zoho or want one platform for a backend.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier renews every month, forever, with no expiry. The free tier page lists the card required as none. Because the limits assume the whole free tier goes to one component, usage changes when you mix components.

For Functions, you get 25,000 GB-seconds a month. AppSail gets 900 GB-minutes, and Circuits get 2,000 state transitions. Data Store allows 10,000 SELECT requests and 5,000 INSERT requests. Stratus file storage includes 5 GB. API Gateway allows 100,000 requests, web client hosting allows 300,000 requests, and mail allows 100 emails a month. Slate front ends get 500 MB of CDN storage.

## When you hit a limit

The free tier page says a minimum billing applies per project if it exceeds the free tier limits, to cover hosting and infrastructure. The fetched pages do not show the amount. The page suggests deleting projects you no longer use.

The pricing page adds that on subscription plans, once you reach the plan limits and do not move to the next tier, application calls fail for the rest of the month. Counters reset at the end of the month with no rollover. Zoho sends notices at 50% and 80% of your quota.

## Gotchas

- Staying inside the free tier is the only way to pay nothing. A project that passes it can carry a minimum monthly charge.
- A separate six-month free trial with $250 in credits needs you to add a card to claim it.
- Limits are per component and the page assumes you use the whole free tier on that one component. Mixed use gives you less of each.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Catalyst free?

Yes, there is a free tier that renews every month with no expiry. Functions get 25,000 GB-seconds a month.

### Does Zoho Catalyst need a credit card?

No for the free tier. The extra six-month free trial with $250 in credits needs you to add a card.

### What happens when I exceed the Zoho Catalyst free tier?

A minimum monthly billing applies per project that goes past the free tier limits. On subscription plans, calls fail once you reach the plan limit.
