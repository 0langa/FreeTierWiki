You work in the Catalyst console or use the Catalyst CLI to create a project, add functions, and deploy. Functions can be written in Java, Node.js, or Python. Catalyst also offers a data store, file storage, hosting for front ends, and AI services in the same project. It suits small teams that already use Zoho or want one platform for a backend.

## What the free plan gives you

Monthly free allowances renew without expiry and applies across all projects and organizations in the same account. Signup needs no credit card. The pricing page markets a credit value (the reviewed regional page showed €5), while the free-tier page says its component limits assume the entire allowance is used on one component. However, the billing guide says exceeding one resource's limit leaves other resources' allowances available. These descriptions conflict for mixed workloads; confirm the calculation before budgeting one.

For Functions, you get 25,000 GB-seconds a month. AppSail gets 900 GB-minutes, and Circuits get 2,000 state transitions. Data Store allows 10,000 SELECT requests and 5,000 INSERT requests. Stratus file storage includes 5 GB. API Gateway allows 100,000 requests, web client hosting allows 300,000 requests, and mail allows 100 emails a month. Slate front ends get 500 MB of CDN storage.

## When you hit a limit

Catalyst alerts you at 90% usage and prompts you to set up payment near exhaustion. On pay-as-you-go, exceeding any shared free allowance triggers usage billing and a $5 minimum per project with billable activity in the organization, including other projects that stayed inside their component allowances. Completely empty projects are exempt.

The reviewed pages do not establish the exact response when an account exhausts the free allowance without adding a payment method. The free tier renews monthly; it is not a fresh allowance for every project.

## Gotchas

- One project's usage can trigger minimum charges for other projects with billable activity.
- A separate six-month free trial with $250 in credits needs you to add a card to claim it.
- The resource limits apply across the whole account, including its organizations. The marketing table and billing guide disagree on whether mixing components reduces other allowances. The Functions guide says Integration Functions are unavailable in the EU, AU, IN and CA data centers.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Catalyst free?

Yes, there is a free tier that renews every month with no expiry. Functions get 25,000 GB-seconds a month.

### Does Zoho Catalyst need a credit card?

No for the free tier. The extra six-month free trial with $250 in credits needs you to add a card.

### What happens when I exceed the Zoho Catalyst free tier?

On pay-as-you-go, exceeding a shared free allowance triggers usage charges and a $5 minimum per project with billable activity. The precise response before a payment method is added is not established in the reviewed current pages.
