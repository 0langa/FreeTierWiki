Fathom is a web analytics service that collects traffic stats without cookies or personal data. Every plan has the same features, and you pick a plan by monthly pageviews across all your sites. It suits developers and agencies who want simple, privacy-friendly stats and are ready to pay for them.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free plan; the FAQ answers "Do you offer a free plan?" with "Nope." Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You choose a plan and add your card to start the trial, the card is not charged until the trial ends, and Fathom emails you before your plan starts billing.

The smallest plan is $15 a month for up to 100,000 monthly pageviews and includes 50 sites. Pageviews and custom events share one allowance across all sites, and you can add extra sites in packs of 50 for $10 a month. The FAQ suggests running Fathom alongside your current analytics during the trial to see your real pageviews.

## When you hit a limit

When the 7-day trial ends, your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel in your account settings. For pageviews, Fathom says it will never turn your analytics off for occasional traffic spikes. If you stay well over your limit, it emails you about a larger plan, sends reminders and gives extra time before it pauses dashboard and API access, and keeps collecting your data throughout.

## Gotchas

- The trial needs a card, and it becomes a paid subscription if you do not cancel.
- Custom events count toward your monthly pageview allowance.
- If you exceed your limit two months in a row, Fathom gets in touch about a larger plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Fathom free?

No. Fathom has no free plan, but every plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

### Does the trial need a credit card?

Yes. You add a card to start the trial, and it is not charged until the trial ends.

### What happens if I go over my pageviews?

Fathom does not turn analytics off for occasional spikes; if you stay well over, it asks you to upgrade before pausing dashboard access.
