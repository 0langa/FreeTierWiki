You connect a git repository and a cloud account in the Cloud 66 dashboard, then Cloud 66 analyzes your code and deploys it. It suits developers who want a managed deploy flow on servers they own.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is meant for personal projects. It allows deploying to 1 server and 1 static site, with 5 deployments per 24 hours. Static sites are sites made with tools such as Jekyll, Hugo, or Gatsby.

You also get 50 free build minutes. Build minutes count only for containerized apps and static sites. Static site traffic is free up to 1GB, and only outgoing traffic from static sites counts. The plan includes 100MB of managed backup storage with daily backups and 1 team member. Metrics are kept for 1 hour. The Developer plan costs $23 per month and allows 2 servers and 2 static sites.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page shows "No overage" for build minutes on the Free plan, so the 50 minutes are a fixed cap. The page does not say what the Free plan does when you ask for a second server, a second static site, or a sixth deployment in 24 hours.

Paid plans bill overages per unit. Extra build minutes cost $0.03 each, extra team members cost $5 per month, and extra static site traffic costs $0.02 per GB. Cloud 66 does not provide servers. You use your own cloud provider and pay it directly for the server.

## Gotchas

- The free plan covers the Cloud 66 platform only. The server in AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, or another provider is your own bill.
- The 5 deployments in 24 hours cap can slow a team that deploys often. The Developer plan raises it to 50.
- The docs expect your code in a publicly accessible git repository, and pre-built images in public image repositories.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloud 66 free?

Yes, there is a Free plan for personal projects. It covers 1 server, 1 static site, and 5 deployments per 24 hours, with no payment information required.

### Does Cloud 66 provide the servers?

No. You connect your own cloud provider, such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure, and pay it directly for the servers. Cloud 66 charges only for its platform.

### What happens when I exceed my Cloud 66 plan limits?

Most features have overage prices, such as $0.03 per extra build minute. The Free plan shows no overage on its 50 build minutes.
