You use it in the browser by submitting a query and its metadata, or from a JetBrains plug-in or a Visual Studio Code extension. It suits developers who want a quick tuning tip for one query.

## What the free plan gives you

Aiven SQL Query Optimizer is free during the Early Availability period. The page gives no query cap. You choose PostgreSQL or MySQL and a database version, submit your SQL, and get index recommendations and query rewrite suggestions.

The tool does not connect to your database. You upload the SQL statement and extra metadata by hand. The older EverSQL site says the tool is non-intrusive and does not access your sensitive data. The EverSQL home page now points to Aiven for the free tool.

## When you hit a limit

The pages show no quota to hit, so there is no stated limit to pass. The risk is the label itself. The tool is free during Early Availability, and the pages do not say when that period ends or what the price will be afterward.

If you need ongoing checks, Aiven offers a different product, the AI Database Optimizer. It adds database integration, continuous monitoring, and performance insights, and it has a 30-day free trial.

## Gotchas

- The free tool works only from SQL and metadata that you upload by hand.
- Free access depends on the Early Availability period, which has no published end date.
- Continuous monitoring is not part of the free tool.

## Questions people ask

### Is EverSQL free?

EverSQL now runs as Aiven's SQL Query Optimizer, and it is free during the Early Availability period. The pages show no query cap.

### What happened to EverSQL?

Aiven acquired it. The EverSQL home page now says the tool is available for free on Aiven.

### Can the SQL optimizer connect to my database?

No. You can only upload SQL statements and metadata by hand. A connected, continuous version is the separate Aiven AI Database Optimizer.
