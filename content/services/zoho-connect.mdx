You use Zoho Connect in the browser, or in the iOS and Android apps. Your team shares one network, and each group inside it works like a small forum.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan covers up to 5 users, 3 groups, and 10 GB of file storage. You can make 3 boards for planning ideas and assigning tasks. Documents attached to a post can be up to 10 MB each, with up to 10 files per post, and videos can be up to 200 MB.

Individual audio calls, video calls, and instant chat are included. The comparison page also lists 3 manuals. Custom apps and the scheduler are not, and you cannot invite external users.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan lists 5 users, 3 groups, and 3 boards. The pricing pages do not say what happens when you go past them.

Zoho gives new accounts a flexible 15-day trial with no credit card. After the trial ends, the account moves to the Forever Free plan on its own, and you can switch to a paid plan at any time. The pricing page does not say what happens to groups or boards above the free limits at that point.

## Gotchas

- The comparison page marks external users, custom apps, the scheduler, workflows, and the developer API as not included on the free plan.
- Video conferencing for several people is listed on paid plans only, while individual video calls are in the free plan.
- Bot notifications in Zoho Cliq, Zoho's chat tool, are not included on the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Connect free?

Yes, for up to 5 users. The free plan includes 3 groups, 10 GB of storage, and 3 boards.

### Does Zoho Connect need a credit card?

No. Zoho states that no credit card is needed, and the 15-day trial works the same way.

### What happens to Zoho Connect after the trial?

Your account moves to the Forever Free plan automatically. You can switch to a paid plan whenever you like.
