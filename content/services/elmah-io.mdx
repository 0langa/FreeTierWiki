You add an elmah.io NuGet package to your .NET app, set your API key and log ID, and errors then appear in the elmah.io web app. It suits .NET developers who want hosted error logging without running their own server.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. Everyone can try elmah.io for 21 days, and no credit card is required. After that you pick a paid plan, unless you qualify for a free subscription.

Open source projects can apply for a free Small Business subscription. Microsoft MVPs can apply for a free Personal subscription. The Small Business plan costs $26 per month and includes 3 users, unlimited applications, 10,000 messages per month, 400 emails per month, and 30 days of retention. Startups get 12 months at a discounted price, and non-profits get a discount.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the monthly message limit. It does mention top-ups, which you can buy with a credit card to add log messages, and a bigger annual plan as the other route.

The trial itself ends after 21 days, so decide on a plan or an application before then. The docs also list tips for staying below your message limit.

## Gotchas

- The free path is by application. Open source and MVP subscriptions are not automatic at sign-up.
- Top-ups are only available when a credit card is added to the subscription. Without one, you must upgrade to a larger annual plan for more messages.
- Bugster's analysis of your log data is available on Business and up, and on Business and Business+ it runs on your own AI provider key.

## Questions people ask

### Is elmah.io free?

Not permanently. You get a 21-day free trial with no credit card. Open source projects and Microsoft MVPs can apply for a free subscription.

### How much does elmah.io cost after the trial?

The Small Business plan costs $26 per month and includes 10,000 messages per month and 30 days of retention. Bigger plans cost more.

### Does elmah.io work with ASP.NET Core?

Yes. The docs say to install the `Elmah.Io.AspNetCore` NuGet package to log warnings and errors from ASP.NET Core.
