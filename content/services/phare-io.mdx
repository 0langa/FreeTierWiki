You work in the Phare dashboard, and there is also an API plus Terraform, Ansible, and NixOS integrations. It suits indie developers who want uptime checks and a status page without paying per monitor.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free and the pricing page says no credit card is needed. It includes 100 000 monitoring events per month and 10 AI incident summaries per month. Projects, team members, integrations, and alerts are unlimited. You also get public status pages with unlimited custom domains and SSL. SMS and voice alerts are not included.

An event counts each time Phare runs an HTTP, TCP, or ICMP ping check. The pricing FAQ gives an example: one check per minute from two regions comes to about 90k events in a month. Intervals can be set from 30 seconds to 1 hour.

## When you hit a limit

Free and paid accounts get a 10% overage buffer above the quota, and Phare notifies you before you use it and before it runs out. After that, your monitoring pauses automatically until the next billing period starts.

The Hobby plan does not include additional usage, so you cannot pay to extend it. You would need the paid Scale plan, which starts at 5 euros per month plus usage.

## Gotchas

- Checks from many regions at short intervals use events fast, so spread them out to stay under the quota.
- The Hobby plan has no SMS or voice alerts, and the Scale plan includes 20 of them.
- Paid plans use Paddle for payment, and Phare does not store your payment details.

## Questions people ask

### Is Phare free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free forever with 100 000 monitoring events per month, unlimited projects and team members, and public status pages.

### Does Phare need a credit card?

No. The pricing page describes the Hobby plan as for people with no credit card. A payment method is only asked when you choose the Scale plan.

### What happens when I go over the Phare free limit?

You get a 10% overage buffer, with notices. After it is used up, monitoring pauses until your next billing period starts.
