You create a build project in the AWS console and describe the build commands in a `buildspec.yml` file. You can also start builds from the AWS CLI, the AWS SDKs, or a CodePipeline stage.

## What the free plan gives you

The CodeBuild free tier includes 100 total build minutes per month on the general1.small or arm1.small instance types, using on-demand Amazon EC2. With on-demand Lambda, it includes 6,000 total build seconds per month on the lambda.arm.1GB or lambda.x86-64.1GB compute types.

The free tier does not automatically expire at the end of the 12-month AWS Free Tier term. Reserved Capacity EC2, Sandbox, and the Docker image server do not have a free tier. The two free amounts are separate, one for EC2 builds and one for Lambda builds. The docs describe CodeBuild as a fully managed build service that compiles source code, runs unit tests, and produces artifacts ready to deploy.

## When you hit a limit

CodeBuild uses pay-as-you-go pricing with no upfront costs or minimum fees. You pay for build duration at a rate that depends on the compute type. After the free minutes are used up, builds keep running and you are billed for the rest.

The pricing page gives an example. If you run 100 builds of 5 minutes each on build.general1.small, that is 500 build minutes. Minus the 100 free minutes, 400 minutes are billable, which comes to $2.

## Gotchas

- A larger runner is not free. Only the small instance types and the 1GB Lambda types count toward the free tier.
- The getting-started tutorial can create other resources, such as an S3 bucket and CloudWatch Logs, and the docs warn they may add charges.
- The free tier needs an AWS account, and this atlas lists the plan as requiring a card.

## Questions people ask

### Is AWS CodeBuild free?

Partly. The free tier includes 100 build minutes per month on general1.small or arm1.small, or 6,000 build seconds per month on the 1GB Lambda compute types. The rest is billed.

### Does the CodeBuild free tier expire?

No. The pricing page says it does not automatically expire at the end of your 12-month AWS Free Tier term.

### What happens when I use more than 100 CodeBuild minutes?

You are billed for the extra minutes at the rate for your compute type. For example, 500 build minutes in a month leaves 400 billable minutes after the free 100.
