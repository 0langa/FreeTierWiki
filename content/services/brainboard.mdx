You draw in the Brainboard web app and read the Terraform it writes next to your diagram. It also has a CI/CD engine that can run Terraform plan, apply, and destroy in a sandbox.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is free forever. The pricing page lists unlimited cloud architectures and unlimited Terraform code generation. It also gives you access to all supported cloud providers: AWS, Azure, GCP, and OCI.

The comparison table lists projects, environments, and cloud architectures as unlimited, and it lists Terraform code generation, an integrated README file, and access to private and public templates. No credit card is needed to get started. New accounts start with a 21-day free trial that includes all features, and then the account switches to the free plan unless you upgrade.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not list a quota for the Free plan. The limits are feature gaps. The Pro plan adds unlimited members and teams, native architecture versioning, RBAC, and unlimited deployments. The free plan therefore has limited members, teams, and deployments, but the page gives no number.

Pro costs $99 per user per month, and a yearly option is shown at 999. The Enterprise plan adds a private self-hosted runner and has custom pricing.

## Gotchas

- Support on the Free plan is listed as in-app best efforts. Priority support through in-app, Slack, Teams, and email is a paid feature.
- The comparison table lists three levels of CI/CD plugins across the plans: few plugins, open source plugins, and all plugins.
- Pro is priced per user, so a team pays for each person.

## Questions people ask

### Is Brainboard free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes unlimited cloud architectures and unlimited Terraform code generation. Pro costs $99 per user per month.

### Does Brainboard need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required to get started.

### What happens after the Brainboard 21-day trial?

If you do not upgrade, the FAQ says your account switches to the free plan automatically.
