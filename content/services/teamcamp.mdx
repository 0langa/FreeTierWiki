You use Teamcamp in the web app, with a desktop timer app and a help center. It is aimed at agencies and small teams that bill clients for their work.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan covers up to 10 users and unlimited projects. It allows 250 tasks and 1 GB of storage, and it includes free support. Inside a workspace you can create projects, assign tasks, set due dates and priorities, comment on tasks, and track time.

The help center also describes a customer log and invoicing. The pricing page does not say which of those extras Free includes. Paid plans add unlimited tasks and 100 GB of storage. Plus costs $6 per user per month billed annually, or $8 billed monthly.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 250 tasks, 10 users, or 1 GB of storage. The Free plan costs nothing, and the page shows no automatic charge. It also does not say whether the limits are enforced by blocking new work or by asking you to upgrade, so check inside the app.

If you outgrow the plan, the Plus plan lifts the limits on users and tasks. Pro Unlimited costs $99 per month billed annually for unlimited users.

## Gotchas

- Free is limited to 250 tasks in total, which a busy agency can reach quickly.
- The pricing page lists client portal access only under the paid plans.
- The Free plan is described as "for early-stage agencies", so it is built for small agency use rather than large teams.

## Questions people ask

### Is Teamcamp free?

Yes. The Free plan allows up to 10 users, unlimited projects, 250 tasks, and 1 GB of storage.

### What does Teamcamp cost after the free plan?

Plus costs $6 per user per month billed annually, or $8 when billed monthly. Pro Unlimited costs $99 per month billed annually for unlimited users.

### Does Teamcamp have time tracking?

Yes. The help center describes time tracking by task and project, with daily, weekly, and monthly views. It also mentions a desktop timer.
