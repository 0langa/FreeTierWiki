You use Proton Mail in a browser, in the mobile apps, or in a desktop app. Its comparison table lists end-to-end encryption on every plan. The free plan suits people who want one private address.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and needs no credit card. It includes 1 user and 1 email address. Storage starts at 500 MB, and you can raise it to 1 GB by finishing a three-task starter checklist in your first 18 days.

The comparison table lists 150 messages per day, 3 folders, 3 labels, 1 custom filter, and a 25 MB attachment size. A free account has no custom email domain. Proton Mail Plus costs $3.99 per month when billed yearly. It adds 15 GB of storage, 10 email addresses, and 1 custom domain.

## When you hit a limit

If your mailbox runs out of storage, you cannot send or receive emails, add events, or upload files. You can free up space by deleting messages and attachments, or by upgrading to a plan with more storage.

Proton says a person who never had a paid subscription cannot exceed the plan limits. The restricted-access rules apply to people who downgrade from a paid plan: their access is limited, and after 12 months their excess data can be deleted. The sending limit of 150 messages per day is the other practical cap.

## Gotchas

- The storage bonus is for free accounts only, and it expires if you do not finish the checklist within 18 days.
- IMAP and SMTP support for other mail apps appears under the paid plans in the comparison table.
- If you upgrade after finishing the checklist, the extra free storage does not carry over, because paid plans include much more storage.

## Questions people ask

### Is Proton Mail free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and gives you 1 email address with 500 MB of storage. You can raise the storage to 1 GB with the starter checklist.

### How many emails can I send with free Proton Mail?

The comparison table lists 150 messages per day for the Free plan. Paid plans list the number as unlimited.

### Can I use a custom domain with free Proton Mail?

No. Custom domain support starts with Proton Mail Plus, which supports 1 custom domain.
