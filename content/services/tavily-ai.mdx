You call Tavily through a REST API, a Python or JavaScript SDK, or the playground in the web dashboard. It fits developers who build chatbots and agents on top of language models.

## What the free plan gives you

The Researcher plan is free and includes 1,000 API credits per month with email support. No credit card is required. Credits reset on the first day of each month.

Each request spends credits based on its type. A basic search costs 1 credit and an advanced search costs 2. Basic extraction costs 1 credit for every 5 successful URLs, and advanced extraction costs 2 credits for every 5. Mapping costs 1 credit for every 10 pages. A research task costs between 4 and 250 credits, depending on the model.

Paid plans start at 4,000 credits for $30 per month. Pay as you go costs $0.008 per credit. The pricing page also lists free access for students.

## When you hit a limit

When you use all 1,000 credits, requests stop until the credits reset or you upgrade to a paid plan. The FAQ says this directly, so the free plan cannot run up a bill.

Rate limits also apply. Production keys, with 1,000 requests per minute, need a paid plan or pay as you go. Without one, expect the development limit of 100 requests per minute. The crawl endpoint is limited to 100 requests per minute on both key types.

## Gotchas

- Credits are not requests. An advanced search, a crawl, or a research task uses more credits than a basic search.
- Failed extractions and failed map requests are not charged.
- The pricing page lists SLAs only under Enterprise, so do not build a critical service on the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tavily free?

Yes. The Researcher plan gives you 1,000 API credits per month with no credit card.

### What happens when I run out of Tavily credits?

Requests stop until your credits reset on the first day of the month, or until you upgrade to a paid plan.

### How much does a Tavily search cost in credits?

A basic search costs 1 credit and an advanced search costs 2 credits.
