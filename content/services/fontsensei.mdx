You use it in the browser. Choose a tag, look through the fonts, and press Embed to get code for your site. It suits designers and developers who know the mood they want but not the font name.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no paid plan. The home page says the tool is free and covers all fonts from Google Fonts. You do not need an account. Every font entry has an Embed button, a link to similar fonts, and a link to the font on Google Fonts.

The main feature is tags. The about page says readable tags on the left make fonts easy to discover. Latin fonts get tags in English, and fonts for languages such as Chinese, Japanese, and Korean get tags in their own language. The interface is available in many languages, and the about page credits Google Fonts as the font source and Font Library for the Latin tag data. A link to a website builder is also credited for direct use there.

## When you hit a limit

The pages show no usage limit, quota, or paid tier. No account or card is involved, so nothing can be billed. The pages also do not describe a rate limit, so treat that as unknown.

The real limit is the catalog. Font sensei only covers fonts from Google Fonts. It is a picker and an embed helper, so it does not host fonts itself, and you cannot upload your own.

## Gotchas

- The tool covers only Google Fonts, so fonts from other foundries are not listed.
- Each font links back to Google Fonts, so the full details of a font live there, not on this site.
- The tags come from outside data, as the credits show, so a tag may not match your own taste.

## Questions people ask

### Is Font sensei free?

Yes. The home page says the tool is free and includes all fonts from Google Fonts. You do not need an account.

### Where do the fonts on Font sensei come from?

The about page credits Google Fonts as the font source. The Latin tag data comes from Font Library.

### Can I search fonts by style on Font sensei?

Yes. You pick a tag, such as Handwritten or Techno, and the page lists the matching fonts with an Embed button and links to similar fonts.
