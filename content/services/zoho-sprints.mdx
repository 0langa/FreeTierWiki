You work in the Zoho Sprints web app or in the iOS and Android apps. The help center has a Quickstart Guide that runs through the Sprints process.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan covers 3 users, 3 projects, and 500 MB of storage. Work items and sprint cycles are unlimited. You get agile boards for Scrum and Kanban, agile reports and dashboards, a due date tracker, a project feed, and real-time chat.

Backlog priority management is limited on the free plan, so expect fewer ordering tools than on paid plans. The plan also includes business hours and holiday lists, integrations with Google Apps and Office 365, data import from Jira, Google and Microsoft Office, and the mobile apps.

## When you hit a limit

The plan stops at its limits instead of billing you. A fourth user or a fourth project needs a paid plan. The paid Starter plan is priced per user for teams of 12 to 100 users and lists 50 projects and 20 GB of storage.

Zoho offers a 15-day trial of the Enterprise features and does not ask for credit card details. The pricing FAQ says that if you downgrade to a free plan, you keep access to two projects with limited functionality.

## Gotchas

- The plan table lists 3 projects, while the downgrade FAQ says two projects with limited functionality. Check your account after a downgrade.
- Chat support is for paid plans. Free plan users get email support around the clock, five days a week.
- Epic management, effort estimation, AI tools, and SCM integration are listed under paid plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Sprints free?

Yes, for up to 3 users and 3 projects. The free plan has 500 MB of storage and Scrum and Kanban boards.

### Does Zoho Sprints need a credit card?

No. Zoho says it does not ask for credit card details when you sign up.

### Can I import from Jira into Zoho Sprints?

Yes. The free plan lists data import from Jira, Google, and Microsoft Office. Import from Azure DevOps and Trello appears under the paid Starter plan.
