PythonAnywhere is a hosting service built around Python. You get a Python IDE and consoles in the browser, and you can run a web app on its servers without managing a machine. It suits students and beginners who want a Python web app online quickly.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Beginner account at $0 a month as a limited free account to explore PythonAnywhere. You get one web app at your-username.pythonanywhere.com and two consoles with restricted outbound internet access.

The help page on free accounts lists 100 CPU seconds, 512 MiB of disk space, one web app with one web worker and a 1 month expiry, and two consoles. CPU seconds count code run in consoles, scheduled tasks, and always-on tasks, not your web app, and the pricing page says they are included each day. All accounts, including free ones, get free SSL and screen-sharing with other PythonAnywhere accounts.

## When you hit a limit

If you use up your CPU seconds, PythonAnywhere does not stop or bill a free account. The pricing page says tarpitted processes run at a lower priority than users who have not hit their limit, though they still get spare capacity on the server cluster. Paid plans can buy more.

The free web app has a 1 month expiry, so plan to come back and keep it running. For more, the Developer plan costs $10 a month and includes 5,000 CPU-seconds per day, 5GB of disk, and one web app on a custom domain.

## Gotchas

- Free accounts have restricted outbound internet access, so calls to many outside APIs from your code will not work.
- Accounts created before 2026-01-15 kept a MySQL database and one daily scheduled task; new free accounts do not list them.
- A custom domain needs a paid plan; the free web app lives at your-username.pythonanywhere.com.

## Questions people ask

### Is PythonAnywhere free?

Yes. The Beginner account costs $0 a month and includes one web app, two consoles, and 512 MiB of disk.

### Can a free PythonAnywhere app call outside APIs?

Only in part. Free accounts have restricted outbound internet access, so many outside sites cannot be reached.

### What happens when I run out of CPU seconds?

Your processes are tarpitted: they run at a lower priority than other users but keep going, and you are not billed.
