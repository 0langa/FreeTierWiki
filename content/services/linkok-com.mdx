You create an account on the site, add your website, and read the report in your browser or in an email. It suits people who run a small site and want a check on dead links.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page says Linkok is free forever for small sites, with no credit card required. The free plan gives monthly scans for a website with up to 100 pages. Links are unlimited, and Linkok retries after temporary errors to cut false positives.

Reports on the free plan are public. After each scan, Linkok sends an email with the summary and a link to the full report. Paid plans add private reports, and the email support option sits with the daily plans.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan covers a website with up to 100 pages. The page does not say what happens to a bigger site on the free plan, and it lists no overage price. The practical answer is to move to a paid plan sized for more pages.

For 100 pages, weekly reports cost $5 a month and daily reports cost $10 a month. Plans for 500, 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 50,000, and 100,000 pages cost more. Paying yearly gives 2 months free.

## Gotchas

- Free reports are public, so anyone with the link can read them.
- Scans on the free plan run monthly, so a link that breaks today may stay broken for weeks.
- The pricing page lists a page limit for each plan, so check your page count before you pick one.

## Questions people ask

### Is Linkok free?

Yes, for small sites. The home page says it is free forever, with monthly scans for a website of up to 100 pages and public reports.

### Does Linkok need a credit card?

No. The home page says no credit card is required for the free plan.

### How often does Linkok scan my site?

The free plan scans monthly. Weekly and daily scans are on paid plans.
