You create sites and keys in the hCaptcha dashboard, add a short script and a div to your form, and verify the token with an HTTP call. Plugins and SDKs exist for WordPress, React, Vue, Angular, Node, and mobile apps. It suits anyone who wants a CAPTCHA that is simple to add.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan costs $0 and covers up to 10,000 requests per month. The plans page lists world-class bot protection, support in every country, and compliance with GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and PIPL. It says hCaptcha retains no personal data.

Pro adds the low friction 99.9% passive mode, custom themes, and analytics. It costs $99 per month billed yearly or $139 billed monthly, and includes 100K monthly evals, then $0.99 per 1K. Enterprise adds risk scores, SLAs, SAML SSO, and advanced reporting through a sales contact.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page does not say what happens when you pass 10,000 requests in a month. It lists no overage price for Basic, so check your dashboard and the terms before you rely on it for a busy site.

Pro bills extra evals above its included 100K at $0.99 per 1K. The page also offers a free 14 day Pro trial with no payment required. If you do not keep Pro, you are switched back to the Free plan after 14 days.

## Gotchas

- A callback in the browser does not authorize anything. You must send the token and your secret to the siteverify endpoint and check that success is true.
- Keep the secret on your server. Only the sitekey belongs in your page.
- Passive mode and custom themes are not in Basic, so free users see a visible challenge.

## Questions people ask

### Is hCaptcha free?

Yes. The Basic plan is $0 and free for up to 10,000 requests per month. It works in every country.

### What happens when I go over the hCaptcha free limit?

The plans page does not say. It lists Pro at $99 per month billed yearly, with 100K monthly evals included, and a free 14 day trial.

### Does hCaptcha need a credit card?

The plans page says no payment is required for the free 14 day Pro trial. It does not mention a card for the Basic plan.
