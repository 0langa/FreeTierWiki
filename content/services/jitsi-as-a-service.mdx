Jitsi as a Service, or JaaS, is 8x8's hosted version of Jitsi Meet, the open-source video meeting software. You embed meetings in your own web or mobile app with the Jitsi IFrame API or mobile SDKs, and 8x8 runs the servers on its 8x8.vc domain.

## What the free plan gives you

The JaaS Dev plan is free and includes 25 monthly active users. JaaS counts a monthly active user as a unique user who attended at least one meeting, with at least one other user, in the same month, and a user is a unique endpoint.

The meeting features are listed for every plan: HD audio, video up to 1080p, chat, desktop and mobile screen sharing, recording, a customizable UI, passcodes, and end-to-end encryption. JaaS supports up to 500 participants in one meeting and unlimited concurrent meetings, served from nine media servers worldwide.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says each plan includes a fixed number of monthly active users, and if you exceed it, an overage charge of $0.99 per MAU applies. It does not say whether the free Dev plan can be billed this way or stops instead, so check the console before you invite more than 25 people in a month.

The next plan, JaaS Basic, includes 300 monthly active users for $99.00 a month. Add-ons cost the same on every plan, for example recordings at $0.01 per minute and real-time transcription at $0.03 per minute.

## Gotchas

- Every distinct device counts as a user, so one person joining from a laptop and a phone can count twice.
- Recording, RTMP streaming, SIP, and phone dial-in are paid add-ons even on the free plan.
- Jitsi Meet guides use meet.jit.si, but JaaS meetings run on 8x8.vc, so swap the domain when you follow them.

## Questions people ask

### Is JaaS free?

Yes, for small use. The JaaS Dev plan is free and covers 25 monthly active users.

### What counts as a monthly active user?

A unique endpoint that attended at least one meeting with at least one other user in the same month.

### Can I record meetings on the free plan?

Recording is available, but it is a paid add-on at $0.01 per minute on every plan.
