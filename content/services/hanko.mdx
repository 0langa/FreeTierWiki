You create a project in the Hanko Cloud Console, then add the Hanko Elements web components or a backend SDK to your app. It suits developers who want passkeys and a login screen that they could also host themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free, and the page says no credit card is required. It includes 10,000 monthly active users and 2 projects. A monthly active user is anyone who gets a session token from Hanko at least once in a month, and later activity that month does not count again.

The plan has passkeys, passwords, and passwordless login, plus social SSO and multi-factor authentication. You also get a custom domain, an admin dashboard, and community support. The Pro plan costs $29 per month, has 5 projects, and adds personal support, team invites, webhooks, the admin API, and custom SMTP. Startups can apply for a plan with one million monthly active users free.

## When you hit a limit

Hanko does not stop you. The FAQ says it does not enforce user limits and will not block sign-ups or logins. If you stay above the limit for more than one month in a row, the team reaches out and asks you to subscribe to a higher tier.

When you upgrade from Free to Pro, you pay only for the monthly active users above 10,000. The Pro price is $0.01 per extra monthly active user. All Hanko Cloud tiers include the first 10,000 users free.

## Gotchas

- Hanko Cloud runs only in the AWS Frankfurt region in Germany today. If you need another region, look at self-hosting.
- SAML SSO is a Pro feature at $49 per month for each connection.
- A project counts toward the 2-project limit. Plan a dev and a production project with that in mind.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hanko free?

Yes. The Hanko Cloud Starter plan is free for 10,000 monthly active users and 2 projects, and it needs no credit card.

### What happens if I go over the Hanko free user limit?

Nothing stops. Hanko does not block users, but if you stay above the limit for more than a month in a row, it asks you to subscribe to a higher tier.

### Can I self-host Hanko?

Yes. Hanko is open source, and the FAQ says you can use Hanko Cloud or self-host Hanko from GitHub.
