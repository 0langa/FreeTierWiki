You use it in a browser and with your own mail system. You send one email to an address at dkimvalidator.com, then open the page to read the results. It suits mail admins and developers who set up DKIM or SPF.

## What the free plan gives you

The service is free, and the pages mention no registration or sign-up. It accepts messages sent to any address at dkimvalidator.com. Your mail travels through regular mail servers, so the headers the tools see match what your real recipients see. The page shows your DKIM and SPF validity and your SpamAssassin score together.

The tests run each time you load the page, while the message is saved once. That lets you change your DNS records and check the same message again without resending it. The tool also looks up DKIM and SPF records directly from the authoritative servers where it can, which avoids long waits for cached DNS records. SpamAssassin rules are updated daily.

## When you hit a limit

The page does not give a daily message cap or a rate limit, and there is no paid plan. The one stated limit is time: messages are deleted a few hours after they arrive. After that, you send a new test email. There is no card and no bill. The page does not say what happens if you send a very large number of messages.

## Gotchas

- Test messages are deleted a few hours after they arrive, so results are not a lasting record.
- The pages describe a web page only, and they mention no API.
- The checks cover DKIM, SPF, and SpamAssassin only.

## Questions people ask

### Is DKIMValidator.com free?

Yes. The service is free, and the pages mention no registration. You send a test email and read the results on the page.

### How long does DKIMValidator.com keep my test email?

It deletes messages a few hours after they arrive. Send a new test email if you need to check again later.

### Do I have to resend the email after I change my DNS records?

No. The message is saved once and the tests run each time you load the page, so you can fix DNS and reload.
