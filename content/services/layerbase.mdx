Layerbase gives you a managed database and a connection string. The same dashboard covers relational engines and key-value stores, so you can try different backends without setting up a server for each one. You can branch a database to try a migration or seed data on a separate copy.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes two databases, 5 GB of storage, and 20 concurrent connections. Its nine Standard engines include PostgreSQL, MariaDB and Valkey. Pick a region when you create the database, then connect using the client for that engine. Layerbase lists MySQL and FerretDB under Solo rather than Free.

When a free database sits idle, Layerbase puts it to sleep. A normal connection wakes a hibernated database, so it can take longer than a connection to one already running. Databases that sit idle for longer can be archived. Restore an archived database from its page before trying to connect.

## When you hit a limit

Storage exhaustion triggers a warning and remedy window; databases may stop if usage remains at/above quota. Free bandwidth counts incoming/outgoing traffic across all databases: 10 GB/rolling 24 hours, 50 GB/7 days and 150 GB/30 days. The first exceeded window starts a 24-hour warning; traffic above five times an allowance stops databases immediately.

Bandwidth stops do not lift automatically as usage drains. Upgrade or contact support after fixing traffic. Plans have fixed prices without metered overages. Paused databases remain exportable without upgrading, limited to five exports per database per 24 hours.

## Gotchas

- The plan expects a free database to sleep for at least 25% of a rolling seven-day window. Polling or uptime probes that keep it awake can lead to a pause. A provider-side pause for excess usage differs from ordinary sleep. Connecting again may fail until the pause is lifted.
- The separate Pro trial lasts seven days, requires a card and automatically converts to $15/month unless cancelled during the trial.
- Free allows one account per person or organization. A product earning recurring production revenue belongs on a paid plan; this eligibility rule triggers a human request to upgrade, not automatic suspension. Nonprofits and borderline cases can request an exception.

## Questions people ask

### Is Layerbase free?

Yes. The Free plan includes two databases and 5 GB of storage. You can create an account without a credit card.

### Can I use MySQL on the free plan?

Layerbase includes MariaDB on Free. For the MySQL engine itself, choose Solo or a higher plan.

### Will a sleeping Layerbase database drop my data?

Hibernation leaves the data in place. Connecting wakes the database unless it has been stopped, archived or paused for another reason.
