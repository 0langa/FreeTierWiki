You send mail with a REST API call or over SMTP, and you manage domains, keys, and logs in a web platform. It suits developers who need app email and want an EU provider.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euros. It sends up to 100 emails a day through the API and SMTP. It allows unlimited domains, 5 templates, and 7 days of log retention. The pricing page does not say whether the free account asks for a card.

SMS is separate. You buy prepaid credits, and they do not depend on your email plan. A 20 euro pack gives about 500 SMS, and credits are valid for 12 months. The pricing page lists 0.040 euros as the price of an SMS in France. The number of SMS per pack is approximate because prices vary by country.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 100 emails a day on the Free plan. Its plan table lists "Overage available" from the Individual plan up, with no Free overage indicator. That does not establish the exact response at exhaustion or a complete billing policy.

The Individual plan costs 8 euros per month billed annually, or 10 euros billed monthly, excluding tax. The selected starting option covers 20 K emails per month and adds more templates and unlimited API keys. Higher plans add longer log retention and a dedicated IP.

## Gotchas

- Sweego presents itself as a transactional email and SMS sending API. The pricing page lists no campaign tools.
- SMS credits expire after 12 months, and they are bought in packs on top of any email plan.
- The documentation site loads with JavaScript, so check it in a browser for the current API details.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sweego free?

Yes, for email. The Free plan sends up to 100 emails a day by API and SMTP. SMS has no free allowance.

### How much does Sweego SMS cost?

You buy credits. A 20 euro pack gives about 500 SMS, and the France price is listed as 0.040 euros per SMS.

### Does Sweego have a sandbox for testing?

Yes. Set the `dry-run` parameter to true to test your email flow without sending to a real address.
