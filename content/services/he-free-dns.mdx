Hurricane Electric, the network operator, runs a Free DNS Hosting portal at dns.he.net. You sign up for a free account, add your domains, and HE's name servers answer forward and reverse DNS queries for them.

## What the free plan gives you

The portal is free: if you do not have an account, you can sign up for a free one from the home page. The service is dual-stack, answering queries over both IPv4 and native IPv6, and the servers are geographically diverse. It supports A, AAAA, ALIAS, CNAME, CAA, MX, NS, TXT, SRV, SSHFP, SPF, RP, NAPTR, HINFO, LOC, and PTR records.

You can host multiple domains per account. Reverse zones have a smart mode for IPv4 and IPv6 and several formats, slave (secondary) zones are supported, and the portal checks delegation and field syntax. Dynamic DNS lets a script or router update a record, and dynamic TXT records help with Let's Encrypt DNS-01 validation.

## When you hit a limit

The portal page lists features but no numbers: it does not state a zone limit, a record limit, or a query limit, and it does not describe what happens if an account uses a lot. Treat it as a free service with unwritten limits.

For dynamic TXT records, the page warns of a propagation delay of up to 5 minutes while the record's TTL expires, so wait before asking for DNS-01 validation. You must create a dynamic record in the web interface before you can update it, and you cannot create or delete those TXT records dynamically.

## Gotchas

- Dynamic DNS is described as a basic implementation, with documentation still being written, so expect to read examples rather than full docs.
- The portal page does not mention DNSSEC.
- Existing admin.he.net customers must contact support for a password instead of signing up again.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hurricane Electric DNS free?

Yes. The dns.he.net portal is free DNS hosting, and you can sign up for a free account.

### Does Hurricane Electric Free DNS support dynamic DNS?

Yes. It supports dynamic updates of address and TXT records, plus a checkip service at checkip.dns.he.net.

### How many domains can I host?

The portal says multiple domains per account but does not give a number.
