You manage Blob Storage in the Azure portal, with the Azure CLI, or from code with the Azure Storage client libraries. It fits developers who want plain file storage inside an Azure subscription.

## What the free plan gives you

New Azure accounts get a free monthly amount of Blob Storage for 12 months. The official free-services comparison lists 5 GB of hot block blob storage on locally redundant storage (LRS), plus 20,000 read operations and 10,000 write operations each month.

The free-services comparison places Blob Storage in the 12-month group. Eligibility depends on a new account and the offered signup route; check the free usage grid in your subscription before deploying.

The free amount covers only the hot tier on LRS. Other tiers and other redundancy options are billed.

## When you hit a limit

After you move to pay-as-you-go, the free amount is an allowance rather than a spending cap. To continue after the initial 30-day credit period, you must choose that upgrade. Extra storage and extra operations are billed at the normal pay-as-you-go rates.

Blob Storage bills data storage per GB per month, plus operations, data retrieval, and data transfer. There is no hard cap on the free amount. After the 12 months end, the free amount ends too, and the storage is billed as normal. Set a budget and an alert in Azure Cost Management to catch a surprise early.

## Gotchas

- You need a phone number and a credit or debit card to sign up, and Azure places a small temporary authorization on the card.
- The published free amount covers hot-tier LRS only. Cool, cold, and archive tiers and other redundancy options have their own prices on the pricing page.
- SFTP is a separate line on the pricing page, charged per hour while it is enabled on a storage account.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Blob Storage free?

Only for new Azure accounts, and only for 12 months. The comparison lists 5 GB of hot LRS block blob storage with 20,000 read and 10,000 write operations each month.

### Does Azure Blob Storage need a credit card?

Yes. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

### What happens when I go over the free Blob Storage amount?

Azure bills the extra at the normal rates. There is no hard cap, so use budgets and alerts in Cost Management.
