You manage Blob Storage in the Azure portal, with the Azure CLI, or from code with the Azure Storage client libraries. It fits developers who want plain file storage inside an Azure subscription.

## What the free plan gives you

New Azure accounts get a free monthly amount of Blob Storage for 12 months. The atlas records it as 5 GB of hot block blob storage on locally redundant storage (LRS), plus 20,000 read operations and 10,000 write operations each month.

The Azure free services page says some free services last 12 months for new customers, and some stay free for as long as you have an account. Blob Storage is in the 12-month group in this entry. The pricing page does not show the free amounts, so check the free services list in the portal before you rely on them.

The free amount covers only the hot tier on LRS. Other tiers and other redundancy options are billed.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page could not be read for the free amounts, so this section follows the entry's recorded data. Azure does not stop your account at the free amount. Extra storage and extra operations are billed at the normal pay-as-you-go rates.

Blob Storage bills data storage per GB per month, plus operations, data retrieval, and data transfer. There is no hard cap on the free amount. After the 12 months end, the free amount ends too, and the storage is billed as normal. Set a budget and an alert in Azure Cost Management to catch a surprise early.

## Gotchas

- You need a phone number and a credit or debit card to sign up, and Azure may place a small temporary hold on the card.
- The free amount covers hot-tier LRS only. Cool or archive tiers and geo-redundant options are billed from the first byte.
- Features such as SFTP, versioning, and soft delete can add cost, because you pay for the space and operations they use.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Blob Storage free?

Only for new Azure accounts, and only for 12 months. The recorded amount is 5 GB of hot LRS block blob storage with 20,000 read and 10,000 write operations each month.

### Does Azure Blob Storage need a credit card?

Yes. The Azure free account asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card for identity checks. Azure says it does not charge the card at sign-up.

### What happens when I go over the free Blob Storage amount?

Azure bills the extra at the normal rates. There is no hard cap, so use budgets and alerts in Cost Management.
