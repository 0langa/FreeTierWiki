You work with B2 in the Backblaze web console, with the command-line tool, or through the Native API and the S3-compatible API. Any tool written for S3 can point at a B2 bucket with few changes.

## What the free plan gives you

The first 10GB of storage is always free. Backblaze also gives you free egress, meaning downloads, up to 3x your average monthly stored data. It works out the average from byte hours over the billing cycle.

Class A, B, and C API calls are free for pay-as-you-go customers. For Class D transactions, the first 2,500 calls per day are free. There are no minimum file size fees and no minimum storage duration fees.

Beyond the free storage, B2 starts at $6.95 per TB per month. The pricing page does not say whether sign-up asks for a card. It lists a credit card or invoice as the payment method.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing is cut off at the free amounts. Storage above 10GB is billed monthly at the pay-as-you-go rate.

Egress above 3x your average storage costs $0.01 per GB. Backblaze says egress is free to and through many partner CDNs and compute services, including Cloudflare and Fastly, so a CDN in front of the bucket removes most of that fee. Class D transactions beyond the daily free amount cost $0.004 per 10,000 calls. Backblaze describes the pricing as no surprise fees and no penalties.

## Gotchas

- To create a public bucket, you must verify your email and have a payment history on file, or pay a small fee by card that is credited to your balance.
- Bucket permission changes can take up to ten minutes to apply.
- The S3-compatible API does not support ACLs, IAM roles, object tagging, or website configuration, so check your tool before you move over.

## Questions people ask

### Is Backblaze B2 free?

Partly. The first 10GB of storage is always free, and downloads are free up to 3x your average monthly storage. Anything above that is billed.

### Does Backblaze B2 need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say whether sign-up asks for a card. To create a public bucket you need a payment history on file or a small card payment.

### What happens when I go over the Backblaze B2 free egress?

Backblaze bills the extra download traffic at $0.01 per GB. Downloads to or through partner CDNs such as Cloudflare and Fastly are free.
