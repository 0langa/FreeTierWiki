You create a snitch in the web app, get a unique check-in URL, and add a curl call to the end of your cron job. If the call does not arrive in time, you get an alert. It suits people with a single scheduled job that must not fail silently.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called The Lone Snitch and sits under the "No Frills Free Plan" heading. It gives you 1 snitch. The plans page does not list its check-in interval or other features, and the free plan is not tied to a trial.

The paid plans add snitches. Little Birdy costs $5 per month for 3 snitches, with an API, Field Agent, and unlimited team members. Private Eye costs $19 per month for 100 snitches and adds integrations. Surveillance Van costs $49 per month for 300 snitches and adds Smart Alerts and Error Notices.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page does not say what happens when you try to add a second snitch on the free plan. You need a paid plan for more snitches. The page lists no overage charge and gives no card requirement for the free plan.

For on-premise hosting, or if you need to monitor more jobs than the top plan allows, the page tells you to email the company.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has only 1 snitch, so one backup or one email job fits, but a second does not.
- The docs say the snitch checks on the hour, every hour. A check-in at 2:10 p.m. is first judged in the 3 to 4 p.m. window.
- Only the Surveillance Van plan has a free trial button, and the page does not give its length.

## Questions people ask

### Is Dead Man's Snitch free?

Yes, in a small way. The Lone Snitch plan is free and gives you 1 snitch. Paid plans start at $5 per month for 3 snitches.

### How do I check in to Dead Man's Snitch?

Send a request to your snitch's unique URL, which uses the nosnch.in domain. The docs chain a curl call after the job, such as a backup script.

### How often does Dead Man's Snitch check?

The docs example uses an hourly interval. On the hour, it looks to see if your snitch checked in during the previous hour.
