MapTiler Cloud is a hosted map platform: vector and raster tiles, ready-made and custom map styles, search and geocoding, and storage for your own geodata. It works with MapTiler SDK and with third-party libraries through tile requests. The Free plan suits developers testing an idea or building a personal or non-commercial map.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and is suitable for testing, proofs of concept, prototyping, personal, or non-commercial use. Each month it includes 5k map sessions, 1k search sessions, 2k 3D sessions, and 100k API requests. A session is an initialized map object, and users can interact with the map within it. You also get 5 custom map styles, 5 GB of storage with a 1 file limit, and 100 data processing uploads a month. Vector and XYZ raster tiles and the search and geocoding service are included, and the MapTiler logo appears on the map.

## When you hit a limit

On the Free plan, service pauses until the next month unless you upgrade to a paid plan. MapTiler says Free plans do not require billing information, so there is no overage bill. The paid Flex plan costs $30 a month and charges automatic overuse fees at the end of the monthly billing period, with an optional spending limit. You can see sessions, requests, data processing, and storage in the Analytics tab of your account.

## Gotchas

- Commercial use is not part of the Free plan; MapTiler lists it as a paid feature.
- Static maps count as many API requests each, so a few images use the request quota quickly.
- When the quota runs out, maps in your app stop loading until the next month.
