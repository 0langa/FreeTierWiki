You work in the Purview portal at purview.microsoft.com, and you can add custom assets through REST APIs. It fits data owners and admins at organizations that already run Azure or Microsoft Fabric.

## What the free plan gives you

The free version is a single, organization-wide instance of Purview that is available without setup. Metadata for your Azure and Microsoft Fabric resources is inventoried automatically, and users can browse and search it in the catalog. Data owners can add descriptions, classifications, and contacts.

The free version supports live view for Azure Blob Storage, Azure Data Lake Storage Gen 2, Azure SQL Database, Azure subscriptions, and Microsoft Fabric. It also includes Data Map history, which logs updates made to assets.

You can annotate up to 1,000 assets. An annotated asset is one you curated in live view or added through the Data Map REST APIs. A description and a classification on the same asset count as one. For example, with 50 live assets and 15 annotated, you can annotate 985 more.

## When you hit a limit

The pages describe the limit but do not say what happens at 1,000 annotated assets. Plan to upgrade to the enterprise version before you reach it. When you upgrade, your data from the free version moves into the enterprise version in the same location.

The free version is also not guaranteed to stay. If your tenant shows three months of no activity, Microsoft may clean up the account and remove unused resources. The page does not list a card requirement, and the free version is in preview.

## Gotchas

- The free version is in preview and is released to organizations in batches. Customers of Azure Enterprise, Microsoft 365 Enterprise, and some other Microsoft products can open it from the portal. Other organizations may have to wait.
- There are no automated scans, workflows, or data policies. Applying classifications is manual. Those need the enterprise version.
- You cannot open support tickets on the free version. You must upgrade to the enterprise version for support.

## Questions people ask

### Is Microsoft Purview free?

There is a free version for data governance with a catalog, search, and up to 1,000 annotated assets. It is in preview and not yet open to every organization.

### What is the annotated asset limit in Purview?

The free version allows 1,000 annotated assets. An asset counts once, even if it has both a description and a classification.

### Can I get support for the Purview free version?

No. You cannot open support tickets on the free version. Upgrade to the enterprise version for support.
