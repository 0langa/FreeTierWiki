You install the CodSpeed GitHub App, add benchmarks to your tests, and run them in CI. CodSpeed then comments on each pull request with a performance report. It suits library authors who care about speed.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever, at $0 per user per month. It allows unlimited runs and unlimited repositories. It also includes CI integration, pull request reports and checks, and the wall-time instrument with 600 macro runner minutes per month. Benchmark history is kept for 3 months.

Users are capped at 5 on private repositories. CodSpeed is free and unlimited on public repositories, with no limit on the number of users. A user is anyone who triggers a run on a private repository of an organization, or opens that organization's dashboard. Bot accounts do not count as users.

## When you hit a limit

On a private repository, the Free plan covers up to 5 users. For more, you need the Pro plan, where each active user takes one seat. A user without a seat is disabled, loses access to CodSpeed for that organization, and sees their runs blocked on private repositories.

For macro runners, the Free plan lists 600 minutes per month. The Pro plan lists the same 600 minutes and then $0.032 per minute. You can set a monthly budget for macro runner usage in the Billing tab. The pricing page does not say what happens on the Free plan after the 600 minutes.

## Gotchas

- The 5-user cap applies to private repositories only. Public repositories have no user cap.
- A person who only opens the organization dashboard counts as a user.
- Pro costs $15 per user per month and adds unlimited history. It has a 14-day free trial.

## Questions people ask

### Is CodSpeed free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and gives unlimited runs and repositories. Up to 5 users are included on private repositories, and public repositories have no user limit.

### Is CodSpeed free on private repositories?

Yes, for up to 5 users. Beyond that, you need the Pro plan at $15 per user per month.

### What are macro runner minutes?

They are time on dedicated bare-metal machines used for the wall-time instrument. The Free plan includes 600 minutes per month.
