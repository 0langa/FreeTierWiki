You sign up in a browser and then connect log sources: you install an agent on a machine, or add an AWS account. After that you work in the web console, which has search, dashboards, and reports. It suits small security and IT teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing section on the official page lists a Free plan at $0 with no credit card. Its advertised storage amounts are 50 GB for search plus 150 GB for archival, rather than 50 GB combined. Search retention is 7 days and archival retention is 15 days.

Search storage is the hot copy you can query at once. Archival storage keeps older logs for compliance, and you pull them back with the Reload Archive Logs option. The marketing page lists three times the search allocation for archive, Basic at $120/year and Standard at $540/year. However, subscription help describes component-based Free, Professional and Enterprise editions with quotes and separate storage add-ons. Confirm the applicable edition and allocation in the account before planning deployment.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say what happens when Free storage fills. The marketing card specifies 7-day searchable and 15-day archival retention, while current help emphasizes licensed component counts and purchasable storage add-ons. The exact exhaustion action and automatic billing policy are unpublished. No-card signup is explicit, but does not establish every future account billing setting. Keep exported copies of logs you need beyond the advertised retention window.

## Gotchas

- The sign-up form still shows a 30 day free trial banner, while the pricing section shows a Free plan. Check which one your new account gets.
- Alert profiles, correlation rules, and anomaly rules appear only in the paid plan lists. The Free plan list does not show them.
- The help page says only 512 MB can be imported at once, and the storage used can be larger than the file because parsed logs also take space.

## Questions people ask

### Is ManageEngine Log360 Cloud free?

Yes, there is a Free plan at $0 with 50 GB of storage, 7 days of search retention, and 15 days of archival retention. The official page says no credit card is required.

### How long does Log360 Cloud keep logs on the free plan?

Search retention is 7 days and archival retention is 15 days on the Free plan. Paid plans list longer periods.

### Does Log360 Cloud need a credit card?

No. The pricing section says no credit card is required to get started with the free plan.
