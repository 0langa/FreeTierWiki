You sign up in a browser and then connect log sources: you install an agent on a machine, or add a cloud account. After that you work in the web console, which has search, dashboards, and reports. It suits small security and IT teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The help pages list three editions: Free, Professional, and Enterprise. Free is described as available for evaluation with limited component counts. It includes Store and Search, Auditing and Reporting, Compliance Reporting, Customizable Dashboards, and Data Export in PDF or CSV. It does not include Advanced Threat Analytics, Alerting, Incident Management, Custom Reports, Log Forwarding, customizable archival retention, or custom storage tiers.

Sign-up starts a separate 30-day free trial with full Enterprise access and no credit card. ManageEngine does not publish the Free edition's component counts, storage allocation, or retention period, so check the License page in your account. Paid editions are billed annually per component, for example $1,095 a year for 10 log sources on Professional, with search storage add-ons at $10 per GB per year.

## When you hit a limit

License help says that when a component reaches 100%, new assets of that type cannot be added until you remove one or upgrade, while log collection from sources already configured continues. When Search Storage fills up, you choose Stop Log Collection or Overwrite Old Logs under Storage Limit Preference. The help does not state these behaviors for Free specifically, and it publishes no Free allocation. Upgrades run through a quote from the License page. No-card signup is explicit, but it does not establish every future billing setting.

## Gotchas

- Sign-up starts a 30-day trial with Enterprise access, and the pages do not say what the account becomes afterward. Check your edition on the License page.
- Alerting, threat analytics, incident management, and log forwarding are listed only on the paid editions.
- The help page says only 512 MB can be imported at once, and the storage used can be larger than the file because parsed logs also take space.

## Questions people ask

### Is ManageEngine Log360 Cloud free?

Partly. The help pages list a Free edition for evaluation with core log management and limited component counts, and sign-up offers a 30-day free trial with full Enterprise access.

### How long does Log360 Cloud keep logs on the free plan?

ManageEngine does not publish a retention period for the Free edition. The License page in your account shows your storage and retention.

### Does Log360 Cloud need a credit card?

No. The Log360 Cloud page says no credit card is required to sign up, and the subscription help says the same about the 30-day trial.
