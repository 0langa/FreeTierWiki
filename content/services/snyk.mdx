You use Snyk in the web app, in an IDE, from the command line, or through your source control host, where it can check pull requests. It suits individual developers and small teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is meant for individual developers and small teams. It gives access to Snyk Open Source (dependency scanning), Snyk Code (static analysis), Infrastructure as Code, and Container scanning. You also get real-time code scanning and integrations with IDEs, the CLI, and source code managers.

The comparison table lists 5 projects and 100 Snyk Code tests per month. The text of the page does not show the Free limits for the other products, so look at your own account for those numbers. The Team plan is for up to 10 developers. It starts at $25 per month and raises the limits to 100 projects and 1,000 Snyk Code tests per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use up your monthly tests. The FAQ headings on test limits have no answer text in the fetched page. The Free plan has no card on the page and no pay-as-you-go price, so the page shows no charge. Higher limits come from the Team plan, and Jira integration and next business day support come with it.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has 5 projects. A repository with several manifest files can use up projects quickly.
- Snyk Evo features, such as AI pentesting and coding agent security, are not on the Free or Team plan. They need an Enterprise subscription.
- Personal access tokens for the CLI and IDE expire after at most 90 days, so set a reminder to rotate them.
