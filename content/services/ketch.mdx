Ketch is a privacy platform that covers consent management, data mapping, and marketing preference management. Its consent tools show a privacy banner, collect visitors' choices, and apply the rules of each privacy law. It suits developers setting up consent for a small business site who want more control over the banner than most free plans give.

## What the free plan gives you

Ketch Free costs $0 a month and is meant for small businesses with basic privacy banner needs. Ketch says it is a genuinely free consent management plan, not a limited-time trial, and it does not expire as long as monthly unique visitors stay under 5,000.

It includes unlimited banner customization, privacy law templates for every major jurisdiction, website tracker scanning, two no-code integrations, and email support, with no credit card required to start. Full consent management platform functionality is included at every tier. Ketch prices consent management on average monthly unique users across web and app properties, whether or not a visitor interacts with the banner.

## When you hit a limit

Once visitor volume passes 5,000 unique users per month, Ketch says moving to a paid plan is required. The page does not say whether the banner keeps running in the meantime or is switched off.

Because no card is needed for the Free plan, there is no automatic charge; the cost comes from the upgrade. Starter is $150 a month billed monthly for up to 30,000 unique users, and Plus starts at $499 a month billed annually for up to 100,000 unique users. Pro has custom pricing.

## Gotchas

- Every unique visitor counts, including those who never touch the banner, so the 5,000 limit tracks your real traffic.
- The jump from Free to Starter is $150 a month, a big step for a small site.
- The Free plan includes two no-code integrations, so plan which tools need consent signals first.
