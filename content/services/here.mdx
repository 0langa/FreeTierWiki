HERE provides map rendering, geocoding, search, routing, traffic and other location APIs. The public documentation distinguishes a no-payment Limited Plan from the Base Plan. Choose the account plan deliberately: free access and paid transaction billing are different arrangements.

## What the free plan gives you

The current official rate-limits page documents Limited Plan customers who do not provide payment information. It specifies 1000 daily requests, with additional per-service requests-per-second ceilings. Geocode, reverse geocode, discover/search, autocomplete and autosuggest allow 5 requests/second. Vector and raster tiles allow 50, map tiles 30 and map images 2.

Car, bicycle, pedestrian, truck and several other routing services allow 10 requests/second; matrix and isoline routing allow 1. These rate ceilings are additional to the daily allowance, not extra daily quotas.

Base uses separate product-specific monthly tiers. Its pricing details list the first 30,000 vector-tile transactions as free and a separate first 30,000 geocode/reverse-geocode transactions as free, with one request counted as one transaction for those products. Other APIs have different allowances. These Base tiers are not additional no-payment Limited entitlements; confirm the plan and product before using them.

## When you hit a limit

The Limited Plan documentation states daily and per-second limits but does not specify the response code, reset timezone, or exact suspension behavior when they are exhausted. Its no-payment scope supports starting without payment details; it does not prove every Base Plan is free or capped.

Base has transaction-based billing and no daily transaction limit. Usage beyond each product's free tier is chargeable; the public prices are indicative and the actual agreement determines final prices. Before moving from Limited to Base, review the displayed allowances, prices and payment requirements. The reviewed pricing pages do not establish an optional spending cap or the precise transition process.

## Gotchas

- Asset management, usage-based insurance/telematics and optimization are excluded use cases on Limited and Base. The Base restrictions specifically permit HERE Tour Planning for optimization; this does not grant general tracking or telematics rights.
- Each separate application needs its own app ID. Mobile/server versions and applications for different customers cannot share one ID; unauthorized use can create fee liability.
- Per-second limits and Base monthly tiers vary by product: a map-tile allowance does not imply the same allowance for matrix routing or geocoding. Permanent Geocoding is excluded from Base.

## Questions people ask

### Can I start HERE without payment information?

Yes. The official rate-limits page documents Limited Plan customers without payment information, with 1000 daily requests and service-specific rate ceilings.

### Is Base the same as Limited?

No. The documentation names them separately, and Base subscription terms can include transaction charges. Confirm current quantities and prices before activating paid access.

### Can I use these plans for fleet tracking?

Asset management and telematics are excluded by the published use-case definitions. Base permits Tour Planning for optimization, but that exception does not include general fleet tracking. Contact HERE for the applicable agreement.
