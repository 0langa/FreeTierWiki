HERE provides map rendering, geocoding, search, routing, traffic, and other location APIs. Its Base Plan gives developers monthly free transactions for individual products and bills for higher usage.

## What the free plan gives you

The Base Plan requires a payment method during onboarding. You get 30,000 vector-tile transactions each month and a separate 30,000 transactions for forward and reverse geocoding combined. Each request to one of those products counts as one transaction, and other HERE APIs have their own quotas and pricing.

The old Limited Plan gave access without a card. HERE stopped offering it to new customers on March 27, 2025 and shut it down on August 31, 2025. Its legacy restrictions page is still online.

## When you hit a limit

HERE charges for transactions above the monthly free tier of each product. For monthly usage from 30,001 through 5 million transactions, the calculator lists $0.088 per 1,000 vector-tile requests and $0.88 per 1,000 geocoding requests. Its prices are estimates and the actual rate depends on your agreement with HERE. The documented free quotas are billing thresholds. The public pricing materials do not establish a spending cap.

## Gotchas

- Base excludes asset management, telematics, and optimization. The published exception allows HERE Tour Planning for optimization.
- Register a separate app ID for each app, including mobile and server versions. HERE holds you responsible for fees from unauthorized use of your app IDs.
- Permanent Geocoding is excluded from Base and needs separate pricing from HERE.

## Questions people ask

### Can I start HERE without payment information?

HERE requires a payment method to activate Base. It retired the old no-card Limited Plan on August 31, 2025.

### How many free geocoding requests does HERE Base include?

The Base Plan gives you a combined 30,000 free forward and reverse geocoding transactions per month. Each request counts as one transaction, and requests above that allowance are billed.

### Can I use Base for fleet tracking?

Base excludes asset management and telematics. Its published exception allows HERE Tour Planning for optimization. General fleet tracking needs a separate agreement.
