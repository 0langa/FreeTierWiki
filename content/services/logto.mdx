You configure sign-in in the Logto Console and add a Logto SDK to your app, which redirects users to a Logto sign-in page. It suits developers who want standard OIDC login and the choice to run it themselves later.

## What the free plan gives you

Free costs $0 without a card for 50,000 MAU and a separate 50,000-access-token allowance. It includes 3 total apps, 1 machine-to-machine app, 1 custom domain, 3 social connectors and 3-day audit logs. RBAC, Organizations, MFA and enterprise SSO are paid add-ons excluded from Free. Pro starts at $24/month with unlimited total apps.

New accounts start in Development tenants, with premium testing features, separate quotas and a sign-in banner. These delete users older than 90 days and must not serve production. Create a separate Production tenant on Free for live use; converting an existing Development tenant requires a paid Production plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe what happens when you pass 50,000 monthly active users, 50,000 access tokens, or the application limits. It points to upgrades. In the console, Logto shows prompts when it sees you need a higher quota or a premium feature, and you pick a plan under Settings, then Your plan and billing.

Upgrades and downgrades take effect at once. If you downgrade, your quota shifts straight to the Free plan quota. Check the console before you add a fourth application or a second custom domain.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan allows 3 total applications. A web app, a mobile app, and a backend can use them up.
- Audit logs are kept for only 3 days on the Free plan.
- RBAC, organizations, MFA, and enterprise SSO are listed as paid add-ons. Plan for this if you build a B2B product.

## Questions people ask

### Is Logto free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 per month for up to 50,000 monthly active users, with no credit card required.

### Can I self-host Logto?

Yes. The docs say you can self-host Logto on your own infrastructure, in addition to using Logto Cloud.

### Does Logto Free include SSO and RBAC?

No. RBAC, organizations, MFA, and enterprise SSO are listed as add-ons billed separately. The Free plan covers core sign-in.
