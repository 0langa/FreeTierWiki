You configure sign-in in the Logto Console and add a Logto SDK to your app, which redirects users to a Logto sign-in page. It suits developers who want standard OIDC login and the choice to run it themselves later.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month, needs no credit card, and covers up to 50,000 monthly active users. It is meant for starting out and trying Logto. Your plan includes 3 total applications, 1 machine-to-machine app, 1 custom domain, and 3 social connectors. Audit logs are kept for 3 days.

The Free plan does not include the RBAC add-on, the Organizations add-on, or enterprise SSO. These are paid options. The Pro plan starts at $24 per month and lists unlimited total applications. Logto Cloud also has a dev tenant that includes every Logto feature for testing.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe what happens when you pass 50,000 monthly active users or the application limits. It points to upgrades. In the console, Logto shows prompts when it sees you need a higher quota or a premium feature, and you pick a plan under Settings, then Your plan and billing.

Upgrades and downgrades take effect at once. If you downgrade, your quota shifts straight to the Free plan quota. Check the console before you add a fourth application or a second custom domain.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan allows 3 total applications. A web app, a mobile app, and a backend can use them up.
- Audit logs are kept for only 3 days on the Free plan.
- RBAC, organizations, and enterprise SSO need paid add-ons. Plan for this if you build a B2B product.

## Questions people ask

### Is Logto free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 per month for up to 50,000 monthly active users, with no credit card required.

### Can I self-host Logto?

Yes. The docs say you can self-host Logto on your own infrastructure, in addition to using Logto Cloud.

### Does Logto Free include SSO and RBAC?

No. RBAC and organizations are paid add-ons, and enterprise SSO is listed with the paid features. The Free plan covers core sign-in.
