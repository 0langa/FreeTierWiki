Cloud Logging stores the logs your Google Cloud services write and lets you search, analyze, and route them. Logs land in log buckets, where you query them in Logs Explorer or with SQL in Log Analytics. It suits developers whose apps already run on Google Cloud and who want logs in the same console.

## What the free plan gives you

Cloud Logging is part of the Google Cloud Free Tier, priced on the Google Cloud Observability pricing page. The first 50 GiB of logs per project each month is free. Storage is a one-time charge for streaming logs into a log bucket, and it includes up to 30 days of storage.

There is no additional charge for querying and analyzing log data, for upgrading a bucket to Log Analytics and running SQL queries, for routing logs, or for using the Cloud Logging API. Logs in the _Required log bucket are not charged and are kept for a fixed 400 days.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a stop at 50 GiB. Logs past the free amount are billed at $0.50/GiB as they are streamed into log bucket storage, and vended network logs at $0.25/GiB.

For the _Default bucket and user-defined buckets, keeping logs longer than the default retention period of 30 days costs $0.01 per GiB per month. Logs kept only for the default period have no retention cost. Storage volume counts the size of the log entries before indexing.

## Gotchas

- The 50 GiB free amount is per project, so a busy project can pay while a quiet one stays free.
- Raising retention past 30 days adds a monthly charge for every GiB you keep.
- Routing is free in Cloud Logging, but the destination you route logs to bills its own storage.

## Questions people ask

### Is Google Cloud Logging free?

Up to 50 GiB of logs per project each month, kept for 30 days. Logs past that are billed per GiB.

### Does querying logs cost extra?

No. Querying and analyzing log data, including SQL in Log Analytics, has no additional charge.

### How long are logs kept for free?

Logs in the _Default bucket and user-defined buckets are kept for the default 30 days at no retention cost. Logs in the _Required bucket are kept for 400 days at no charge.
