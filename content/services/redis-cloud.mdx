You manage the database in the Redis Cloud console and connect with Redis Insight, `redis-cli`, or a Redis client library. It suits students and developers who want a hosted Redis without installing anything.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Free plan as always $0, with up to 30 MB on a shared cloud deployment. You get a single database, with a best-effort SLA and community support. The docs add that the database allows 30 concurrent connections. You can choose AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure as the cloud vendor.

A free plan is a type of Essentials plan, built for training and prototyping. You can have only one free database per account. To create a new one, delete the old one or upgrade it to a paid Essentials plan. Upgrades keep your data, with no loss.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say what happens when you fill the 30 MB or pass the 30 connections. The docs say connections beyond the limit may be limited when capacity is reached, to keep the service stable. No page shows a charge for the Free plan.

If you outgrow it, the paid Essentials plans start from $0.007 an hour, with a total of $5 a month, and range from 250 MB up. You need to add a payment method when you upgrade a free plan.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan allows only 30 concurrent connections. Use connection pooling in your client if your app opens many.
- The free tier has no replication or auto-failover, so treat the data as disposable.
- The Free plan has no REST API for managing the database. Use the console instead.

## Questions people ask

### Is Redis Cloud free?

Yes. The Free plan is always $0 and gives one 30 MB database on a shared cloud deployment. You can have only one free database per account.

### Does Redis Cloud need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan, based on the sign-up steps in the docs, which ask only for a Google or GitHub login or a form. You need a payment method if you upgrade to a paid plan.

### How many connections does the Redis Cloud free plan allow?

The docs list 30 concurrent connections for the free Essentials plan. Paid Essentials plans allow 256 or more.
