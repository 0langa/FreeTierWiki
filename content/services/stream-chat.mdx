You add chat with a client SDK in your app and a small server that creates user tokens. A dashboard manages the app and its keys. It suits developers who want chat features quickly.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is called Build. It gives you 1,000 monthly active users and 100 concurrent connections. A monthly active user is anyone who connected to chat within the last calendar month. A concurrent connection is an open WebSocket between a device and Stream, and the peak in the billing month counts.

The plan includes all Chat features and community support. It asks for no credit card. Monthly allowances are up to 1M API calls, up to 100K query channel calls, and up to 10 GB of API bandwidth.

Makers get more. If your team has fewer than 5 people and under $10k in monthly revenue, Stream offers a $100 monthly credit. The page says availability is limited.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing table shows overage rates for the Free plan as "Limits Apply" and does not say what happens next. Paid plans list rates, such as $0.09 per extra monthly active user. The pricing page does not say whether free apps stop or slow down at the cap.

The next plan up is Start at $399 per month billed annually, or $499 billed monthly. It covers 10,000 monthly active users and 500 concurrent connections.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page tells you that cron jobs polling query channels are the most common cause of overage on the query channel limit.
- Messages are stored for as long as your plan is active.
- Maker credit is limited in availability, so do not plan around it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stream Chat free?

Yes. The Free plan covers 1,000 monthly active users and 100 concurrent connections, includes all Chat features, and needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Stream Chat free limits?

The pricing page lists the Free plan overage as "Limits Apply" and does not explain it. Paid plans show per-unit overage rates.

### Does Stream Chat need a credit card?

No. The Free plan page says no credit card is required.
