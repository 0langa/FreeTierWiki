You add chat with a client SDK in your app and a small server that creates user tokens. A dashboard manages the app and its keys. It suits developers who want chat features quickly.

## What the free plan gives you

Build includes 1,000 MAU and 100 concurrent connections without a card. MAU counts users connected in the previous calendar month; concurrency measures peak open device-to-Stream WebSockets during the billing month.

Core chat, SDKs, UI components and community support are included; multi-tenancy/automatic translation require paid plans. Monthly limits are 1M API calls, 100K query-channel calls and 10 GB API bandwidth, including responses, WebSocket messages and server webhooks. Message storage, channel counts and image/file CDN allowances say Limits Apply without figures.

Eligible makers with fewer than 5 team members and under $10k monthly revenue can receive $100 monthly credit, subject to limited availability.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing table shows overage rates for the Free plan as "Limits Apply" and does not say what happens next. Paid plans list rates, such as $0.09 per extra monthly active user. The pricing page does not say whether free apps stop or slow down at the cap.

The next plan up is Start at $399 per month billed annually, or $499 billed monthly. It covers 10,000 monthly active users and 500 concurrent connections.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page tells you that cron jobs polling query channels are the most common cause of overage on the query channel limit. Messages are stored for as long as your plan is active, subject to the plan storage allowance; Free does not publish that quantity.
- One user can hold several WebSocket connections across devices or through duplicate connections, so 100 concurrent connections is not necessarily 100 people.
- Maker credit is limited in availability, so do not plan around it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stream Chat free?

Yes. The Free plan covers 1,000 monthly active users and 100 concurrent connections, includes core chat features, and needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Stream Chat free limits?

The pricing page lists the Free plan overage as "Limits Apply" and does not explain it. Paid plans show per-unit overage rates.

### Does Stream Chat need a credit card?

No. The Free plan page says no credit card is required.
