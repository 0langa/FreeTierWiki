You manage CacheFly from its control panel, and the FAQ says an API covers cache purging, analytics, and configuration changes. The free account suits one small site or download area that needs a global cache.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan costs $0 a month. The main pricing card says it includes up to 100 GB of free bandwidth each month. It covers one domain, email support, and Universal SSL, and you can also bring your own certificate. API access is included.

Security and edge features are limited. The comparison table shows no WAF, no single sign-on, no WebSockets, no HTTP/3, and basic logs and DDoS protection. It lists a 99.95% SLA for the Developer plan.

The pricing page contradicts itself on size. A second free card on the same page says 5TB of data and 19 PoPs, with overage on pay-as-you-go. This entry uses the 100 GB figure from the Developer card, because that card also gives a price for extra data.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a hard stop. The Developer card gives an overage rate of 5 cents per GB, and the free page says you can later move to a pay-as-you-go plan. Plan for extra traffic to be billed, not blocked.

The sign-up note on the free page says self-signup plans need a credit card at registration. CacheFly charges and refunds $1 to check your future payment method, and it says you are not charged during the free trial period. The pages do not say how long that period lasts.

## Gotchas

- You must add a credit card to sign up, even though the plan costs nothing. The $1 check is refunded.
- The free plan covers one domain. The Business plan allows 50 domains and starts at $300 a month.
- Free-plan limits differ across the pricing page. Check the control panel for the allowance on your own account before you rely on it.

## Questions people ask

### Is CacheFly free?

Yes, there is a free Developer account. The main pricing card gives up to 100 GB of free data each month for one domain. A second card on the same page says 5TB.

### What happens when I go over the CacheFly free limit?

The pricing page lists an overage rate of 5 cents per GB and says pay-as-you-go applies. It does not say the service stops.

### Does CacheFly need a credit card?

Yes. Self-signup plans need a card at registration. CacheFly charges $1 and refunds it to check the card.
