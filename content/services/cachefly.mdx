You manage CacheFly from its control panel, and the FAQ says an API covers cache purging, analytics reporting, and configuration changes. The free account suits one small site or download area that needs a global cache, and it is mainly a way to test the network before a paid commit.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says a free developer account is available and sums it up in one line: start free with 100 GB and one domain. It does not list the features of the free account beyond that, and it no longer shows a comparison table for a Developer plan.

The FAQ describes features that apply across CacheFly. It says you get full SSL and TLS support, including bring your own certificates and automatic certificate issuance, and that an API can purge cache, pull analytics, and change configuration.

The paid tiers are where the page puts the bigger features. Adoption costs $299 a month and includes 3 domains, 5 origins, and 10 TB a month. Production adds WAF and DDoS options and WebSockets, and Governance adds SSO, RBAC, and audit logs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after the 100 GB. It does not give an overage rate for the free account, and it does not say whether the allowance resets each month. The free page says that later you can select pay-as-you-go plans or a special pricing contract.

The sign-up note on the free page says self-signup plans need a credit card at registration to streamline future billing. CacheFly charges and refunds $1 to check your payment method, and it says you are not charged during your free trial period. The pages do not say how long that period lasts, so watch your usage in the control panel.

## Gotchas

- You must add a credit card to sign up, even though the account costs nothing. The $1 check is refunded.
- The free account covers one domain. The cheapest paid tier, Adoption, allows 3 domains and costs $299 a month.
- The free terms are thin: no overage rate, no reset period, and no trial length are published, so confirm them in the control panel.

## Questions people ask

### Is CacheFly free?

Yes, there is a free developer account. The pricing page describes it as 100 GB and one domain, and the free page calls it an all-access developer account.

### What happens when I go over the CacheFly free limit?

The pricing page does not say. It gives no overage rate for the free account, and the free page only says you can later pick pay-as-you-go plans.

### Does CacheFly need a credit card?

Yes. Self-signup plans need a card at registration. CacheFly charges $1 and refunds it to check the card.
