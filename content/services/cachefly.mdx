CacheFly's free "Developer" tier suits small static content delivery, with a 100 GB monthly bandwidth cap. It's ideal for indie developers and side projects needing fast, reliable content distribution without upfront costs. Overages are billed automatically at $0.05/GB, so monitor usage if you expect high traffic.
