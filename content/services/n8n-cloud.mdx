n8n is a workflow automation tool: you connect triggers, apps, code, and AI models in a visual editor, and it runs the workflow when something happens. n8n Cloud is the version n8n hosts for you. It suits developers who want automations with the option to drop into code.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free Cloud plan, but the Starter and Pro plans each offer a free trial with no credit card required. The FAQ says a Cloud trial includes Pro-level features such as global variables, insights with 14-day history, 5-day workflow history, advanced execution filters, unlimited evaluations, folders, and the Assistant.

Trial limits are 1,000 executions, 5 concurrent executions, a 180-second execution timeout, and 1 team project, with 800 Assistant credits plus 100 AI credits. Admin roles, the project editor role, named versions, workflow diffs, and the n8n public API are not included. All plans include unlimited users, unlimited workflows, and every integration, and executions are counted per full workflow run, not per step.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say how long the Cloud trial lasts or what happens when its 1,000 executions run out. It says trial Assistant credits expire when the trial ends. After the trial, Starter is 20€ a month billed annually for 2.5K executions, and Pro is 50€ a month billed annually for 10K executions. Monthly billing costs more; the page says annual billing saves 17%.

## Gotchas

- The 180-second execution timeout in the trial can stop long workflows.
- A credit card is required for the Business plan trial, unlike the Cloud trial.
- The public API is not available during the trial.

## Questions people ask

### Is n8n Cloud free?

Not permanently. It has a free trial with no credit card, limited to 1,000 executions, and paid plans after that.

### Does the trial need a credit card?

No. The FAQ says no credit card is required for the n8n Cloud trial; only the Business plan trial needs one.

### What counts as an execution?

One full run of a workflow, regardless of how many steps it has.
