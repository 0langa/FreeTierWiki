Spacelift is a platform for running infrastructure as code. It connects to your Git repository and runs OpenTofu, Terraform, Terragrunt, Pulumi, or Kubernetes changes on workers, with plan and approval policies, state management, and an audit trail. It suits teams that have outgrown running infrastructure tools from laptops or plain CI jobs.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free plan that is always free for small teams, with no credit card and no time limit. Its FAQ says the Free plan includes 2 users and 1 public worker. Public workers are run by Spacelift in a shared, secure pool, and each customer run executes in its own isolated container instance without access to other accounts' source or state.

The pricing FAQ says Free, Starter, and Starter+ all provide access to public workers. The page does not list which policy, scheduling, or registry features each paid plan adds beyond workers and users, so check the feature table before you rely on one.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan's limits are users and workers rather than metered usage. The pricing page does not say what happens if you need more than 1 public worker on Free, such as runs queuing; it points to upgrading instead.

Starter+ is an annual subscription with unlimited users, 2 public workers, and 1 or 2 private workers, which run inside your own cloud. You can cancel it up to 14 days after an executed contract if the software has not been activated; after that it cannot be cancelled. Business and Enterprise plans add more private workers and are quoted by sales.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has 1 public worker, and the pricing page does not say how many runs it handles at once, so busy stacks may wait.
- Public workers run in Spacelift's shared pool, so infrastructure that is only reachable from your private network needs a private worker on a paid plan.
- Paid plans are annual commitments, so moving off Free is a bigger step than a monthly upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is Spacelift free?

Yes. The Free plan is always free for small teams, with no credit card and no time limit, and includes 2 users and 1 public worker.

### What is a public worker?

A worker is the compute that runs your infrastructure code. Public workers are run by Spacelift in a shared pool, with each run isolated in its own container.

### Can I run Spacelift jobs inside my own network for free?

No. Private workers, which run inside your own cloud, start with the Starter+ plan.
