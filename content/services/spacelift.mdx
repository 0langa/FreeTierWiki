Spacelift is a platform for running infrastructure as code. It connects to your Git repository and runs OpenTofu, Terraform, Terragrunt, Pulumi, or Kubernetes changes on workers, with plan and approval policies, state management, and an audit trail. It suits teams that have outgrown running infrastructure tools from laptops or plain CI jobs.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a $0 plan that is free forever for individuals and small teams. It includes 2 users, 1 API key, spaces, IaC support, workflow customization, VCS and cloud integrations, OIDC SSO, cloud cost estimation, dependencies, resource visualization, and the Spacelift Terraform provider. Signup advertises a 14-day trial with no credit card.

Spacelift uses a public worker pool by default, with each run isolated from other accounts. The current Free card no longer specifies a worker count. Starter starts at $399 per month with 10 users and 2 public workers, adding private modules, webhooks, policies, notifications, and custom tasks. Business lists public or private workers and unlimited users.

## When you hit a limit

The reviewed pages do not specify how Free enforces its user/API-key caps or its exact worker allowance. Worker documentation describes queues and says each worker processes one run at a time; confirm Free concurrency before relying on it.

The pricing page mentions monthly Starter subscriptions, but its Starter FAQ describes an annual subscription. Annual cancellation is allowed only within 14 days of an executed contract before activation. Confirm the term before purchasing. Private workers need the Business plan or above; the worker documentation also lists the legacy Starter+ plan.

## Gotchas

- The current pricing page omits Free's worker count; do not assume the older one-worker allowance.
- Public workers run in Spacelift's shared pool, so infrastructure that is only reachable from your private network needs a private worker on a paid plan.
- The paid billing FAQ conflicts on monthly versus annual terms; annual contracts have strict cancellation conditions.

## Questions people ask

### Is Spacelift free?

Yes. The Free plan is always free for small teams, with no credit card and no time limit, and includes 2 users and 1 API key. Signup offers a no-card 14-day trial.

### What is a public worker?

A worker is the compute that runs your infrastructure code. Public workers are run by Spacelift in a shared pool, with each run isolated in its own container.

### Can I run Spacelift jobs inside my own network for free?

No. Private workers need the Business plan or above, or the legacy Starter+ plan, so they are not part of Free.
