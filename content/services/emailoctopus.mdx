You use EmailOctopus in its web app, with an API for working with lists and contacts from code. It suits people who run a newsletter and want a free plan with a visual editor.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free. It includes 2,500 subscribers and 10,000 emails per month. You get 1 landing page, 1 form, and access for 1 user. Campaign reports are available for 30 days.

Emails sent on the free plan carry EmailOctopus branding. Support is included, with email support and chat support for the first 30 days. The pricing page says there is no time limit and no credit card needed. You can use the drag and drop editor or write your own HTML.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that if your list grows beyond your current plan, EmailOctopus moves you up to the plan that fits your new contact size. The help center adds that upgrades happen automatically based on your list size, and that you are charged the price difference.

Sending limits work separately. Your plan is based on subscribers as long as you stay within your sending limit, and you need a plan with more sending if you send more emails. The page does not say what the free plan does at 10,000 emails.

## Gotchas

- Subscribers are counted per list. If the same person is on two lists, they count twice, so keep contacts in a single list.
- Only subscribed contacts count. Unsubscribed contacts and contacts removed for bounces or complaints do not.
- Plans move up automatically as your list grows, so a growing list can lead to a paid plan without a manual choice.

## Questions people ask

### Is EmailOctopus free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free for up to 2,500 subscribers and 10,000 emails per month, with no time limit and no credit card.

### Does EmailOctopus have a free trial?

No. It does not offer a free trial, and recommends starting on the free plan, which has no time limit.

### What happens if I exceed my EmailOctopus contact limit?

EmailOctopus moves you up to the plan that fits your new contact size. You pay the price difference for the upgrade.
