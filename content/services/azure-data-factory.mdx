You build pipelines in the Azure Data Factory Studio web interface, and the factory runs them on a schedule or a trigger. It suits data engineers and small teams who already use Azure.

## What the free plan gives you

Azure Data Factory has one free amount: the first 5 low frequency activities in a month are free, in both the cloud and on-premises variants. A low frequency activity does not run more than once in a day. Daily, weekly, and monthly schedules count as low frequency.

The pricing page says there is no charge for the first five cloud and the first five on-premises activities. Storage and HDInsight are billed separately at their own rates. High frequency activities, such as hourly runs, are not part of the free amount. The pricing page you can read here shows the price tables only as placeholders, so this page gives no dollar rates. Check the Azure pricing calculator for them.

## When you hit a limit

There is no hard cap. Activities beyond the free 5 are billed, and nothing stops a pipeline from running past the free amount. Because you need an Azure payment method, a bill is the likely result of going over.

The pricing page notes that usage beyond 100 activities a month gets a 20% discount, for both low and high frequency activities. That means the discount starts only after you already pay for activities. Keep each activity on a daily or slower schedule to stay inside the free amount. Watch your Azure cost reports during your first month.

## Gotchas

- Microsoft now calls Data Factory in Microsoft Fabric the next generation of Azure Data Factory, and says new users should start with it. Existing factories can upgrade.
- The free amount covers only low frequency activities. An hourly or every-15-minute activity is billed from the first run.
- Studio works only in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, and the factory name must be globally unique.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Data Factory free?

Only a small part. The first 5 low frequency activities each month are free, and everything above that is billed.

### Does Azure Data Factory need a credit card?

You need an Azure subscription, and Azure offers a free account to start. This entry lists a payment method as required, and the pricing page does not say otherwise.

### What is a low frequency activity in Azure Data Factory?

It is an activity that runs no more than once a day, such as a daily, weekly, or monthly run. Activities that run more often count as high frequency and are not free.
