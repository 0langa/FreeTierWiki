You design pipelines in Data Factory Studio and run activities to move or transform data. The current V2 service is consumption billed. This listing covers a short evaluation funded by eligible Azure signup credit, rather than a recurring free pipeline plan.

## What the free plan gives you

Eligible new Azure customers receive $200 of credit in their billing currency for the first 30 days. Microsoft's free-account documentation says it applies to any Azure service except third-party Marketplace purchases. That includes first-party Data Factory V2 usage, but the credit is shared across your Azure subscription, not $200 reserved for this product.

Data Factory bills orchestration activity runs, data movement and integration-runtime compute according to their meters. Microsoft's cost-management guide explicitly shows Azure Data Factory v2 costs. No ongoing V2 pipeline allowance is established by the reviewed current sources. The pricing page's first-five low-frequency activities belong to its legacy V1 section; selecting V2 does not make those rows applicable.

## When you hit a limit

Signup credit expires after 30 days, even if unused. If the credit runs out sooner, or the 30 days end, Azure disables services unless you choose to upgrade to pay-as-you-go. Azure says it does not charge your card without that choice.

After upgrading, remaining credit lasts only until the original expiry date. Usage beyond applicable credits and free service quantities is billed. A daily pipeline schedule is not a spending cap: runtime duration, data movement and connected resources can all consume credit. Use Cost Management to inspect actual charges and remove test resources when finished.

## Gotchas

- The credit is a new-customer offer with eligibility restrictions, not a monthly reset. Other Azure services consume the same balance.
- Source and destination storage, network transfer and other infrastructure can add costs. A Data Factory budget alert does not automatically stop every resource.
- Microsoft recommends Fabric Data Factory for new data-integration users. Its product and trial terms are separate from this Azure Data Factory V2 evaluation.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Data Factory V2 free forever?

No ongoing V2 free pipeline allowance is confirmed here. Eligible new customers can pay for a short evaluation with their shared $200 Azure signup credit, valid for 30 days.

### Does this offer require a card?

Yes. Azure signup asks for a phone number and a non-prepaid credit or debit card, and may place a temporary one-dollar authorization hold.

### Do five daily activities stay free on V2?

The five low-frequency activities shown on the pricing page belong to legacy V1 pricing. They do not establish a free allowance for a new V2 factory.
