Seline is a web and product analytics tool that works without cookies. It is based and hosted in the EU and says it complies with the GDPR. It suits developers and small businesses who want simple analytics with events, funnels, and user journeys.

## What the free plan gives you

Seline has no free plan; its pricing FAQ says so directly, because the team runs only on subscriber fees. What it offers is a 7-day free trial, and you sign up with no credit card required.

The trial covers the single Pro plan, which has no locked features or tiers. Pro includes forever data retention, profiles and journeys, a public API, unlimited websites, revenue analytics, and advanced bot detection. Pro starts at $9 a month, and pageviews and custom events share one monthly event limit; for example, 25,000 events a month costs $14 a month. Yearly billing gives 2 months free.

## When you hit a limit

The trial ends after 7 days. The pricing page does not say what happens to your account or data if you do not subscribe.

On the Pro plan, Seline is lenient about traffic. If you reach your monthly limit for the first time, it notifies you but takes no action, since traffic spikes happen. It starts asking for an upgrade if you exceed your limit for two consecutive months, or if you go 5 times over your limit. It bases counting on your regular monthly activity.

## Gotchas

- There is no free plan to fall back to after the 7 days.
- Pricing is per website; to share one event limit across sites, link them under a single Pro subscription.
- Pageviews and custom events share the same event allowance, so estimate both before choosing a volume.

## Questions people ask

### Does Seline have a free plan?

No. Seline says it does not have a free plan, but it offers a 7-day free trial.

### Do I need a credit card for the Seline trial?

No. The pricing page says the 7-day trial needs no credit card.

### What happens if I go over my event limit?

The first time, Seline notifies you and takes no action. It asks for an upgrade after two consecutive months over, or if you go 5 times over.
