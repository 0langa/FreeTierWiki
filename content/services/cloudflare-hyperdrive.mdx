You set Hyperdrive up with the Wrangler CLI and a binding in your Worker, then use your usual database driver. It suits Workers developers who already have a database somewhere else.

## What the free plan gives you

Hyperdrive is included in the Workers Free plan with 100,000 database queries per day. A query means any statement through Hyperdrive, including selects, inserts, updates, deletes, and schema changes. Cached and uncached queries count the same. Limits reset daily at 00:00 UTC.

Connection pooling and query caching are included at no extra charge. On the Free plan you can configure 10 databases per account, with about 20 connections to the origin database per configuration. A single query can run for 60 seconds, and cached responses can be up to 50 MB.

## When you hit a limit

Once you pass the daily query limit, further operations of that type fail with an error. Nothing is billed on the Free plan, so you cannot run up a Hyperdrive bill by accident. Your own database provider still bills you under its own terms. The limits reset at 00:00 UTC, or you can move to the Workers Paid plan, where Cloudflare adjusts Hyperdrive limits for you and queries are unlimited.

## Gotchas

- Hyperdrive does not host data. You pay your database provider separately.
- Cached reads are not invalidated when you write, so use a separate cache-disabled configuration for reads that must be fresh.
- Hyperdrive limits origin connections, and long-running queries or transactions can exhaust the pool.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Hyperdrive free?

Yes, on the Workers Free plan, up to 100,000 database queries per day. Pooling and caching add no charge.

### What happens when I exceed the Hyperdrive free limit?

Further operations of that type fail with an error until the daily reset at 00:00 UTC. Upgrading to Workers Paid removes the daily cap.

### Does Hyperdrive work with MySQL?

Yes. Hyperdrive works with PostgreSQL, MySQL, and many compatible databases, including CockroachDB and PlanetScale.
