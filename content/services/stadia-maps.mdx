Stadia Maps sells hosted basemaps, geocoding and search, and routing built largely on open data. Every API call costs a set number of credits, and each plan includes a fixed monthly credit pool. The Free plan suits hobby, research, and open source projects that are not commercial.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 200,000 credits a month, standard basemaps, and basic APIs only. Credits are spent per call: a standard vector or raster basemap tile costs 1 credit, forward and reverse geocoding cost 20 credits per request, autocomplete search v2 costs 1 credit, and standard routing costs 20 credits per request. That means the free pool covers 200,000 map tiles, or far fewer geocoding calls. All new accounts also get a no-obligation 14-day trial of the Professional plan without a credit card, and the account stays on the Free plan afterward unless you upgrade.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan lists no additional usage, so you cannot buy more credits on it. Stadia Maps says usage caps prevent billing shock and unexpected overages. The pricing page does not describe exactly what responses you get once the credits are used. Paid plans add credits at a set price: Starter costs $20 a month for 1,000,000 credits plus 3 cents per 1,000 additional credits, and allows commercial use.

## Gotchas

- Commercial use is not allowed on the Free plan, even at low volume.
- Geocoding and routing cost 20 credits per call, so the credit pool shrinks fast for search-heavy apps.
- The Free plan lists standard basemaps only; Starter adds static maps and Standard adds satellite basemaps.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stadia Maps free?

Yes, for non-commercial use. The Free plan includes 200,000 credits a month with standard basemaps and basic APIs.

### Do I need a credit card to try Stadia Maps?

No. New accounts get a 14-day Professional trial without a credit card and stay on the Free plan afterward unless they upgrade.

### Can I use the Stadia Maps Free plan in a business app?

No. The pricing page says commercial use is not allowed on the Free plan; the Starter plan allows it.
