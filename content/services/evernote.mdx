You use Evernote in the browser, in desktop apps for Mac and Windows, or in mobile apps. It suits people who want a note app with notebooks, tags, tasks, and a calendar, and who can live with the small free limits.

## What the free plan gives you

The compare-plans page gives the Free plan these limits: 50 notes, 1 notebook, 5 spaces, 20 tags, 200 attachments, 1GB of storage, and sync on 1 device. It also shows one external calendar connection, so you can link a Google or Microsoft Office calendar to your notes. The core feature list also names tasks, the Evernote Calendar, and the Web Clipper, but the text version of the table does not show which plan has each. The apps cover iOS, Android, web, Windows, and Mac.

The paid Starter plan raises these to 1000 notes, 20 notebooks, 10 spaces, 100 tags, 1000 attachments, 5GB of storage, and 3 devices. Advanced is unlimited on notes, spaces, tags, and attachments.

## When you hit a limit

The page does not say exactly what happens when you reach 50 notes or the storage cap. The plan table shows hard counts, so expect to delete notes or upgrade to create more. To upgrade, open your account settings in Evernote Web and choose Manage Subscription; the page says paid plans take a credit card or PayPal. Paid-plan trials last 7 days, and the page says your card is not charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

## Gotchas

- The free plan syncs on 1 device only, so using a phone and a laptop together needs a paid plan.
- The notebook limit is 1, which means you organize with tags and spaces, and those are limited to 5 and 20.
- The page notes that unlimited paid limits still have safeguard limits to protect performance and prevent abuse.

## Questions people ask

### Is Evernote free?

Yes, with tight limits. The Free plan has 50 notes, 1 notebook, 1GB of storage, and sync on 1 device.

### How many notes can I have on Evernote Free?

50 notes. The Starter plan raises that to 1000, and Advanced is unlimited.

### Can I use Evernote Free on my phone and laptop?

Not at the same time. The Free plan syncs across 1 device. Starter allows 3 devices.
