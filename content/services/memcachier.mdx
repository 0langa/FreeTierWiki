MemCachier is a managed Memcache service. Memcache is an in-memory key-value store that web apps use to cache data and reduce load on their servers, and MemCachier runs and scales the clusters for you. It suits developers who want a cache for a small app without running Memcached themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan costs $0 and is described as totally free forever, to try out the service. It is fully managed with 24/7 monitoring and includes a 25MB cache, 1 proxy server, 10 connections, and basic analytics.

The getting-started guide says you tell MemCachier how much memory you need and get started for free instantly, then add capacity later. The pricing page offers a sign-up for a free cache, and you connect to it from your app with a Memcache client.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 25MB cache is full or when an eleventh connection is opened. There is no metered usage on the Developer plan, so you are not billed for using it.

To grow, you pick a paid size: Basic starts at about $14 a month for a 100MB cache and adds unlimited connections, historical analytics, a high-availability cluster, and alerting. Larger Advanced and Business plans start at about $58 and $440 a month.

## Gotchas

- The free plan allows 10 connections, which several app processes or workers can use up quickly.
- A high-availability cluster, credential management, and alerting start on the Basic plan, not the free one.
- Memcache is a cache, so keep the real copy of every value in your database.
