You use Everything Fonts in a web browser, with a separate page for each tool. It combines font inspection tools with conversion and web-font preparation. Automated conversion is available through a paid REST API.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page distinguishes completely free tools from paid tools with a free allowance. Free tools include font inspection, coverage and kerning checks, PDF font analysis, font-name checks, unit conversion, and a local font viewer. Font Doctor offers free diagnosis but charges for repair.

Font conversion, the @font-face generator, subsetting, hinting, and accenting are labelled paid with a free allowance. The converter and @font-face pages both allow uploads up to 400 KB as a guest or 1 MB with a free account. Paid subscriptions start with 10 MB uploads. The reviewed pages do not state how many free conversion jobs you receive or when that allowance renews, so do not plan on unlimited free conversions.

The free @font-face kit includes five font formats and standard CSS. Advanced format selection and custom CSS settings are paid features.

## When you hit a limit

Larger uploads require an account or paid plan. API requests can return 429 when the monthly job allowance is exhausted or a per-minute rate limit is reached. Failed jobs do not count, and response headers report the remaining allowance and its reset time.

API access comes with Premium and Enterprise, while Starter is website-only. For conversion alone, Premium costs $24.99 per month for 1,000 jobs and 50 MB uploads; Enterprise costs $49.99 for 3,000 jobs and 200 MB uploads. The broader Font Tools Premium package costs $49.99 per month for 1,500 jobs shared across tools. These are monthly prices; yearly billing offers two months free.

## Gotchas

- The free allowance for a paid tool is different from the diagnostic tools labelled entirely free.
- Confirm that your font license permits conversion and web embedding before uploading it.
- The API documentation says uploaded fonts are kept for 180 days and results for 30 days.

## Questions people ask

### Is Everything Fonts free?

Some diagnostics are free. Conversion and web-font preparation are paid tools with a free allowance whose job count is not published on the reviewed pages.

### Is the Everything Fonts API free?

No. API access requires Premium or Enterprise; Starter subscriptions only include website use.

### Do I need an account?

Guest converter uploads are allowed up to 400 KB. A free account raises the upload ceiling to 1 MB, but does not establish an unlimited conversion allowance.
