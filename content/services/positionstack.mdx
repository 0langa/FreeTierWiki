positionstack is a geocoding API from APILayer. It turns addresses and place names into coordinates and coordinates into addresses, using data sources such as OpenStreetMap. Paid plans add batch requests, embeddable maps, and commercial use. The Free plan is a small personal-use tier for trying the API.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for personal use and costs $0 a month. It includes 100 API requests, forward geocoding, reverse geocoding, and HTTPS encryption, with no support, and it is for non-commercial use. The closing call to action on the page describes it as free for 100 monthly API requests. A request that returns an error instead of data does not affect your quota. Batch requests, embeddable maps, multiple languages, and an extended rate limit appear on paid plans. The cheapest paid plan, Basic, costs $9.99 a month for 100,000 requests with commercial use.

## When you hit a limit

positionstack notifies you by email and in the account dashboard when you reach at least 75%, 90%, and 100% of the monthly allowed API volume. The FAQ then says overage fees apply after 100% so that your application keeps running. The pricing page lists a per-request overage price for paid plans, but it does not say how overage works on the Free plan or whether requests stop there. Check the dashboard before you get near 100 requests.

## Gotchas

- 100 requests a month is very small; a single test script can use it up.
- The Free plan is non-commercial, so a business app needs a paid plan from the start.
- The FAQ's overage wording is written for all plans, so confirm in your dashboard what happens on a free account.

## Questions people ask

### Is positionstack free?

Yes, with a small Free plan of 100 monthly API requests for personal, non-commercial use.

### Can I use positionstack's Free plan for a business?

No. The Free plan is listed for non-commercial use; commercial use starts with the Basic plan.

### Does a failed request count against my positionstack quota?

No. The FAQ says requests that return an error do not affect your quota.
