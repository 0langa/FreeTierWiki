You use Lumenfall with the OpenAI SDK or plain HTTP, pointed at the Lumenfall base URL, plus a web dashboard that shows each request. It suits developers who want image or video generation without opening an account with every provider.

## What the free plan gives you

Every new account gets a $1 credit the moment you sign up, and no credit card is needed. Lumenfall has no monthly fee, no subscription, and no platform fee. It charges the provider's own price with zero markup, so a request that costs a provider $0.04 costs you $0.04.

The credit works across the models Lumenfall offers. The home page lists models from providers such as OpenAI, Google, ByteDance, Alibaba, and Black Forest Labs. You also get one wallet and one invoice. Each response includes the exact cost of that request.

## When you hit a limit

Lumenfall uses a prepaid balance. When the balance is too low, requests return a 402 error with the code `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`. To continue, add funds, turn on auto top-up, or switch to postpaid billing. Top-ups can be any amount. After the $1 credit is gone, every request is billed at the provider's rate. Auto top-up and postpaid billing are optional, so leaving them off keeps you inside what you loaded.

## Gotchas

- The $1 credit is one-time. There is no monthly free allowance.
- Prices follow each provider's rate, so costs vary by model and output. In-progress video jobs show an estimated cost, and the final cost appears when they finish.
- Auto top-up and postpaid billing can charge you without a manual step, so enable them on purpose.

## Questions people ask

### Is Lumenfall free?

Partly. New accounts get a $1 credit with no credit card. After that you pay providers' prices with no markup.

### Does Lumenfall add a markup?

No. The pricing page says it charges nothing extra, with no platform fee, monthly fee, or hidden costs.

### What happens when my Lumenfall balance runs out?

Requests return a 402 insufficient balance error. You can add funds or turn on auto top-up to continue.
