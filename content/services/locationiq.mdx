LocationIQ is a geocoding and maps platform with forward and reverse geocoding, autocomplete, routing, and street and static map APIs. It counts usage in request credits per day. The Free plan suits small apps and side projects that need address lookups and can credit LocationIQ on the page.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes 5000 requests a day at up to 2 requests a second and 60 requests per minute. It covers the Geocoding APIs, the Routing APIs, and Street and Static Maps, with 2 access tokens, IP and HTTP restrictions, best-effort email support, and US and EU datacenters. A request credit covers one map view or one call to any API, such as search, reverse geocoding, or autocomplete, and unsuccessful requests are not charged. You may use the Free plan in commercial projects if you add a prominent link back to LocationIQ on your website or app.

## When you hit a limit

When you go past the daily limit, the API returns a 429 error code with a Rate Limited Day message. Most paid plans have soft limits that allow up to an additional 100% of the daily limit before that happens; the pricing page describes these for paid plans. If your account exceeds plan limits often, LocationIQ says its team will reach out with help or larger plans. The published response to exceeding the quota is rejection. The page does not explicitly state the Free payment-card requirement.

## Gotchas

- The per-second and per-minute limits are low, so batch jobs need throttling even when the daily quota has room.
- Commercial use depends on keeping the link back to LocationIQ visible.
- Free API request-response pairs may be cached for up to 48 hours; server-side map-tile caching is prohibited. The Free plan includes 2 access tokens.

## Questions people ask

### Is LocationIQ free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 5000 requests a day for geocoding, routing, and maps.

### Can I use LocationIQ's Free plan commercially?

Yes, if you add a prominent link back to LocationIQ on your website or app.

### What happens when I reach the daily limit?

The API returns a 429 error with a Rate Limited Day message until the daily quota resets.
