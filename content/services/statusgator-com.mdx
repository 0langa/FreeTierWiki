You use StatusGator in a web dashboard where a board holds your monitors and a status page. It also offers a REST API, webhooks, and an MCP server, as listed on its features page. It suits IT and DevOps teams that want to know when a vendor is down.

## What the free plan gives you

The free forever plan includes 3 monitors, 1 board with one status page, and 1 user. A monitor is one item on your status page. You also get 100 status page subscribers, 1 month of historical data, and 10 notifications per month. The plan lists chat integrations (Discord, Google Chat, Microsoft Teams, Slack, and Webex) and embed integrations (Iframe, Status Embed, and Status Modal).

New accounts can also take a 14-day free trial with all paid features. The trial needs no credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page lists 10 notifications per month as the maximum number of outage alerts on Free. It does not say what happens after you pass 10, or what happens when you try to add a fourth monitor.

Paid plans remove the notification cap. The Starter plan costs $72 per month when paid annually, or $79 per month billed monthly, and has unlimited notifications and 3 months of data.

## Gotchas

- Free has only 10 notifications per month, which a busy provider can use in a single bad day.
- Historical data covers 1 month on Free, compared with 3 months on Starter and up to 24 months on Enterprise.
- Free allows 1 user and 1 board, so a team that wants separate boards needs a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is StatusGator free?

Yes. The free forever plan includes 3 monitors, 1 board with one status page, 10 notifications per month, and 1 month of data.

### Does StatusGator need a credit card?

No. The plans page says no credit card is required, including for the 14-day free trial of paid features.

### What does StatusGator monitor?

It collects the status from the official status pages of more than 11,320 cloud services and shows them in one dashboard.
