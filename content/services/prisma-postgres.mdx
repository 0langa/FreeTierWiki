You can create a database from the web interface or with the `create-db` CLI, then connect with Prisma ORM or any Postgres client. It suits developers who want a managed Postgres database billed by operation, not by compute time.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is $0 per month, free forever, with no credit card required. Prisma Postgres on Free includes 200k operations a month, 1.01 GB of storage, and 50 databases. One query counts as one operation. Database data transfer is unlimited and free on every plan.

The Free plan also covers part of Prisma Compute, the hosting side. You get 1M requests, 360 GB-hours of memory, 4 active vCPU-hours, and 10 GB of outbound bandwidth each month. The plan allows 10 pooled connections and 10 direct connections. Support is community only.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing table shows no overage price and no spend limit for the Free plan. It does not say what happens after you use 200k operations or fill 1.01 GB. Prisma says paid plans carry a hard spend limit that is on by default, so a paid bill cannot surprise you.

For paid plans, the Starter plan costs $10 a month. It includes 1M operations and 10 GB of storage, then charges $8 per million operations and $2 per GB.

## Gotchas

- Databases made with `create-db` are temporary. Prisma deletes them after 24 hours unless you claim them in the Prisma Data Platform.
- The Free plan has no backups. Daily backups with 7-day retention start on the Starter plan.
- The Free plan allows only 10 pooled and 10 direct connections. The connection guide lists a "Too many connections" error as a common problem, so read it before you pick a connection string.

## Questions people ask

### Is Prisma Postgres free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and has no time limit. It includes 200k operations a month, 1.01 GB of storage, and 50 databases.

### What happens when I go over the Prisma Postgres free limits?

The pricing page does not say. The Free column shows no overage price and no spend limit. Paid plans have a hard spend limit that is on by default.

### Does Prisma Postgres need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan is free forever with no credit card required. You do need a Prisma Data Platform account to claim a database.
