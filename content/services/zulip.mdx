You use Zulip in the browser, the desktop app, or the mobile apps. You pick a channel, then start a topic with a short subject, much like an email subject line.

## What the free plan gives you

The Zulip Cloud Free plan keeps 10,000 messages of search history and allows up to 5 GB of file storage in total. The plans page does not state a user limit for it.

Zulip calls it a complete team chat solution, with paid plans adding further capabilities. Topics keep each conversation under its own subject, so busy channels stay easy to follow. It also lists guest accounts. All plans use the same 100% open-source software, and you can move between Zulip Cloud and your own servers with its export tools.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page does not describe a bill or a cutoff for the free plan. The billing guide explains that only the latest 10,000 messages can be viewed and searched. Older messages remain stored and become accessible again after an upgrade. The reviewed pages do not explicitly establish the Free signup card policy or storage-exhaustion handling.

The Standard plan has unlimited search history and 5 GB of file storage per user, at $6.67 per user per month billed annually or $8 billed monthly. Plus needs at least 10 users and adds SSO with SAML and priority commercial support.

## Gotchas

- Free storage is 5 GB in total for the whole organization, while Standard gives 5 GB per user.
- Search history on the free plan is limited to the latest 10,000 messages. Older messages remain stored and return after an upgrade.
- Many organizations qualify for a free Standard plan, including open-source projects, academic research, and most small non-profits. Apply before you settle for the limited free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zulip free?

Yes. The Zulip Cloud Free plan has 10,000 messages of search history and 5 GB of total file storage. The software is open source, and you can also self-host it.

### Does Zulip have a user limit on the free plan?

The plans page states no user limit for the free plan. The limits it lists are search history and file storage.

### Can my open-source project get Zulip Standard for free?

Possibly. Zulip lists open-source projects, academic research, non-profit events, and most small non-profits as eligible for a free Standard plan. Check the plans page for how to apply.
