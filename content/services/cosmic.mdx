You work in the Cosmic dashboard to build a project, a bucket, object types, and objects, then fetch content through the REST API or the `@cosmicjs/sdk` JavaScript package. It suits developers who want an API-first CMS with a small team of editors.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is labeled free forever, with no card required. It includes 2 team members, 1 bucket, and 1,000 objects. API reads are metered in two groups: 10k uncached requests and 100k cached requests per month. API bandwidth is 1GB per month. For media you get 1,000 files and 1GB of storage.

AI is part of every plan. Free gives 300k input and 300k output tokens, plus 1 agent and 1 workflow per project. Every plan, Free included, has comments, an audit log, unlimited extensions, and all 4 user roles. Insights analytics allows 100k events per month on Free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says every allowance is metered and never blocked. Paid plans list a rate for use above their included amount, such as $0.23 per 10k uncached requests and $0.36 per GB of API bandwidth. The Free column lists no such rate, and the page does not say how extra use on Free is billed or whether a card is then needed.

Object, bucket, and team limits are different from usage. The Builder plan costs $99 per month and raises you to 3 team members, 2 buckets, and 5,000 objects. Extra team members cost $29 each per month on paid plans. Check the usage view in your project as you grow.

## Gotchas

- Webhooks, localization, revision history, and automatic backups are paid add-ons, not part of Free.
- Single sign-on is listed as not available except on the custom plan.
- Cached and uncached reads are metered separately, so a cache-friendly site stretches the free allowance further.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cosmic free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and the pricing page says no credit card is required. It includes 1 bucket, 1,000 objects, and 2 team members.

### What are the Cosmic free tier API limits?

Free includes 10k uncached and 100k cached API requests per month, and 1GB of API bandwidth. Cached and uncached reads are metered separately.

### What happens when I go over the Cosmic free tier?

The pricing page says allowances are metered and never blocked. It does not list an overage rate for the Free plan.
