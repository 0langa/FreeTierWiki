You set up the page and monitors in the Instatus web dashboard, then post incident updates from there. The help center also covers an API and integrations. It suits small SaaS teams that want a status page quickly.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free forever and needs no credit card. It includes a public status page, 15 monitors with 2-minute checks, and email alerts. You can have 200 subscribers, 5 team members, and 2 on-call members.

Monitor types include website, API, cron, ping, TCP and UDP, and DNS checks, according to the help pages. The Starter plan has no custom domain and no SAML SSO. The pricing page says Pro starts from $20 per month, with 5,000 subscribers. Business has 25,000 subscribers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the monitor or subscriber counts on Starter. The page lists them as plan limits, so plan on upgrading to add more. Check the live pricing table before you build a large setup, because the limits can change.

Open-source projects and non-profits can email Instatus to get a free Pro account. Paying for a year up front gives you three months free. The Pro and Business plans are on a free trial path, as the buttons on the pricing page offer a trial for them.

## Gotchas

- Starter has no custom domain, so you cannot put the page on your own domain.
- Checks run every 2 minutes on Starter, so short blips can pass between checks.
- Subscribers stop at 200 on Starter, while Pro raises that to 5,000.

## Questions people ask

### Is Instatus free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever with a public status page, 15 monitors, and 2-minute checks. No credit card is required.

### Does Instatus let me use my own domain for free?

No. The pricing table lists no custom domain on the Starter plan.

### Can open-source projects get Instatus for free?

Yes. The pricing page says non-profits and open-source projects can email Instatus to get a free Pro account.
