You work in the Localizely web app. You upload and download files to move strings in and out. The pricing page lists Git integrations and API access under paid plans. It suits developers and translators who share one app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It includes 250 hosted string keys and 1 project. Users and languages are unlimited. The plan lists machine translation too.

Localizely makes an organization for you when you sign up, and the plan belongs to that organization. Every project in the organization counts toward its plan. You can add as many contributors to a project as you like.

When you sign up, you also get a 15-day trial with every feature of the Gold plan. When the trial ends, your organization drops to the Free plan automatically. API access and history of changes come only with paid plans.

Open source projects can get Localizely for free after they apply.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you go over 250 hosted string keys. The FAQ says you can localize up to 250 string keys for free, and that you upgrade to go beyond that.

You can check your use at any time. Open the organization menu, click Billing, and see the hosted string keys and projects you use next to the limits of your plan. The paid Personal plans start at $19 per month when billed monthly, or $16 per month billed annually.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has 1 project. The Personal plans have 1 to 3 projects, and the Organization plans have 5 to 20. The pricing page lists AI translation, screenshots, and the GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket integrations under the paid Personal plans, not under Free.
- The plan belongs to your organization, not to your account. If another team invites you, you work under their plan there.
- The trial ends after 15 days and does not ask for a card. Your organization then moves to the Free plan on its own.

## Questions people ask

### Is Localizely free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 250 hosted string keys and 1 project, with unlimited users and languages. It needs no credit card.

### Does Localizely need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says you do not need a card for the trial or the Free plan. You need one only to upgrade.

### Is Localizely free for open source?

Yes. The pricing page says Localizely is completely free for open source projects, and you apply on the site.
