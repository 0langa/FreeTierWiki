You run Rocket.Chat yourself, most often with Docker Compose on a Linux server, and then use it through the web app. It suits teams that want control of their data and can manage a server.

## What the free plan gives you

Rocket.Chat has two free entry points, both self-hosted. Every workspace on version 6.5 or later starts as a Starter workspace, for up to 50 users. Starter includes many premium capabilities with usage limits, such as LDAP, single sign-on, end-to-end encryption, and unlimited push notifications.

Community is the open-source MIT edition. It is self-hosted only, for up to 100 concurrent users, with push notifications capped at 10,000 per month. It covers core messaging and video calls through Jitsi and BigBlueButton only. The pricing pages list no vendor-hosted free plan.

## When you hit a limit

On Starter, your workspace is restricted from adding new users when you reach 50. Existing users and data stay accessible. You can contact sales to upgrade without migrating data, or downgrade to a Community workspace if you need only basic capabilities for non-production use.

The two user limits work differently. Starter counts how many users you add. Community counts how many are connected at the same time. Nothing bills automatically, since you host the software yourself.

## Gotchas

- You pay for the server and run updates yourself. Each Rocket.Chat version is supported for six months after release.
- The docs say Community is not suitable for production by organizations that depend on it for business-critical operations.
- Starter has no air-gapped deployment and no paid support add-ons. Support is community knowledge and self-serve docs.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rocket.Chat free?

Yes, if you host it. Starter is free for up to 50 users, and the open-source Community edition is free for up to 100 concurrent users.

### What happens when I reach the Rocket.Chat Starter limit?

The workspace cannot add new users. Existing users and data stay accessible. You can upgrade or downgrade to Community.

### Can I use Rocket.Chat free without my own server?

The pages describe free options as self-managed and self-hosted. They list vendor-hosted cloud workspaces under a premium hosting service, not as a free plan.
