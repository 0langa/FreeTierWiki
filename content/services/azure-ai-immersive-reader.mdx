You add the Immersive Reader client library to your web or mobile app. Your server gets a Microsoft Entra token, and the library opens the reader in an iframe over your page. It suits teams that build education and accessibility features. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says every tier gets 3 million characters free per month. This includes the Standard tier and the Education/Nonprofit tier. There is no separate free tier to sign up for. You create a normal resource, and the first 3 million characters each month cost nothing.

The service reads, translates, and highlights the text that your app sends to it. Features include text to speech, translation in real time, picture dictionary, syllable splitting, and parts-of-speech highlighting. The docs say Immersive Reader does not store any customer data.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists a price per 1 million characters above the free amount, but the price field is blank. So usage past 3 million characters is billed, not stopped. The pages do not describe a hard cap or a spending limit.

You create the resource under an Azure subscription. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- Each resource must be set up with Microsoft Entra authentication. The docs provide a script that creates the resource and configures it.
- You need at least the Contributor role on the subscription and the Application Developer role in Microsoft Entra ID, unless you own the subscription.
- Resource tiers in the docs are S0 for Standard and S1 for Education or Nonprofit.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Immersive Reader free?

Partly. Every tier includes 3 million free characters per month. Characters above that are billed per 1 million.

### What happens when I go over 3 million characters?

You are billed at the per-1M-character rate. The pricing page does not show the price and does not mention a hard cap.

### Does Immersive Reader store my content?

The docs say Immersive Reader does not store any customer data.
