DocSpring can populate an existing PDF form with data from your app. The hosted API can fill fields repeatedly from a saved template, which helps when the layout already comes from a form you cannot easily redesign. Use test PDFs to check how real inputs fit before sending a live output.

## What the free plan gives you

Developer includes 200 credits each month without a card. DocSpring estimates that the balance covers about ten filled PDFs at the pricing page’s example rate. The allowance is measured in credits, and the estimate should not be reused for every kind of operation. The free plan also includes unlimited test PDFs, ten templates, two users, and access to the API. Hosted forms can carry DocSpring branding. A useful first test uses typical input lengths and checks where long labels, names, or addresses land in the generated result.

## When you hit a limit

Budget by operation and credit cost, not document count alone. Paid plans offer larger allowances. Exhausted live PDFs wait with HTTP 402 and insufficient_credits, then process automatically when credits return; submissions waiting over 30 days are not processed. Test PDFs continue.

Optional automatic top-up buys paid packs at low balance, with an optional monthly spending cap. Packs work on Developer and expire after 12 months; free credits reset monthly. Live signature requests require a first payment even when the feature appears in the plan. Treat document generation and signing as separate workflows when estimating a live integration's cost.

## Gotchas

- Live and test PDFs have different allowances. Verify which mode your test integration uses. Request limits are shared across account API keys and IPs, separately for live and test requests.
- Filled PDF forms cost 20 credits per unit; HTML and page-design PDFs cost 4. A unit is the highest of 10 pages, 10 MiB input, 20 seconds processing, or 50 MiB output, rounded up. Combining PDFs costs credits even for test files. Developer includes 50 AI field setup pages monthly, subject to a shared daily free allowance. Additional AI pages cost $0.25 paid from credits. Hosted forms on Developer show DocSpring branding.
- Storage beyond the included 30 days costs 1 credit monthly per 50 documents or 50 MiB, whichever is greater. The FAQ says 31 days while the detailed rules say 30; use the shorter period when budgeting.

## Questions people ask

### Does DocSpring have a time limit on Free?

The Developer plan has no time limit and the credits refresh monthly.

### Can I test a template without buying credits?

The plan lists unlimited test PDFs. Use the testing workflow to review output before switching an integration to live generation.

### Can I send a live signature request for free?

The provider says the live version requires a first payment.
