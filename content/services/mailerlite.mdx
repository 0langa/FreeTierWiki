You build campaigns, forms, and automations in the MailerLite web app. A REST API and several SDKs exist for developers, though the free plan does not let you send email through them.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan covers up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails a month for 2 user seats. It includes campaigns, automations, one website, one landing page, and signup forms, plus all 3 email editors: drag and drop, custom HTML, and simple.

The pricing table limits the free plan to 3 active forms and 3 active automations, with 5 steps per automation. A send counts as one email to one subscriber, so a campaign to 500 people uses 500 emails. On Free, the sending allowance resets each month on your account registration date; paid plans use their billing date. Every new account also gets a 14-day trial of premium features, and nothing is deleted when the trial ends.

## When you hit a limit

Above 250 active subscribers, Free locks campaign/automation sending and manual/API/integration imports until reduction or upgrade. Unsubscribed/bounced contacts do not count. Existing forms/landing pages keep collecting subscribers.

At 2,500 monthly emails, sending pauses until reset or manual upgrade; Free never upgrades automatically. Paid subscriber tiers can upgrade automatically. Paid sending-limit guidance conflicts: one FAQ describes automatic upgrades, another lets Comfort wait for renewal. Confirm before upgrading.

Feature overages restrict creation, editing or activation and disable email sending/imports until reduction or upgrade. Monitor subscribers, sends and feature use separately, since any can block work.

## Gotchas

- Premium trial features switch off after 14 days, but your data stays. Over-limit active features can restrict sending even when your subscriber and monthly send counts are within their limits.
- Email sending through the API and the MCP server is available on paid plans only. On the free plan, API and MCP access is limited and does not include sending.
- The API has a global limit of 120 requests per minute, and extra requests get a 429 error.

## Questions people ask

### Is MailerLite free?

Yes. The free plan has no time limit and includes up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails per month. You do not need a credit card.

### What happens if I go over 250 subscribers on MailerLite?

Sending is locked for campaigns and automations until you upgrade or remove active subscribers. Unsubscribed and bounced contacts do not count.

### Can I send emails with the MailerLite API on the free plan?

No. Sending through the API and MCP server is for paid plans. The free plan has limited API access that does not include sending.
