You build campaigns, forms, and automations in the MailerLite web app. A REST API and several SDKs exist for developers, though the free plan does not let you send email through them.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan covers up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails a month for 2 user seats. It includes campaigns, automations, one website, one landing page, and signup forms, plus all 3 email editors: drag and drop, custom HTML, and simple.

The pricing table limits the free plan to 3 active forms and 3 active automations. A send counts as one email to one subscriber, so a campaign to 500 people uses 500 emails. Your monthly allowance resets on your billing date. Every new account also gets a 14-day trial of premium features, and nothing is deleted when the trial ends.

## When you hit a limit

On a free account, a list that grows past 250 active subscribers locks sending for both campaigns and automations. MailerLite then asks you to sign up for a paid plan, or you can remove active subscribers to send again. Unsubscribed and bounced subscribers do not count.

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use all 2,500 monthly emails. Paid accounts move up to the next tier and are charged by card. The free plan needs no credit card, so there is no card to charge.

## Gotchas

- Premium features from the 14-day trial switch off when the trial ends, but your subscribers, automations, forms, and sites stay.
- Email sending through the API and the MCP server is available on paid plans only. On the free plan, API and MCP access is limited and does not include sending.
- The API has a global limit of 120 requests per minute, and extra requests get a 429 error.

## Questions people ask

### Is MailerLite free?

Yes. The free plan has no time limit and includes up to 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails per month. You do not need a credit card.

### What happens if I go over 250 subscribers on MailerLite?

Sending is locked for campaigns and automations until you upgrade or remove active subscribers. Unsubscribed and bounced contacts do not count.

### Can I send emails with the MailerLite API on the free plan?

No. Sending through the API and MCP server is for paid plans. The free plan has limited API access that does not include sending.
