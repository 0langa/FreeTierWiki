Stormkit is an application platform for the web. It builds and deploys your app from git, with preview environments, and it comes both as a hosted cloud and as software you run on your own servers. It suits developers who want a Vercel- or Netlify-style workflow and may want to self-host later.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing section on the Stormkit homepage has a Cloud tab and a Self-Hosted tab. On the Cloud tab, the Free tier costs $0 a month and lists 300 build minutes, 100 GB of bandwidth, 100 GB of storage, and 500,000 function invocations.

The page says all limits are per seat, and you buy more seats to multiply them. Stormkit describes itself as a self-hostable application platform with git-driven deployments, preview environments, a managed Postgres database, end-user authentication, a mailer, cron triggers, and analytics. On the Self-Hosted tab, the Free tier lists unlimited usage and community support.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing section does not say what happens when you use up build minutes, bandwidth, storage, or function invocations, and it does not say whether the amounts reset each month.

The next tier, Premium, costs $20 a month per seat and raises the cloud limits to 1,000 build minutes, 1 TB of bandwidth, 1 TB of storage, and 1.5 million function invocations, with access to all features. Ultimate costs $100 a month and adds premium support.

## Gotchas

- The limits are per seat, so a team's allowance depends on how many seats it buys.
- Built-in analytics, audit logs, approval mode, and teams are listed as Premium features.
- The self-hosted Free tier lists community support only; premium support over Slack, Discord, or Teams comes with Ultimate.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stormkit free?

Yes. The Free tier of Stormkit Cloud costs $0 a month, and the self-hosted Free tier lists unlimited usage.

### What does the free cloud tier include?

It lists 300 build minutes, 100 GB of bandwidth, 100 GB of storage, and 500,000 function invocations, per seat.

### How much is Stormkit Premium?

Premium costs $20 a month per seat and raises the limits, for example to 1,000 build minutes and 1 TB of bandwidth.
