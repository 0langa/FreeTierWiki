Numverify is a phone number validation and lookup API from APILayer. You pass a national or international number and get back whether it is valid, its line type, carrier, and country and location. It has a Free plan and paid monthly or yearly plans. The Free plan suits developers who want to test phone validation in a personal project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and includes 100 API requests. It covers number validation, real-time carrier and line type detection, and country and location detection, for non-commercial use with no support. The FAQ describes the Free plan as a trial version without a time limit, offering a volume of 100 monthly API requests. Accessing any type of Numverify data with a valid API access key counts as one API request. Paid plans start with Basic at $14.99 a month for 5,000 API requests with commercial use and standard support.

## When you hit a limit

Numverify notifies users by email and on the account dashboard when they reach at least 75%, 90%, and 100% of their monthly API request allowance. The FAQ says overage fees apply after 100% so that your application keeps running. The Free plan card lists an overage price of zero per request, and the page does not say whether requests stop on a free account, so check your dashboard before you near 100 requests. Paid plans list a per-request overage price.

## Gotchas

- 100 requests a month is enough for testing, not for validating every signup on a live site.
- The Free plan is non-commercial, so a business needs a paid plan from the start.
- The FAQ mentions HTTPS access for paid customers, while the Free plan card lists HTTPS encryption, so confirm before sending numbers over HTTP.

## Questions people ask

### Is Numverify free?

Yes, with a Free plan of 100 monthly API requests for non-commercial use.

### Does the Numverify Free plan expire?

No. The FAQ calls it a trial version without a time limit.

### Can I use Numverify's Free plan commercially?

No. The Free plan is listed for non-commercial use; commercial use starts with the Basic plan.
