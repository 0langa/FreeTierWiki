You use Object Storage through the Oracle Cloud Console, the OCI command-line tool, SDKs, or the REST API. The Amazon S3 Compatibility API lets existing S3 tools work with few changes.

## What the free plan gives you

Every tenancy gets a total of 20 GB of Always Free object and archive storage. Once your Free Trial has ended and the account is in an Always Free only state, you get 20 GB of combined Standard, Infrequent Access, and Archive tier data. You also get 50,000 Object Storage API requests per month.

If you have a paid account or free trial credits, the 20 GB is split into 10 GB of Standard tier, 10 GB of Infrequent Access, and 10 GB of Archive tier data. The same 50,000 requests apply.

Always Free resources are free of charge in the home region of your tenancy, for the life of the account. They show an Always Free-eligible label in the Console.

## When you hit a limit

During a Free Trial you can store unlimited data, and the first 20 GB takes nothing from your trial credit. Upgrade to a paid account to keep unlimited storage.

If you do not upgrade before the trial ends, the account is limited to 20 GB. If you are using more than 20 GB when the trial ends, all of your objects are deleted. You can then upload again up to the Always Free limit. Oracle says it will not charge for Always Free resources after an upgrade, only for use above the limits.

## Gotchas

- Going over 20 GB at the end of a trial wipes your objects. Check usage before the trial ends.
- Oracle says Always Free resources are free in the home region of your tenancy. Check the Always Free-eligible label before you create a bucket elsewhere.
- Access runs through compartments and IAM policies. A tenancy administrator must grant you rights before you can create buckets or upload objects.

## Questions people ask

### Is Oracle Object Storage free?

Yes, in part. Every tenancy gets 20 GB of combined Standard, Infrequent Access, and Archive storage and 50,000 API requests per month under Always Free.

### Does Oracle Object Storage need a credit card?

The entry records a phone number and a credit card at sign-up. The Oracle docs fetched for this entry do not say what sign-up asks for.

### What happens when my Oracle free trial ends?

The account is limited to 20 GB of Always Free storage. If you use more than 20 GB at that point, Oracle deletes all of your objects.
