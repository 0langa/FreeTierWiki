You send traces and metrics with OpenTelemetry SDKs, then query them in the Honeycomb web UI. It suits developers who debug production problems across services and want to ask new questions of their data.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is the introductory plan and is free forever. It allows up to 20M events per month and up to 100M metrics data points per month. It includes 2 triggers, plus distributed tracing, BubbleUp, OpenTelemetry support, team query history, and Honeycomb Metrics.

The docs say events and logs have a fixed retention of 60 days. The pricing page also says it has unlimited seats and unlimited querying. Pro starts at $150 per month with 100 triggers and Honeycomb support.

## When you hit a limit

Honeycomb warns you before it throttles. After one month over the limit, you get a warning email at the start of the next month. After a second month in a row, you get a danger notice with a 10-day window to fix it. If you do not, Honeycomb begins throttling events.

While throttled, the Event API accepts one of every ten events and rejects the rest. Burst protection helps with spikes: when you send more than twice the daily target in one day, the excess events do not count against your limit. This can trigger up to three times per calendar month. The docs do not say Free overages are billed.

## Gotchas

- Only 2 triggers fit in the Free plan, so alerting stays small.
- Throttling can drop most of your events if you stay over the limit for two months in a row.
- Choose the US or EU data region at sign-up, because the endpoints differ.

## Questions people ask

### Is Honeycomb free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever with up to 20M events and 100M metrics data points per month, and 2 triggers.

### What happens when I go over the Honeycomb event limit?

You get a warning email after the first month over. After a second month, you have 10 days to fix it before Honeycomb begins throttling events.

### How long does Honeycomb keep my data?

The docs say events and logs have a fixed retention of 60 days.
