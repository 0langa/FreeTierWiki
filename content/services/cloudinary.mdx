You upload through the Console, the Upload API, or an SDK for Node.js, Python, PHP, React, and many other languages. You then change images with options in the delivery URL. It suits developers who need resizing and CDN delivery.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan gives you 25 monthly credits for 3 users on 1 account. Cloudinary says it is free forever and needs no credit card. Image and video transformations are included, along with access to free add-ons.

One credit is shared across all services. It equals 1,000 transformations, or 1GB of managed storage, or 1GB of image bandwidth. Cloudinary counts the credits for you and shows the split in your dashboard. A 2GB video bandwidth rate is listed as paid plans only.

The docs say the Free plan can run in production without restrictions, as long as you stay within the credit allowance. Custom domain (CNAME) support starts on a paid plan.

## When you hit a limit

Base credits are soft limits. Cloudinary sends warnings as usage approaches the allowance, repeatedly asks you to upgrade after excess use, and eventually disables the account if you take no action. It publishes no fixed grace period. Add-on quotas instead stop that add-on when exhausted.

Transformations and bandwidth use a rolling 30-day window, with older activity dropping out daily; storage is the current stored total. There is no calendar-month reset to zero. Free signup needs no card, but paid subscriptions and add-ons have separate billing.

## Gotchas

- The credits are shared. A video-heavy or transformation-heavy month can use them up through bandwidth alone.
- Stored derived assets and backed-up revisions count toward storage, not only your original uploads.
- Support on the Free plan is through forums, tickets, and email. Expedited support is a paid feature.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudinary free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 25 monthly credits for 3 users on 1 account. Cloudinary says it is free forever with no credit card.

### Does Cloudinary need a credit card?

No. You can register with a Google or GitHub account, or with an email address. No card or other financial details are required.

### What happens when I use all my Cloudinary credits?

Cloudinary sends warnings and upgrade requests, then eventually disables the account if you take no action. Base credits are soft limits; add-on quotas can stop immediately.
