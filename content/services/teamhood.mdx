You use Teamhood in the browser, and the pricing page lists a REST API, webhooks, and an MCP server. It suits small teams that plan work on boards and Gantt charts.

## What the free plan gives you

Teamhood now has two pricing pages. The main page shows one plan, priced per user per month, with a free first month, no credit card, and a start from 3 users. The Classic plans page still lists a free Personal plan for up to 10 users.

Personal has unlimited projects and workspaces, Kanban, Gantt, and List views, subtasks and dependencies, Teamhood AI, and Slack and Teams integrations. It includes 100 MB of storage. The Team plan adds time tracking, workload, reports, custom fields, and 2 GB of storage.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing pages do not say what happens when Personal reaches 10 users or 100 MB of storage. Plan on upgrading before you reach them. On the main plan, the FAQ says that if you do not subscribe after the free month, the trial ends and nothing is deleted. Nothing is charged before you choose billing and a number of licenses.

On the Classic plans, the paid Team plan has a 14-day free trial with no credit card. The Classic page says it is billed yearly for 3 or more users.

## Gotchas

- The main pricing page no longer shows a free plan. The free Personal plan appears only under Classic plans, so check it before you rely on it.
- Personal does not include time tracking, workload, or reports. Those start in the Team plan.
- The new main plan counts every active member and open invite as a license, from a minimum of 3 licenses.

## Questions people ask

### Is Teamhood free?

Partly. The Classic plans page lists a free Personal plan for up to 10 users. The main pricing page shows one paid plan with a free first month and no credit card.

### Does Teamhood need a credit card?

Not to start. The main plan gives a free month with no credit card, and the Classic Team trial lasts 14 days with no credit card.

### How much storage does the Teamhood free plan have?

The Classic Personal plan includes 100 MB of storage. The Team plan raises it to 2 GB.
