Skyvia is a cloud data platform. It lets you integrate data between cloud apps and databases with no coding, automate data flows, back up cloud data, query it from the browser with a visual builder or SQL, and expose data sources to other applications. Each of these products has its own plans. It suits developers and analysts who need small data syncs without building pipelines.

## What the free plan gives you

The free Data Integration plan, described as basic integration for a small volume of data, costs $0 a month. It includes 10k records a month, scheduling once a day, and 2 scheduled integrations with a 30-day schedule expiration. Source and target connections and users are unlimited.

Automation includes 1,000 monthly tasks, two active automations and daily polling. Backup includes 1 GB and keeps snapshots for three months, with CSV export. Query allows five successful queries daily. Connect includes one cloud-source endpoint and 100 KB monthly; databases are excluded. These are separate subscriptions.

## When you hit a limit

Running integrations finish even above the monthly record allowance. Subsequent manual and scheduled runs are blocked. Record overages require an eligible paid plan and explicit opt-in; successful inserts, updates and deletions count, including unchanged updates. Manual runs are otherwise available beyond the daily schedule.

The next Data Integration plan, Basic, costs $99 a month, or $79 a month billed annually, and removes the 30-day schedule expiration. Higher plans add hourly and per-minute scheduling.

## Gotchas

- Scheduled integrations on the free plan expire after 30 days, so a daily sync stops unless you renew it.
- Schedules run at most once a day, so the free plan does not suit near-real-time syncs.
- Every product has a separate plan, so free limits in Data Integration do not cover Automation or Backup.

## Questions people ask

### Is Skyvia free?

Yes. Data Integration includes 10k records monthly; Automation, Backup, Query and Connect have separate free plans. The Data Studio Connector is separately priced.

### How often can a free Skyvia integration run?

Schedules run once daily, with two scheduled integrations. Manual runs are available as often as needed until the record allowance is exceeded.

### Do free Skyvia schedules expire?

Yes. Scheduled integrations on the free plan have a 30-day expiration.
