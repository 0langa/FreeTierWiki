You use Keybase through a desktop or phone app, and the command-line client adds signing, encrypting, and following other users. It suits people who want private chat and a large encrypted folder and are happy to manage their own keys.

## What the free plan gives you

Every account gets 250 GB of storage. The files page says the 250 GB free accounts will stay free. You can write to a private folder that only you and the users you name can read, and to a public folder where every file is signed. Teams and encrypted chat come with the app. Keybase also lets you prove that you own accounts on sites such as Twitter, GitHub, and Reddit, or on your own website or domain.

When you write into a shared folder, only your quota counts, not the quota of the other people. History data counts toward your quota too, and Keybase says controls for how long deleted blocks stay around are coming.

## When you hit a limit

The files page does not say what happens when you fill the 250 GB quota. It says there is no paid upgrade today, so more storage is not for sale. It adds that Keybase will likely offer paid storage later for people who want to store more data. Because there is no paid plan and no card to enter, you cannot get a surprise bill. Plan to clear out old files or use a second service when you run out.

## Gotchas

- Keybase cannot recover lost encrypted data. If you throw away all your devices, you lose your private data, because it is encrypted only for your devices and paper keys.
- The files page is an early alpha announcement. Check the app for the current state of the features before you rely on them.
- There is no sync model. Files stream in on demand, so this is not a drop-in replacement for a sync folder.

## Questions people ask

### Is Keybase free?

Yes. Every account gets 250 GB of storage for free, and Keybase says there is no paid upgrade today.

### Does Keybase need a credit card?

No. The pages describe a free account with no paid plan, so there is no card step.

### What happens if I lose my devices?

Keybase cannot recover lost encrypted data. Your private data is encrypted only for your devices and paper keys, so keep a paper key somewhere safe.
