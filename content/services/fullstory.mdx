Fullstory captures what visitors do on your website or app and lets you replay sessions, see heatmaps, and analyze behavior. It suits product teams and developers who want to see the exact steps behind a bug report or a drop in conversions.

## What the free plan gives you

FullstoryFree is a permanent free plan designed for individuals and small teams, and it requires no credit card. Your account includes 30,000 sessions per month with 12 months of data retention.

The plan gives you core capabilities such as Session Replay, basic analytics, and debugging tools, for up to 10 users. Advanced capabilities such as Dashboards and StoryAI, or higher session limits, need a paid plan. The paid plans are Business, Advanced, and Enterprise, and their prices are given on request through a demo rather than listed on the page.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when an account records more than 30,000 sessions in a month. It does not describe a stop, a sampling rule, or an overage charge for the free plan.

Because the free plan needs no credit card, there is no payment method on file to charge. Fullstory says you can upgrade to a paid plan or request a demo at any time if you need higher session limits or advanced features.

## Gotchas

- Paid prices are not published, so plan the cost of growing past the free plan with a sales conversation.
- Dashboards and StoryAI summaries are paid features, so the free plan leans on replay and basic analytics.
- The user limit counts teammates, so a larger team outgrows the free plan before the session limit does.
