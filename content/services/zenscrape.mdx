You call the API with your own key and a target URL, and the docs generate code snippets with your key filled in. It suits developers who need a page fetched without running their own proxy setup.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and gives you 1,000 credits every month. The free plan card lists only the credits, and the pricing page does not break out features per plan. Zenscrape says its system rotates IP addresses at regular intervals to avoid bans, and it detects Cloudflare protection and handles it without work on your side.

Credits are tied to request types, and the docs have a Request Costs section for the exact amounts. The free plan is meant for getting started and does not list a concurrency figure. Paid plans show one, from 10 concurrent requests on the first paid plan up to 100 on the largest.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens at 100% of your credits. It does say Zenscrape emails you when monthly usage hits 90% and again at 100%, so you can react and change plans.

Errors tied to captchas are usually not deducted from your credit volume. If one is, you can contact Zenscrape and ask for the credits back. Paid plans start at $59.99 a month for 250,000 credits, and annual billing takes 10% off.

## Gotchas

- The SLA and unlimited support belong to the larger paid plans, not the free one.
- Worldwide geotargeting and premium proxies are listed on each paid plan. The free plan card does not list them.
- The first paid plan has 10 concurrent requests, so heavy parallel jobs need an upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zenscrape free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 1,000 credits per month. Zenscrape's pricing page offers 1,000 free credits to try it.

### Do failed requests count against my Zenscrape credits?

It depends on the error code. Captcha-related errors are usually not deducted. If it happens anyway, contact Zenscrape and it will refund the credits.

### Will Zenscrape warn me before my credits run out?

Yes. It sends an email when your monthly usage reaches 90% and again at 100%.
