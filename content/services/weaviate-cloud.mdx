You create clusters in the Weaviate Cloud console and work with them through client libraries or the REST endpoint with an API key. It suits developers who want hybrid search and a managed vector store for a small project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is always $0 and needs no credit card. You get 1 cluster per user, with 100,000 objects, 1 GB of memory, and 10 GB of disk. The cluster supports 1 collection and up to 3 tenants, and it includes hybrid search, vector compression, and multi-tenancy.

Weaviate Embeddings allow 2,000 requests per day, and the Query Agent allows 1,000 requests per month. Free clusters run only on AWS in a limited set of regions. Support is by email.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page shows hard numbers for objects and collections, but it does not say what happens when you pass them. The compare table lists the Free object limit as 100,000 and the collection limit as 1, while Flex has no object limit. Upgrade to Flex, which starts at $45 a month, to remove them.

The limit you meet sooner is inactivity. After 7 days of inactivity, Weaviate suspends the cluster and keeps your data. You can reactivate it from the console. After 30 days of total inactivity, Weaviate deletes the cluster for good and sends a warning email 1 day before.

## Gotchas

- A free cluster has no backups or replication. Backups start on Flex with 7 days of retention.
- Free clusters use the Cost Optimized profile with the HFresh index. To choose Performance Optimized, upgrade to Shared Cloud.
- Shared Cloud clusters require billing details, but the Free cluster does not.

## Questions people ask

### Is Weaviate Cloud free?

Yes. The Free plan is always $0 with no credit card. It gives 1 cluster per user with 100,000 objects, 1 GB of memory, and 10 GB of disk.

### Does the Weaviate Cloud free cluster get deleted?

Yes, if you stop using it. Weaviate suspends a free cluster after 7 days of inactivity and deletes it after 30 days of total inactivity.

### What does the Weaviate Cloud free plan not include?

It has no backups, replication, or uptime SLA, and it allows only 1 collection. Flex starts at $45 a month and raises the object and collection limits.
