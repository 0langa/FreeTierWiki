Statuspage is Atlassian's hosted status page product. You list the components of your service, then post incidents and scheduled maintenance so users can see what is wrong and subscribe to updates. It suits developers who want a familiar public status page without building one.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says the Free plan "gives you access to 100 subscribers, 25 components, two team members, two metrics, email notifications, Slack notifications, Microsoft Teams notifications, and access to REST APIs."

The core feature set listed for all plans includes incidents, components, scheduled maintenances, incident templates, page analytics, component groups, basic customization, REST API access, third-party components, chat integrations, a status embed, and an activity log. SSL comes free on all plans. Team members are the people who log in to create incidents and update component statuses; subscribers are the people who receive updates.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free page reaches 100 subscribers. The next public plan is $29 a month for 250 subscribers, 5 team members, and 5 metrics, and the page says all plans come with unlimited notifications, so the limits are on subscribers and seats rather than on how many updates you send. Nothing on the page suggests you are billed automatically on the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- SMS and webhook notifications are listed only on higher plans; the Free plan sends email, Slack, and Microsoft Teams notifications.
- Two team members means only two people can log in and post updates.
- Private pages with authenticated subscribers are a separate set of paid plans starting at $79 a month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Statuspage free?

Yes. The Free plan covers 100 subscribers, 25 components, two team members, and two metrics.

### Does the Free plan send SMS notifications?

No. The Free plan lists email, Slack, and Microsoft Teams notifications; SMS and webhooks are on higher plans.

### Is SSL included?

Yes. The pricing page says SSL comes free on all plans.
