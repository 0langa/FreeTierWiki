Plausible is a web analytics service that runs without cookies and does not track individual visitors. Its script is much smaller than Google Analytics, and all visitor data is processed on servers owned by European companies in the EU. It suits developers who want simple traffic stats without a consent banner.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. The pricing section says to sign up for a 30-day free trial, and that no credit card is required.

After the trial, plans are based on traffic. At up to 10k monthly pageviews, Starter costs $9 a month for one site with 3 years of data retention, email and Slack reports, Google Analytics import, goals and custom events, saved segments, and annotations. Growth costs $14 a month for up to 3 sites and 3 team members, and Business costs $19 a month for up to 10 sites, 10 team members, 5 years of retention, funnels, and the Stats API at 600 requests per hour.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing section does not say what happens when the 30-day trial ends or when a site goes over its plan's monthly pageviews. Plans are chosen by pageview tier, from 10k up to 10M+ a month, so more traffic means a higher tier. Yearly billing is listed with 2 months free. Accounts with more than 10 sites or team members fall under the custom-priced Enterprise plan, which adds SSO and raw event exports.

## Gotchas

- There is no free plan to fall back to after the 30 days.
- Starter covers one site; more sites need Growth or Business.
- Funnels and the Stats API start on the Business plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Plausible free?

Not permanently. It has a 30-day free trial with no credit card, then plans start at $9 a month.

### Does Plausible need a cookie banner?

The site says Plausible uses no cookies or persistent identifiers, so there is no need for cookie banners or GDPR consent.

### How many sites does Starter cover?

One site. Growth covers up to 3 sites and Business up to 10.
