You set up Hookdeck in its dashboard, through an API, or with the Hookdeck CLI. A connection links a source URL to a destination. It suits developers who receive webhooks and want them queued and retried.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan costs $0 per month. It includes up to 10,000 events, 3 days of retention, 1 user, and SOC 2 compliance. Connections and transformations are unlimited. The plan also includes 100,000 discarded requests per month.

An event is a message that is delivered to a destination, such as your API. A request is any inbound HTTP request from a source. One request can create 0, 1, or several events. Retries are free, because an event price includes all its retries. A project gets 5 events per second of throughput per destination.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ answers the question for the Developer plan directly. If you exceed 10,000 events, you continue receiving your events, but the dashboard is locked until you upgrade your plan or a new billing period starts. If overages are excessive, Hookdeck reserves the right to stop processing all requests and events.

Throughput works differently. When a destination goes over its throughput ceiling, extra events are queued and delivered as capacity frees up, and inbound requests are always accepted. The Team plan starts at $39 per month and adds 7 days of retention and unlimited users.

## Gotchas

- Delivering one request to several destinations counts as several events, so fan-out uses up the 10,000 faster.
- The 14 day trial only lets you invite team members. The Free Developer plan limits still apply during the trial.
- Hookdeck can delete event data older than your retention period at any time, so export what you need to keep.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hookdeck free?

Yes. The Developer plan is always $0 and includes up to 10,000 events and 3-day retention. No credit card is required.

### What happens if I exceed 10,000 events on Hookdeck?

You keep receiving your events, but the dashboard is locked until you upgrade or the billing period renews. Hookdeck can stop processing if the overage is excessive.

### Are Hookdeck retries free?

Yes. The price of an event includes all its retries.
