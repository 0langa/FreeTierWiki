You point a Sentry SDK in your app at Bugsink by changing its DSN, and errors then show up in the Bugsink web app. It suits developers who want simple error tracking, hosted or on their own server.

## What the free plan gives you

The hosted free plan is called 15K / Evaluation. It includes 15K monthly events for a single user, with 5K events retained and chat and email alerts. Alerts reach Slack, Discord, email, or a custom webhook, and the home page says they are included on the free plan.

Bugsink is also free to self-host, with unlimited users and all features. Hosted paid plans start at 75K events for 16 euros per month, with unlimited users. Premium support for self-hosted installs costs 15 euros per user per month.

## When you hit a limit

The docs say that when a rate limit is exceeded, Bugsink returns an HTTP 429 status code, and clients are expected to back off, typically for 60 seconds. The docs do not say whether the rejected events are dropped or retried.

Retention is not a fixed number of days. Older events are assessed for relevance over time, and with low volumes events can stay for years. The pricing page does not describe any overage bill for the free plan, and it asks for no card.

## Gotchas

- The free hosted plan is for a single user. Unlimited users start on the 75K events plan, or you can self-host.
- Only 5K events are retained on the free hosted plan, even though 15K events arrive each month.
- Paid hosted plans are priced in euros and start at 75K events a month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bugsink free?

Yes. The hosted free plan gives you 15K events per month for one user, and the self-hosted version is free with unlimited users.

### Does Bugsink work with Sentry SDKs?

Yes. The site says you point your existing Sentry SDKs at Bugsink by updating the DSN.

### What happens when I go over the Bugsink rate limit?

Bugsink returns an HTTP 429 status code, and clients should back off, typically for 60 seconds.
