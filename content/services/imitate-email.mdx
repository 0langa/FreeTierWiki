You point your app's SMTP settings at Imitate Email and read what it sends in a web mailbox. It suits developers and testers who want to check email flows without a real mail server.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says you sign up for free and get 15 emails every day, forever. The home page puts it as 450 test emails a month, free forever, for a solo developer. All you need to sign up is an email address.

Everyone who signs up gets a personal mailbox, which only they can see and which is meant for developers while they build software. The free plan shows the Imitate Email logo and colours on the web widget. The concepts guide places team mailboxes on multi-user plans, although the paid Developer pricing column now lists one team mailbox. Neither page promises a shared team mailbox on Free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after the 15th email of the day. The quick start describes email verification, but that initial signup description does not establish a universal card policy. Neither page publishes the exact rejection or retention behavior after the daily allowance is exhausted.

When you outgrow the free plan, the pricing page offers paid plans and optional extras. The Developer plan is 6 euros per month, or 35 euros per year. It adds 2000 monthly emails, 10 daily forwards, 30 day retention, and POP3/IMAP support. Extra emails cost 4.50 euros per month for each 5000.

## Gotchas

- The 15 email limit is per day, so a large test run in one day can use it up.
- The web widget carries Imitate Email branding on the free and Developer tiers.
- Do not assume Free has shared team mailboxes. The paid pricing table and concepts guide differ on which paid plans include them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Imitate Email free?

Yes. The pricing page says you get 15 emails every day, forever, when you sign up for free. The home page says the same plan is 450 test emails a month.

### What happens when I go over the Imitate Email free limit?

The pricing page does not say. Paid plans start with the Developer plan at 6 euros per month.

### Does Imitate Email need a credit card?

The reviewed pages do not explicitly state the card policy. They describe email signup and verification, which alone is not a complete no-card guarantee.
