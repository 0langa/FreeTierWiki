You add DeepAR to an iOS, Android, web, or desktop app with its SDK, using a license key from the developer portal. Effects load into the SDK, and you can design your own in DeepAR Studio. It suits developers who build camera apps with face effects and expect few users while testing.

## What the free plan gives you

DeepAR prices the SDK by monthly active users, or MAU. A MAU is one person who uses DeepAR features in a month. The same person on a second device or browser can count again. The first tier covers up to 10 MAU and costs nothing. The page also says the SDK is free for testing and development with a free license, and that the free version comes with a watermark. The web guide says DeepAR offers unlimited free licenses for testing and small projects, and you can create new free projects as you like.

DeepAR Studio is free to use. Effects you make in Studio are free to use. DeepAR also lists both free and paid effects. The Beauty API is in beta and free for testing, with a watermarked preview.

## When you hit a limit

Above 10 MAU you move to a paid tier. Up to 1,000 MAU costs $25 per month or $250 per year. Up to 5,000 MAU costs $100 per month or $1,000 per year. Higher tiers go up from there, and the page asks you to get in touch above 100,000 MAU. The page does not say whether the SDK stops working or just flags you when you pass 10 MAU. The Beauty API beta shuts itself off after 3 minutes.

## Gotchas

- Devices and browsers count separately, so one person can use up more than one of your 10 free MAU.
- The free version carries a watermark, and the page does not describe a way to remove it without a paid plan.
- The Beauty API beta has an automatic shutdown after 3 minutes. ShopAR is priced differently from the SDK, so ask DeepAR for ShopAR pricing.

## Questions people ask

### Is DeepAR free?

Partly. The SDK is free for testing and development with a watermark, and the first pricing tier covers up to 10 monthly active users at no cost. Paid tiers start at $25 per month.

### What happens when I go over DeepAR's free users?

You need a paid tier. Up to 1,000 monthly active users costs $25 per month. The page does not say how DeepAR enforces the limit.

### Is DeepAR Studio free?

Yes. DeepAR Studio is free to use, and effects made in it can be used free of charge.
