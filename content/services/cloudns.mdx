ClouDNS is a managed DNS provider. You host your domain's zone on its name servers and manage records in a web panel, with paid plans adding anycast, failover, and DNSSEC.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free DNS plan is marked Free Forever. It includes 1 DNS zone, 50 DNS records, 500K DNS queries per month, and 1 mail forward, served from 4 unicast DNS servers. That is enough for one personal domain or side project with a handful of subdomains and mail records.

ClouDNS supports primary, secondary, and reverse DNS, plus email forwarding, web forwarding, dynamic DNS, domain parking, and DNS statistics across its plans; the free plan gets the basic set. The paid plans come with a free trial, and the page says the trial needs no credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The plan's limits are the zone count, the record count, and 500K queries a month. The page does not say what happens when a free zone goes past its monthly queries, so watch the statistics if a site gets a burst of traffic.

The next plan, Premium S, raises the limits to 5 DNS zones, 200 DNS records, and 5M DNS queries per month, adds 1 DNS failover check, and runs on the anycast network. It has a free 30-day trial; the page also offers free zone migration when you move to Premium.

## Gotchas

- The free plan runs on 4 unicast servers; the page names the anycast network as a Premium feature, which matters for latency far from those servers.
- DNSSEC, zone transfers, and TTL management are listed as Premium DNS features.
- A second domain needs a paid plan, since the free plan has 1 zone.

## Questions people ask

### Is ClouDNS free?

Yes. The Free DNS plan is free forever with 1 DNS zone, 50 records, and 500K queries a month.

### Does the ClouDNS free plan use anycast?

No. The free plan lists 4 unicast DNS servers, and the anycast network is described as a Premium feature.

### Do I need a credit card for the ClouDNS trial?

No. The page says the free trial needs no credit card.
