You manage domains, email ids, and recipient inboxes in the Mutant Mail dashboard. Your daily mail reading and replying happens in your own inbox, such as Gmail.

## What the free plan gives you

The site offers a free forever plan with 1 custom domain and 10 email ids. The email ids work as aliases that forward to a recipient inbox you control. When you reply from that inbox, Mutant Mail sends the message so it looks like it came from your domain.

The site says the DMARC report and alert system is free for all domains connected to Mutant Mail. It also says it does not store, log, or read the mail that passes through, apart from its optional buffer feature. The pricing page could not be read, so this entry does not list the free bandwidth amount.

## When you hit a limit

Mutant Mail counts bandwidth each time it forwards a mail or sends a reply. The FAQ says you get an email when you pass 80 percent of your limit. If you go over the limit, the server discards mail until the next month starts or you upgrade.

Bandwidth resets on the 1st of each month, not in a rolling window. Blocked mail, such as mail to a deleted or inactive alias, does not count. The maximum size of one email is 20MB, including attachments.

## Gotchas

- You need a real inbox outside Mutant Mail. It does not store your mail, so that inbox holds everything.
- A domain can only have one mail server. If the domain already runs mail elsewhere, you must move its MX records, or add a subdomain to Mutant Mail instead.
- If a reply loops back to you, you are probably sending from an address that is not a verified recipient on your account.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mutant Mail free?

Yes. The site offers a free forever plan with 1 custom domain and 10 email ids.

### Does Mutant Mail store my email?

No. The FAQ says it does not store, log, or read the mail that passes through, so your recipient inbox holds it. Only the optional buffer feature keeps mail until it is sent on.

### What happens if I go over my bandwidth limit?

You get a warning at 80 percent. After that, the server discards mail until the limit resets next month or you upgrade.
