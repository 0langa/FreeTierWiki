You use Pendulums from the web version or from a desktop or Android app. It suits freelancers and small teams who want a simple timer instead of a full timesheet suite.

## What the free plan gives you

The whole product is free. The site says Pendulums will always be free to use without any limits. You can create as many projects as you want, with as many users per project as you need.

You can run it as a web version, as a desktop app for Windows, Mac, and Linux, or as an Android app. The site lists downloads from Snap, deb, AppImage, F-Droid, and Cafebazaar. The source code for the web, desktop, and mobile apps is available to read. The makers say they built it because they needed a free tool with unlimited projects and a simple interface that non-technical teammates could use.

## When you hit a limit

The site lists no limits, quotas, or paid plans, so there is nothing to hit. The homepage says the product needs funding to stay alive and asks users to show support through donations. That is a request, not a bill. If the project loses funding, the site gives no promise of how long it will run, so export or note your hours often.

## Gotchas

- The product is funded by donations, and the site gives no service level promise.
- The site describes statistics but does not list invoicing, billing, or integrations with other tools.
- The pages do not describe an export feature, so keep your own records of important hours.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pendulums free?

Yes. The site says it will always be free to use without any limits. It asks for donations but lists no paid plan.

### Does Pendulums limit projects or users?

No limit is shown. The site describes unlimited projects and an unlimited number of users per project.

### Which devices does Pendulums run on?

It has a web version, desktop apps for Windows, Mac, and Linux, and Android apps. The site links to several download sources.
