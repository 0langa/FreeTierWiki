You create a file share inside an Azure storage account, then mount it from your machines. You can set it up in the Azure portal, PowerShell, or the Azure CLI, and you can also reach it through the REST API and the Azure Storage client libraries.

## What the free plan gives you

New Azure customers get a 12-month Azure Files allowance. Microsoft's free-services table lists 100 GB of LRS transaction optimized, hot, and cool files plus 2 million read, list, and other file operations each month. The 5 GB figures elsewhere are Cloud Shell's storage row and an example in Microsoft's cost guide. Confirm the allowance in your subscription free-usage grid before you rely on a number.

The free amount is monthly. Azure's cost guide says unused free storage does not roll over, so a month of light use does not save space for the next month. The same guide says only certain tiers within a service are free. Azure Files has an SSD tier and an HDD tier, and several billing models, so check which share type the free amount covers before you deploy.

## When you hit a limit

After upgrading to pay-as-you-go, the applicable allowance is not a spending cap. If your usage goes above the free amount, you pay the extra at pay-as-you-go rates. The cost guide says this applies once your account is upgraded.

The free account also has a starting credit. You must move to pay-as-you-go within 30 days, or when the credit is used up, to keep receiving free services. After the 12 months end, services you still use keep running and are billed at normal rates. Delete the shares you no longer need.

## Gotchas

- A phone number and a credit or debit card are required. Azure may place a small temporary hold on the card and remove it after verification.
- The free amount ends after 12 months. It is not an always-free amount.
- Azure Files has SSD and HDD tiers and several billing models, and snapshot storage that does not fit inside a share's provisioned storage is billed separately in the provisioned v2 model. Read the pricing table before you pick a tier.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Files free?

Partly. Eligible new accounts get 100 GB of LRS files and 2 million file operations a month for 12 months, according to Microsoft's free-services table. Above that allowance or after expiry, normal rates apply.

### Does Azure Files need a credit card?

Yes. Azure uses a phone number and a credit or debit card to check your identity. It does not charge the card at sign-up.

### What happens when I go over the free Azure Files amount?

Azure bills the extra storage at pay-as-you-go rates. There is no hard cap, so watch your usage in the portal.
