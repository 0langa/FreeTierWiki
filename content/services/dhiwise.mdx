You work in the Rocket web app, or its mobile app, and describe what you want in chat. Rocket builds web apps with Next.js and mobile apps with Flutter.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It gives you a one-time grant of 20 credits, and the pricing page says no credit card is required. All plans include unlimited team members, because Rocket does not charge per seat.

With the free credits you can use the Build capability for websites, landing pages, web apps, and mobile apps. You also get Light Solve, the fast, conversational research mode. Full Solve and competitive intelligence need a higher plan.

One credit balance pays for everything: chat messages, builds, research, and monitoring. A small tweak costs few credits, and a large dashboard costs many. The Pro plan costs $25 per month and includes 100 credits per month. Rocket costs $50 per month for 250 credits.

## When you hit a limit

When your credit balance reaches zero, Rocket pauses generation. Your work stays safe, and you keep access to all projects and assets. On the Free plan, the 20 credits do not refill.

To continue, you move to a paid plan or add credits, and credit add-on packs are for paid users only. Because the Free plan needs no card, nothing can be charged until you choose to upgrade. Rocket prorates the charge when you upgrade in the middle of a billing cycle.

## Gotchas

- The 20 credits are a one-time grant, not a monthly allowance. A big app can use them up fast.
- Unused credits expire if you cancel a paid plan, so use them before cancellation takes effect.
- DhiWise was a Figma-to-code tool. Rocket now covers research and monitoring too, so older guides may show a different product.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rocket free?

Yes, to start. The Free plan gives a one-time grant of 20 credits with no credit card. After that, paid plans start at $25 per month.

### What happened to DhiWise?

DhiWise became Rocket. The old pricing page now says DhiWise is now Rocket, and the new plans use credits for building, research, and intelligence.

### What happens when I run out of Rocket credits?

Rocket pauses generation. You keep your projects and assets, and you can add credits on a paid plan or wait for the next billing cycle.
