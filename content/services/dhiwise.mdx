You work in the Rocket web app, or its mobile app, and describe what you want in chat. Rocket builds web apps with Next.js and mobile apps with Flutter.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It gives you a one-time grant of 20 credits, and the pricing page says no credit card is required. The pricing page advertises unlimited members on every plan, but the current documentation limits Free to 10 invitation recipients across all workspaces, including expired or cancelled invitations. Confirm the applicable limit before inviting a team.

With the free credits you can use the Build capability for websites, landing pages, web apps, and mobile apps. You also get Light Solve, the fast, conversational research mode. Full Solve and competitive intelligence need a higher plan.

One credit balance pays for everything: chat messages, builds, research, and monitoring. The Pro plan costs $25 per month and includes 100 credits per month. Rocket costs $50 per month for 250 credits.

## When you hit a limit

When your credit balance reaches zero, Rocket pauses generation. Your work stays safe, and you keep access to all projects and assets. On the Free plan, the 20 credits do not refill.

To continue, you can upgrade. The pricing page also offers additional credit purchases on every plan, while the docs describe packs for active subscriptions; Free pack eligibility therefore needs confirmation. Because the Free plan needs no card, nothing can be charged until you choose to upgrade. Rocket prorates the charge when you upgrade in the middle of a billing cycle.

## Gotchas

- The 20 credits are a one-time grant, not a monthly allowance. A big app can use them up fast.
- Unused credits expire if you cancel a paid plan, so use them before cancellation takes effect.
- DhiWise was a Figma-to-code tool. Rocket now covers research and monitoring too, so older guides may show a different product.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rocket free?

Yes, to start. The Free plan gives a one-time grant of 20 credits with no credit card. After that, paid plans start at $25 per month.

### What happened to DhiWise?

The old DhiWise pricing URL now leads to Rocket. Its current plans use credits for building, research, and intelligence.

### What happens when I run out of Rocket credits?

Rocket pauses generation and keeps your projects and assets. Free credits do not renew; paid subscriptions renew credits, while Free credit-pack eligibility conflicts between pricing and documentation.
