You upload a CSV in the web app or with the Sofodata CLI, and Sofodata hosts the data behind a secure API. You then call it from a client such as Postman. It suits people who want to share a dataset quickly.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs €0 a month. It allows 2 APIs, 2,500 API calls a month, 10 MB of disk space, and 1 API call per second. The site calls this enough to get started.

The site says common data types in your CSV are detected for you, and that the data is end-to-end encrypted. It also says more plans and features are coming.

Paid plans raise every limit. Starter costs €19.99 a month and allows 5 APIs, 100,000 calls, 100 MB of disk space, and 3 calls per second. Premium costs €39.99 a month and Business costs €79.99 a month.

## When you hit a limit

The site does not say what happens when you pass 2,500 calls, fill the 10 MB of disk space, or go over 1 call per second. It lists no pay-as-you-go option and no overage price.

This entry therefore treats the free plan as a hard limit with no extra charge. Because the page is silent, test your own usage before you rely on it in a demo. If you need more room, the next plan up is Starter.

## Gotchas

- The 1 call per second rate is slow for a front end that makes several requests at once.
- Disk space is only 10 MB, so large CSV files do not fit on the Free plan.
- The site gives no documentation link, and the old documentation address no longer loads.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sofodata free?

Yes. The Free plan costs €0 a month with 2 APIs, 2,500 API calls, and 10 MB of disk space.

### What is the Sofodata free API rate limit?

The Free plan allows 1 API call per second. Starter raises it to 3 calls per second, and Business to 10.

### Does Sofodata need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say. This entry lists none as required.
