Activepieces is an automation platform: you build flows that connect apps, add AI agents and chat, and keep data in built-in tables. It is open source, so you can run it on your own servers or use the hosted cloud. The cloud Free plan suits individuals and small teams building lightweight automations.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is labelled free forever, no card. It includes 1,000 credits a month, unlimited flows, up to 5 users, chat to automation, flows, agents, tables, MCPs, and API access.

The FAQ says the Free plan has no card and no time limit, and is meant for exploring the product and building lightweight automations. It says you get a fresh 1,000 credits every month, so if you use them all, you wait until the next month. Support on the Free plan is from the community.

## When you hit a limit

On the Free plan, runs over the quota wait rather than add charges: the plan table lists going over on Free as waits for tomorrow, while Plus and Team bill extra credits at $0.007 per credit.

The page is not consistent about timing. The table lists the Free credit reset as daily, while the plan card and FAQ say 1,000 credits a month. Either way, there is no bill on Free. The Plus plan costs $20 a month for 10,000 credits a month, and Team costs $200 a month for 50,000 credits.

## Gotchas

- AI steps cost more credits than plain runs, so agent-heavy flows use the free credits faster.
- The pricing page disagrees with itself about whether Free credits reset daily or monthly, so watch your usage.
- Free and Plus allow up to 5 users; Team includes 25 users.

## Questions people ask

### Is Activepieces free?

Yes. The cloud Free plan costs $0, needs no card, has no time limit, and includes 1,000 credits a month.

### What happens when I run out of free credits?

On Free, runs over the quota wait until credits refresh; you are not billed.

### Can I self-host Activepieces instead?

Yes. Activepieces says you can run it on its cloud or on your own servers.
