You manage databases with the `turso` CLI or the dashboard and connect from code with an official SDK or SQL over HTTP. It suits developers who like SQLite and want to run many small databases without managing servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is $0 a month, and the pricing page says you can start free with no credit card. It includes 100 databases and 5GB of storage. Each month you get 500 million rows read, 10 million rows written, and 3GB of syncs. Point-in-time restore reaches back 1 day, and support comes from the community.

Turso counts a row read as a row scan, so a query can scan more rows than it returns. Queries without an index scan the whole table. Idle databases cost only storage, which keeps a large set of mostly idle databases cheap.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ explicitly says Free databases are blocked when any one storage, row-read, row-write, or sync limit is exceeded, even if the other metrics remain under quota. The usage docs describe the BLOCKED error for quota-exceeding queries.

Extra storage, reads, writes, and syncs are billed automatically only once you enable overages. On Developer, which costs $5.99 monthly or $4.99 per month under yearly billing, this means $1 per billion extra rows read. The Free plan has no overage billing shown on the pricing page.

## Gotchas

- Row reads are row scans. Missing indexes, aggregate functions like count, and joins can use up the 500 million reads faster than you expect.
- Aborted transactions still count as rows written for any row they inserted or updated.
- The `VACUUM` command is disabled, so you cannot compact a database to cut storage.

## Questions people ask

### Is Turso free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 a month and needs no credit card. It includes 100 databases, 5GB of storage, and 500 million rows read per month.

### What happens when I go over the Turso free limits?

The usage docs say queries that exceed a quota fail with a BLOCKED error. Free needs a paid upgrade for extra usage. On paid plans, extra usage is billed only once overages are enabled.

### Does Turso offer point-in-time restore on the free plan?

Yes, with a short window. The Free plan lists 1 day of point-in-time restore. The Developer plan lists 10 days.
