You run the `bencher` command line tool in CI or on your machine, and it sends benchmark results to the Bencher web app, where you see graphs and alerts. It suits open source projects that want to catch slow-downs early.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It supports public projects only. Private projects are not included. The Free plan allows 65,535 metrics per day, and a project nobody has claimed yet gets 255 metrics per day.

Bare Metal runners are included. On Free you get one concurrent job and a 5 minute job timeout. Without an account, the timeout is 1 minute per run. Free jobs sit behind paid jobs in the queue, but run right away when the queue is idle. On-demand runners are free on this plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says it does not describe what happens when a Free project passes its daily metric limit.

The Free plan lists a 5 minute job timeout for Bare Metal runners; the page does not say more about what happens past it. Private projects need the Pro plan, which costs $100 per month for up to 250 active series. Pro has a 1-month free trial that needs a credit card.

## Gotchas

- Free is for public projects only, so do not send results you need to keep private.
- The Free queue waits behind paid jobs during busy periods.
- Pro bills by active benchmark series, and on-demand runners cost $1.00 per hour on Pro.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bencher free?

Yes, for public projects. The Free plan costs $0 per month and supports public projects only. Private projects need a paid plan.

### Does Bencher need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan. Only the 1-month Pro trial requires a credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Bencher free limits?

The pricing page does not say. The Free plan lists a limit of 65,535 metrics per day and a 5 minute timeout per Bare Metal job.
