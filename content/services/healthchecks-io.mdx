You create a check in the web dashboard, then add one HTTP request to the end of your job, so the job pings Healthchecks.io each time it runs. It suits people who run a few cron jobs or backup scripts. The docs also cover a self-hosted version.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobbyist plan costs $0 per month. It lets you monitor 20 jobs, and each job keeps 100 log entries. The log entries are for auditing past activity of your cron jobs. When the limit is reached, the oldest entries are removed to make room for new ones.

The paid Supporter plan at $5 per month has the same limits as Hobbyist. It exists for people who want to support the service. The Business plan at $20 per month monitors 100 jobs and keeps 1000 log entries per job. The home page says you can monitor 20 cron jobs for free with no credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a cutoff for the 20-job limit, but it says the log limit works by deleting the oldest entries, so you lose old history and not new pings. To go past 20 jobs you move to a paid plan.

Do not create several accounts to dodge the limit. The pricing page says that is abuse. It also says some pings may be rate limited if you ping a single check more than 5 times per minute.

## Gotchas

- Healthchecks.io is for cron jobs and similar tasks, not for probing a website. The docs say it is not the right tool for website uptime.
- Log history is short at 100 entries per job. A job that runs often keeps only its most recent runs.
- Open-source projects and non-profits can get the Business plan free, but you have to ask for it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Healthchecks.io free?

Yes. The Hobbyist plan costs $0 per month and lets you monitor 20 jobs with 100 log entries each. No credit card is required.

### What happens when a Healthchecks.io job does not ping?

Healthchecks.io listens for HTTP requests from your jobs and alerts you through your notification methods when a ping does not arrive. You can set up several methods, such as email and Slack.

### Can I use Healthchecks.io to monitor my website?

Not well. The docs say it is not the right tool for probing website uptime with HTTP requests. Use an uptime monitor for that.
