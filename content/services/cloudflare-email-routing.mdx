Email Routing receives mail sent to addresses on your domain and routes it to custom addresses, Workers, or external destinations, such as your existing inbox. It now sits inside Cloudflare Email Service, next to the paid sending feature. It suits developers and small teams with a domain on Cloudflare who want addresses like support@ without paying for mailboxes.

## What the free plan gives you

Email Routing is available on both the Workers Free and Workers Paid plans. Inbound emails handled by Email Routing are unlimited on both plans. Sending to verified destination addresses in your account is free on all plans, even when only Email Routing is configured.

Each domain can have 200 routing rules, and each rule maps an email pattern to one destination address or one Worker. An account can have 200 verified destination addresses, shared across all its domains. A zone can have 30 domains configured for Email Routing or Email Sending, including the apex domain.

## When you hit a limit

There is no bill for inbound email. The limits are hard ones: messages larger than 25 MiB are rejected, and you cannot add rules or destinations past the counts above.

If a Worker handles incoming email, it counts toward the standard Workers CPU and memory limits. On the Workers Free plan, a complex handler may exceed those limits and fail to process a message. Sending email to arbitrary recipients is a separate feature that requires the Workers Paid plan, with 3,000 emails included per month.

## Gotchas

- Each routing rule sends to one destination; to forward one pattern to several inboxes you need a Worker that calls forward() once per destination.
- Every destination must be verified before it receives mail.
- You must be using Cloudflare DNS for your domain to use Email Service.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Email Routing free?

Yes. Email Routing is available on the Workers Free plan, and inbound emails are unlimited.

### Can I send email with Email Routing for free?

Only to verified destination addresses in your account. Sending to arbitrary recipients requires the Workers Paid plan.

### How large can an incoming email be?

The inbound message size limit is 25 MiB, and larger messages are rejected.
