You create a namespace and a hub in the Azure portal, connect the push services of each platform, and send from your back end. It suits mobile teams that want one send call for iOS, Android, and Windows.

## What the free plan gives you

Notification Hubs has three tiers: free, basic, and standard. The Free tier includes 1 million pushes per subscription and has no base charge per namespace. Quotas apply per namespace. A subscription can have 100 namespaces and each namespace can have 100 hubs.

The Free tier allows 500 active devices per namespace. An active device is a device eligible to receive notifications, such as a registration ID or a device token. Push variables, cross-platform push to individual devices, and registration queries are available. Telemetry is limited, and the Free tier has no SLA.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no extra pushes for the Free tier, so there is nothing to buy on it. It also gives an example: a free namespace that goes over its limits had only half of its 2 million pushes go through, chosen at random. For the active device limit, the FAQ says new registrations against any hub in the namespace are interrupted.

To go higher, you change the tier on the Scale tab of your namespace. The paid tiers allow more devices and bill extra pushes at published rates, so check the live page for the amounts.

## Gotchas

- The 1 million pushes count is per subscription for the tier, not per namespace.
- Scheduled push, bulk import, and multi-tenancy are not available until the Standard tier.
- Pushes count every notification delivered to the platform services, such as Apple's and Google's.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Notification Hubs free?

There is a Free tier. It includes 1 million pushes per subscription and 500 active devices per namespace. It has no SLA.

### What counts as a push in Azure Notification Hubs?

Pushes include all notifications delivered to the platform notification services, such as the Apple Push Notification Service and Google's services.

### What happens when I pass 500 active devices?

New registrations against any hub in that namespace are interrupted. You move to a paid tier to allow more devices.
