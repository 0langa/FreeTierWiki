You manage certificates in the CertKit web app. You can also use its read-only REST API, and you install an agent on servers that should receive certificates. It suits homelab owners who want automatic renewals without running a certificate client on each machine.

## What the free plan gives you

The Homelab plan is free for personal, non-commercial use. It includes 2 certificates, 1 agent, and 1 seat. You also get 3 domain SSL monitors and certificates with up to 2 SANs each. Automated issuance and renewal, delegated DNS validation, and wildcard and multi-domain certificates are in every plan.

A certificate in CertKit means one managed identity. A wildcard certificate counts as one, and renewals and reissues do not use another slot. The pricing page lists email support from the Professional plan up and none for Homelab.

All plans include a free 90-day trial with no credit card. Professional costs $99 per month and Business costs $399 per month, both billed yearly.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that when you reach your plan limits, you can add more certificates or agents at $2 per month each, or move up a plan. The Homelab plan is listed as free, so extras apply to paid plans.

After the 90-day trial, CertKit contacts you. If you do not subscribe, your account deactivates. Logins stop, monitoring alerts stop, and renewals stop. Certificates already deployed keep working until they expire. Personal users can move to the free Homelab plan.

## Gotchas

- The Homelab plan is not for your business or your clients. Businesses get the 90-day trial, and consultants who manage client certificates need a paid plan.
- You need one agent per server. Network appliances such as load balancers cannot run the agent and use an agent on another server as a deployment hub.
- CertKit uses delegated DNS validation with a one-time CNAME record, so you need access to your DNS.

## Questions people ask

### Is CertKit free?

Partly. The Homelab plan is free for personal, non-commercial use, with 2 certificates, 1 agent, and 1 seat. All plans also have a free 90-day trial with no credit card.

### Can I use the CertKit Homelab plan for my business?

No. The FAQ says Homelab is for personal projects and personal systems. Businesses evaluating CertKit use the free 90-day trial, and clients' certificates need a paid plan.

### What happens to my certificates if I stop using CertKit?

Certificates already deployed keep running on your servers until they expire. After that you must renew them yourself, or resubscribe.
