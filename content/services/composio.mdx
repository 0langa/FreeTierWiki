You use Composio through its Python or TypeScript SDK, and you manage keys and accounts in a web dashboard. It suits developers who build AI agents that need to act in other people's apps.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. Each month it includes 100,000 tool calls, 50,000 triggers, and 3 team members. You can connect unlimited accounts, such as a user's Gmail account. Creating sessions, the context an agent runs in, is free.

The Hobby plan has a rate limit of 2,000 requests per minute. Pro raises that to 10,000. Composio also runs shared OAuth apps so you can skip app setup. The pricing page says these managed apps have lower allowances that count toward your free tier, and it advises bringing your own app as you grow.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says the Hobby plan stops at your included limit, so Composio will not charge you unexpectedly.

To go past the limits, you move to Pro at $29 a month. Pro includes a $29 monthly usage credit that expires at the end of each billing month. Past the free amounts, usage bills at the rates on the pricing page. Pro also gives you unlimited team members and spend controls.

## Gotchas

- Composio-managed OAuth apps are easy to start with, but their allowances are lower and they count toward your free tier.
- Advanced white-labeling of the connection page is not on Hobby. On Pro it costs a one-time $0.30 per connection.
- The Python package is `composio`. The quickstart warns you not to install `composio-core`.

## Questions people ask

### Is Composio free?

Yes, the Hobby plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It includes 100,000 tool calls and 50,000 triggers each month, plus 3 team members.

### What happens when I go over the Composio free limits?

The Hobby plan stops at the included limit, and Composio says this means no unexpected charges. You can move to Pro at $29 a month for more.

### Does Composio need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the Hobby plan needs no credit card.
