You write pipeline steps in YAML, either in the web editor or in a file in your repository. Buildkite then dispatches the jobs to agents. It suits small teams who want pipelines as code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and allows up to 5 users and up to 10 concurrent jobs. It includes up to 2,000 Linux vCPU minutes per month on Buildkite hosted agents, in the Small size on AMD64 only. A vCPU minute is one minute of one virtual CPU.

You also get 250k test executions per month. A test execution is one recorded result from one test run, and sending this data is optional. Artifact storage is 50GB per month and artifact downloads are 250GB per month. The plan includes dynamic pipelines and test splitting. Support is community support. Pro costs $30 per active user per month and adds macOS hosted agents and larger machine sizes.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use all 2,000 minutes or pass the other monthly amounts. It lists the amounts as "up to", so treat them as caps. The page also shows no card requirement for the Free plan, so Buildkite has no card to bill.

A user limit or a job limit stops you from adding more. If you need more, move to Pro. Buildkite's docs say spend alerts for Pro only warn you and do not cap spending, so set one before you scale up.

## Gotchas

- The hosted minutes cover only Small Linux agents on AMD64. Mac agents, bigger sizes, and ARM64 are not part of the free allowance.
- The 30-day all-access trial is separate from the Free plan. It includes a Developer Success Engineer for 30 days, then you choose a plan.
- A pipeline needs a Git repository. You can connect GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, or any remote Git URL.

## Questions people ask

### Is Buildkite free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and gives you up to 5 users, 10 concurrent jobs, and 2,000 Linux vCPU minutes per month. No credit card is required.

### How much does Buildkite cost after the free plan?

The Pro plan costs $30 per active user per month. It allows up to 50 users and includes 4,000 Linux vCPU minutes per month.

### Does Buildkite need a credit card to start?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan and the 30-day all-access trial need no credit card.
