You work in the hosted Grafana web interface and send data with a collector such as Grafana Alloy or with data source connections. It fits people who already know Grafana or Prometheus.

## What the free plan gives you

The Always free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It includes all Grafana Cloud services, with usage limits. Metrics are limited to 10k active series per month. Logs and traces are each limited to 50 GB ingested per month. Grafana visualization is limited to 3 active users per month.

Retention is 14 days for metrics, logs, traces, profiles, and k6 performance tests. Support comes from the community. The pricing page also lists free allowances for other products, such as 3 active IRM users per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page describes the free plan as limited to these quotas, but it does not say what Grafana does when you go over them. The Free plan is described as "no charges ever". The Pro plan costs from $19 per month plus usage, and the pricing page says you pay as you go above the free tier on Pro.

Pro also brings 8x5 email support, 13 months of metrics retention, and 30 days of retention for logs, traces, and profiles. Check your usage in the Grafana Cloud portal before you send a lot of data.

## Gotchas

- Retention is short: 14 days on Free for every signal, against 13 months for metrics on Pro.
- Only 3 active users fit in Grafana visualization on the Free plan, so a growing team will hit that first.
- Usage is counted in different ways per product: active series for metrics, GB ingested for logs and traces.

## Questions people ask

### Is Grafana Cloud free?

Yes. The Always free plan costs $0 with no credit card. It has limits such as 10k active series for metrics and 50 GB of logs per month.

### How long does Grafana Cloud keep free data?

The Free plan keeps metrics, logs, traces, profiles, and k6 performance tests for 14 days. Pro keeps metrics for 13 months.

### Does Grafana Cloud need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the free tier.
