EventBridge has two parts most developers use: an event bus that routes events to targets by rules, and EventBridge Scheduler, which invokes a target on a schedule. Over 250 AWS services send events to the bus automatically. It suits developers on AWS who need cron jobs or want to react to changes in their account.

## What the free plan gives you

AWS management events, the control plane operations that over 250 AWS services send to the default event bus, are ingested for free. Delivering events to a service in the same account is free.

EventBridge Scheduler has its own Free Tier: you can make 14,000,000 invocations per month for free. The Schema Discovery feature of the Schema Registry includes a Free Tier of 5 million ingested events per month, and the Schema Registry itself is free for AWS and custom schemas. Free Tier usage is calculated each month across all Regions, except GovCloud, and unused free usage does not roll over.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops EventBridge at the free amounts. Scheduler invocations past 14 million are billed per million; the pricing page example of 20M invocations comes to $6.00 for the month.

Custom events you publish, partner events from SaaS apps, and opt-in data events have no free amount. The examples bill partner event ingestion at $1.00 per million, and delivering 5M AWS management events to a different account costs $5.00. Data transfer between Regions is charged separately.

## Gotchas

- Each 64 KB chunk of a payload counts as one event, so a 256 KB event is billed as 4 events.
- Delivering events to a different account is billed even when ingestion was free.
- A new AWS account on the Free plan closes after 6 months or when its credits run out, unless you upgrade to a Paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amazon EventBridge free?

Partly. AWS management events and same-account delivery are free, and Scheduler includes 14,000,000 free invocations a month, but custom events are billed from the first one.

### Can I run cron jobs on EventBridge for free?

Yes, within the Scheduler Free Tier of 14,000,000 invocations per month. The targets it calls, such as Lambda, bill on their own.

### Does unused free usage carry over?

No. Free Tier usage is calculated each month and unused free usage does not roll over to the next month.
