You use it through the Python library, the web dashboard, and a command line login. You add a few lines to your training script and the results show up in a browser. It suits solo practitioners, students, and researchers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is meant for personal development of AI applications and models. It allows up to 5 model seats, 5 GB per month of storage, and 1 GB per month of Weave data ingestion. Weave is the part of the platform that traces and evaluates LLM apps.

Teams, team-based access controls, and service accounts need the Pro plan. Pro starts at $60 per month, billed monthly, and has a 30-day free trial. It gives 100 GB per month of storage and 1.5 GB per month of Weave ingestion.

Academics can apply for a free Pro license. It includes 200GB of storage, up to 25GB per month of Weave ingestion, and up to 100 seats. The work must be unrelated to a for-profit entity, and you need an active academic email address.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free account passes 5 GB of storage or 1 GB of Weave ingestion. It lists overage prices only for paid plans: extra storage on Pro costs $0.03 per GB, and extra Weave ingestion costs $0.10 per MB.

The Free plan lists no overage price, and the pages do not mention a credit card. Check your usage before large jobs.

## Gotchas

- Pricing now lives on CoreWeave Forge pages, and the docs link to CoreWeave. The product name Weights & Biases still appears on the platform.
- Only metadata stored in the service counts toward storage. Reference artifacts kept in your own bucket do not count.
- Storage is averaged over the last 30 days, so a short spike counts less than steady use.

## Questions people ask

### Is Weights & Biases free?

Yes, for personal use. The Free plan has up to 5 model seats, 5 GB per month of storage, and 1 GB per month of Weave ingestion.

### Is Weights & Biases free for students?

Academic researchers can apply for a free license that includes 200GB of storage and up to 100 seats. You need an active academic email address and research unrelated to a for-profit entity.

### Can I self-host Weights & Biases for free?

The pages do not list a free self-hosted plan. W&B Self-Managed runs W&B Server on your own cloud or on-premises setup.
