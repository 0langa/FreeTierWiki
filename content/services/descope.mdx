You build your login flow in the Descope console, then drop it into your app with a client SDK, a hosted login page, or a backend SDK. It suits teams that want to change login screens without shipping code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan costs $0 and has no overages. It includes 7,500 monthly active users, 10 monthly active tenants, and 3 SSO connections. You also get 10,000 M2M exchanges, 2,000 monthly active consents, and 2,000 monthly active tokens.

Everything runs through flows, which you design visually. The Pro plan starts at $249 per month, billed annually, and it raises the limits to 35 tenants, 5 SSO connections, and 50,000 M2M exchanges. It also adds custom OIDC providers, SSO for third-party apps, and self-service SSO setup. Early-stage startups can apply for Pro free for one year.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says there are no overages on the Free Forever plan. If you want to go over a limit, you must upgrade. So the Free Forever plan itself does not bill overages.

The pricing page says you must move to Pro if you pass 7,500 MAUs, 10 tenants, or 3 SSO connections. For the MAU and tenant limits, Descope sends a warning email first and gives a grace period. After the grace period, your app may lose access to Descope. Paid plans do have usage-based overages, which the detailed pricing section describes. Check your usage in the console before a launch so a busy month does not stop your login.

## Gotchas

- There are four separate counters on the free plan: users, tenants, SSO connections, and M2M exchanges. Passing any one of them means an upgrade.
- SCIM provisioning and bot protection are not in Free or Pro. They start in a higher plan.
- The old standalone React SDK repository is deprecated. The package name stays `@descope/react-sdk`, but issues now go to the descope-js repository.

## Questions people ask

### Is Descope free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan costs $0 and includes 7,500 monthly active users, 10 monthly active tenants, and 3 SSO connections.

### What happens when I go over the Descope free limits?

The pricing page says there are no overages on the free plan. You have to upgrade to a paid plan to go beyond the limits.

### Does Descope need a credit card?

The pricing page shows the Free Forever plan at $0 and does not mention a card for it. The atlas lists it as needing no card.
