Descope offers a developer-friendly platform for adding passwordless authentication, multi-factor authentication, and user management to web and mobile applications. The Free Forever plan covers up to 7,500 monthly active users, 10 tenants, and 3 SSO connections with no card required; exceeding it means upgrading rather than being billed.
