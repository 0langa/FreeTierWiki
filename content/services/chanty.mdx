You use Chanty in the browser or in its apps for iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows. The free plan suits a very small team that wants chat and simple task tracking in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs 0 euros forever. It allows up to 5 team members, plus 1 guest user and 1 multi conversation guest. You get unlimited public and private chats, unlimited announcement channels, voice messages, scheduled and disappearing messages, and unlimited searchable history. One-on-one audio and video calls are unlimited. Built-in task management lets you convert a message to a task. File storage is 20GB per team. You can connect up to 10 integrations, and support comes from the community.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you add a sixth member or fill the 20GB of storage. The Business plan is the next step up. Business costs 3 euros per user per month and adds unlimited group calls, unlimited screen sharing, task boards, and more. The pricing page shows a free sign-up that asks for an email address and does not mention a card for the free plan.

## Gotchas

- Group audio and video calls and screen sharing are in the Business plan list, not the free one.
- The free plan lists up to 10 integrations. The pricing page lists unlimited integrations under the Enterprise plan, and its cost example mentions unlimited integrations on Business.
- Free guests are limited to 1 guest user and 1 multi conversation guest.

## Questions people ask

### Is Chanty free?

Yes. The free plan costs 0 euros forever for up to 5 team members. It includes unlimited chats, one-on-one calls, task management, and 20GB of file storage per team.

### Does Chanty need a credit card?

The pricing page shows a free sign-up that asks for an email address and does not mention a card for the free plan. The free plan is listed at 0 euros forever.

### Can I make group video calls on the free plan?

Not on the free plan. It lists unlimited one-on-one audio and video calls, while unlimited group calls and screen sharing belong to the Business plan.
