You manage everything in the ImprovMX dashboard: add a domain, point its DNS at ImprovMX, and create aliases. It suits owners of a personal or project domain who just want mail to land in their normal inbox.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and covers one domain with 25 aliases. It forwards up to 500 emails per day. The email log keeps 7 days of history, and the pricing page lists a 99% uptime guarantee.

A catch-all alias is available through a wildcard alias. The free plan has no Rules routing, and webhooks start on the Premium plan. The pricing page says sending email is a paid feature, so the free plan is for receiving and forwarding only, not for sending from your own domain.

## When you hit a limit

ImprovMX emails you as you get close to the daily limit and again when you reach it. After that it stops processing mail for the domain until the quota resets at 00:00 UTC. Mail that arrives in the meantime bounces to the sender with a short over-quota message that asks them to try again later.

Nothing is billed automatically. You either wait for the reset or upgrade to get higher limits right away.

## Gotchas

- The free plan covers one domain. A second domain needs a paid plan.
- DNS changes can take 24 to 48 hours to propagate, so forwarding may not work right after setup.
- Mail over the daily limit bounces, so a busy catch-all address can lose messages that senders do not retry.

## Questions people ask

### Is ImprovMX free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and covers 1 domain, 25 aliases, and 500 forwarded emails per day. The pricing page says the free plan stays free.

### Does ImprovMX need a credit card?

No. The home page says no credit card is required to get started.

### What happens when I go over the ImprovMX limit?

ImprovMX stops processing mail for that domain until 00:00 UTC. Senders get a bounce message, and you can upgrade to raise the limit at once.
