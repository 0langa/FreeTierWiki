Rime provides speech models through a hosted service. An application can generate speech from text or try a different audio model for a voice workflow. A first evaluation should use representative audio or text so you can compare the result as well as the amount of usage.

## What the free plan gives you

Free provides $50 of usage each month and requires no card. The plan advertises access across Rime’s listed models, including speech synthesis, transcription and speech-to-speech. Each operation has a published rate on the pricing page, so the dollar balance does not translate to the same number of minutes for every model. For example, a synthesis-only demonstration has a different meter from an interactive call. Review which requests you plan to make and inspect the account’s balance after a test. Use the ongoing monthly credit when planning regular usage, since temporary model promotions can end.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says Free credit does not roll over and continuing after it is exhausted requires moving to Flex and adding a card. Flex has no monthly fee or minimum; charges follow model usage. The exact exhaustion error is not documented. Check the balance before a scheduled voice test and leave capacity available for any follow-up. Separate transcription, generated speech, and interactive call usage in your logs. That makes it easier to estimate which parts of the workflow will need extra capacity in a paid deployment.

## Gotchas

- The balance renews monthly without rollover. Synthesis is metered by generated characters, approximately 1,000 per displayed minute; transcription and Prism use audio duration.
- Keep other services in a call pipeline separate when estimating total cost.
- The all-plan transcription promotion ended November 19, 2026. From November 20, STT costs $0.002 per minute on Flex or $0.0016 on Scale; Free still includes STT within its monthly balance.

## Questions people ask

### Does Rime require a card for Free?

The provider says no. Free includes a fresh monthly usage balance.

### Is $50 a fixed number of generated speech minutes?

No. Check the rate for the model and operation you use.

### How does paid usage start?

Rime lists Flex separately as a plan with minute-based billing to a card.
