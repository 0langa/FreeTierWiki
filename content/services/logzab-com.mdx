You call a single HTTP endpoint from your app each time a user does something worth recording, and review the entries in the LogZab dashboard. It suits small apps that need an audit log without building one.

## What the free plan gives you

The Always Free tier includes all audit trail features. It allows up to 5 apps, 1,000 audit logs per month, and 1 month of retention. You register for free and can upgrade at any time.

LogZab counts audit logs from the first day of the month to the last. The paid plans have unlimited apps. The first paid plan costs $29 per month with 10,000 audit logs per month and 1 year of retention. The next costs $49 per month with 20,000 audit logs and 3 years of retention.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 1,000 audit logs on the free tier. The limit is a monthly count, and it resets automatically the next month.

Deleting an app does not give the limit back. Only the monthly reset does. To log more entries, move to a paid plan, which you can do at any time.

## Gotchas

- The home page says plans start free for development purpose, so do not plan production audit trails on the free tier.
- Deleting an app does not raise the audit log limit.
- Retention on Free is 1 month, which is short for audits.

## Questions people ask

### Is LogZab free?

Yes. The Always Free tier gives you up to 5 apps and 1,000 audit logs per month, with 1 month of retention. You can register for free.

### Can I use the LogZab free tier in production?

The home page says plans start free for development purpose. Treat the free tier as a development start and move to a paid plan for production.

### What does an audit log entry contain in LogZab?

Each entry has the user who acted, the object, the action such as create or edit, and the app path. Fields such as description and tag are optional.
