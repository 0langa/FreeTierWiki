You use Brevo through its web app for contacts and campaigns, and through a REST API, an SMTP relay, and SDKs for Node.js, Python, and PHP for app email. It suits small senders who want newsletters and transactional email on one account.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page calls the Free plan free forever, with no credit card needed. When you create an account you get the Free plan, and once Brevo approves your account for sending you can send up to 300 emails per day.

You can store up to 100,000 contacts without paying extra. The same limit applies to the free SMTP server, which works for bulk email, transactional messages, and testing. Paid plans start from 5,000 emails per month. SMS credit is sold separately, so SMS is not part of the free sending.

## When you hit a limit

The daily allowance resets without rollover. At 300 sends, transactional mail can hold up to 1,000 extra messages in a retry queue; messages beyond that are not delivered. Campaigns sent to larger lists need manual resumption on later days, up to 300 sends per day.

Free automations stop admitting new contacts after 2,000 unique contacts, while existing participants continue. Paid plans and prepaid credits have separate billing. Branding removal requires the Starter add-on or Standard and above; buying Starter alone does not include removal.

## Gotchas

- Sending starts only after Brevo approves your account, so you cannot send a campaign in the first minutes.
- Free emails carry the Sent with Brevo footer. Removing it is a paid feature.
- Before you send transactional email through the API, you must set up a sender and a sending domain.

## Questions people ask

### Is Brevo free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever with no credit card. It lets you send up to 300 emails per day after Brevo approves your account.

### Does Brevo have a free SMTP server?

Yes. The free SMTP server uses the same Free plan, with a sending limit of 300 emails per day. You can use it for bulk email, transactional messages, and testing.

### How many contacts can I store on the Brevo free plan?

You can store up to 100,000 contacts without paying extra, according to the free SMTP server page.
