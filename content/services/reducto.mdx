You can try documents in the Studio web app, call the REST API, use the Python or Node SDK, or use a command line tool. It fits teams that already process many documents in AI workflows. This page covers the free credit.


## What the free plan gives you

The Standard plan is pay as you go, and every new account starts with $150 in free usage. The pricing page lists no page limit on the Standard plan. It includes up to 5 seats for Reducto Studio.

The credit covers every product. List rates are $10 per 1,000 pages for Parse and $20 per 1,000 pages for Extract, and Extract includes parsing. Deep Extract costs $40 per 1,000 pages. Classify costs $7.50 per 1,000 pages.

Reducto also offers up to $5,000 in migration credits if you move from another document processor. A Startup Program gives free credits to early-stage teams.

## When you hit a limit

The $150 is a one-time credit. After it runs out, the page says standard rates apply. This means the free plan turns into a paid one, so watch your usage in the dashboard before you run large batches.

The pricing page says nothing about a hard stop. It does not describe what happens to a request when the credit reaches zero. The pages do not mention a credit card at sign-up.

## Gotchas

- The credit is one-time. It is not a monthly allowance.
- Options add cost. OCR data return costs $2 per 1,000 pages, and prompted blocks and custom regions cost $5 per 1,000 pages.
- Batch processing takes 20% off Parse and Extract, per the pricing page.

## Questions people ask

### Is Reducto free?

Only for the first $150 of usage. New accounts get that credit on the Standard plan, and standard rates apply after it.

### How much does Reducto cost after the free credit?

Parse costs $10 per 1,000 pages and Extract costs $20 per 1,000 pages. Other products cost more, and options add to the price.

### Does Reducto have a free trial of higher plans?

You can start on the Standard plan and get an API key right away. For a trial of the Growth or Enterprise tier, you fill in the contact form.
