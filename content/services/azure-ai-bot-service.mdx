Azure AI Bot Service provides a managed platform for building, deploying, and scaling intelligent bots with integration to Azure and popular messaging channels. Standard channels are free with unlimited messages, and premium channels include 10,000 free messages per month. Underlying compute and premium channel overages require paid tiers.
