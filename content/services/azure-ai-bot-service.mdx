You create the bot resource in the Azure portal and write the bot code with an SDK in C#, JavaScript, or Python. It suits developers who already work on Azure and want their bot on Teams or a website.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free tier gives you unlimited messages on standard channels. Standard channels are Microsoft first-party services, such as Microsoft Teams, and services with public bot APIs, such as Facebook and Slack. The pricing page points to the docs for the full list.

Premium channels get 10,000 messages per month on the Free tier. These channels let a bot talk to users inside your own app or website. They use the open-source Direct Line and Web Chat clients, which you can customize.

The page shows a second tier called S1. It prices premium messages per 1,000 messages, but the price field on the page is blank.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you send more than 10,000 premium messages in a month. It lists S1 as the tier that charges for premium messages by the 1,000, so going past the free amount likely means moving to S1.

You also pay for the resources your bot uses. The page says that apart from the Bot Service price, you pay for resources consumed, such as hosting. A free bot channel does not make the rest of the bot free. An Azure subscription with a card is needed.

## Gotchas

- Premium channels are the ones used for your own website or app, and they are the ones with the monthly message cap.
- The hosting that runs your bot code is billed separately from the Bot Service tier.
- The S1 premium price is not shown on the pricing page, so use the Azure pricing calculator before you plan to exceed the free amount.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Bot Service free?

Partly. The Free tier has unlimited messages on standard channels and 10,000 premium channel messages per month. Resources your bot uses are billed on top.

### What happens when I go over the free message amount?

The pricing page does not say. It shows an S1 tier that charges premium messages per 1,000, with no price listed.

### Does Azure AI Bot Service need a credit card?

You need an Azure subscription, and the atlas records that a card is required for it. The pricing page itself only offers a free Azure account link.
