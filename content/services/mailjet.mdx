You send through the Email API, SMTP relay, or one of the language libraries, and you build campaigns and templates in the web app. It suits teams that want one account for app mail and newsletters.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It includes 6,000 emails a month, with a daily sending maximum of 200 emails. The current pricing card also caps Free at 1,000 contacts. You get the API and SMTP relay, email templates, and GDPR-compliant subscription forms, so you can build a small mailing list and send to it from your own app.

The pricing table lists segmentation, email address validation, and email previews as paid features. A dedicated IP is for the Premium plan or larger plans on request. Online customer support is included for the first month only on the Free plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says the free plan has a sending maximum of 200 emails per day, and the 6,000 emails per month. To get more features or send more, you must upgrade. Starter costs $9 per month and has no daily sending limit.

The pricing page says that when you run out of email credit, further emails are billed per 1,000 beyond the plan limit. It does not say whether that applies to the Free plan, and it does not describe what happens at 200 a day beyond the upgrade note. Email credits do not roll over to the next month.

## Gotchas

- The daily cap of 200 is separate from the monthly 6,000, so a busy day can stop sending before the month is used up.
- Segmentation is not part of the free plan. The pricing table lists it from the Essential plan at $19 per month.
- You must verify the domain or subdomain you send from before you send your first email.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mailjet free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and lets you send 6,000 emails a month, with a maximum of 200 per day. It does not need a credit card at sign-up.

### Does Mailjet need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the free plan at sign-up. You must upgrade to get more features or higher volume.

### Does Mailjet have an SMTP relay on the free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes the Email API and the SMTP relay, so you can send from an app with either one.
