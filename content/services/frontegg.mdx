You set up login in the Frontegg Portal and use a hosted login page or embed the login with a Frontegg SDK. It suits SaaS teams that sell to businesses and want organizations and SSO built in.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 per month. It covers 7,500 monthly active users and 5 enterprise connections for SSO or SCIM. You also get unlimited organizations, and Frontegg lists enterprise SSO and unlimited tenants on the page.

Login runs on a hosted page by default. You can switch to an embedded login that lives inside your own app. Both styles support social login, SSO through SAML or OIDC, and passwordless methods such as passkeys and magic links. The pricing page also lists a self-service admin portal, entitlements, and M2M tokens as separate pricing options. It does not say which of them the free plan includes.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says you pay only for what you use, starting free up to 7,500 users. It shows a calculator with sliders for monthly active users and monthly enterprise connections. The page does not give a price once you pass the free amount, and it does not say whether your app stops.

For large or custom needs, the Enterprise plan uses a Contact sales button. If you expect more than 7,500 monthly active users, ask Frontegg what the next step costs before you launch.

## Gotchas

- The free amount counts monthly active users, not sign-ups.
- The pricing page does not say that entitlements, M2M tokens, or the self-service admin portal are in the free plan. Ask Frontegg before you rely on them.
- The pricing page does not state a price past the free amount. Use the calculator or ask sales.

## Questions people ask

### Is Frontegg free?

Yes, up to a point. The free plan costs $0 per month and covers 7,500 monthly active users and 5 enterprise connections.

### Does Frontegg need a credit card?

The pricing page shows a Start for free button and does not mention a card. The atlas lists the free plan as needing no card.

### What happens when I pass the Frontegg free user limit?

The pricing page says you pay for what you use after the free amount, but it does not publish a price. Contact Frontegg sales for the next plan.
