You set up login in the Frontegg Portal and use a hosted login page or embed the login with a Frontegg SDK. It suits SaaS teams that sell to businesses and want organizations and SSO built in.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 per month. It covers 7,500 monthly active users and 5 enterprise connections for SSO or SCIM. You also get unlimited organizations, and Frontegg lists enterprise SSO and unlimited tenants on the page.

Login runs on a hosted page by default. You can switch to an embedded login that lives inside your own app. Both styles support social login, SSO through SAML or OIDC, and passwordless methods such as passkeys and magic links. The pay-as-you-go comparison includes the embedded self-service admin portal and authorization features. Custom domains are also listed as always included. M2M token volume is a separate input to the price calculator.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says you pay only for what you use, starting free up to 7,500 users. It shows a calculator with sliders for monthly active users and monthly enterprise connections. The calculator also includes M2M tokens. The page text does not publish a per-user price past the free allowance, so use the calculator for an estimate. The page does not state whether your app stops at the free limit.

For large or custom needs, the Enterprise plan uses a Contact sales button. If you expect more than 7,500 monthly active users, ask Frontegg what the next step costs before you launch.

## Gotchas

- The free amount counts monthly active users, not sign-ups.
- M2M tokens are separately metered in the calculator; do not assume an unlimited free token allowance.
- Prices depend on the calculator inputs; the Enterprise option uses custom sales pricing.

## Questions people ask

### Is Frontegg free?

Yes, up to a point. The free plan costs $0 per month and covers 7,500 monthly active users and 5 enterprise connections.

### Does Frontegg need a credit card?

The pricing page shows a Start for free button and does not mention a card. The free plan card requirement is not confirmed.

### What happens when I pass the Frontegg free user limit?

The pricing page says you pay only for what you use and offers a calculator for estimates. It does not say whether your app stops at the free limit, so confirm your expected mix and limit behavior before launch.
