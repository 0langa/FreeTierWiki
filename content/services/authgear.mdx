You manage your project in the Authgear Portal and add login with a client SDK or the OIDC protocol. It suits small teams that want the login screens handled for them.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It includes unlimited monthly active users, 2 applications, and 2 admin seats. Every plan includes all auth features, so the Free plan has social login, MFA, and passkeys too.

You also get 100 SMS or WhatsApp one-time password messages per month, and logs are kept for 1 day. Support comes from the community. The paid Developers plan costs $50 per month and also has unlimited users, but it meters SMS and WhatsApp messages and adds priority email support. The Business plan costs $500 per month, with 25,000 monthly active users, 5 applications, and 60 days of logs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after the 100 free SMS or WhatsApp messages in a month. Paid plans bill those messages by use, and the Free plan has no add-ons for extra applications, seats, or environments.

If you want more capacity, you subscribe from the Billing section of the Authgear Portal. You can drop back to the free version at any time, and you then lose the extra features and capacity of the paid plan. Authgear is open source, so you can also export your data and run it on your own servers.

## Gotchas

- The managed cloud runs on Google Cloud in the USA today. An EU data region is listed as coming soon.
- Only 100 SMS or WhatsApp messages are included each month, so phone-based login can use them up fast.
- The Free plan keeps logs for just 1 day.

## Questions people ask

### Is Authgear free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and has unlimited monthly active users, 2 applications, and 2 admin seats. No credit card is needed.

### Is Authgear open source?

Yes. The pricing FAQ says Authgear is open source and based on PostgreSQL, so you can export the data and run it on your own servers.

### What do I lose if I cancel a paid Authgear plan?

Cancelling puts you back on the free version. You lose the extra features and the extra capacity for monthly active users.
