Contentful is a headless CMS: you model content in its web editor and deliver it to applications through APIs. Its Free plan is a place to learn and test the product. The current usage terms explicitly exclude commercial use, so do not choose it as free hosting for a business website or production marketing project.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists Free at $0, free forever, with 10 users, 2 roles, and 2 locales. It includes 1 Starter Space. That space provides 25 content types, 2 environments, and 10,000 records, which gives you a bounded place to experiment with content models and publishing.

Monthly utility allowances are 100,000 API calls and 50 GB of asset bandwidth. The API allowance combines calls to the Content Management API, Content Preview API, and Content Delivery API; these are not three separate allowances. Asset delivery consumes bandwidth independently of the request count. Community support resources are available on Free.

## When you hit a limit

The official usage-limits article, updated August 14, 2026, says additional usage is not allowed on Free. It also says Contentful can throttle, suspend, or terminate services for noncompliance. It does not promise one particular endpoint response or a precise restoration time after the allowance is exhausted, so do not assume assets continue serving without interruption.

Paid plans have different utility allowances and billing terms. The paid overage policy is not permission to exceed Free quotas. If your test is becoming a commercial project, choose an appropriate paid plan before launch rather than treating Free as a commercial production tier. The reviewed pricing and usage pages do not explicitly confirm Free's card requirement.

## Gotchas

- Free is exclusively for testing and learning. The commercial-use restriction matters even when your traffic is comfortably below the numerical limits.
- Content Management, Preview, and Delivery API calls share the monthly request allowance. Development scripts and preview traffic can consume it too.
- A Starter Space has its own model, record, locale, and environment limits. The user allowance is not the only capacity to check.

## Questions people ask

### Is Contentful free?

Yes, for testing and learning. Free includes 10 users, 1 Starter Space, 100,000 monthly API calls, and 50 GB of monthly asset bandwidth.

### Can I use Contentful Free commercially?

No. The current usage-limits article explicitly excludes commercial use on Free.

### Does Contentful bill Free overages?

Additional usage is not allowed on Free. The provider reserves throttling, suspension, or termination for noncompliance; it does not publish a guaranteed single cap response.
