Contentful is a headless CMS. You model content in a web app and read it through APIs. The notes below come from the entry data, because the Contentful site could not be read for this review.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is described as free forever, with no credit card needed. It includes 10 users and 1 Starter Space. It also includes 50 GB of asset bandwidth through the CDN each month, with no overages. A space is the container that holds your content model, entries, and assets, so the Starter Space is where one small project lives.

Support on the Free plan comes from the community only. The entry lists the Lite and Enterprise plans as the way to get more bandwidth and more spaces. The limits listed here are the ones we could not re-check against the Contentful pricing page, so confirm them there before you plan around them.

## When you hit a limit

The Contentful pricing page could not be read for this review, so this section follows the entry data only. The entry says the Free plan has a hard bandwidth cap with no overages. In plain terms, you should not get a surprise bill from asset bandwidth on the Free plan, because there is no card on file and no overage rate.

What happens at the cap, for example whether assets stop loading until the month resets, is not confirmed here. If you need more than 50 GB of CDN bandwidth, or more than 1 Starter Space, the entry says to look at the Lite or Enterprise plan.

## Gotchas

- Community support only on the Free plan, so plan to solve problems with the docs and community forums.
- The Free plan has 1 Starter Space, so a second project likely needs a paid plan.
- The pricing page and docs could not be read for this review, so check the live Contentful pages before you rely on any number here.

### Is Contentful free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever, with 10 users, 1 Starter Space, and 50 GB of CDN bandwidth each month.

### Does Contentful charge overages on the free plan?

The entry says no. It lists the bandwidth as a hard cap with no overages, but the pricing page could not be read to confirm that.

### Does Contentful need a credit card?

No. The entry says the Free plan does not need a credit card.
