ConnectyCube is a backend with SDKs for chat and calling. You add its SDK to your app and get 1-1 and group messaging, multimedia sharing, typing indicators, and voice and video calls without building the realtime server yourself. SDKs cover Flutter, React Native, Web, Cordova, NativeScript, iOS, and Android. The Free plan suits prototypes and small apps.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and runs on the public cloud. It allows up to 1,000 users per account, 5 GB of storage, and a retention policy of 1 month. Group chats can have at most 10 participants.

Core features on Free include 1-1 chat, group chat, and peer-to-peer audio and video calls, with video recording on the client side only. Support on the Free plan is community support through GitHub and Stack Overflow. The page lists the user, storage, and participant numbers as monthly usage limits.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when an account passes 1,000 users or fills 5 GB of storage. The Free plan has no metered billing, so going over does not add a charge on its own.

The next plan, Hobby, costs $39 a month for 10,000 users, 10 GB of storage, 3 months of retention, and group chats of up to 30 people. Conference calls and meetings start on the Advanced plan at $99 a month, which allows 25,000 users.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has a retention policy of 1 month, so plan on losing older history.
- Calls on Free are peer-to-peer; conference audio and video calls need a paid plan.
- Group chats are capped at 10 participants on the Free plan.

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month and allows up to 1,000 users with 5 GB of storage.

### Does the free plan include video calls?

Yes, peer-to-peer audio and video calls. Conference calls and meetings are on paid plans.

### How long does ConnectyCube keep messages on the free plan?

The Free plan has a retention policy of 1 month.
