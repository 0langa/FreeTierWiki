You use Xitoring in a web dashboard, with a CLI, a mobile app, and an API on paid plans. For servers you install a small agent called Xitogent with one command. It suits small teams that want uptime checks and server metrics in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

The website monitoring page says the free plan has 20 monitors with 1-minute checks from 15+ global locations, with no credit card and no trial expiry. HTTP/HTTPS, DNS, SSL, ping, and keyword checks are included. Every plan includes a hosted, branded status page.

The pricing page lists paid plans from $4.99 per month for Solo, with a 14-day free trial on any paid plan. It also lists special plans: students free while they study, startups 1 year free, and non-profit and open-source projects free for life.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says you get a notification when you approach your plan's limit. Existing monitors keep running, but you cannot add new ones until you upgrade or remove unused monitors.

At the end of the paid trial, you can downgrade to the Free plan or subscribe. The FAQ says all plans include email alerts and that paid plans unlock more channels such as Slack, SMS, and webhooks.

## Gotchas

- The website monitoring page says Free includes email, push, and webhook alerts, but the pricing FAQ lists webhooks as a paid channel. Check which alert channels your account has.
- Optional 30-second intervals are only on paid plans.
- Full REST API access is listed for the Business and Enterprise plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Xitoring free?

Yes. The free plan has 20 monitors at 1-minute intervals with email alerts and a hosted status page. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I exceed my Xitoring monitor limit?

You get a notification when you approach the limit. Existing monitors keep running, but you cannot add new ones until you upgrade or remove some.

### Does Xitoring have a free trial of paid plans?

Yes. Any paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required. After the trial you can downgrade to the Free plan.
