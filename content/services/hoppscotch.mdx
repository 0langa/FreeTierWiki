You use Hoppscotch in the browser at hoppscotch.io, with no install needed to send a request. It suits developers who want a light API client, and it can be self-hosted.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says Hoppscotch is free to use for everyone, and the Free plan is $0 forever. It lists collections and environments, pre-request scripts and tests, and a collection runner. The docs add that the REST client supports HTTP methods, headers, parameters, body, auth, and pre-request scripts, and that it can run tests on responses.

The Community Edition is free and open source under the MIT License. The docs say you can use it for personal and commercial projects. You host it yourself. Paid plans add team features: Business is $6 per user per month, billed annually, and Enterprise is $11.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no usage caps for the Free plan, so you do not hit a quota or a bill. The limit is in features. Team workspaces and real-time collaboration need Business, and identity and access management needs Enterprise.

If you want shared collections for a team without paying, the open-source edition on your own server is the path. You then run and update the servers yourself.

## Gotchas

- Team workspaces and real-time collaboration are part of the Business plan, not the Free plan.
- Business and Enterprise prices are per user and billed annually on the pricing page.
- The GitHub README describes unlimited teams, but the current pricing page puts team workspaces in Business, so follow the pricing page.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hoppscotch free?

Yes. The pricing page says Hoppscotch is free to use for everyone, and the Free plan is $0 forever. Paid plans add team workspaces and access management.

### Can I self-host Hoppscotch?

Yes. The Community Edition is free and open source under the MIT License. You can use it for personal and commercial projects.

### Does Hoppscotch need a credit card?

The Free plan needs no payment, and the pricing page lists no card step. Business and Enterprise are billed per user.
