You create and manage deployments in the EMQX Cloud console, then connect any MQTT client or SDK. The docs recommend MQTTX for testing. It suits developers who want a broker without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

Every month you get a free quota that is used first. It covers 1 million session minutes, 1 GB of traffic, and 1 million rule action executions. One session minute is one client connection for one minute. The product page says this is enough to keep 23 devices online all day for 30 days.

Traffic counts all public network traffic in and out of your deployment. Rule actions are the executions of data integration rules. The product page says you do not need a credit card to start.

## When you hit a limit

Each deployment has a spend limit, and the default is 0. That means the deployment can only use the free quota. When the spend limit is 0 and the quota runs out, the docs list troubleshooting topics for free connection minutes, free traffic, and free rule actions limits. The pages do not describe the exact behavior.

If you raise the spend limit above 0, usage past the free amounts is billed. You then choose what happens at the limit: suspend the deployment, or get a reminder and keep billing. Rates are $2.00 per extra million session minutes, $0.15 per GB, and $0.25 per extra million rule actions.

## Gotchas

- A deployment with no active client connections for 30 days is stopped by the system. If you do not restart it within 30 days, it can be deleted.
- A connection shorter than a minute is rounded up and billed as a full session minute.
- Offline clients with persistent sessions count as sessions, so they can use up your connection allowance.

## Questions people ask

### Is EMQX Serverless free?

Yes. The forever-free quota is 1 million session minutes, 1 GB of traffic, and 1 million rule actions each month. No credit card is required.

### What happens if I use up the EMQX free quota?

With the default spend limit of 0, only the free quota is used. If you set a limit above 0, you can have the deployment suspend at the limit or keep billing.

### Does an EMQX Serverless deployment stop when it is idle?

Yes. If no clients connect for 30 consecutive days, the system stops the deployment. You can start it again in the console.
