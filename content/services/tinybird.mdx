You work with the Tinybird CLI and datafiles, or with SDKs, and call published endpoints over HTTP. It suits developers who add analytics features to their own products.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is a production-grade Tinybird instance with no time limit. Ingest connectors, real-time querying, and managed endpoints are all available. It runs on shared infrastructure with no credit card.

You get 0.25 vCPU with up to 3x burst, 10 GB of included storage, and 1,000 requests per day per organization. Queries through API endpoints and the Query API use at most 1 thread. Support is through the community Slack.

AI tools in the MCP server and the Explorations UI include $25 of LLM credits per billing cycle. When the credits run out, LLM requests stop until the next billing cycle.

The Developer plan is the next step up. The pricing page lists it from $25 or $49 per month, depending on the section, with 25 GB of storage.

## When you hit a limit

The vCPU limit is hard. Requests that exceed the burst capacity return HTTP 429. The LLM limit is a soft stop, and requests resume in the next billing cycle. The docs do not spell out what happens when you pass 1,000 requests per day.

Paid plans handle bursts differently. Instead of throttling, the Developer and SaaS plans let queries burst beyond their vCPUs and charge $0.0002 per second for the extra compute. To upgrade, go to Organization settings and Billing, then pick a vCPU size.

## Gotchas

- The limit is per organization, so all your projects and workspaces share 1,000 requests a day.
- Clients should retry 429 responses with exponential backoff. Tinybird can also return them when it applies concurrency limits.
- The pricing page shows two different starting prices for the Developer plan, $25 and $49, so check the current price before you upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tinybird free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and needs no credit card. It includes 10 GB of storage, 0.25 vCPU, and 1,000 requests per day.

### What happens if I exceed the Tinybird free plan limits?

Requests that exceed the vCPU burst capacity return HTTP 429. LLM requests stop until the next billing cycle when the credits run out.

### How do I upgrade Tinybird from Free?

Go to Organization settings and Billing, then select your vCPU size for the Developer plan. SaaS and Enterprise plans go through sales.
