You install the `flyctl` command line tool, run `fly launch` in your project, and then `fly deploy`. A dashboard shows trial status and billing. It suits developers who are comfortable with containers and the command line.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free plan for new organizations. The pricing docs say new organizations have no free tier or monthly free usage allowance. What you get is a free trial of up to 2 hours of Machine runtime or 7 days, whichever comes first. You do not need a credit card to start it.

During the trial you can run up to 10 Machines with up to 2 vCPUs and 4GB of memory each, and use 20GB of volume storage. Trial Machines stop on their own after running for 5 minutes. Dedicated IPv4 addresses and performance-optimized vCPUs are not included. Some things stay free later, such as the first 10GB of volume snapshots, the first 10 single-hostname certificates, and inbound data.

## When you hit a limit

When the trial ends, or you use up its resources, your apps stop running. You cannot launch new Machines, attach volumes, or deploy changes until billing is set up. Adding a card is optional during the trial, but it ends the trial and starts billing from that point.

After that you pay for what you use. The cheapest always-on app is a shared-cpu-1x Machine with 256MB of RAM, which costs $2.19 per 30 days of continuous runtime plus data transfer. Managed Postgres starts at $29 per month for Standard. The docs also say all organizations except Linked Organizations need a card on file.

## Gotchas

- Organizations on plans discontinued in October 2024 keep the free allowances from those plans, but new organizations do not.
- Adding a card ends the free trial at once, and usage counts toward your bill from that point.
- Outbound data to the internet is billed per GB, and the rate depends on the region group.

## Questions people ask

### Is Fly.io free?

Not for new organizations. They get a trial of up to 2 hours of Machine runtime or 7 days, then pay as they go.

### Does Fly.io need a credit card?

Not to start the trial. After the trial ends you need to add a credit card to keep using billable resources.

### What happens when the Fly.io free trial ends?

Your apps stop running. You cannot launch Machines, attach volumes, or deploy changes until you add a payment method.
