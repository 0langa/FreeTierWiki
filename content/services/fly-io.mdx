Fly.io lets you deploy containerized applications globally, with simple CLI tools and a developer-friendly experience. New organizations no longer get a free allowance: you get a one-time trial of 2 machine-hours or 7 days, then you pay as you go, and apps stop after the trial unless you add a payment method. Legacy Hobby plans keep their old allowance. Fly.io still suits developers who want fast, global deployments and are ready to pay for ongoing usage.
