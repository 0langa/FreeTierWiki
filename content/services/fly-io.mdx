Fly.io runs apps from container images as Machines. You use the flyctl command line and a generated fly.toml configuration to deploy them, then inspect resource usage and billing in the dashboard. The Free plan is for the personal organization created with your account, with community support and one member.

## What the free plan gives you

New accounts start on Free: one-time $1 without a card, expiring after 7 days. A card enables $5 monthly, fully available immediately and renewed on the 1st; remaining $1 is spent first. New card-backed Free organizations also receive $30 Sprites credit, used before the $5.

Allowances cover Machines, volumes/snapshots, transfer, Sprites and one $4/month Developer Managed Postgres cluster. Limits: 3 Machines, 5 GB/volume, 3 Sprites, one Free organization/card. Performance Machines, other Postgres plans, Tigris/Upstash extensions, dedicated IPv4, static egress and linked organizations are excluded.

All plans separately include first 10 GB monthly snapshots, first 10 single-hostname certificates, inbound transfer and shared IPv4/IPv6. Plans discontinued October 2024 retain legacy allowances.

## When you hit a limit

The $5 is not a spending cap. At $1 remaining, Fly emails; exhaustion automatically moves the organization to Pay As You Go and bills the entire month's usage from the first cent. Voluntary upgrades have the same effect.

Without a card, $1 exhaustion or seven-day expiry blocks new resources and triggers email. Existing-resource behavior is unspecified; former trial stop/deletion rules should not be assumed.

The smallest continuously running Machine costs $2.19/30 days in Ashburn/Secaucus, plus transfer; regions differ. Stopped root filesystems cost $0.15/GB/month. Volumes cost $0.15/provisioned GB/month, including detached or stopped-machine volumes.

## Gotchas

- `fly deploy` creates two Machines per app by default. Use `--ha=false` for one to conserve the three-Machine allowance. Crossing $5 makes the entire month payable. A $5 allowance is not a $5 discount on a larger bill. The Free-plan guide lists a $4 Developer Postgres option; the general resource table still starts its main Postgres list at $38 Basic. Use the plan-specific guide for Free eligibility.
- A card is required for recurring free usage, even though the initial $1 credit needs none.
- An existing personal Pay As You Go organization can switch to Free only after removing resources and members that the plan does not permit.

## Questions people ask

### Does Fly.io have an ongoing free plan?

Yes. The personal organization can receive $5 of usage monthly after adding a card, subject to the Free plan's resource limits. Without a card, the credit is one-time and lasts at most 7 days.

### Will Fly.io stop before charging me?

Not on the recurring allowance. Exceeding $5 automatically moves the organization to Pay As You Go and bills the entire month's usage, including usage previously covered by the allowance.

### Does adding a card remove the Free plan limits?

No. Adding a card keeps Free and enables the monthly allowance. An explicit upgrade or automatic conversion after exceeding $5 removes the limits and starts full-month billing.
