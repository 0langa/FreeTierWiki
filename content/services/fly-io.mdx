You install the `flyctl` command line tool, run `fly launch` in your project, and then `fly deploy`. A dashboard shows trial status and billing. It suits developers who are comfortable with containers and the command line.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free plan for new organizations. The pricing docs say new organizations have no free tier or monthly free usage allowance. What you get is a free trial: $1 of credit for Machines, Sprites, and Managed Postgres, which lasts 7 days or until you use it up. You do not need a credit card to start it.

During the trial you can run up to 10 Machines at a time, each up to shared-cpu-2x with up to 4GB of memory, plus up to 20GB of Volumes and 3 Sprites. Trial Machines stop on their own after running for 5 minutes. Dedicated IPv4 addresses, performance-optimized vCPUs, and extensions such as Tigris are not available. Some things stay free later, such as the first 10GB of volume snapshots each month, the first 10 single-hostname certificates, and inbound data.

## When you hit a limit

When your credit is used up or expires, your Machines stay stopped, and you cannot start them or create new resources until you add a credit card. Fly.io may then delete your trial resources, though your apps are kept. Adding a card during the trial lifts its limits, and any credit you have left still applies.

After that you pay for what you use. The cheapest always-on app is a shared-cpu-1x Machine with 256MB of RAM, which costs $2.19 per 30 days of continuous runtime in Ashburn or Secaucus, plus data transfer; regional rates differ. Managed Postgres starts at $38 per month for Basic. The docs also say all organizations except Linked Organizations need a card on file.

## Gotchas

- Organizations on plans discontinued in October 2024 keep the free allowances from those plans, but new organizations do not.
- Adding a card lifts the trial limits, and usage counts toward your bill once any remaining credit is used up.
- Outbound data to the internet is billed per GB, and the rate depends on the region group.

## Questions people ask

### Is Fly.io free?

Not for new organizations. They get a trial of $1 in credit that lasts 7 days or until it is used up, then pay as they go.

### Does Fly.io need a credit card?

Not to start the trial. After the trial ends you need to add a credit card to keep using billable resources.

### What happens when the Fly.io free trial ends?

Your Machines stay stopped, and you cannot start them or create new resources until you add a credit card. Fly.io may delete trial resources if you do not add one.
