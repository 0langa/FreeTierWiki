You create an account, copy an API key from the dashboard, and call one HTTPS endpoint, `/v3/ipgeo`, from any language. It suits developers who want one call that returns location, currency, ASN, and time zone for testing or a small side project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and gives you 1000 API credits per day, for life. The credits reset at 00:00 UTC. The provider states no separate per-second or per-minute rate limit, including on Free. The pricing page says no credit card is required. A successful lookup charges 1 credit, so the plan covers up to 1000 basic lookups a day.

The free plan returns location, country metadata, currency, ASN, and time zone. Lookup by domain, hostname, company data, security flags, bulk lookup, and translated responses need a paid plan. The first paid plan, Starter, costs $19 a month for 150000 credits. The pricing page explicitly allows production and commercial use on Free, within its daily allowance.

## When you hit a limit

Free, also called Developer, has no surcharges or overages. At its daily credit limit, the API returns HTTP 429 rather than billing.

Paid overages are enabled by default, charging excess in blocks up to a plan-specific surcharge cap. Disable Allow Surcharge Usage to stop at included credits; requests return HTTP 429 there or when the surcharge cap is reached. Starter charges $5 per 25,000 excess credits, capped at $500 in surcharges. Monthly paid credits reset monthly and annual credits yearly, without rollover. Check both the toggle and cap before upgrading; the paid defaults differ from Free.

## Gotchas

- Paid modules can cost multiple credits: security adds 2 and abuse contact adds 1. These modules are unavailable on Free, where a successful basic lookup costs 1 credit. Use Free from your server: browser Request Origin support and extra API keys require a paid plan. Successful responses expose `X-Credits-Charged` for exact credit accounting.
- Asking for a language other than English with a free key returns a 401 response. Naming a paid module in `include` also returns 401; `fields` and `excludes` remain available on Free.
- Production and commercial use are allowed on Free, but workloads exceeding 1,000 daily credits need paid capacity.

## Questions people ask

### Is IPGeolocation.io free?

Yes, including production and commercial use within its allowance. The Free plan gives you 1000 API credits per day and needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the IPGeolocation.io free limit?

The free plan stays capped at its daily credits and has no overage billing. The API error reference specifies HTTP 429 when a free subscription reaches its usage limit.

### Can I use the IPGeolocation.io free plan commercially?

Yes. The pricing page explicitly says production and commercial use are allowed on the Free plan. Its 1,000-credit daily limit still applies.
