WunderGraph Cosmo is a platform for GraphQL Federation: you combine several GraphQL services, called subgraphs, into one graph. The control plane and Studio web interface are hosted by WunderGraph, the wgc CLI pushes and validates schemas, and the router that serves traffic runs on your own infrastructure. The Developer plan suits a solo developer trying federation.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is described as for individual developers and small projects and is free forever. It includes a self-hosted router, 1 federated graph or monograph, up to 10 subgraphs, and up to 10M requests a month, with Discord community support.

The comparison table lists a max team size of 1, 7 days of data retention, 7 days of traffic-based breaking change detection, 1 feature flag, and 3 plugins. Features such as Apollo Federation v1 and v2 support, JWT and JWKS auth, federated subscriptions, Prometheus metrics, OpenTelemetry tracing, and advanced request tracing are included in Developer.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a graph passes 10M requests a month. The router runs on your own servers and the docs say it operates independently of the control plane, so you pay for its hosting yourself.

The next plan, Launch, costs $499 a month and raises the limits to 2 federated graphs, 250M requests a month, a team of 10, and 14 days of data retention, with email support.

## Gotchas

- The router is self-hosted on every plan, so the free plan does not cover the servers that serve your traffic.
- Rate limiting, persisted operations, and subgraph check extensions are not included in Developer.
- The jump from free to Launch at $499 a month is steep for a small team.

## Questions people ask

### Is WunderGraph Cosmo free?

Yes. The Developer plan is free forever and includes 1 federated graph and up to 10M requests a month.

### Do I need to host the Cosmo router myself?

Yes. Every plan on the pricing page lists a self-hosted router; the control plane and Studio are hosted.

### How many people can use the free plan?

The Developer plan has a max team size of 1.
