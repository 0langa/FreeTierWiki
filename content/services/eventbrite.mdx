You run Eventbrite in the browser and in the organizer app. You build an event page, add tickets, and publish; buyers find the event on the Eventbrite marketplace or through your link.

## What the free plan gives you

Eventbrite says free events do not incur ticketing platform fees. There is no overall limit stated for how many free events you can run, but each organization can have up to 100 events published at a time. On US pricing, paid tickets carry a 3.7% + $1.79 service fee per ticket, plus a 2.9% payment processing fee per order. By default the buyer pays the ticket fees, or you can absorb them when you set up your ticket types.

Tools include event pages, ticket types, promo codes, email campaigns, waitlists, and mobile check-in. The organizer pricing page still advertises Pro, but the current help center says Pro is closed to new subscribers. Existing Pro subscribers can keep their plans. Premium and Ultimate are optional paid subscriptions that Eventbrite is rolling out by country and account.

## When you hit a limit

The event-creation guide lists an organization limit of 100 events published at one time. Free events don't incur ticketing charges. Paid tickets and paid add-ons carry fees; optional subscriptions and advertising can add separate costs. Standard US online ticket sales carry a per-ticket service fee plus payment processing on the order total. If you absorb ticketing fees, Eventbrite deducts them from your payout.

## Gotchas

- The organizer subscriptions are available in selected countries and are rolling out by account. Ticketing fees vary by payout country and currency.
- Paid tickets carry two fees: a per ticket service fee and a per order processing fee.
- The page says 24/7 chat support is for paid events. Free events get help center and email support.

## Questions people ask

### Is Eventbrite free?

Yes, there are no ticketing platform fees for free tickets. Each organization can have up to 100 published events at once. US paid online tickets carry service and processing fees.

### How much does Eventbrite charge for paid tickets?

The US organizer pricing page lists 3.7% + $1.79 per ticket as a service fee, plus a 2.9% processing fee per order. Buyers pay the ticket fees by default, or you can absorb them.

### Does Eventbrite need a credit card?

The pricing page does not mention a card. It points you to Get started for free.
