You run Eventbrite in the browser and in the organizer app. You build an event page, add tickets, and publish; buyers find the event on the Eventbrite marketplace or through your link.

## What the free plan gives you

Eventbrite says the platform is always free for events with free tickets, with no ticketing platform fees, and you can publish unlimited free events. For paid tickets there is no monthly fee. Instead you pay a 3.7% + $1.79 service fee per ticket, plus a 2.9% payment processing fee per order. By default the buyer pays the ticket fees, or you can absorb them when you set up your ticket types.

The page lists tools such as custom event pages, multiple ticket types, promo codes, email campaigns, waitlists, mobile check-in, and visibility on the marketplace. Eventbrite Pro, an optional add-on for more email sending, starts at $15 per month.

## When you hit a limit

There is no stated cap on free events, so no throttle or bill applies to them. Costs appear only when you sell paid tickets, and the fees come out of each sale. Because the fee is charged per ticket and order, a small ticket price loses a larger share. You choose whether the buyer or you pays the fee. The pricing page does not say how payouts or refunds interact with fees.

## Gotchas

- The pricing plans are offered in the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom and Ireland.
- Paid tickets carry two fees: a per ticket service fee and a per order processing fee.
- The page says 24/7 chat support is for paid events. Free events get help center and email support.

## Questions people ask

### Is Eventbrite free?

Yes for free events. Eventbrite says you can publish unlimited free events with no ticketing platform fees. Paid tickets carry a service fee and a processing fee.

### How much does Eventbrite charge for paid tickets?

The pricing page lists 3.7% + $1.79 per ticket as a service fee, plus a 2.9% processing fee per order. Buyers pay the ticket fees by default, or you can absorb them.

### Does Eventbrite need a credit card?

The pricing page does not ask for a card to start. It points you to Get started for free.
