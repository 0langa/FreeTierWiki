You use Stytch through its API, SDKs for React, Next.js, and several backend languages, or prebuilt Stytch UI components. It suits developers who want control over the login flow, in either a B2B or a consumer app.

## What the free plan gives you

Stytch lists a pay-as-you-go plan that starts at $0 per month, with an always-free amount. The B2B version includes 10,000 monthly active users and AI agents, unlimited organizations, 5 SSO or SCIM connections, and 1,000 M2M tokens. It also lists 10,000 device fingerprints.

The page says there is no feature gating. You get the full suite of authentication and authorization tools, including OAuth, SSO, sessions, and RBAC. Prebuilt UI components for login and an admin portal are included. Full email customization and Stytch brand removal is an optional add-on that costs $99.

## When you hit a limit

There is no hard cap. The pricing page says there are no pricing cliffs. If you pass the 10,000 monthly active users, the 5 SSO or SCIM connections, the 1,000 M2M tokens, or the 10,000 fingerprints, you pay only for what you use, at the listed rates. For example, extra device fingerprints cost $.005 each.

This means a surge in traffic turns into a bill rather than a stop. The pricing page has a calculator that estimates the monthly cost for your numbers. Check it before launch.

## Gotchas

- The free tier does not stop at its limits. Usage past the free amount is billed at list rates.
- B2B and consumer apps use different project types and different pricing tables. Pick the right one when you create the project.
- The brand removal and full email customization add-on is paid.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stytch free?

Yes, up to a point. The always-free amount is 10,000 monthly active users and AI agents, 5 SSO or SCIM connections, and 1,000 M2M tokens.

### Does Stytch have a hard cap?

No. The pricing page says there are no hard caps or pricing cliffs. Past the free amount, you pay for what you use.

### Does Stytch need a credit card?

The pricing page shows a Start Building for Free button and does not mention a card.
