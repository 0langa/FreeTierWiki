Fizzy is a free kanban board for tracking bugs, issues, ideas, and small projects, made by 37signals (the team behind Basecamp and HEY). It is entirely free — subsidized by 37signals' paid products — with file storage capped at 1 GB. Fizzy is open source, so you can self-host your own instance, and it supports webhooks for Slack and Campfire plus a custom integration API.
