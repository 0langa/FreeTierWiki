You use Deepnote in the browser: create a project, add code and SQL blocks, and share it with your team. It suits data analysts, students, and small teams who want to edit the same notebook at once. You can also import and export .ipynb files.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever. It allows up to 3 editors and up to 5 projects, with unlimited viewers. You get unlimited Basic machines with 5GB of RAM and 2 vCPU, plus 7 days of revision history. The pricing page lists "Limited Deepnote AI": 10 code completions per month and 5 calls per month each for the Agent and for generate, edit, and explain code.

The Free plan also lists code, SQL, text, and chart blocks, real-time collaboration, public projects, and import and export of .ipynb files.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens at the editor, project, or AI limits. The table shows those as caps, and the Team plan lifts them. Team costs $39 per editor per month when billed yearly, offers a 14-day free trial, and adds unlimited editors and projects, 30 days of revision history, and more powerful machines. The table also lists an inactivity period of 15 minutes on Free and 24 hours on Team, so a Free machine stops sooner when you stop working.

## Gotchas

- Only 3 people can edit. Anyone else can view, but cannot change the notebook.
- Free keeps 7 days of revision history, so older versions are gone. Team keeps 30 days.
- Only Basic machines are listed for Free. Plus machines (16 GB RAM, 4 vCPU) and larger ones are on paid plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Deepnote free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing and lasts forever. It allows 3 editors, 5 projects, and unlimited Basic machines with 5GB of RAM and 2 vCPU.

### Does Deepnote need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say. It shows a button to get started for free and a separate 14-day trial for the Team plan.

### What happens when Deepnote's free machine is idle?

The pricing table lists an inactivity period of 15 minutes for Free. It does not describe what happens to your running state, so save your work.
