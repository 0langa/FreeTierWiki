You use Deepnote in the browser: create a project, add code and SQL blocks, and share it with your team. It suits data analysts, students, and small teams who want to edit the same notebook at once.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever. It allows up to 3 editors and up to 5 projects, with unlimited viewers. You get unlimited Basic machines with 5GB of RAM and 2 vCPU, plus 7 days of revision history. The pricing page lists "Limited Deepnote AI": 10 code completions per month and 5 calls per month each for the Agent and for generate, edit, and explain code.

The pricing page also shows unlimited viewers on Free. It describes the plan as a free version for aspiring data analysts and scientists. The Team plan is the next step up, and the docs also mention a separate free Education plan for teachers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not explain what happens at the Free editor and project limits. General billing docs describe usage-based AI overage and an administrator-set monthly spending limit, but do not clearly say whether Free accounts can incur that overage. Confirm the billing settings before relying on a zero-spend guarantee. Team raises the editor and project allowances. Team costs $39 per editor per month when billed yearly, offers a 14-day free trial, and adds unlimited editors and projects, 30 days of revision history, and more powerful machines. The table also lists an inactivity period of 15 minutes on Free and 24 hours on Team, so a Free machine stops sooner when you stop working.

## Gotchas

- Only 3 people can edit. Free allows unlimited viewers. Scheduled runs, public apps, the notebook API and advanced database integrations are paid features.
- Local VS Code/Cursor extensions and the CLI can run notebooks independently; they do not include the hosted platform's real-time collaboration.
- Free keeps 7 days of revision history. Team keeps 30 days. Only Basic machines are listed for Free. Plus machines (16 GB RAM, 4 vCPU) are on Team and Enterprise.

## Questions people ask

### Is Deepnote free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing and lasts forever. It allows 3 editors, 5 projects, and unlimited Basic machines with 5GB of RAM and 2 vCPU.

### Does Deepnote need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say. It shows a button to get started for free and a separate 14-day trial for the Team plan.

### What happens when Deepnote's free machine is idle?

The pricing table lists an inactivity period of 15 minutes for Free. It does not describe what happens to your running state, so save your work.
