You work in a hosted web app, and your customers see a board on a rightfeature.com address. There is no code to install for the basic board.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free forever and asks for no credit card. It includes 1 product and 1 product board, and each board holds up to 100 posts. One team member can manage the feedback.

Tracked users and customers are unlimited. The site says it will never charge you for more end users. Custom statuses are included. Starter cards advertise guest posting and AI, but the detailed comparison marks guest posting, AI summarization, auto-tagging, approvals, and AI tokens as excluded on Free. Treat those features as unconfirmed until the vendor resolves this conflict.

Pro plans are sold as one-time lifetime payments in the founding member deal. Pro Plan 1 costs $199 and gives 1 product, unlimited boards, and 5 team members. Pro Plan 2 costs $299 and gives 2 products. Check the available billing choices before upgrading.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a board passes 100 posts or when you try to add a second team member. It lists them only as plan limits.

A paid upgrade is a one-time payment, not a subscription. The site says Starter asks for no credit card. The displayed Pro cards are one-time lifetime offers for founding members, so check the available terms before upgrading.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has one team member. A cofounder cannot help you triage unless you upgrade.
- The detailed comparison includes brand colors and a logo on Free. Removing RightFeature branding is excluded on both displayed plans, and custom domain is coming soon.
- Several features are marked coming soon on the pricing page, including private boards, webhooks, and a REST API. Do not plan around them yet.

## Questions people ask

### Is RightFeature free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever. It includes 1 product, 1 board, 100 posts per board, and 1 team member.

### Does RightFeature need a credit card?

No. The site says no credit card is required, and the free plan sets up in about 5 minutes.

### Is there a limit on how many customers can vote?

No. The FAQ says the number of tracked users or customers is not limited, and you are not charged for more of them.
