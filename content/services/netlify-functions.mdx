You add a TypeScript or JavaScript file to the netlify/functions folder of your project and push it to Git. Netlify builds and deploys it with your site, and you can also deploy with the Netlify CLI. It suits front-end developers who want a small API next to a static or framework site.

## What the free plan gives you

Functions have no separate free quota. They draw from the account-wide credit pool of the Free plan, which is 300 credits per month. Compute costs 10 credits per GB-hour, and the default function memory is 1 GB. Web requests, including requests to your functions, cost 2 credits per 10k requests.

Production deploys cost 15 credits each, and bandwidth costs 20 credits per GB. All of these share the same 300 credits. Background functions can run up to 15 minutes. Raising memory up to 4 GB needs a Pro or Enterprise credit-based plan.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan has a hard limit. Netlify says the free plan has hard monthly limits that cannot be exceeded or incur any costs. When the credits are used up, your web projects pause until the next billing cycle, and visitors see a Site not available page.

If one project passes its limits, all projects on your account pause. Netlify warns you by email and in the app at 50%, 75%, and 100% of usage. You can resume projects by upgrading your plan. Paid plans can add credits with auto recharge, which is off by default.

## Gotchas

- Your functions, builds, and bandwidth all share one 300 credit pool, so a busy site can pause your functions too.
- Accounts created before September 4, 2025 may be on a Legacy plan, where functions are billed by request count instead of credits.
- Pausing affects the whole account, not only the function that used the credits.

## Questions people ask

### Are Netlify Functions free?

Yes, on the Free plan. They share the 300 credits you get each month with builds, bandwidth, and requests.

### What happens when I run out of Netlify credits?

All your web projects pause until the next billing cycle, or until you upgrade. The free plan cannot incur costs.

### Do Netlify Functions need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say a card is needed for the Free plan. It says the free plan is always free with hard monthly limits.
