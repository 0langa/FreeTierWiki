You use Arize AX through its web app, a command line tool, and OpenInference instrumentation packages for Python and TypeScript. It suits people who build LLM agents and want to see what each request did.

## What the free plan gives you

The AX Free plan costs nothing, and the pricing page says no credit card is required. It includes 25k trace spans per month and 1 GB of ingestion per month. Data is kept for 15 days. You also get 10 issues per month from Signal, the feature that groups recurring failures in your traces.

Users, evaluations, experiments, human annotations, labeling queues, playgrounds, datasets, and prompts are all listed as unlimited on the free plan. The Pro plan costs $50 per month and raises the numbers to 50k spans, 10 GB of ingestion, and 30 days of retention. The pricing page also mentions startup pricing, without giving details.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 25k spans or 1 GB in a month. No Free overage price or card is published, but that does not establish what happens to excess spans or whether an account transition is offered. Do not assume a particular blocking rule.

Retention works the same way as a limit. Traces older than 15 days are no longer kept on the free plan, so export anything you need to keep. The Pro plan keeps data for 30 days.

## Gotchas

- Signal issues are capped at 10 per month on the free plan, against 25 on Pro.
- Retention is 15 days on the free plan, which is short for slow investigations.
- The docs setup command starts a coding agent session that edits your app, so read the changes it makes before you run the app.

## Questions people ask

### Is Arize AX free?

Yes. The AX Free plan costs nothing, needs no credit card, and includes 25k trace spans and 1 GB of ingestion per month.

### How long does Arize AX keep my traces on the free plan?

The pricing page lists 15 days of retention for the free plan and 30 days for Pro.

### What happens when I go over the Arize AX free limits?

The pricing page does not say. It lists no overage price for the free plan, so check your account before you send a large batch of traces.
