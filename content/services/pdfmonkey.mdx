PDFMonkey lets you define a layout once and send data when you need a document. An internal tool could use it to generate a receipt or summary, then download the resulting PDF into its own storage. That is useful when you need a printable document but do not want to operate a rendering service.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan lists 20 documents per month and unlimited templates. The service retains generated files for one day and allows up to 30 seconds to generate each free document. Start with a representative document instead of estimating capacity from a blank page. Images, content length, and templates that depend on outside assets can affect how your layout renders. Store a copy once you receive it if the document will be needed after the retention window. The hosted copy may be unavailable when a customer looks for it later.

## When you hit a limit

The comparison table shows that Free has no pay-as-you-go extra document option. If you need more documents, wait for the next month or move to a plan that includes more or permits paid extra documents. The 30-second generation limit remains independent of monthly usage. A complicated PDF can reach that ceiling even on the first day of a new month.

## Gotchas

- Download each finished PDF before the one-day retention window closes.
- A template slot is not a document credit. You can configure several templates while using the same monthly allowance for their output.
- Keep a realistic sample so you can retest the layout after changing its data or styles.

## Questions people ask

### Is PDFMonkey free?

Yes. The pricing FAQ lists an ongoing Free plan with 20 documents per month.

### Can I use it as a PDF archive?

Free retention is one day. Copy documents to storage you control if readers need longer access.

### Can I buy an extra PDF on Free?

The pricing matrix excludes paid extra documents on Free. An upgrade is the published route to additional capacity.
