You sign in at datalore.jetbrains.com with a Google or JetBrains account. Then you work in notebooks in the browser. It suits learners, teachers, and small teams who want a hosted Python notebook.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called Cloud Free. It gives you 120 CPU S machine hours and 10 GB of cloud storage. Notebooks are unlimited, with real-time collaboration, coding assistance for Python and other languages, and on-the-fly environment customization.

The paid Cloud plan adds more. It gives running unlimited notebooks in parallel, reactive mode, version history, Datalore AI, unlimited SQL queries and database connections, shareable reports, unlimited scheduled notebooks, and team management. It also brings 750 CPU L machine hours per team member and 6 Datalore credits per team member for advanced CPUs and GPUs. Storage grows to 20 GB.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page does not say what happens when your 120 CPU S hours or 10 GB run out. The free plan has no listed charge, so you are not billed. To get more compute or storage, you move to the Cloud plan.

JetBrains suggests a free Cloud trial before you buy. The trial has limited Cloud functionality: a maximum of 2 computations at once and a maximum of 2 scheduled runs, with at least a 24-hour interval. The Cloud plan lasts a minimum of 1 month, and a 1-year license is an option.

## Gotchas

- Compute is counted in machine hours on a small CPU. Long-running notebooks use the 120 hours quickly.
- The old Community plan is gone. Its users moved to Cloud Free, after a 14-day period with all Cloud features.
- Version history, scheduled notebooks, and Datalore AI are Cloud plan features, not Cloud Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Datalore free?

Yes. The Cloud Free plan gives 120 CPU S machine hours and 10 GB of cloud storage, with unlimited notebooks and real-time collaboration.

### What happened to the Datalore Community plan?

It is archived. Community users were moved to Cloud Free, after 14 days with all Cloud features unlocked.

### Does Datalore Cloud Free include GPUs?

No. GPUs and advanced CPUs come with the paid Cloud plan, which includes 6 Datalore credits per team member for them.
