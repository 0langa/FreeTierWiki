You add one tracking script to your site, or use a platform integration or an NPM package, and read the results in the Clarity dashboard. It suits site owners and UX teams who want to see how people really use a page.

## What the free plan gives you

Clarity is free forever. The FAQ says you never hit traffic limits and are never pushed to upgrade to a paid version. 
You get session recordings and heatmaps. The FAQ also lists no limits on the number of sites per account and no sampling, so Clarity does not track only a portion of your traffic. You can track several domains in one account by creating one project per domain. You add the project's script to each subdomain you want to track.

Data shows up in near real time. Recordings are kept for 30 days. Favorites and a random sample are kept for up to 9 months. Clarity masks sensitive content on your site by default. The data collection code is open source on GitHub.

## When you hit a limit

There is no traffic limit, so there is no cap to hit and no bill. The limits that matter are about rules and time. Recordings are kept for 30 days from the time of recording, so favorite the ones you need.

Privacy rules also set limits. Without valid consent, Clarity limits data collection in regions that require consent. Clarity should not be used on websites or apps that target users under the age of 18 anywhere in the world.

## Gotchas

- Microsoft uses the data under its own terms. Check your privacy rules, and tell your users about Clarity.
- Session recordings and behavior analytics need valid consent for users in consent-required regions.
- URL parameters are not masked by default. Contact Clarity support to request URL parameter masking.

## Questions people ask

### Is Microsoft Clarity really free?

Yes. The FAQ calls Clarity a free service forever. You never encounter traffic limits or have to upgrade to a paid version.

### How long does Clarity keep my data?

Web admins can access recorded data for up to 30 days from the time of recording. Favorite recordings and a random sample are kept for up to 9 months, and heatmaps are available for up to 9 months.

### Can I use Clarity on more than one site?

Yes. There is no limit on the number of sites per account. Create a project for each domain, or use one project across several subdomains.
