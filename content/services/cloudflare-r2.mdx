You manage R2 in the Cloudflare Dashboard, from a Worker through the Workers API, or with any S3 SDK, the aws CLI, or rclone. It fits developers who already use S3 tools and want to avoid download fees.

## What the free plan gives you

Each month you can use 10 GB-month of storage, 1 million Class A operations, and 10 million Class B operations for free. Class A operations tend to change data, such as PutObject and ListObjects. Class B operations read it, such as GetObject.

Egress, meaning data sent to the internet, is free on every storage class. Delete operations are free too. Storage is measured in GB-month, which averages your daily peak storage over a 30-day billing period.

The free tier applies only to Standard storage. Infrequent Access storage gets no free amount.

## When you hit a limit

Cloudflare does not stop your bucket at the free amounts. It bills extra usage automatically at standard rates. Standard storage costs $0.015 per GB-month. Class A operations cost $4.50 per million requests, and Class B operations cost $0.36 per million.

Cloudflare rounds usage up to the next billing unit. One million and one operations bills as two million. The r2.dev public address is for testing only, and heavy traffic there can get a 429 Too Many Requests response.

## Gotchas

- The free tier covers Standard storage only. Infrequent Access has its own prices, data retrieval fees, and a 30-day minimum storage duration.
- The entry records that turning on R2 needs a subscription checkout that asks for a payment method.
- The r2.dev address is not meant for production. Use a custom domain on the bucket for real traffic.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare R2 free?

Partly. Each month includes 10 GB-month of Standard storage, 1 million Class A operations, and 10 million Class B operations. Egress is always free.

### Does Cloudflare R2 charge for egress?

No. Cloudflare says there is no charge for egress bandwidth on any storage class.

### What happens when I go over the R2 free tier?

Cloudflare bills the extra at standard rates and does not cap usage. Standard storage is $0.015 per GB-month.
