NocoDB is an open source no-code database with a spreadsheet-style editor, several view types, forms, automations, and REST APIs on every table. You can self-host it or use NocoDB Cloud. This entry covers the hosted cloud Free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

NocoDB Cloud's Free plan is aimed at personal applications and costs $0 with no credit card required. It includes 3 editor seats and 10 commenter seats, 1,000 records, 1 GB of storage, 100 automation runs, and 1,000 API calls per month. You also get 1 extension and 1 interface with up to 3 pages.

Bases are unlimited inside a single workspace. Record revision history covers 3 days, and the Free plan has no base snapshots. The Plus plan costs $12 per seat per month billed annually and raises the limits to 50,000 records, 20 GB of storage, and 100,000 API calls per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 1,000 records, 1 GB of storage, or 1,000 API calls in a month. It lists no overage prices for the Free plan, and no card is needed to sign up, so there is nothing to bill automatically. Plan an upgrade to Plus or a cleanup before the limits matter.

## Gotchas

- The 1,000-record limit is for the whole Free plan as listed, so several bases share it.
- Free self-hosted NocoDB has different terms from NocoDB Cloud; the numbers here are for the cloud.
- With only 3 days of revision history and no snapshots, an accidental bulk edit is hard to undo.

## Questions people ask

### Is NocoDB free?

The open source version is free to self-host, and NocoDB Cloud has a Free plan with 1,000 records, 3 editors, and 1,000 API calls per month.

### Do I need a credit card for NocoDB Cloud?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan needs no credit card.

### How many API calls does the NocoDB Free plan include?

1,000 API calls per month. The Plus plan includes 100,000.
