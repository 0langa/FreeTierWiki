You use Queues from Wrangler and your Worker code. A producer Worker binds to a queue and calls `send()`, and a consumer Worker handles batches. It suits developers who already build on Cloudflare Workers.

## What the free plan gives you

Queues is part of the Workers Free plan. It includes 10,000 operations per day. An operation is counted for each 64 KB of data that is written, read, or deleted, and operations are per message, not per batch.

In most cases a message takes 3 operations: 1 write, 1 read, and 1 delete. That means only a few thousand messages a day fit in the free amount. Free plan messages are kept for 24 hours, and you cannot change that. The paid plan keeps them 4 days by default, and up to 14 days. There are no data transfer or bandwidth charges.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing and limits pages do not say what Queues does after the 10,000 daily operations on the Free plan. The free plan has no card, so the page lists no bill.

Other limits are explicit. A message can be up to 128 KB, and a queue can take 5,000 messages per second. Past that rate, `send()` and `sendBatch()` throw a Too Many Requests error. A queue that reaches its 25GB backlog returns a Storage Limit Exceeded error on send. Each retry also costs a read operation.

## Gotchas

- Each queue can have only one consumer Worker connected to it.
- A batch of 10 messages counts as 10 writes, 10 reads, and 10 deletes. Batching does not cut the operation count.
- Without a consumer Worker, messages stay in the queue until they expire.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Queues free?

Yes, on the Workers Free plan. It includes 10,000 operations per day and keeps messages for 24 hours.

### How many messages can I send on the free plan?

Most messages use 3 operations, so the 10,000 daily operations cover only a few thousand messages a day. Larger messages and retries use more.

### How long does Cloudflare Queues keep messages?

On the Workers Free plan, messages are kept for 24 hours and you cannot change it. On the Workers Paid plan the default is 4 days, up to 14 days.
