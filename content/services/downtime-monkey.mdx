Downtime Monkey is a website uptime monitoring service. It requests your pages on a schedule, records response times, and alerts you when a site stops responding. It suits developers and agencies who look after many small websites and want simple alerts.

## What the free plan gives you

The plans page says the Free Plan "will always be free." It allows up to 60 monitors, checked every 5 minutes from 1 monitoring location in the UK. Uptime monitoring and response time monitoring are included, with email alerts and Slack alerts.

For stats, the Free plan shows uptime to 1 decimal place, keeps stats for up to 90 days, includes graphs of individual response times, and shows daily average response times for 7 days. SMS alerts are available, but the Free plan comes with 0 free SMS credits and no reduced-price top-ups. Support is by email.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page does not describe overage charges; the Free plan is a fixed allowance of 60 monitors, and Pro plans are bought separately by number of monitors. Pro1 is listed at $0.69 a month paid yearly, and Pro1000 at $56.09 a month. The Pro column lists 5 free SMS credits, reduced-price SMS top-ups, and rate limits for SMS and Slack alerts. You need a free account before you can buy a Pro plan.

## Gotchas

- Checks come from one location in the UK, so a regional network problem can look like downtime.
- Downtime logs, including reasons and durations, are not available on the Free plan.
- Repeat alerts and custom alert times are not available on the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Downtime Monkey free?

Yes. The Free plan "will always be free" and covers 60 monitors at 5-minute intervals.

### Where do the free checks run from?

From 1 location in the UK; choosing monitoring locations is not available on the Free plan.

### Can I get SMS alerts on the Free plan?

SMS alerts are listed as available, but the Free plan includes 0 free SMS credits.
