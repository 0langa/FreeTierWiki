Typeform builds forms, surveys, and quizzes that show one question at a time. You design a typeform in the builder or with Typeform AI, share it by link or embed it, and read answers in the Results panel. It also has developer APIs for creating forms and collecting responses without the builder.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists Basic, Plus, Business, and Growth plans and points you to a Free plan. The help center describes that Free plan: unlimited typeforms and 10 responses per month across all your forms, which means 10 unique submissions in total each month.

The Free plan includes logic with branching, calculations, scores, and variables; URL parameters; custom endings; embedding; downloading results as a spreadsheet; basic reports; integrations such as Calendly, Zapier, Google Sheets, Slack, and HubSpot; Typeform AI; and the developer APIs. All you need to sign up is an email address.

## When you hit a limit

Typeform emails you and shows a notice in your account at 90% and 100% of your response limit. When you reach 100%, your forms go into Private mode and respondents cannot fill them in. The forms become public again automatically when the next month begins or when you upgrade.

Limits reset every month and unused responses do not carry over. Deleting responses does not give them back: a deleted response still counts toward that month's limit.

## Gotchas

- Ten responses a month is a test amount. One shared link can use it up in a day.
- You can try many paid features on the Free plan, but a form that uses them goes into trial mode and cannot be published or collect responses until you upgrade or remove the features.
- Typeform says it experiments with pricing, so the plans in your account may differ from the help center.

## Questions people ask

### How many responses does the Typeform Free plan include?

Ten responses per month across all your forms, according to Typeform's help center.

### What happens when I reach the response limit?

Your forms go into Private mode. They reopen when the next month begins or when you upgrade your account.

### Do deleted responses free up space in my limit?

No. A deleted response still counts toward your limit for that month.
