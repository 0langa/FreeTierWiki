DigitalOcean App Platform is a managed platform that builds and runs apps from your Git repository. It handles the infrastructure, runtimes, and dependencies, and an app is either free, when it only uses static site components, or paid, when it uses container instances. It suits developers who want simple Git-based deploys on DigitalOcean.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Tier starts at $0 a month. It lets you build and deploy up to 3 apps with static sites for free, and each free app includes 1 GiB of outbound data transfer per month. Free Tier features include deployment from GitHub and GitLab, automatic HTTPS, bringing your custom domain, and a global CDN.

DigitalOcean describes the Free Tier as ideal for trying out App Platform, hosting personal websites, portfolios, or small projects. Data transfer for App Platform is calculated separately from data transfer for Droplets.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says additional apps with static sites only are charged at $3.00 per month, and outbound data transfer beyond the allowance is charged at $0.02 per GiB. It does not describe a hard stop, so a site that gets more traffic than expected is billed for the extra transfer. Adding a service, worker, or job turns the app into a paid app priced by container instance.

## Gotchas

- Static sites inside a paid app cost $0 but have no bandwidth allowance of their own.
- 1 GiB of transfer per month is small for a site with images or downloads.
- Deploying from container registries starts on the $5 a month plan, not the Free Tier.

## Questions people ask

### Is DigitalOcean App Platform free?

For static sites, yes. The Free Tier hosts up to 3 apps with static sites, each with 1 GiB of outbound transfer a month.

### Can I use a custom domain on the Free Tier?

Yes. Bringing your custom domain, automatic HTTPS, and a global CDN are listed as Free Tier features.

### What does a fourth static site cost?

Additional apps with static sites only are charged at $3.00 per month.
