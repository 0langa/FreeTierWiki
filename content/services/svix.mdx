You use Svix through its API and SDKs to create consumer applications, add endpoints, and send messages. A dashboard shows deliveries. It suits SaaS developers who add webhooks to their product.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and needs no credit card. It includes up to 50,000 messages a month, 7 days of payload retention, a message rate of 50 per second, and an embeddable webhooks UI. Environments, consumer applications, transformations, and team members are unlimited. The Free plan has 1 connector.

Only attempted or transformed messages count toward usage. Messages that Svix filters out, for example when there are no endpoints, and retries are free. Each 64 KiB chunk of payload bills as one message. You can also test webhooks with Svix Play without signing up.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say whether Svix blocks sending after 50,000 messages on the Free plan. It lists no per-message price for Free, so this entry treats the plan as a hard limit with no charge.

On paid plans, extra messages bill at $0.0001 each. The Basic plan starts at $20 a month and the Professional plan at $490 a month. Both include 50k free messages a month. Professional starts with a 30-day free trial.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has no uptime SLA. Basic has 99.9% and Professional has 99.99%.
- White label headers need Basic, and the unbranded application portal needs Professional.
- Payload retention is only 7 days on Free, against 30 days on Basic.

## Questions people ask

### Is Svix free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and includes up to 50,000 messages a month.

### Does Svix have an SLA on the free plan?

No. The FAQ says the Free plan has no SLA. The Basic plan offers 99.9%.

### Can I test webhooks with Svix without an account?

Yes. The pricing page says you can use the Svix Play webhooks tester without even signing up.
