Kraken.io compresses and optimizes images through an API, a web interface, and plugins for WordPress and Magento. It supports JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, AVIF, HEIC, and PDF, with lossy, lossless, and expert modes, resizing, and format conversion.

## What the free plan gives you

All plans start out as a free account with 100 MB of testing quota, and the pricing page says no credit card is required to sign up. The API sandbox section adds that you can integrate with the API free of charge and use the 100 MB to perform real optimizations.

The web interface lets you drag and drop images, including ZIP archives, and download the optimized results without writing code. All plans come with full API access, the web interface, the WordPress plugin, the Magento extension, and Kraken.io cloud storage.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say that the 100 MB testing quota renews or what happens once it is used. To keep optimizing through the API you choose a paid plan: Micro costs $5 a month for 500 MB of images per month with $5 per additional GB, and Basic costs $9 a month for 2 GB.

On paid plans the quota counts unprocessed source images, is shared across the API, web interface, and plugins, and resets at the start of every billing cycle. Usage past it is billed at the plan's per-GB overage rate.

## Gotchas

- Optimized images are available on Kraken.io servers for one hour only, so download them or store them elsewhere straight away.
- The quota counts the size of your original images, not the smaller results.
- The 100 MB is a testing amount, not a monthly free tier.

## Questions people ask

### Is Kraken.io free?

Partly. A free account gets 100 MB of testing quota without a credit card, and ongoing API use needs a paid plan.

### How long are optimized images kept?

Optimized images are available on Kraken.io servers for one hour only.

### Does the quota count original or optimized size?

The original size. The quota is the total amount of unprocessed image data you optimize.
