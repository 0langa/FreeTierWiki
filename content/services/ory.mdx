You create a project in the Ory Console and call Ory's headless APIs from your app, with hosted screens or your own UI. It suits developers who want to test the managed cloud before they pay or self-host.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free and meant for testing and proof-of-concept work. It gives you 2 development environments and 1 team member. It has 0 production environments and 0 staging environments, so it is not for live traffic.

The paid Production plan costs $770 per year and adds 1 production environment and 3 staging environments. The Growth plan costs $9,350 per year, with 2 production environments and 5 staging environments. Ory says every core service in Ory Network is the same Apache 2.0 licensed software available on GitHub, so you can also run it yourself.

## When you hit a limit

The Developer plan has no production environment, so the real limit is that you cannot go live on it. When you are ready to launch, you buy the Production plan, or you deploy the open-source Ory services on your own infrastructure.

The pricing page does not describe what happens if you add more users to a development project. The Production plan also bills by use, for example per active daily user, so read its price table before you switch. The free plan has no included monthly credit.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has no staging or production environment. Treat it as a test lab only.
- Only 1 team member fits in the Developer plan, so a team cannot share one project on it.
- Ory has several services: Kratos for login, Hydra for OAuth2, Keto for permissions, and Polis for enterprise SSO. Pick the ones you need first.

## Questions people ask

### Is Ory free?

The Developer plan on Ory Network is free, but only for testing and proofs of concept. The open-source software is also free to run on your own servers.

### Can I use Ory Network free in production?

No. The Developer plan has 0 production environments and 0 staging environments. You need the Production plan, which costs $770 per year.

### Does Ory need a credit card?

The pricing page shows a Start for free button for the Developer plan and does not mention a card. The atlas lists it as needing no card.
