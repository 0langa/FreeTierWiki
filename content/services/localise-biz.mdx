You use Loco in its dashboard, or you call its API to publish translation files during a build. It suits developers, translators, and project managers who share one set of translations.

## What the free plan gives you

Loco is free for up to 2,000 translations. The free plan also allows 2 private projects, 1,000 translatable assets per project, and 10 languages per project. Translators are unlimited. API access is rate limited. Revision history keeps the current translation and the previous edit.

A translation is one asset in one language. One asset in five languages uses five translations. Untranslated assets do not count, but your source language text does. The limit applies to the total stored in your whole account.

The Pro plans remove the extra limits. They start at $5.95 per month, with prices also shown in pounds and euros. Loco says it charges for resources, not features.

## When you hit a limit

The pages tell you to go Pro when you need more. They do not say what happens when you pass the free quota. The plans page lists extra translations as a paid add-on for the Pro plans.

To stay free, download your translations when a project is done, delete it, and start a new project with a fresh quota. You can re-import the translations if the project starts again. Loco also says you can ask for more free quota while you evaluate the service.

## Gotchas

- Quotas apply to accounts, not to users. Loco says it may merge the users of an account syndicate that abuses free accounts.
- Emptying a string does not free quota. An empty translation still counts, so delete the translation and its history with the eraser icon.
- You bring your own key for AI translation. Loco connects to DeepL, Google Translate, Microsoft Translator, and LLMs from OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic, and it does not charge for this.

## Questions people ask

### Is Loco free?

Yes. Loco is free for up to 2,000 translations, with 2 private projects. Free accounts need no card, and you can register by email.

### How does Loco count translations?

One asset in one language is one translation. An asset in five languages uses five. Source language text counts, but untranslated assets do not.

### Does Loco have an API on the free plan?

Yes. Loco says API access is rate limited on the free plan and unlimited on Pro plans.
