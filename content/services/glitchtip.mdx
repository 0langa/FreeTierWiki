You use GlitchTip through its web dashboard and the Sentry SDK you add to your app. It also offers uptime checks, slow-request views, and a logs view.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and the pricing page calls it free forever. It covers up to 1,000 events per month. The pricing page lists unlimited projects and unlimited team members under the Small plan and gives no project or member limits for Free.

An event is the unit GlitchTip uses to measure usage. Each error and each performance transaction counts as one event. Uptime checks and logs are lighter: ten of either count as one event. Release and debug file uploads count as one event per megabyte. Uptime checks, logs and performance data therefore use up the same monthly quota as errors. The page describes the free plan as a way to explore features or build side projects.

## When you hit a limit

GlitchTip does not bill you for going over. After your quota is full, it starts dropping about 10% of events and drops more as usage climbs. At twice the quota it blocks events fully. You can upgrade from Free to a paid plan yourself at any time to raise the limit.

The paid plans on the pricing page are Small at $15 a month, Medium at $50 a month, and Large at $250 a month. Small raises the limit to up to 100k events per month. On any paid plan you can turn on on-demand billing to keep collecting events past the limit, up to a monthly spend cap you set; Free has no such option.

## Gotchas

- Performance transactions count as one event each and uptime checks and logs count one event per ten, so chatty monitoring can use your free quota before errors do.
- Events are dropped once throttling reaches the full block, so you can lose errors during a bad day.
- The pricing page does not mention a credit card for the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is GlitchTip free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and allows up to 1,000 events per month. The pricing page calls it free forever.

### What happens when I go over the GlitchTip event limit?

GlitchTip drops about 10% of events once your quota is full and drops more as usage climbs. At twice the quota it blocks events fully. Upgrading raises the limit.

### Does GlitchTip work with Sentry SDKs?

Yes. The docs list SDK guides for many platforms and give general setup instructions for any Sentry-compatible SDK.
