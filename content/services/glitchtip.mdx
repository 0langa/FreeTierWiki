You use GlitchTip through its web dashboard and the Sentry SDK you add to your app. It also offers uptime checks, slow-request views, and a logs view.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and the pricing page calls it free forever. It covers up to 1,000 events per month. You can create unlimited projects and invite unlimited team members.

An event is the unit GlitchTip uses to measure usage. It counts four things: each error occurrence, each uptime status check, each performance transaction report, and each megabyte of release file storage. Uptime checks and performance data therefore use up the same monthly quota as errors. The free plan is meant for personal projects and lists error tracking as its feature.

## When you hit a limit

GlitchTip does not bill you for going over. After your quota is full, it throttles incoming events by 10%. It raises that gradually, and at 2x the quota it blocks events fully. You can upgrade at any time to raise the limit, and plan changes take effect immediately.

The paid plans on the pricing page are Small at $15 a month, Medium at $50 a month, and Large at $250 a month. Small raises the limit to up to 100k events per month.

## Gotchas

- Uptime checks and performance reports count as events, so a chatty monitor can use your free quota before errors do.
- Events are dropped once throttling reaches the full block, so you can lose errors during a bad day.
- The pricing page does not mention a credit card for the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is GlitchTip free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and allows up to 1,000 events per month, with unlimited projects and team members. The pricing page calls it free forever.

### What happens when I go over the GlitchTip event limit?

GlitchTip throttles events by 10% once your quota is full and increases that gradually. At 2x the quota it blocks events fully. Upgrading raises the limit.

### Does GlitchTip work with Sentry SDKs?

Yes. The docs list SDK guides for many platforms and give general setup instructions for any Sentry-compatible SDK.
