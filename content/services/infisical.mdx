You manage secrets in the Infisical web dashboard, with the `infisical` CLI, or through SDKs. The company also lets you self-host the open-source platform. It suits individuals and small projects that want secrets in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is $0 forever, for individuals and personal projects. It includes 5 identities and unlimited projects. An identity is any human or machine that logs in to Infisical, so the 5 cover both teammates and CI pipelines.

The comparison table lists 3 environments and 10 secret syncs on the free plan. You also get 100+ integrations, secret sharing, secret references and imports, secret overrides, and 2FA.

The Pro plan costs $20 per identity per month, billed annually, and adds unlimited identities, access controls, secret rotation, SAML SSO, secret versioning, and 30 day audit log retention.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists free resource allowances without a Free overage tariff. It does not publish whether creating a sixth identity or an eleventh sync is blocked or starts another upgrade flow. Card requirements for Free and the advertised 14-day Pro and Advanced trials are also unconfirmed in the current canonical pricing text.

The upgrade path is Pro, which has unlimited identities and costs per identity. Advanced raises syncs to 50 and environments to 12 per project. Enterprise removes the listed limits and adds enterprise self-hosting options and audit log streaming. The open-source self-hosted edition is separate from those enterprise features.

## Gotchas

- Projects are unlimited, but identities are not. The 5 identities are counted across the whole organization.
- Access controls, secret versioning, and SAML SSO are not on the free plan.
- Honey tokens start on Pro, and audit log streaming to a SIEM is Enterprise only.

## Questions people ask

### Is Infisical free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 forever, with 5 identities and unlimited projects. The platform is also open source, so you can host it yourself.

### What counts as an identity in Infisical?

An identity is any human or machine that authenticates to Infisical. The pricing page says this includes apps, CI pipelines, and services.

### Does Infisical have a free trial of paid plans?

Yes. The current pricing page offers a 14-day free trial of Pro and Advanced. Check the trial terms before starting; the public text reviewed does not confirm a card requirement.
