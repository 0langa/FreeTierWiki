You drag a build onto the Diawi web page, get a link and a QR code, and send them to your testers. Testers open the link on their phone and tap Install. It suits mobile developers and small test groups.

## What the free plan gives you

Diawi has two free levels. Without signup you get a 20 MB maximum upload size, 2 installations per app, and 1 day before the uploaded app expires. With a free account at 0,00€ you get a 50 MB maximum upload size, 10 installations per app, and 3 days before expiration.

Both levels let you upload iOS and Android apps, send installation links, and protect a link with a password. iOS builds can be development, ad hoc, or in-house signed. Android builds can be any signed APK.

The paid Starter plan costs 2,99€ per month. It raises the limits to 250 MB, 7 days, and 50 installations per app, and it adds the dashboard and API access.

## When you hit a limit

A link becomes "Not available" when its time runs out, when the installations run out, or when you disable it by hand. With an account you can re-enable a link for a short time: the free account gets 1 day. Add time or installations to bring it back.

After that period the link turns "Gone", and Diawi deletes the file. A gone link cannot come back, so upload the build again. Links made without an account cannot be re-enabled at all.

## Gotchas

- Diawi takes APK files. It does not support Android App Bundles (.aab), so you must build an APK first.
- On iOS, testers must open the link in Safari. Other browsers and in-app web views do not work.
- Diawi is not an app store. The docs forbid deploying apps to the general public.

## Questions people ask

### Is Diawi free?

Yes. You can upload without signup (20 MB, 2 installations per app, 1 day), or make a free account for 50 MB, 10 installations per app, and 3 days.

### What happens when a Diawi link expires?

The link shows "Not available". With an account you can re-enable it for a short time, which is 1 day on the free account. After that it is "Gone" and the file is deleted.

### Does Diawi need a credit card?

No. The free level and the free account show a price of 0,00€, and the pricing table lists no payment step for them.
