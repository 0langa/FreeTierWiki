Codehooks is a hosted backend aimed at webhooks and small APIs. You write Express-like routes in JavaScript and get a NoSQL database with MongoDB-like queries, a key-value store, queues with workers for retries, cron jobs, and JWT and JWKS authentication, with nothing to provision. It comes from the team behind restdb.io.

## What the free plan gives you

The Development plan is free, for exploring and prototyping, and needs no credit card. It includes 1 developer and 1 space. API and function calls are allowed at 1 per second and 500 per day.

Storage is 150 MB for the database and 1 GB for files. Queues get 1 worker per queue with a 30-second worker timeout. Chat and email support are included. All data and backups are stored in the EU. The Pro plan costs $19 per month, with up to 3600 calls per minute, and adds daily backups.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing section does not say what happens when a free project passes 1 call per second or 500 calls in a day, or fills its 150 MB database. Codehooks describes its paid pricing as flat rate with no per-request fees and no metering, and the free plan needs no card, so there is no overage bill. Check how your webhook sender retries failed deliveries before you rely on the free limits.

## Gotchas

- The daily limit of 500 calls is easy to pass with a busy webhook source such as a store or a payment provider.
- The free plan has no daily backups; paid plans include them.
- If you cancel a paid plan, your data is deleted automatically after 2 weeks.

## Questions people ask

### Is Codehooks free?

Yes. The Development plan is free with no credit card and allows 500 API and function calls a day.

### Where does Codehooks store data?

All data and backups are stored in the EU.

### What happens to my Codehooks data if I cancel?

It is deleted automatically 2 weeks after you cancel, so export what you need first.
