Secret Manager keeps API keys, passwords, certificates, and other sensitive values in Google Cloud. You store each value as a version of a named secret, and your app or service reads it when it starts or when it needs it. It suits developers who already deploy to Google Cloud and want secrets out of code and environment files.

## What the free plan gives you

Secret Manager is part of the Google Cloud Free Tier, and the free amounts last after the free trial ends. Each month you get 6 active secret versions, 10,000 access operations, and 3 rotation notifications at no charge.

Management operations are free: creating secrets, destroying them, and changing the state of a version cost nothing. Destroyed versions are free too. The monthly free amounts are aggregated across all projects on one billing account and reset every month. Google bills only the usage past them.

A version counts as active while it is enabled or disabled. An automatic replication policy is billed as a single location.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops at the free amounts. Past them, Google bills at the rates on the pricing page: about $0.06 per active version per month, $0.03 per 10,000 access operations, and $0.05 per rotation notification.

Billing follows actual use. A version that is active for 2 days is billed for those days, not the whole month, and 25,000 access operations are billed as 2.5 units of 10,000. In Google's worked example, 50,000 access requests cost $0.12 after the free 10,000.

## Gotchas

- A disabled version still counts as active. Destroy old versions you no longer need to stop paying for them.
- With a user-managed replication policy, every location is billed, so one secret in 3 locations uses 3 active versions.
- Rotation notifications are billed for every message sent to your Pub/Sub topic, so a misconfigured topic can add charges.

## Questions people ask

### Is Google Secret Manager free?

Up to a point. Each month 6 active secret versions, 10,000 access operations, and 3 rotation notifications are free, and you pay for use past that.

### Does deleting a secret version stop the charge?

Disabling does not, because a disabled version still counts as active. Destroying the version does, since destroyed versions are free.

### Are the free amounts per project?

No. They are aggregated across projects by billing account, so all your projects share one monthly allowance.
