You install Data Fetcher as an extension inside Airtable and build requests in its own interface. It suits no-code builders who want API data in a base.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and, in the site's words, is free forever. Each month it includes 100 runs, 10,000 response records, 5 saved requests, and 1 user. Scheduled requests, triggers, and all integrations are part of the Free plan. The home page says you can try it with no credit card.

A run is one API request. Running on many Airtable records or on paginated data uses one extra run per record or page, and failed requests do not count. A response record is one item in the transformed API response, so 100 items from an API count as 100 records. Starter, at $15 a month billed annually, raises the limits to 3,000 runs and 300,000 records.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ says that when you reach your monthly limit for runs or response records, you cannot run more requests until the limits reset at the start of your next billing cycle. Scheduled runs pause when you hit the limit.

You do not get a bill on the Free plan. The site says it has no free trial, only this free plan that never expires. Paid plans are billed by Stripe, and the FAQ says Data Fetcher never sees or stores your card information.

## Gotchas

- The home page lists a limit of 5MB for the maximum import size.
- Pagination and multi-record runs cost one run per page or record, so 100 runs go fast on big imports.
- Team members need a Pro, Business, or Business plus plan. The Free and Starter plans have 1 user.
