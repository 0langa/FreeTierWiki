PixelVault can turn images into hosted URLs that you can share with teammates or embed in an app. It also offers transformations such as resize and format conversion. The free image plan fits small assets like screenshots, test evidence and lightweight site images.

## What the free plan gives you

Free covers one project, 200 MB storage, 500 monthly uploads and 1 GB bandwidth. Images are capped at 5 MB each, with 1,000 unique monthly transformations and branded URLs. The 100 private signed-URL assets span images/video/audio. MP4 and supported audio originals can reach 25 MB, sharing storage/bandwidth rather than the image-size cap.

Monthly AI allowances include ten FLUX.1 schnell generations and five fast edits; descriptions/OCR cover twenty images total. Video conversion includes two output minutes monthly with sixty-second clips; video/audio enrichment shares two monthly source minutes. Choose asset access mode before embedding, and budget each processing allowance separately.

## When you hit a limit

Emails warn at 80%/100%. Uploads stop at quota; storage, monthly-upload and private-asset excess return HTTP 402. Bandwidth exhaustion shows image placeholders and stops video until reset/upgrade, without overage billing. Paid-to-Free downgrades receive fourteen days' bandwidth grace; excess stored images remain but block uploads.

Images older than six months and unused thirty days may be removed after fourteen days' grace. Every plan allows export. Unverified accounts permit five uploads; disposable emails are rejected. Anonymous keyless uploads expire after thirty days, and registration does not preserve their URLs. Reupload using your API key and replace the URL.

## Gotchas

- The free file size limit still applies when you have storage available.
- Frequent CI runs can consume the upload allowance if they publish a new screenshot on every run.
- Video and audio share storage, bandwidth and private-asset capacity; check their separate processing allowances.

## Questions people ask

### Is PixelVault free for screenshots?

Yes. Small screenshots can fit the image plan’s storage and upload limits, subject to the individual file size cap.

### Can I resize images after upload?

Image transformations are included in the plan. Choose the output dimensions and format suitable for the page.

### Can I share an uploaded image privately?

The pricing table lists a limited number of private images accessible through signed URLs. Check the URL and its intended audience before distributing it.
