PixelVault can turn images into hosted URLs that you can share with teammates or embed in an app. It also offers transformations such as resize and format conversion. The free image plan fits small assets like screenshots, test evidence and lightweight site images.

## What the free plan gives you

The image plan covers one project with 200 MB of storage, 500 uploads each month and 1 GB of bandwidth. The pricing page lists a 5 MB limit per file, so a large original needs to be reduced before it fits even if the rest of the account is empty. Free includes image transformations and provider-branded URLs. The plan also lists private images with signed URLs. Before embedding an image, make sure you have chosen the intended access mode. Review the separate product terms if your workflow will also host video.

## When you hit a limit

Uploading images uses storage, while serving them to readers uses bandwidth. A screenshot embedded on a popular page can use much more bandwidth than a preview sent to one person. The pricing table describes higher allowances on paid plans. The cited pages leave Free enforcement unclear, so review project usage and the product’s current limits before routing a busy page through it.

## Gotchas

- The free file size limit still applies when you have storage available.
- Frequent CI runs can consume the upload allowance if they publish a new screenshot on every run.
- Check video terms separately. This listing uses the provider’s image plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is PixelVault free for screenshots?

Yes. Small screenshots can fit the image plan’s storage and upload limits, subject to the individual file size cap.

### Can I resize images after upload?

Image transformations are included in the plan. Choose the output dimensions and format suitable for the page.

### Can I share an uploaded image privately?

The pricing table lists a limited number of private images accessible through signed URLs. Check the URL and its intended audience before distributing it.
