You send standard HTTP requests to one chat completions endpoint, or use an SDK, and change the model ID to switch models. This page covers the free models, whose IDs end in `:free`.


## What the free plan gives you

You can call any model with a `:free` ID without paying. Free models run at 20 requests per minute. The daily cap depends on how many credits you have bought over the life of the account.

The published table lists 50 requests per day below 10 lifetime credits purchased and 1,000 at 10 or more. The accompanying note grants the higher allowance one credit below that nominal threshold to absorb rounding and top-up fees. Check your key’s effective limit when you are near the boundary.

New users also get a small free allowance to test the service. OpenRouter also offers a Free Models Router that picks a free model for you. The FAQ calls free models low-limit and usually not suitable for production use.

## When you hit a limit

OpenRouter rejects the request with a 429 error and the message "Rate limit exceeded". Platform-limit errors carry quota/reset headers; provider-side 429 responses can differ. Wait and retry, or switch to the paid version of the same model.

The limits page lists no charge for hitting a cap. But a negative credit balance can cause 402 errors, even on free models. Add credits to bring the balance above zero.

## Gotchas

- Accounts below the higher-tier threshold normally get 50 requests per day; the documented one-credit tolerance applies near the nominal 10-credit boundary.
- The daily cap is based on all-time credits purchased. Spending credits does not lower it again.
- The pages do not say whether a credit card is needed for free models. The limits page lists no card rule.

## Questions people ask

### Is OpenRouter free?

Partly. Models with a `:free` ID cost nothing to call, within the rate limits. Paid models cost credits.

### What are the OpenRouter free model limits?

Free models allow 20 requests per minute. The nominal daily tiers are 50 requests below 10 lifetime credits purchased and 1,000 at 10 or more, with a documented one-credit tolerance for the higher allowance.

### What happens when I go over the OpenRouter free limit?

You get a 429 "Rate limit exceeded" error. Retry later, or move to the paid version of the model. A negative credit balance can also block free models with a 402 error.
