Nile is a Postgres platform designed for B2B applications, where each customer company or workspace is a tenant. It separates storage from compute, gives each tenant a virtual database with isolation, and adds user management such as authentication. It suits developers building a multi-tenant SaaS who want tenant isolation built into the database.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is described as for prototyping or side projects, with no pauses and always available. It includes unlimited databases, unlimited tenant databases, and unlimited vector embeddings, with tenant isolation.

Serverless compute is measured in query tokens: the Free plan includes 50 million query tokens, and storage is 1 GB. Active users, active tenants, and monthly active users per tenant are unlimited, and tenant insights are kept for 1 day. The general docs describe broader tenant capabilities, but the pricing page marks provisioned compute, branching, backups, and global placement as coming soon. Confirm availability before depending on them.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say whether the Free plan stops or bills when you pass 50 million query tokens or 1 GB of storage. It promises notification near 70% usage and says additional capacity is typically available even on Free. Its Free column lists $0.06 per million additional tokens and $1.50 per additional GB, so check your billing settings before you rely on it.

The Pro plan costs $15 a month and includes 150 million query tokens and 5 GB of storage, then $0.05 per million tokens and $1.00 per additional GB.

## Gotchas

- Query tokens are Nile's own unit, so estimate your usage on the Free plan before you plan around it.
- Tenant insights are kept for 1 day on the Free plan, against 2 weeks on Pro.
- The rendered Free column includes social auth, magic auth, and email verification. MFA and enterprise SAML are absent.

## Questions people ask

### Is Nile free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 50 million query tokens and 1 GB of storage, with unlimited databases and no pauses.

### Does a free Nile database pause when idle?

No. The pricing page describes the Free plan as having no pauses and always available.

### How much is Nile Pro?

Pro costs $15 a month with 150 million query tokens and 5 GB of storage.
