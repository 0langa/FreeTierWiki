Sematext Cloud is a hosted observability service with separate products for logs, infrastructure monitoring, service monitoring, tracing, network maps, real user monitoring, and synthetic checks. Each product runs as an App with its own plan. It suits teams that want logs and metrics in one place with per-product pricing.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page offers a 14-day free trial with no commitment, no credit card needed, and unlimited users. Every product card has a Start free trial button.

After the trial, starting prices on the pricing page are $5 a month for Logs, $2.8 a month for Infra Monitoring, $8.64 a month for Service Monitoring, $19 a month for Tracing, $9 a month for Experience, and $2 per monitor a month for Synthetics. The pricing FAQ says each Monitoring App can have a different plan, "including the free plan", but the page does not list what a free plan includes, so treat the trial as the reliable free offer.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 14-day trial ends. One FAQ answer says you can cancel by simply stopping sending logs or switching to the Basic plan, and the Logs pricing lists charges for data received and data stored. Tracing price depends on plan tier, daily span volume, and retention, which is 1 day on Basic and 7 days on Standard and Pro.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is mentioned in the FAQ without any listed limits.
- Each App has its own plan, so costs add up across Logs, Infra, and Synthetics.
- Prices depend on hosts, containers, plan, and data, so estimate with the calculator.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sematext free?

It has a 14-day free trial with no credit card. The FAQ mentions a free plan, but the pricing page lists no free limits.

### Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is needed.

### Can different products use different plans?

Yes. Each Monitoring App is independent and can have its own plan.
