You work inside your pull requests. The app writes into the description or posts a comment, and you manage repositories in a dashboard.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and needs no credit card. It gives you 25,000 tokens per month, which the page puts at about 10 pull requests. Repositories are unlimited, and tokens belong to the account, not to a repository.

Tokens depend on the size of the diff. The FAQ says an average pull request is around 2,300 tokens, and big diffs need more. The plan includes automated PR descriptions and inline code refactoring, and it is rate limited.

The Pro plan costs $19 per month for 200,000 tokens, about 40 pull requests, with larger token tiers above it. The page says What The Diff does not store your code or diffs, and does not train on them.

## When you hit a limit

If you run out of tokens, you can upgrade to a bigger plan or wait until your next billing cycle. The FAQ says What The Diff does not upgrade your plan automatically, so you cannot be billed by surprise.

Unused tokens do not roll over. On the Free plan this is simple: the monthly tokens reset and the rest are lost. Settings such as a maximum tokens per pull request, skipping CI pull requests, and delaying drafts help you stay within the allowance.

## Gotchas

- The update trigger regenerates the comment on each push. The docs warn it can use many tokens without adding value.
- The service needs full access to your code, because GitHub and GitLab have no permission to read only a pull request diff.
- One login covers one platform. Using the other platform needs a second account.

## Questions people ask

### Is What The Diff free?

Yes. The Free plan has 25,000 tokens per month, about 10 pull requests, and unlimited repositories. It needs no credit card.

### What happens if I run out of What The Diff tokens?

You can upgrade to a bigger plan or wait for the next billing cycle. It does not upgrade you on its own.

### Is there a free plan for open source?

Yes, for qualifying projects. What The Diff sponsors open source projects with a free plan. Projects must be in active development, not backed by a company, and have 10 or more contributors or 100 or more GitHub stars.
