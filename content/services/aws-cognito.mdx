You set up user pools in the AWS console, then call Cognito through its managed login page, the AWS SDKs, or the Amplify libraries. It suits teams that already build on AWS.

## What the free plan gives you

Cognito has a free tier on the Essentials and Lite pricing tiers. It does not end after the first 12 months of an AWS account, and it covers both new and existing customers. Users who sign in directly or through a social identity provider get 10,000 monthly active users (MAU) free per month. The limit applies per account or per AWS organization.

Users who come in through a SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect provider get 50 MAU free per month, whatever tier you pick. Identity pools, which hand out unique IDs and temporary AWS credentials, cost nothing. Some older Lite user pools keep a free tier of 50,000 MAU. The free tier is not available in the AWS GovCloud (US) Regions.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops at the limit. Cognito bills every MAU above the free amount, so a busy month turns into a charge on your AWS bill. The pricing page shows a rate per MAU that drops as your user count grows. For SAML or OIDC users above the 50 MAU free tier, the price is $0.015 per MAU.

The Plus tier has no free tier at all. Machine-to-machine token requests have no free tier either. Because the AWS account needs a card, watch your MAU count and set up an AWS billing alert before you launch.

## Gotchas

- The free tier covers only the Lite and Essentials tiers. A user pool on Plus is billed from the first user.
- Machine-to-machine token requests have no free tier, so a service that fetches many tokens costs money from the start.
- Enterprise users from SAML or OIDC providers get only 50 free MAU. A B2B app with many such users pays quickly.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amazon Cognito free?

Partly. The Lite and Essentials tiers include 10,000 MAU free each month, and the free tier does not expire after the first 12 months. You pay for every MAU above that.

### Does Amazon Cognito need a credit card?

You need an AWS account to use Cognito, and AWS asks for a payment method when you sign up. The Cognito pricing page does not describe a card-free option.

### What happens when I go over the Cognito free tier?

Cognito keeps working and bills the extra MAU at the per-user rate on the pricing page. There is no hard cap, so monitor your usage.
