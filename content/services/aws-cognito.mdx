You set up user pools in the AWS console, then call Cognito through its managed login page, the AWS SDKs, or the Amplify libraries. It suits teams that already build on AWS.

## What the free plan gives you

Cognito has a free tier on the Essentials and Lite pricing tiers. It does not end after the first 12 months of an AWS account, and it covers both new and existing customers. Users who sign in directly or through a social identity provider get 10,000 monthly active users (MAU) free per month. The limit applies per account or per AWS organization.

Users who come in through a SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect provider get 50 MAU free per month, whatever tier you pick. Identity pools, which hand out unique IDs and temporary AWS credentials, cost nothing. The free tier is not available in the AWS GovCloud (US) Regions.

## When you hit a limit

On Paid AWS accounts, usage above Cognito’s allowance incurs charges unless configured project controls intervene. SAML/OIDC usage above 50 MAU costs $0.015 per MAU. Plus direct/social users and machine-to-machine tokens have no free allowance.

AWS offers optional Paid-project monthly spend limits starting at $20 that pause resources at the threshold. These are not a default Cognito cap. New Free accounts instead consume promotional credits for eligible excess usage and suspend when the account’s Free period or credits end. Monitor both service usage and account-plan status.

## Gotchas

- The direct/social 10,000-MAU allowance covers Lite and Essentials. Plus still has the separate 50-MAU SAML/OIDC allowance.
- Machine-to-machine token requests have no free tier, so a service that fetches many tokens costs money from the start.
- Enterprise users from SAML or OIDC providers get only 50 free MAU. A B2B app with many such users pays quickly.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amazon Cognito free?

Partly. The Lite and Essentials tiers include 10,000 MAU free each month, and the free tier does not expire after the first 12 months. You pay for every MAU above that.

### Does Amazon Cognito need a credit card?

Not for most new customers. AWS says a payment method is not required to sign up for most new customers, but it may ask for one to verify your identity. New Free accounts suspend at six months or credit exhaustion, with 90 days to upgrade and recover retained data.

### What happens when I go over the Cognito free tier?

Paid accounts incur per-user charges beyond the allowance unless configured project controls intervene. New Free accounts use credits and can suspend when those or the Free period run out.
