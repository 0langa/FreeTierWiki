You capture a Jam with the Chrome extension, Jam for Mac, or Jam for iOS, then share the link or send it to your issue tracker. It suits support, QA, and engineering staff who report web bugs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. Per month it includes 30 Jams, 5 Recording Links, and 30 AI summaries. Browser recordings can last up to 5 minutes, and recordings from Jam for Mac up to 10 minutes. Viewer seats and folders are unlimited.

Each Jam keeps console logs, network requests, user events, and device details with the recording. Recording Links let a customer record their screen without an extension, account, or download. The pricing page also says open source teams can apply for a free plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists the free limits per month but does not say what happens at the 30th Jam or the 5th Recording Link. The pricing page lists no overage charge for the Free plan.

The Team plan removes the Jam cap. It costs $14 per creator per month, billed yearly, and includes a 14 day free trial. It adds 150 Recording Links and 200 AI summaries a month, with browser recordings up to 15 minutes. Enterprise has unlimited Recording Links.

## Gotchas

- Recording Links are limited to 5 a month on the free plan, which can run out quickly if you send one to every customer.
- By default, Recording Links capture only screen recordings. Console logs and network requests need you to connect your domain and install Jam's scripts on your site.
- Mac recordings include console logs and network requests only when the Chrome extension is installed in the browser you record.

## Questions people ask

### Is Jam free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 and includes 30 Jams and 5 Recording Links a month. Viewer seats are unlimited.

### Does Jam need a credit card?

No card is listed for the Free plan. You install the extension and enter your email to set up an account. The Team plan has a 14 day free trial.

### How long can a Jam recording be?

On the Free plan a browser recording can run up to 5 minutes, and a Jam for Mac recording up to 10 minutes. The Team plan allows 15 and 30 minutes.
