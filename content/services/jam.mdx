You capture a Jam with the Chrome extension, Jam for Mac, or Jam for iOS, then share the link or send it to your issue tracker. It suits support, QA, and engineering staff who report web bugs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. Per month it includes 30 Jams, 5 Recording Links, and 30 AI summaries. There are 5 creator seats; viewer seats and folders are unlimited. Recordings can run up to 5 minutes, in the browser and in Jam for Mac. The pricing cards, the comparison table and the video docs agree on this.

Each Jam keeps console logs, network requests, user events, and device details with the recording. Recording Links let a customer record their screen without an extension, account, or download. The pricing page also says open source teams can apply for a free plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists the free limits per month but does not say what happens at the 30th Jam or the 5th Recording Link. It lists no Free overage price, and neither the pricing page nor the setup docs explicitly establish the Free card requirement.

The Team plan removes the Jam cap. It costs $14 per creator per month, billed yearly, and includes a 14 day free trial. It adds 150 Recording Links and 200 AI summaries a month, with a browser duration discrepancy too: plan cards say 15 minutes, while the video docs/table say 30 minutes. Enterprise has unlimited Recording Links.

## Gotchas

- Recording Links are limited to 5 a month on the free plan, which can run out quickly if you send one to every customer.
- By default, Recording Links capture only screen recordings. Console logs and network requests need you to connect your domain and install Jam's scripts on your site.
- Paid workspaces can auto-upgrade Viewers to billable Creators when they create content or send it to integrations. Admins can disable Auto-approve seats; this is separate from Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Jam free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 and includes 30 Jams and 5 Recording Links a month. Viewer seats are unlimited.

### Does Jam need a credit card?

The reviewed official pages do not explicitly confirm the Free signup card policy. Team has a separate 14-day trial; do not treat that trial as the ongoing Free plan.

### How long can a Jam recording be?

Free recordings run up to 5 minutes, in the browser and in Jam for Mac. Team recordings run up to 30 minutes in the table and the video docs, but the Team plan card says 15 minutes.
