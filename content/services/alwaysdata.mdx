Alwaysdata runs a managed public cloud for websites, databases, and email. You manage these through its administration interface while Alwaysdata maintains the servers. Free is intended for personal projects, with stricter eligibility and website-address rules than the general hosting service.

## What the free plan gives you

Free costs €0 monthly, is ad-free, and is described as available for life. It includes 1 GB of SSD disk, 256 MB of RAM, a quarter CPU, and three rolling days of backups. Disk usage includes files, mail, and databases together.

The detailed pricing guide says Free cannot be used for profit. Websites are limited to the account's alwaysdata.net address, and Free cannot run standalone services. The main pricing page separately advertises email on your own domains; that does not grant custom domains for websites. General hosting offers allow multiple sites, domains, databases, and email addresses within resource limits, but those general features do not override Free restrictions.

## When you hit a limit

The reviewed pages do not specify exact behavior when Free disk, CPU, or memory is exhausted, or explicitly establish its signup card requirement. A fixed allowance is not proof that service expands automatically or that over-limit requests always stop without consequences.

For more capacity, the smallest Plus plan has 50 GB disk and 1 GB RAM. Its €5 monthly equivalent requires annual payment; monthly payment is €6, excluding VAT. Larger Plus plans offer more resources. Changing configuration through Subscriptions has no migration charge; the new paid subscription still costs money.

## Gotchas

- Free cannot be used for profit, and custom website domains are unavailable. Email on your own domain is a separately advertised feature.
- Only three days of backups are retained, so keep an independent copy of important data.
- The public cloud is shared with hundreds of users. Its documented availability rate is 99.7%, below dedicated offers.

## Questions people ask

### Is Alwaysdata free forever?

Its Free offer is advertised as available for life at €0. It is restricted to non-profit personal use and the account website address.

### How much disk and memory does Free include?

It includes 1 GB SSD, 256 MB RAM, and a quarter CPU. Files, email, and databases share the disk quota.

### Can I use my own domain on Free?

The main page advertises own-domain email, but the detailed guide restricts websites to the account's alwaysdata.net address. Do not assume the email feature permits custom website domains.
