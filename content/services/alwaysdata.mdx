Alwaysdata is a hosting company that runs a managed public cloud for websites, applications, databases, and email. Alwaysdata runs the servers, and you manage sites and services from your account. It suits students and hobby developers who want a traditional host with a free plan that does not expire.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free offer costs 0 € a month and is described as for personal needs, ad-free, and available for life. It includes 1 GB of SSD disk space, 256 MB of RAM, and a quarter of a CPU, and the page says the CPU allowance also lasts for life. You get 3 days of backups.

You can use email on your own domain names, and each account gets an alwaysdata.net address for its sites. The help pages say that on all offers you can have unlimited sites, domains, databases, and email addresses, so the real limit on the Free plan is the disk, memory, and CPU share. Free and Plus accounts run on the public cloud, where one hosting account is spread over servers that host hundreds of users.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you fill the 1 GB of disk or use more than the memory and CPU share. The Free plan has no metered usage, so you are not billed for going over it.

To get more room you move to Plus, which starts from 5 € a month excluding VAT with 50 GB of disk and up to 8 GB of RAM, and has 30 days free. The help pages say that moving to a higher offer is free of charge from the Subscriptions menu.

## Gotchas

- The page labels the Free plan for personal needs, so check the terms before you host a business site on it.
- Only 3 days of backups are kept, so keep your own copy of anything you cannot rebuild.
- The help pages list a 99.7% availability rate for the public cloud, lower than the dedicated Max offers.

## Questions people ask

### Is Alwaysdata really free forever?

The pricing page calls the Free offer ad-free and available for life, at 0 € a month.

### How much disk and memory does the Free plan include?

It includes 1 GB of SSD disk space and 256 MB of RAM, with a quarter of a CPU.

### Can I host email on the Free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes email on your own domain names, plus an alwaysdata.net address for your sites.
