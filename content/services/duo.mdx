Duo is Cisco's multi-factor authentication and access service. After a user enters a password, Duo asks them to approve the login on their phone, and admins manage users and protected applications from one panel. Small teams use it to put a second factor in front of servers, VPNs, and internal tools.

## What the free plan gives you

Duo Free is listed at $0 per user per month and is meant for small businesses with 10 or fewer users. You can add up to 10 users and get strong MFA, integrations, and the free authenticator app. The Duo Free page says you can secure as many cloud and on-premises applications as you need at no extra cost.

Users verify their identity with push-based approvals, and you can add more authentication methods. The editions page also offers a 30-day free trial of the paid editions, which you can activate in minutes.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan's limit is 10 users. The pricing pages do not say what happens if you try to add more users; they point to the paid editions. Duo Essentials is $3 per user per month and adds Duo Directory, phishing-resistant MFA, passwordless sign-in, single sign-on, Trusted Endpoints, and unlimited applications. Advantage is $6 and Premier is $9 per user per month.

Self-service subscriptions under 100 users are bought in increments of 10, and above 100 users in increments of 25, so you pay for seats in blocks rather than one at a time.

## Gotchas

- The cap is 10 users in total, so a growing team reaches it quickly.
- Single sign-on is not part of Duo Free, so you still sign in to each app separately.
- Paid seats come in blocks of 10 below 100 users, which makes the step up from Free larger than one seat.

## Questions people ask

### Is Duo free?

Yes, for up to 10 users. Duo Free costs $0 per user per month and includes MFA and the free authenticator app.

### How many applications can I protect with Duo Free?

The Duo Free page says you can secure as many cloud and on-premises applications as you need, at no extra cost.

### Is there a trial of the paid Duo editions?

Yes. The editions page offers a 30-day free trial.
