You use Zoho Checkout in a web dashboard. You connect a payment gateway, build a payment page, and share its link. It suits small sellers who want to take payments without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is for a single user. It includes 3 payment pages and lets you collect up to 50 payments. The pricing page says the 50 is a maximum for the whole account and does not reset every month. The plan lists Stripe as its integrated gateway.

Payment pages cover one-time and recurring payments. For recurring payments you can set weekly, monthly, or yearly billing cycles. The help pages say Stripe charges its own transaction fees under its pricing policy, and Zoho Checkout adds no extra fees. Recurring payments work only when customers pay by card.

The paid Standard plan costs $9 a month, billed annually, and gives 500 payments a month and 3 users.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach the 50 payment limit. Because it is a lifetime total, it never refills. You must move to a paid plan to collect more.

Zoho Checkout itself does not take a fee on payments. The free plan asks for no card, so no Zoho bill follows, but your gateway still charges its fees on payments you collect.

## Gotchas

- The 50 payment limit is for the whole life of the account. It does not reset each month.
- The free plan has 1 user. Standard adds users, and Professional adds more.
- Prices are shown per organization per month, billed annually, and they do not include taxes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Checkout free?

Yes, up to a point. The Free plan costs $0 and allows 1 user, 3 payment pages, and 50 payments in total.

### Does the Zoho Checkout payment limit reset every month?

No. The pricing page says the maximum of 50 payments is overall and does not reset every month.

### Does Zoho Checkout charge fees on payments?

The help pages say Zoho Checkout adds no fees. Stripe charges transaction fees under its own pricing.
