You build routes in the Zuplo Portal or in code, and deploy from a GitHub repo. It suits developers who want API management without running gateway servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and covers 100K requests per month. The compare table lists 5 environments, 2 projects, 2 developer seats, and 100 consumers and API keys. Analytics keep 1 day of history, and live logs and traces keep 15 minutes.

It includes the AI Gateway with 1 team and a developer portal. The MCP Gateway runs in development mode only, with 1K tool invocations a month. DDoS protection is basic, and GitOps works with GitHub.

Custom domains are not part of the free plan. The Builder plan costs $25 a month and adds 2 custom domains, 10 environments, 1000 consumers, and 7 days of analytics. Zuplo says Builder is for solo entrepreneurs and hobbyists going live, not for teams.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says that on the Free tier, requests beyond your limit are rate-limited until the next billing cycle. You are not billed. The answer also says your API will never suddenly stop working due to limits.

On Builder, you can buy more request capacity, with $100 per 100K, and Zuplo says it will reach out to help you size your plan. Enterprise plans start at $1,000 a month on an annual contract and add SSO and an SLA.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has no custom domains, so your API uses a Zuplo address until you move to Builder.
- The MCP Gateway servers on Free are development mode only. Production MCP use needs Builder or Enterprise.
- Free keeps 1 day of analytics and only 15 minutes of live logs, so debug a problem soon after it happens.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zuplo free?

Yes. The free plan covers 100K requests a month with no credit card. It includes the developer portal and a development-mode MCP Gateway.

### What happens when I go over the Zuplo free limit?

Requests beyond the limit are rate-limited until the next billing cycle. The pricing FAQ says your API will not suddenly stop working.

### Does the Zuplo free plan support custom domains?

No. Custom domains start on the Builder plan, which adds 2 for $25 a month.
