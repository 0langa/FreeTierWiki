You build routes in the Zuplo Portal or in code and deploy from source control. Zuplo provides a managed gateway for authentication, rate limits and a developer portal in front of your API. The current signup route requires contacting support while Zuplo prepares its next pricing model.

## What the free plan gives you

As observed on November 23, 2026, the pricing page says self-service Free signups are temporarily paused. It directs developers to support@zuplo.com to arrange a free account and says self-service signup will return when usage-based pricing launches. No launch date is published.

The replacement pay-as-you-go plan is still described as forthcoming, with a free tier planned and its feature list to be announced. The former request allowance, seat and project counts, retention periods, MCP invocation quota, and Builder plan are no longer presented as current offers. Do not assume those older terms apply to an account arranged now.

The page lists API Gateway, MCP Gateway, AI Gateway, developer portal and API key management across plans. It does not specify the current Free entitlement limits, custom-domain allowance or production MCP eligibility. Confirm these with support before designing an integration around them.

## When you hit a limit

Current Free request limits, reset cadence, enforcement, card requirements and overage billing are not published. The general FAQ promises continuity and discusses customized limits and flexible overages, but it does not establish a specific Free billing policy. Ask for the terms of the account you are offered.

Enterprise remains available through an annual contract and a custom quote. Its base package includes 1 million requests per month, a 99.5% SLA and one observability integration. SSO and role-based access, premium support, custom analytics retention, monetization, secure connectivity and dedicated or self-hosted deployment are individually priced additions.

Separate trials can be arranged with the team to evaluate enterprise features. Their public description does not give a universal duration or card requirement.

## Gotchas

- The setup tutorial still describes creating a free account directly. The newer pricing notice directs new free users to support during the pause.
- A future free tier is announced, but its quotas and launch date are not. Existing account terms should be checked separately.
- After support provisions an account, the documented gateway setup still uses a blank API & MCP Gateway project, routes.oas.json, and the URL Forward Handler. Save the route and test it before connecting source control.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zuplo free?

Support can arrange a free account during the temporary self-service signup pause. The forthcoming usage-based plan promises a free tier, but current quotas are not published.

### What happens when I go over the Zuplo free limit?

The current page does not publish a specific Free cap or overage policy. Confirm the limits and billing terms of the account support offers you.

### Does the Zuplo free plan support custom domains?

Current Free custom-domain eligibility is not published. The previous Builder offer is no longer listed, so confirm available domain options and their price with support.
