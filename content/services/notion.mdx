You work in the Notion web app, the desktop app, or the mobile app, and you can reach your workspace through an API with a personal access token. The Free plan suits one person organizing their own work.

## What the free plan gives you

On the Free plan, a workspace you use alone can hold as many pages and blocks as you like. A block is one piece of content, such as a paragraph, a to-do item, or an image. In a Free workspace with 2 or more members, the number of blocks is limited.

You can invite up to 10 external guests, such as contractors or partners. File uploads are limited to 5MB each. Page history on the Free plan goes back 7 days. Notion AI features, meeting notes, and research mode are a limited trial on Free. Paid plans add unlimited uploads, longer history, private teamspaces, and SAML SSO.

## When you hit a limit

The block limit only applies to Free workspaces with more than one member. The pricing page says teams can try Notion with up to a certain number of blocks before upgrading. It does not say how many blocks that is, and it does not say what happens when you reach it.

The Free plan allows uploads up to 5MB each, and paid plans allow unlimited uploads. The pricing page does not describe any automatic charge on the Free plan, which costs €0.

## Gotchas

- The unlimited blocks promise holds for individuals only. A team that shares a Free workspace can hit the block limit.
- Page history is short on Free, so restoring an older version works for 7 days at most.
- The AI features, meeting notes, and research mode are trials on the Free plan, not ongoing features.

## Questions people ask

### Is Notion free?

Yes. The Free plan costs €0. It is meant for individuals, and it limits blocks in workspaces with 2 or more members.

### What happens when I hit the Notion free plan limit?

The pricing page does not say. It says teams can use the Free plan with up to a certain number of blocks before upgrading, and it does not give the number or describe what happens at the limit.

### Does the Notion free plan have a file size limit?

Yes. You can upload images, videos, and files up to 5MB each. Paid plans remove that cap.
