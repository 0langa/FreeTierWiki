You work in the Notion web app, the desktop app, or the mobile app, and you can reach your workspace through an API with a personal access token. The Free plan suits one person organizing their own work.

## What the free plan gives you

The current block-usage guide limits a solo Free workspace to 5,000 blocks. The pricing table still says its block restriction is for 2+ members, so the pages are inconsistent; do not rely on unlimited solo blocks. A block is one piece of content, such as a paragraph, a to-do item, or an image. In a Free workspace with 2 or more members, the allowance is 1,000 blocks, according to the block-usage help article.

You can invite up to 10 external guests, such as contractors or partners. File uploads are limited to 5MB each. Page history on the Free plan goes back 7 days. Notion AI is a limited trial on Free. Paid tiers raise the per-file upload limit to 5GB and add longer history; private teamspaces and SAML SSO require Business or Enterprise.

## When you hit a limit

Free workspaces get 5,000 blocks for one person or 1,000 for multiple members, counted cumulatively since signup. Content added through the API or connected tools such as MCP counts too. Deleting content or emptying the trash does not recover the allowance. Notion gives a three-day grace period at the limit, then stops new blocks; existing content remains readable, editable, and organizable. Removing extra members raises the allowance to 5,000; it does not restore unlimited blocks. Adding a second member lowers it to 1,000.

Uploads on Free are capped at 5MB each. Paid plans list a 5GB maximum per file. The official Teams page says the Free plan has no time limit or credit-card requirement.

## Gotchas

- The block guide now caps solo Free workspaces too. Deleted content does not recover the cumulative allowance.
- Page history is short on Free, so restoring an older version works for 7 days at most.
- The Free AI allowance is a limited trial, not an ongoing entitlement.

## Questions people ask

### Is Notion free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing. The block guide gives individuals up to 5,000 blocks and workspaces with 2 or more members up to 1,000.

### What happens when I hit the Notion free plan limit?

Solo Free workspaces reach 5,000 blocks and multi-member workspaces 1,000, then get a three-day grace period. After that, you cannot add blocks, but existing content stays accessible. Deleting blocks does not recover the allowance.

### Does the Notion free plan have a file size limit?

Yes. You can upload images, videos, and files up to 5MB each. Paid plans list a maximum of 5GB per file.
