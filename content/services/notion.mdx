You work in the Notion web app, the desktop app, or the mobile app, and you can reach your workspace through an API with a personal access token. The Free plan suits one person organizing their own work.

## What the free plan gives you

On the Free plan, a workspace you use alone can hold as many pages and blocks as you like. A block is one piece of content, such as a paragraph, a to-do item, or an image. In a Free workspace with 2 or more members, the allowance is 1,000 blocks, according to the block-usage help article.

You can invite up to 10 external guests, such as contractors or partners. File uploads are limited to 5MB each. Page history on the Free plan goes back 7 days. Notion AI features, meeting notes, and research mode are a limited trial on Free. Paid plans add unlimited file-upload counts (about 5GB maximum per file), longer history, private teamspaces, and SAML SSO.

## When you hit a limit

A Free workspace with multiple members gets 1,000 blocks, counted cumulatively since signup. Deleting content or emptying the trash does not recover the allowance. Notion gives a three-day grace period at the limit, then stops new blocks; existing content remains readable, editable, and organizable. Removing extra members restores the single-person unlimited allowance.

Uploads on Free are capped at 5MB each. Paid plans remove the upload-count limit but still have an approximately 5GB maximum per file. The official Teams page says the Free plan has no time limit or credit-card requirement.

## Gotchas

- The unlimited blocks promise holds for individuals only. A team that shares a Free workspace can hit the block limit.
- Page history is short on Free, so restoring an older version works for 7 days at most.
- The AI features, meeting notes, and research mode are trials on the Free plan, not ongoing features.

## Questions people ask

### Is Notion free?

Yes. The Free plan costs €0. It is meant for individuals, and it limits blocks in workspaces with 2 or more members.

### What happens when I hit the Notion free plan limit?

Multi-member Free workspaces reach 1,000 blocks, then get a three-day grace period. After that, you cannot add blocks, but existing content stays accessible. Deleting blocks does not recover the allowance.

### Does the Notion free plan have a file size limit?

Yes. You can upload images, videos, and files up to 5MB each. Paid plans allow unlimited upload counts, with an approximately 5GB maximum per file.
