deSEC is a DNS hosting service run by deSEC e.V., a non-profit based in Berlin. It runs on free and open-source software and hosts your domain's records on global anycast name servers, signed with DNSSEC.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no paid plan: the site says deSEC is free for everyone to use. You host domains you own as managed domains, and every zone is signed with DNSSEC using elliptic-curve cryptography. Queries are answered from anycast frontend servers around the world, and record changes are published within a few seconds.

You manage records in the web interface or through a REST API that supports bulk operations, with Terraform providers and Go, Python, and JavaScript bindings. Record types include HTTPS and SVCB, CDS and CDNSKEY, TLSA, and OPENPGPKEY. Accounts support 2FA with TOTP tokens, and there is Let's Encrypt integration for certbot, acme.sh, and lego.

## When you hit a limit

The API documentation says every account has a maximum number of domains. When you reach it, creating another domain returns 403 Forbidden, and you can contact support if you have a legitimate use case for more. All API operations are also rate limited.

Subdomains that deSEC offers itself, such as names under dedyn.io, are limited to one per account. The home page currently says dedyn.io registration is suspended until further notice after a spike of abusive registrations, so today deSEC is for domains you already own.

## Gotchas

- Free dedyn.io dynDNS names are not available while registration is suspended; bring your own domain.
- The fetched pages do not give the per-account domain cap as a number, only the 403 response when you reach it.
- deSEC is donation-funded with grants and sponsors, and the pages state no service-level agreement.

## Questions people ask

### Is deSEC really free?

Yes. The site says deSEC is free for everyone to use, and it is run by a non-profit that asks for donations.

### Can I get a free dedyn.io subdomain from deSEC?

Not at the moment. The home page says dedyn.io registration is suspended until further notice because of abusive registrations.

### Does deSEC support DNSSEC?

Yes. The site says DNS information hosted at deSEC is always signed with DNSSEC.
