You sign up with an email, copy an API key, and send HTTPS requests to one search endpoint or to a batch endpoint. It suits developers who add customs codes to a checkout, a shipping tool, or a product feed.

## What the free plan gives you

HS Ping has no permanent free plan. Every new account gets 100 free lookups, one time, with no credit card and no plan chosen at sign-up. The lookups cover 32 countries and do not expire, but they never renew.

When the 100 lookups run out, you pick a paid plan to continue. Professional costs $49 a month for 50K lookups. Volume costs $129 for 500K, and Scale costs $299 for 2M. Every plan has a rate limit of 1,000 lookups per minute.

## When you hit a limit

Once the 100 trial lookups are gone, nothing is charged automatically. The FAQ says you can choose a plan to continue.

On a paid plan, the site says lookups stop until your next monthly reset or until you move up a plan. HS Ping says it does not meter overage, so you are never billed more than your plan price. Paid plan credits reset every month, but the first 100 do not.

## Gotchas

- The 100 lookups are a trial. After they are used, the free path ends and the lowest plan is a paid one.
- Each paid plan lists the 100 free lookups again, but the FAQ says those trial credits are one-time and non-recurring.
- A wrong classification can mean customs delays or penalties. The site stresses source, version, and last-updated fields so you can audit a stored code later.

## Questions people ask

### Is HS Ping free?

Only for a trial. Every new account gets 100 free lookups, one time, with no credit card. After that you need a paid plan.

### Does HS Ping need a credit card?

No, not to start. The site says you can sign up with just an email and get an API key in a few minutes.

### What happens when I use up the HS Ping free lookups?

Nothing is charged automatically. You choose a plan to keep going. The FAQ says there is no overage metering, so a plan never bills more than its price.
