You sign up with an email, copy an API key, and send HTTPS requests to one search endpoint or to a batch endpoint. It suits developers who add customs codes to a checkout, a shipping tool, or a product feed.

## What the free plan gives you

HS Ping has no permanent free plan. Every new account gets 100 free lookups, one time, with no credit card and no plan chosen at sign-up. The free lookups are one-time credits that never renew. Country counts on the pricing page conflict: its FAQ says 32, while its call to action says 35. Check the coverage list for the country your integration needs.

When the 100 lookups run out, you pick a paid plan to continue. Professional costs $49 a month for 50K lookups. Volume costs $129 for 500K, and Scale costs $299 for 2M. Every plan has a rate limit of 1,000 lookups per minute.

## When you hit a limit

Once the 100 trial lookups are gone, requests return `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`. Nothing is charged until you choose a subscription and add payment details. The 1,000-lookups-per-minute rate limit also applies during the trial.

Paid plan credits reset every month, but the first 100 do not. The current reviewed pricing and usage pages do not specify paid-quota exhaustion or overage billing, so confirm those terms before subscribing.

## Gotchas

- The 100 lookups are a trial. After they are used, the free path ends and the lowest plan is a paid one. Each paid plan lists the 100 free lookups again, but the FAQ says those trial credits are one-time and non-recurring.
- The introduction explicitly says HS Ping is not a classification service. Results provide tariff references with source, version and last-updated fields; selecting the right classification is a separate task.
- Country is optional: without it, the single-lookup endpoint uses WCO six-digit codes.

## Questions people ask

### Is HS Ping free?

Only for a trial. Every new account gets 100 free lookups, one time, with no credit card. After that you need a paid plan.

### Does HS Ping need a credit card?

No, not to start. The site says you can sign up with just an email and get an API key in a few minutes.

### What happens when I use up the HS Ping free lookups?

Requests return `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`. Nothing is charged until you choose a plan and add payment details; the current pages do not establish paid-quota overage terms.
