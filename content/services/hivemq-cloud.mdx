HiveMQ makes an MQTT broker, the message hub that IoT devices and industrial systems publish to and subscribe from. HiveMQ Cloud is the fully managed version, where HiveMQ runs the broker for you. The managed Starter plan offers a 15-day trial, which suits teams evaluating MQTT for an IoT or industrial project.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page offers a 15-day trial of the Starter plan, described as production-ready MQTT connectivity, fully managed. Starter lists managed MQTT connectivity with 10,000 connections and 1 MB/sec, priced by capacity in connections and throughput.

The only permanently free offer on the page is Lab, a free software license for 1 broker deployment on your own infrastructure. Lab is for learning, evaluation, and small-scale testing, with 25 connections and 25 messages per second, and needs no credit card. Its license lasts 30 days and can be renewed manually at no charge. Lab is self-hosted, so it is not a free tier of HiveMQ Cloud.

## When you hit a limit

The trial lasts 15 days. The official quick-start guide has you provide payment details to start it and says you are not charged during the trial. Pricing applies only after the 15-day trial ends, so decide before then whether to keep the broker.

After the trial, Starter costs from $0.34 an hour plus $0.80 per million messages. Self-managed Launch and Run plans cost $299 and $499 a month for 1 broker deployment, and Enterprise pricing is based on capacity.

## Gotchas

- There is no free managed plan after the trial; the page points people looking for free to the self-hosted Lab package.
- Starter asks for payment details up front and is billed by the hour and by messages, so a busy test fleet adds to the bill once the trial ends.
- Contextualize and Analyze features are not available on Starter.

## Questions people ask

### Is HiveMQ Cloud free?

Not permanently. The managed Starter plan has a 15-day trial that asks for payment details, and the free Lab package is self-hosted.

### How much does HiveMQ Cloud Starter cost after the trial?

Starter costs from $0.34 an hour plus $0.80 per million messages.

### What is HiveMQ Lab?

Lab is a free software license for one broker deployment on your own infrastructure, for learning and small-scale testing. Renew its license manually every 30 days at no charge.
