You use the web console at console.deno.com to make an organization and apps. The getting-started guide has you pick a GitHub repository for each app, and the docs say you can deploy from GitHub or directly from the dashboard. It suits JavaScript and TypeScript developers who want to host small apps without managing servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is meant for personal use and smaller projects. It includes 1M requests per month, 20GiB of egress bandwidth, 10 hours of active CPU, and 150 GiB-hours of memory time. You can run 10 apps, attach 5 custom domains, and keep 10 GiB of revision storage. The page also lists 15 builds per hour.

Active CPU is billed only while your code runs, not while it waits for requests or network I/O. Memory is counted at 768 MB per second while the app is loaded, and idle apps shut down after about 20 to 30 seconds. Wildcard subdomains and sandboxes are not included on Free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free plan passes its included amounts. It shows the next plans with rates for extra use. Pro costs $20 per month with 5M requests, 200GiB of egress, and 50 hours of CPU. Extra requests cost $2 per million, extra egress costs $0.20 per GiB, and extra CPU costs $0.10 per hour.

Because the pages do not describe a Free overage rule, treat the included amounts as the real limit and watch usage in the console. The Builder plan costs $200 per month and is aimed at Subhosting users and growing platforms.

## Gotchas

- The docs say Deno Deploy Classic will be shut down on July 20, 2026, so older guides may describe the old product.
- Organization names and slugs cannot be changed after creation, and you cannot reuse a slug from an existing Classic project.
- Monorepos, where the app lives in a subdirectory, are not yet supported.

## Questions people ask

### Is Deno Deploy free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 1M requests, 20GiB of egress, and 10 hours of active CPU each month, for up to 10 apps.

### Does Deno Deploy sleep when idle?

Idle apps shut down after about 20 to 30 seconds, so memory time is not billed while they are scaled to zero.

### How many custom domains does the Deno Deploy free plan allow?

The Free plan allows 5 custom domains. Wildcard subdomains are not included.
