You use the web console at console.deno.com to make an organization and apps. The getting-started guide has you pick a GitHub repository for each app, and the docs say you can deploy from GitHub or directly from the dashboard. Deno says the current service will shut down in April 2027, and existing applications will keep running until then.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is meant for personal use and smaller projects. It includes 1M requests per month, 20GiB of egress bandwidth, 10 hours of active CPU, and 150 GiB-hours of memory time. You can run 10 apps, attach 5 custom domains, and keep 10 GiB of revision storage. The page also lists 15 builds per hour.

Active CPU is billed only while your code runs, not while it waits for requests or network I/O. Memory is counted at 768 MB per second while the app is loaded, and idle apps shut down after about 20 to 30 seconds. Wildcard subdomains are not included on Free.

## When you hit a limit

The official metering changelog says Free applications pause until the next billing cycle when request, bandwidth, CPU, or memory allowances are exceeded. Full Free quotas require linking a credit card to verify the organization; unverified organizations have reduced limits whose quantities are not given there. Paid plans list rates for extra use. Pro costs $20 per month with 5M requests, 200GiB of egress, and 50 hours of CPU. Extra requests cost $2 per million, extra egress costs $0.20 per GiB, and extra CPU costs $0.10 per hour.

Free exhaustion pauses apps rather than creating the paid-plan usage bill described in the changelog. Watch usage in the console before a pause disrupts your app.

## Gotchas

- Deno Deploy Classic closed on July 20, 2026. Older Classic guides describe a separate service.
- The current getting-started guide says you cannot change organization names or slugs after creation.
- The getting-started guide says monorepos, where the app lives in a subdirectory, are not yet supported. The changelog of December 18, 2025 says Deno and npm workspace members can now be deployed, so check the setup flow.

## Questions people ask

### Is Deno Deploy free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 1M requests, 20 GiB of egress, and 10 hours of active CPU each month for up to 10 apps with card verification. Deno plans to shut down the service in April 2027.

### Does Deno Deploy sleep when idle?

Idle apps shut down after about 20 to 30 seconds, so memory time is not billed while they are scaled to zero.

### How many custom domains does the Deno Deploy free plan allow?

The Free plan allows 5 custom domains. Wildcard subdomains are not included.
