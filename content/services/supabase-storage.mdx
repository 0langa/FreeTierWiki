You create buckets in the Supabase dashboard and work with files through the client libraries, resumable uploads, or the S3 protocol. Access rules sit next to your data, so one login covers both.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It includes 1 GB of file storage, 5 GB of egress, and 5 GB of cached egress. You can run 2 active projects. The largest file you can upload on Free is 50 MB.

Storage holds files, folders, and buckets. Buckets are separate containers, so you can keep videos in one and profile pictures in another, each with its own security rules.

Image transformations are listed as not included in the Free plan. On a paid plan, 100 origin images are included, then the pricing page lists $5 per 1,000 origin images.

## When you hit a limit

You are not charged on the Free plan. Supabase's cost-control guide says the spend cap is a Pro plan feature, and that you will not be charged while on Free. Over-use of a quota can lead to restricted services under the Fair Use Policy if it keeps happening.

The other limit is inactivity. The pricing page says free projects are paused after 1 week of inactivity. A paused project cannot serve its files until you restore it. Pro raises the file size limit to 500 GB per file.

## Gotchas

- Free projects pause after 1 week without activity, and files in a paused project are not served until you restore it.
- The 50 MB file size limit is a hard ceiling on Free. You can set a lower limit per bucket, but not a higher one.
- HTML files are returned as plain text for security, so you cannot host a web page from a bucket.

## Questions people ask

### Is Supabase Storage free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan includes 1 GB of file storage, 5 GB of egress, and 5 GB of cached egress, with a 50 MB limit per file.

### Does Supabase Storage need a credit card?

No. The entry records that the Free plan needs no card, and Supabase says you are not charged while on it.

### What happens when I go over the Supabase Storage limit?

You are not billed on Free. A project that keeps going over its quota can be restricted, and you can upgrade to Pro for more.
