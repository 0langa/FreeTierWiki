You use TimeCamp in the web app at app.timecamp.com, in a free desktop app, or in the mobile app. The desktop app can record computer activity. It suits teams and freelancers who need hours tracked by project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is a free forever plan. It has unlimited users and unlimited projects. It includes timesheets, a browser plugin, web, desktop, and mobile apps, and two-factor authentication. The comparison table lists one integration on Free.

The FAQ also says the free plan includes the new AI time tracking. Paid plans start with Starter at $3.99 per user per month billed annually, or $5.49 month to month. Starter adds unlimited tasks, time-off, and overtime tracking. Higher plans add screenshots, unlimited integrations, and more.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists feature limits rather than usage caps, so there is no point where the Free plan stops. You run into missing features instead, such as time-off or unlimited integrations.

One cost needs care. The pricing page says the AI Time Tracker is billed by token usage, with an estimated cost of about $6 per user per month, and that the free trial includes $10 in credits. It does not explain how this applies on the Free plan, so check the account settings before you turn the AI tracker on.

## Gotchas

- The AI Time Tracker is listed as billed by token usage on every plan, including Free.
- Free allows one integration. Unlimited integrations need a higher paid plan.
- Only the account administrator can open Account Settings and the Subscription menu.

## Questions people ask

### Is TimeCamp free?

Yes. TimeCamp has a free forever plan with unlimited users, unlimited projects, and timesheets. Paid plans start at $3.99 per user per month billed annually.

### Does TimeCamp need a credit card?

No for the free plan and the trial. The FAQ says the 14-day trial of the premium features needs no credit card.

### Can TimeCamp track computer activity?

Yes. The desktop app records websites and documents you use, and it is free to download from the web app.
