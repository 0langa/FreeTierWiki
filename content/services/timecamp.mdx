You use TimeCamp in the web app at app.timecamp.com, in a free desktop app, or in the mobile app. The desktop app can record computer activity. It suits teams and freelancers who need hours tracked by project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is a free forever plan. It has unlimited users and unlimited projects. It includes timesheets, a browser plugin, web, desktop, and mobile apps, and two-factor authentication. The current pricing cards put one native integration on Premium; some official integration pages still promise one on Free. Confirm the current entitlement in your account.

The current official product index also lists AI time tracking on Free. Paid plans start with Starter at $3.99 per user per month billed annually, or $5.49 month to month. Starter adds unlimited tasks, time-off, and overtime tracking. Higher plans add screenshots, unlimited integrations, and more.

## When you hit a limit

Basic time tracking has no stated user or project quota. The main constraints are available features. You run into missing features instead, such as time-off or unlimited integrations.

One cost needs care. The pricing page says the AI Time Tracker is billed by token usage, with an estimated cost of about $6 per user per month, and that the free trial includes $10 in credits. It does not explain how this applies on the Free plan, so check the account settings before you turn the AI tracker on.

## Gotchas

- The AI Time Tracker is listed as billed by token usage on every plan, including Free.
- Official pages disagree about one native integration on Free. The browser timer plugin is separate from native project synchronization.
- Only the account administrator can open Account Settings and the Subscription menu.

## Questions people ask

### Is TimeCamp free?

Yes. TimeCamp has a free forever plan with unlimited users, unlimited projects, and timesheets. Paid plans start at $3.99 per user per month billed annually.

### Does TimeCamp need a credit card?

TimeCamp’s official product index explicitly says its Free plan requires no card. The premium-feature trial is also advertised without a card.

### Can TimeCamp track computer activity?

Yes. The desktop app records websites and documents you use, and it is free to download from the web app.
