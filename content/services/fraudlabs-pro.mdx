You can use a ready plugin for a shop platform such as Shopify, WooCommerce, or Magento, or call the REST API with an official SDK. A mobile app lets you review orders. It suits small online stores that want a fraud check on each order.

## What the free plan gives you

The Micro plan is free with no commitment. It covers 500 queries per month for merchants with sales under 25K USD per month. You get 5 validation rules, 50 custom blacklists, and no grouping rules or sub accounts.

The basic validations cover IP geolocation, billing, shipping, user, proxy, email address, payment, blacklist, and velocity checks. Basic features include a fraud score, email notification, and the FraudLabs Pro app for Android and iOS. The plan also includes 10 free SMS verification credits and standard support.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 500 queries in a month. It also does not say what happens if your sales pass the 25K USD cap. The paid plans all list Auto-Recharge, but the Micro plan lists it too without describing it.

The paid Mini plan has 1,500 queries per month and priority support. The Small plan has 5,000 queries per month. A 14 day free trial is offered on the bigger plans.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is for merchants with sales under 25K USD per month. Larger merchants need a paid plan.
- The Micro plan has no grouping rules and no sub accounts, and it only has standard support.
- Features such as disposable email checks, IP usage type, and phone number validity start on the Small plan.

Yes. The Micro plan is free with 500 queries per month, for merchants under 25K USD per month in sales. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I use more than 500 FraudLabs Pro queries?

The pricing page does not say. It lists a paid Mini plan with 1,500 queries per month, and Auto-Recharge on each plan.

### Which fraud checks does the free FraudLabs Pro plan include?

It includes basic validations of IP geolocation, billing, shipping, user, proxy, email address, payment, blacklist, and velocity, with a fraud score and email notification.
