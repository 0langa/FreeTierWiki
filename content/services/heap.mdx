Heap is a product analytics tool that captures user interactions automatically, so you can build charts, funnels, and retention reports without tagging every event first. It is now part of Contentsquare, and it suits developers and product people who want answers about user behavior early.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan covers up to 10k monthly sessions. It includes core analytics charts, unlimited enrichment sources, guides integrations, SSO, and 6 months of data history. Heap's comparison table lists features such as charts, funnels, journeys, retention, autocapture, and APIs across its plans.

A session is a period of activity from a single user. On web, a session ends after 30 minutes of pageview inactivity; on mobile, it ends after 5 minutes of inactivity. The Growth plan adds Contentsquare's AI assistant, unlimited users and reports, chart customization, CSV exports, 12 months of data history, and email support.

## When you hit a limit

Heap says it will not drop or throttle your data when you go over. Once you exceed your plan's monthly limit, you get an email asking you to upgrade to the right tier, and you need to upgrade before you can view your data again.

So collection keeps running, but the reports are locked until you move to a paid plan. Growth pricing is estimated after you sign up and install the snippet, and Pro and above use custom session pricing.

## Gotchas

- Going over the session limit locks access to your reports until you upgrade.
- Session Replay is sold as an add-on on paid plans, not part of the Free plan.
- Mobile sessions end after 5 minutes of inactivity, so an app can use sessions faster than a website.

## Questions people ask

### Is Heap free?

Yes, up to 10k monthly sessions on the Free plan, with 6 months of data history.

### What happens if I go over 10k sessions?

Heap keeps collecting your data but emails you to upgrade, and you must upgrade before you can view your data again.

### Does Heap's free plan include session replay?

No. Session Replay is listed as an add-on on paid plans.
