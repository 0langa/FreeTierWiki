You create databases in the Upstash Console and connect with `redis-cli`, any Redis client, or the HTTP-based `@upstash/redis` SDK that works in serverless and edge runtimes. It suits developers who want Redis without managing a server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is meant for prototypes and hobby projects. It includes 250MB of data (the interactive table still says 256MB), 10 GB of monthly bandwidth, and 500K commands per month. The plan table shows 1 database on Free, with up to 10,000 commands per second. The maximum request size is 10 MB and the maximum record size is 100 MB.

Free includes community and email support. Pay as you go raises the data size to 100 GB, and Fixed plans start at $10 a month. Some operational commands such as AUTH, PING, and INFO are not charged.

## When you hit a limit

Commands beyond Free's 500K monthly quota return exceptions. Commands-per-second excess temporarily throttles, often waiting for capacity; oversized requests are rejected. Pay-as-you-go excess can trigger email/rate limiting.

Entering a card upgrades to pay-as-you-go at $0.2/100K commands with higher limits. Its optional monthly budget sends alerts at 70%/90%, then rate-limits the database and caps billing when exceeded. Mid-month Fixed-to-pay-as-you-go moves count only pay-as-you-go spending toward that budget. Treat monthly quotas, throughput and payload size as separate limits: queueing a burst cannot fix an exhausted monthly allowance.

## Gotchas

- Free has 1 database. Machine-readable pricing scopes the first 10 database base-fee exemption to pay-as-you-go; commands and storage still incur usage charges.
- Global databases count replication as commands. A write to a database with one primary and one read region costs two commands.
- A regular free database is archived after at least 30 days of inactivity; Upstash keeps a backup you can restore into a new database.

## Questions people ask

### Is Upstash Redis free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 250MB of data (the interactive table still says 256MB), 500K commands a month, and 10 GB of bandwidth. Upgrading to pay as you go needs a credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Upstash Redis free tier?

Commands above 500K per month return an exception on Free. On pay as you go, Upstash emails you and may rate limit the database; exceeding a configured monthly budget also rate limits it.

### Does Upstash Redis work without signing up?

The current pricing page no longer advertises the previously listed no-signup instant database. Use the documented account-based Free setup; continued availability of that older endpoint is not confirmed.
