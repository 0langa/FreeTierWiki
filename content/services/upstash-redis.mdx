You create databases in the Upstash Console and connect with `redis-cli`, any Redis client, or the HTTP-based `@upstash/redis` SDK that works in serverless and edge runtimes. It suits developers who want Redis without managing a server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and is meant for prototypes and hobby projects. It includes 250MB of data (the interactive table still says 256MB), 10 GB of monthly bandwidth, and 500K commands per month. The plan table shows 1 database on Free, with up to 10,000 commands per second. The maximum request size is 10 MB and the maximum record size is 100 MB.

Support comes from the community. Pay as you go raises the data size to 100 GB, and Fixed plans start at $10 a month. Some operational commands such as AUTH, PING, and INFO are not charged.

## When you hit a limit

Current pricing does not spell out the response above 500K monthly Free commands. A help FAQ says excess requests return exceptions but still names the old 10K/day quota replaced in 2025. It does describe other cases. On pay as you go, you get an email, and Upstash may rate limit your database. Requests above the maximum request size are rejected with an exception.

To go past the free limits you enter a credit card, which upgrades the database to pay as you go and updates the limits. That plan costs $0.2 per 100K commands and lets you set a monthly budget cap.

## Gotchas

- Free has 1 database. Machine-readable pricing scopes the first 10 database base-fee exemption to pay-as-you-go; commands and storage still incur usage charges.
- Global databases count replication as commands. A write to a database with one primary and one read region costs two commands.
- A free instant database, made through an API call with no signup, is deleted after 3 days unless you claim it in your account. A regular free database is archived after at least 30 days of inactivity; Upstash keeps a backup you can restore into a new database.

## Questions people ask

### Is Upstash Redis free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 250MB of data (the interactive table still says 256MB), 500K commands a month, and 10 GB of bandwidth. Upgrading to pay as you go needs a credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Upstash Redis free tier?

The pricing page does not say for the Free plan. On pay as you go, Upstash emails you and may rate limit the database, and you can set a monthly budget.

### Does Upstash Redis work without signing up?

Yes, in one case. The pricing page says a POST request to its start-redis endpoint creates a free database with no signup, and Upstash deletes it after 3 days unless you claim it.
