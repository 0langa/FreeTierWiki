You call a REST API, the official Node SDK, or SMTP to send, and you build campaigns and workflows in the dashboard. The software is open source under AGPL-3.0, and you can also self-host it.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan sends up to 1,000 emails per month at no cost, with unlimited contacts. It includes transactional emails and click and open tracking. The docs also list campaigns and workflow automation as features on every plan.

Free projects carry a Plunk-branded footer in marketing emails. Inbound emails count toward the 1,000-email allowance. An email with attachments costs 2 credits, no matter how large the files are. A transactional email can carry up to 10 attachments and 10MB in total by default.

## When you hit a limit

The docs say that when you exceed your plan allowance, outbound API calls return a 402 Payment Required error with the code `BILLING_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`. Your app should queue the work or show an error. Inbound emails are dropped silently until the limit resets, and they are not queued or replayed.

Limits reset at the start of each billing period, and you get an email or in-app notice when you near or hit a cap. The paid plan costs $0.001 per email and lets you set a monthly spend cap.

## Gotchas

- The free plan puts Plunk branding on emails. Removing it needs the pay-as-you-go plan.
- Activating a paid subscription places two charges of 1.00 on your card, which Plunk credits back to your balance in full.
- Inbound emails over the allowance are dropped without a retry, so do not rely on the free plan for important inbound mail.

## Questions people ask

### Is Plunk free?

Yes. The free plan sends up to 1,000 emails per month with unlimited contacts, and it needs no credit card. Free emails carry Plunk branding.

### What happens when I go over the Plunk free limit?

Outbound API calls return a 402 error with the code `BILLING_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` until the limit resets. Inbound emails are dropped silently.

### Can I self-host Plunk?

Yes. Plunk is open source, and the docs have a self-hosting section. Self-hosted instances run on your own infrastructure and are not billed by Plunk.
