Back4App is a backend as a service built on open source technologies, with Parse Server at its core. You get a database with relational data models, REST and GraphQL APIs, Cloud Code functions and jobs, and file storage, managed for you. It suits developers who want a hosted backend for a mobile or web app without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

Back4App's free plan is meant for developing, learning, and prototyping, and needs no credit card. Each month it includes 25K requests, 250 MB of database storage, 1 GB of data transfer, and 1 GB of file storage, with a 20 MB maximum upload size.

You get 1 Cloud Code job, the REST API, and the GraphQL API with a custom schema. Access logs are kept for 1 day, and the free plan runs on shared infrastructure in the USA. You can have up to five projects on the free tier. The MVP plan costs $15 per app per month billed annually and adds daily backups and 500 K requests.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a free app passes 25K requests, 250 MB of data, or 1 GB of transfer in a month. The overage prices it lists, such as $5 per 100 K extra requests, are for the MVP and Pay As You Go plans. No credit card is needed for the free plan, so there is no card to charge.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has no backups, so keep your own exports of anything you cannot lose.
- Back4App charges per app, and each paid plan covers 1 app.
- Free apps run only in the USA; other regions start on the MVP plan.
