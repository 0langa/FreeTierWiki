You create and manage indexes with the Wrangler CLI and call them from a Worker through a binding. It suits developers who add semantic search or RAG to a Cloudflare app.

## What the free plan gives you

Vectorize is included in the Workers Free plan. You get 30 million queried vector dimensions per month and 5 million stored vector dimensions. A dimension count is vectors times their size. For example, 10,000 vectors of 768 dimensions use 7.68 million stored dimensions, which is over the stored allowance.

Empty indexes do not count as stored dimensions. A free account can have 100 indexes, and each index can hold up to 1000 namespaces. Each vector can have up to 1536 dimensions. A single index can hold up to 20,000,000 vectors, though the stored dimension allowance on the Free plan is reached much sooner.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use up the Free plan allowance. It says only that the Free plan will always let you prototype with Vectorize for free. It shows billing rules for the Workers Paid plan, which includes more and charges per million dimensions beyond that.

Watch your totals: each query adds the queried vectors plus the stored vectors, multiplied by the dimensions. A small index with a small embedding model fits the free plan best.

## Gotchas

- Dimensions count vectors multiplied by their size, so large embedding models use the allowance quickly.
- Each query counts the queried vectors plus the stored vectors, times the dimensions.
- Vectorize V2 needs Wrangler 3.71.0 or later, and older V1 indexes are deprecated.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Vectorize free?

Yes, on the Workers Free plan. It includes 30 million queried vector dimensions per month and 5 million stored vector dimensions.

### How do vector dimensions count against the Vectorize free tier?

Stored dimensions equal your vectors times their size. Queried dimensions equal the queried plus stored vectors times their size, so 10,000 vectors of 768 dimensions use 7.68 million stored dimensions.

### Will Cloudflare Vectorize always have a free tier?

Cloudflare says the Workers free tier will always include the ability to prototype and experiment with Vectorize for free.
