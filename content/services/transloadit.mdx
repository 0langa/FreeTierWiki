Transloadit builds a pipeline from operations the provider calls Robots. A workflow can receive an upload, process it in steps, and pass the outputs to the destination you configure. Community is useful for checking the mechanics of that pipeline before the resulting files go into use.

## What the free plan gives you

The Community plan supplies 5 GB of processing per month. The provider calls it a trial in its pricing FAQ because the results have restrictions, and it also says the allowance refreshes every month and remains free. You can try the available Robots. Image outputs carry a watermark, and audio and video results are trimmed. A prototype can test that an upload is accepted and that the intended steps run. Include a sample from the actual files you expect to process so you can inspect whether the output has the proportions or characteristics your application will need.

## When you hit a limit

Community stops processing when the 5 GB allowance runs out. There is no Community overage charge. Wait for the monthly reset or choose a paid plan if the workflow needs to keep running. Paid plans remove the output restrictions and can include billable overage. Check those rates and account spending settings separately before switching the integration to a paid account.

## Gotchas

- A free result helps test a workflow. Share it with the watermark or trimmed duration in mind.
- The processing allowance has a monthly reset. Repeatedly processing the same test files still consumes capacity.
- Review pricing for the whole workflow before relying on a paid plan for ongoing traffic.

## Questions people ask

### Is the plan a fixed-duration trial?

The Community allowance renews each month and remains free. The trial restrictions apply to the outputs.

### Can I use the free output for a full-length transcode?

The provider trims audio and video on Community. Use an eligible paid plan for full-length output.

### What happens after 5 GB?

Processing stops until more allowance is available. Community has no paid overage.
