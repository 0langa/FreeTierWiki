You install a host agent or SDK, then explore metrics, logs, traces, and alerts in the Middleware dashboard. It suits small teams that want infrastructure and application monitoring together.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Forever plan costs $0 and gives free access to all features with monthly limits. The calculator shows a free tier of 100GB of data volume, 1000 RUM sessions, 20000 synthetic checks, and 10 browser test runs. You also get 2 million OpsAI tokens, unlimited users, and community based support.

Data is kept for 14 days on the free tier, while pay as you go keeps it for 30. The pricing FAQ says usage is measured daily, per data type and source, and counts data actually stored. Data you drop in the data pipeline is not charged.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the free allowance. It does show the next step: pay as you go costs $0.3 per GB of metrics, logs, and traces. Extra RUM sessions cost $1 per 1K, extra synthetic checks cost $1 per 5K, and extra browser test runs cost $10 per 1K.

Error detection with OpsAI is free. Root cause analysis and automated fixes are charged by tokens used. You can use the ingestion control and data pipeline to drop data you do not need.

## Gotchas

- The pricing FAQ answers "Is Middleware free to use?" with a 14-day trial with unlimited data ingestion, while the plan card says Free Forever. Check your plan after sign-up.
- Free retention is 14 days, against 30 days on pay as you go.
- Data volume is measured on stored data each day, so ten GB a day adds up to 300GB in a 30 day month.

Yes. The Free Forever plan costs $0 with 100GB of data volume and unlimited users. The pricing FAQ also mentions a 14-day trial with unlimited ingestion.

### How long does Middleware keep my data?

The pricing FAQ says the free tier keeps data for 14 days and pay as you go keeps it for 30.
