You sign up at the Invantive Cloud site, pick the platforms you want, and read the data from Excel, Word, or a reporting tool. An English version is available at cloud.invantive.com/en. The service suits teams that report from business platforms.

## What the free plan gives you

Free has no user limit, entry fee or setup fee. Its explicit connector list includes Autotask, Brevo and Calendly, alongside Excel/Word add-ins, Data Hub and Data Replicator. Availability among all 115 paid-plan platforms does not establish Free access.

The rendered Office for Entrepreneurs calculator defaults to €59/month, up to 25 companies and 2 interactive users, with Exact Online Premium/Twinfield unchecked. Platform selections and company count affect the quote; the optional label does not mean inclusion in the default price. Office Premium advertises €119, 10 interactive users and higher fair-use allowances. A separate paid-plan trial lasts 180 days.

## When you hit a limit

The site states no volume cap for the free plan. It says special terms, acceptance criteria, and fair use rules apply, and it does not give the numbers. Office Premium raises the fair use limits.

The Free plan is advertised for free use with no further obligations. A paid platform needs separate paid-plan terms; the page does not promise automatic free overage handling. The site says that if you do not use a plan, you get no invoice, and you can cancel at any time. The pages we could read do not say whether a payment card is needed to sign up.

## Gotchas

- Service is limited to selected countries, including the EU, UK, US, Canada, Vietnam, and Taiwan.
- Office for Entrepreneurs allows up to 2 interactive users. Office Premium raises this to 10.
- Exact Online Premium is absent from the Free list and unchecked in the default paid calculator. Confirm the configured quote before buying.

## Questions people ask

### Is Invantive Cloud free?

Yes, there is a free plan with no user limit. It includes the free connectors; the paid plans let you pick any of 115 platforms.

### Does Invantive Cloud have a free trial?

Yes. The site offers a free 180-day trial of a paid plan.

### How much does Invantive Cloud cost?

The default Office for Entrepreneurs calculator shows €59 a month for up to 25 companies with Exact Online Premium and Twinfield unchecked; Office Premium is advertised at €119. The site says you pay nothing for a month you do not use the service.
