You sign up at the Invantive Cloud site, pick the platforms you want, and read the data from Excel, Word, or a reporting tool. An English version is available at cloud.invantive.com/en. The service suits teams that report from business platforms.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan has no user limit, no entry fee, and no setup fee. It includes connectors for free platforms such as Autotask, Brevo, and Calendly, and the full list is on the site. It also includes the Excel and Word add-in, Data Hub, and Data Replicator.

On the paid plan the site lets you pick any combination of the 105 available platforms. Use the explicit Free connector list to check your platform; availability across the broader catalog does not imply free access. Office for Entrepreneurs costs €59 a month for at most 2 interactive users. Office Premium costs €119 with 10 interactive users and higher fair-use limits. Exact Online Premium appears in both paid descriptions, so confirm its entitlement before buying.

The site also offers a free 180-day trial of the paid plan.

## When you hit a limit

The site states no volume cap for the free plan. It says special terms, acceptance criteria, and fair use rules apply, and it does not give the numbers. Office Premium raises the fair use limits.

The Free plan is advertised for free use with no further obligations. A paid platform needs separate paid-plan terms; the page does not promise automatic free overage handling. The site says that if you do not use a plan, you get no invoice, and you can cancel at any time. The pages we could read do not say whether a payment card is needed to sign up.

## Gotchas

- Service is limited to selected countries, including the EU, UK, US, Canada, Vietnam, and Taiwan.
- The paid plan allows at most 2 interactive users for the interactive Invantive products.
- The page’s paid Exact Online Premium descriptions conflict; the explicit Free connector list does not include it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Invantive Cloud free?

Yes, there is a free plan with no user limit. It includes the free connectors; the paid plans let you pick any of 105 platforms.

### Does Invantive Cloud have a free trial?

Yes. The site offers a free 180-day trial of a paid plan.

### How much does Invantive Cloud cost?

The Office for Entrepreneurs plan costs €59 a month, and Office Premium costs €119. The site says you pay nothing for a month you do not use the service.
