You sign up at the Invantive Cloud site, pick the platforms you want, and read the data from Excel, Word, or a reporting tool. The pages we could read are in German. The service suits teams that report from business platforms.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan has no user limit, no entry fee, and no setup fee. It includes connectors for free platforms such as Autotask, Brevo, and Calendly, and the full list is on the site. It also includes the Excel and Word add-in.

The site lets you pick any combination of the 105 available platforms, but premium connectors need a paid plan. The paid Office for Entrepreneurs plan costs €59 a month, and the site says you pay as little as €2.36 per company and platform. It allows at most 2 interactive users. Office Premium costs €119 and adds Exact Online Premium, Data Hub, and Data Replicator.

The site also offers a free 180-day trial of the paid plan.

## When you hit a limit

The site states no volume cap for the free plan. It says special terms, acceptance criteria, and fair use rules apply, and it does not give the numbers. Office Premium raises the fair use limits.

The free plan does not charge you for use. If you add a paid platform, billing follows the paid plan. The site says that if you do not use a plan, you get no invoice, and you can cancel at any time. The pages we could read do not say whether a payment card is needed to sign up.

## Gotchas

- The sign-up and pricing pages are in German, and the plan names differ from the English ones used elsewhere.
- The paid plan allows at most 2 interactive users for the interactive Invantive products.
- Premium connectors such as Exact Online, Salesforce, and Twinfield are not part of the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Invantive Cloud free?

Yes, there is a free plan with no user limit. It covers only the free connectors, and premium connectors need a paid plan.

### Does Invantive Cloud have a free trial?

Yes. The site offers a free 180-day trial of a paid plan.

### How much does Invantive Cloud cost?

The Office for Entrepreneurs plan costs €59 a month, and Office Premium costs €119. The site says you pay nothing for a month you do not use the service.
