You call a plain HTTP API with an API key from the Sheetson dashboard. Each tab of your Google Sheet becomes a table, and the header row becomes the JSON keys. It suits makers who already keep data in a sheet.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever and needs no credit card. You can connect unlimited spreadsheets, and each sheet can hold up to 1,000 rows. Sheetson does not meter requests on any plan. The Free plan includes pagination queries and email support.

The rate limit is shared. Sheetson says the server is shared across all Free users within its available resources. The Professional plan costs $99 a year and adds unlimited rows, filtering with pagination, priority support, and a dedicated quota of 60 requests per minute.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a sheet passes 1,000 rows. If you need more rows, upgrade or split the data across sheets.

Requests are not counted, but Google Sheets API quotas still apply, and these apply on every plan. Because the Free rate limit is shared, your calls can be slower when other Free users are busy. Search with the `where` parameter is only available in paid plans.

## Gotchas

- Share the sheet with `google@sheetson.com` as an editor. You do not need to make the sheet public.
- The first row must be a header row. The tab name is case sensitive, so use the exact name in your API path.
- Every value comes back as a string, except `rowIndex`, which Sheetson adds to each row.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sheetson free?

Yes. The Free plan is forever and needs no credit card. It allows unlimited spreadsheets and unmetered requests, with up to 1,000 rows per sheet.

### Does Sheetson store my data?

No. Sheetson proxies between the Google Sheets API and your apps, so your data stays in your Google Sheet.

### What does the Sheetson Professional plan add?

It costs $99 a year and gives unlimited rows per sheet, filtering and pagination, priority support, and a dedicated 60 requests per minute quota.
