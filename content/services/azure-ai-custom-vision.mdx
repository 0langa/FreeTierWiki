You work in the Custom Vision web portal, a client SDK, or the REST API. It suits developers who want a small custom image model, but the service is planned for retirement.

## What the free plan gives you

The F0 tier lets you create 2 projects. Each project can hold 5,000 training images and 50 tags, with up to 20 iterations. You get 1 hour of training per month and 10,000 predictions per month.

Rate limits are low. Prediction calls and training calls run at 2 per second on F0, and other API calls run at 10 per second. Prediction images are stored for 30 days. Training images can be up to 6 MB, and prediction images up to 4 MB. The service accepts jpg, png, bmp, and gif files.

The paid S0 tier allows 100 projects, 100,000 training images per project, and unlimited predictions.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use up the monthly predictions or the training hour. The limits page only lists the F0 numbers next to the S0 numbers. S0 is billed per 1,000 transactions, and the price on the page is blank.

You move to S0 yourself in the Azure portal. Nothing on the pages says that F0 changes tier on its own. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- Microsoft plans to retire Custom Vision. It promises full support for existing customers until 9/25/2028, and it points new work to Azure Machine Learning AutoML or Foundry models.
- You must create a training resource and a prediction resource in Azure. Both need an Azure subscription.
- Use at least 30 images per tag for a first model, and more is better. The overview suggests 50 per tag as a start.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Custom Vision free?

Yes, the F0 tier is free. It gives you 2 projects, 5,000 training images per project, 1 hour of training per month, and 10,000 predictions per month.

### Is Azure AI Custom Vision being retired?

Microsoft has announced a planned retirement. Existing customers keep full support until 9/25/2028, so a new project still works for now.

### What happens when I go over the Custom Vision free limits?

The pages do not say. The S0 tier has higher limits and is billed per 1,000 transactions.
