You manage indices and relevance in the Algolia dashboard and send records through the API or a client library such as `algoliasearch`. UI libraries like React InstantSearch draw the search box and results. It suits developers who want to add search to a site or app without running a search server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs nothing and the pricing page says no credit card is required. Each month it includes 10,000 search requests, 5,000 recommendation requests, and 5,000 crawls. It also allows 50,000 records at a time. A record is one item that someone can search for, such as a product or a page. The count covers all your indices together, and each pre-sorted copy of your records adds to the total.

The Free plan covers search and recommendations. Paid plans add things like analytics retention, SSO, and a stronger SLA. The page lists three data center regions for the paid plans: US, UK, and EU.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what Algolia does when a Free plan hits its monthly limits. It shows a message that your selected usage exceeds the limits of the free plan and points you to a monthly plan instead. Because the Free plan needs no card, Algolia has no payment method to bill.

On paid plans, going over the included records is billed as an over-quota charge based on the highest record count you reached. Search requests above the included amount are billed per extra 1,000 requests. Plan for an upgrade before a launch with real traffic.

## Gotchas

- Records from every index count together, and each pre-sort of your records creates a copy that raises the total.
- The pricing page does not describe an SLA for the Free plan; the SLA and SSO items are listed under higher plans.
- The quickstart needs Node.js 20.19 or later, and it uses three keys from the dashboard: Application ID, Write API key, and Search API key.

## Questions people ask

### Is Algolia free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 10,000 search requests, 5,000 recommendation requests, and 5,000 crawls each month, plus 50,000 records. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I go over the Algolia free tier?

The pricing page does not say. It tells you to choose a monthly plan when your usage exceeds the Free plan limits.

### Does Algolia need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the Free plan.
