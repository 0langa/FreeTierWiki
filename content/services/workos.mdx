You add WorkOS to your app with an SDK, send users to the hosted AuthKit login, and manage settings in the WorkOS Dashboard. It suits B2B teams that want login now and enterprise features later.

## What the free plan gives you

WorkOS User Management is free for up to 1 million monthly active users. A user counts as active if they sign up, sign in, or update a profile within the calendar month. WorkOS is free to get started, and you do not need to add a credit card until you are ready to move to production.

All WorkOS products are available in the staging environment at no cost, so you can test SSO and Directory Sync before you pay. The Radar bot and fraud product is also free at first. Each enterprise customer you support with SSO or Directory Sync counts as one connection, and connections are billed per month, with the price per connection dropping as you add more.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a hard stop. After the first 1 million users, each extra million users costs $2,500 per month. Contact WorkOS for volume pricing.

The costs you are more likely to meet are connections. Enterprise SSO starts at $125 per connection per month, and the page lists lower rates from $100 down to $65 as the count grows. Directory Sync uses the same per-connection pricing. Every connection is billed the same, no matter which identity provider or how many end users it has.

## Gotchas

- The free tier covers User Management only. SSO and Directory Sync connections are billed per connection.
- Staging is for testing and production is for live apps. The pricing page says you need a credit card only once you are ready to move to production.
- Radar has a free amount, then costs $100 per month for each 50k checks.

## Questions people ask

### Is WorkOS free?

Partly. User Management is free for up to 1 million monthly active users. Enterprise SSO and Directory Sync are billed per connection.

### Does WorkOS need a credit card?

Not to start. The pricing page says you do not need to add a credit card until you are ready to move to production.

### How much does WorkOS SSO cost?

Enterprise SSO starts at $125 per connection per month. The price per connection drops as you add more connections.
