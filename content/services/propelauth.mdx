You configure PropelAuth in its dashboard, then drop in a frontend library such as the React one and a backend library for your framework. It suits small B2B teams that want login, organizations, and roles without building them.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes 10,000 monthly active users. It has unlimited organizations and unlimited waitlist users. You also get a custom domain, 2FA and RBAC, and customizable login screens, including a split-screen layout.

PropelAuth counts only users who log in during the month. Waitlist users do not count toward the total until they become regular users. Transactional emails are limited to 10,000. The Growth plan costs $150 per month. It adds user impersonation, unlimited SAML and enterprise SSO connections, a separate staging environment, API keys for M2M, and MCP authentication. Growth Plus costs $500 per month and adds SCIM and directory sync.

## When you hit a limit

PropelAuth does not lock you out. The pricing FAQ says that if you pass 10,000 active users on the Free plan, it emails you to ask you to upgrade, and it does not prevent your users from logging in.

On paid plans, extra monthly active users cost $0.05 per user. The comparison table shows that extra users are not available on Free. The free plan also caps transactional emails at 10,000, and the pages do not say what happens beyond that.

## Gotchas

- SAML and enterprise SSO connections, user impersonation, and a separate staging environment need the paid Growth plan.
- Waitlist users cannot log in until you invite them to the product.
- SMS-based 2FA is not on the Free plan. The comparison table shows only TOTP 2FA there.

## Questions people ask

### Is PropelAuth free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes 10,000 monthly active users, unlimited organizations, and a custom domain.

### What happens if I go over 10,000 users on PropelAuth Free?

PropelAuth emails you to ask you to upgrade. It says it will not prevent your users from logging in.

### Does PropelAuth Free include SAML SSO?

No. Unlimited SAML and enterprise SSO connections are part of the Growth plan, which costs $150 per month.
