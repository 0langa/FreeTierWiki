You use Userforge in the browser. A signed-out builder makes your first personas, and a free workspace keeps them and reviews your pages. It suits product managers, marketers, and agencies.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 forever. It includes 3 personas with portraits and scenes, 3 pages, and 5 persona reviews a month. You also get 10 context sources to ground the personas, and you can bring a collaborator. The site says no credit card is needed to start.

A persona review means your personas read a page you wrote and leave margin notes in their own voice. Every persona has a live link that opens without an account and prints to paper or PDF. Pro costs $59 per month, or $479 billed yearly, and seats five people per workspace.

## When you hit a limit

Free has fixed caps: 3 personas, 3 pages, 5 reviews a month, and 10 context sources. Pro removes the caps on personas, pages, and reviews, and raises the strength of the AI on each draft. The pricing section does not say what happens when you reach a cap. The review limit resets monthly, according to the "a month" wording.

You upgrade in the app, and the site says you can start free and go Pro in-app. Nothing bills you unless you choose Pro, and you can cancel any time.

## Gotchas

- The product is built around AI persona reviews, and free accounts get only 5 of them a month.
- The signed-out builder saves your work in the browser until you create an account.
- Pro seats five people per workspace. Clients or executives can follow a read-only link without an account.

## Questions people ask

### Is Userforge free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 forever with 3 personas, 3 pages, and 5 persona reviews a month. No credit card is required.

### Do I need an account to use the Userforge persona creator?

No. The builder runs signed out and saves your work in the browser. A free account keeps the personas in a workspace.

### How much does Userforge Pro cost?

Pro costs $59 per month, or $479 billed yearly. It removes the caps on personas, pages, and reviews.
