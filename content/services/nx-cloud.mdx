You connect an existing Nx workspace with one command or from the Nx Cloud web app. After that, your normal `nx` commands use the shared cache. It suits small teams and open source projects that already use Nx.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free forever and needs no credit card. It includes 50,000 credits per month, 5 contributors, and 10 concurrent CI connections, with community support. Credits are the usage currency, and each operation costs a different amount depending on the resource class.

The remote cache shares task results across developer machines and CI jobs. A cache hit restores the terminal output and files without running the task again. Nx itself is free and open source. Task running, the project graph, local caching, and `nx affected` all work without an Nx Cloud account.

## When you hit a limit

On the Hobby plan, Nx Cloud features turn off once the monthly credits are gone. They turn back on at the next reset. Unused credits do not carry over. Your own local builds keep working, because local caching does not need Nx Cloud. Nothing is billed on Hobby. The Team plan has no such cap. It costs $29 per month, and usage beyond its included $29 of credit costs $5.50 per 10,000 credits.

## Gotchas

- Credits, not build minutes, set the limit. Check the credit pricing page to see what each operation costs.
- The Hobby plan covers 5 contributors. On the Team plan, extra contributors cost $19 each.
- Qualifying open source organizations get Nx Cloud free, but they have to talk to the Nx team to set it up.

## Questions people ask

### Is Nx Cloud free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free forever with 50,000 credits per month, 5 contributors, and 10 concurrent CI connections. No credit card is needed.

### What happens when I use up my Nx Cloud credits?

On Hobby, Nx Cloud features turn off until the next monthly reset. Unused credits do not carry over.

### Do I need Nx Cloud to use Nx?

No. Nx is free and open source, and local caching works without an account.
