You work in a project canvas in the dashboard, or from the Railway CLI. Each project holds services and databases, and Railway builds and deploys them for you. It suits makers who want a quick deploy with little server setup.

## What the free plan gives you

New accounts start with a free trial. The trial gives a one-time grant of $5 for up to 30 days. After 30 days, or after the $5 is spent, the account reverts to the Free plan. The Free plan costs $0 and gives $1 of credit per month. That credit does not roll over.

The pricing page lists these Free limits: 1 project, 3 services per project, up to 1 vCPU and 0.5 GB of RAM per service, and 0.5 GB of volume storage. It also lists a 10-minute build timeout and 1 concurrent build. Support is through the community, and no credit card is required to deploy a new project.

## When you hit a limit

The docs do not say what Railway does when the Free plan's $1 of credit runs out. The pricing page only says the Hobby plan charges for extra resource usage once its credits are used. Custom domains are available during the trial but not on the recurring Free plan; generated Railway domains remain available.

You can move to the Hobby plan for $5 per month, which includes $5 of usage credits. Railway deletes volumes on Free or Trial accounts 30 days after expiry, so upgrade first if the data matters.

## Gotchas

- The Hobby plan always charges its $5 fee, even if you use less than $5 of resources.
- Trial accounts that Railway cannot verify through GitHub get restricted outbound network access and a limited set of ports. Railway does not answer requests for manual verification.
- During the trial, projects are limited to 5 services each. After it, the Free plan allows 3 services per project.

## Questions people ask

### Is Railway free?

Partly. New accounts get a one-time $5 trial credit for 30 days. After that, the Free plan gives $1 of credit per month with no card required.

### What happens when my Railway free credit runs out?

The docs do not say. The Hobby plan, at $5 per month with $5 of usage included, is the next step up.

### Does Railway need a credit card?

No, not for the trial or the Free plan. The pricing page says no credit card is required to deploy a new project.
