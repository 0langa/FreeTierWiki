You work in a project canvas in the dashboard, or from the Railway CLI. Each project holds services and databases, and Railway builds and deploys them for you. It suits makers who want a quick deploy with little server setup.

## What the free plan gives you

New accounts start with a free trial. The trial gives a one-time grant of $5 for up to 30 days. After 30 days, or after the $5 is spent, running deployments stop. Switch to Free from the Plans page and redeploy within its limits, or upgrade to a paid plan. The Free plan costs $0 and gives $1 of credit per month. That credit does not roll over.

The pricing page lists these Free limits: 1 project, 3 services per project, up to 1 vCPU and 0.5 GB of RAM per service, one 0.5 GB volume, and one 10 GB bucket. Bucket storage draws from the same $1 credit. It also lists a 10-minute build timeout and 1 concurrent build. Support is through the community, and no credit card is required to deploy a new project.

## When you hit a limit

At $1 exhaustion, apps and bucket access pause until reset/upgrade; bucket files remain. Free services/databases sleep after 5–10 minutes without outbound traffic, waking on requests/connections. WebSockets/polling can prevent sleep. Trial-only custom domains, cron and region selection disappear on recurring Free; Railway domains remain.

Hobby costs $5/month including $5 usage. Free-plan guidance retains recurring-plan volumes/buckets, but its trial section deletes volumes 30 days after expiry. The plans FAQ broadly applies that deletion to Free or Trial volumes. Free-volume retention therefore remains unresolved: keep backups rather than assuming indefinite retention.

## Gotchas

- The Hobby plan normally charges $5 even below $5 of usage. Railway waives that fee for a small set of eligible builders.
- Trial accounts that Railway cannot verify through GitHub get restricted outbound network access and a limited set of ports. Railway does not answer requests for manual verification.
- During the trial, projects are limited to 5 services each. After it, the Free plan allows 3 services per project.

## Questions people ask

### Is Railway free?

Partly. New accounts get a one-time $5 trial credit for 30 days. After that, the Free plan gives $1 of credit per month with no card required.

### What happens when my Railway free credit runs out?

Running services stop and storage bucket access pauses until the next billing cycle or an upgrade. The Free-plan guide says volumes and bucket files remain, but a separate plans FAQ retains broader expiry-based volume deletion wording.

### Does Railway need a credit card?

No, not for the trial or the Free plan. The pricing page says no credit card is required to deploy a new project.
