You set Zoho Bookings up in the web app and share the booking link it gives you. A mobile app for iOS and Android lets you manage appointments on the go.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is for 1 user. Zoho calls it a forever free plan for single users who need the basics of scheduling software. It includes online meetings, notification emails and reminders, and two-way calendar sync with Zoho, Google, and Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com.

The pricing FAQ adds customizable booking links for staff, services, and workspaces, automatic email notifications, and free email support. Zoho says it does not ask for credit card details for the free plan. The pricing page publishes no booking-volume cap; that is not a promise of unlimited usage.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you outgrow the free plan, and it lists no caps on bookings. The limit it names is the single user.

New accounts get a 15-day premium trial with no card. Zoho says your data and customizations remain after the trial. A forever-free edition is available, but the FAQ does not explain which premium customizations remain usable without a subscription.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is for one user. A second person who takes bookings needs a paid plan.
- The pricing page does not list excluded features for the free plan. Check your account before you plan around SMS reminders or custom workflows.
- Zoho Bookings can take payments through providers such as Stripe and PayPal, according to the product page. The free plan list does not name payments.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Bookings free?

Yes, for one user. The free plan includes online meetings, reminder emails, and calendar sync. Zoho also offers a 15-day premium trial.

### Does Zoho Bookings need a credit card?

No. Zoho says you do not need a card for the free plan, and the 15-day trial also runs without one.

### Can Zoho Bookings sync with Google Calendar?

Yes. The free plan lists two-way calendar sync with Zoho, Google, and Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com calendars. This helps avoid double bookings.
