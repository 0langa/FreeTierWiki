You set Zoho Bookings up in the web app and share the booking link it gives you. A mobile app for iOS and Android lets you manage appointments on the go.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is for 1 user. Zoho calls it a forever free plan for single users who need the basics of scheduling software. It includes online meetings, notification emails and reminders, and two-way calendar sync with Zoho, Google, and Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com.

Zoho advertises booking links and automatic email notifications. It offers cardless signup, and you can stay on the Free plan after the introductory trial. The pricing page publishes no booking-volume cap; that is not a promise of unlimited usage.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you outgrow the free plan, and it lists no caps on bookings. The full comparison specifies one user, one event type, one workspace and one synced calendar, while stored customer records are unlimited.

New accounts get a 15-day premium trial with no card. You can continue on the forever-free edition or move to a paid plan. Check which customizations are available on the Free plan before relying on features you set up during the trial.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is for one user. A second person who takes bookings needs a paid plan.
- The full comparison excludes group/resource bookings, custom email templates, page branding customization, workflows, appointment reports, Zapier and custom domains. Free includes custom booking fields, duration/buffer settings, rescheduling, one-time booking URLs and up to 10 invited guests.
- Payment gateways and SMS-provider integrations require Premium. Free includes API support, Zoho Flow, the Zoho Desk booking plugin, website embedding and meeting integrations with Zoho Meeting, Google Meet, Teams or Zoom; those providers have their own terms.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Bookings free?

Yes, for one user. The free plan includes online meetings, reminder emails, and calendar sync. Zoho also offers a 15-day premium trial.

### Does Zoho Bookings need a credit card?

No. Zoho says you do not need a card for the free plan, and the 15-day trial also runs without one.

### Can Zoho Bookings sync with Google Calendar?

Yes. The free plan lists two-way calendar sync with Zoho, Google, and Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com calendars. This helps avoid double bookings.
