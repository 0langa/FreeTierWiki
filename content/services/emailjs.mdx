You work in the EmailJS dashboard and call a JavaScript SDK or a REST API from your code. No server is required. It suits people who need a form to send mail without building a backend.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and allows 200 monthly requests. It includes 2 email templates, requests up to 50Kb, and a contact list of up to 500 people. The account has 1 user seat. The email history in the dashboard is kept for 7 days.

You can use the REST API, dynamic content in templates, CC and BCC recipients, and tracking through internal analytics or Google Analytics. Support is community only. Attachments, the domain whitelist, and removing the EmailJS logo footer are not part of the Free plan. The pricing FAQ says no credit card is required.

## When you hit a limit

EmailJS does not charge overage fees. After you reach 100% of the monthly request limit, extra API requests are dropped by the servers. They do not cost you anything.

EmailJS sends email notices when your account reaches 80%, 95%, and 100% of the quota, so you have time to upgrade. The Personal plan raises the limit to 2,000 requests for $9 per month. The SDK send method also has a rate limit of 1 request per second.

## Gotchas

- Requests over the monthly limit are dropped, so a form can stop sending mid-month. Watch the quota emails.
- The Free plan has no domain whitelist, which is a paid feature on Personal and above.
- You can turn off email history in a template's settings if you do not want request data stored.

## Questions people ask

### Is EmailJS free?

Yes. The Free plan allows 200 requests per month and 2 templates. No credit card is required, only your name and email address.

### What happens when I hit the EmailJS monthly limit?

Extra requests are dropped once you reach 100% of the monthly limit. EmailJS does not charge automatic overage fees, and it emails you at 80%, 95%, and 100%.

### Can I send attachments with EmailJS for free?

No. Attachments are not part of the Free plan. The Personal plan allows attachments up to 500kb.
