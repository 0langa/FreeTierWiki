TwicPics optimizes images and videos in real time and serves them from a CDN. You keep your originals where they are, map a TwicPics domain path to that storage, and request each image through a URL that says how to resize, crop, or compress it. Components for frameworks such as React, Vue, Next, and Svelte wrap this in ready-made tags.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and is listed as Free Forever, for hobby and small websites. It includes 3 GB of CDN bandwidth and 1 user, and the pricing page says no credit card is required.

Sources, assets, and transformations are unlimited on every plan, so there is no fee based on the number of images or how many sizes you generate. You can optimize media wherever it is stored. The Free plan has 1 domain and no workspaces, video optimization, or custom domains.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no cost per additional GB on the Free plan, unlike paid plans, and it does not say what happens when Free reaches 3 GB. Expect to upgrade if your traffic grows past it.

The Business plan costs $19 a month for 40 GB of CDN bandwidth, with $0.50 per additional GB, and adds video optimization and custom domains. Original images are limited to 20Mb; an original over a limit makes the API return a 502 error.

## Gotchas

- 3 GB of CDN bandwidth is small for a public site with large photos, so check your traffic first.
- Images are served from a twic.pics subdomain on Free, since custom domains need a paid plan.
- Video optimization is a paid feature, so Free is for images only.

## Questions people ask

### Is TwicPics free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month with 3 GB of CDN bandwidth and needs no credit card.

### Is there a limit on the number of images or transformations?

No. Sources, assets, and transformations are unlimited on all plans, including Free.

### Can I optimize video on the Free plan?

No. Video optimization is listed for Business plans, not Free.
