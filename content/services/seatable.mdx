SeaTable is a no-code database with tables, many column types, views, web forms, an app builder, automations, and JavaScript and Python scripting. It runs as SeaTable Cloud or on your own servers. This entry covers the SeaTable Cloud Free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

SeaTable says the Free subscription lets you use SeaTable Cloud permanently with up to 25 users at no cost; registering starts it. Bases and tables are unlimited. The team gets 10,000 rows in total and 2 GB for files, and snapshots are kept for 1 month.

Each month the Free plan includes 3,000 API calls, 100 Python script runs, 100 automations, and 12.5 AI credits, all fixed amounts. JavaScript script runs are unlimited. The App Builder, web forms, common datasets, and plugins are on every plan. Plus costs 7 euros per user per month billed annually.

## When you hit a limit

SeaTable answers this in its FAQ. When you reach your row or storage limit, SeaTable informs you. You can still open your bases and tables, but you cannot create additional rows or upload files. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time.

The pricing page does not describe a bill for going over on the Free plan; Plus raises the limits to 50,000 rows, 50 GB for files, and 10,000 API calls per user per month.

## Gotchas

- The 10,000 rows are a total per team, not per base, so all bases share them.
- API calls on Free are a fixed 3,000 a month for the whole team, not per user as on paid plans.
- Snapshot retention is only 1 month, so keep your own exports of anything important.

## Questions people ask

### Is SeaTable free?

Yes. SeaTable Cloud has a permanent Free subscription for up to 25 users with 10,000 rows and 2 GB for files.

### What happens when I reach the SeaTable row limit?

SeaTable informs you, and you keep access to your bases, but you cannot create more rows or upload files until you are under the limit or upgrade.

### Does SeaTable have an API on the Free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3,000 API calls per month for the team.
