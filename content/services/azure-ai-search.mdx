You create a search service in the Azure portal, then load documents and query them through the portal, the REST API, or an SDK. It suits developers who want to prototype search, or retrieval for an AI app, before they pay.

## What the free plan gives you

Each Azure subscription can have one free search service. It runs on shared hardware. Storage is limited to 50 MB, and you can create up to 3 indexes. You also get up to 3 indexers, 3 data sources, and 3 skillsets.

Indexers on the Free tier have tight limits. A run can last 3 minutes for blob sources and 1 minute for other sources. An indexer can start only once every 180 seconds. AI enrichment is limited to 20 free transactions per indexer per day. Each indexing run can load up to 10,000 documents.

Free services cannot scale. You cannot add replicas or partitions. The docs call the Free tier a short-term, non-production way to evaluate the product.

## When you hit a limit

The limits are hard walls, not bills. The Free tier has no scale-up, so you stop at 50 MB or 3 indexes. To get more room, create a billable service on the Basic tier or higher. The pricing page shows the paid price fields as blank dashes.

Microsoft can also delete a free service. The docs say it may do so after an extended period of inactivity, to make room for other services. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page says the semantic ranker is not available in the Dedicated Free tier.
- The custom entity lookup skill is not available on the free plan.
- The free service shares infrastructure with other customers, so it is not suitable for production or uptime promises.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Search free?

The Free tier costs nothing. It gives you one service per subscription, 3 indexes, and 50 MB of storage.

### Does an Azure AI Search free service get deleted?

It can be. The docs say Microsoft might delete free services that are inactive for an extended period.

### Can I scale the Azure AI Search free tier?

No. On the Free tier you cannot scale, and you cannot configure replicas or partitions. You must use a billable tier for that.
