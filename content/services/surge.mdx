Surge is a static web publishing service you drive from the command line. You run one command in a folder of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and Surge uploads it to its CDN with HTTPS at a surge.sh domain or your own. It suits front-end developers who want to ship a static build or a prototype without a dashboard.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says Surge is free to publish, with no trial period and no project cap. Free lists unlimited projects and domains, custom domains with automatic certificates, previews, the last 100 revisions with instant rollback, and scoped tokens for CI and agents.

The getting-started guide shows the flow: install the CLI from npm, which needs Node.js version 18 or later, then run surge publish. The first run creates an account with an email and password in the terminal and suggests a domain such as your-project.surge.sh. Each publish creates a new revision, production switches over when the upload completes, and surge rollback undoes a bad publish. The guide also says custom domains are free and the SSL certificate is provisioned automatically, and that surge publish --preview uploads a revision with its own URL.

## When you hit a limit

Publishing is rate limited on every plan: each domain gets 30 publishes per 15 minutes and 200 per day, and each account gets 60 and 500. A project can be up to 450 MB and 10,100 files. The pricing page says limits pause publishing but never stop a live project from serving, and that nothing is metered, with no bandwidth billing or overage charge. Neither the pricing page nor the CLI guide states a card rule at sign-up.

The paid upgrade is Surge Pro at $30 a month. It adds password protection for a domain or specific paths, and redirects and rewrites.

## Gotchas

- Password protection is a Pro feature, so you cannot lock a free site behind a password.
- Redirects and rewrites are a Pro feature. Free covers 200.html, 404.html, clean URLs, forced HTTPS, and CORS.
- Surge only hosts static files; a single-page app needs a 200.html shell for deep links, as the guide notes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Surge free?

Yes. The pricing page says Surge is free to publish, with unlimited projects and domains and custom domains with automatic certificates on the free plan.

### Can I use my own domain on the free plan?

Yes. The free plan lists a custom domain, and the guide says the SSL certificate is provisioned automatically.

### How much does Surge Pro cost?

Surge Pro costs $30 a month and adds password protection and redirects and rewrites.
