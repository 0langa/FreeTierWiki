Surge is a static web publishing service you drive from the command line. You run one command in a folder of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and Surge uploads it to its CDN with HTTPS at a surge.sh domain or your own. It suits front-end developers who want to ship a static build or a prototype without a dashboard.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says Surge is free to use. The free plan is for publishing any folder easily and lists unlimited publishing, a custom domain, and basic SSL.

The getting-started guide shows the flow: install the CLI from npm, which needs Node.js version 18 or later, then run surge publish. The first run creates an account with an email and password in the terminal and suggests a domain such as your-project.surge.sh. Each publish creates a new revision, production switches over when the upload completes, and surge rollback undoes a bad publish. The guide also says custom domains are free and the SSL certificate is provisioned automatically, and that surge publish --preview uploads a revision with its own URL.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say. It lists no storage, bandwidth, or request limits for the free plan, and there is no metered billing, so publishing more does not create a bill. Upgrading is a choice you make for features, not a charge that starts on its own.

The paid upgrade is Surge Professional at $30 a month, described as for unlimited production-ready projects. It adds custom SSL, forcing http to https, Cross-Origin Resource Sharing, custom redirects, and password protection.

## Gotchas

- Forcing http to https is listed only under Surge Professional, so plan for that if your site must redirect to HTTPS.
- Custom redirects and password protection are Professional features, so you cannot lock a free site behind a password.
- Surge only hosts static files; a single-page app needs a 200.html shell for deep links, as the guide notes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Surge free?

Yes. The pricing page says Surge is free to use, with unlimited publishing, a custom domain, and basic SSL on the free plan.

### Can I use my own domain on the free plan?

Yes. The free plan lists a custom domain, and the guide says the SSL certificate is provisioned automatically.

### How much does Surge Professional cost?

Surge Professional costs $30 a month and adds custom SSL, forced HTTPS, CORS, custom redirects, and password protection.
