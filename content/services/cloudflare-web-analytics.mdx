You add a JavaScript snippet to your pages, or switch it on in the Cloudflare dashboard for a proxied site, and read the stats there. It fits bloggers and docs sites that want traffic numbers without cookies.

## What the free plan gives you

Web Analytics is free and needs no credit card. Cloudflare says it uses no client-side state such as cookies or localStorage, and it does not fingerprint people by IP address or user agent. You get page views, page load time, and Core Web Vitals, measured from your visitors' browsers.

You can track up to 10 sites that do not run through Cloudflare. Sites proxied through Cloudflare have no limit. You can read data for the previous six months. Cloudflare keeps unsampled beacon data for the past 7 days. After that, it aggregates the data down to around 10% of the original volume.

The Free plan has no Web Analytics rules. Without rules, the snippet is injected on all subdomains of a proxied site.

## When you hit a limit

The docs list a site limit of 10 for sites that are not proxied. The FAQ calls it a soft limit that can be adjusted. The pages do not describe a bill or a cutoff, and the tool is free on every plan.

Sampling is the more likely thing you will notice. Low-traffic sites are sampled at higher percentages to keep the numbers reliable. The dashboard picks a sampling level based on your filters and the volume of matching rows. The GraphQL API shows which level it used in the sampleInterval field.

## Gotchas

- Ad blockers block the JavaScript beacon, so counts for sites without Cloudflare proxying can run low. Proxied sites can also use edge analytics, which blockers cannot stop. The FAQ says Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans get that advanced version.
- Single-page apps may show fewer metrics than you expect. The last route change is only sent when the visitor leaves the tab or closes the window.
- There are no funnels or user paths. It is a traffic and speed tool.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Web Analytics really free?

Yes. It is free and needs no credit card. You can use it on sites that are not on Cloudflare.

### Does Cloudflare Web Analytics use cookies?

No. Cloudflare says it uses no cookies or localStorage and does not fingerprint visitors.

### How long does Cloudflare keep Web Analytics data?

You can access data for the previous six months. Unsampled data stays for 7 days and is then aggregated to around 10% of its original volume.
