You add transformation options to an image URL, or you use an SDK for JavaScript, Node.js, Python, PHP, React, and other stacks. It suits site owners who want faster, smaller images.

## What the free plan gives you

The Forever free plan costs $0 per month. It is for low-traffic websites and apps, side projects, and testing. You get 20 GB of bandwidth each month, 3 GB of DAM storage, 2 users, and 500 purge requests per month.

It also includes 500 video units and 650 extension units each month. ImageKit says the plan has access to all media optimizations, transformations, and streaming features, with monthly limits. AI-powered operations and Creative Automation exports are limited.

You also get community support and an integrated media library. Custom domain names are not included.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe what happens at the limit. It marks bandwidth, video units, extension units, and purge requests as monthly limits, and storage and users as fixed limits. It says the Lite plan adds overage for bandwidth and storage, so the free plan does not allow overage.

No card is needed, so no bill can arrive. Lite costs $9 per month plus pay-as-you-go, with 40 GB of bandwidth and 10 GB of storage. Extra bandwidth then costs $0.5 per GB.

## Gotchas

- Older notes list 40 GB of bandwidth and 10 GB of storage for the free plan. Those figures belong to the paid Lite plan.
- Free has 2 external origins and 2 URL endpoints. A third needs a paid plan.
- Email support and custom domains such as images.example.com need a paid plan. Free has community support only.

## Questions people ask

### Is ImageKit free?

Yes. The Forever free plan gives you 20 GB of bandwidth per month, 3 GB of storage, and 2 users. It has fixed limits.

### Does ImageKit need a credit card?

The entry records that the free plan needs no card. The plan has no overage, so it cannot bill you for extra use.

### How much does ImageKit cost after the free plan?

The Lite plan costs $9 per month plus pay-as-you-go. It includes 40 GB of bandwidth and 10 GB of storage.
