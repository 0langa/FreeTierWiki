Xirsys runs STUN and TURN servers for WebRTC. STUN helps peers find each other, and TURN relays media when two endpoints cannot connect directly. Xirsys bills for TURN bandwidth, not for minutes or sessions.

## What the free plan gives you

The free Developer account starts with every production feature for the first 30 days: all 12 regions, user-geo routing, static credentials, and up to 2 channels. Channels separate apps, environments, or customers for usage reports.

After day 30 the account stays active, and Xirsys says it never expires. It drops to testing limits: one region, 2 channels, unlimited STUN, and 500 MB of TURN a month. Xirsys describes this free tier as great for testing and prototypes. Direct peer-to-peer traffic and STUN do not count toward the TURN allowance.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says the 500 MB of monthly TURN on the free account is a hard cap: the relay stops until the monthly reset. STUN stays unlimited, so peers that can connect directly are not affected by the TURN cap.

Production plans remove the hard cap and bill per-GB overage instead. The smallest costs $39 a month, includes 50 GB of monthly TURN bandwidth with $0.50 per GB over, and adds all 12 regions, 10 channels, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.

## Gotchas

- The first month looks like a production plan; plan for the drop to one region and 500 MB when the trial ends.
- Video relayed through TURN uses the 500 MB quickly, so the free tier suits short test calls, not real usage.
- The 2-channel cap applies during and after the trial.

## Questions people ask

### Is Xirsys free?

Yes. The Developer account is free forever, with 500 MB of TURN a month after a 30-day production trial.

### What happens when I use the free TURN allowance?

The relay stops until the monthly reset. The free account has a hard cap and no overage.

### Does STUN count toward the free limit?

No. The free account includes unlimited STUN; the 500 MB limit applies to TURN relay traffic.
