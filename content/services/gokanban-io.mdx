You use Gokanban.io in the browser. The board is plain text: you type columns and items, and the board view updates, so it suits people who like keyboard-driven tools.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no pricing page. The site says the board needs no registration, that sharing is instant, and that it is syntax driven. Opening the site gives you a new board at its own address. A column starts with a slash, an item starts with an at sign, and a double dash makes a divider. Items can carry a description, tags, a color, and a completed flag that adds a checkmark.

The page shows buttons to add a column, a divider, or an item, and an Advanced Kanban button. It lists use cases such as personal tasks, backlog, project breakdown, product work, and retrospective.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say, because the site has none, and it lists no board, item, or storage limits. We cannot tell you what happens at a limit. It also does not say how long a board is kept. Treat the board link as the only copy, and keep your own backup of anything important. The Advanced Kanban button promises more features, but the page does not say whether they are free or paid.

## Gotchas

- The site does not say who can open a board link or whether boards are private, so do not put private data on it.
- The site publishes no limits, retention period, or terms on the page, so you cannot plan around them.
- The first board is a text editor, so people who expect drag-and-drop cards may need a minute to learn the syntax.

## Questions people ask

### Is Gokanban.io free?

The site shows no price and no paid plan. You can open a board and use it without registration.

### Does Gokanban.io need an account?

No. The page title and the first board both say there is no registration, and sharing works by link.

### How do I add cards on Gokanban.io?

Type them in the text view with an at sign, or use the Add item button. Use a slash for a column and a double dash for a divider.
