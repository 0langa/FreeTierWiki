You use Gokanban.io in the browser. The board is plain text: you type columns and items, and the board view updates, so it suits people who like keyboard-driven tools.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no pricing page. Opening the site gives you a blank board at its own address without any account form. The controls show a slash for a column, an at sign for an item, and a double dash for a divider. These are the syntax markers for the board.

The page shows buttons to add a column, a divider, or an item, and an Advanced Kanban button. Use the editor for personal tasks, a quick backlog, or a project breakdown when you do not need shared access controls or a formal service plan.

## When you hit a limit

The public board lists no board, item, or storage limits and has no pricing page. We cannot tell you what happens at a limit. It also does not say how long a board is kept. Treat the board link as the only copy, and keep your own backup of anything important. The page also shows an Advanced Kanban button without an explanation of its pricing or limits.

## Gotchas

- The site does not say who can open a board link or whether boards are private, so do not put private data on it.
- The site publishes no limits, retention period, or terms on the page, so you cannot plan around them.
- The first board is a text editor, so people who expect drag-and-drop cards may need a minute to learn the syntax.

## Questions people ask

### Is Gokanban.io free?

The site shows no price and no paid plan. You can open a board and use it without registration.

### Does Gokanban.io need an account?

No account form appeared when opening the initial board, and the board has its own link. Access controls for that link are not documented.

### How do I add cards on Gokanban.io?

Type them in the text view with an at sign, or use the Add item button. Use a slash for a column and a double dash for a divider.
