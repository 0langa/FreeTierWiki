You enter a URL on gtmetrix.com, run a test, and read the report in your dashboard. A REST API exists for automation. It suits site owners, developers, and marketers who want a clear speed report.

## What the free plan gives you

Basic requires login to generate and save reports in your dashboard. It provides 5 on-demand tests monthly for 3 months, plus 5 one-time signup API credits without refills. Data retention is 1 week. Free accounts unlock additional locations, analysis options and connection speeds without a credit card.

The FAQ permits monitoring 1 URL daily, weekly or monthly. Paid PRO plans increase limits. Lite's regular annual-equivalent price is $4.99/month for 50 monthly tests, 1 monitored slot, 5 locations and 1-month retention. The November 23, 2026 page advertises a temporary $2.99/month Black Friday annual-billing offer ending December 2; distinguish it from the regular rate.

## When you hit a limit

Once you reach your test limit, GTmetrix suggests upgrading to a PRO plan to keep testing. The free monthly test allowance also ends after 3 months. After that, you need a paid plan. You are not billed unless you choose one.

The current pricing page lists only paid plans; the free Basic account is offered through the Try for FREE sign-up. Its test and credit figures come from GTmetrix's own post about the Basic account changes, which took effect on December 1, 2023. The current FAQ confirms the single monitored URL and lists 1-week Basic retention in its downgrade policy. The announcement page was last updated in September 2026; these Basic limits remain published there.

## Gotchas

- The 5 monthly tests last for 3 months. Free use is not open-ended.
- The 5 trial API credits never refill. A Lighthouse report uses 1 credit, so API testing ends quickly.
- Reports are kept for 1 week on the Basic account. Save the results you need.

## Questions people ask

### Is GTmetrix free?

Yes, with limits. A free Basic account gives 5 on-demand tests per month for 3 months. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I run out of GTmetrix tests?

GTmetrix suggests upgrading to a PRO plan to continue testing. Lite’s regular annual price works out to $4.99 per month.

### Can I use the GTmetrix API for free?

Only a little. A Basic account gets 5 trial API credits at sign-up, with no refills. A Lighthouse report costs 1 credit.
