You enter a URL on gtmetrix.com, run a test, and read the report in your dashboard. A REST API exists for automation. It suits site owners, developers, and marketers who want a clear speed report.

## What the free plan gives you

The free account is called Basic. A login is required to generate a report. Once you log in or create an account, the report is shown and saved to your GTmetrix dashboard.

The Basic account has these limits: 5 on-demand tests per month, for 3 months. It also gives 5 trial API credits at sign-up, with no refills after they are used up. Data is kept for 1 week. The site says a free account unlocks more test locations, analysis options, and connection speeds. It needs no credit card.

The FAQ adds that Basic users can monitor 1 URL daily, weekly, or monthly. Paid PRO plans raise those limits. The cheapest is Lite, at $4.99 per month billed yearly, with 50 on-demand tests a month, 1 monitored slot, 5 test locations, and 1 month of data retention.

## When you hit a limit

Once you reach your test limit, GTmetrix suggests upgrading to a PRO plan to keep testing. The free account also runs for only 3 months of monthly tests. After that, you need a paid plan. You are not billed unless you choose one.

The current pricing page lists only paid plans; the free Basic account is offered through the Try for FREE sign-up. Its test and credit figures come from GTmetrix's own post about the Basic account changes, which took effect on December 1, 2023. The FAQ, read in a browser, still confirms the 1-week retention and the single monitored URL.

## Gotchas

- The 5 monthly tests last for 3 months. Free use is not open-ended.
- The 5 trial API credits never refill. A Lighthouse report uses 1 credit, so API testing ends quickly.
- Reports are kept for 1 week on the Basic account. Save the results you need.

## Questions people ask

### Is GTmetrix free?

Yes, with limits. A free Basic account gives 5 on-demand tests per month for 3 months. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I run out of GTmetrix tests?

GTmetrix suggests upgrading to a PRO plan to continue testing. The cheapest listed plan is Lite at $4.99 per month, billed yearly.

### Can I use the GTmetrix API for free?

Only a little. A Basic account gets 5 trial API credits at sign-up, with no refills. A Lighthouse report costs 1 credit.
