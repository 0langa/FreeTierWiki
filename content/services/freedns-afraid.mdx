FreeDNS, run at afraid.org, is a long-running DNS host for dynamic DNS, static DNS, subdomains, and whole domains. Members can share their domains in a registry so other members can create subdomains under them.

## What the free plan gives you

A free account covers DNS hosting for your own domains, and the home page says the number of domains per account is unlimited. You can also attach subdomains from the shared domain registry, where members offer their domains for others to use. Free features include subdomain and domain hosting, backup DNS, IPv6 reverse DNS, and URL redirection.

Dynamic updates are a single request: you curl your record's update URL at sync.afraid.org and the record changes to your current IP, or use v6.sync.afraid.org for IPv6. Records can point at private LAN addresses too. The site describes itself as engineer operated, with no ads, solicitations, or upsells.

## When you hit a limit

The fetched pages do not say how many subdomains a free account can create, or what happens if you reach that number. The premium offer, at $5 a month, adds 50 subdomains, 3 stealth flags, and wildcard DNS, which suggests the free account has a smaller subdomain allowance.

The pages show no metered pricing for DNS queries or updates. There is no usage-based billing: going past a free limit means upgrading to premium or removing records, not an overage charge.

## Gotchas

- A subdomain from the shared registry lives under someone else's domain, and that owner controls the domain.
- Wildcard DNS is a premium feature, so free accounts need a record per name.
- Keep the email address on your account current; the site says it is the only way to recover a forgotten password.

## Questions people ask

### Is FreeDNS at afraid.org free?

Yes. Free accounts host DNS and subdomains, and premium membership costs $5 a month for extra features.

### How do I update a FreeDNS record from a script?

Request the record's update URL at sync.afraid.org with curl, for example from a cron job. Use v6.sync.afraid.org for IPv6.

### Does the FreeDNS free plan include wildcard DNS?

No. Wildcard DNS is listed as a premium feature.
