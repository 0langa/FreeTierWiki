You use UptimeToolbox in a web dashboard, with an Intro Tour for new users. A small Linux agent reports server metrics. It suits small sites and projects that need a status page and a few checks.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan costs $0 per month. The home page lists 5 monitors with 3-minute checks, 1 status page, and 1 server. Data is kept as 7 days of hi-res data and 3 months of historical data. The rendered Basic table excludes custom domains, SSL monitoring, white labeling, team members, and SMS or voice credit.

The product watches domain expiry; SSL monitoring is a paid feature. The Professional plan costs $9 per month and has 25 monitors, 1-minute checks, 3 status pages, and 10 servers. The Business plan costs $29 per month with 100 monitors and 30-second checks.

## When you hit a limit

The home page does not say what happens when you reach 5 monitors, and it does not describe any charge on Basic. Higher counts need a paid plan.

The rendered table and changelog agree that phone alerts need Pro. A 14-day paid-plan trial needs no credit card, but the site does not separately state the ongoing Basic signup requirement. Basic is explicitly free of charge; no exact quota enforcement policy is published.

## Gotchas

- Basic has 5 monitors and 1 server, so a second server needs a paid plan or a custom plan from support.
- SMS and voice alerts, custom domains and SSL monitoring require a paid plan.
- The Linux agent needs no extra software and uses tools built into most Linux distributions, such as awk, curl, and sed. The Linux FAQ calls server monitoring an early preview without alerting, while the changelog lists CPU, RAM, and disk thresholds, so test server alerts before you rely on them.

## Questions people ask

### Is UptimeToolbox free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free and lists 5 monitors, 3-minute checks, 1 status page, and 1 server.

### Does UptimeToolbox need a credit card?

The site confirms no card for the 14-day paid-plan trial. It does not separately state the ongoing Basic signup requirement.

### Does UptimeToolbox send SMS alerts for free?

No. The rendered Basic table excludes phone credit, and the changelog requires Pro for SMS and voice alerts.
