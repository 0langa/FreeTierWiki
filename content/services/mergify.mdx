You install the Mergify GitHub App, choose which products to turn on per repository, and then drive the queue with pull request comments such as `@mergifyio queue`. It suits maintainers and small teams that use GitHub.

## What the free plan gives you

Open Source is free with unlimited users; private Free permits 5. Both include CI Insights, Test Engine and Merge Queue, with differing feature coverage in the pricing table. CI Insights retention is 24 hours and Test Engine 7 days; paid Max retains 30 days.

Billing counts active contributors for 30 days after opening/updating a PR, including head-branch updates attributed to their author. Bots, inactive teammates, reviews and comments alone do not count; each person counts once across repositories. Pricing FAQ excludes one-off contributors, while detailed billing counts anyone opening/updating a PR. Confirm that exception in the dashboard rather than assuming a single contribution is exempt.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a private team grows past 5 active contributors. The next step is the Max plan, which costs $21 per seat per month and covers up to 100 users. Mergify also offers a free 14-day trial of Max with no credit card. The pages also do not separately state whether permanent Free requires a card. Paid subscriptions may generate a final invoice for contributor-count adjustments when cancellation takes effect; cancellation does not erase accrued usage.

## Gotchas

- Adding a repository needs GitHub admin rights, and each product is switched on per repository.
- Free CI Insights retention is 24 hours, while Test Engine retention is 7 days. Max keeps both for 30 days.
- Mergify works with GitHub. The docs describe installing a GitHub App and do not mention other hosts.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mergify free?

Yes. It is free for open source projects and for private teams of up to 5 active contributors. All three products are included.

### Does Mergify need a credit card?

The 14-day Max trial needs no card. The reviewed pages do not separately spell out a card requirement for the permanent Free plan.

### Who counts as an active contributor on Mergify?

A real user who opened or updated a pull request in the last 30 days. Updates to its head branch count, while bots and inactive teammates do not.
