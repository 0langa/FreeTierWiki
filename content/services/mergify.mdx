You install the Mergify GitHub App, choose which products to turn on per repository, and then drive the queue with pull request comments such as `@mergifyio queue`. It suits maintainers and small teams that use GitHub.

## What the free plan gives you

Mergify is free for open source projects, with unlimited users. Private teams get the Free plan for up to 5 users. Both plans include all four products: CI Insights, Test Engine, Merge Queue, and Merge Protections. The pricing page says the Open Source and Free plans do not cover the same features, so check its table.

Data retention is 24 hours on the Free plan and on the Open Source plan. The paid Max plan keeps data for 30 days. Billing counts only active contributors. A contributor is active if they open a pull request or push commits to one in the last 30 days. Inactive teammates, bots, and one-off contributors do not count.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a private team grows past 5 active contributors. The next step is the Max plan, which costs $21 per seat per month and covers up to 100 users. Mergify also offers a free 14-day trial of Max with no credit card. The free plan asks for no card, so no surprise charge shows up.

## Gotchas

- Adding a repository needs GitHub admin rights, and each product is switched on per repository.
- Free data retention is short at 24 hours, while Max keeps data for 30 days.
- Mergify works with GitHub. The docs describe installing a GitHub App and do not mention other hosts.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mergify free?

Yes. It is free for open source projects and for private teams of up to 5 active contributors. All four products are included.

### Does Mergify need a credit card?

No. The free plan needs no card, and the 14-day Max trial also needs none.

### Who counts as an active contributor on Mergify?

A person who opened a pull request or pushed commits to one in the last 30 days. Inactive teammates, bots, and one-off contributors do not count.
