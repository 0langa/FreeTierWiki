You send logs to Better Stack with its collector, Vector, OpenTelemetry, or a plain HTTP request, then search them in the web app and watch them live. It suits small apps that need searchable logs without running a logging server.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is "Free for personal projects" and costs $0 per month. For logs it includes 3 GB retained for 3 days. It also includes 3 GB of traces retained for 3 days, 30 GB of metrics, and 3 GB of web events retained for 3 days.

The plan also covers 100,000 exceptions per month, 5,000 session replays, 10 monitors and heartbeats, and 1 status page. Team members are unlimited, and alerts go to Slack and email. Everything sits in one account, so logs, uptime checks, and error tracking share the same free plan and the same limits.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 3 GB of logs on the free plan. It does list paid Telemetry bundles that include 30 days of log retention, and per-GB prices for ingestion and retention beyond the bundle.

The short retention is the limit you will notice first. Logs, traces, and web events stay for only 3 days on the free plan, so an old problem will be gone by the time you look.

## Gotchas

- Logs are kept for only 3 days on the free plan, while paid bundles keep them for 30 days.
- The free plan is labeled "Free for personal projects"; the pricing page does not say whether company use is allowed.
- Generic SAML SSO is for enterprise plans only, and Azure and Okta SSO cost extra.

Yes, for personal projects. The free plan costs $0 per month and includes 3 GB of logs retained for 3 days, plus metrics, traces, and uptime monitors.

### How long does Better Stack keep logs on the free plan?

Logs are retained for 3 days on the free plan. Paid bundles list 30 days of log retention.

### What happens when I use more than 3 GB of logs?

The pricing page does not say for the free plan. Paid bundles list per-GB prices for ingestion and retention.
