You work in the Appwrite Console and call your project through client SDKs or its REST and GraphQL APIs. You can also self-host the open-source version. This page covers the Cloud Free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. You get 2 projects on shared resources, with 1 database, 1 bucket, and 2 functions in each. Auth covers 75,000 monthly active users. Storage is 2 GB with a 50 MB file size limit, and API bandwidth is 5 GB per month. Functions get 750,000 executions and 100 GB-hours per month. The database allows 500,000 reads and 250,000 writes per month. Realtime allows 250 concurrent connections and 2 million messages. Messaging allows 1,000 messages per month. Your organization has 1 member, and logs are kept for 1 hour.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan does not bill you. If you reach a resource limit, your project freezes and the Console keeps running in read-only mode. You can upgrade to Pro, remove resources that exceed the limit, or wait for the next billing cycle, which resets usage. Pro starts at $25 per month.

Free projects are also paused after 1 week of inactivity.

## Gotchas

- Free projects are paused after 1 week of inactivity, and you can run only 2 of them.
- The Free plan has no backups, and the organization has a single member.
- Each project has 1 database, 1 bucket, and 2 functions, with a 15-minute build limit for functions.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [Supabase](/services/supabase/): when you want a Postgres-based backend with auth and storage.
- [Nhost](/services/nhost/): when you want Postgres with a GraphQL API plus auth and storage.
- [Convex](/services/convex/): when you want a reactive backend where you write your data logic as functions.

## Questions people ask

### Is Appwrite free?

Yes. The Appwrite Cloud Free plan costs $0 and has no end date, with limits on projects, users, storage, and usage. The open-source version is also free to self-host.

### What happens when I go over the Appwrite free limits?

Your project freezes and the Console turns read-only. You can upgrade to Pro, remove the resources over the limit, or wait for the next billing cycle.

### Does Appwrite pause free projects?

Yes. Free projects are paused after 1 week of inactivity. The pricing page limits free accounts to 2 projects.
