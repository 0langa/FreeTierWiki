You install the extension in Chrome, start a crawl, and read the list of issues in the extension. It fits site owners and developers who check small sites after each change.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called Basic. It crawls up to 100 URLs per crawl. You can run unlimited crawls on unlimited sites, so you can check every site you work on after every change. The tool tests pages against more than 50 SEO, speed, and security best practices, and you can limit crawls by subdomain, subfolder, search depth, pattern, and type.

The Basic plan is supported by ads. The site lists no credit card step for it. The extension is free to add to Chrome.

The paid Professional plan costs $10 per month when billed yearly, or $15 per month when billed monthly. It raises the crawl size to 10,000 URLs, removes ads, crawls localhost URLs, and unlocks CSV and PDF export. New customers can get a full refund within 14 days.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a site has more than 100 URLs. The Basic plan is described as a crawl of up to 100 URLs per crawl. Treat that number as the cap, and check a large site in sections.

You cannot be billed on the free plan. Professional is a separate subscription that you must choose. It is aimed at people who want to audit large sites, test local sites, and export reports.

## Gotchas

- Basic crawls cap at 100 URLs, so large sites are only partly covered. Narrow each crawl to one subfolder or subdomain to cover more.
- Crawling localhost and exporting to CSV or PDF are Professional features.
- The free plan shows ads. Professional is ad free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Checkbot free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free with ads. It crawls up to 100 URLs per crawl, with unlimited crawls and sites.

### How much does Checkbot Professional cost?

It costs $10 per month on a yearly plan or $15 per month on a monthly plan. New customers get a full refund if they cancel within 14 days.

### Can Checkbot crawl localhost?

Not on the free plan. Crawling localhost URLs is a Professional feature.
