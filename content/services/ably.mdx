You work with Ably through its dashboard, the Ably CLI, and SDKs for many languages, including JavaScript, Python, Go, Java, and Swift. It suits developers who want realtime features without running their own WebSocket servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free package costs $0 and has no time limit. Ably now prices its packages by monthly active users (MAUs), and Free includes 500 MAUs each month. A user who connects several times in a month counts once, and servers are not counted. Free covers every Ably product, including Pub/Sub, Chat, Spaces, and LiveObjects.

You also get 6 million messages per month, a top rate of 500 messages per second, and 500 concurrent connections and 500 concurrent channels. Each channel can hold up to 200 presence members, and one message can be up to 64KiB. History defaults to 24 hours and can be kept up to 30 days. Support is community and email support on a best effort basis. The Free package has no credit card requirement.

## When you hit a limit

Ably says it won't cut you off when Free usage goes over a limit. Instead, the account is restricted until it recovers. After 6 million messages in a month, you are limited to 1,600 messages per hour until the next month starts. Connections and channels above 500 are rejected until you are back under the limit.

Going over 500 MAUs in a month may degrade service until the start of the following month. Rate limits, such as messages per second, can reject the extra publish attempts with an error code. Ably sends email and dashboard notices when you near a limit and when you pass it. Free has no cost, so more capacity means a paid package.

## Gotchas

- Every device must connect with a `clientId`, and each distinct `clientId` counts as an MAU, so giving one user different IDs on two devices uses two MAUs.
- The 1,600 messages per hour throttle after the monthly allowance is far below the normal 250,000 messages per hour, so a busy app can slow sharply late in the month.
- If you move from a paid package back to Free, the change takes effect at the end of the current month, and the final invoice includes any extra MAUs used that month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Ably free?

Yes. The Free package costs $0 with no time limit. It includes 500 MAUs, 6 million messages per month, 500 concurrent connections, and 500 concurrent channels.

### Does Ably need a credit card?

No. Ably says no credit card is needed to sign up for the Free package, and there is no limit on how long you can use it.

### What happens when I go over the Ably free limits?

Ably does not cut you off. Past 6 million messages you are limited to 1,600 messages per hour for the rest of the month, extra connections and channels are rejected, and going over 500 MAUs may degrade service.
