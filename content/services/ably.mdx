You work with Ably through its dashboard, the Ably CLI, and SDKs for many languages, including JavaScript, Python, Go, Java, and Swift. It suits developers who want realtime features without running their own WebSocket servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free package costs $0 and has no time limit. Ably calls it a place to build a proof of concept. It includes 6 million messages per month and a top rate of 500 messages per second. You can have 200 concurrent connections and 200 concurrent channels at the same time.

Each channel can hold up to 200 presence members. The number of subscribers per channel is unlimited. Ably keeps messages for 1 day, and one message can be up to 64KiB. Support is community support with a best-effort target. The Free package has no credit card requirement.

## When you hit a limit

Ably splits limits into two kinds. Count-based limits, such as concurrent connections and channels, restrict new use until existing resources are removed. Rate-based limits, such as messages per second, can reject the extra publish attempts and return an error code.

Ably says it will not block usage for most limits just because you passed them, and that normal service can continue for a while. It sends email and dashboard notices when you near a limit and when you pass it, and it will ask you to upgrade. The Free package has no card on file, so the limits are the only consequence. The pages do not say what happens after the 6 million monthly messages are used.

## Gotchas

- Message history lasts only 1 day on the Free package. Longer storage needs a paid package.
- Support on the Free package is community support (Discord and Stack Overflow) with a best-effort target. The pricing page lists a shared Slack or Teams channel only on Enterprise.
- If you downgrade from a paid package, you keep the paid limits until the billing cycle ends, then get a final invoice for the full package fee and the usage.

## Questions people ask

### Is Ably free?

Yes. The Free package costs $0 with no time limit. It includes 6 million messages per month, 200 concurrent connections, and 200 concurrent channels.

### Does Ably need a credit card?

No. Ably says no credit card is needed to sign up for the Free package, and there is no limit on how long you can use it.

### What happens when I go over the Ably free limits?

Ably restricts or rejects the extra usage for count-based and rate-based limits, and it emails you as you near and pass a limit. It will ask you to upgrade to a paid package.
