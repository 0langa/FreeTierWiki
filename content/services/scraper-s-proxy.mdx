You call Scraper's Proxy through the RapidAPI hub with an HTTP request, and you can test it from the browser first. It suits developers who want to try proxy scraping on a few pages.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan costs $0 a month and sits on RapidAPI. It includes 100 requests a month with a hard limit, and a rate limit of 5 requests per second. It also includes proxy and cookie sessions and JavaScript rendering. Email support is not included on Basic. Failed requests are free and do not count toward the quota.

The API has endpoints to fetch raw pages by GET or POST, to fetch pages with a rendered browser, and to turn a page into JSON. Allow for the extra time browser rendering takes when choosing a request timeout.

The current listing warns that providers may require a card for overage plans. Its older general guide says freemium APIs require one; confirm before subscribing.

## When you hit a limit

The request limit is hard: at 100 requests a month, the Basic plan stops. The bill risk comes from a separate bandwidth fee. The plan includes 10240MB a month, and after that RapidAPI charges $0.001 per 1MB.

RapidAPI's own rules also apply. Its docs say freemium plans can charge overage fees, and that you must watch your usage in the dashboard. It emails you when you reach 85% and 100% of a quota, but it says an email is not guaranteed.

## Gotchas

- Basic includes paid bandwidth overages. Confirm card requirements and billing controls before subscribing.
- The 100 requests are a hard limit, but the bandwidth fee is not, so the two limits behave differently.
- Large pages use more of the bandwidth quota. The listing advises setting the accept-encoding header to gzip, which uses less bandwidth.

## Questions people ask

### Is Scraper's Proxy free?

There is a free Basic plan with 100 requests a month. You need a RapidAPI account, extra bandwidth is billed, and the listing warns that a card may be required.

### What happens when I go over the Scraper's Proxy free limit?

The Basic plan has a hard limit on requests, so it stops at 100 a month. Bandwidth past 10240MB is billed at $0.001 per 1MB.

### Does Scraper's Proxy need a credit card?

The current listing says providers may require a card for plans with overage fees, while older general docs say freemium APIs require one. Basic has bandwidth overages, so confirm the requirement before subscribing.
