You create monitors in the Cronitor web app or through its API, then make each cron job or script call a ping URL when it runs. It suits homelabs and small projects with a few scheduled jobs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hacker plan is free forever and is meant for homelabs and small projects. It includes 5 monitors, which can be cron jobs, heartbeats, websites, or API checks. It has 1 dashboard user and 1 status page.

Alerts go to email and chat tools: Slack, Discord, Teams, and webhooks. SMS alerts and synthetic browser checks are not included. Web analytics and error tracking come with 100,000 events free.

The paid Business plan costs $2 per month for each monitor plus $5 per month for each user. It starts with a 14 day free trial and adds SMS and on-call alerts and 12 months of data retention.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to add a sixth monitor on the Hacker plan. It asks for no card and lists no overage charge for the free plan, so a surprise bill looks unlikely.

The paid Business plan has no base fee or minimum. You pay only for the monitors and users you have, billed monthly with no contract. Plan on upgrading, or on removing a monitor, when you pass 5.

## Gotchas

- Only 5 monitors are free, and the count includes every cron job, website, and API monitor together.
- SMS and on-call alerts need the paid Business plan.
- The docs example for creating a monitor uses the API with your account API key, so keep that key out of your repo.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cronitor free?

Yes. The Hacker plan is free forever and includes 5 monitors for homelabs and small projects.

### Does Cronitor need a credit card?

Not for the Hacker plan. The Business plan has a 14 day free trial, then bills for the monitors and users you use.

### What happens when I go over 5 Cronitor monitors?

The pricing page does not say. The Business plan costs $2 per month for each monitor.
