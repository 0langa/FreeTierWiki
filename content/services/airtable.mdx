Airtable is a hosted database that looks like a spreadsheet. You build tables with typed fields, link records between tables, and add views, forms, interfaces, and automations without code. Developers also use its Web API as a quick backend for prototypes and internal tools.

## What the free plan gives you

Airtable's Free plan is for individuals, very small teams, and lightweight needs, and all users on it are free. You get unlimited bases, each holding up to 1,000 records and 1 GB of attachments. One workspace can have up to 5 editors and 50 commenters.

Each month the Free plan includes 100 automation runs and 1,000 API calls. Revision and snapshot history goes back 2 weeks. Each editor also gets 500 AI credits each month. Grid, kanban, calendar, and list views are included, and so is the Interface Designer.

## When you hit a limit

Airtable answers this on its pricing page. If you reach or go over the record or attachment limits, you can still use your bases and Airtable never removes your data. Airtable notifies you, and you cannot add more records or attachments until you upgrade.

Automation runs are capped. API calls have a one-time 30-day grace period when the workspace first exceeds its monthly allowance; after that, calls above the limit are blocked until the next calendar month. Team costs $20 per seat per month billed annually, or $24 billed monthly. Adding a payment method during the initial 14-day Team trial automatically upgrades and bills the workspace.

## Gotchas

- The record limit is per base, so splitting data across bases keeps you under it but breaks links between tables.
- 1,000 API calls a month is far too few for an app that reads Airtable on every page view.
- Team bills collaborators with Commenter permissions or higher; Business billing starts at Editor. Workspaces have separate plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Airtable free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing and includes unlimited bases with 1,000 records each, up to 5 editors, 100 automation runs, and 1,000 API calls per month.

### What happens when my base reaches 1,000 records?

You keep full access and your data stays. You cannot add more records until you upgrade the workspace to a paid plan.

### Can I use the Airtable API on the Free plan?

Yes, with 1,000 API calls per workspace per month. Exceeding the quota starts a one-time 30-day grace period; later over-limit calls are blocked until the monthly reset or an upgrade.
