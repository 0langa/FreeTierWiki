DeployHQ takes code from a Git repository and deploys it to servers you already have, over SSH, SFTP, and other targets, with an optional build step first. It suits developers and agencies who host on a VPS or shared hosting and want push-to-deploy without writing their own pipeline.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is $0 a month for personal projects and needs no credit card. It includes 3 deployments per day, 1 project, 5 servers, 1 concurrent deployment, 30 build minutes, and 1 user. A project is a connected repository plus one or more servers to deploy it to.

DeployHQ offers build pipelines and deployment automation alongside these resource allowances. Advanced capabilities such as turbo deployments, custom domains, deploying behind firewalls, team management and SSO have plan-specific availability. Check the selected plan before relying on them. The free grant is intended for one user deploying one personal project to existing servers, rather than providing a general-purpose team deployment service.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan counts deployments per day, so the fourth deployment in a day needs a paid plan. The pricing page does not say whether that deployment is queued or refused, and the free plan does not require a card.

Solo costs €10 monthly or €9 per month billed annually, with unlimited deployments, 3 projects, 3 concurrent deployments, and 200 build minutes. Pro costs €21 monthly or €19 per month billed annually, with 10 projects and unlimited build minutes. Paid deployment trials last 10 days without a card, then automatically downgrade to Free if you do not subscribe, retaining data without a charge.

## Gotchas

- 3 deployments per day is easy to pass on a busy day of small fixes, especially with auto-deploy on every push.
- The pricing page lists 30 build minutes on Free without saying whether they reset monthly.
- Free has 1 user, so a second developer who needs to trigger deployments needs a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is DeployHQ free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month and covers 1 project with 3 deployments per day.

### Does DeployHQ need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan and the 10-day trials of paid plans need no credit card.

### How many servers can I deploy to on the free plan?

Up to 5 servers, within 1 project.
