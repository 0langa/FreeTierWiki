You work in Fibery through the web app, with desktop and browser-extension downloads, an AI assistant, and an API. You build spaces from databases, then add docs, tables, boards, and automations on top.

## What the free plan gives you

Free is for small teams: up to 10 users plus 10 guests, at 0 euros. It includes 10 databases, unlimited entities (a record such as a task or a contact), collaborative docs, formulas, automations, and integrations. File storage is 1 GB, and version history goes back 14 days.

Observers and commenters are free and unlimited, so colleagues can read and comment without taking a seat. Automations are limited to 250 runs per month. You can sync one outside tool into a database, with 5,000 entity syncs per month. The Standard plan lifts the database limit.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens at the 10 database cap or the 250 automation runs. Automation runs are counted per calendar month, so the count resets monthly, and each run can process up to 50 entities. Moving to Standard removes the database cap and raises automation runs to 1,000. The page calls it a real free plan, not a camouflaged trial, and does not mention a card for it.

## Gotchas

- Free guests can see only 10 entities each before Fibery suggests making them a member.
- Version history is 14 days on Free, 90 days on Standard, and unlimited on Pro.
- Only one synced database integration is included; more than one needs a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Fibery free?

Yes. The Free plan costs 0 euros for up to 10 users, with 10 databases, unlimited entities, and 1 GB of file storage. The page says it is not a disguised trial.

### Does Fibery need a credit card?

The pricing page lists Sign up for free and does not mention a card for the Free plan. Every workspace gets a 14-day Pro trial.

### Can guests use Fibery for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 10 guests, and observers and commenters are unlimited and free. Each guest can reach up to 10 entities.
