Forward Email is an open-source email service for custom domains. On the free plan it receives mail at your domain and forwards it to the inbox you already read, to several recipients, or to a webhook in your app.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and covers unlimited domains. It forwards your domain's mail with unlimited inbound email and spam protection, and it has a 50 MB attachment limit.

Forwarding rules can be flexible: catch-all addresses, multiple recipients per alias, regular expression filtering, and delivery to webhooks are all on the free plan. The plan also supports Send Mail As with Gmail, so you can reply from Gmail through its own sending. Every tier, the free one included, has unlimited domains, so one free account can serve several side projects.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan's limit is a feature line, not a count. The pricing FAQ says the free plan only supports email forwarding: you can receive mail at your domain and forward it, but you cannot send emails directly from your custom domain or store emails on Forward Email's servers.

Sending, storage, and the developer API start on Enhanced Protection at $3 a month, which adds outbound SMTP, 10 GB of pooled storage, and API access. The pricing page does not describe a monthly volume cap for free forwarding.

## Gotchas

- You cannot send mail from your domain on the free plan, so it does not replace a transactional email service.
- Attachments over 50 MB are past the free plan's limit.
- Developer API access is a paid feature, even though webhook forwarding is free.

## Questions people ask

### Is Forward Email free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 a month for unlimited domains and forwards incoming mail.

### Can I send email with the Forward Email free plan?

No. The FAQ says the free plan only forwards mail and cannot send it or store it.

### Can Forward Email send incoming mail to my app?

Yes. Sending emails to webhooks is listed on the free plan.
