Forward Email is an open-source email service for custom domains. On the free plan it receives mail at your domain and forwards it to the inbox you already read, to several recipients, or to a webhook in your app.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and covers unlimited domains. It forwards your domain's mail with unlimited inbound email and spam protection, and it has a 50 MB total message limit, including content, headers, and attachments.

Forwarding rules can be flexible: catch-all addresses, multiple recipients per alias, regular expression filtering, and delivery to webhooks are all on the free plan. The pricing table still lists Send Mail As with Gmail. Forward Email's help center marks the legacy Gmail setup deprecated. Every tier, the free one included, has unlimited domains, so one free account can serve several side projects.

## When you hit a limit

Free only forwards incoming domain email. It cannot send directly from your custom domain or store mail on Forward Email servers.

Enhanced Protection starts at $3/month with outbound SMTP, 10 GB pooled storage and developer API access. Pricing lists no monthly recipient cap. Forwarding differs from paid IMAP trust-tier limits: non-allowlisted senders get 100 connections/hour per sender/envelope recipient, then SMTP 421 responses. Messages above 50 MB total size are rejected. A universal Free card requirement is not established by either reviewed page. Keep forwarding, mailbox storage and sending requirements separate when deciding whether Free is sufficient.

## Gotchas

- Free forwarding is limited to the domain extensions listed in the FAQ. Domains that expired within 90 days or have a pending registrar transfer, update, or deletion require a paid plan. Newly registered names can be free, but prior use, a registrar transfer, drop-catching, certain extensions, or abuse classifications can require payment for the first 90 days. From December 1, 2026, Forward Email says new .id, .jp, and .ws domains require a paid plan. You cannot send mail from your domain on the free plan, so it does not replace a transactional email service. Developer API access is paid, even though webhook forwarding is free.
- The 50 MB limit covers the whole message, not just its attachment. Receiving providers may impose lower limits.
- Free forwarding destinations are stored in public DNS TXT records and are publicly searchable. Do not use this setup expecting to keep the destination inbox private.

## Questions people ask

### Is Forward Email free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 a month for unlimited domains and forwards incoming mail.

### Can I send email with the Forward Email free plan?

No. The FAQ says the free plan only forwards mail and cannot send it or store it.

### Can Forward Email send incoming mail to my app?

Yes. Sending emails to webhooks is listed on the free plan.
