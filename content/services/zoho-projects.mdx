You work in the Zoho Projects web app or in the iOS and Android apps. Projects hold tasks, and the product page says free plan users can also schedule work with Gantt charts.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is for up to 5 users and covers 3 projects. You get subtasks, a whiteboard, 5 GB of storage, and iOS and Android apps. It also includes 50 workflow action runs per month.

Integration with Google Apps and Office 365 is listed, and so are a Zoho MCP server and Zia AI features such as summaries, translation, and task creation. The comparison table marks time tracking, project templates, custom views, blueprints, budgeting, and the workload report as not available.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a bill or a throttle for the free plan. It stops at the plan limits. Unlimited projects, custom views and statuses, and more storage come with the paid plans.

Zoho offers a 15-day trial of the paid plans with no credit card. The FAQ says that if you downgrade to a free plan, your data is not lost, but you lose access to features that only the higher plan has.

## Gotchas

- The plan table lists 3 projects, but the downgrade FAQ says you keep access to two projects with limited functionality. Check your portal after a downgrade.
- Time tracking and invoices through Zoho Invoice appear on the product page, but the comparison table marks time tracking as not on the free plan.
- Read-only users are not available on the free plan, so you cannot add viewers beyond the 5 users.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Projects free?

Yes. The free plan covers up to 5 users and 3 projects, with 5 GB of storage and iOS and Android apps.

### Does Zoho Projects need a credit card?

No. Zoho says it does not ask for credit card details when you sign up, and you start on a free plan.

### What happens if I downgrade Zoho Projects to the free plan?

Your data is not lost, but you cannot use features that only the higher plan has. The FAQ says you keep access to two projects with limited functionality.
