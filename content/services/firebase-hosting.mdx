You use the Firebase CLI to initialize a project folder and deploy it, and you can manage sites and domains in the Firebase console. It suits developers who want to publish a front end from the command line.

## What the free plan gives you

Without the Blaze plan, Hosting is free within quotas. The pricing page lists 10 GB of storage and 360 MB per day of data transfer, which is about 10 GB per month. The quota docs say the quota is shared across the whole project, not per site or channel. A single file may be up to 2 GB. Custom domains and SSL are listed on the pricing page, and each site is backed by a global CDN at no charge.

Hosting storage is separate from other Firebase storage, such as Cloud Storage or database storage. After `firebase deploy --only hosting`, your site is live on two Firebase subdomains, PROJECT_ID.web.app and PROJECT_ID.firebaseapp.com.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan has hard stops. If you are not on Blaze and your storage reaches 10 GB, you cannot deploy new content until you delete older versions or upgrade. If you reach the 10 GB monthly data transfer limit, you get a short grace period and then your sites are disabled until the start of the next month. You can reenable them at once by upgrading to Blaze.

On Blaze, you pay for extra use instead: $0.15 for each extra GB of transfer and $0.026 for each extra GB of storage. Budget alerts only send alerts. The docs say they do not limit your usage or costs.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page shows the daily transfer amount, and the quota docs show 10 GB per month, so a traffic spike on one day can matter.
- Budget alerts on Blaze do not cap spending; they only notify you.
- If the CLI detects a framework such as Next.js or Angular Universal, it may recommend Firebase App Hosting instead.

## Questions people ask

### Is Firebase Hosting free?

Yes, within quotas. The free amounts are 10 GB of storage and about 10 GB of data transfer each month.

### What happens when I exceed the Firebase Hosting free limit?

Without the Blaze plan, your sites are disabled after a short grace period until the next month, or you cannot deploy once storage is full. On Blaze, extra use is billed.

### Does Firebase Hosting need a credit card?

Not for the no-cost quotas. Going past those amounts needs the paid Blaze plan.
