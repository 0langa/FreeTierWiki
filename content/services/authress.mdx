You configure Authress in its management portal and call it through a REST API or SDKs from your own code. It suits teams that want identity and permissions kept apart from their business logic.

## What the free plan gives you

Authress has no fixed free plan. It charges per call, and the first 1,000 billable calls each month are free. Only authentication and authorization calls are billable. Everything else, including configuration, is free.

The pricing section also lists unlimited SSO connections, unlimited social login connections, and unlimited machine tokens. There is no enterprise tax, so you get all features out of the box. Every customer gets default SLAs, with a 99.995% availability SLA for the default regions. Developer support queries get a response within 24h. A dashboard shows your current and projected spend.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops at 1,000 calls. After the first 1,000 billable calls in a month, each call costs 0.0012 USD, billed monthly. The site states no cap on the monthly total, so a busy app can pay more as its traffic grows.

The dashboard shows current and projected spend, so you can watch the bill during the month. The pricing section does not say whether you must add a payment method before you pass the free calls, so check this in the portal before you launch.

## Gotchas

- There is no spending cap. The site states none, so cost grows with the number of authorization checks your app makes.
- Authorization checks count as billable calls. An app that checks permissions on every request uses the free calls quickly.
- The pricing information lives on the home page, not on a separate pricing page. Read it there before you plan costs.

## Questions people ask

### Is Authress free?

Partly. The first 1,000 billable calls each month are free, and configuration, tenants, and applications cost nothing. After that, each call costs 0.0012 USD.

### How does Authress charge?

It charges per authentication or authorization call, billed monthly. Everything else is free, and a dashboard shows current and projected spend.

### Does Authress have an SLA?

Yes. The site lists default SLAs for all customers, with a 99.995% availability SLA for the default regions.
