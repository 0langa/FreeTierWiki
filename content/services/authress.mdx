Authress provides login and fine-grained access control through a portal, SDKs, and REST APIs. Applications delegate identity and permission decisions to it instead of keeping those rules inside business logic. Pricing depends on billable calls, so evaluate actual routes rather than only user counts.

## What the free plan gives you

The homepage advertises the first 1,000 billable calls free and a base price of $0.0012 per call, billed monthly. It does not explicitly state when the free counter resets. Unlimited SSO connections, social-login connections, and machine tokens are listed, with configuration generally free.

The detailed billing guide is more specific than the homepage's broad authentication/authorization wording. Token routes, user/resource queries, public-key verification, and some administrative routes are billable too. Password-based login adds $0.011 per request; other authentication mechanisms use the standard account rate. Review the route table when estimating your own integration. Current and projected spend appear in the dashboard.

## When you hit a limit

Beyond the advertised free calls, billable API usage incurs the applicable per-call rate. The reviewed sources do not establish a monthly spending ceiling or explicitly confirm the card requirement and payment transition, so do not assume either a universal no-card offer or an automatic stop before charges.

Operational rate limits are separate. Limited routes return HTTP 429, and the default account ceiling is 1,000 requests per second. Individual routes can be much lower. SDK caching and backoff help with those limits but do not create a spending cap.

## Gotchas

- Some queries and administrative calls are billable; a homepage claim that everything except auth is free does not replace the detailed route table.
- Password authentication has a separate $0.011 surcharge per login request beyond the normal rate.
- The homepage quotes 99.995% availability, while billing docs give route-specific 99.999% and 99.99% terms. Confirm the applicable SLA rather than assuming one uniform guarantee.

## Questions people ask

### Is Authress free?

The homepage advertises the first 1,000 billable calls free, followed by per-call pricing. It does not explicitly publish the free counter's reset period.

### What calls are charged?

Use the official billing-route table: authentication, authorization, token, selected query, and some administrative calls are charged. Password-based login has an additional surcharge.

### Does Authress provide an SLA?

Yes, but the homepage and route-specific billing documentation use different figures. Confirm the terms for the routes and regions your application uses.
