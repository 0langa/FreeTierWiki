Azure AI Document Intelligence provides AI-powered OCR and form recognition to extract structured data from documents, supporting automated workflows and digitization. The free (F0) tier offers 500 pages per month across Read, Layout, and prebuilt/custom models, making it suitable for small to medium workloads. Moving past that requires the paid S0 tier, and processing is cloud-based, so consider compliance and data residency requirements.
