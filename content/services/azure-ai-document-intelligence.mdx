You call it through the REST API, a client SDK, or Document Intelligence Studio. It suits developers who automate invoices, receipts, and forms.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free (F0) tier covers 0 to 500 pages per month. The pricing page lists one pool of pages for everything: Read, Layout, the prebuilt models (invoice, receipt, ID, tax forms, health insurance card, contract), and custom models. The pricing page says training a custom template model is free, and training a custom neural model is free for the first 10 hours.

The F0 quotas are small. You get 1 analyze request per second. The maximum document size is 4 MB. The free tier limits the analyze response to the first 2 pages of each request. You can create up to 500 template models and 100 neural models.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 500 pages. The footnote says the free tier does not support premium features and does not include the Query Meter. The paid S0 tier raises the limits, for example to 2,000 pages per analysis and 500 MB per document. Its page prices appear as blank dashes, so check the Azure pricing calculator.

Free usage and paid usage are separate tiers, so you must pick S0 on purpose. Nothing on the pages says F0 moves up on its own.

## Gotchas

- The F0 analyze response covers only the first 2 pages of a request, even if your file is longer.
- The max document size is 4 MB on F0, so scanned multi-page files may not fit.
- The Layout model is needed to make label files for custom training. If you have no label files, the service makes them and bills you for Layout use.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Document Intelligence free?

Partly. The F0 tier gives you 500 pages per month across Read, Layout, prebuilt, and custom models. Training a custom template model is free, and neural model training is free for the first 10 hours.

### What is the page limit per document on the free tier?

The free tier limits analysis to the first 2 pages of each request. It also caps document size at 4 MB.

### Does Azure AI Document Intelligence need a credit card?

Yes. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.
