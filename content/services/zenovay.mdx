Zenovay collects website analytics and shows them in a dashboard. A website owner can use it to inspect traffic and a desktop heatmap from the same account. The free plan is geared toward a hobby site or a small team trying the service.

## What the free plan gives you

The published Free plan covers one website, two team members, and 10,000 events per month. Its feature matrix puts a second limit of 350 events per day on Free. The dashboard supports real-time traffic metrics and period comparisons, and the free plan also includes a desktop heatmap and uptime monitoring. Start with the tracking script and see which events are reaching the dashboard. If you add more event sources for a launch or campaign, check how those change your volume. Two team members can be enough if one person maintains the site and another reviews traffic.

## When you hit a limit

A daily spike can hit the daily limit at the beginning of a quiet month. Check both numbers when deciding whether the plan suits you. Paid plans are advertised for more events and more websites. The public pricing matrix lists the allowances but does not explain exactly how excess free events are handled or whether a signup payment method is requested.

## Gotchas

- A heatmap gives a view of recorded interactions. Compare it with traffic volume before drawing conclusions from a small sample. The desktop heatmap keeps 30 days of history, with automatic page snapshots every 30 days and no manual refresh allowance.
- Underlying analytics data is retained for one year, but Free dashboard reports show 30 days. Revenue attribution shows 14 days and Core Web Vitals shows the last 24 hours. A second unrelated project needs a plan that supports more websites or a different analytics setup.
- Free excludes session replay, error tracking, funnels, goals, report exports, API access and webhooks. Google Search Console is included; chart annotations are limited to 10 per month.

## Questions people ask

### How much traffic can Zenovay track on Free?

It lists 10,000 events per month and 350 per day. Actual traffic capacity depends on how many events each visit generates.

### Can I monitor several sites?

The Free plan allows one website. Plans above Free list higher site limits.

### Can a teammate see the data?

The pricing page lists two team members for Free.
