You build in a web dashboard and reach your project through Supabase client libraries or its generated APIs. This page covers the Free plan.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. Each project gets its own Postgres database with 500 MB of database size, shared CPU, and 500 MB of RAM. You also get 1 GB of file storage, 5 GB of egress, and 5 GB of cached egress. Auth covers 50,000 monthly active users. Realtime allows 200 concurrent peak connections and 2 million messages per month. Edge Functions include 500,000 invocations. API requests are unlimited, and so are team members.

You can run two free projects. That limit counts across every organization where you are an Owner or Administrator. Logs are kept for 1 day, and the largest file you can upload is 50 MB.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan does not bill you. Supabase's cost-control guide says you are not charged while you are on the Free plan. If you keep going over a quota, Supabase can restrict your services under its Fair Use Policy. To get more, you upgrade the whole organization to Pro, which starts at $25 per month and includes $10 per month in compute credits. The upgrade covers every project in that organization.

Inactivity is the other limit. A free project that sees no activity for 1 week is paused. You restore it from the dashboard.

## Gotchas

- Free projects pause after 1 week of inactivity. You have 1 year to restore a paused project with one click; after that you can only download its backup.
- The two-project limit is per person, not per organization. Paused projects do not count toward it.
- Automatic backups, point-in-time recovery, branching, and custom domains are not included in the Free plan. One organization cannot mix Free and paid projects.

## Questions people ask

### Is Supabase free forever?

The Free plan costs $0 per month and the pricing page shows no end date. The catch is inactivity: a free project pauses after 1 week without activity.

### What happens when a free Supabase project is paused?

It stops until you restore it from the dashboard. The one-click restore works for 1 year. After that, you can still download the backup file and your Storage objects.

### Can I pay for extra usage on the Supabase Free plan?

No. Paying for usage beyond the quota needs a paid plan. On the Free plan you are not charged, and a project that keeps exceeding its quota can be restricted.
