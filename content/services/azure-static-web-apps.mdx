You connect a GitHub repository, and a GitHub Actions workflow builds and deploys your site. The quickstart uses the Azure Static Web Apps extension in Visual Studio Code, so it suits developers who already work in that editor. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for hobby and personal projects. It costs nothing and includes 100 GB of bandwidth per subscription each month. You can have 10 apps in a subscription, with 2 custom domains per app and free SSL certificates. Each app gets 500 MB of total storage across all environments, with 250 MB for a single environment. It also allows 3 preview environments, 15,000 files, and a request size of 30 MB.

The pricing page says you can add serverless APIs with Azure Functions and gives 1 million free executions. The quotas page lists custom-role authorization through invitations. The Standard plan raises storage and domain counts, adds an SLA, and is billed per hour.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ is direct about this. If a Free plan site goes over the 100 GB quota, Azure cannot serve your site. There is no overage billing on Free, because overage bandwidth is listed as unavailable. Standard apps that pass the quota are billed at overage rates instead.

To get past the Free caps, upgrade to the Standard plan. The pricing page links a guide for the upgrade. Storage limits and the file count are quotas for each app, so a larger site can also hit those before it hits the bandwidth cap.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has no SLA. Microsoft describes it as intended for hobby and personal use.
- GitHub Actions and Packages store your build artifacts, and they follow their own GitHub storage quotas.
- The quickstart asks you to sign in to both Azure and GitHub, and GitHub may ask you to enable Actions on the repository.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Static Web Apps free?

Yes, the Free plan costs nothing. It includes 100 GB of bandwidth per subscription each month and up to 10 apps.

### What happens when I go over the Azure Static Web Apps free quota?

On the Free plan, Azure cannot serve your site once it passes the 100 GB quota. Standard apps are billed at overage rates instead.

### Can I use a custom domain on Azure Static Web Apps free?

Yes. The Free plan allows 2 custom domains per app, with free SSL certificates.
