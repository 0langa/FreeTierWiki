You add an SDK to your app, send errors with an access token, and read the results in the Rollbar web app. SDKs cover server, client, and mobile code, and there is a REST API and a Terraform provider. It suits developers who want error tracking in a few minutes.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and includes 5,000 occurrences and 1,000 session replays per month. It has unlimited users and unlimited projects. It also includes deploy and version tracking, 1 service link per project, and the REST API with a Terraform provider.

Included credits are 0 on Free, so credit-based analysis features are not part of the plan. The paid Essentials plan adds 4K credits, data retention up to 90 days, and fine grained rate limiting. Rollbar also offers a 14-day full access trial, which includes 200K occurrences, 10K replays, and 15K credits.

## When you hit a limit

Rollbar gives three ways to handle overages. The first is on by default: it stops processing new events when you reach the plan's monthly limit. The pricing page says this means no surprise charges, just a pause until your account renews.

The other options let you keep sending events beyond the limit and pay for them. The pricing page lists overage budgets with on-demand occurrences and sessions, and the page does not show them as part of the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- Events over the monthly limit are not processed until renewal, so errors during that time are not recorded.
- Free has 0 credits, so features that run on credits need a paid plan or the trial.
- Replays are limited to 1,000 a month, separate from the 5,000 occurrences.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rollbar free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 per month with 5,000 occurrences, 1,000 session replays, unlimited users, and unlimited projects.

### What happens when I go over the Rollbar free limit?

By default Rollbar stops processing new events when you reach the monthly limit and resumes when your account renews. You get no surprise charges.

### Does Rollbar have a free trial of paid features?

Yes. Rollbar offers a 14-day full access trial with 200K occurrences, 10K replays, and 15K credits, so you can try the Advanced feature set.
