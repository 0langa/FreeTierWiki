You set it up in the Cloudflare dashboard under Zero Trust, with a guided onboarding for four use cases. You can also use the API and Terraform. It suits small teams that want to secure internal apps and web traffic without running their own VPN.

## What the free plan gives you

Cloudflare launched the Free plan to protect up to 50 users at no cost. The launch post says it brings Zero Trust security to internal and SaaS applications and works with identity providers such as Azure AD and GitHub. It also includes DNS content and security filtering for multiple network locations, with 24 hour log retention.

The account limits page shows what the Free plan can run for Digital Experience Monitoring: 10 tests per account and 100 remote captures per day. Other account limits, such as 500 Access applications, apply to every plan unless Enterprise raises them.

Cloudflare's pricing page for Zero Trust currently shows no plan table. It only says to start a proof of concept with the free plan.

## When you hit a limit

The seat management docs say a user takes one seat the first time they log in to Access or connect a device through Gateway. Once all seats are used, additional users who try to log in are blocked. You can remove a user to free a seat, and inactive users do not count toward billing.

Seat expiration can remove users who have not logged in for a period between one month and one year. The docs do not describe an overage bill for the Free plan. To add seats you change your subscription in Zero Trust settings.

## Gotchas

- Onboarding asks you to choose a plan and enter payment details. The setup page says that on the Free plan this step is still needed but you will not be charged.
- The 50-user figure comes from Cloudflare's launch announcement. The current pricing page does not repeat it, so check the plan in your dashboard.
- Removing a user frees the seat but does not stop them signing in. You must change your policies or identity provider for that.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Zero Trust free?

Yes, there is a Free plan. Cloudflare's launch post says it protects up to 50 users at no cost. Setup still asks for payment details, but the docs say you are not charged on Free.

### Does Cloudflare Zero Trust need a credit card?

The setup guide says you must select a plan and enter payment details to finish onboarding. If you chose the Free plan, you will not be charged.

### What happens when I go over 50 users on Cloudflare Zero Trust?

Once all seats are used, additional users who try to log in are blocked. You can remove inactive users to free seats, or buy more seats in your subscription settings.
