You create the VM on the Create an instance page of the Google Cloud console, or with the gcloud compute instances create command. After that you connect to it like any Linux server. It suits learners and hobbyists who want one small always-on machine for tests, bots, or a personal project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Compute Engine Free Tier covers 1 non-preemptible e2-micro VM instance per month. The instance must run in one of three US regions: Oregon (us-west1), Iowa (us-central1), or South Carolina (us-east1). The offer also includes 30 GB-months of standard persistent disk.

You also get 1 GB of outbound data transfer from North America to all region destinations, excluding China and Australia, each month. The limit is by time, not by instance. Eligible use of all your e2-micro instances is free until the hours you use equal the total hours in the current month. Usage is combined across the supported regions.

## When you hit a limit

Google bills anything past the free amounts at standard rates. That includes a second instance running at the same time, a different machine type, a region outside the three listed, extra disk, and extra outbound data. GPUs and TPUs are never part of the Free Tier, so you are always charged for them.

The Free Tier page says there is no cost while you stay within the monthly limits. It also says a Paid billing account is billed for overage, and a Free Trial account uses its $300 welcome credit.

## Gotchas

- The free e2-micro must be non-preemptible and must run in us-west1, us-central1, or us-east1. A VM in another region is billed.
- The free hours are shared across all your e2-micro instances, so two instances running for the whole month use up the allowance in half the time.
- Google can change or end Free Tier limits with 30 days of advance notice, and limits are calculated per billing account.

## Questions people ask

### Is Compute Engine e2-micro free?

Yes, 1 non-preemptible e2-micro VM instance per month in Oregon, Iowa, or South Carolina is free. You also get 30 GB-months of standard persistent disk.

### Does the Google Cloud free VM need a credit card?

You need a Cloud Billing account to use the Free Tier. New customers can start a Free Trial with $300 in credit to get one.

### What happens when I go over the e2-micro free limit?

Google bills the extra usage at standard rates. That includes extra hours, other regions, other machine types, and GPUs or TPUs.
