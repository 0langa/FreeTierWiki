You use JWT.io in the browser. There is no sign-up, dashboard, or API; you paste a token and the page decodes it. It suits developers who are debugging tokens or learning how they are built.

## What the free plan gives you

JWT.io has no plans or prices. The Debugger decodes a pasted token into its header, payload, and a claims breakdown. It also checks the signature when you enter the secret or a public key, and it has an encoder tab for building tokens.

The Introduction page explains the standard in RFC 7519, the three parts of a token (header, payload, and signature), and when to use JWTs. The Libraries page lists libraries for signing and verifying tokens, with checks for claims such as iss, sub, aud, exp, nbf, and iat. You can also download the JWT Handbook for free.

## When you hit a limit

The site lists no usage limits, no quotas, and no paid tier, so there is nothing to hit or pay for. It is a web page for manual work, and it offers no API to call from your own code.

For tokens in production, use one of the libraries from the Libraries page inside your application instead. The home page also points to Auth0, which presents the site, if you want a hosted login service.

## Gotchas

- The page is for debugging by hand. Validate tokens in your own code for anything automated.
- Treat live production tokens and signing secrets carefully, and prefer sample tokens when you test on any website.
- The site is presented by Auth0, so you will see prompts to create an Auth0 account. Your cookie and sharing choices are on the privacy settings link.

## Questions people ask

### Is JWT.io free?

Yes. The Debugger and the Introduction and Libraries pages are free to use, and the site needs no account. The JWT Handbook is a free download.

### Does JWT.io have an API?

The site lists none. It is a page for manual use, and it points to libraries when you need to sign or verify tokens in your own code.

### Can JWT.io verify a token signature?

Yes. Enter the secret or public key in the signature verification section, and the page shows whether the signature is valid.
