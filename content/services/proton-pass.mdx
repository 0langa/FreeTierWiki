You use Proton Pass through a browser extension, a desktop or phone app, or the web app. Autofill fills in your logins, and a built-in generator makes new passwords. It suits individuals who want their password vault available on every device.

## What the free plan gives you

Proton Free costs $0, and the pricing page says no credit card is required. You get unlimited logins, notes, and credit cards on unlimited devices. It includes a password generator, weak/reused-password alerts, passkeys, easy import, and up to three 2FA items, according to the plan guide. You also get 10 hide-my-email aliases. These are addresses that forward mail to your inbox while hiding your real one.

On Free you can create and share up to two vaults, with up to three people per vault including yourself. You choose whether each person can view, edit, or manage the items.

## When you hit a limit

Free has fixed caps and no bill: 10 aliases, two shared vaults, three people per shared vault, and three 2FA items. The plan guide says downgrading leaves codes visible only for the first three 2FA items by creation date. Exact alias/vault over-limit behavior is not described. Any paid plan raises sharing to 50 vaults with 10 people per vault. Pass Plus also lists unlimited aliases, unlimited 2FA codes, file attachments, dark web monitoring, emergency access, and a command line tool.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page lists "Secure vault sharing" as a Pass Plus feature, but the support page allows limited sharing on Free. Check the app before you plan around sharing.
- Aliases are limited to 10 on Free. Custom domains for aliases need a paid plan.
- The marketing page highlights the authenticator under Plus, but the plan guide grants three 2FA items on Free. Unlimited codes require Plus.

## Questions people ask

### Is Proton Pass free?

Yes. The Free plan has unlimited logins, notes, and credit cards on unlimited devices. It includes 10 hide-my-email aliases.

### Can I share passwords with Proton Pass for free?

Yes, within limits. Free lets you create and share up to two vaults, with up to three people per vault including you.

### Does Proton Pass need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan needs no credit card.
