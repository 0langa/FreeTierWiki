You use Proton Pass through a browser extension, a desktop or phone app, or the web app. Autofill fills in your logins, and a built-in generator makes new passwords. It suits individuals who want one free vault on every device.

## What the free plan gives you

Proton Free costs $0, and the pricing page says no credit card is required. You get unlimited logins, notes, and credit cards on unlimited devices. The plan includes a password generator, alerts for weak and reused passwords, passkey support on all devices, and easy password import. You also get 10 hide-my-email aliases. These are addresses that forward mail to your inbox while hiding your real one.

On Free you can create and share up to two vaults, with up to three people per vault including yourself. You choose whether each person can view, edit, or manage the items.

## When you hit a limit

Free has fixed caps and no bill: 10 aliases, two shared vaults, and three people per shared vault. The pages do not say what the app does when you try to go past a cap. Any paid plan raises sharing to 50 vaults with 10 people per vault. Pass Plus also lists unlimited aliases, a built-in 2FA authenticator, file attachments, dark web monitoring, emergency access, and a command line tool.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page lists "Secure vault sharing" as a Pass Plus feature, but the support page allows limited sharing on Free. Check the app before you plan around sharing.
- Aliases are limited to 10 on Free. Custom domains for aliases need a paid plan.
- The built-in 2FA authenticator is listed under Pass Plus, not under the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Proton Pass free?

Yes. The Free plan has unlimited logins, notes, and credit cards on unlimited devices. It includes 10 hide-my-email aliases.

### Can I share passwords with Proton Pass for free?

Yes, within limits. Free lets you create and share up to two vaults, with up to three people per vault including you.

### Does Proton Pass need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan needs no credit card.
