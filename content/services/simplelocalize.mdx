SimpleLocalize manages the translations for an app. You import translation files, translate in a web editor or with machine and AI translation, and sync the results back with a CLI or serve them from its translation hosting CDN. It suits developers who want translation files out of the codebase and into a shared editor.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Community plan, described as a free plan for small projects at $0 for ever. It includes 500 translation keys, 1 team member, unlimited strings, and unlimited contributors. The comparison table adds 10 projects, 10 languages, and a 90-day activity and history log, and the plan card lists crowd-sourced translations, AI Actions, translation hosting, and auto-translation.

One translation key can hold its translations in many languages, so 500 keys covers the same text in each of your languages. Paid plans start with Developer at $12 a month billed yearly for 1,000 keys and 5 team members.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Community project reaches 500 translation keys. Paid plans can add keys in blocks of 500 for $5.00 each. All plans are subject to a Fair Use Policy, which the page says sets generous limits most users never reach.

The page also offers a 14-day trial of paid plans with no credit card required.

## Gotchas

- Only one team member is included; translators can join as contributors, but a second developer needs a paid seat.
- The table gives auto-translation character amounts only for paid plans, so check what machine translation the free plan actually allows before relying on it.
- History is kept for 90 days on the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is SimpleLocalize free?

Yes. The Community plan is free for small projects with 500 translation keys and 1 team member.

### What counts as a translation key?

One key is a piece of text with its translations into many languages, so you do not pay per language.

### Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The pricing page says you can start for free with no credit card required.
