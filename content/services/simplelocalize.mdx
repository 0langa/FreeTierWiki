SimpleLocalize manages the translations for an app. You import translation files, translate in a web editor or with machine and AI translation, and sync the results back with a CLI or serve them from its translation hosting CDN. It suits developers who want translation files out of the codebase and into a shared editor.

## What the free plan gives you

The rendered pricing card lists Community as free forever, alongside the comparison table and free-plan documentation. It includes 500 translation keys, 1 team member, unlimited strings, and unlimited contributors. The comparison table adds 10 projects, 10 languages and a 90-day activity and history log. The free plan also has API access under fair use, and the CLI syncs local translation files.

One translation key can hold its translations in many languages, so 500 keys covers the same text in each of your languages. The Developer plan raises the allocation to 2,000 keys and 5 team members. It costs €15 per month, or €12 per month billed yearly, excluding applicable taxes.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says exceeding the translation-key limit triggers an email and pauses new key creation, importing and exporting. Existing data is not deleted. Remove unused keys or move to a bigger plan to resume; extra key add-ons are offered on paid plans. All plans are subject to a Fair Use Policy, which the page says sets generous limits most users never reach.

New users start with a no-card 14-day trial, then automatically downgrade to free Community. Fair-use violations prompt email and an opportunity to adjust; ongoing or severe abuse can lead to throttling, restricted features or suspension.

## Gotchas

- Only one team member is included. Invited translators, reviewers, admins and pending invitations count toward seats; people who only suggest translations through Community are unlimited contributors.
- The table gives auto-translation character amounts only for paid plans, so check what machine translation the free plan actually allows before relying on it.
- History is kept for 90 days on the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is SimpleLocalize free?

Yes. The Community plan is free for small projects with 500 translation keys and 1 team member.

### What counts as a translation key?

One key is a piece of text with its translations into many languages, so you do not pay per language.

### Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The pricing page says you can start for free with no credit card required.
