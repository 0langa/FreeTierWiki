You manage deployments in the Cloud-IAM console, then use the Keycloak admin console and its standard OIDC, OAuth 2.0, and SAML endpoints from your app. It suits people who want Keycloak without hosting it.

## What the free plan gives you

The Freemium plan gives you one isolated realm on a shared Keycloak cluster. It supports up to 100 users. You get MFA with OTP and WebAuthn, unlimited social identity providers, unlimited integrations with AD and LDAP, and access to the Keycloak API.

The pricing table lists active users as unlimited for Freemium, but Cloud-IAM counts total user accounts, not monthly active users, so the 100 user limit is the one that matters. Support comes from the community and documentation. Paid plans start at 225 euros per month, and they add a dedicated deployment, a custom domain, and an uptime SLA. The docs say the free plan is meant for testing and learning.

## When you hit a limit

The user limit is not a hard stop. The Cloud-IAM FAQ says that if you reach it, the team contacts you to discuss a plan that suits you better, or to suggest reducing your user base.

You can switch to a bigger plan later. The free plan has no built-in export and import, so you ask support to move your data to a new deployment, and your deployment URL changes. The free plan has no SLA, no on-call team, and no backups.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is shared infrastructure. Cloud-IAM does not recommend it for production.
- The pricing table says no to custom extensions (.jar) and to Cloud-IAM REST API usage on the free plan, and it lists only AWS as a cloud provider for Freemium.
- To move to a paid plan, you create a new deployment and ask support to migrate your data. Your deployment URL changes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloud-IAM free?

Yes, with the Freemium plan. It gives you one realm on a shared Keycloak cluster for up to 100 users, and the pages checked mention no credit card.

### What happens when I reach the Cloud-IAM free user limit?

It is not a hard stop. Cloud-IAM contacts you to discuss other plans, or to suggest reducing your user base.

### Can I use the Cloud-IAM free plan in production?

The docs say the Freemium plan is for testing and learning and is not recommended for production. It has no SLA and only community support.
