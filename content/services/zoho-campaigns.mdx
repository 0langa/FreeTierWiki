You work in a web dashboard: set up a sender, import a list, and build emails from templates. It suits people who send marketing mail by hand and do not need developer tools.

## What the free plan gives you

Forever Free accounts created after November 11, 2026 can manage up to 2,000 marketing contacts and send up to 2,000 emails per month. The pricing page lists 5 users, simple prebuilt templates, sign-up forms, and a compliance check. It still shows the older 6,000-email allowance. Zoho's subscription help dates the change and says it applies only to new users after November 11.

New accounts start with a 14-day free trial that needs no credit card. Zoho's pricing FAQ describes the trial as access to all features for up to 2,000 contacts and three campaigns. When it ends, the account moves to Forever Free automatically.

Zoho Campaigns is for marketing email only. For transactional email, Zoho points you to its ZeptoMail service.

## When you hit a limit

On the new Free allowance, Zoho pauses all campaigns and restricts sending when you hit the email limit. Adding contacts beyond 2,000 or sending beyond the allowance requires a paid upgrade.

The Standard plan adds unlimited emails on top of the free features. For more than 100,000 contacts, Zoho tells you to choose the Professional plan. The pricing FAQ describes automatic contact-tier upgrades and charges when a purchased plan exceeds its contact allowance. The subscription guide says Auto-Upgrade is enabled by default and can be viewed in Settings → Subscription; its separate Free section explicitly requires an upgrade and pauses sending, so do not apply the paid automation policy to Free.

## Gotchas

- One campaign to all 2,000 contacts uses the entire new monthly send allowance.
- Authenticate the sender domain before sending campaigns; DNS setup is separate from choosing a free plan.
- Paid contact-tier upgrades have billing consequences; review that policy before purchasing or enabling contact synchronization.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Campaigns free?

Yes. Signups after November 11, 2026 get up to 2,000 marketing contacts and 2,000 emails per month on Forever Free, after a 14-day trial.

### Does Zoho Campaigns need a credit card?

The 14-day trial needs no credit card. Zoho moves the account to Forever Free automatically when the trial ends.

### Can I send transactional emails with Zoho Campaigns?

No. Zoho allows only marketing emails in Zoho Campaigns and recommends ZeptoMail for transactional email.
