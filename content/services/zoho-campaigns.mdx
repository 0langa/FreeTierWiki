You work in a web dashboard: set up a sender, import a list, and build emails from templates. It suits people who send marketing mail by hand and do not need developer tools.

## What the free plan gives you

Zoho Campaigns has three editions: Free, Standard, and Professional. The Forever Free plan lets you manage up to 2,000 contacts and send up to 6,000 emails per month. Zoho explains the math as 2,000 contacts times 3 campaigns. It includes 5 users, sign-up forms, simple prebuilt email templates, and a compliance check.

New accounts start with a 14 day free trial that needs no credit card. The trial opens all features of Zoho Campaigns for up to 2,000 contacts and up to three campaigns. When it ends, the account moves to the Forever Free plan by itself.

Zoho Campaigns is for marketing email only. For transactional email, Zoho points you to its ZeptoMail service.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page shows a label that the contact limit was exceeded on the free plan, but it does not say what happens next. Treat 2,000 contacts as the line for the free plan.

The Standard plan adds unlimited emails on top of the free features. For more than 100,000 contacts, Zoho tells you to choose the Professional plan. The help page says the Feature Availability table shows the limits of each edition, so check it before you plan around a feature.

## Gotchas

- The 6,000 emails per month are tied to your list size and campaign count, so a list of 2,000 contacts gets three campaigns a month.
- Sender setup needs DNS records for SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, and DNS changes can take up to 24 hours.
- Import files can be up to 25 MB, in CSV or XLSX format.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Campaigns free?

Yes. The Forever Free plan covers up to 2,000 contacts and up to 6,000 emails per month. New accounts also get a 14 day trial of all features first.

### Does Zoho Campaigns need a credit card?

The 14 day trial needs no credit card. The pricing page does not mention a card for the Forever Free plan.

### Can I send transactional emails with Zoho Campaigns?

No. Zoho allows only marketing emails in Zoho Campaigns and recommends ZeptoMail for transactional email.
