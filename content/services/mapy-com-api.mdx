The Mapy.com REST API is the developer offering of Mapy.com, the map service run by Seznam.cz. It provides map tiles, address search and reverse geocoding, route planning, matrix routing, static maps and panoramas, elevation, and time zones. Every call costs credits, and every project gets a free credit allowance each month.

## What the free plan gives you

You get the Basic tariff automatically when you register a project, with 250,000 free credits every month for testing or running smaller projects. Projects that are publicly and freely accessible to all users can apply for the Extended tariff, with 10,000,000 free credits a month. Credit costs per call are 1 credit per map tile, 4 credits for an address search such as geocoding, reverse geocoding, or autocomplete, 4 credits for route planning, 40 credits for a 10×10 matrix, 4 credits for a static map, and 1 credit for a time zone call. Above the free credits, the price is 1.60 CZK per 1,000 credits, with automatic discounts for high usage.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ says that if your project consumes all the free credits, you need to activate Paid Consumption and pay for additional credits. Paid use is charged through Seznam Peněženka (Wallet), and billing happens on the first day of the month for the previous month. When you turn paid consumption on, you set a Monthly Consumption Limit, and once it is reached, API consumption stops until the end of the month. If the wallet lacks funds at billing time, API consumption is suspended until the debt is paid.

## Gotchas

- The Extended tariff needs an affidavit renewed once a year and a listing in the Reference Projects Catalogue.
- A map is made of many tiles, and each tile costs a credit, so busy maps use credits faster than search calls.
- Paid consumption needs a Seznam Peněženka (Wallet), which you top up by bank transfer, card, or other methods.

## Questions people ask

### Is the Mapy.com API free?

Yes, up to the free credits. Every project gets 250,000 free credits a month on the Basic tariff.

### How do I get 10 million free credits?

Apply for the Extended tariff. Your project must be publicly and freely accessible and meet the other listed conditions, confirmed by a yearly affidavit.

### What happens when the free credits run out?

You need to activate paid consumption through Seznam Peněženka to keep calling the API; a Monthly Consumption Limit then caps paid use.
