You call Doczilla from your own code with an API key. A direct endpoint returns the file, a sync endpoint returns a URL, and an async endpoint sends the result to your webhook. It suits developers who already work with HTML.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan costs 0 euros a month and needs no credit card. It includes 250 requests per month and 30 requests per minute. The plan table also lists queuing. The pricing page shows one request quota for the plan and does not split PDF and screenshot requests.

The Pro plan costs 4,99 euros a month. It includes 2,000 requests a month and 120 requests per minute. Each extra request costs 0.00372 euros. A higher tier has custom pricing, custom rate limits, and priority support. The pricing page says you pay only for what you use. Direct responses over 32 MiB need the other content type described in the docs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists "Queuing" on the Hobby plan and gives no more detail. It does not say whether requests over 250 a month are refused or held. The homepage FAQ says there are no strict limits, which seems to describe paid use. The Hobby plan needs no credit card. On Pro, extra requests are billed one by one at the listed price. Moving up to Pro is the way to get more room.

## Gotchas

- The plan table shows queuing for Hobby, but the pages do not explain how it works when you pass 250 requests.
- A blank page or missing background in your PDF usually has a simple fix: turn off the adblocker with page.adblocker set to false, or set pdf.printBackground to true.
- Doczilla describes itself as a pay-as-you-go service, so Pro bills per extra request beyond the 2,000 included.

## Questions people ask

### Is Doczilla free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free, needs no credit card, and gives you 250 requests per month at 30 requests per minute.

### What does Doczilla cost after the free plan?

The Pro plan is 4,99 euros a month with 2,000 requests included. Extra requests cost 0.00372 euros each.

### Does Doczilla do screenshots as well as PDFs?

Yes. It has a Screenshot API next to the PDF API. The pricing page shows one request quota and does not split the two.
