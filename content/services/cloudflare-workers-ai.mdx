You call models from a Cloudflare Worker through an AI binding, or through the REST API. It suits developers who already deploy on Cloudflare and want small, capped AI inference without a card.

## What the free plan gives you

The Workers Free plan includes 10,000 Neurons per day at no charge. A Neuron measures the GPU work a request needs. Different models use different amounts, so the number of free requests depends on the model you pick. The pricing page shows each model price in tokens and in Neurons.

Limits reset daily at 00:00 UTC. The service also sets rate limits by task type. Text generation is 300 requests per minute by default, and text embeddings are 3,000 requests per minute. Local mode in Wrangler counts toward these limits too.

Some large models need a paid billing method. The pricing page lists them by name, for example the Kimi, GLM, and DeepSeek V4 models. You can pay for them with Workers Paid or with prepaid AI Gateway credits.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page is direct. If you exceed a limit, further operations fail with an error. On Workers Free there is no upgrade path built in, and the table shows the paid price as not available until you move to Workers Paid.

To go beyond 10,000 Neurons per day, sign up for Workers Paid. It charges $0.011 per 1,000 Neurons above the free allocation. Workers AI is included in both the Free and Paid Workers plans.

## Gotchas

- Neuron cost differs by model, so a large model can use up the daily amount in a few requests.
- Frontier models that use prepaid credits get higher rate limits.
- Some models say on their model page that they need the Workers Paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Workers AI free?

Yes, within a daily cap. The Workers Free plan includes 10,000 Neurons per day, and no card is needed.

### What happens when I exceed the Workers AI free limit?

Further operations fail with an error. Limits reset daily at 00:00 UTC.

### How much does Workers AI cost after the free allocation?

On the Workers Paid plan, usage above the free allocation costs $0.011 per 1,000 Neurons.
