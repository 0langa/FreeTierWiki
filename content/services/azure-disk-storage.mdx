You create and attach managed disks in the Azure portal, with the Azure CLI, or from templates. It suits people who run virtual machines on Azure.

## What the free plan gives you

The entry records two 64 GiB (P6) Premium SSD managed disks, free for 12 months for new Azure free account customers. They are meant to pair with the free virtual machines.

The Azure free page lists the free services and says some are free for 12 months to new customers only. It does not list managed disks, and the Marketplace page behind the original source returned an error. Treat the disk amounts as recorded data, and confirm the exact disk size and redundancy setting in the portal before you deploy.

Azure says managed disks come in five types: Ultra Disks, Premium SSD v2, Premium SSD, Standard SSD, and Standard HDD. Only the Premium SSD of 64 GiB matches this offer.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page could not be read for the free disk amounts, so this section follows the entry's recorded data. Azure's free account page says a new account has a starting credit that you must use within 30 days. When the credit runs out or expires, your subscription and services are disabled until you upgrade.

After you move to pay-as-you-go, you keep the free services for 12 months. Anything beyond the free amounts, any ineligible disk, and any disk still running after 12 months bills at normal rates. The entry rates this risk as high, because there is no hard cap.

## Gotchas

- You must move the free account to pay-as-you-go within 30 days to keep the first-year benefits. Until then, services stop when the credit ends.
- Only certain tiers and sizes are free. A bigger disk, a different disk type, or a third disk is a paid resource.
- The free disks end after 12 months, but a running VM and its disks keep running and keep billing. Delete what you do not use.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Disk Storage free?

Only in part. The entry records two 64 GiB P6 Premium SSD managed disks free for 12 months for new Azure free account customers. After that, disks bill at normal rates.

### Does Azure Disk Storage need a credit card?

Yes. Azure asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card at sign-up. It places a small temporary hold and removes it after verification.

### What happens after the 12 months end?

Your free amounts end. Any disks you still use keep running and are billed at pay-as-you-go rates.
