LoginTC adds two-factor authentication to the systems people log in to at work: VPNs and remote access through RADIUS, firewalls, Active Directory and LDAP, Outlook Web App, and RD Web Access. The second factor is a push approval. It is aimed at IT teams protecting infrastructure logins rather than at sign-in for your own app.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists Cloud Starter at $0, free for 3 users. It protects VPNs and firewalls with push authentication. The FAQ says Cloud Starter is $0 for up to three users and that all plans include unlimited push and customer support.

Paid cloud plans are Professional at $3 per user per month, which adds hardware-based 2FA, live chat, and email support, and Business at $5 per user per month, which adds Windows connectors and all 2FA methods. An On-Premises Managed option is $7 per user per month.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan's limit is the number of users. The pricing page does not say what happens if you add a fourth user to Cloud Starter.

Moving up is a big step. All paid plans have a 15-user minimum, are paid annually, and add licenses in bundles of 5, so going from three free users to a paid plan means paying for at least fifteen. Business tier and above include 100 telephony credits per user for phone and SMS methods.

## Gotchas

- The jump from three free users to a fifteen-user minimum makes the free plan best for a lab or a tiny team.
- Windows logon protection needs the Business plan, not the free one.
- LoginTC protects infrastructure logins; it is not a login SDK for your own web app.

## Questions people ask

### Is LoginTC free?

Yes, for up to three users on Cloud Starter, which costs $0.

### What does the free plan protect?

VPNs and firewalls, using push authentication.

### Is there a trial of the paid plans?

Yes. The pricing page offers a free trial and says no credit card is required.
