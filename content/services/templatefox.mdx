You design a template in a visual editor, then send its ID and your JSON data to the API. Native libraries exist for several languages. Zapier, Make, and n8n work without code.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It gives you 60 PDFs and images per month and 3 templates. One PDF is 1 credit no matter how many pages it has, and images cost the same. The limits per file are 50 pages and 2 MB. The rate limit is 10 requests per minute. The free plan has 1 team member and 1 API key, and keeps usage analytics for 7 days. Support is community support.

You can get the result as a download, a time-limited signed URL, or an upload to your S3 bucket. Starter has 1,000 PDFs per month and 10 templates. Pro has 3,000 PDFs and 25 templates. TemplateFox does not store the data you send. It uses the data to render the file and then discards it.

## When you hit a limit

PDF generation simply stops until your credits reset or you upgrade. There is no automatic overage charge and no surprise bill. Upgrading is instant and prorated. Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle, and unused credits do not roll over. If you go over the per-minute cap, you get a clear error and your credits are not touched, so a short pause clears it. Failed PDFs are refunded automatically, so only successful ones cost a credit.

## Gotchas

- The free plan stops at 50 pages and 2 MB per PDF, so long reports need a paid plan.
- Unused credits do not roll over to the next month.
- Free support is community support. Live support starts on Starter.

## Questions people ask

### Is TemplateFox free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 60 PDFs and images per month and 3 templates, with no credit card.

### What happens when I run out of TemplateFox credits?

PDF generation stops until credits reset or you upgrade. There is no overage charge.

### Does a long PDF cost more on TemplateFox?

No. One PDF is 1 credit regardless of page count. The free plan caps PDFs at 50 pages and 2 MB.
