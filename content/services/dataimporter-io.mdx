You work in the Dataimporter.io web app: you connect a Salesforce org, build a job, and run or schedule it. It suits Salesforce admins who need a data loader and a backup tool.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0. It includes 20,000 records, 1GB of backup, 1 production instance, 1 integration, and community support. The usage table also lists one sandbox org and one schedule. Backups run weekly and are kept for 28 days. Log history covers 30 days.

The pricing page does not say whether the 20,000 records are a monthly or a total allowance. The help center says only that the Free Tier is limited by the number of records you process. It also says you do not need a credit card to register.

The Premium plan lists 150,000 records a month, 10GB of backup, and 2 production instances. It costs $109 per user a month billed annually, down from $129 listed as the monthly price.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 20,000 records. The help center describes the Free Tier as limited only by the number of records you can process, and the plan needs no card. That points to a stop rather than a charge, but the pages do not state it.

If you need more records, a second production org, or more backup space, the next step is a paid plan. The help center also explains that Salesforce itself can return errors during a job run, and it points to an article on the 10 most common ones.

## Gotchas

- The limit is 20,000 records, and the pricing page does not say if it resets each month.
- You pick one of three data regions when you register, and your data stays in that region.
- Paid plans are priced per user per month, so the cost grows with your team.

## Questions people ask

### Is Dataimporter.io free?

Yes, there is a Free plan at $0 with 20,000 records, 1GB of backup, and 1 production instance.

### Does Dataimporter.io need a credit card?

No. The help center says you do not have to enter a credit card when registering.

### Can Dataimporter.io load data into anything other than Salesforce?

No. The product is for Salesforce data loading, migration, and backup. Look at other tools in this atlas for non-Salesforce targets.
