You work with Pinecone through its console, REST API, SDKs for Python, Node.js, Java, and Go, or the CLI. It suits developers who need a hosted vector index for an AI feature and do not want to run one themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free and is meant for trying things out and small applications. It includes dense, sparse, and full-text indexes, Pinecone Inference, Pinecone Assistant, and console metrics. Support comes from the Pinecone community on Discord.

Storage is capped at 2 GB per organization. Each month you get up to 2M write units, 1M read units, and 5M embedding tokens per model. An organization can have 1 project, 2 users, and up to 5 serverless indexes, with up to 100 namespaces per index. Starter indexes must be in the AWS us-east-1 region. Reranking with the `bge-reranker-v2-m3` model is limited to 500 requests.

## When you hit a limit

Pinecone stops the request instead of billing you. When you reach a monthly read or write limit, the API returns a 429 error and asks you to upgrade your plan. When you exceed an object limit, such as the number of projects or indexes, it returns a 403 error.

For the storage limit, you can also delete records to get back under it. The monthly allowances are small, so a busy test can use up your reads early in the month.

## Gotchas

- Starter indexes are only available in AWS us-east-1. Choosing another cloud or region needs the Builder plan or higher.
- Use namespaces to split data inside one index instead of creating many indexes. The Starter plan allows only 5 indexes.
- Backups are not available on Starter. The limits page lists serverless backups only for Standard and Enterprise.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pinecone free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and is for trying out Pinecone and small applications. It includes up to 2 GB of storage and monthly read and write allowances.

### What happens when I go over the Pinecone free tier limits?

Reaching a monthly usage limit returns a 429 error until you upgrade. Exceeding an object limit returns a 403 error.

### Can I choose a region on the Pinecone free plan?

No. On the Starter plan, all serverless indexes must be in the AWS us-east-1 region. Builder and higher plans can pick any supported region.
