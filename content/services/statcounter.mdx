StatCounter is a web tracker and hit counter that shows who visits your site, where they came from, and the paths they take. It also sells session replay recordings as an add-on. It suits developers and site owners who want a plain visitor log rather than a full analytics suite.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan is free forever. It covers 5,000 sessions per month, keeps data for 14 days, and includes traffic trend charts, a reports dashboard, recent activity feeds that track individual visitors in real time, and basic weekly or monthly email reports. Each website visit counts as one session.

Session replay plays back a visual recording of a visit like a video. On Basic it is set to 0 monthly recordings, and recordings are an add-on, for example 1,000 monthly recordings for €9 a month. New accounts can try all StatCounter features free for 30 days with no credit card required, then choose Premium, Premium Plus, or the free Basic plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Basic project records more than 5,000 sessions in a month, whether tracking pauses or extra sessions are dropped. It asks you to select enough sessions per month to cover all of your traffic.

There is no paid overage described for Basic. More sessions come with Premium, which starts at 20,000 sessions per month for €9 a month and adds CSV exports, detailed email reports, and the StatCounter API.

## Gotchas

- Data retention on Basic is 14 days, so detailed history disappears quickly.
- Session replay is not included on Basic unless you buy recordings.
- The 30-day trial shows Premium features that Basic does not keep.

## Questions people ask

### Is StatCounter free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever with 5,000 sessions per month and 14 days of data retention.

### Is there a StatCounter free trial?

Yes. You can try all features free for 30 days with no credit card required.

### Does the free plan include session replay?

No. Basic comes with 0 monthly recordings; recordings are a paid add-on.
