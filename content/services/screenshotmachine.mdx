Screenshot Machine is an API that captures a web page as an image or converts it to a PDF. You build a request with your key and the page URL, and you can hot-link the result straight into a page. It suits directories, link lists, and tools that show the same page thumbnails many times.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Starter plan as free with 100 fresh screenshots monthly. It includes custom error images, unlimited impressions from cache, unlimited traffic, and full-length captures, with basic support.

Only fresh captures count. The FAQ says Screenshot Machine charges only for new, fresh screenshots; website screenshots are cached for 14 days and loading a cached screenshot is not billed. You may download and store captures and hot-link them. The API's cacheLimit parameter, from 0 to 14 days, sets how old a cached image you accept, with 14 as the default.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the free Starter plan uses its 100 fresh screenshots. On paid plans, extra screenshots are billed at the end of each billing period, counted and rounded down to thousands, so 999 or fewer extra screenshots cost nothing beyond the package price.

Repeated requests for a page already in the cache do not count, so the practical way to stretch the free amount is to let captures come from the cache. Keep the default cacheLimit unless you need a fresher image.

## Gotchas

- Setting cacheLimit to 0 forces a fresh capture on every call and uses the free amount fast.
- Paid prices are in euros, and VAT may be added for non-business customers in the EU.
- Hot-linking is allowed, so a popular page that embeds captures can trigger fresh captures whenever the cache expires.

## Questions people ask

### Is Screenshot Machine free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free with 100 fresh screenshots monthly.

### Do cached screenshots count toward the free amount?

No. Loading a cached screenshot is not billed; only new, fresh screenshots count.

### How long are screenshots cached?

For 14 days. You can ask for a fresher capture with the cacheLimit parameter.
