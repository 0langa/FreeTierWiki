You use the Hub in the browser, with git, with the `hf` command-line tool, or from Python with the `huggingface_hub` library. It suits researchers, students, and open-source developers who share models or datasets.

## What the free plan gives you

A free user or organization gets best-effort public storage and 100GB of private storage. The docs say Hugging Face aims to give the AI community generous free space for public repositories. Storage limits apply to all repository types: models, datasets, and buckets. You can create repos with a public or private setting and pick a license.

The pricing page also lists Spaces hardware. CPU Basic with 2 vCPU and 16 GB is free, and ZeroGPU is free. The Hub pages say community GPU grants may be available for side projects with high impact. Pull requests, discussions, and model cards come with every repo.

## When you hit a limit

For public repos, the docs ask you to use storage responsibly beyond the first few gigabytes. They want large uploads to offer value to others, measured by likes or downloads. Hugging Face can limit abusive use. For private storage over 100GB, you need a paid plan. PRO costs $9 per month and lists 2x public storage capacity. The docs list 1TB of private storage per seat on PRO, then $18 per TB per month for more. Storage add-ons for public space start at $12 per TB per month on paid plans.

## Gotchas

- Public storage is "best-effort", not a fixed number, so very large public uploads with little community use may be limited.
- The 100GB private limit covers models, datasets, and buckets together.
- Large repos are harder to manage. The docs suggest structuring big repos carefully and contacting Hugging Face for repos in the terabytes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hugging Face free?

Yes. A free account gets best-effort public storage and 100GB of private storage.

### How much storage do I get on the Hugging Face Hub for free?

You get 100GB of private storage. Public storage is best-effort, with free space aimed at useful community work.

### What happens when I run out of Hugging Face storage?

You need to upgrade to PRO, Team, or Enterprise, or contact Hugging Face for large repos. PRO costs $9 per month.
