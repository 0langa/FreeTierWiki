You use v0 in the browser at v0.app, or on iOS. You chat with it to build a project, then publish through Vercel or GitHub. It fits developers and designers who want to prototype quickly.

## What the free plan gives you

Free costs $0 with Vercel deployment, GitHub sync and 7 daily messages. New users receive $5 once per personal/team account; existing Free users retain $5 monthly. Billing settings identify applicable credits/expiry. Free excludes daily-login credits, shared projects and training-data opt-out.

Plus costs $30/user/month with $30 monthly credits, $2 daily login credits, all models and shared team chats. The $20/month Premium plan is closing and unavailable to new users.

Daily login credits expire at midnight UTC. Monthly credits roll over but expire after 65 days. Purchased credits last one year and require an active paid plan. A one-time Free grant does not renew.

## When you hit a limit

You hit two limits. The Free plan caps you at 7 messages per day. Credits work like a prepaid balance. When credits run out, generation pauses.

You are not billed by surprise. Only Premium, Plus, and Business plans can buy more credits, so a Free account cannot go over. The pages do not say whether the daily message limit resets at a set time. The pages do not mention a credit card for the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- The $5 is one-time for new users. It is not a monthly gift, even though older Free users keep a monthly amount.
- The November 23 pricing table lowered Mini to $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million tokens, Max to $4/$20, and Max Fast to $8/$40. Mini prompts above 272K input tokens use separate higher rates. Cached reads and writes also have their own rates. Credits are spent as tokens. The docs say that chat history, source files, and Vercel knowledge count as input, so short prompts can still use many tokens.
- Connecting ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Business can cover eligible model responses, but platform fees and supporting work still spend v0 credits. It does not remove the need for a v0 credit balance. Reaching the connected ChatGPT limit pauses generation; v0 does not automatically switch those model responses to v0 credits.

## Questions people ask

### Is v0 free?

Yes, with limits. The Free plan has a 7-message daily limit and $5 of credits for new users, once.

### What happens when I run out of v0 credits?

Generation pauses. On Free, you cannot buy more credits, so you need a paid plan to continue.

### Does v0 need a credit card?

The cited pricing pages do not publish an explicit card requirement for Free. A Start Building button alone does not establish that policy.
