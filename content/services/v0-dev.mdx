You use v0 in the browser at v0.app, or on iOS. You chat with it to build a project, then publish through Vercel or GitHub. It fits developers and designers who want to prototype quickly.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It includes free access to v0, deploys to Vercel, and sync with GitHub. Free accounts have a limit of 7 messages per day.

Credits depend on when you joined. New users under the one-time Free credit offer get $5 once per personal or team account. Existing Free users keep a $5 monthly allowance. The one-time credit does not renew. Your billing settings show the allowance that applies to you.

The Plus plan costs $30 per user per month and includes $30 of monthly credits per user. It also adds $2 of free daily credits on login, access to all models, and shared chats for teams. The Premium plan at $20 per month is being sunset and is closed to new users.

## When you hit a limit

You hit two limits. The daily message limit of 7 messages stops new messages for the day. Credits work like a prepaid balance. When credits run out, generation pauses.

You are not billed by surprise. Only Premium, Plus, and Business plans can buy more credits, so a Free account cannot go over. The pages do not say whether the daily message limit resets at a set time. The pages do not mention a credit card for the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- The $5 is one-time for new users. It is not a monthly gift, even though older Free users keep a monthly amount.
- Credits are spent as tokens. The docs say that chat history, source files, and Vercel knowledge count as input, so short prompts can still use many tokens.
- v0 never pushes directly to your main branch. Changes go through pull requests and Publish.

## Questions people ask

### Is v0 free?

Yes, with limits. The Free plan has a 7-message daily limit and $5 of credits for new users, once.

### What happens when I run out of v0 credits?

Generation pauses. On Free, you cannot buy more credits, so you need a paid plan to continue.

### Does v0 need a credit card?

The pricing pages do not ask for a card on the Free plan. They show a Start Building button for it.
