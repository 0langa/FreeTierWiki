You build guides and surveys in the Amplitude web app and show them in your product through the Guides and Surveys SDK. It fits product and growth teams that already send events to Amplitude.

## What the free plan gives you

Guides & Surveys has no plan of its own. You get it through the Amplitude Free plan, which has no time limit and needs no credit card. The pricing page lists Guides & Surveys on the Free plan as "Limited". It gives no count, so the old figure of one guide at a time is not confirmed.

The rest of the Free plan sits around it. You get 2 million analytics events per month and 10,000 session replays per month. You also get 2,000 AI feedback records per month. Feature flags and team seats are unlimited. Because guides can use behavior data, you can target a guide at a group of users built from your Amplitude events. The Free plan is aimed at individuals and teams who are just starting out.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach the Free cap on guides and surveys. It does say that Guides & Surveys are priced by how many guides and surveys you create. They do not depend on user counts or event volume.

To get more, you buy Guides & Surveys as an add-on to the Plus plan. Growth and Enterprise plans sell them with other products. The Plus plan needs a credit card. The Free plan never asks for one.

## Gotchas

- You cannot buy Guides & Surveys on its own. It is part of Amplitude, so you also need the Amplitude SDK and events set up.
- Paid pricing counts the guides and surveys you create. It does not count users or events, so a large library costs more.
- Unlimited live guides and surveys come only with higher plans. The Free plan has a limited allowance.

## Questions people ask

### Is CommandBar free?

CommandBar is now Amplitude Guides & Surveys. A limited amount is free through the Amplitude Free plan, with no credit card.

### How many guides can I run on the Amplitude Free plan?

The pricing page says guides and surveys are limited on the Free plan, but it does not give a number. Unlimited live guides and surveys need Growth or Enterprise.

### Do I need a credit card for Amplitude Guides & Surveys?

Not for the Free plan. A credit card is required when you move to the Plus plan.
