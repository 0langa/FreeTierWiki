Orshot renders assets from a template and values your application supplies. You can use it for a small batch of social images, page-based documents, or short clips. The same workflow can populate several variants without manually changing the template for each render.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes 100 render credits each month in one workspace and requires no card. The units depend on the output. The pricing page counts an image, a PDF page, or a second of video as one credit. A multi-page document draws more from the allowance than a single page. Free videos can last up to 30 seconds each. The provider also includes one-time AI credits on Free. These are a separate initial allowance and should not be included when calculating recurring capacity for a render-only workflow.

## When you hit a limit

Estimate the cost of a batch using its pages or seconds, then try one specimen from the template. The pricing table lists larger render allowances on paid plans. It does not clearly specify the exact free-plan response once credits are gone, so check what your workspace reports before relying on an automated batch at the end of the month. Retry one item after editing the template instead of rerendering an entire batch to see whether the fix worked.

## Gotchas

- A single project can use the same render allowance for several kinds of output.
- The per-video cap applies even if your recurring credit balance has room.
- Keep AI use separate when budgeting. The sign-up allowance does not turn into a monthly pool.

## Questions people ask

### Is Orshot free to try over several months?

The render allocation refreshes monthly. The published Free plan lists 100 render credits without a card.

### How many PDFs can I generate?

Count pages. A longer document needs multiple credits, so the number of finished documents depends on their length.

### Can I render a long video with all the free credits?

The individual video duration cap still applies and is shorter than the total monthly credit allowance.
