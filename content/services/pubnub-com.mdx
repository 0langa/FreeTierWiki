You work in the Admin Portal to create keysets, then use one of the many PubNub SDKs to publish and subscribe to channels from your app. The free account suits a developer who wants to try the platform.

## What the free plan gives you

A free PubNub account covers up to 200 monthly active users or 1 million transactions at no cost, whichever limit you reach first. A monthly active user is one unique User ID that connects to your keyset in a calendar month. The plan has no time limit and needs no credit card.

You get 1GB of data storage with 7 days of retention. The plan includes unlimited channels. A Testing keyset gives you Pub/Sub, Presence, data storage, and Functions, so you can build and try a real application.

## When you hit a limit

The docs say a free account is limited to Testing keysets, which are rate-limited and not suitable for live traffic. Creating a Production keyset requires at least the Starter plan.

The pricing pages do not say what happens when you pass 200 users or 1 million transactions on a Testing keyset. The Starter plan costs $98 per month for 1,000 monthly active users, and you can buy more by credit card month to month.

## Gotchas

- Free accounts cannot create Production keysets. Plan for the paid tier before you launch.
- A single User ID counts as one monthly active user, however often it reconnects. Reuse a stable User ID, or the same person counts more than once.
- Auto Moderation and Event Forwarding need a paid plan even when you only test.

## Questions people ask

### Is PubNub free?

Yes, for testing. A free account covers up to 200 monthly active users or 1 million transactions at no cost, on Testing keysets.

### Can I use PubNub free in production?

No. Production keysets need at least the Starter plan, which costs $98 per month. Testing keysets are rate-limited and not for live traffic.

### What counts as a monthly active user on PubNub?

One unique User ID that connects to your keyset at least once in a calendar month. Several devices that share a User ID count as one.
