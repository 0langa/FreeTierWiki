You work in the Admin Portal to create keysets, then use one of the many PubNub SDKs to publish and subscribe to channels from your app. The free account suits a developer who wants to try the platform.

## What the free plan gives you

A free PubNub account covers up to 200 monthly active users or 1 million transactions at no cost, whichever limit you reach first. A monthly active user is one unique User ID that connects to your keyset in a calendar month. The plan has no time limit and needs no credit card.

You get 1GB of data storage with 7 days of retention. The plan includes unlimited channels. Up to ten active keysets give you Pub/Sub, Presence, data storage, and Functions, and every plan, including Free, can create Production keysets. Free Functions typically handle 15 requests/second without scaling and pause seven days after deployment; redeploy to run them again.

## When you hit a limit

The docs say Testing keysets are rate-limited and not suitable for live traffic, so choose a Production keyset if yours will carry live traffic. Every plan, including Free, can create one.

The pricing FAQ says excess Free usage requires an upgrade, but another FAQ groups Free and Starter together and describes proportional overage MAUs. It does not establish whether an unbilled Free account stops, continues, or creates an obligation. Confirm this before exceeding the allowance. The Starter plan costs $98 per month for 1,000 monthly active users, and you can buy more by credit card month to month.

## Gotchas

- Testing keysets are rate-limited and not for live traffic, so create a Production keyset before you launch.
- A single User ID counts as one monthly active user, however often it reconnects. Reuse a stable User ID, or the same person counts more than once.
- Auto Moderation needs a paid plan. Events & Actions, Insights, and Illuminate are only trials on a Free account, according to the current docs.

## Questions people ask

### Is PubNub free?

Yes, within limits. A free account covers up to 200 monthly active users or 1 million transactions at no cost, whichever you reach first, with no credit card.

### Can I use PubNub free in production?

Yes, within the free allowance. The docs say every plan, including Free, can create Production keysets, while Testing keysets are rate-limited and not for live traffic. The Starter plan costs $98 per month.

### What counts as a monthly active user on PubNub?

One unique User ID that connects to your keyset at least once in a calendar month. Several devices that share a User ID count as one.
