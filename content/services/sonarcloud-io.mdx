## What the free plan gives you

Sonar runs two free offers. Public repositories with an OSI-licensed open source license get the open source plan, free forever. The pricing page says they get the same features as the paid Pro plan.

For private code, the free tier covers private projects up to 50k lines of code. The pricing page says no card and no expiry. It is the Team plan, free up to that size. Sonar counts only the largest branch, and analyzing more often does not change the count.

The free tier supports many languages and DevOps integrations. Paid Team plans start at $34 per month for analysis of up to 100k lines of code, and other size steps are available.

## When you hit a limit

The private limit is a size limit, not a usage meter. The FAQ says that if you get close to the threshold, you are notified to either upgrade your plan or reduce the lines of code in your projects. The pricing page does not describe a block.

Because the free tier takes no card, nothing is charged by accident. A paid private organization is billed monthly by credit card. You can cancel, delete a paid organization, or downgrade to the free tier to keep analyzing public projects.

## Gotchas

- The open source offer is for public repositories under an OSI-licensed license. Check your license before you rely on it.
- Only the largest branch counts toward your lines of code. Extra branches do not add to the total.
- The docs warn that Atlassian is retiring the Bitbucket Connect app on a set date, and Bitbucket users must move to the Forge app.

## Questions people ask

### Is SonarQube Cloud free?

Yes, for open source and small private projects. Public OSI-licensed repositories are free forever, and private projects are free up to 50k lines of code.

### Is SonarCloud the same as SonarQube Cloud?

Yes. SonarCloud is the former name of SonarQube Cloud.

### Does SonarQube Cloud need a credit card?

No. The free tier asks for no card and has no expiry. The 14 day paid-plan trial also asks for no credit card.
