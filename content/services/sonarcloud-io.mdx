You sign in with your DevOps platform account and read the results on the web. You can also add the SonarQube for IDE extension to your editor.

## What the free plan gives you

OSI-licensed public repositories qualify for free-forever Open Source: unlimited public repositories/members, branch/PR analysis, custom quality gates/profiles, no private projects. Cloud documentation defines this separately from pricing's broad paid-Pro comparison.

Private Free allows 50k lines, five organization members, main-branch analysis and PRs targeting main, without card or expiry. Pricing calls it free Team, but detailed subscriptions distinguish Free. It excludes custom gates/profiles, webhooks, AI CodeFix and Advanced Security. Usage sums each private project's largest branch across the organization; scan frequency does not change it.

Many languages and DevOps integrations are supported. Paid Team starts at $34/month for 100k lines, with other size steps available.

## When you hit a limit

The private limit is a size limit, not a usage meter. The FAQ says that if you get close to the threshold, you are notified to either upgrade your plan or reduce the lines of code in your projects. The troubleshooting guide says analysis fails when the organization exceeds its subscription’s line limit.

The pricing page says the free tier takes no card. A paid private organization is billed monthly by credit card. You can cancel, delete a paid organization, or downgrade to the free tier to keep analyzing public projects.

## Gotchas

- The open source offer is for public repositories under an OSI-licensed license. Check your license before you rely on it.
- Only each project’s largest branch counts, but private projects are added together within the organization.
- The current Bitbucket guide supports automatic analysis through the Forge app as well as CI-based analysis. Workspaces bound before August 31, 2026 need to migrate to the new app; the Connect app retirement is December 1, 2026. Automatic analysis does not cover pull requests from forks, and it must be disabled before using CI-based analysis.

## Questions people ask

### Is SonarQube Cloud free?

Yes, for open source and small private projects. Public OSI-licensed repositories are free forever, and private projects are free up to 50k lines of code.

### Is SonarCloud the same as SonarQube Cloud?

Yes. SonarCloud is the former name of SonarQube Cloud.

### Does SonarQube Cloud need a credit card?

No. The free tier asks for no card and has no expiry. The 14 day paid-plan trial also asks for no credit card.
