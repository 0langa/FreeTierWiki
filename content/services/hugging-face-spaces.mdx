You create a Space on the Hub website, then push code to its Git repository. Each commit rebuilds and restarts the Space. It suits people who want to show a model or a page without managing a server.

## What the free plan gives you

Static HTML Spaces are free for everyone. They are served without running on compute, so you never need a paid plan for them. A static Space can also run a build command first, which helps with React, Svelte, or Vue apps.

Gradio and Docker Spaces run on compute and normally need a paid plan. The exception is ZeroGPU: a free personal account in good standing can host up to 2 Gradio Spaces on it. Good standing means a verified email and an account older than 30 days. A free account gets 5 minutes of ZeroGPU quota per day. The default CPU Basic hardware has 2 vCPU, 16 GB of RAM, and 50 GB of disk that is not persistent.

## When you hit a limit

Free hardware has a hard ceiling rather than a bill. The ZeroGPU daily quota for a free account is 5 minutes, and the quota resets 24 hours after your first GPU use. Remaining quota also affects your place in the GPU queue. Only PRO, Team, and Enterprise users can buy extra ZeroGPU time with pre-paid credits.

A Space on free hardware goes to sleep after a period of no use. The overview does not say how long that period is. You can also pause a Space from its Settings tab.

## Gotchas

- Creating a new Gradio or Docker Space on regular compute needs a paid plan. PRO costs $9 per month and lets a personal account host up to 10 ZeroGPU Spaces.
- The disk on the default hardware is not persistent, so anything you write at runtime is lost on restart.
- Private and protected visibility are paid features. A free Space is public.

## Questions people ask

### Is Hugging Face Spaces free?

Static HTML Spaces are free for everyone. A free personal account in good standing can also host up to 2 Gradio Spaces on ZeroGPU. Other Gradio and Docker Spaces need a paid plan.

### Does a Hugging Face Space sleep?

Yes. On free hardware, a Space goes to sleep and stops running after a period of no use. You can also pause it from the Settings tab.

### Does Hugging Face Spaces need a credit card?

Signing up and hosting free Spaces does not need a card. A card matters only if you subscribe to PRO or Team, or add credits for extra GPU time.
