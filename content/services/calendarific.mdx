You sign up for a key in the dashboard, then send GET requests over HTTPS and read JSON. It suits developers who build calendars, scheduling, or payroll tools and want holiday data without keeping their own list.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and gives you 500 calls per month. It supports a single language. It carries limited historical data and limited upcoming data, and the dataset updates quarterly. You must show attribution to Calendarific.

The pricing page also lists an Enterprise plan with 50,000,000 calls a month, and the Business plan has 50,000. The free plan is enough to test a calendar feature or run a small side project.

Every request to any endpoint counts toward the quota, including requests that return an error. Paid plans start at $12 a month, or $100 a year, for 10,000 calls a month and drop the attribution rule.

## When you hit a limit

The API returns HTTP 429 with the error type `rate_limit_exceeded` once your monthly quota is used. It keeps returning that error until the quota resets or you upgrade. The docs say to wait for the monthly reset or upgrade, and to cache responses, because holiday data for a country and year rarely changes.

The pricing page does not list any overage fee for the Free plan, and the docs describe a stop, not a bill.

## Gotchas

- Attribution to Calendarific is required on the Free plan. Paid plans do not need it.
- CORS is on, so you can call the API from the browser. That exposes your key and quota, so call it from your server on public sites.
- The country and year parameters are required on every holidays request. A request without them is rejected, and it still counts as a call.

## Questions people ask

### Is Calendarific free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan gives you 500 calls a month, one language, and limited historical and upcoming data. You must credit Calendarific.

### What happens when I go over the Calendarific free limit?

The API answers with HTTP 429 until your monthly quota resets. You can wait or move to a paid plan.

### Does Calendarific need a credit card?

The pages used for this entry do not mention a card for the Free plan. You sign up for a free account and copy your API key from the dashboard.
