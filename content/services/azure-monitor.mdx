You set up Azure Monitor in the Azure portal, or with PowerShell and templates, by creating a Log Analytics workspace and connecting your resources. It suits teams that already run on Azure.

## What the free plan gives you

Azure Monitor has no single free plan. It has free amounts inside each part of the service. The first 5 GB per month per billing account of Analytics Logs ingestion is free. The pricing table lists 31 or 90 days of retention included for Analytics Logs, and 30 days for Basic and Auxiliary Logs.

Platform metrics are ingested for free, and the first 1,000,000 platform metric API calls per month are free. Metric alerts include 10 monitored metric time-series per month. Notifications include 1,000 emails per month, 1,000 ITSM connector events, and 1,000 push notifications. Standard metrics and activity logs collect at no cost, and so do the advanced monitoring tools that use your ingested data.

## When you hit a limit

Azure bills you. Log data is billed by ingestion, retention, and export, and the free amounts only cover the first part of each month. A new workspace uses a pay-as-you-go tier by default, and no charges start until data collection begins.

The pricing page does not describe a hard stop or a spending cap. Check your costs in Azure before you connect many resources, because verbose logs can grow fast.

## Gotchas

- The 5 GB of free log ingestion is per billing account, not per workspace, so several workspaces share it.
- Retention beyond the included days costs money, and you can extend it up to 12 years.
- If a workspace has Microsoft Sentinel enabled, ingestion is billed with Sentinel meters instead.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Monitor free?

Partly. Standard metrics and activity logs cost nothing, and the first 5 GB per month of Analytics Logs ingestion is free per billing account. Other usage is billed.

### Does Azure Monitor need a credit card?

You need an Azure account with an active subscription, and the entry lists a card as required. Creating a workspace starts no charges until data collection begins.

### What happens when I pass the free log ingestion?

Extra ingestion is billed per GB at pay-as-you-go rates. The pricing page does not describe a cap or a stop.
