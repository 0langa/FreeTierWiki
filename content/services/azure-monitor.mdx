You set up Azure Monitor in the Azure portal, or with PowerShell and templates, by creating a Log Analytics workspace and connecting your resources. It suits teams that already run on Azure.

## What the free plan gives you

Azure Monitor has no single free plan. It has free amounts inside each part of the service. The first 5 GB per month per billing account of Analytics Logs ingestion is free. Analytics Logs normally include 31 days of retention; Application Insights and Sentinel-enabled Analytics Logs include 90 days. Basic and Auxiliary Logs include 30 days.

Standard platform metrics are ingested for free; advanced platform metrics have a separate ingestion meter. The first 1,000,000 platform metric API calls per subscription per month are free. Metric alerts include 10 monitored metric time-series per month. Notifications include 1,000 emails per month, 1,000 ITSM connector events, and 1,000 push notifications. Standard metrics and activity logs collect at no cost, and the listed Insights tools add no separate charge for analyzing ingested data. The Copilot Observability Agent has its own Azure Agent Credit charges.

## When you hit a limit

On pay-as-you-go, Azure bills usage beyond the applicable monthly quantities. Log data is billed by ingestion, retention, and export; Basic and Auxiliary log queries also incur data-scanning charges. A new workspace uses a pay-as-you-go tier by default, and no charges start until data collection begins.

The free allowance does not impose a spending cap. The FAQ describes an optional daily Application Insights ingestion cap, which does not cap every Azure Monitor charge. Check your costs in Azure before you connect many resources, because verbose logs can grow fast.

## Gotchas

- The 5 GB of free log ingestion is per billing account, not per workspace, so several workspaces share it. On Sentinel-enabled workspaces, Basic and Auxiliary Logs ingestion uses Sentinel meters. Analytics Logs ingestion uses Sentinel meters on Sentinel's simplified pricing plans.
- The ten free metric-alert time series do not waive dynamic-threshold charges. Alerts and their notification deliveries are separate meters.
- Retention beyond the included days costs money, and you can extend it up to 12 years.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Monitor free?

Partly. Standard metrics and activity logs cost nothing, and the first 5 GB per month of Analytics Logs ingestion is free per billing account. Other usage is billed.

### Does Azure Monitor need a credit card?

Yes. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

### What happens when I pass the free log ingestion?

Extra ingestion is billed per GB at pay-as-you-go rates. The pricing page does not describe a cap or a stop.
