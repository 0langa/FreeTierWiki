You work in the Beeceptor web dashboard: create an endpoint, send requests to it, and add mock rules. Docs also cover a management API, OpenAPI imports, and an MCP mode for AI tools.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 per month and the pricing page calls it forever-free. It allows 50 requests per day per endpoint and 3 mock rules. The endpoint is public, and dynamic and conditional mock responses are enabled.

The page says no signup and no credit card are required. AI Agents and Copilot are limited on this plan. You can use the free plan for commercial work as long as you stay within the daily request limit. Mock rules let you match a request and return the status, headers, body, and delay your test needs, and you can also import an API specification to get a mock server.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists the daily limit of 50 requests but does not say what the endpoint does after the 50th request. It does not describe a bill for the free plan either, and it needs no card.

To get more, the Individual plan costs $10 per month and allows 15,000 requests per month per endpoint. The Team plan costs $25 per month with 100,000 requests per month. Unclaimed free endpoints are deleted 30 days after creation, and claiming an endpoint extends that to 90 days from creation.

## Gotchas

- The endpoint access on the free plan is listed as public, while paid plans list private access.
- An unclaimed endpoint is cleaned up after 30 days, and a claimed one after 90 days from creation.
- The free plan has 3 mock rules per endpoint, while the Individual plan allows up to 50.

## Questions people ask

### Is Beeceptor free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and allows 50 requests per day per endpoint. It needs no credit card.

### Does Beeceptor need a signup?

The pricing page says the free plan needs no signup and no credit card. You can create an endpoint from the home page.

### How long do Beeceptor free endpoints last?

Unclaimed free endpoints are cleaned up 30 days after creation. Claiming an endpoint extends retention to 90 days from its creation date.
