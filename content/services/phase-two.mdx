You sign up in the Phase Two Dashboard, then use the Keycloak admin tools and the Phase Two Management and Extensions APIs. It suits teams that want Keycloak on a managed cluster, mostly for B2B SaaS products.

## What the free plan gives you

Phase Two has no free plan. It has a trial. Every Starter cluster starts with a 30-day free trial, and after that it costs $149 per month. You can cancel any time before the trial ends and you will not be charged.

Hosting is priced per cluster, not per user, and users are unlimited on every tier. The Starter tier is sized for about 5,000 active users and runs a dedicated, independently provisioned cluster. It includes unlimited SSO connections and 2 custom domains. Registered users are always unlimited, and the sizing only describes the authentication load the cluster handles.

## When you hit a limit

After 30 days, the trial turns into a $149 monthly charge unless you cancel first. That is the main limit to watch. The atlas lists a card as needed to start, so set a reminder before the trial ends.

Phase Two does not cap users. Nothing is blocked, throttled, or surcharged when you go past the sizing for your tier. Performance may degrade, usually as higher latency on the token endpoints. If your traffic is heavier than the sizing assumes, with short token lifespans or chatty machine clients, the cluster carries fewer users than the headline number.

## Gotchas

- The trial converts to a paid plan on its own. Cancel before day 30 if you only want to test.
- The tiers differ by authentication load, not by user count. Heavy token traffic can slow a cluster below its headline size.
- The Starter tier includes 2 custom domains. The next tier up has 5.

## Questions people ask

### Does Phase Two have a free tier?

No. It has a 30-day free trial of the Starter plan. After the trial it costs $149 per month, unless you cancel.

### Is the Phase Two trial really free?

The pricing page says yes. A Starter cluster starts with a 30-day free trial, and you pay nothing if you cancel before it ends.

### How many users can Phase Two handle?

Users are unlimited on every tier. The Starter tier is sized for about 5,000 active users, and going past that does not block anyone but may slow the cluster.
