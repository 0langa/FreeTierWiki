You create a Speech resource in Azure, then call it from the REST API, an SDK, the Speech CLI, or the Foundry portal. It suits developers who add voice input, spoken output, or live translation to an app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free (F0) tier has monthly allowances for three jobs. Real-time speech to text gets 5 audio hours per month, and that pool is shared between standard and custom models. Neural text to speech gets 0.5 million characters per month. Speech translation gets 5 audio hours per month.

One custom model endpoint can be hosted for free. The pricing page says unused custom models are decommissioned after 7 days. Batch transcription is not supported on the free tier.

The F0 quotas cannot be raised. The docs set the limit at 1 concurrent request for the base model endpoint and 1 for a custom endpoint. Real-time speech-to-text and speech translation share that concurrency count. Several features, such as Voice Live and large language model speech, show as not applicable for F0.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the free hours or characters are used up. The docs say F0 quotas are not adjustable. Standard (S0) quotas can be adjusted, and the default for S0 is 100 concurrent requests.

If you switch a resource from F0 to S0, the new quotas can take up to several hours to apply. Paid prices are shown as blank dashes on the page, so use the Azure pricing calculator. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## Gotchas

- Free audio hours for speech to text are shared between standard and custom models.
- Free custom models vanish after 7 days if you do not use them.
- Custom voice is a limited access feature, so it is not a free self-serve path.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Speech free?

Yes, within limits. The F0 tier gives 5 audio hours of speech to text, 0.5 million characters of text to speech, and 5 audio hours of speech translation each month.

### Does Azure AI Speech free tier support batch transcription?

No. The pricing page says batch is not supported for the free audio hours.

### Can I raise the Azure AI Speech free tier limits?

No. The docs say the quotas and limits for F0 resources are not adjustable. You must move to Standard (S0) for higher limits.
