Azure AI Speech offers cloud APIs for speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and translation. The free (F0) tier includes 5 audio hours/month transcription, 0.5 million characters/month neural TTS, and 5 audio hours/month translation, suitable for development and light production workloads.
