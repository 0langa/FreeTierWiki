You manage everything in a web dashboard after you register. Pingmeter scans from several countries and keeps scan logs you can review later. It suits owners of a few small sites who only need email alerts.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing table lists the Free plan with 5 uptime monitors, a scan interval of 10 minutes, and 14 days of detailed data history. Email notification is available. The footnote says a free account is limited to 1 category and excludes SMS and voice notification, scheduling, and recovery actions.

The page also offers "a free 10 days trial" of the paid features. Paid plans start at $4.99 per month and add shorter scan intervals, more monitors, and longer data history. The home page describes public status pages, DNS failover, and SMS or voice calls as features, but it does not say which plans include them.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you go over the free limits. The footnote only says the free account is limited, so plan on upgrading if you need more.

The table lists 5 monitors for Free, but the footnote says fewer than 5 monitors. Check the dashboard before you plan around the full count. Nothing on the page describes a bill that starts by itself.

## Gotchas

- The pricing table and its footnote disagree on the free monitor count: 5 in the table, but "less than 5" in the footnote.
- The free account is limited to 1 category and has no SMS, voice, scheduling, or recovery actions.
- The 10-minute scan interval on Free is slow. Plans from the Basic plan up scan every minute.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pingmeter free?

Yes, with limits. The Free plan lists 5 uptime monitors, a 10-minute scan interval, and 14 days of data history. It sends email notifications.

### Does Pingmeter offer a free trial?

Yes. The page offers a free 10-day trial for registering. Paid plans start at $4.99 per month.

### Does Pingmeter send SMS alerts on the free plan?

No. The footnote says the free account excludes SMS and voice notification, scheduling, and recovery actions.
