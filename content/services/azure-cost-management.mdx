You use it in the Azure portal, and you can pull the same data through exports and APIs. It fits anyone who pays an Azure bill, from solo developers to finance teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the price as "Free for Azure". You pay nothing extra to use Cost Management on your Azure spend. You still pay for the Azure resources you run. The tool needs an Azure subscription to read from.

Cost analysis gives you built-in views of cost by service and over time. Budgets track your spend against an amount you pick, and you can set them monthly, quarterly, or yearly. Alerts cover budgets, anomalies, and scheduled reports by email. Tags let you add your own labels, and scheduled exports send cost details to a storage account.

Summarized data in cost analysis covers the last 13 months. A new subscription can take up to 48 hours before every feature works.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not list a usage cap, so there is no quota to run out of. The tool itself stays free for Azure.

The limits that matter are about data, not money. Daily views span at most 3 months. If a subscription changes offer type, older costs are not available. Budgets only warn you. A subscription or resource group budget can call an action group to take automated steps, which is how you can reduce or stop further charges.

## Gotchas

- Classic Cloud Solution Provider and sponsorship subscriptions are not supported. They will be supported after they move to a Microsoft Customer Agreement.
- Support costs and some Marketplace or commitment purchases are not included for every account type. Check the data page for your offer.
- Budgets and alerts do not stop spending on their own. You must connect an action group if you want automatic action.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Cost Management free?

Yes for Azure. The pricing page lists it as free for Azure. You still pay for the resources you use.

### Does Azure Cost Management stop my spending at a budget?

No. A budget sends alerts when cost passes your amount. You can link an action group to take automated steps.

### How far back does Azure cost analysis go?

Summarized data in cost analysis covers the last 13 months. If your subscription changes offer type, costs before the change are not available.
