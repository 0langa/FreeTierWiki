Stackby is a hosted no-code database that works like a spreadsheet with typed columns, views, forms, automations, and an interface designer. Its column-linked API connectors pull data from outside services into a table. It suits small teams that want a tracker or a light database without code.

## What the free plan gives you

Stackby's Free plan is free forever, and the pricing page says no credit card is needed. It is for individuals and small teams getting started.

The plan includes 5 editors plus 50 read-only guests, 20 stacks, 1,500 rows per stack, and 2 GB of attachments per stack with a 2 MB file upload limit. You get 100 internal automation runs, 1 app per stack, 2 column-linked API connectors, and 2 weeks of activity history. Connector cells refresh manually on Free. The Economy plan starts at $4.2 per user per month billed annually and raises rows to 7,000 per stack.

## When you hit a limit

Stackby answers this on its pricing page. If you reach or go over the row or attachment limits, you can still use your stacks and Stackby will never remove your data. It notifies you, and you cannot add more rows or attachments until you upgrade the workspace.

For automations and API limits, you are capped at the usage limit of your current plan. No card is on file for the Free plan, so nothing is billed.

## Gotchas

- Column-linked API connectors refresh only manually on Free; automatic refresh starts on Economy.
- The 2 MB file upload limit blocks larger attachments even when the stack has space left.
- Activity history covers only 2 weeks, so older changes cannot be traced.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stackby free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever with no credit card, and includes 20 stacks with 1,500 rows each.

### What happens when a Stackby stack reaches 1,500 rows?

You can still use the stack and your data stays, but you cannot add more rows until you upgrade.

### Can Stackby pull data from APIs on the Free plan?

Yes, with 2 column-linked API connectors, but cells refresh manually on the Free plan.
