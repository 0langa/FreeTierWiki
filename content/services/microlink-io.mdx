You call the API with one HTTP GET request, so it works from a browser, cURL, or any language. A JavaScript SDK and a command line tool wrap the same API, and developers who need previews or screenshots will find it quick to try.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and needs no card. It allows 25 requests per day, which Microlink puts at about 1.5K requests a month. You get the same toolbox as paid plans for screenshots, PDFs, metadata, markdown, insights, and the SDK. The plan runs against the public endpoint with a 30-second request timeout.

One request is one API call to any Microlink endpoint. Responses served from the cache do not count against your quota. Concurrency is limited and shared, and support comes from the community.

## When you hit a limit

Microlink says you are never billed by surprise. It notifies you at 80% of usage. At 100%, requests pause until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade. There are no overage fees.

The free plan has a daily quota of 25 requests, while Pro has no daily quota. Pro costs $49 a month for 46,000 requests a month. A free account cannot be billed because it has no card.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has a best-effort SLA, and the 99.9% uptime commitment applies to paid plans only.
- The request timeout is 30 seconds on Free and 60 seconds on Pro.
- The free plan uses the shared global cache, and a custom cache key is not part of it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Microlink free?

Yes. The free plan is free forever and needs no credit card. You get 25 requests per day against the public endpoint, with screenshot, PDF, metadata, and SDK access.

### What happens when I exceed the Microlink free quota?

Requests pause until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade. Microlink says there are no overage fees.

### Does Microlink need an API key or credit card for the free plan?

No card is needed. The free plan runs against the public endpoint, so you can send a request right away.
