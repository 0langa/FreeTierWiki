You sign in with your GitHub account, pick repositories, and use the Gerrit web interface at review.gerrithub.io for review. GerritHub replicates reviewed commits back to GitHub. It suits people who prefer Gerrit's patch set review to pull requests.

## What the free plan gives you

GerritForge, the company behind GerritHub, says you can learn and experience the Gerrit workflow for free on your GitHub repositories. It points to a free sign-up. The service signs you in with GitHub, imports your repositories, and syncs GitHub pull requests into Gerrit for review.

The pages show no price table and no usage limits. They do not give a number for repositories, users, or storage. The GerritForge page says GerritHub is hosted by GerritForge, integrated with GitHub authentication, and runs on several sites around the world.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say what happens at a limit, because they publish none. They also publish no terms for the free service beyond the terms and privacy links.

One rule matters in daily use. The intro page says you should push to Gerrit and use GitHub only for pulls and pull requests. A direct push to GitHub makes the replication stop. To resume, clone from Gerrit, pull and merge the latest GitHub changes, and push the merged branch to Gerrit.

## Gotchas

- Replication stops if someone pushes directly to GitHub. Your team has to agree to push to GerritHub.
- GerritHub asks for the GitHub user:email and public_repo scopes. The intro page says it reads only your name, email, and SSH public keys from your profile.
- The intro page also mentions a 30-day free trial, but the pages do not explain it. Treat the service as free, and check the GerritHub sign-up flow for current terms.

## Questions people ask

### Is GerritHub free?

GerritForge says you can experience the Gerrit workflow for free on your GitHub repositories. The pages publish no price or usage limits.

### Does GerritHub need a credit card?

The pages show no payment step. You sign in with a GitHub account and allow GerritHub to access it.

### Why did GerritHub stop syncing with my GitHub repository?

Someone probably pushed directly to GitHub. The intro page says that stops replication, and it explains how to merge the GitHub changes and push them to Gerrit.
