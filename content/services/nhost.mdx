You manage projects in the Nhost Dashboard, run queries with GraphQL, and use the `@nhost/nhost-js` SDK from your frontend. Guides cover React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte, and React Native.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan costs $0 per month. It allows 1 project, and your free organization can hold more projects as long as only 1 is active at a time. You can pause and unpause free projects as needed.

Starter includes a 1 GB database and 1 GB of storage. Egress is 5 GB. For functions you get 10 included functions, 1 GB-hours of execution, and 10 seconds of run time per call. You also get realtime APIs, deployments, unlimited users, OAuth providers, and community support. The plan has 1 workspace member. Automated backups and point in time recovery start on the Pro plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page FAQ says Nhost never stops your project when you exceed the limits. It says you are charged for the excess usage instead, and it calls this pay as you grow. The page does not say how this works for a Starter project with no payment method.

The inactivity pause is a separate rule. Nhost pauses a Starter project after 1 week of inactivity. The FAQ says you can pause and unpause free projects as needed, so you can unpause a project yourself.

## Gotchas

- Only 1 free project can be active at a time. A second project must stay paused, or you need a paid plan.
- Starter has no automated backups. The FAQ says you have full access to your database and storage, so you can export data yourself.
- Function runs end after 10 seconds on Starter. Pro allows 180 seconds.

## Questions people ask

### Is Nhost free?

Yes. The Starter plan costs $0 per month and allows 1 project with a 1 GB database and 1 GB of storage. It includes unlimited users and OAuth providers.

### Does Nhost pause free projects?

Yes. Nhost pauses a Starter project after 1 week of inactivity. You can pause and unpause free projects as needed.

### What happens if I exceed the Nhost limits?

The pricing FAQ says Nhost never stops your project and charges for the excess usage. Upgrading to Pro raises the included database to 10 GB and storage to 50 GB.
