Metered sells a global TURN server network for WebRTC, with automatic routing to the nearest server. It also runs Open Relay, a free TURN server, and offers a managed signalling service and realtime SDKs.

## What the free plan gives you

There are two free options. Open Relay is a free TURN server provided by Metered Video with 20 GB of free TURN usage every month. It runs on ports 80 and 443 to get through corporate firewalls, supports TURNS over SSL, and routes to the nearest server. You need a free account to get an API key.

On the TURN server pricing, the first tier costs $0 a month and includes 500 MB of monthly TURN usage, counting ingress and egress, with global TURN server access and email support. The page says no credit card is required, and STUN usage is unlimited and free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a free tier's monthly TURN usage runs out. The paid plans show what comes next: Growth costs $99 a month for 150 GB with overage at $0.40 per GB, and Business costs $199 a month for 500 GB with overage at $0.20 per GB.

The page also describes projects with their own bandwidth quotas, where you choose what happens when a quota is exceeded: notify only, disable credentials, or none. Quota alert webhooks can warn you as usage approaches a limit.

## Gotchas

- The 500 MB tier is labeled Free Trial even though it is priced at $0 a month, so confirm in the dashboard that it continues.
- TURN usage counts both ingress and egress, so relayed media is counted in both directions.
- The Open Relay key and the signalling publishable key are separate keys in the dashboard.

## Questions people ask

### Is there a free TURN server from Metered?

Yes. Open Relay gives 20 GB of free TURN usage every month once you sign up for a free account.

### Do I need a credit card?

No. The TURN pricing page says no credit card is required for the free tier.

### Is STUN free?

Yes. The pricing page says unlimited STUN usage is included free.
