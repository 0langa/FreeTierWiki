IBM Cloud Object Storage keeps files of any format in buckets inside a service instance on IBM Cloud. You choose a storage class per bucket, such as Smart Tier, which classifies data into hot, cool, and cold tiers by how often it is read. It suits developers who use IBM Cloud and want object storage for uploads, backups, or static files.

## What the free plan gives you

The Standard plan, a pay-as-you-go option with no minimum fee, includes a Free Tier with 5GB of Smart Tier storage for 12 months. The IBM docs describe it as 5GB of free monthly usage for up to 12 months from the sign-up date.

The catalog lists the Free Tier allowance per month: storage up to 5GB, up to 2000 Class A requests, up to 20,000 Class B requests, up to 10GB of data retrieval, and up to 5GB of egress. It applies to the total across all Smart Tier buckets in your account. Separately, the TRYCOS promo code gives up to USD 500 in free credits for your first 3 months.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops your buckets at the Free Tier amounts. After 12 months, or if you exceed the Free Tier limits, you are billed at standard pay-as-you-go rates.

Smart Tier billing is based on usage across all Smart Tier buckets in an instance, not per bucket. Buckets in other classes, such as Standard, Vault, or Cold Vault, are billed on their own terms, and Vault and Cold Vault add a retrieval charge each time data is read.

## Gotchas

- The Free Tier is tied to Smart Tier storage, so create buckets in that class if you want them covered.
- IBM Cloud registration asks for credit card information for identity verification, even if you use only free plans.
- Deleting an instance does not delete data right away; it is scheduled for reclamation after 7 days by default.

## Questions people ask

### Is IBM Cloud Object Storage free?

For 12 months. The Standard plan's Free Tier covers up to 5GB of Smart Tier storage plus limited requests, retrieval, and egress each month for 12 months from sign-up.

### What happens after 12 months?

You are billed at standard pay-as-you-go rates, the same as when you exceed the Free Tier limits.

### Is there a promo code for IBM Cloud Object Storage?

Yes. The TRYCOS promo code gives up to USD 500 in free credits for your first 3 months when you provision.
