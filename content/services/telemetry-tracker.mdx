You point a Telemetry Tracker SDK in your JavaScript or TypeScript app at the hosted ingest URL or at your own self-hosted endpoint. Errors, events and sessions then show up in the dashboard, with source maps for readable stacks when you upload them.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs €0 forever and the pricing page says there is no credit card. It covers 250K ingest units per month, 14-day retention, 1 project and 2 API keys, with community support.

An ingest unit is one accepted telemetry item: one event, one item inside a batch, one error, or one session. A calendar month is the quota window. The free plan is aimed at solo developers and side projects. You can also self-host the MIT open-source stack with Docker and PostgreSQL if you want the data on your own servers.

## When you hit a limit

Ingest stops at your plan cap. At the cap, new ingest is rejected with HTTP 429 (`monthly_ingest_quota`) until the next month or a higher plan. Stored data stays for the plan retention window. There is no overage charge.

Paid plans raise the limits. Pro is 15 € per org per month with 5M ingest units, 90-day retention and up to 10 projects. Business is 99 € per org per month with 50M ingest units, 365-day retention and up to 50 projects.

## Gotchas

- Free includes one project for data isolation. Multiple apps can share that project and use the SDK's app name to filter their telemetry.
- Ingest units count errors, events and sessions together, so high-volume event instrumentation can burn the free quota quickly.
- SDKs target JavaScript and TypeScript stacks, including React Native on iOS and Android. The React Native SDK captures JavaScript errors, not native crashes.

## Questions people ask

### Is Telemetry Tracker free?

Yes. The Free plan is €0 forever with 250K ingest units per month, 14-day retention, 1 project and 2 API keys, and no credit card. The product is also MIT open source if you prefer to self-host.

### What happens when I exceed the free quota?

New ingest is rejected with HTTP 429 until the next calendar month or until you move to a higher plan. Stored data is kept for the retention window and there is no overage charge.

### Can I self-host instead of using the free hosted plan?

Yes. The GitHub repository ships Docker and PostgreSQL deployment docs. Self-hosting puts the API, dashboard and database on infrastructure you control.
