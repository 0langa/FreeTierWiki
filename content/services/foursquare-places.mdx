The Foursquare Places API gives apps access to Foursquare's point-of-interest data: place search, place details, autocomplete, place matching, and premium data such as photos and tips. Pricing is pay-as-you-go by monthly call volume, split into Pro and Premium endpoints. A small free amount of Pro calls lets developers test and run light workloads.

## What the free plan gives you

Pro endpoints include Place Search, Place Details, Autocomplete, Place Match, and Geotagging Candidates when requested with default fields. On the pricing page, the first tier of 0 to 500 Pro calls a month costs $0.00 CPM. The developer docs say that from June 1, 2026 these rates apply to all users and you receive 500 free Pro calls. The pricing page also mentions testing in a sandbox and up to 10,000 free calls on Pro endpoints, so check your console for which applies. Premium endpoints, such as Tips and Photos, start at $18.75 CPM with no free tier. Separately, the Movement SDK's first 1,000 monthly active users are free.

## When you hit a limit

Pro calls past 500 a month move to the next tier at $15.00 CPM, then $12.00 CPM above 100,000 calls. Pay-as-you-go accounts draw on a credit balance. Automatic payments are on by default and recharge your account when you have used your current credits. A credit card is not required for Pay As You Go, but Foursquare recommends adding one so that you stay covered if you make more calls than you have credit for. The pricing page does not say whether calls stop when a free account has no balance.

## Gotchas

- Asking Search or Details for premium tier fields turns them into Premium calls, which have no free tier.
- The pricing page and the docs give different free amounts for Pro calls, so read your console before relying on more than 500.
- Ask API endpoints have their own higher rates and their own rate limits.

## Questions people ask

### Is the Foursquare Places API free?

Partly. Pro endpoint calls are free for the first 500 a month, and calls past that are billed.

### Do I need a credit card for the Places API?

No. The billing docs say a card is not required for Pay As You Go, though Foursquare recommends adding one.

### Are photos and tips free?

No. Tips and Photos are Premium endpoints, which are billed from the first call.
