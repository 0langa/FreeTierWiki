You work in the Cube web app, and you can reach the same metrics through SQL, REST, and GraphQL APIs or an MCP server. It suits data teams and developers who want governed metrics for dashboards, chat, and customer-facing analytics.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is called free forever on the pricing page. It is meant for hobbyists finishing small projects. You get up to 5 workbooks, and the daily request limit is 1,000. The Starter plan lifts the limit to 10K on shared compute or 50K on dedicated compute. Premium and Enterprise have no request limit.

Workbooks are where you explore data and then publish dashboards. The pricing page also lists a semantic model agent, a workbooks and dashboards agent, analytics chat, and an MCP server for AI agents. Free has limited agent use, and premium language models need a paid plan.

The Free plan has no dedicated deployment listed. Support is by community channels. Starter costs $40 per developer per month. Premium costs $80 per developer per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page gives the limits but does not say what happens when you pass 1,000 requests in a day or add a sixth workbook. The page also does not state a card requirement for Free.

Paid plans are billed monthly by credit card through Stripe. If a payment keeps failing, Cube says the account may be restricted or suspended until the balance is paid.

## Gotchas

- The daily request limit is 1,000 on Free and 10K on Starter with shared compute.
- Dedicated deployment is not on the Free plan. It is priced by the hour on paid plans.
- The pricing table lists SSO with SAML 2.0, workspace access control, and bring your own language model. The page text does not show which plans include them, so check before you rely on Free for them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cube free?

Yes. Cube Cloud has a free forever plan with up to 5 workbooks and 1,000 requests per day. Paid plans start at $40 per developer per month.

### How many requests does the Cube free plan allow?

The Free plan has a daily request limit of 1,000. Starter raises it to 10K on shared compute or 50K on dedicated compute.

### Is Cube open source?

Cube is built on an open-source semantic layer called Cube Core. Cube also documents a path to migrate from Cube Core.
