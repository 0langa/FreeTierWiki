You manage projects and secrets in a web dashboard. Machines read their secrets with an SDK or a single-binary CLI, and an MCP server lets AI clients work with the vault. It suits solo developers and small teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free, and the pricing FAQ says it is permanent, not a trial. It includes 10 machines. A machine here is anything that can read secrets right now, such as a server, a service, a CI runner, or a temporary contractor machine.

Secrets, projects, reads, and rotations are unlimited on every plan. You also get 1 month of audit retention and 20 webhook deliveries per day. All plans list the same core features: ephemeral machines, AI identities, managed and leased secrets, access policies, the CLI, SDKs, and passkey sign-in.

## When you hit a limit

Going over 10 machines costs money, not access. Each additional machine on the Developer plan is billed at $5 per month, and the FAQ says you are never blocked from adding one. Machines that cannot read, such as disabled or expired ones, do not count. AI identities and dashboard users do not count either. For the paid plans, a failed payment opens a seven-day window before the plan moves back to Developer.

## Gotchas

- Extra machines bill by the month. A busy CI setup with many live ephemeral machines can raise the count.
- Organizations with members, vault roles, and access roles need Teams at $29 per month, which includes 25 machines.
- Adding a machine to a project is not enough. Each secret needs its own grant for each machine.

## Questions people ask

### Is SikkerKey free?

Yes. The Developer plan is permanent, with 10 machines, unlimited secrets and reads, and no expiry.

### What happens if I go over 10 machines on SikkerKey?

Additional machines bill at $5 per month each on the Developer plan. The pricing FAQ says you are never blocked from adding one.

### Does SikkerKey charge for reads or rotations?

No. Secret count, project count, read volume, and rotation frequency are unlimited on every plan.
