You manage projects and secrets in a web dashboard. Machines read their secrets with an SDK or a single-binary CLI, and an MCP server lets AI clients work with the vault. It suits solo developers and small teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The [official pricing page](https://sikkerkey.com/pricing) was unavailable on November 15. These plan terms were last verified on November 10. The Developer plan was listed as permanently free with 10 machines. A machine here is anything that can read secrets right now, such as a server, a service, a CI runner, or a temporary contractor machine.

The last-verified plans listed unlimited secrets, projects, reads and rotations. Developer included 1 month of audit retention and 20 webhook deliveries per day. All plans listed ephemeral machines, AI identities, managed and leased secrets, access policies, the CLI, SDKs and passkey sign-in.

## When you hit a limit

The last-verified FAQ gave conflicting answers. One said the limit stops new machines while existing machines keep running. Another said extra machines are never blocked and bill at $5 per month each on Developer. Current enforcement and card rules are unconfirmed. The FAQ also excluded disabled or expired machines, AI identities and dashboard users from the count. It described a seven-day window after a failed payment before a paid account moved back to Developer.

## Gotchas

- A busy CI setup with many live ephemeral machines can raise the count.
- The last-verified Teams plan listed members, vault roles, access roles and 25 machines at $29 per month.
- Adding a machine to a project is not enough. Each secret needs its own grant for each machine.

## Questions people ask

### Is SikkerKey free?

The last-verified Developer plan was permanently free, with 10 machines and unlimited secrets and reads. Current availability is unconfirmed.

### What happens if I go over 10 machines on SikkerKey?

The last-verified FAQ listed $5 per month for each extra Developer machine and also said the cap could block new machines. Current enforcement and charges are unconfirmed.

### Does SikkerKey charge for reads or rotations?

The last-verified plans listed unlimited secrets, projects, reads and rotations. Current quotas are unconfirmed.
