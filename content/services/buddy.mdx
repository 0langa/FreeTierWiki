You build pipelines in the web editor by adding actions one after another, or you define them in YAML. It suits a single developer who wants build, test, and deploy steps for one project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs nothing and includes 1 seat, 1 concurrent pipeline run, 300 pipeline GB-minutes per month, and 1 GB of pipeline cache. A GB-minute is the RAM you give a pipeline multiplied by its run time. A pipeline with 3GB of RAM that runs for 10 minutes uses 30 GB-minutes. The pool resets every month.

Buddy says the Free plan includes the base features, but not 2FA or Sandboxes. The pricing page still lists sandbox allowances for Free, so treat Sandboxes as unconfirmed. New workspaces start with a 14-day trial that has Hyper features. After the trial you pick Free, Pro, or Hyper, and the workspace stays read-only until you choose.

## When you hit a limit

Buddy says that once you spend all GB-minutes in your plan, it charges per minute by action type. The base price is $0.0015 per GB-minute for containers and Linux VMs. macOS and Windows VMs cost more. The pricing page does not say whether a free workspace stops or waits when the pool runs out, or what happens to a workspace that has no payment method. Only one pipeline can run at once on the Free plan.

## Gotchas

- A free workspace freezes after 60 days with no activity, and Buddy deletes it for good if you do nothing in the next 30 days. Only a login counts as activity, not scheduled pipeline runs.
- The workspace goes read-only when the 14-day trial ends, until you select a plan.
- The pricing page prices Pro in euros and lists extra seats and concurrency in dollars, so check the currency before you upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is Buddy free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing and gives you 1 seat, 1 concurrent pipeline, 300 pipeline GB-minutes per month, and 1 GB of cache. The pricing page says no credit card is required.

### What happens when I run out of Buddy GB-minutes?

Buddy says it then charges per minute at $0.0015 per GB-minute for container and Linux VM actions. The pricing page does not say what happens to a free workspace that has no payment method.

### Does a free Buddy workspace get deleted?

It can. After 60 days with no login the workspace is frozen, and Buddy deletes it permanently if you do nothing within the next 30 days.
