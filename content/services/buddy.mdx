You build pipelines in the web editor by adding actions one after another, or you define them in YAML. It suits a single developer who wants build, test, and deploy steps for one project.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs nothing and includes 1 seat, 2 concurrent pipeline runs, $1 of usage credit every month, and 1 GB of pipeline cache. Compute and storage share the credit, so it is not a fixed number of build minutes. The allowance depends on the resources assigned to each job and the storage it uses.

Free includes Buddy's features except SSO and advanced permissions, including Sandboxes. New workspaces start with a 14-day trial with Team features, $5 of usage credit, 10 GB of pipeline cache, and free extra seats. When it ends, choose Free, Pro, or Team; the workspace stays read-only until a plan is selected.

## When you hit a limit

Free has no pay-as-you-go billing. Once the $1 credit is exhausted, pipelines and sandboxes stop until the credit renews at the start of the next month or you upgrade. Compute is charged against that credit at $0.00024 per CPU-minute and per GB-minute for container and Linux VM actions. Windows and macOS apply higher RAM multipliers, while storage is billed per GB-hour. Resource settings therefore affect how quickly the credit runs out. Free allows two concurrent runs and does not sell additional seats or concurrency.

## Gotchas

- A free workspace freezes after 60 days with no activity, and Buddy deletes it for good if you do nothing in the next 30 days. Only a login counts as activity, not scheduled pipeline runs.
- The workspace goes read-only when the 14-day trial ends, until you select a plan.
- The pricing page prices Pro in euros and lists extra seats and concurrency in dollars, so check the currency before you upgrade.

## Questions people ask

### Is Buddy free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 1 seat, 2 concurrent pipeline runs, $1 of monthly compute and storage credit, and 1 GB of cache. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I use all the Buddy credit?

Pipelines and sandboxes stop until the monthly credit renews or you upgrade. The Free plan does not bill additional usage on a pay-as-you-go basis.

### Does a free Buddy workspace get deleted?

It can. After 60 days with no login the workspace is frozen, and Buddy deletes it permanently if you do nothing within the next 30 days.
