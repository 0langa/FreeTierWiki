You build a survey in a web builder and share a short link. Respondents answer on any device, and you read the results in a dashboard.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It allows 1 active survey with 25 responses per survey. An active survey is a live survey that can accept responses. When you archive a survey, it becomes inactive and its link stops working.

The plan includes survey templates. Surveys can be anonymous, or you can ask respondents for a name, email, or phone number. Respondents do not need an account.

Vidhook uses tags as its answer format. Respondents select tags to express their opinion, and the tags from all responses combine into one overall result. The free plan lists no redirect and no AI insights. Those start on the paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

When a survey reaches its response limit, it can no longer accept responses. The FAQ says respondents see a friendly message that the survey is unavailable.

The free plan has no card on file, so no bill can come from it. To raise the limit you move to a paid plan. Basic costs $9 a month for 5 active surveys and 500 responses per survey. Pro costs $19 a month for unlimited active surveys and unlimited responses per survey. The pricing page does not say if you can clear old responses to make room.

## Gotchas

- The 25 response limit applies to each survey. When it fills, the survey stops accepting answers.
- Only 1 survey can be active at a time. Archiving a survey makes its link stop working.
- AI insights need at least 3 responses and a paid plan, and redirect after completion is a paid feature.

## Questions people ask

### Is Vidhook free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and includes 1 active survey with 25 responses per survey. Paid plans start at $9 a month.

### What happens when I go over the Vidhook response limit?

The survey stops accepting responses. Respondents see a message that the survey is unavailable.

### Do respondents need an account to answer a Vidhook survey?

No. The FAQ says respondents do not need an account to complete your survey.
