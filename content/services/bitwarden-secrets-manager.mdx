Bitwarden Secrets Manager is a separate product from the Bitwarden password manager. You use a web app to organize secrets and the `bws` command-line tool or an SDK to read them from code. It suits developers who need secrets in scripts and CI.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan has unlimited secret storage at no additional cost. You can share secrets with up to 2 users. You also get up to 3 projects and up to 3 machine accounts. Machine accounts are the non-human users that read secrets through access tokens.

Two-step login is available with authenticator apps and email. Teams costs $6 per user per month and raises the limits to unlimited users and projects and up to 20 machine accounts. It also adds event logs and user groups.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists fixed free limits and no overage fee for the free plan. Paid plans charge $1 for each machine account beyond the included amount. On the free plan, you stay within 3 machine accounts, 3 projects, and 2 users, or you move to Teams.

The pricing page does not say what the app does when you try to pass a free limit. Pricing is shown in USD on an annual basis for paid plans. The page also offers a free trial for the paid plans.

## Gotchas

- Only organization owners can add Secrets Manager to an organization. Members need Secrets Manager access switched on by an owner or admin.
- The access token for a machine account appears once. Copy it before you close the window, because Bitwarden cannot show it again.
- A secret belongs to one project at a time, and machine accounts only see secrets in projects they can access.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bitwarden Secrets Manager free?

Yes. The free plan has unlimited secrets for up to 2 users, with up to 3 projects and 3 machine accounts. No credit card is needed.

### How many machine accounts does Bitwarden Secrets Manager give me for free?

The free plan allows up to 3 machine accounts. Teams includes up to 20, and Enterprise up to 50, with $1 per additional machine account.

### Do I need a paid Bitwarden password manager plan for Secrets Manager?

No. Bitwarden offers Secrets Manager for Free, Teams, and Enterprise organizations. A Families plan cannot hold it, but you can create a separate free organization.
