You work in a web dashboard and a browser extension, and you can also reach your robots through a REST API with a Bearer key. It suits people who want data from a page without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan gives you 50 credits every month, unlimited robots, 2 websites, and 3 users. It needs no credit card and has full platform access. Browse AI calls it free forever.

Credits are the unit that matters. One credit extracts 10 rows of data from a page or captures one screenshot. The pricing page gives an example: 50 products on a page cost 5 credits. Pulling each product's detail page costs 50 more. Sites marked Premium need stronger proxies, so each run on them costs a minimum of 2 to 10 credits.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 50 credits run out. There is no card on file, so no bill can follow.

Credits reset when the billing cycle ends, and unused ones do not carry over. If you upgrade before the cycle ends, unused credits are added on top of the new plan's credits. The first paid plan adds 2,000 credits a month. It costs $19 a month when billed annually; the monthly billing tab lists $48 a month.

## Gotchas

- Monitoring multiplies cost. The pricing page works out that checking 50 detail pages every 3 days costs about 500 credits a month, far above the free 50.
- The free plan covers 2 websites. More websites are a paid add-on on higher plans.
- Sites that block bots are marked Premium and use rotating residential proxies and captcha solving, which raises the credit cost per run.

## Questions people ask

### Is Browse AI free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 50 credits per month, unlimited robots, 2 websites, and 3 users. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I run out of Browse AI credits?

The pricing page does not say. Credits reset at the end of the billing cycle, and unused credits do not carry over. You can move to a paid plan for more credits.

### Does Browse AI need a credit card?

No. The sign-up steps on the pricing page say no credit card is required.
