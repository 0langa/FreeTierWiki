Geoapify is a location platform with map tiles, geocoding and autocomplete, places search, routing, route matrices, and isochrones. Usage is counted in credits per day. The Free plan stands out because Geoapify allows it in commercial projects and in production, as long as you stay within its limits and show attribution.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan includes 3,000 credits a day, needs no credit card, and allows up to 5 requests a second. Isochrones go up to 15 min and isodistances up to 10 km, and support is best effort. In general, 1 API request costs 1 credit for simple requests like the Geocoding API, Places API, and Routing API. You can use the Free plan for commercial websites, apps, and business projects, including in production, if you stay within its credit quota, rate limits, and feature limits and include the required Geoapify and data-source attribution. You can stay on it for as long as it meets your needs.

## When you hit a limit

Geoapify says its limits are soft. If your usage constantly goes over the daily quota, Geoapify contacts you and asks you to upgrade, and it reserves the right to block your account if you ignore those emails. For the per-second rate limit, it says it generally does not throttle when you go over, but it may restrict you if your rate stays above your plan and affects the platform. The smallest paid plan, API 10, includes 10,000 credits a day for $59 billed monthly.

## Gotchas

- Attribution is required on the Free plan, including a follow link to Geoapify near the map or results.
- Heavier calls such as isolines cost more resources than map tiles, so check credit costs per API before relying on the daily total.
- Unused daily credits do not help on a busy day; the quota is per day, not per month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Geoapify free for commercial use?

Yes. Geoapify says the Free plan can be used for commercial websites and apps, including in production, with attribution.

### How many requests does the Geoapify Free plan include?

It includes 3,000 credits a day, and a simple request such as a geocode or a route costs 1 credit.

### What happens if I go over the daily quota?

The limits are soft. Geoapify contacts you to upgrade if you keep going over and may block accounts that ignore its emails.
