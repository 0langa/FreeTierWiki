You create resources in the Upstash Console and reach them over REST with SDKs for TypeScript, Python, and other languages. This page covers the products together. The Redis and Vector pages in this atlas go deeper on each one.

## What the free plan gives you

Each product has a Free plan at $0 for prototypes and hobby projects. Redis includes 256 MB of data, 10 GB of monthly bandwidth, and 500K commands per month. Vector includes 1 GB of data and 10K queries and updates per day, with 1,536 dimensions at most per vector.

QStash, the HTTP message queue, allows 1,000 messages per day, with messages up to 1 MB and a delay of up to 7 days. Workflow, built on QStash, allows 1,000 steps per day and up to 1,000 steps per workflow run. Both cap monthly bandwidth at 50 GB. The pricing page says you can create up to 10 databases for free.

## When you hit a limit

For QStash and Workflow, the pricing FAQ calls the daily limits soft limits that apply only if you consistently exceed them. Short spikes do not block you at once. If usage stays over, Upstash contacts you about an upgrade. If it cannot reach you, workflow runs may start failing with quota errors.

Past the daily step limit, QStash returns errors for step execution, the Workflow SDK retries after a short delay, and failed runs land in the dead letter queue. For Redis and Vector, the pricing pages do not say what happens on the Free plan. Pay as you go starts after you enter a credit card.

## Gotchas

- Each delivery attempt counts as one QStash message, so retries use your 1,000 messages a day.
- Some Workflow steps count as two billed steps, such as `context.call` and `context.invoke`. Parallel steps add one extra billed step each.
- Kafka does not appear on the current pricing pages. Use QStash for queued HTTP delivery instead.

## Questions people ask

### Is Upstash free?

Yes. Each product has a Free plan. Redis includes 256 MB of data and 500K commands a month, and QStash allows 1,000 messages a day.

### What happens when I hit the Upstash free limits?

For QStash and Workflow, the daily limits are soft limits that apply when you consistently exceed them. Upstash contacts you first, and runs may fail with quota errors if you do not respond.

### Does Upstash still offer Kafka?

The current pricing pages do not list Kafka. They list QStash and Workflow for messaging and queues.
