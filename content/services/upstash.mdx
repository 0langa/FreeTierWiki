You create resources in the Upstash Console and reach them over REST with SDKs for TypeScript, Python, and other languages. This page covers the products together. The Redis and Vector pages in this atlas go deeper on each one.

## What the free plan gives you

Each product has a Free plan at $0 for prototypes and hobby projects. Redis includes 250MB of data, 10 GB of monthly bandwidth, and 500K commands per month. Vector includes 1 GB of data and 10K queries and updates per day, with 1,536 dimensions at most per vector.

QStash, the HTTP message queue, allows 1,000 messages per day, with messages up to 1 MB and a delay of up to 7 days. Workflow, built on QStash, allows 1,000 steps per day and up to 1,000 steps per workflow run. Both cap monthly bandwidth at 50 GB. Redis Free has 1 database. Machine-readable pricing scopes the first 10 database base-fee exemption to pay-as-you-go; its usage is still billed.

## When you hit a limit

The QStash and Workflow FAQs describe daily quotas as soft: occasional spikes are tolerated, with contact about an upgrade before persistent excess can cause errors. Another Workflow answer says daily exhaustion causes errors, retries and eventual dead-letter failures. Their scope is not reconciled, so do not assume every Free spike is accepted.

Redis storage is 250MB on the card but 256MB in the comparison. Current Redis monthly and Vector daily exhaustion are not fully explained. Pay-as-you-go requires a card and has separate usage billing.

## Gotchas

- Each delivery attempt counts as one QStash message, so retries use your 1,000 messages a day.
- Some Workflow steps count as two billed steps, such as `context.call` and `context.invoke`. Parallel steps add one extra billed step each.
- Kafka does not appear on the current pricing pages. Use QStash for queued HTTP delivery instead.

## Questions people ask

### Is Upstash free?

Yes. Each product has a Free plan. Redis includes 250MB of data and 500K commands a month, and QStash allows 1,000 messages a day.

### What happens when I hit the Upstash free limits?

The FAQs describe soft quotas and eventual errors, but another Workflow answer describes errors at the daily cap. Exact Free enforcement remains unclear. Redis and Vector have separate limits.

### Does Upstash still offer Kafka?

The current pricing pages do not list Kafka. They list QStash and Workflow for messaging and queues.
