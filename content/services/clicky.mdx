Clicky is a web analytics service known for showing visitors on your site in real time. It also tracks outbound links, downloads, goals, and campaigns. It suits developers and site owners who want a live view of a small site's traffic.

## What the free plan gives you

Clicky has free and paid plans. The free plan tracks 1 website with up to 3,000 daily page views in total, and free plans are limited to 30 days of history.

All new accounts automatically get a free 21-day trial of premium service. The trial gives access to all features and lets you track up to 3 websites and 1,000,000 daily page views in total across all sites. You can upgrade any time during or after the trial. Clicky is compatible with Google Analytics campaign tags, and you can set up to 30 goals per website.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a free site passes 3,000 page views in a day, whether tracking pauses or data is dropped. The limit counts per day, so a single busy day matters more than a monthly total.

The pricing page describes no paid overage for the free plan. To go past the limits, the Pro plan is listed at $9.99 a month and covers up to 1,000 websites and up to 20M daily page views, with indefinite history except individual visitors and actions, which are kept for 6 months.

## Gotchas

- The daily page view limit means a traffic spike from one link can push you over even if the monthly total is small.
- Free history is only 30 days, so long-term trends need a paid plan or exports.
- The 21-day trial shows premium features that the free plan may not keep.

## Questions people ask

### Is Clicky free?

Yes. The free plan tracks 1 website with up to 3,000 daily page views and 30 days of history.

### Is there a Clicky trial?

Yes. Every new account gets a free 21-day trial of premium service for up to 3 websites.

### How long does Clicky keep data on the free plan?

Free plans are limited to 30 days of history.
