Moesif is an analytics and monetization platform for API and AI products. It logs API calls and user actions, shows usage per customer and endpoint, and can meter usage for billing. It suits developers who run a public or partner API and want to see who uses it and how. Moesif has been acquired by WSO2 and operates as an independent subsidiary.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 a month and is described as the essentials for small projects. It covers 30K events per month, keeps the last 7 days of data, and charges no transaction fees. The comparison table lists unlimited team members on Free.

Events include logged API calls and user actions tracked from a Moesif client integration; events skipped by sampling or skip logic do not count. You can also start a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan with 10M events and no credit card required. After the 14-day trial, the account is converted to the Free plan automatically.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when an account on the Free plan goes past 30K events in a month. For self-serve paid plans, additional events are pay-as-you-go and invoiced monthly, and you can set usage alerts in subscription settings.

Growth is pay as you go from $132 a month for 1M to 15M events and keeps the last 1 year of data. All paid plans include 1 year of data retention. Each additional team member on self-service plans is $75 a month.

## Gotchas

- Only the last 7 days of data are kept on Free, so weekly comparisons are the most you get.
- API traffic adds up quickly; at 30K events a month, a busy endpoint can use the allowance in days.
- The trial falls back to Free when it ends, so data older than 7 days stops being available.

## Questions people ask

### Is Moesif free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 a month for 30K events per month with the last 7 days of data retained.

### What happens after the Moesif trial?

After the 14-day trial, your account is converted to the Free plan automatically.

### What counts as an event in Moesif?

Logged API calls plus user actions tracked from a client integration. Events skipped by sampling do not count.
