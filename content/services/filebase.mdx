You use Filebase through its web console, any S3 client or AWS SDK, or the IPFS tools. It suits developers who want an easy place to pin files or to try S3 storage.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is meant for testing and learning. It includes 5 GB of storage, shared across object storage and IPFS. You also get 500 pinned files, which are IPFS objects kept for persistence on the network.

IPFS egress is 10 GB. Object egress, meaning downloads from object storage, is listed as free. Class A operations are write-heavy requests such as uploads, and Class B operations are reads such as downloads. The Free plan includes 100K Class A and 1M Class B operations.

You get 1 bucket and 1 IPNS name. Image storage is 5 GB too, with limited transformations.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what Filebase does when you reach a limit. It lists additional storage, extra IPFS bandwidth, and extra operations as not included on the Free plan, and no card is needed. So the entry records no overage bill.

To get more, you move to Pro at $7.50 per month. Pro includes 500 GB of storage, unlimited pinned files, 250 GB of IPFS egress, and custom domains. Extra storage then costs $0.015 per GB.

## Gotchas

- The sign-up guide mentions 5 GB of bandwidth per month for S3 storage, but the pricing page lists object egress as free. Check your limits in the console.
- Free accounts get one bucket and one access key pair per account.
- Custom domains are a Pro feature, and disposable email addresses are not accepted at sign-up.

## Questions people ask

### Is Filebase free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan gives you 5 GB of storage shared across object storage and IPFS, 500 pinned files, and 10 GB of IPFS egress.

### Does Filebase need a credit card?

No. The sign-up guide says a Filebase account is free to create with no credit card. You do need a confirmed email address.

### What does Filebase Pro cost?

Pro costs $7.50 per month and includes 500 GB of storage. Extra storage is $0.015 per GB.
