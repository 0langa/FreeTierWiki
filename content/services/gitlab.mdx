You use GitLab.com in the browser and push code with Git. Each project has its own repository, issues, and merge requests. It suits solo developers, open-source maintainers, and private groups of up to 5 people; public top-level groups are exempt from that user cap.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for personal projects and open-source work. It includes 5 users per private top-level group, 400 compute minutes per month, source code management with CI/CD, 10 GiB of Git repository and LFS storage per project, and static application security testing. When you create a project you can also turn on Secret Detection.

The compute quota is on by default for Free namespaces on GitLab.com. Usage resets on the first day of each month. You can bring your own runners and storage.

The Premium plan costs $29 per user per month, billed annually. It has unlimited licensed users and 10,000 compute minutes per month.

## When you hit a limit

When a compute quota is on, you get notices as you near it. At exhaustion, instance runners stop new jobs and drop pending or retried jobs. Already-running jobs can continue until namespace usage exceeds the quota by 1,000 compute minutes, then they are dropped too.

You can purchase additional compute minutes. The docs say purchased minutes are used only after the monthly quota runs out and roll over if unused, with a stated 12-month validity. Expiry is not yet enforced, but GitLab does not guarantee use after expiry. Runners that you host yourself do not use the quota, so a self-hosted runner is the way to keep running pipelines without paying for more minutes.

## Gotchas

- GitLab may ask for a phone number or card before you can use hosted runners. A self-managed runner avoids the hosted-runner verification step.
- The five-user limit applies only to private top-level groups; public top-level groups and qualifying community programs are exempt. The compute quota applies to the group, no matter how many users it has.
- AI features come from a separate credits add-on and are not part of the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is GitLab free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 5 users per private top-level group, 400 compute minutes per month, and 10 GiB of storage.

### Does GitLab need a credit card?

There is no card for the Free subscription itself. GitLab can ask some users for a card to verify their identity, including before running jobs on GitLab-hosted runners. Verification is separate from billing and does not charge the card.

### What happens when I use up my GitLab CI minutes?

GitLab applies enforcement measures once you exceed the quota. You can buy more compute minutes or run your own runners.
