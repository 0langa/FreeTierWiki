You use GitLab.com in the browser and push code with Git. Each project has its own repository, issues, and merge requests. It suits solo developers, open-source maintainers, and groups of up to 5 people.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for personal projects and open-source work. It includes 5 users per top-level group, 400 compute minutes per month, source code management with CI/CD, 10 GiB of adjustable storage, and static application security testing. When you create a project you can also turn on Secret Detection.

The compute quota is on by default for Free namespaces on GitLab.com. Usage resets on the first day of each month. You can bring your own runners and storage.

The Premium plan costs $29 per user per month, billed annually. It has unlimited licensed users and 10,000 compute minutes per month.

## When you hit a limit

When a compute quota is on, you get notices as you near it. GitLab applies enforcement measures once you exceed it.

You can purchase additional compute minutes. The docs say purchased minutes are used only after the monthly quota runs out, and they roll over if unused. Runners that you host yourself do not use the quota, so a self-hosted runner is the way to keep running pipelines without paying for more minutes.

## Gotchas

- The shared runners need a credit or debit card on file. GitLab verifies it with a one-dollar authorization and makes no charge. A bring-your-own runner needs no card.
- The free plan allows 5 users per top-level group. The compute quota applies to the group, no matter how many users it has.
- AI features come from a separate credits add-on and are not part of the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is GitLab free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 5 users per top-level group, 400 compute minutes per month, and 10 GiB of storage.

### Does GitLab need a credit card?

The pricing page says no card is needed for the Free plan. You do need a credit or debit card to use the shared runners, and GitLab verifies it with a one-dollar authorization and makes no charge.

### What happens when I use up my GitLab CI minutes?

GitLab applies enforcement measures once you exceed the quota. You can buy more compute minutes or run your own runners.
