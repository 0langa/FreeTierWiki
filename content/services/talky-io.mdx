You use Talky in a web browser. You create a room with a name, share the link, and everyone joins from that link. It suits friends, families, and small teams that want a call without accounts.

## What the free plan gives you

Talky is free. The site says it needs no downloads, no signup, and no payment. It offers video chat and screen sharing for groups up to 6 people. Instead of accounts, Talky uses unique room links that you share.

The About page also lists locked rooms. Talky works in Chrome, Firefox, Opera, Safari, and Edge, and Android users can use a compatible browser. The iOS app is no longer maintained, according to the system requirements page. The help page says screen sharing works only in Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox, though shared screens show in all supported browsers. The site shows ads to cover its running costs.

## When you hit a limit

The site says groups can have up to 6 people. It does not say what happens when a seventh person tries to join. There is no paid plan, so nothing bills you. The FAQ says the costs of running the site are significant and that ads keep Talky free for all users.

If a call fails, the FAQ suggests checking that your browser can use your camera and microphone, and that your pasted link matches exactly.

## Gotchas

- Rooms are for groups up to 6 people, so larger meetings need another tool.
- The iPhone and iPad app is no longer maintained. Use a supported browser on the device instead, if it works for you.
- Privacy-oriented browsers or add-ons can break calls if `media.peerconnection.enabled` is set to false in `about:config`.

## Questions people ask

### Is Talky.io free?

Yes. The site says no downloads, signup, or payment are required. It shows ads to cover its costs.

### Does Talky.io need an account?

No. Talky does not use accounts. You create a room with a name and share its link.

### How many people can join a Talky call?

The homepage says Talky is for groups up to 6 people.
