You use Zoho Assist from the web console, a Windows desktop plugin, or the mobile apps. A support session needs the customer present, while unattended access reaches computers where an agent is installed.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan has 1 remote support technician, 5 unattended computers, and 1 concurrent session. Zoho says session length has no limit when you connect to Windows, Mac, or Linux computers. Zoho lists Windows, Mac, Linux, and Chromebook OS as supported platforms in the free version.

The free plan list includes instant chat, the desktop app, UAC compatibility, clipboard sharing, Mac support, user management, organization roles, and integrations with Zoho Desk and Zoho SalesIQ. You do not need a credit card to sign up.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan lists 1 concurrent session and 5 unattended computers. The pricing pages do not say what happens when you go past them, and they do not describe a bill or a throttle for the free plan. Zoho says you can upgrade to a paid plan at any time if you need more. The pricing page also shows a 15-day trial for paid plans with no card.

## Gotchas

- The free plan list does not name file transfer, remote commands, remote audio, multi-monitor navigation, or the Zoho Assist API. Check the plan comparison before you rely on them.
- A live support session needs the customer to be present. The customer gets a small runtime application when they join, so they do not install anything beforehand.
- The Windows desktop plugin works only on Windows.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Assist free?

Yes. The free plan covers 1 technician, 5 unattended computers, and 1 concurrent session. Zoho does not limit the length of sessions on the free plan.

### Does Zoho Assist need a credit card?

No. Zoho says you do not need to enter credit card details to sign up for an account.

### Can I use Zoho Assist for free on Mac and Linux?

Yes. Zoho lists Windows, Mac, Linux, and Chromebook OS as supported in the free version. The free plan page does not say which features work on each system.
