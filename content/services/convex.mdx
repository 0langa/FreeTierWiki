You write backend functions in TypeScript in a `convex/` folder, run `npx convex dev` to sync them, and call them from your app with a Convex client library. It suits realtime apps built by small teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free and Starter plan. Free is $0 per month, and Starter is pay as you go. Both allow 1 to 6 developers and 40 deployments, and both share the same performance tier.

Included each month on Free: 1 million function calls, 0.5 GB of database storage, 1 GB of database I/O, 1 GB of file storage, and 0.5 GB of search storage. You also get 20 GB-hours of action compute and 1 GB of data egress. Query and mutation compute is free on serverless deployments.

## When you hit a limit

The Convex limits page says Free has hard resource caps. Starter can exceed its included amounts with usage-based pricing, for example $2.20 per additional 1 million function calls. The pages do not say exactly what the app sees when a Free cap is reached.

If you want to go past the caps, switch the plan to Starter or move to Professional, which costs $25 per developer per month.

## Gotchas

- Function calls include explicit client calls, scheduled runs, subscription updates, and file accesses, so a realtime app can use them quickly.
- Each index is priced as another copy of the table for database storage.
- Included amounts are shared across regions, and other regions cost more than US regions.

## Questions people ask

### Is Convex free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 1 million function calls, 0.5 GB of database storage, and 1 GB of file storage each month.

### What happens when I go over the Convex free limits?

The Convex limits page says the Free plan has hard resource caps. Starter lets you pass the included amounts and pay for the extra usage.

### How many developers can use the Convex free plan?

The Free and Starter plan covers 1 to 6 developers. Professional covers 1 to 20 developers.
