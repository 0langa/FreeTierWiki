deed.page hosts static sites that are published by a single HTTP request. Send an HTML file or a tarball of a folder with PUT or POST to the sites endpoint, or call the remote MCP server, and the JSON response returns a live HTTPS address on a subdomain plus a token. It is aimed at AI agents and scripts that need to put a page online without a human step.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free plan at $0 with 25 sites, 25 MB per site, 60 publishes an hour, 10 versions kept, 1 custom domain, public sites only, and 1 webhook. Free also has a 10 GB monthly fair-use transfer cap per owner; files served from the CDN cache do not count. It says there is no signup and that an email is never required. Analytics, the MCP server and subdomains come with every plan, and sites never expire. Free sites show a small deed.page badge; a one-time $9 clean publish removes it from one site.

Paid plans attach to the same token. Hobby is $3 a month for 250 sites of 100 MB with no badge, and Pro is $15 a month for 2,500 sites of 500 MB. A human pays a Stripe checkout link.

## When you hit a limit

Publishing past the site limit returns quota_sites (403); exceeding the site-size cap returns quota_bytes (413). A request body over 4 MB returns request_too_large. Larger sites need manifest uploads and must still fit the per-site cap.

At 80% of the monthly transfer cap, the agent receives an upgrade warning. Above the cap, that owner’s sites show a limit page until the next month or an upgrade.

Exceeding the hourly publish limit returns rate_limited (429), retry_after and a Retry-After header. Free limits stop requests or serving instead of adding charges.

## Gotchas

- The token is shown once and the service keeps only a hash of it. Without the optional recovery email, a lost token cannot be restored, and the sites can no longer be updated.
- Publishing new slugs with the same token keeps them under one owner, so the 25-site limit applies per token.
- Free sites are always public; the site password is a Hobby and Pro feature, and only static files are served.

## Questions people ask

### Is deed.page free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 with 25 sites of up to 25 MB each, and the pricing page says it needs no signup and no email.

### Do I need an account to publish?

No. The first publish returns a token in the response. Keep it and send it as a Bearer header to update, list or delete your sites.

### What happens when I reach 25 sites?

New sites are refused with quota_sites and status 403 until you delete a site or move the token to a paid plan. Existing sites stay online unless a separate limit, such as the monthly transfer cap, is reached.
