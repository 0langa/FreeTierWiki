You model products in the Crystallize web app and read them through GraphQL APIs from your own frontend. Accelerators give you a ready storefront to start from. It suits developers and small teams who build custom shops.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called Particle and is described as a free tier with pay as you go, for hobbyists and testing. It gives 1 tenant per project, unlimited users, unlimited languages, and the full feature set. Support is by community and online docs.

Metering includes 25 orders, 100 items, 5 GB of bandwidth, and 25000 API calls each month. Items count products, documents, folders, assets, grids, and topics, per language version. API calls count inbound requests and outbound webhooks. Billing is by credit card. The next plan, Atom, costs $949 per month plus metering.

## When you hit a limit

Use above the included amounts is billed, not blocked. The pricing page lists the Particle rates: $2.50 for each extra order, $0.10 for each extra item, $0.50 for each extra GB, and $10.00 for each extra 25k API calls. Because the plan bills a credit card, a busy catalog or traffic spike can create a charge.

The page says Particle and Atom plans can be ended at any time, and usage in the current month is still billed. It also says prices can change on 12 months of notice. Watch items closely, since they count per language and are counted at the end of each month.

## Gotchas

- A credit card is the billing method on the Particle plan, so there is no card-free way to start.
- Items are counted per language version, so a multilingual catalog uses the 100 included items faster.
- Tokens for the Flare AI tools are metered only on custom plans, and the Particle column shows no token line.

## Questions people ask

### Is Crystallize free?

Yes, the Particle plan is free to start. It includes 25 orders, 100 items, 5 GB of bandwidth, and 25000 API calls each month, and then bills for extra use.

### Does Crystallize need a credit card?

Yes. The pricing page lists credit card as the billing method for the Particle plan.

### What happens when I go over the Crystallize free limits?

Extra use is billed at set rates, such as $2.50 per extra order and $0.50 per extra GB.
