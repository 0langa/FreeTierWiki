You work in the Aura console, which has tools for importing, querying, and visualizing, or you connect from code with a Neo4j driver. It suits students and developers who want to try graph databases without installing anything.

## What the free plan gives you

AuraDB Free costs $0, and the pricing page says no credit card or other payment method is required. Each account can create one Free instance. To create more, you must upgrade.

The quotas guide lists 200,000 nodes, 400,000 relationships, and 1GB storage. The instance runs in a single availability zone and holds one database. It includes the Aura Console, Data Importer, Query API, Dashboards, and Aura Agent, and it gives you the basic version of Bloom. The quotas and backup guides include one on-demand backup at a time, but no scheduled backups, point-in-time recovery, or SLA. Support comes from the community.

## When you hit a limit

The quotas guide publishes fixed graph-size and storage limits. It does not specify the exact error or write behavior when a count is reached. Free needs no payment method; upgrading to a paid instance is a separate choice. Do not rely on automatic expansion.

Inactivity is the limit that bites more often. Aura pauses a Free instance after 72 hours of inactivity. You cannot pause it yourself. If it stays paused for more than 30 days, Aura deletes the instance and all its data.

## Gotchas

- The generated instance password appears once. Copy it or download the credentials file, because Aura cannot recover it later.
- Free instances do not resume on their own after a long pause. You resume them with the Play button on the instance card, and resuming can take a few minutes to a few hours.
- Free includes only one manually triggered backup at a time. Keep a separate export if data matters, because an instance paused for over 30 days is deleted.

## Questions people ask

### Is Neo4j AuraDB free?

Yes. AuraDB Free costs $0 and needs no credit card. Each account gets one Free instance with 200,000 nodes, 400,000 relationships, and 1GB storage.

### Does Neo4j AuraDB Free pause or delete my data?

Aura pauses a Free instance after 72 hours of inactivity. If it stays paused for more than 30 days, Aura deletes the instance and its data.

### How many Neo4j AuraDB Free instances can I create?

You can create one Free instance per account. To create more instances, you need to upgrade your tier.
