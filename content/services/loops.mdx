You work in the Loops editor and workflow builder, and your app talks to it through the REST API, SDKs, or integrations. It fits small SaaS teams that want email handled without a separate tool for each kind of message.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan stores unlimited contacts. It lets you send up to 4,000 emails in any rolling 30-day window, with campaign and workflow emails limited to the first 1,000 subscribed contacts. Marketing and transactional sends count together toward the 4,000.

Free emails include Loops branding. Free accounts can send up to 10 emails per second. Loops does not charge for team seats, so you can invite your whole team; one person can join at most 3 free teams. Paid plans are priced by the number of subscribed contacts and remove the branding.

## When you hit a limit

The billing documentation says adding contacts past 1,000 does not lock your account. The first 1,000 subscribed contacts can receive campaign and workflow emails; later contacts can enter workflows but will not receive those messages. Transactional emails can still reach recipients outside that subscribed-contact limit, and count toward the same 4,000-send allowance.

If you send faster than 10 emails per second, Loops queues the extra emails until it can send them. The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 4,000 sends inside the 30-day window, so check the dashboard before a big send.

## Gotchas

- New subscribed contacts beyond the first 1,000 cannot receive campaign or workflow emails on Free. Transactional recipients are not restricted by that contact count.
- You must use your own domain. Free subdomains from hosts such as Vercel or Netlify cannot be sending domains.
- Loops recommends a subdomain such as `mail.yourcompany.com` for sending, rather than your root domain.

## Questions people ask

### Is Loops free?

Yes. The free plan has unlimited stored contacts and up to 4,000 sends in any rolling 30 days, with campaign and workflow sends restricted to the first 1,000 subscribed contacts. Free emails carry Loops branding.

### Does Loops need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says Loops is free to start, with no credit card required.

### Can I send transactional email on the Loops free plan?

Yes. The free plan counts transactional and marketing sends together toward the 4,000 sends. You create the email in Loops and send it through the API.
