X-Ray receives trace data from your application and from AWS services that already integrate with it, such as Lambda. You view the traces and a trace map in the AWS console. It suits developers running on AWS who want to see where a request spends its time across functions, databases, and APIs.

## What the free plan gives you

X-Ray pricing now appears on the Amazon CloudWatch pricing page, in the CloudWatch free tier. Each month the first 100,000 traces recorded are free, and the first 1,000,000 traces retrieved or scanned are free.

A trace represents one request to your application and can include many data points, such as calls to other services and to a database. You can set a sampling rate so that only part of your requests are recorded. Instrumenting often needs only configuration changes, and the X-Ray daemon is included on Lambda.

## When you hit a limit

On Paid accounts without optional project controls, tracing continues past the free amounts. Past them, traces recorded cost $0.000005 per trace, and traces retrieved or scanned cost $0.0000005 per trace.

AWS gives an example: 2,000 requests per hour with a 10% sampling rate over 31 days records 148,800 traces. After the 100,000 free traces, 48,800 are billable, for a bill of $0.244 that month. Lowering the sampling rate is the simplest way to stay inside the free amount.

## Gotchas

- The number of traces follows your traffic and sampling rate, so a traffic spike raises the count without any change to your code.
- The newer transaction search and Application Signals features are priced separately from classic X-Ray traces.
- A new AWS Free account is suspended after 6 months or depleted credits; data is retained 90 days for a Paid upgrade before deletion.

## Questions people ask

### Is AWS X-Ray free?

Within limits. The first 100,000 traces recorded and the first 1,000,000 traces retrieved or scanned each month are free.

### How do I keep X-Ray inside the free tier?

Lower the sampling rate so that fewer requests are recorded. AWS's own example records 148,800 traces at a 10% sampling rate.

### Where is the X-Ray pricing page?

The old X-Ray pricing address now leads to CloudWatch, and the X-Ray trace prices are listed on the Amazon CloudWatch pricing page.
