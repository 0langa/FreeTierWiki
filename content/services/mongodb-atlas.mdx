You manage Atlas from a web console, the Atlas CLI, or the Clusters API, and your app talks to it through the official MongoDB drivers. The Free cluster suits students and solo developers who want a real hosted MongoDB without a bill.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Free cluster at no cost, with 512 MB of storage and shared RAM and vCPU. The docs add the rest. Data storage counts uncompressed documents plus their indexes. The cluster is a 3-node replica set, and you cannot change its memory or storage size.

Throughput is capped at 100 operations per second, and sorts in memory are limited to 32 MB. A Free cluster accepts up to 500 connections and holds up to 100 databases and 500 collections. Data transfer is limited to 10 GB in and 10 GB out per rolling seven days. You get one Free cluster per project, in a subset of regions on AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure.

## When you hit a limit

The docs describe throttling, not charges. If you pass the operations per second limit or the transfer limit, Atlas throttles the network speed and adds a one second cooldown on the affected connection before it resumes. Queued work waits its turn, so requests can take more than a second.

Storage works differently. The cap is 0.5 GB, and Free clusters do not expand storage on their own. To get more room, you move to a paid cluster. The docs do not describe what happens to writes the moment you pass the cap.

## Gotchas

- Atlas pauses a Free cluster after 30 days with zero connections. You can resume it, but the docs advise exporting your data if you will not use the cluster for a long time.
- Free clusters have no backups. The docs point you to `mongodump` and `mongorestore` for manual copies.
- Free clusters do not support server-side JavaScript, so `$where` and map-reduce do not work. The docs also list limits on access to collections in the `local`, `admin`, and `config` databases.

## Questions people ask

### Is MongoDB Atlas free?

Yes. The pricing page lists a Free cluster at no cost, and it says the cluster is free forever. It has 512 MB of storage and shared compute.

### What happens when I go over the MongoDB Atlas free tier limits?

Atlas throttles the cluster when you pass the operations or data transfer limits. The docs describe no charge for this. To get past the storage cap, you upgrade to a paid cluster.

### Does a MongoDB Atlas free cluster pause?

Yes. Atlas pauses a Free cluster after 30 days of inactivity with zero connections. You can usually resume it, unless it ran an old version Atlas cannot restore.
