You send your LLM calls through one gateway endpoint, or add the Respan SDK to trace calls you already make. You read the results in a web platform, a command line tool, or an MCP server. It fits developers and small teams who run AI features in production.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and covers the full platform. The pricing page says you can start with your team and need no credit card. You get 100k logs and 1k scores per month, 5 datasets, 5 prompts, and 2 evaluators.

Log retention on the Free plan is 7 days. The gateway proxy accepts 412 requests per minute, and ingestion is capped at 412 logs per minute. The Team plan costs $199 per month, billed yearly, and raises these limits. It also allows unlimited datasets, evaluators, and prompts, and it adds a SOC 2 report.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists extra logs at $8 per 100k and extra scores at $1 per 1k. It does not say whether a Free account can buy them or is stopped at the cap. Treat 100k logs as the ceiling and check your usage in the platform.

Gateway calls that use Respan's own provider keys are paid with prepaid gateway credits. When the balance runs out, those requests fail with a 401 error and the code `activation_required`. Requests that use your own provider key keep working.

## Gotchas

- Gateway credits are separate from your plan. They pay for model calls made with Respan's provider keys. Use your own provider keys to avoid buying credits.
- Logs stay for only 7 days on the Free plan, so export what you need to keep.
- Team plan features such as a SOC 2 report and a private Slack channel start on the Team plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Respan free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card, and includes 100k logs and 1k scores per month.

### Is Respan the same as Keywords AI?

Yes. Respan is the new name of Keywords AI, and the pricing page still shows Keywords AI, Inc. in its footer.

### What happens when I run out of gateway credits?

Gateway requests that need Respan's provider keys fail with a 401 error until you add credits. Requests that use your own provider key keep working.
