You write workflows in Python with the `prefect` library, log in through the `prefect-cloud` command, and manage runs in the Cloud dashboard. It suits data and ML engineers who want orchestration without running their own server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby tier is free forever. It includes 2 users, 1 workspace, and up to 5 deployments. You also get 500 minutes of Prefect Serverless each month, which is managed compute run by Prefect. Run history is kept for 7 days, and the API rate limit is 625 requests per minute.

Hobby includes workflow scheduling, observability, logging, and alerting. Plan allowances cover users, workspaces, deployments, and managed Serverless compute. Support on Hobby comes through community channels, and the pricing table lists no uptime SLA.

## When you hit a limit

Hobby lists a maximum of 5 deployments; the current cited sources do not establish the precise behavior when you try to exceed that allowance. The pricing page does not say what happens when you use all 500 Serverless minutes, so check the dashboard before you plan a long run. The official self-serve plan announcement explicitly says Hobby needs no credit card. Execution credits are consumed during actual runtime, but no Free overage price or exhaustion mechanism is published. The next plan, Starter, costs $100 per month for 3 users and 20 deployments.

## Gotchas

- Five deployments count flows you deploy, so plan which flows need a schedule.
- The Hobby tier has one workspace and two users, so a shared team account runs out fast.
- Paid plans add higher workflow and compute allowances; review the plan table before moving a larger workload onto managed Serverless compute.

## Questions people ask

### Is Prefect Cloud free?

Yes. The Hobby tier is free forever with 2 users, 1 workspace, up to 5 deployments, and 500 minutes of Prefect Serverless per month.

### What happens when I reach 5 deployments on Prefect?

The plan lists five deployments, but the current sources do not confirm the exact enforcement behavior. Remove an unused deployment or upgrade if you need more capacity.

### Does Prefect Cloud need a credit card?

No. Prefect’s official self-serve plan announcement explicitly says the Hobby tier requires no credit card. You create or sign in to the account in your browser after running the login command.
