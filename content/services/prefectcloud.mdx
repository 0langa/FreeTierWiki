You write workflows in Python with the `prefect` library, log in through the `prefect-cloud` command, and manage runs in the Cloud dashboard. It suits data and ML engineers who want orchestration without running their own server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby tier is free forever. It includes 2 users, 1 workspace, and up to 5 deployments. A deployment is a server-side definition of a flow that can be scheduled and run remotely. You also get 500 minutes of Prefect Serverless each month, which is managed compute run by Prefect. Run history is kept for 7 days, and the API rate limit is 625 requests per minute.

The pricing FAQ says Hobby gives full access to core features for up to five deployed workflows. Prefect prices by seats and workspaces, not by compute or task volume. Support on Hobby comes through community channels.

## When you hit a limit

When you reach 5 deployments, you cannot create new ones until you delete an existing deployment or upgrade to a paid plan. The pricing page does not say what happens when you use all 500 Serverless minutes, so check the dashboard before you plan a long run. No card is asked, and Prefect says its pricing does not fluctuate with usage. The next plan, Starter, costs $100 per month for 3 users and 20 deployments.

## Gotchas

- Five deployments count flows you deploy, so plan which flows need a schedule.
- The Hobby tier has one workspace and two users, so a shared team account runs out fast.
- The FAQ lists no trial of paid plans. You contact the team to explore premium features.

## Questions people ask

### Is Prefect Cloud free?

Yes. The Hobby tier is free forever with 2 users, 1 workspace, up to 5 deployments, and 500 minutes of Prefect Serverless per month.

### What happens when I reach 5 deployments on Prefect?

You cannot create a new deployment until you delete an existing one or upgrade to a paid plan.

### Does Prefect Cloud need a credit card?

No card is listed for Hobby. You create the account in your browser after running the login command.
