Counter.dev is a small web analytics service that aims to make analytics simple and, by keeping it simple, privacy-friendly. It shows unique visitors and referrals without cookies, and it suits developers who want a quick count of who visits a personal site or side project.

## What the free plan gives you

Counter.dev says it offers a free and privacy-friendly analytics service for everyone. There is no paid tier to unlock: the main goal is the smoothest analytics experience possible, so the service does not enforce payments. Instead it asks you to consider paying what you want, and you can start paying whenever you want after you log in.

The service uses no cookies, no logging, and no IP address fingerprinting. Metrics are based on the number of unique visitors per day, and you can see which other websites link to yours. The source code is published under the AGPL-v3 license on GitHub, with separate frontend and backend projects, and collaboration is welcome.

## When you hit a limit

The site lists no traffic limits, no paid overage, and no data retention terms, so there is no documented point where tracking stops or a bill starts. Because payments are voluntary, the pages describe nothing that would charge you automatically.

The flip side is that there are no written limits to plan around. If you depend on the numbers for a business, keep your own exports or logs, and contact the team at the address on the site with questions.

## Gotchas

- The help guide says to disable tracking blockers in your own browser while you test, or your visits may not show.
- Tracking scripts can be blocked by browser extensions, so counts will miss some visitors.
- The dashboard is intentionally minimal, so it is not a place for events, funnels, or goals.

## Questions people ask

### Is Counter.dev free?

Yes. It describes itself as a free analytics service for everyone, and payment is optional: you pay what you want.

### Does Counter.dev use cookies?

No. The site says it uses no cookies, no logging, and no IP address fingerprinting.

### How do I add Counter.dev to my site?

Log in, copy the tracking code, and paste it just before the closing head tag in your site's HTML.
