DigitalOcean Functions is a serverless computing service that runs your code on demand, so you do not maintain servers for small endpoints or background tasks. You deploy functions from the control panel, the CLI, or App Platform. It suits developers who want simple serverless functions with a monthly free allowance.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists 90,000 GiB-seconds of compute free per month per account. The FAQ says the monthly free allowance includes 90,000 GiB-seconds for the account or team, that it is not prorated, does not roll over, and can be used on any apps under the same team. Functions deployed through the developer console and CLI count toward the same allowance as functions deployed through App Platform.

GiB-seconds are the number of seconds a function runs multiplied by the memory it uses, and there are no additional charges for function invocations.

## When you hit a limit

After you exceed the free allowance, additional GiB-seconds are charged at $0.0000185 each, and usage is pooled at the account level. At the end of each billing period, DigitalOcean aggregates usage and charges for the GiB-seconds above the free allowance; the invoice shows the GiB-seconds used from the allowance and those charged. The pricing page does not describe a hard stop.

## Gotchas

- Each invocation is billed for a minimum runtime of 100 ms.
- The allowance is shared across the whole account or team, not per function.
- Unused GiB-seconds do not roll over to the next month.

## Questions people ask

### Is DigitalOcean Functions free?

Within limits. Each month the first 90,000 GiB-seconds of compute per account or team are free, and invocations are not charged.

### Does the free allowance roll over?

No. The FAQ says it is not prorated and does not roll over.

### What does extra usage cost?

Additional GiB-seconds are charged at $0.0000185 each.
