Gigalixir is a platform as a service known for Elixir and Phoenix, and its pricing page also names Ruby and Node.js applications. You deploy your app, and Gigalixir runs it on replicas with optional managed PostgreSQL. The Free Tier is aimed at students and hobby projects.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Tier costs $0 a month and is described as ideal for students and hobby projects. It includes 1 app replica with 0.5 GB of memory, a Free Tier PostgreSQL database, and custom domains with SSL.

The plan comparison sets the free maximum at 0.5 GB of replica memory and 1 replica. The free database is limited to 10,000 rows and 2 concurrent connections, which the page calls perfect for prototyping. The FAQ answers that the Free Tier has no time limit and that Free Tier usage is not charged.

## When you hit a limit

The plan caps you at one replica and the row limit on the database; the pricing page does not say exactly what happens when a write would pass 10,000 rows.

To conserve resources, Free Tier apps sleep: if you do not deploy for 30 days, Gigalixir scales the app to zero. Standard Tier apps never sleep. Moving to Standard is billed per second for what you use, so a short scale-up costs only for the hours it runs.

## Gotchas

- A credit card is required at signup for all plans, including the Free Tier.
- The free app is scaled to zero after 30 days without a deploy, so an untouched app goes offline.
- SSH and remote console access, clustering, and high-availability databases are Standard features.

## Questions people ask

### Is Gigalixir free?

Yes. The Free Tier costs $0 a month and has no time limit, though a credit card is required at signup.

### How big is the free Gigalixir database?

It is limited to 10,000 rows and 2 concurrent connections.

### Does a free Gigalixir app sleep?

Yes. If you do not deploy for 30 days, Gigalixir scales the app to zero.
