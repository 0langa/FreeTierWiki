Estuary is a managed data movement platform. It captures data from databases, SaaS apps, and streams, using log-based change data capture where it can, and delivers it to warehouses, data lakes, and other destinations in real time or in batch. It also offers Kafka compatibility and a CLI, flowctl, for managing pipelines from a terminal.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Developer plan that is free, for launching your first pipelines. It covers up to 10 GB a month and 2 concurrent connector instances. The FAQ confirms the free tier allows indefinite use with a 10 GB a month limit for up to 2 connector instances.

A connector instance is a connection to one source, called a capture, or one destination, called a materialization. Two instances is therefore one source feeding one destination. Usage is measured on the volume of data sourced, transformed, and delivered.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Developer pipeline passes 10 GB in a month. The paid Cloud plan charges $0.50 per GB plus $100 per connector, with a 30-day free trial. On Cloud, the first 6 connector instances cost $100 a month each and additional instances $50 a month each.

Pay-as-you-go billing runs through Stripe, and the FAQ says a credit card can be added for it.

## Gotchas

- Change data capture of a busy database can move more data than you expect, because every change counts toward the volume.
- Adding a second destination for the same source needs a third connector instance, which is past the free limit.
- The Cloud trial and the free Developer plan are different things; check which one your account is on.

## Questions people ask

### Is Estuary free?

Yes. The Developer plan allows indefinite use up to 10 GB a month on up to 2 connector instances.

### What is a connector instance?

A connection to a source or a destination. One pipeline from one database to one warehouse uses two.

### Is there a trial of the paid plan?

Yes. The Cloud plan has a 30-day free trial.
