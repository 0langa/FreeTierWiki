CookieFirst is a consent management platform for websites. It shows a cookie banner, logs each visitor's consent, and loads third-party tracking scripts only after the visitor agrees. It integrates with Google Tag Manager and Google Consent Mode v2, so it suits developers who must meet the GDPR or similar laws on a site that runs analytics or ads.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs €0.00 a month. It includes 1 third-party script, a cookie banner only, a one-time cookie scan, one language, and IAB TCF 2.2 support. You can upgrade at any time.

Paid plans start with Basic at €9 a month or €99 a year, which covers all third-party scripts, a cookie banner or box, a monthly cookie scan, and multiple languages. You can also start a 14-day free trial, and CookieFirst says no credit card is needed for it. Consents are logged in an anonymous, encrypted database, and setup means replacing your tracking scripts with a single code snippet or installing a plugin.

## When you hit a limit

All plans have a soft limit of page views per domain per month, which includes an additional overuse of 25% on two consecutive months. If you have many domains or a lot of traffic, CookieFirst asks you to contact it about a custom offer.

The page does not say what happens if a free site goes past that soft limit for longer, or whether the banner stops showing. The tighter limit in practice is the single third-party script: managing a second tracker means moving to a paid plan.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan covers 1 third-party script, so a site with analytics and an ad pixel already needs Basic.
- The cookie scan on Free runs once, so new cookies added later are not detected automatically.
- The banner comes in one language on Free, which may not meet the needs of a multilingual audience.

## Questions people ask

### Is CookieFirst free?

Yes. The Free plan costs €0.00 a month for 1 third-party script, a cookie banner, a one-time scan, and one language.

### Do I need a credit card to try CookieFirst?

No credit card is needed for the 14-day free trial, according to CookieFirst.

### Does CookieFirst support Google Consent Mode?

Yes. CookieFirst integrates with Google Consent Mode v2 and Google Tag Manager.
