You open CloudWatch in the AWS console, or send data to it with the AWS CLI, SDKs, and the CloudWatch agent. It suits teams that already run on AWS and want monitoring next to their other services. The CloudWatch agent can also ingest telemetry from on-premises systems and other clouds; ingestion charges do not depend on where the data originated.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier is a monthly allowance, not a separate plan. The free tier lists 5 GB for ingestion, archive storage, and Logs Insights scans. The pricing page’s log example applies a 5 GB allowance separately to ingestion and archive storage. Live Tail gets 1,800 minutes per month, which is about an hour per day.

Metrics get 10 custom or detailed monitoring metrics and 1 million API requests. Dashboards get 3 custom dashboards with up to 50 metrics each, and automatic dashboards are free. Alarms get 10 alarm metrics, for standard resolution alarms only. Contributor Insights gets 1 rule and the first 1 million matching log events. Synthetics gets 100 canary runs per month.

## When you hit a limit

On Paid accounts without optional project controls, service allowances do not stop usage. CloudWatch has no up-front commitment or minimum fee, and you pay for what you use. The bill arrives at the end of the month. Usage above the free allowance is charged at the paid rates on the pricing page.

Live Tail shows how fast this adds up: after the free minutes, it costs $0.01 per minute. Three calls are always charged and never count against the free API requests: GetMetricData, GetInsightRuleReport, and GetMetricWidgetImage.

## Gotchas

- Most AWS services send their basic metrics to CloudWatch for free, but custom and detailed monitoring metrics count against the allowance of 10.
- Logs Insights scans count toward free usage. AWS’s pricing example calculates ingestion and archive storage separately, so check the meter for each kind of log usage.
- Alarm metrics in the free tier apply only to standard resolution alarms that list metrics directly and do not use a Metrics Insights query.

## Questions people ask

### Is CloudWatch free?

Partly. CloudWatch has a free tier with monthly allowances for logs, metrics, dashboards, and alarms. Usage above the allowance is billed.

### What happens when I go over the CloudWatch free tier?

On a Paid account, excess usage is charged at paid rates after applicable credits. Free accounts instead consume credits, and optional Paid project controls can pause resources at their configured limit.

### How many CloudWatch dashboards are free?

You get 3 custom dashboards with up to 50 metrics each per month. Automatic dashboards are free on top of that.
