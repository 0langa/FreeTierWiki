You open CloudWatch in the AWS console, or send data to it with the AWS CLI, SDKs, and the CloudWatch agent. It suits teams that already run on AWS and want monitoring next to their other services.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier is a monthly allowance, not a separate plan. Logs get 5 GB of data, counted across ingestion, archive storage, and data scanned by Logs Insights queries. Live Tail gets 1,800 minutes per month, which is about an hour per day.

Metrics get 10 custom or detailed monitoring metrics and 1 million API requests. Dashboards get 3 custom dashboards with up to 50 metrics each, and automatic dashboards are free. Alarms get 10 alarm metrics, for standard resolution alarms only. Contributor Insights gets 1 rule and the first 1 million matching log events. Synthetics gets 100 canary runs per month.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe any shutoff at the limit. CloudWatch has no up-front commitment or minimum fee, and you pay for what you use. The bill arrives at the end of the month. Usage above the free allowance is charged at the paid rates on the pricing page.

Live Tail shows how fast this adds up: after the free minutes, it costs $0.01 per minute. Three calls are always charged and never count against the free API requests: GetMetricData, GetInsightRuleReport, and GetMetricWidgetImage.

## Gotchas

- Most AWS services send their basic metrics to CloudWatch for free, but custom and detailed monitoring metrics count against the allowance of 10.
- The 5 GB log allowance covers ingestion, archive storage, and Logs Insights scans together, so heavy queries use it up as well.
- Alarm metrics in the free tier apply only to standard resolution alarms that list metrics directly and do not use a Metrics Insights query.

## Questions people ask

### Is CloudWatch free?

Partly. CloudWatch has a free tier with monthly allowances for logs, metrics, dashboards, and alarms. Usage above the allowance is billed.

### What happens when I go over the CloudWatch free tier?

You are charged at the paid rates at the end of the month. The pricing page does not say the service stops or pauses when you pass an allowance.

### How many CloudWatch dashboards are free?

You get 3 custom dashboards with up to 50 metrics each per month. Automatic dashboards are free on top of that.
