You use Neon from the web console, the `neon` CLI, or any Postgres driver or ORM with a connection string. It suits developers who want a managed Postgres database without running a server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs nothing, has no time limit, and needs no credit card. You can create up to 100 projects. Each project gets 100 CU-hours of Postgres compute per month, which covers about 400 hours of a 0.25 CU compute. Computes can grow up to 2 CU, or 8 GB of RAM.

Storage is 1 GB of Postgres data per project, and the total across your Free projects cannot pass 20 GB. Each project allows 10 branches and 5 GB of egress. The plan also includes 5 GB of Object Storage per project, a Functions allowance, and Managed Better Auth for up to 60k monthly active users. Object Storage and Functions do not use your CU-hours.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free project passes its compute or storage allowance. It does say you pay nothing on this plan. The Free plan needs no credit card.

The limit you will notice first is sleep. Neon scales a compute to zero after 5 minutes of inactivity, and on Free you cannot disable it. When you need more, you move to a paid plan, where usage is metered from zero at the paid rates.

## Gotchas

- The 1 GB storage limit applies per project, and a separate 20 GB cap covers all your Free projects together. Both limits are enforced.
- The Free plan allows 10 branches per project. The pricing page does not say what happens past 10, so delete old branches before you reach that count.
- Instant restore on Free defaults to a 6 hour history window, and scheduled backups are not available. Take your own dumps if the data matters.

## Questions people ask

### Is Neon free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and needs no credit card. It includes up to 100 projects with 100 CU-hours of compute each and 1 GB of storage per project.

### Does the Neon free tier sleep?

Yes. Neon scales a compute to zero after 5 minutes of inactivity, and you cannot turn this off on the Free plan.

### What happens when I go over the Neon free tier limits?

The pricing page does not describe what happens at the limit. Free plan quotas do not carry over when you upgrade, because paid plans meter usage from zero.
