You create the app in the Azure portal, the Azure CLI, or Visual Studio Code, then deploy your code to an azurewebsites.net address. The Free plan suits students and developers who want to learn the service or share a demo. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is called F1 and runs on shared compute with other customers. The pricing page gives it 60 CPU minutes per day and 1 GB of storage. The limits table adds more detail for Free: up to 10 apps in one plan, a CPU cap of 3 minutes in any 5 minute window, 1,024 MB of memory per plan, and 165 MB of bandwidth. It also allows 1 shared instance and no scale out.

Free apps get no custom domains. You use the azurewebsites.net name, and that default domain has SSL at no extra charge. Custom domain SSL is not supported on Free. Always On is not offered. The pricing page says there is no SLA for free and shared plans.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page and the limits table list the Free caps, but they do not say what happens to your app when it reaches them. Nothing on these pages describes a surprise bill from the Free plan itself. The pricing FAQ says an App Service plan carries a compute charge except for the Free tier.

Stopped apps on paid tiers still cost money. The FAQ tells you to delete apps you do not use or move them to the Free tier to avoid charges. Other Azure services you connect, such as a database, bill on their own terms. To go past the Free caps, scale the plan up to a paid tier.

## Gotchas

- Microsoft says free and shared plans are for trials, experimentation, and learning, and that production use is not supported.
- The Free plan allows 0 custom domains, so a branded address needs a paid plan.
- Azure sign-up may place a temporary $1 authorization on your card, which is reversed after verification.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure App Service free?

Yes, the Free F1 plan costs nothing. It gives 60 CPU minutes per day and 1 GB of storage on shared compute.

### Can I use a custom domain on the Azure App Service free tier?

No. The limits table lists 0 custom domains for Free, and you use the azurewebsites.net subdomain only. Custom domain SSL is not supported either.

### Is the Azure App Service free tier good for production?

No. Microsoft says production use of free and shared plans is not supported, and there is no SLA.
