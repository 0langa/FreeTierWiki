You work in the Chrome extension and the web dashboard. You can also start recipes and fetch results through an API with a Bearer key. It suits people who want data from a page without writing code.

## What the free plan gives you

Browser runs of up to 5 pages are free. A run of 6 pages costs 6 credits, including the first 5; the allowance also applies across a batch of URLs. The Free card includes 3 saved recipes, CSV/JSON export, API access, and 100 cloud credits at sign-up.

Credits are priced by where and how a page runs. One page in the cloud with JavaScript on costs 2 credits, and one page with JavaScript off costs 1. JavaScript is on by default because most modern sites need it. Browser runs above 5 pages cost 1 credit per page, and every 800 words read or generated by AI features costs 1 credit.

## When you hit a limit

The API guide documents an out-of-credits 402 response. The 100 credits are a starter amount; credits refresh each billing period on paid subscriptions and expire when the subscription ends. Paid plans start at $39 per month for 6,000 credits. The public pages do not state a free-account card requirement.

The API guide accepts up to 5,000 URLs in a bulk call, but the number actually scraped is capped by available credits. Keep free browser runs separate from cloud jobs: their credit rules differ.

## Gotchas

- The 100 credits arrive once at sign-up. They do not refill monthly on Free, and saved recipes are limited to 3.
- Turning JavaScript off halves the cloud cost of a page, but many modern sites need it to load their data.
- The 1-credit rate for a cloud page applies to single-page runs only, because multi-page runs load a full browser for every page.

## Questions people ask

### Is Simplescraper free?

You can scrape up to 5 pages in one browser run for free. You also get 100 cloud credits at sign-up and 3 saved recipes.

### How do Simplescraper credits work?

Credits pay for cloud scraping, multiple-page browser scrapes, and AI features. A cloud page costs 2 credits with JavaScript and 1 without. Credits renew monthly only on paid plans.

### Does Simplescraper need a credit card?

The public docs and pricing page do not state a card rule for the free account. They advertise 100 starting cloud credits.
