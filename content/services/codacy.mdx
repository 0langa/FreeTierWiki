You sign in with your Git provider, add a repository in the web app, and read the results there. You can also install a plugin in your editor. It suits open source maintainers and developers who want quick feedback on code quality.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan costs $0 and is free forever. It is an IDE plugin for VS Code, JetBrains, and Cursor. It scans as you type, runs security scans (SAST, SCA, and secrets) and code quality scans, and supports 38 programming languages. It also hands issues to an AI agent for auto-fixing.

For open source, the Team plan is free forever. The Team plan adds cloud-hosted scans, GitHub, Bitbucket, and GitLab integration, AI Reviewer, merge gates for pull requests, and coverage reports. Codacy's FAQ says you can keep scanning public repositories for free on the Open Source plan. Pricing says "No usage limits".

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists no usage caps for the free plans, only limits on what is included. The Developer plan does not scan in the cloud. Open source projects use the free Team plan; the page does not say how Codacy decides what counts as open source.

Private repositories are the line. The Team plan costs from $18 per developer per month billed yearly, or $21 billed monthly. It covers up to 100 private repositories and teams of up to 30 contributors. Codacy needs a seat for every Git contributor who commits to a private repository. The Business plan is a custom quote.

## Gotchas

- The 14-day trial gives full access to the platform with no credit card. After it, you pay for private repositories or keep scanning public ones for free.
- The free Developer plan is the editor plugin only. Cloud scans, pull request checks, and coverage need a Team plan or open source status.
- Seats count Git contributors on private repositories, so a larger team pays more.

## Questions people ask

### Is Codacy free?

Partly. The Developer plan is free forever as an IDE plugin, and cloud scans are free for open source projects. Cloud scans of private repositories need the Team plan.

### How much does Codacy cost for private repositories?

The Team plan starts at $18 per developer per month billed yearly, or $21 billed monthly. It covers up to 100 private repositories.

### Does Codacy need a credit card?

No. You can sign up with your Git provider and use the 14-day trial without a credit card.
