You work with Cloud Storage in the Google Cloud console, with the gcloud command-line tool, through client libraries, or through the JSON and XML APIs. It needs a Google Cloud project with billing turned on.

## What the free plan gives you

Cloud Storage has Always Free quotas that last beyond any free trial. Each month you get 5 GB-months of regional storage, which is 5 GB stored for a month. You also get 5,000 Class A operations and 50,000 Class B operations, and 100 GB of outbound data transfer from North America to all regions.

The quotas apply only to usage in us-east1, us-west1, and us-central1. Google adds up the usage across those 3 regions. The quotas do not apply to the Rapid Storage class, and Google says Always Free is subject to change.

The free quotas are tracked per billing account.

## When you hit a limit

Google does not stop your buckets at the free amounts. Usage beyond the Always Free limits is charged according to the pricing tables, once any trial credit is gone.

There is no hard cap on the free tier, so a busy bucket can run up a bill. Google suggests setting API request caps to avoid being billed for usage beyond the Always Free limits. Check your usage in the billing details for the project.

## Gotchas

- A bucket in any other region, such as one in Europe or Asia, gets no free quota.
- Tag operations are not eligible for the Always Free program, and general network usage applies to data read out to the internet beyond the free transfer.
- You must have billing enabled on the project, even if you plan to stay inside the free limits.

## Questions people ask

### Is Google Cloud Storage free?

Partly. The Always Free tier gives you 5 GB-months of regional storage per month in us-east1, us-west1, and us-central1, plus 5,000 Class A and 50,000 Class B operations.

### Does Google Cloud Storage need a credit card?

Yes. Your project needs billing enabled, which means a payment method on the billing account.

### What happens when I go over the free Cloud Storage limits?

Google bills the extra at the normal prices. There is no hard cap, so use API request caps and check your billing details.
