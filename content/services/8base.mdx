8base is a backend as a service built around GraphQL. You build data models, and 8base generates a GraphQL API with authentication, role-based access, and subscriptions; you add custom serverless functions and manage it from a CLI. It suits developers who want a GraphQL backend without running a database and API server.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month for building backend applications. It includes serverless functions, the GraphQL API, CLI access, and community support, with 1 team member.

Capacity on Free is 2,500 database rows, 0.5 GB of file storage, 1 GB-hours of serverless computing, and 2 GB of bandwidth per month. It includes 100,000 GraphQL API calls and 100,000 GraphQL subscription events per month, and up to 5 client app users, meaning people who sign up to the app you build. The Professional plan costs $50 per developer per month with a minimum of 3 developers.

## When you hit a limit

8base's FAQ says every paid plan has overages for each metered resource, billed at the end of the month, so limits do not affect a paid workspace's availability. It does not say what happens when a Free workspace reaches 2,500 rows, 100,000 API calls, or 5 client app users. The pricing page does not establish a Free-plan card requirement or a no-charge overage guarantee; paid overage behavior must not be assumed to apply to Free.

## Gotchas

- The 5 client app user limit counts everyone who registers in your app, so a public app outgrows Free almost immediately.
- Database rows are counted across all tables in a workspace, not per table.
- 8base looks quiet: the newest blog post is from August 2024 and the pricing page footer still says 2023, so check that the platform is still maintained before you build on it.

## Questions people ask

### Is 8base free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 2,500 database rows, 100,000 GraphQL API calls a month, and up to 5 client app users.

### What counts as a client app user on 8base?

Someone who signs up or registers for the application you build counts as one client app user.

### Does 8base bill overages?

Paid plans are billed for overages above their included limits at the end of each month.
