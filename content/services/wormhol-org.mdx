You use Wormhol.org in a web browser. You choose a file with the Browse button, press SHARE, and get a link to pass on. It suits people who want a plain, free way to send a file now.

## What the free plan gives you

Wormhol.org is free. The terms of service say you may share unlimited files, up to 5 GB each, with multiple people. There is no account to make and nothing to pay.

The privacy policy says the site stores your uploaded files, their names, and the time of upload, for functional reasons. Files are stored encrypted in Germany for a maximum of 3 natural days. The policy says staff do not access the contents of your files and do not share them with third parties.

It describes itself as a non-profit service. It runs no ads and accepts no donations.

## When you hit a limit

The pages list two limits: a size of 5 GB per file and a lifetime of 3 natural days per upload. A larger file is not allowed, and an older file is gone.

Nothing is billed, since the service is free and takes no payment details. If you need a longer lifetime or larger files, the pages offer no paid plan, so use another service from the list below. The terms say the site is provided with no guarantees about availability or reliability.

## Gotchas

- The privacy policy says it cannot guarantee confidentiality. Anyone who has the link can open the file, whether they are the intended recipient or not.
- Sharing copyrighted, forbidden, or harmful media is prohibited, and abuse of the site leads to a ban.
- The site stores IP addresses and access logs, and other metadata, for other purposes.
