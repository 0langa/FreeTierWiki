You manage checks in the OnlineOrNot web app, through an HTTP API, or with the `onlineornot` command line tool. It suits solo developers who run a few sites at home or in small projects.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free and aimed at home labs and personal projects. It includes 3 uptime monitors with a 3-minute check interval, and 1,000 browser check runs per month. Data is kept for 14 days. You get a limited status page with 100 email subscribers, and 2 users.

The free tier docs say notifications go through Slack, Discord, and email only. SMS alerts are not on Hobby. Pro costs from $15 per month with 10 monitors and 30-second checks, and keeps data for 12 months.

## When you hit a limit

Neither the pricing page nor the free tier docs say what happens when you pass the free limits. The docs describe the free tier as a limited plan, so plan to upgrade for more monitors.

On Pro, usage above the plan quota is billed at add-on rates. The page says that the bill shows what you see in the estimate, with no surprises. Hobby shows no usage billing.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page shows a Start trial button for Hobby and does not say how long the trial lasts. The docs say you can downgrade from a trial to the free tier.
- Alerts reach only Slack, Discord, and email on the free tier.
- The status page is limited, with 100 email subscribers.

## Questions people ask

### Is OnlineOrNot free?

Yes, in a limited form. The Hobby plan includes 3 uptime monitors with 3-minute checks, 2 users, and 14 days of data retention.

### Does OnlineOrNot have a free trial?

The pricing page shows a Start trial button, and the docs say that during a trial you can downgrade to the free tier at any time from the Team settings page.

### Which alerts does the OnlineOrNot free tier support?

The free tier docs list notifications via Slack, Discord, and email only. SMS is not available on Hobby.
