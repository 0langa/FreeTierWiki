You use Stickies in the browser or in the desktop app for Windows and macOS. It suits individuals and teams who think in notes and walls. The site lists integrations such as Google Drive, Slack, and Jira.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free-forever plan. The site offers a 14-day free trial and says no credit card is required. It lists two paid plans, Plus and Pro. The pricing page does not say which plan the trial covers.

Both plans include unlimited users and unlimited collaborators. Plus costs $5 per month billed annually, or $7 per month billed monthly, and includes 25 walls and uploads up to 500 MB per file. Pro costs $10 per month billed annually, or $14 per month billed monthly, and includes unlimited walls and uploads up to 2 GB per file.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens to your walls when the 14-day trial ends. It only says you can try Stickies for free with no credit card. Copy anything important out of your walls before day 14, since the site does not say what happens to them.

If you subscribe, you can cancel any time with refunds for unused time. An annual plan gives a discounted rate.

## Gotchas

- The free offer is a 14-day trial, not a permanent plan.
- Plus allows 25 walls. Pro removes the wall limit.
- File upload size is capped per file by plan: 500 MB on Plus and 2 GB on Pro.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stickies free?

Only as a trial. You get a 14-day free trial with no credit card. Paid plans start at $5 per month billed annually.

### Does Stickies need a credit card for the trial?

No. The site says the 14-day free trial needs no credit card. You enter a valid email address to start.

### How many walls can I make in Stickies?

Plus includes 25 walls. Pro includes unlimited walls. Both plans include unlimited users.
