You use LingoHub in the web app, through Git repository integrations, or through its API. It suits product teams and agencies that want AI translation with human review.

## What the free plan gives you

LingoHub has no permanent free plan for commercial work. It gives a 14-day free trial, and the site says no credit card is needed. The trial lets you set up your localization workflow before you choose a paid plan.

The pricing page also lists "Free for non-profits" and "Free for open source projects". It does not say how to apply or what these licenses include.

The paid plans start at 45 euros per month for Starter, with unlimited seats and 100k linguistic credits per month. Team costs 115 euros per month and Pro costs 345 euros per month. LingoHub measures translation capacity in linguistic credits, not word counts. The credits depend on the translation engine you pick and on how much content the engine processes.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say what happens when the 14-day trial ends. They do not say whether the trial has a credit cap either. Each paid plan has a monthly set of linguistic credits and a hosted character limit.

The FAQ says you can raise these limits with monthly or yearly add-ons for hosted characters, linguistic credits, and CDN use. The pricing page does not say what happens if you use all your credits before the month ends.

## Gotchas

- The trial is 14 days. The pages do not say what happens to your projects when it ends.
- You cancel a subscription by deleting your organization. The FAQ says this cannot be undone and removes the projects, data, and billing details.
- Capacity is in linguistic credits, so the same text can use more or fewer credits depending on the translation engine.

## Questions people ask

### Is LingoHub free?

LingoHub has a free 14-day trial and says it needs no credit card. It also lists free use for non-profits and open source projects. Other use needs a paid plan.

### Does LingoHub need a credit card for the trial?

No. The FAQ says LingoHub offers a 14-day free trial without requiring a credit card.

### How do I cancel LingoHub?

You cancel by deleting your organization. The FAQ says this is irreversible and removes the projects, data, and billing information.
