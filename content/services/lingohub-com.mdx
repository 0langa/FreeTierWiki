You use LingoHub in the web app, through Git repository integrations, or through its API. It suits product teams and agencies that want AI translation with human review.

## What the free plan gives you

LingoHub has no permanent free plan for commercial work. It gives a 14-day free trial, and the site says no credit card is needed. The trial lets you set up your localization workflow before you choose a paid plan.

The pricing page also lists "Free for non-profits" and "Free for open source projects". It provides a Request Sponsored Plan link, but does not publish the sponsored license allowances or approval criteria.

Platform is €209 per month billed yearly, excluding VAT, with unlimited seats and projects, 500k AI credits, 5 Mio. hosted characters, and 5 Mio. CDN requests monthly. Enterprise is quoted. Existing Starter, Team, and Pro plans keep their prior pricing and conditions. AI credit use depends on the engine and context processed.

## When you hit a limit

The trial does not automatically convert to a paid subscription. The pages do not specify post-trial project access or a numeric trial credit cap. Paid plans have monthly AI-credit, hosted-character, and CDN allowances.

The FAQ says you can raise these limits with monthly or yearly add-ons for hosted characters, AI credits, and CDN use. The pricing page does not say what happens if you use all your credits before the month ends.

## Gotchas

- The trial is 14 days with no automatic paid conversion. Post-trial project access is not specified.
- Manage subscriptions under Subscriptions & Billing. Cancellation keeps the plan active until the current billing period ends.
- Capacity is in AI credits, so the same text can use more or fewer credits depending on the translation engine.

## Questions people ask

### Is LingoHub free?

LingoHub has a free 14-day trial and says it needs no credit card. It also lists free use for non-profits and open source projects. Other use needs a paid plan.

### Does LingoHub need a credit card for the trial?

No. The FAQ says LingoHub offers a 14-day free trial without requiring a credit card.

### How do I cancel LingoHub?

Manage your subscription under Subscriptions & Billing in your workspace. You can cancel at any time, and the plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.
