100ms offers SDKs and hosted infrastructure for video conferencing, interactive live streaming, recording, and transcription. Its pricing is per minute and does not change with video resolution for conferencing.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is described as a way to launch your app free of cost. Each month it includes 10,000 conferencing minutes and 10,000 live streaming minutes, plus 1,000 encoding minutes.

Smaller allowances cover the extras: 300 minutes per month each of live transcription, recording, external streaming over RTMP, post call transcription, and AI generated summary. Chat is listed as free of cost. The Developer support plan is listed as free, while the Premium support plan costs $1,500 a month or 5% of MRR, whichever is greater.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say whether usage stops at the free minutes. It lists Pay-As-You-Grow rates that apply after the included minutes: $0.004 per conferencing minute after 10,000, $0.0012 per streaming minute after 10,000, $0.04 per encoding minute after 1,000, and $0.0135 per recording minute after 300.

Discounts start when usage passes $5,000 a month. The page's own estimate shows 18,000 conferencing minutes leaving 8,000 billable minutes after the free 10,000.

## Gotchas

- The page does not say whether a card is needed or what happens at the limits, so check the dashboard before you rely on it.
- Recording has only 300 free minutes a month, far less than the conferencing allowance.
- Encoding minutes are counted separately from streaming minutes, so live streams use two allowances.

## Questions people ask

### How many free minutes does 100ms give?

The Free plan includes 10,000 conferencing minutes and 10,000 live streaming minutes each month, plus smaller allowances for encoding, recording, and transcription.

### What does 100ms charge after the free minutes?

Conferencing costs $0.004 per minute after the included 10,000, and streaming costs $0.0012 per minute after 10,000.

### Is chat included?

Yes. The pricing page lists chat as free of cost.
