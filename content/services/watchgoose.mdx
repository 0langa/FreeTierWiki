Watchgoose is a monitoring service for scheduled work. Your cron job, backup, queue worker, or script sends an HTTP ping when it runs, and Watchgoose alerts you when the pings go quiet. It is run by Pixeware LTD. It suits developers who want to know when background jobs silently stop.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is "for individuals and side projects. Forever, no card required." It includes 10 checks, unlimited projects, 1 user, and 200 ping log entries. The FAQ repeats that the Free plan includes 10 checks and does not require a credit card.

Every plan includes HTTP pings, alert channels, and project-scoped API access. The site has guides for monitoring cron jobs, systemd timers, GitHub Actions, and shell scripts. New accounts can also start a 14-day Startup or Business trial from their account without entering payment details.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 10 checks or fill the 200-entry ping log on the Free plan. The page lists no metered charges on the Free plan; moving up means choosing a paid plan. Startup costs EUR 17 a month for 100 checks, team access, and 50 SMS and 20 phone calls; the page also shows prices in USD and GBP.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has 1 user; team access starts on Startup.
- SMS and phone call alerts are listed only on paid plans.
- 200 ping log entries is a short history for a job that pings often.

## Questions people ask

### Is Watchgoose free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 10 checks forever, with no credit card required.

### Can open source projects get more?

Yes. Qualifying open source projects and nonprofits can get any paid plan for free, matched to their needs.

### Can I try a paid plan without a card?

Yes. New accounts can start a 14-day Startup or Business trial without entering payment details.
