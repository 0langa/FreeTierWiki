Matomo is an open-source analytics platform that gives you full ownership of your data, and Matomo Cloud is the version Matomo hosts for you. It covers web analytics with options such as heatmaps, session recordings, and A/B testing. It suits developers and teams moving off Google Analytics who want privacy compliance without running their own server.

## What the free plan gives you

Matomo Cloud has a free trial rather than a free plan. The trial page offers a 21-day free trial with all features and no card required. To buy a Cloud plan, you first create an account by signing up for this trial.

Cloud plans include hosting, maintenance, automatic updates, security monitoring, daily backups, and access to all Matomo features, and data is stored in Europe. The Cloud plan lists raw data retention of 24 months. If you want Matomo free beyond the trial, the On-Premise edition can be downloaded for free and run on your own infrastructure, which this entry does not cover.

## When you hit a limit

The trial lasts 21 days. The pricing page does not say what happens to the account or its data if you do not pick a plan at the end.

Paid Cloud plans are sized by monthly traffic in hits, with tiers such as 50,000, 100,000, and 300,000 hits per month, up to more than 10 million, where you contact Matomo for custom pricing. The FAQ covers what happens if you exceed your allocated hits with a cost for additional hits per month. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time and pay only the prorated difference.

## Gotchas

- There is no hosted free plan after the trial; staying free means self-hosting.
- Plans are counted in hits, not pageviews, so use Matomo's guide to estimate your hits first.
- Heatmap and session recording volumes are separate allowances on Cloud plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Matomo Cloud free?

Not permanently. Matomo Cloud has a 21-day free trial with all features; the free option after that is self-hosting Matomo.

### Do I need a card for the Matomo Cloud trial?

No. The trial page says no card is required.

### How is Matomo Cloud priced?

By monthly hits, with tiers from 50,000 hits per month upward. You can switch plans at any time with prorated billing.
