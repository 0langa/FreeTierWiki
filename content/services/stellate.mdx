Stellate sits in front of a GraphQL API as an edge layer. It caches GraphQL query responses, records metrics on usage, performance, and errors, and adds security features such as rate limiting. The site's footer now credits The Guild.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says Stellate is free for indie hackers and individual developers. The Free plan, aimed at indie hackers and side projects, costs $0 a month and covers up to 100,000 requests a month, with unlimited seats and unlimited services. A fair use policy applies.

The comparison table lists metrics and rate limiting on the Free plan up to the same 100,000 a month. Stellate's features are GraphQL edge caching to reduce origin traffic, GraphQL metrics that need no configuration, and GraphQL security against scrapers and traffic spikes. It names WPGraphQL, Hasura, and custom GraphQL APIs as supported technologies, and the pricing page says setup takes under 5 minutes.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says going over the plan's limits disables the features but does not block any requests. So traffic past the free amount still reaches your API, without the caching or metrics that the plan covers.

The next plan, Business, starts at $249 a month with 25M metrics requests included and $10 a month per additional 1M requests, plus add-ons such as rate limiting. It has a 14 days trial. If you cancel a paid plan, you downgrade to Free and may need to remove users or services to fit its limits.

## Gotchas

- Past the free amount the features switch off, so an API that relies on the cache can suddenly send all traffic to the origin.
- Stellate works with GraphQL; a REST API gets nothing from it.
- Paid plans take major credit cards, with wire transfer only for Enterprise, and invoices are in US dollars only.

## Questions people ask

### Is Stellate free?

Yes. The Free plan covers up to 100,000 requests a month for indie hackers and side projects, with a fair use policy.

### What happens if I go over Stellate's free limit?

The features are disabled, but requests are not blocked. Your API keeps answering without Stellate's caching.

### Does Stellate work with REST APIs?

The pricing and home pages describe GraphQL caching, metrics, and security only.
