You set up tests in the StatusCake web dashboard, and there is also an API with Terraform guides. The knowledge base walks you through a basic website test in a few minutes. It suits individuals and small businesses.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing view rendered in a browser lists Free at EUR 0, with no credit card required. It includes 3 uptime monitors at 15-minute intervals, HTTP(S) tests, a single personal login, 1 contact group, and 1 email for alerts.

That view excludes page-speed, domain, SSL and server monitors, SMS, integration alerts, and reporting. It also excludes string matching, SSL validation, and custom locations. The separate Free monitoring page and fetched pricing text still advertise 10 monitors at 5-minute intervals and auxiliary monitors. These official versions conflict; this entry describes the smaller rendered allowance rather than promising the higher offer. Confirm the allowance displayed for your account before relying on an extra feature.

## When you hit a limit

The sources do not fully describe enforcement or billing when an ongoing Free account exceeds its allowance. The absence of a published Free overage price does not establish a guaranteed spending cap.

Superior lists 100 uptime monitors with 1-minute tests, 75 SMS credits, and 3 server monitors. Its displayed euro price is EUR 19.99 monthly or EUR 16.66 per month billed yearly. A no-card paid trial is advertised as 7 days. The trial FAQ says accounts revert to Free with paid features locked and excess monitors reset; the expiry FAQ instead mentions 14 days. Adding a card and choosing to continue paid leads to a charge at trial end.

## Gotchas

- The Free marketing page promises 10 uptime monitors, 1 page-speed monitor at 24-hour intervals, and 1 domain monitor at 7-day intervals. The rendered pricing view excludes those auxiliary monitors and lists only 3 uptime monitors.
- SSL inclusion and frequency are inconsistent: rendered Free pricing excludes it, fetched pricing lists 1 SSL monitor every 24 hours, and the Free marketing page says every 30 minutes.
- The Free marketing page also promises integrations and reporting, while the rendered comparison excludes both. These differences cannot be resolved from the public sources reviewed.

## Questions people ask

### Is StatusCake free?

Yes. Rendered pricing lists EUR 0 with 3 uptime monitors at 15-minute intervals. Other official material advertises a conflicting 10-monitor allowance, so the higher offer is not promised here.

### Does StatusCake need a credit card?

The rendered Free plan explicitly says no credit card is required. Paid trials are also advertised without a card; adding one and choosing to continue paid can lead to a charge at trial end.

### How often does StatusCake check my site on the free plan?

Every 15 minutes in the rendered Free pricing view. The separate Free marketing page still says every 5 minutes; this is an unresolved provider conflict.
