You set up tests in the StatusCake web dashboard, and there is also an API with Terraform guides. The knowledge base walks you through a basic website test in a few minutes. It suits individuals and small businesses.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euro per month. The comparison table lists 10 uptime monitors with a 5-minute test interval. You also get 1 page speed monitor tested every 24 hours, 1 domain monitor tested every 7 days, and 1 SSL monitor tested every 24 hours. The plan has 1 team member, and the free card lists alerts through integrations.

Paid plans come with a 7-day free trial and no credit card is required. The Superior plan starts at 16.66 euro per month with annual billing and has 100 uptime monitors with 1-minute tests, 75 free SMS credits, and 3 server monitors.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach a free limit. The comparison table shows the maximum counts per plan, such as 10 uptime monitors on Free and 100 on Superior.

The page also does not describe any overage charge on the Free plan. To get more monitors, faster tests, SMS credits, server monitors, or more team members, you move to a paid plan, which you can try first with the 7-day trial.

## Gotchas

- Free page speed and SSL tests run only every 24 hours, and the domain test runs every 7 days.
- Custom test locations are described in the knowledge base for paid plans. On Free, locations are chosen for you.
- Free SMS credits and server monitors are not in the Free column of the table. They start on Superior.

## Questions people ask

### Is StatusCake free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing and includes 10 uptime monitors with 5-minute tests, plus 1 page speed, 1 domain, and 1 SSL monitor.

### Does StatusCake need a credit card?

No. The site says its paid plans start with a 7-day free trial and no credit card is required.

### How often does StatusCake check my site on the free plan?

Every 5 minutes for uptime tests. Page speed and SSL tests run every 24 hours, and domain tests run every 7 days.
