LuaDNS hosts DNS zones on an anycast network and lets you manage them as code. You can keep zone files in git and deploy them, generate records with Lua scripts and templates, or use the web interface and API.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free package includes 3 domains and 30 records on the anycast network, with unlimited queries a month. The minimum TTL is 5 minutes. It includes web redirects, email forwardings, real-time statistics, reverse zones, AXFR transfers, dynamic DNS, DNSSEC, and API access, the same feature rows as the paid packages.

The home page lists four anycast name servers and 22 points of presence in the USA, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia. Git integration is meant for teams: the site says git helps several people collaborate on DNS, track changes, and deploy configurations, and Lua scripting can generate records.

## When you hit a limit

The Free package has fixed counts for domains and records, and the pricing page does not describe what happens when you try to add more. Queries are unlimited on every package, so traffic does not push you onto a paid plan.

The paid packages are billed yearly. Basic is $29 per year for 10 domains, 500 records, and a 60 seconds minimum TTL, with vanity name servers and 1 health check. Pro is $39 per year for 30 domains and 1,000 records. The page accepts cards, PayPal, wire transfers, and purchase orders for paid packages.

## Gotchas

- The 5 minutes minimum TTL on the free package makes quick DNS changes and failover slower than on paid packages.
- The page lists 30 records but does not say whether that count applies per domain or across the account; confirm before planning three populated zones.
- Vanity name servers and health checks are paid features.
