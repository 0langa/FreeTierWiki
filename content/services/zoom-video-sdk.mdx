Zoom Video SDK is Zoom's toolkit for building video sessions into your own web, mobile, or desktop app, with your own interface on top of Zoom's infrastructure. Zoom sells it through the Zoom Build Platform, the new name for the Zoom SDK Universal Credit Plan, together with the Cobrowse SDK and Zoom AI Services.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free monthly allowance. The Zoom Build Platform offers a free trial of 20 credits, and Pay as you go has no upfront cost and is billed monthly after that. Credits are consumed by usage across the products in the plan, and the page's example shows 28571 Video SDK session minutes consuming 100 credits, equivalent to $100.

Video SDK sessions cost $0.0035 per minute, measured as participant session minutes: five users in a 10 minute session use fifty session minutes. Cloud recording costs $0.01 per minute, recording storage $0.25 per GB, and live translation and transcription $0.01 per minute. A new account is required for both new and existing customers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say how long the trial lasts, whether it needs a card, or what happens when the 20 credits are used. After the trial, usage is paid from prepaid credits at the listed rates, and the FAQ says the Video SDK is billed on a minutes usage basis.

For more than 500 credits, the page asks you to contact sales. All add-ons on the page require a paid base plan.

## Gotchas

- You cannot reuse an existing Zoom account; the page says a new account is required.
- The checkout, plan management page, and invoices still use the old name, Zoom SDK Universal Credit Plan.
- Session minutes count every participant, so group sessions use credits several times faster than one-to-one calls.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoom Video SDK free?

No, but it has a free trial. The Zoom Build Platform offers 20 credits free, then usage is paid from credits.

### How much does a Video SDK minute cost?

Video SDK sessions cost $0.0035 per participant session minute, paid from credits.

### Can I use my existing Zoom account?

No. The pricing page says a new account is required for both new and existing customers.
