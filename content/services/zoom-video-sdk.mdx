Zoom Video SDK is Zoom's toolkit for building video sessions into your own web, mobile, or desktop app, with your own interface on top of Zoom's infrastructure. Zoom sells it through the Zoom Build Platform, the new name for the Zoom SDK Universal Credit Plan, together with the Cobrowse SDK and Zoom AI Services.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free monthly allowance. The Zoom Build Platform offers a free trial of 20 credits. The paid pay-as-you-go option lists monthly billing. Usage across the products in the plan draws from the same balance.

Video SDK sessions are measured in participant session minutes: five users in a 10-minute session use fifty session minutes. The rate disclosure describes separate recording measurements but did not display current U.S. dollar unit prices in the reviewed view. A new account is required for both new and existing customers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say how long the trial lasts, whether it needs a card, or what happens when the 20 credits are used. Zoom describes the platform as using prepaid credits, but its pay-as-you-go selector also lists monthly billing. Do not read the credit model as a promise of a prepaid-only hard stop. The FAQ says the Video SDK is billed on a minutes usage basis.

For more than 500 credits, the page asks you to contact sales. The pricing notes describe metering for composed and individual recordings. A translation minute uses twice the transcription units of a transcription minute.

## Gotchas

- You cannot reuse an existing Zoom account; the page says a new account is required.
- The checkout, plan management page, and invoices still use the old name, Zoom SDK Universal Credit Plan.
- Session minutes count every participant and partial minutes round up, so group calls and short connections use more credits than elapsed time suggests.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoom Video SDK free?

No, but it has a free trial. The Zoom Build Platform offers 20 credits free, then usage is paid from credits.

### How does Zoom count Video SDK session minutes?

It counts the time of each participant and rounds partial minutes up. A group call consumes more participant minutes than the elapsed time of the session.

### Can I use my existing Zoom account?

No. The pricing page says a new account is required for both new and existing customers.
