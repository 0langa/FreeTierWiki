SolarWinds Incident Response is an on-call and incident management service. It was Squadcast; SolarWinds's own page quotes a customer on "Squadcast (SolarWinds Incident Response)". It takes alerts from your monitoring, routes them to whoever is on call through schedules and escalations, and tracks the incident through notes, response actions, and postmortems.

## What the free plan gives you

The marketing pricing page lists a trial and paid plans. The pricing page shows Start Free Trial on every plan, each fully functional for 14 days. The FAQ says that on the Trial plan you have access to all SolarWinds Incident Response features for 14 days, with a notification limit of 100, phone and SMS combined.

After the trial, Pro starts at $15 per user a month and Premium at $24 per user a month, both billed annually. Pro includes schedules and escalations, unlimited email and push, SMS and voice notifications, postmortems with templates, automation rules, and the mobile app. Premium adds status pages, runbooks, an SLO tracker, and unlimited SMS and voice notifications. Enterprise has custom pricing.

## When you hit a limit

The marketing trial includes 100 phone and SMS notifications, but does not explain the trial's cap enforcement or card requirement. Official billing documentation also describes a five-user Free plan after the trial. On that Free plan, the first 100 phone/SMS notifications are a one-time account allowance; after exhaustion, only email and push continue. Because the marketing table omits Free, confirm the fallback offered to your account. On paid Pro, notification overages cost $0.10 in the U.S., India and Brazil, or $0.35 elsewhere, after geography-dependent included allowances.

## Gotchas

- One hundred phone and SMS notifications go fast if you test with a noisy alert source; use email and push for most tests.
- Paid plans are billed annually per user, so budget for a year, not a month.
- Status pages are on Premium, not Pro.

## Questions people ask

### Is SolarWinds Incident Response free?

A 14-day full-feature trial is advertised. Paid plans start at $15 per user a month billed annually, while official billing docs also describe a five-user Free plan absent from the marketing table.

### Is this the same product as Squadcast?

Yes. SolarWinds's page refers to Squadcast as SolarWinds Incident Response.

### How many notifications does the trial include?

The trial's notification limit is 100, phone and SMS combined.
