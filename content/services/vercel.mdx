You can deploy from the Vercel dashboard by importing a repository, or from the command line with the Vercel CLI. Every branch can get its own preview deployment.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan costs $0 per month and is meant for personal projects and small apps. Each month it includes the first 100 GB of Fast Data Transfer, the first 10 GB of Fast Origin Transfer, and the first 1,000,000 CDN requests. It also includes 1 million function invocations and 1 GB of Blob storage.

You get automatic CI/CD, a global CDN, DDoS mitigation, a web application firewall, and traffic and performance insights. A Hobby team has 1 seat. The limits page also sets 100 deployments per day, 100 builds per hour, and one hour of runtime log storage.

## When you hit a limit

Hobby has no billing cycle, because it is a free tier. The docs say that if you exceed a usage limit, you usually have to wait until 30 days have passed before you can use the feature again. Some limits pause for shorter periods. Web Analytics, for example, pauses after a grace period and can resume after 7 days.

The Pro plan costs $20 per month. It includes $20 of usage credit and then charges on demand for extra use. A Hobby team does not get charged for overage.

## Gotchas

- The fair use guidelines limit Hobby to non-commercial, personal use. Commercial sites need Pro.
- A Hobby team cannot connect to Git repositories owned by Git organizations. You must switch to a team or create one.
- The deployment limits are 100 per day and 100 builds per hour. A busy repository can hit them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Vercel free?

Yes, for personal and non-commercial use. The Hobby plan costs $0 and includes 100 GB of Fast Data Transfer and 1 million CDN requests per month.

### What happens when I go over the Vercel free limits?

On Hobby, you usually have to wait until 30 days have passed before you can use that feature again. Some limits, such as Web Analytics, pause for 7 days. Vercel does not bill Hobby teams.

### Can I use Vercel Hobby for a business site?

No. The docs say the Hobby plan is limited to non-commercial, personal use under the fair use guidelines. A business site needs the Pro plan.
