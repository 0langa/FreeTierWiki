LogRocket records user sessions in web and mobile apps and shows them next to analytics, errors, logs, and network activity. It is built for developers and product teams who want to see what a user saw when something broke.

## What the free plan gives you

LogRocket offers a free trial rather than a permanent free plan. The trial gives you access to all of LogRocket's features free for 14 days, and you can cancel any time. The pricing page says no credit card is required to start it.

All LogRocket products are included: Session Replay, Product Analytics, and Error Monitoring. LogRocket says analytics events, error events, and logs plus network data are unlimited on every plan, and you pay for the sessions you capture. The Core plan also lists clickmaps, heatmaps, rage-click maps, unlimited projects and environments, and MCP and API access.

## When you hit a limit

The trial ends after 14 days. The pricing page does not say what happens to your account or your recorded data if you do not upgrade at that point.

On paid plans, cost scales with the sessions captured each month. Core is meant for fewer than 100K sessions per month, and at 100K or more sessions per month Pro is the selected plan. The final price depends on seats, retention length, and add-ons such as Streaming Export, and annual contracts lock the rate for the term.

## Gotchas

- There is no free plan to fall back to after the trial on the current pricing page.
- Conditional Recording captures only some sessions, so you choose which sessions are worth paying for.
- AI session summaries and issue detection belong to the Pro plan, not Core.

## Questions people ask

### Is LogRocket free?

Not permanently. It offers a 14-day free trial with all features, and then you choose a paid plan.

### Do I need a credit card for the LogRocket trial?

No. The pricing page says the free trial requires no credit card.

### How is LogRocket priced?

By the sessions you capture each month. Analytics, errors, and logs are unlimited on every plan.
