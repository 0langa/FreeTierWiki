LogRocket records user sessions in web and mobile apps and shows them next to analytics, errors, logs, and network activity. It is built for developers and product teams who want to see what a user saw when something broke.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page's list-price table includes a Free plan with 1,000 sessions a month, free forever, with Galileo AI not included. LogRocket also offers a free trial that gives you access to all of its features for 14 days, and you can cancel any time. The pricing page says no credit card is required to start the trial; it does not state the card rule for the Free plan.

All LogRocket products are included: Session Replay, Product Analytics, and Error Monitoring. LogRocket says analytics events, error events, and logs plus network data are unlimited on every plan, and you pay for the sessions you capture. The Core plan also lists clickmaps, heatmaps, rage-click maps, unlimited projects and environments, and MCP and API access.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens to a Free account past 1,000 sessions a month. The docs say each account has a monthly session quota, quotas reset on the same day each month, and a red warning appears on the dashboard when the web or mobile quota is met.

The pricing page does not say what happens to your account or recorded data when the 14-day trial ends without an upgrade.

On paid plans, cost scales with the sessions captured each month. Core starts at $83 a month for 10,000 sessions and is meant for fewer than 100K sessions per month, and at 100K or more sessions per month Pro is the selected plan.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan appears in the list-price table rather than as a plan card, so confirm your session quota on the Plans page after the trial.
- Conditional Recording captures only some sessions, so you choose which sessions are worth paying for.
- AI session summaries and issue detection belong to the Pro plan, not Core.

## Questions people ask

### Is LogRocket free?

Yes, within limits. The pricing page lists a Free plan with 1,000 sessions a month, free forever, and a 14-day free trial with all features.

### Do I need a credit card for the LogRocket trial?

No. The pricing page says the free trial requires no credit card. It does not state the card rule for the Free plan.

### How is LogRocket priced?

By the sessions you capture each month. Analytics, errors, and logs are unlimited on every plan.
