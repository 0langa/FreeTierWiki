You work in the Localit web app or its mobile app, and you connect a Git repository to sync files. It suits small teams that build for iOS, Android, and the web.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0. It allows 2 users on the team, 500 keys for all projects, and 100 MB of storage. It includes Google Translate machine translation. It also includes the GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket integrations.

This entry could not read a pricing page when it was checked, so a current official page does not confirm these numbers.

Open source projects can apply for a free Advanced plan. The open source page lists 30 users, 30,000 keys, and AI assistant tokens. Non-commercial use is a condition. You must also mention Localit somewhere in the project, such as the README, and you apply by email.

The pricing page could not be read, so this entry cannot say what happens at the limit. No official page found says whether the free plan stops at its limits or charges for more.

## Gotchas

- The free plan counts keys across all projects, not per project. A second project uses the same 500 keys.
- The open source plan is not automatic. It needs an email application and a public mention of Localit.
- This entry could not fetch a pricing page, so confirm the plan limits when you sign up.

## Questions people ask

### Is Localit free?

Yes, there is a free plan with 2 users, 500 keys, and 100 MB of storage. This entry could not confirm that on a current pricing page.

### Is Localit free for open source?

Open source projects can apply by email for a free Advanced plan. It is for non-commercial use, and the project must mention Localit.

### Which file formats does Localit support?

The docs list JSON, Android XML, iOS .strings and .stringsdict, PO, POT, MO, and SRT subtitle files.
