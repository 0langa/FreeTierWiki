Localit is a translation management system for mobile apps, websites, games and subtitles. A shared key list can export to several platform formats, and repository integrations synchronize translation files. The current public offer that could be established is its conditional free Advanced plan for open-source projects.

## What the free plan gives you

The official open-source page offers qualifying projects Advanced access without a subscription charge. It lists 30 team users, 30000 keys across all projects, storage written as 100 Gb, and 10000000 AI Assistant tokens per month. Because the page uses Gb rather than a clearly defined byte unit, do not silently interpret that storage figure as 100 GB.

Google Translate, key history, screenshots, chat with files and voice, and GitLab, GitHub and Bitbucket integration are also listed. Access requires an application, rather than being an automatic entitlement for every new user. The previously listed general Free plan quantities could not be re-established on the current live homepage or its rendered German variant; search snippets still surface older plan text, which is insufficient to resolve the live-page gap.

## When you hit a limit

The open-source offer does not explain what happens when users, keys, storage or AI tokens are exhausted. It also does not explicitly describe a signup-card requirement, reset timezone, rollover policy, or automatic excess charges. Confirm those terms with Localit before building a workflow around a precise enforcement promise.

Apply by emailing the address published on the offer page after creating a localization project. Approval and eligibility remain provider decisions. Paid translations or other plan changes may have separate terms; free Advanced access is not a promise that every external translation service is unmetered.

## Gotchas

- The applicant must lead the project or hold submission rights, and the project must have a public repository, an OSI-approved license and active maintainers.
- The Localit license must be used non-commercially, and the project must mention Localit in a README, website, credits or similar location.
- Keys are counted across all projects. The general Free offer, storage unit and exact excess-usage behavior require confirmation; do not reuse older plan quantities as current verified pricing.

## Questions people ask

### Is Localit free for open source?

Eligible non-commercial projects can apply for a free Advanced plan. A public repository, active maintenance, an OSI-approved license and a Localit mention are required.

### Is approval automatic?

No automatic approval is promised. The page asks an authorized project representative to create a project and submit an email application.

### Are the general Free plan limits confirmed?

No. The live homepage did not expose its older general Free pricing table during this review; the conditional open-source offer is separately published.
