Localit is a translation management system for mobile apps, websites, games and subtitles. A shared key list can export to several platform formats, and repository integrations synchronize translation files. It suits small app teams that want translations and Git sync in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing table on the home page lists the Free plan at $0 with 2 users in the team, 500 keys for all projects, 100 Mb of storage, and 10,000 AI Assistant tokens a month. Google Translate and GitLab, GitHub, and Bitbucket integrations are included. Key history, screenshots, and chat with files and voice are listed only on paid plans.

Paid plans start with Standard at $9 per month when paid annually, with 5 users and 5,000 keys. Business is $30 per month and Advanced is $90 per month.

Eligible non-commercial open-source projects can apply for a free Advanced plan. The open-source page lists 30 team users, 30,000 keys across all projects, storage written as 100 Gb, and 10,000,000 AI Assistant tokens per month, plus Google Translate, key history, screenshots, chat, and Git integrations.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing table does not explain what happens when users, keys, storage or AI tokens are exhausted, and it does not state whether a card is required at signup, how often the tokens reset, or whether excess use is charged. Confirm those terms with Localit before relying on a precise limit.

To go beyond the Free plan, upgrade to a paid plan, or apply for the open-source Advanced plan by emailing the address published on the offer page after creating a localization project. Approval and eligibility are Localit's decision.

## Gotchas

- Keys are counted across all projects, so 500 keys are shared by everything on the Free plan.
- The open-source plan needs a public repository, an OSI-approved license, active maintainers, a project leader applying, non-commercial use of the Localit license, and a Localit mention in the project.
- Key history, screenshots, and chat are paid-plan features unless you qualify for the open-source Advanced plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Localit free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 2 users, 500 keys across all projects, 100 Mb of storage, and 10,000 AI Assistant tokens a month.

### Is there a bigger free plan for open source?

Eligible non-commercial projects can apply for a free Advanced plan with 30 users and 30,000 keys. The project needs a public repository, active maintenance, an OSI-approved license, and a Localit mention.

### Does Localit need a credit card?

The pricing table does not say. The card rule for the Free plan is not confirmed.
