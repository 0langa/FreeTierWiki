You give Leapcell a Git repository plus a build command and a start command. It builds a container, deploys it, and serves it on a leapcell.dev address. You manage everything from the web dashboard and the service logs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan costs $0. It includes 20 projects, 100,000 serverless invocations per month, and 3 GB-hours of serverless compute. You also get 2 GB of outbound networking and 10 GB of inbound large payloads each month, plus 1 free PostgreSQL database that runs on shared infrastructure.

Builds get 60 minutes per month, and one deployment can build for up to 10 minutes. A single request can run for 15 minutes. The service can run up to 100 concurrent executions. Deployment logs and metrics are kept for 7 days, while the pricing page lists 3 days of function log retention. You can add up to 10 custom domains.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Hobby account passes its monthly quotas. The paid plans list pay-as-you-go prices after their included amounts, but the Hobby column lists no such price.

Some limits are hard caps on a single action. A deployment that builds for more than 10 minutes stops, and you can create at most 100 deployments a day. The fair use page also lets Leapcell stop a service without notice if it judges the use unfair.

## Gotchas

- Persistent servers are a separate mode from serverless. The pricing page lists them under the paid features.
- The fair use policy bans open proxies, VPN services, crypto mining, and creating several accounts to get around the free limits.
- The docs say a service is served from a `leapcell.dev` domain, and you must give a build command, a start command, and a port.

## Questions people ask

### Is Leapcell free?

Yes. The Hobby plan costs $0 and includes 20 projects, 100,000 invocations per month, 3 GB-hours of compute, and 1 PostgreSQL database.

### What happens when I go over the Leapcell free limits?

The pricing page does not say what happens on Hobby. Paid plans list per-use prices after their included amounts, and the Hobby plan lists none.

### Does Leapcell have a free database?

Yes. The Hobby plan includes 1 free PostgreSQL database. The pricing page calls it a multi-tenant database for development, testing, and hobby projects.
