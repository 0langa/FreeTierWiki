You add a site to a Ruttl project and then click directly on the page to pin comments. Guests can comment without an account. It suits freelancers and small agencies who want feedback tied to the page, not buried in email.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan is free. It is meant for solo creators and covers 1 project with 5 pages, plus all project types: websites, mobile apps, PDFs, and images. You get 10 comments, 1 GB of workspace storage, and integrations with Trello, Slack, Asana, and Jira.

The pricing page gives two numbers for people. The plan card says up to 5 users and unlimited guests. The comparison table says 1 user and up to 5 guests. Guest comments and attachments in comments are unlimited in both places.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach the 10 comments, the 5 pages, or the 1 GB of storage. It gives the numbers only. Nothing on the page shows a charge for the free plan.

The Pro plan costs $18 per month per user. It gives 1 admin and up to 50 users, unlimited projects, unlimited pages, and unlimited comments. It also adds versioning, mobile responsiveness, 15 device sizes, and 50 GB of workspace storage.

## Gotchas

- The comparison table lists 10 comments and 10 private comments on Basic. Guest comments are unlimited.
- Basic does not include versioning or mobile responsiveness. Those start on Pro.
- The mobile app feedback tool installs as an npm package named `@ruttl/mobile-sdk`, and bug reports from the app become tickets in your project.

## Questions people ask

### Is Ruttl free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free and allows 1 project with 5 pages and 10 comments. It includes 1 GB of workspace storage.

### Do clients need a Ruttl account to leave feedback?

No. The support page says you can invite your client without their login needed. Guest comments are unlimited on every plan.

### How much does Ruttl cost after the free plan?

The Pro plan costs $18 per user per month. It removes the project, page, and comment limits and raises storage to 50 GB.
