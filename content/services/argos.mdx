Your test run uploads screenshots to Argos, and Argos compares them with a baseline picked from your Git history. You then review the differences as a check on the pull request. It works with Playwright, Cypress, Vitest, Storybook, WebdriverIO, Puppeteer, or any tool that makes screenshots, through an SDK or the CLI.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is $0 forever and is meant for personal projects. It includes up to 5,000 screenshots and community support. The FAQ says it is designed for personal GitHub repositories.

Argos counts the screenshots uploaded during successful builds. Screenshots from failed builds do not count. The pricing page says the plan is free to start with no credit card. Argos is also an open-source platform, and the docs say the managed cloud service is the supported way to run it. Self-hosting is not officially supported.

## When you hit a limit

On a regular plan such as Hobby, you cannot upload more screenshots until your billing period renews. Argos does not charge you for them. Usage-based plans charge for each extra screenshot instead. Check which kind of plan you are on before you plan a large upload.

The Pro plan starts at $100 per month and includes 35,000 screenshots. Extra screenshots cost $0.004 each, or $0.0015 each for Storybook.

## Gotchas

- Team collaboration, higher limits, and use in a repository in a private GitHub organization all need the Pro plan.
- Screenshots from open-source projects are public. Screenshots from private repositories are visible only to team members.
- Support on every plan goes through the Argos Discord channel or GitHub issues.

## Questions people ask

### Is Argos free?

Yes, for personal projects. The Hobby plan is free forever and includes up to 5,000 screenshots per month with community support.

### What happens when I go over the Argos screenshot limit?

On a regular plan, you cannot upload more screenshots until your billing period renews. Usage-based plans charge for every extra screenshot.

### Does Argos need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says Argos is free to start with no credit card.
