Neocities is a host for static websites, with a community of personal sites around it. You build pages in its in-browser HTML editor or upload files, and the site goes live on a neocities.org subdomain. It suits students, educators, and anyone who wants a small personal site with no ads.

## What the free plan gives you

A free account includes 1 GB of storage and 200 GB of bandwidth. The supporter page lists no advertising, a global anycast CDN, a free site subdomain with SSL, one-click backups, RSS feeds, site tags, the Neocities site editor, and a CLI tool among the features.

The homepage describes Neocities as free static web hosting with an in-browser HTML editor, a command line tool, custom domain support, and file uploading. Neocities says it will never sell your personal data or embed advertising on member sites, and it is funded by supporter plans and donations. Neocities also says educators use it to teach students how to program and create web sites.

## When you hit a limit

The supporter page does not say what happens when you reach 1 GB of storage or 200 GB of bandwidth. There is no metered billing on the free account, so going over does not add charges.

For more room, the Supporter plan costs $5 a month and raises storage to 50 GB and bandwidth to 3000 GB. You can downgrade back to a free account at any time, and Neocities says it refunds the amount you did not use.

## Gotchas

- Custom domains, custom domain SSL certificates, and multiple site creation are Supporter features; a free site lives on its neocities.org subdomain.
- The free account has file upload type restrictions, and CORS support, hotlinking support, and a less strict Content Security Policy are Supporter features.
- Hosting is static: there is no server-side language or database, so dynamic features need an outside service.

## Questions people ask

### How much storage does Neocities give for free?

A free account has 1 GB of storage and 200 GB of bandwidth.

### Does Neocities put ads on my site?

No. Neocities says it will never put advertising on your site.

### Can I use my own domain on a free Neocities site?

No. Custom domains are listed as a Supporter feature, which costs $5 a month; a free site uses its neocities.org subdomain with SSL.
