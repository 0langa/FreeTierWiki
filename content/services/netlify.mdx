You connect a Git repository from the dashboard, and Netlify builds and publishes it on every push. It also offers a command-line tool and an API, and it suits people who want fast front-end deploys without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and has a limit of 300 credits per month. One pool of credits pays for everything. A production deploy costs 15 credits. Compute costs 10 credits per GB-hour, which covers serverless functions and Netlify Database. Bandwidth costs 20 credits per GB, and web requests cost 2 credits per 10,000.

Deploy previews and branch deploys are unlimited and do not use credits. The plan includes a global CDN, Blob storage for files and images, and basic firewall rate limiting. On new credit-based accounts, projects start private and become public only when you choose to publish them.

## When you hit a limit

When the monthly credits are used up, Netlify pauses all web projects on your team. Visitors see a "Site not available" page. A paused project receives no new web requests or form submissions, and you cannot start a new production deploy.

On the Free plan, the credit amount is fixed. You can wait for the next billing cycle, or upgrade to a paid plan. The credits reset each cycle and do not roll over on the Free plan. You are not billed for going over, because the plan stops instead.

## Gotchas

- One busy day can use credits fast. Bandwidth at 20 credits per GB means that 15 GB would use all 300 credits.
- Accounts created before September 4, 2025 are on a legacy plan. New accounts use the credit-based plans.
- A Free plan or Personal plan cannot add extra team member seats. Reviewer roles are free on any plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Netlify free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever and gives you 300 credits per month. You do not need a credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Netlify free limit?

Your web projects are paused until the next billing cycle, or until you upgrade. Visitors see a "Site not available" page, and you cannot publish new deploys. Netlify does not bill you for the overage.

### Does Netlify need a credit card?

No. The pricing page lists the Free plan at $0 forever and does not ask for a card to start. A card is needed only for paid plans.
