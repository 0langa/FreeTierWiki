You use Groq through an API key from the console, through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint or a Groq library. It also has a browser Playground. It suits developers who want fast hosted models for prototypes.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan needs no credit card. Limits differ by model and apply to your whole organization, not to each user. For the gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b models, the docs list 30 requests per minute, 1,000 requests per day, 8K tokens per minute, and 200K tokens per day. The whisper-large-v3 speech model allows 20 requests per minute and 2,000 requests per day, plus 7.2K audio seconds per hour and 28.8K audio seconds per day.

Some models are tighter. The Orpheus text-to-speech models allow 10 requests per minute and 100 requests per day. Cached tokens do not count toward your limits. Exact limits for your account are on the limits page in your settings.

## When you hit a limit

You can hit any limit type first: requests per minute, requests per day, or tokens. When you pass one, the API returns a 429 Too Many Requests error, with a `retry-after` header in seconds. Response headers show your remaining daily requests and per-minute tokens. To get higher limits, upgrade to the Developer plan. The upgrade needs a payment method and bills usage at month-end or at spend thresholds. You can return to Free at any time.

## Gotchas

- Limits are shared by everyone in the organization, so one busy script can use up the daily quota for your teammates.
- Token limits vary a lot by model. The listed per-day limits for gpt-oss models are 200K, but other models differ.
- Upgrading to the Developer plan means adding a credit card, US bank account, or SEPA debit account.

## Questions people ask

### Is Groq free?

Yes. The Free plan has no card requirement and gives per-model limits, such as 30 requests per minute and 1,000 requests per day on gpt-oss models.

### What happens when I go over Groq's free limits?

The API returns a 429 error until the limit resets. You can retry later or upgrade to the Developer plan for higher limits.

### Does Groq need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan. You need a payment method only when you upgrade to the Developer plan.
