LoginLlama is not a login system. You call its REST API, or an SDK for Node.js, Python, or PHP, right before you finish a login, and act on the score it returns.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page says you get 1,000 free checks every month, with no card. Each check scores one login from 0 to 10 and returns risk codes such as impossible travel, new device, and known VPN. Your app then decides to block the login, step up to MFA, or allow it.

The pricing page itself loads without plan details, so the free amount comes from the home page. Requests are rate limited to 100 per minute per API key, or 200 for staging keys. The home page says a check responds in under 500 milliseconds and fails open, so a slow call does not lock users out.

## When you hit a limit

The pages we could read do not say what happens when the 1,000 free checks run out. The docs mention that a call can fail with an out-of-credits error when no API credits remain. They also say the API answers with a 429 error when you pass the rate limit, and they suggest backoff.

Because your login flow calls this API, plan for both cases. Decide in your code whether a failed check should allow the login or block it, and watch your credit use in the dashboard.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page does not show plan details when we fetch it. Confirm the free amount and paid prices in your account before you rely on them.
- The rate limit is 100 requests per minute per API key. A login burst can reach it.
- LoginLlama scores logins only. You still need your own auth provider to sign users in.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [Auth0](/services/auth0/): when you need a full hosted login system and not only a risk score.
- [Supabase Auth](/services/supabase-auth/): when you want sign-in bundled with a database and are fine without a separate risk API.
- [Stytch](/services/stytch/): when you want a hosted API that handles sign-in itself, including passwordless methods.

## Questions people ask

### Is LoginLlama free?

Yes, to start. The home page lists 1,000 free checks every month with no card.

### Does LoginLlama need a credit card?

The home page says the free checks need no card.

### Does LoginLlama replace my login system?

No. It gives a risk score for a login that your own system handles. You call it once before you finalize the login.
