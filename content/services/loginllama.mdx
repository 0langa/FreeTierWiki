LoginLlama is not a login system. You call its REST API, or an SDK for Node.js, Python, or PHP, right before you finish a login, and act on the score it returns.

## What the free plan gives you

The home page and pricing page say you start with 100 free checks and no card, and the FAQ says every new account gets 100 free login checks. They do not say the checks renew each month, so treat them as a starting allowance. Each check scores one login from 0 to 10 and returns risk codes such as impossible travel, new device, and known VPN. Your app then decides to block the login, step up to MFA, or allow it.

The pricing table lists paid Starter at $9 per month for 5,000 checks, Professional at $49 for 50,000, and Enterprise at $199 for unlimited checks. Requests are rate limited to 100 per minute per API key, or 60 for test keys. Test keys are free but never score a real login.

## When you hit a limit

The API docs define HTTP 402 Payment Required for no API credits remaining and HTTP 429 for rate limits. They do not say whether the free checks behave like paid API credits, or when free requests resume after the checks run out.

Because your login flow calls this API, plan for both cases. Decide in your code whether a failed check should allow the login or block it, and keep an eye on your remaining credits. The home page says the SDKs and plugins let a login through if LoginLlama cannot answer.

## Gotchas

- The 100 free checks are a starting allowance, and the pages do not say they renew each month.
- The rate limit is 100 requests per minute per API key. A login burst can reach it.
- LoginLlama scores logins only. You still need your own auth provider to sign users in.

## Alternatives in this atlas

- [Supabase Auth](/services/supabase-auth/): when you want sign-in bundled with a database and are fine without a separate risk API.
- [Stytch](/services/stytch/): when you want a hosted API that handles sign-in itself, including passwordless methods.

## Questions people ask

### Is LoginLlama free?

Partly. Every new account gets 100 free login checks with no card, and paid plans start at $9 a month.

### Does LoginLlama need a credit card?

No card is needed for the 100 free checks, the pricing page and FAQ say.

### Does LoginLlama replace my login system?

No. It gives a risk score for a login that your own system handles. You call it once before you finalize the login.
