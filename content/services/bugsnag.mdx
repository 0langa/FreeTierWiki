You create a project in the BugSnag dashboard, add a notifier library for your platform, and set the project API key in your app. BugSnag then reports crashes and unhandled exceptions to the dashboard. It suits solo developers with a small app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is for solo users and their passion projects. It includes 7.5K events and 1M spans per month, for 1 user. An event is an unhandled exception or crash, or any handled exception that you choose to report. You can create an unlimited number of projects.

The plan covers error and performance monitoring on 50+ platforms, 30+ third-party integrations, and basic notifications. The free tier keeps event data for 7 days. Paid plans keep it for longer, such as 60 days on one plan.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists a question about going over your event quota, but the fetched page does not show the answer. Check that FAQ before you send a large volume of errors.

The home page says BugSnag uses dynamic sampling and burst protection so you do not exceed quotas or get surprise bills. The Free plan has no card listed on the page, so treat surprise charges as unlikely.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has only 1 user. Unlimited users start on the next plan up.
- Automatic error assignment and automatic error prioritization are not on Free.
- The getting started guide says to sign up for a free trial, so confirm in the sign-up flow that you land on the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bugsnag free?

Yes, there is a Free plan for solo users. It includes 7.5K events and 1M spans per month and unlimited projects.

### How long does Bugsnag keep data on the free plan?

The pricing page says the Free tier includes 7 days of data retention.

### Does Bugsnag need a credit card for the free plan?

The pricing page does not say. The entry lists no card as required.
