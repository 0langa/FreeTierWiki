You create a project in the BugSnag dashboard, add a notifier library for your platform, and set the project API key in your app. BugSnag then reports crashes and unhandled exceptions to the dashboard. It suits solo developers with a small app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and is for solo users and their passion projects. It includes 7.5K events and 1M spans per month, for 1 user. An event is an unhandled exception or crash, or any handled exception that you choose to report. The detailed feature comparison describes unlimited projects, but its columns cover paid plans; the Free card does not establish that entitlement.

The Free card covers error and performance monitoring on 50+ platforms and 30+ third-party integrations. Basic notifications appear in the paid Select feature list. The free tier keeps event data for 7 days. Paid plans keep it for longer, such as 60 days on one plan.

## When you hit a limit

The billing guide describes two event-overage strategies: capture every event and upgrade after more than 3 daily quota breaches in a rolling 30-day window, or rate-limit events. Administrators choose the strategy in billing settings. The guide does not separately explain Free-plan event enforcement.

For the free route, the guide says canceling a subscription moves you to the limited Free plan with no further charges unless you choose to upgrade again. Span sampling also avoids charges above purchased spans. These are stronger billing evidence than the absence of a card statement; check that your account is actually on Free.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan has only 1 user. Unlimited users start on the next plan up.
- Automatic error assignment and automatic error prioritization are not on Free.
- The getting started guide says to sign up for a free trial, so confirm in the sign-up flow that you land on the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Bugsnag free?

Yes, there is a Free plan for solo users. It includes 7.5K events and 1M spans per month for one user.

### How long does Bugsnag keep data on the free plan?

The pricing page says the Free tier includes 7 days of data retention.

### Does Bugsnag need a credit card for the free plan?

The pricing page does not say. The card requirement is not confirmed.
