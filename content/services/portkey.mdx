You keep calling your model providers, but through the Portkey SDK or REST API, and read the logs and metrics in a web dashboard. It fits developers who use more than one provider. This page covers the free Developer plan.


## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free forever. It records up to 10,000 logs per month. Logs stay for 3 days, and metrics stay for 30 days.

You get the AI gateway, observability with logs, traces, feedback, custom metadata, and filters, and simple caching. Prompt management allows 3 prompt templates, with a playground, API endpoints, versioning, and variables. Support comes from the community.

The pricing page says this plan is for prototyping and testing, and it is not suitable for production workloads. Portkey also says its gateway is open source and free to use.

## When you hit a limit

Passing 10,000 logs does not block your requests. The pricing page says only the logs beyond the limit are not recorded. You lose visibility, not traffic.

The comparison table lists "No Overage Allowed" for the free plan. The Production plan costs $49 per month and adds paid overages. The docs say Portkey sets no explicit timeout on requests on free or paid plans. The pages do not mention a card for the free plan.

## Gotchas

- Logs from the Developer plan are kept for only 3 days, so export or review them quickly.
- The plan has 3 prompt templates. Unlimited templates need a paid plan.
- The docs estimate that Portkey adds between 20 and 40ms of delay compared with calling a provider directly.

## Questions people ask

### Is Portkey free?

Yes. The Developer plan is free forever and records up to 10,000 logs per month. The paid Production plan starts at $49 per month.

### What happens when I go over the Portkey free log limit?

Your requests keep working. Only the logs beyond the limit are not recorded.

### Does Portkey slow down my API calls?

A little. The docs estimate that it adds between 20 and 40ms compared with direct calls to the provider.
