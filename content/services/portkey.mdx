You keep calling your model providers, but through the Portkey SDK or REST API, and read the logs and metrics in a web dashboard. It fits developers who use more than one provider. This page preserves the previously listed Developer terms. On 2026-11-23, pricing redirected to a Prisma AIRS AI Gateway enterprise demo page without the former public plan table. The older docs still mention 10,000 free requests per month, which does not establish the former log allowance or retention. Confirm current terms before relying on this offer.


## What the free plan gives you

The previously reviewed Developer plan was advertised as free forever. It recorded up to 10,000 logs per month. That offer kept logs for 3 days and metrics for 30 days.

The previous offer included the AI gateway, observability with logs, traces, feedback, custom metadata, and filters, and simple caching. Prompt management allowed 3 prompt templates, with a playground, API endpoints, versioning, and variables. Support came from the community.

The previous pricing page described the plan as suitable for prototyping and testing, not production workloads. Portkey also says its gateway is open source and free to use.

## When you hit a limit

Under the previously listed terms, passing 10,000 logs stopped recording additional logs without blocking gateway requests. Current enforcement could not be reconfirmed.

The previous comparison table listed "No Overage Allowed" and a $49/month Production plan with paid overages; these prices and policies are not confirmed current. The docs say Portkey sets no explicit timeout on requests on free or paid plans. The pages do not confirm a card requirement for the free plan. Model-provider inference charges are separate from the gateway allowance.

## Gotchas

- The former Developer plan retained logs for 3 days; current retention is unconfirmed.
- The former plan included 3 prompt templates and reserved unlimited templates for paid service.
- The docs estimate that Portkey adds between 20 and 40ms of delay compared with calling a provider directly.

## Questions people ask

### Is Portkey free?

Current free availability is unconfirmed. The former Developer offer listed 10,000 logs/month; its pricing URL now leads to a Prisma AIRS enterprise demo page.

### What happens when I go over the Portkey free log limit?

Previously, requests continued while logs beyond the allowance were not recorded. Current handling is unconfirmed.

### Does Portkey slow down my API calls?

A little. The docs estimate that it adds between 20 and 40ms compared with direct calls to the provider.
