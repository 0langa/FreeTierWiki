GraphHopper sells the Directions API, a hosted version of its open source routing engine. It covers routing, route optimization for fleets, distance matrices, geocoding, map matching, isochrones, and clustering, all priced in daily credits. The Free plan suits non-commercial projects and developers who want to try the APIs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euros a month and needs no credit card. It includes 500 credits a day, up to 5 locations per routing request, and route optimization with 1 vehicle per request. It has a limited credit count per minute, a limited set of vehicle profiles, and cannot use the flexible mode. Support is forum only. The rendered Free column marks an SLA as included, but the footnote says Free has no guarantees; do not rely on an SLA for this plan. Credit costs depend on the API and options; the default geocoding provider costs 0.3 credits per request, for example. The plan is for non-commercial use only. The rendered Free column excludes Map Matching and Isochrone APIs.

## When you hit a limit

The current credit-limit FAQ says daily excess initially triggers an email warning while the key keeps working. Frequent excess prompts a request to upgrade or arrange more capacity; substantial excess reaches an abuse-protection hard limit whose threshold is not published. The API documentation describes HTTP 429 for exhausted daily or per-minute limits, so that status is not proof that requests stop exactly at 500 credits. Per-minute excess is blocked until the next minute. The Free plan needs no credit card, but these sources do not explicitly publish a Free overage-billing policy. The smallest paid plan, Basic, costs 69 euros a month for 5,000 credits a day and allows commercial use.

## Gotchas

- Commercial use needs a paid plan, even at low volume.
- Limiting a route to 5 locations rules out most multi-stop delivery routes on the Free plan.
- Credit costs vary by API and request size, so check the credit cost details before counting on 500 a day. OpenStreetMap attribution is mandatory. When using the default elevation data, also credit Mapterhorn or link to GraphHopper’s attribution page. The Free table excludes white label, although the attribution page says GraphHopper branding itself is optional; confirm that mismatch before removing it.

## Questions people ask

### Is GraphHopper free?

Yes, for non-commercial use. The Free plan includes 500 credits a day with no credit card.

### Can I use GraphHopper's Free plan in a commercial product?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan is for non-commercial use only.

### Does the GraphHopper Free plan include route optimization?

Yes, with 1 vehicle per request and up to 5 locations.
