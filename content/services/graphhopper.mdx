GraphHopper sells the Directions API, a hosted version of its open source routing engine. It covers routing, route optimization for fleets, distance matrices, geocoding, map matching, isochrones, and clustering, all priced in daily credits. The Free plan suits non-commercial projects and developers who want to try the APIs.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euros a month and needs no credit card. It includes 500 credits a day, up to 5 locations per routing request, and route optimization with 1 vehicle per request. It has a limited credit count per minute, a limited set of vehicle profiles, and cannot use the flexible mode. Support is forum only, and the Free plan does not include any guarantees. Credit costs depend on the API and options; the default geocoding provider costs 0.3 credits per request, for example. The plan is for non-commercial use only.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says requests that violate the limits might be blocked to keep the service stable for all customers. It does not say exactly what happens once the 500 daily credits are used up, so plan for requests to fail until the next day. The Free plan needs no credit card, so there is no card to bill. The smallest paid plan, Basic, costs 69 euros a month for 5,000 credits a day and allows commercial use.

## Gotchas

- Commercial use needs a paid plan, even at low volume.
- Limiting a route to 5 locations rules out most multi-stop delivery routes on the Free plan.
- Credit costs vary by API and request size, so check the credit cost details before counting on 500 a day.

## Questions people ask

### Is GraphHopper free?

Yes, for non-commercial use. The Free plan includes 500 credits a day with no credit card.

### Can I use GraphHopper's Free plan in a commercial product?

No. The pricing page says the Free plan is for non-commercial use only.

### Does the GraphHopper Free plan include route optimization?

Yes, with 1 vehicle per request and up to 5 locations.
