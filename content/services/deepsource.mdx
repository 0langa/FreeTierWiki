You sign up with your Git host, pick a repository and a pull request, and DeepSource posts its findings as comments on the pull request. You can also read the full breakdown in the dashboard. It suits open-source maintainers and small teams that want review on every change.

## What the free plan gives you

The Open Source plan covers unlimited public repositories and unlimited team members. Each month it includes 1,000 reviewed pull requests and 1,000 automated code formatting runs. Static analysis and code coverage are included.

AI Review and Autofix are pay-as-you-go on this plan. Private repositories, secrets detection, OSS vulnerability scanning, and the OWASP and CWE reports are not part of the Open Source plan. Slack and Jira integrations, monorepo support, and audit logs are also left out.

The paid Team plan costs $24 per user per month, billed yearly. It adds unlimited private repositories and unlimited reviewed pull requests.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 1,000 reviewed pull requests or 1,000 formatter runs in a month. The Team plan lifts both limits.

The bigger cost risk is AI Review and Autofix. Both are pay-as-you-go, so you can be billed if you turn them on. The Team plan includes a yearly AI credit per user, and a new trial includes up to $50 of AI Review credit with no credit card.

## Gotchas

- Private repositories are not part of the Open Source plan. They need the Team plan after the 14-day trial.
- DeepSource joined Harness in September 2026. Its announcement says existing workspaces and workflows keep working the same.
- The first review needs an open pull request in the repository you pick.

## Questions people ask

### Is DeepSource free?

It is free for open-source projects. The Open Source plan has unlimited public repositories and 1,000 reviewed pull requests per month.

### Does DeepSource need a credit card?

No for the trial. The pricing page says the 14-day free trial needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the DeepSource free limit?

The pricing page does not say what happens after 1,000 reviewed pull requests. AI Review and Autofix are pay-as-you-go on the free plan, so those can add charges.
