BigQuery is a data warehouse you open in the Google Cloud console. You load data into tables and query it with SQL, and Google runs the servers. It suits developers and analysts who want SQL over app events, logs, or exports without running a database.

## What the free plan gives you

BigQuery is part of the Google Cloud Free Tier, and its free amounts last after the free trial ends. Each month the first 10 GiB of storage is free, and the first 1 TiB of query data processed is free under on-demand pricing. BigQuery ML models and training data stored in BigQuery count toward the storage free tier.

Some operations are free in every location: batch loading data through the shared slot pool costs nothing, though quotas and limits apply. You can try the free tier in the BigQuery sandbox without a credit card. Queries that return an error or are served from the cache are not charged.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a hard stop for the free amounts. With a billing account attached, usage past them is charged at the listed rates, for example $6.25 per TiB of on-demand query data after the first 1 TiB each month.

Charges are rounded up to the nearest MB, with a minimum of 10 MB processed per table a query references and per query. Canceling a running query might still incur charges up to the full cost of the query. Pricing models apply to accounts, not individual projects, so all your projects draw on the same free amounts.

## Gotchas

- A query is billed by the bytes it processes, not the rows it returns, so scanning many columns of a large table uses the free terabyte fast.
- Loading data from Cloud Storage means you also pay Cloud Storage for keeping the source files.
- Streaming reads and writes are billed, unlike batch loading through the shared slot pool.

## Questions people ask

### Is BigQuery free?

Within limits. Each month the first 10 GiB of storage and the first 1 TiB of query data processed are free, and you pay for use past that.

### Can I use BigQuery without a credit card?

Yes. Google says you can try BigQuery's free tier in the BigQuery sandbox without a credit card.

### Do failed queries count against the free terabyte?

No. You are not charged for queries that return an error or for queries served from the cache.
