You publish with a plain HTTP PUT or POST from curl, a script, or the `ntfy` command line tool, and you read the message in the Android or iOS app, the web app, or the desktop. It suits one person or a small team that wants alerts from their own machines.

## What the free plan gives you

You can use ntfy.sh without signing up. There is no account, and you publish and subscribe to any topic name. The docs list the limits on ntfy.sh: 250 messages per day, 4,096 bytes per message, and 5 emails per day once you verify your email.

Attachments can be up to 2 MB each, with 20 MB in total per visitor, and they expire after 3 hours. The daily bandwidth limit is 200 MB. The paid plans start at $6 per month, or $5 per month billed annually. The software is open source, so you can run your own server and change the limits.

## When you hit a limit

When you pass a limit, the server rejects the request. Traffic over the daily bandwidth limit is rejected, and the request limit refills slowly: you get 60 requests at once, then one more every 5 seconds.

If you repeatedly exceed the limits or abuse the service, the docs say your IP address may be temporarily banned. You can check a ban at am-i-banned.ntfy.sh. Nothing bills you, because the free use needs no account or card.

## Gotchas

- Without sign-up, the topic name works like a password. Anyone who guesses it can read and post to it, so use a long random name.
- The FAQ says logs contain topic names and IP addresses, and messages are cached for 12h by default. Self-host if your messages are sensitive.
- Anonymous email sending is turned off on ntfy.sh because of abuse, so you must verify your email in the web app to use it.

## Questions people ask

### Is ntfy free?

Yes. You can use ntfy.sh without signing up, and the server software is open source, so you can self-host it.

### Do I need an account to use ntfy?

No. You pick a topic name, subscribe in the app, and publish with an HTTP request. Because there is no sign-up, the topic name acts like a password.

### How many messages can I send with ntfy for free?

On ntfy.sh the daily message limit is 250. Paid plans raise the limits, and a self-hosted server lets you set your own.
