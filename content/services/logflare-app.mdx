You send log events to Logflare over an HTTP endpoint or through its integrations, and search them in the web dashboard. It suits solo developers who need a small log store.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It allows up to 12,960,000 events per month. The rate limit is 5 events per second, with a burst of 300. You can create unlimited sources, but the plan has 0 additional team members.

Event retention is up to 3 days, and events can have up to 50 fields. Alerts run every 4 hours, and there are no dashboards. Email, webhook, Slack, and Discord alerts are available to all users, while SMS alerts need a paid subscription.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan is rate limited at 5 log events per second on average over the last 60 seconds. When you reach that average, Logflare stops ingesting log events. It warns you with an email and an alert in the interface.

Paid plans use metered pricing. The home page compares them at $15 per million events, and the pricing page lists a 14-day trial after which your credit card is charged. Retention on the metered plan goes up to 90 days.

## Gotchas

- Ingestion stops at the rate limit, so a burst of logs can be dropped.
- Three days of retention is short for slow bug hunts.
- Free has no dashboards, and alerts run only every 4 hours.

## Questions people ask

### Is Logflare free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month with up to 12,960,000 events and 3 days of retention. Paid plans start with a 14-day trial.

### What happens when I hit the Logflare rate limit?

The free plan is limited to 5 events per second on average over 60 seconds. Logflare then stops ingesting events and warns you by email and in the interface.

### How long does Logflare keep logs on the free plan?

The pricing page lists up to 3 days of event retention on Free. The metered plan keeps events up to 90 days.
