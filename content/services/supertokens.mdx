You add a SuperTokens SDK to your frontend and backend, and the SDK talks to a SuperTokens core that you host or run in the managed cloud. It suits developers who want prebuilt login screens and the option to keep control of their data.

## What the free plan gives you

There are two ways to run SuperTokens. Self-hosting the open-source features is free with no limits and no cap on monthly active users. The managed cloud is free under 5,000 monthly active users, and it costs $0.02 per monthly active user after that. A monthly active user is a unique logged-in user in a month, including new sign-ups.

The core features cover email and password, social login, passwordless magic links, SMS or email one-time passwords, and phone number login. You also get the sign-up and sign-in page UI, RBAC, a user management dashboard, and session management. Support comes from the community. Three dashboard users are free.

## When you hit a limit

On the managed cloud, there is no stop at 5,000 monthly active users. You pay $0.02 for each monthly active user past that, so the cost grows with your traffic.

Add-ons have their own prices. Multi-factor authentication costs $0.01 per monthly active user, and account linking costs $0.005 per user, each with a minimum bill of $100 per month. Extra dashboard users cost $20 per user per month after the 3 free ones. Multi-tenancy needs a conversation with sales. If a bill worries you, self-host the open-source core instead.

## Gotchas

- The add-ons for MFA and account linking have a minimum bill of $100 per month, even for a small user base.
- The first setup flow gives you a development environment only. For production, you create a separate deployment.
- Self-hosting is free, but you run and patch the infrastructure yourself.

## Questions people ask

### Is SuperTokens free?

Yes, in two ways. Self-hosting the open-source core is free with no limit, and the managed cloud is free under 5,000 monthly active users.

### How much does SuperTokens cost after the free tier?

The managed cloud costs $0.02 per monthly active user after 5,000. MFA and account linking are paid add-ons with a $100 minimum per month.

### Can I self-host SuperTokens?

Yes. The pricing page says self-hosting the open-source features is free without any limits.
