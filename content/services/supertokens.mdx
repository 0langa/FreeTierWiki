You add a SuperTokens SDK to your frontend and backend, and the SDK talks to a SuperTokens core that you host or run in the managed cloud. It suits developers who want prebuilt login screens and the option to keep control of their data.

## What the free plan gives you

There are two ways to run SuperTokens. Self-hosting the open-source features is free with no limits and no cap on monthly active users. The managed cloud is free under 5,000 monthly active users, and the paid rate is $0.02 per MAU once outside that Free threshold. The calculator charges $100 at exactly 5,000 MAUs. The published 7000-MAU example applies the base rate to all 7000 users, not merely those above 5000. A monthly active user is a unique logged-in user in a month, including new sign-ups.

The core features cover email and password, social login, passwordless magic links, SMS or email one-time passwords, and phone number login. You also get the sign-up and sign-in page UI, RBAC, a user management dashboard, and session management. Support comes from the community. Three dashboard users are free.

## When you hit a limit

Managed hosting is free under 5000 MAU, then $0.02/MAU; the 7000-user example bills all users, not just excess. Signup-card and unbilled-transition rules are unclear.

MFA costs $0.01/MAU and account linking $0.005/user, each with a $100 monthly minimum. Dashboard users beyond 3 cost $20/user/month. Email/password, social and passwordless multi-tenancy includes 25 tenants with fewer than 5 users; additional small tenants cost $2/month and tenants with 5+ users $5/month. Enterprise SSO costs $50/tenant/month; extra apps/environments $50/app/month. Self-hosting the open-source core avoids managed-service charges but leaves infrastructure costs.

## Gotchas

- The add-ons for MFA and account linking have a minimum bill of $100 per month, even for a small user base.
- The first setup flow gives you a development environment only. For production, you create a separate deployment.
- Self-hosting is free, but you run and patch the infrastructure yourself.

## Questions people ask

### Is SuperTokens free?

Yes, in two ways. Self-hosting the open-source core is free with no limit, and the managed cloud is free under 5,000 monthly active users.

### How much does SuperTokens cost after the free tier?

Outside its free-under 5000 threshold, managed pricing is $0.02 per MAU; the 7000-MAU example bills the whole user count. MFA and account linking are paid add-ons with a $100 minimum per month.

### Can I self-host SuperTokens?

Yes. The pricing page says self-hosting the open-source features is free without any limits.
