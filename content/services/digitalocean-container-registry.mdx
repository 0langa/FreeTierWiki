DigitalOcean Container Registry, or DOCR, is a private registry for container images. You push images to it and pull them into DigitalOcean Kubernetes, App Platform, or any Docker host. It suits developers who already deploy on DigitalOcean and want images close to their clusters.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Starter plan at $0 a month with 1 repository and 500 MiB of storage. The docs describe Starter as "a free offering with a single registry and repository to let you try out DOCR."

The storage allowance is monthly and prorated by how many days the registry is active, against a standard 28 days. The docs give an example: a Starter registry active for 16 days receives about 285 MiB of allowance. Registry credentials apply to every registry your account owns, and a Kubernetes cluster integrated with one registry can pull images from all of them.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists storage overage as N/A for Starter, while Basic and Professional charge $0.02 per GiB over their allowance. The docs say extra storage is charged at the end of the day and prorated by the number of days storage exceeds the quota. If you need more room, Basic costs $5.00 a month for 5 repositories and 5 GiB, and Professional $20.00 a month for unlimited repositories and 100 GiB.

## Gotchas

- Untagged images count toward your storage quota, so clean up old pushes.
- Destroying a registry early shortens its active days and can cause a small overage charge.
- Registry names must be globally unique across all DigitalOcean users.

## Questions people ask

### Is DigitalOcean Container Registry free?

Yes, on the Starter plan: 1 repository and 500 MiB of storage at $0 a month.

### Do untagged images count toward storage?

Yes. The docs say untagged images count toward your storage quota.

### What is the largest image I can push?

The limits page says you cannot upload images over 100 GB, with a maximum of 20 GB per image layer.
