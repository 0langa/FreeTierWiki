Many hosting providers get Let's Encrypt certificates for you with a settings switch. If yours does not, you run an ACME client such as Certbot on your server, and the client does the work from the command line. No part of getting a certificate happens on the Let's Encrypt website.

## What the free plan gives you

Certificates cost nothing. Let's Encrypt is a nonprofit and says it does not charge a fee. It issues Domain Validation certificates, and they work for web servers, mail servers, FTP servers, and other servers that use a domain name. Wildcard certificates are available, but you must prove control with the DNS-01 challenge.

Default certificates are valid for 90 days, and Let's Encrypt recommends renewing every 60 days. You can opt in to six-day certificates. A single certificate can hold up to 100 identifiers, and a new account can place up to 300 orders every 3 hours.

## When you hit a limit

Rate limits are the only cap, and they are a hard stop, not a bill. You can issue up to 50 certificates per registered domain every 7 days, and up to 5 certificates for the exact same set of names every 7 days. Capacity refills slowly over time, and Let's Encrypt cannot reset a limit for you. Renewals through ACME Renewal Info are exempt from all rate limits. Test against the staging environment, which has much higher limits.

## Gotchas

- Let's Encrypt does not offer Organization Validation or Extended Validation certificates, and it does not issue certificates for email encryption or code signing.
- Reinstalling your client over and over, or deleting its configuration on every deploy, is a common way to hit the duplicate certificate limit.
- The team does not give direct support. Use the documentation and the community forum.

## Questions people ask

### Is Let's Encrypt really free?

Yes. Let's Encrypt says it does not charge a fee for certificates. Some hosting providers charge a small fee for managing the certificates for you.

### How long do Let's Encrypt certificates last?

Default certificates are valid for 90 days. You can opt in to certificates that last six days. The lifetimes cannot be adjusted.

### Does Let's Encrypt issue wildcard certificates?

Yes. You must use the DNS-01 challenge to prove you control the domain.
