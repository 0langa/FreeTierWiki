You manage secrets in the Doppler dashboard and read them with the Doppler CLI, SDKs, or syncs to platforms such as AWS, Vercel, and GitHub Actions. It suits small teams who want to stop passing `.env` files around.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan is free for 3 users, then $8 per month for each additional user, up to 25 users. It includes the CLI for local development, service tokens, secret referencing, API and webhook access, and email alerts.

The plan limits are 10 projects, 4 environments, and 10 configs per environment. You can have 50 service tokens, 5 config syncs, 5 webhooks, and 5 CLI tokens per user. Activity logs are kept for 3 days.

The Team plan costs $21 per month per user after a 14 day trial. It adds Change Requests, SAML SSO, role-based access controls, service accounts, and 90 days of activity logs.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach a project, environment, token, or sync limit. It only lists the numbers per plan, and the Team plan raises each of them. The page also does not say what happens when a fourth user joins.

Additional users on the Developer plan are priced at $8 per month each, so a growing team sees a bill rather than a block. The Team plan lifts the user cap to 500. Enterprise plans are custom priced.

## Gotchas

- The Developer plan includes only 3 days of activity logs. The Team plan keeps them for 90 days.
- SSO, Change Requests, role-based access controls, and automatic secret rotation are not on the Developer plan.
- The Windows `doppler update` command does not work when you installed through Scoop. Doppler suggests winget there.

Yes. The Developer plan is free for 3 users. Extra users cost $8 per month each, and the plan includes 5 config syncs and 3 days of activity logs.

### How many projects can I have on the free Doppler plan?

The comparison table lists 10 projects, 4 environments per project, and 10 configs per environment on the Developer plan.

### Does Doppler have a free trial of the Team plan?

Yes. The Team plan has a 14 day free trial and then costs $21 per month per user.
