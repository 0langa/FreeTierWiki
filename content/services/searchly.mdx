Searchly hosts Elasticsearch for you. You create indices, send documents, and run search queries against a managed, highly available cluster instead of running Elasticsearch yourself. Its free Starter plan suits prototypes and learning, since it holds only a few megabytes of data.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says you can start free and pay as you grow, with no credit card required. The Starter plan is the free tier, and it includes 20 MB of storage and 2 indices.

The FAQ explains both limits. For Elasticsearch, an index is like a database in a traditional relational database, and the storage limit is the total size of the indices you create. The page describes the service as simple Elasticsearch hosting, managed, highly available, and built for developers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when your indices reach 20 MB or when you try to create a third index. With no card on file, there is no metered usage to bill.

To grow, you change plan, and the FAQ says you can scale up and down at any time without downtime. Micro costs $9 a month for 1 GB and 3 indices, and Small costs $19 a month for 3 GB and 5 indices. Payments go through Paddle as the merchant of record.

## Gotchas

- 20 MB is small for search data, so the free plan fits a demo or a small catalog, not a production index.
- The page lists daily backups, access control, and dashboard access for paid plans, so treat free data as disposable.
- Storage is measured as the total size of your indices, which grows as you add fields and documents.

## Questions people ask

### Is Searchly free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free with 20 MB of storage and 2 indices, and no credit card is required.

### What counts toward the 20 MB?

The storage limit is the total size of the indices you create in Elasticsearch.

### How much is the first paid Searchly plan?

Micro costs $9 a month for 1 GB of storage and 3 indices.
