You host meetings from a web browser and send people a link or a meeting ID. Zoho Meeting is part of the Zoho suite, so it also connects with other Zoho apps.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan allows an unlimited number of meetings, each up to 60 minutes long, with up to 100 participants. It includes screen and file sharing, in-session chat, virtual backgrounds, a whiteboard, hand raising, and emoji reactions.

You can schedule meetings, send email invitations with RSVP replies, and set reminders before the start. Moderators can mute or remove participants. Toll-free dial-in numbers are a separate add-on that you buy. Zoho does not state a card requirement on the free plan page.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens at the end of the 60 minutes. Plan to restart the meeting or upgrade, because the paid Standard plan lists meetings up to 24 hours.

New accounts get a 14-day trial with no credit card. When it ends, you must subscribe monthly or yearly to keep all members and customizations. Otherwise Zoho moves your account to a free plan.

## Gotchas

- Cloud storage for recordings of 5 GB per host is listed under the paid plans, so do not count on recording storage from the free plan.
- Co-hosts are listed as a paid addition on the pricing page, while the free page also mentions them. Check your account before you rely on them.
- The product page free card shows webinars with 100 participants, but the pricing page lists webinars as a separate product with paid tiers.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Meeting free?

Yes. The free plan allows unlimited meetings of up to 60 minutes with up to 100 participants.

### Does Zoho Meeting need a credit card?

The 14-day trial needs no credit card, according to the pricing page. The free plan page does not mention a card.

### What happens when my Zoho Meeting trial ends?

You need a monthly or yearly subscription to keep all members and customizations. If you do not subscribe, your account moves to a free plan.
