You manage Better Stack Uptime in a web dashboard, and a Terraform provider and REST API exist on paid plans. It suits people who want a quick uptime check and a public status page for a personal or small project.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called "Free for personal projects" and costs $0 per month. It includes 10 monitors and heartbeats and 1 status page. Alerts go out by Slack and email. The product page says checks run every 3 minutes on the free plan.

The same free account also includes Telemetry limits: 100,000 exceptions per month, 5,000 session replays, and 3 GB each of logs, traces, and web events kept for 3 days, plus 30 GB of metrics. Team members are unlimited.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to add an eleventh monitor or a second status page. It lists paid extras instead: an additional public status page costs $15 per month, billed monthly.

Phone call and SMS alerts are not part of the free plan. They need a Responder license, which starts at $34 per license per month billed monthly, or $29 billed yearly.

## Gotchas

- The free plan is described as for personal projects, so read the terms before you use it for a company.
- Slack and email alerts are free, but phone calls and SMS need a paid Responder license.
- The status page on the free plan is a single page, and extras like removing the Better Stack footer, password protection, and SSO are paid add-ons.

## Questions people ask

### Is Better Stack Uptime free?

Yes, for personal projects. The free plan costs $0 per month and includes 10 monitors and heartbeats and 1 status page.

### Does Better Stack Uptime send SMS or phone alerts for free?

No. The free plan sends Slack and email alerts. Phone call and SMS alerts need a paid Responder license.

### How often does Better Stack check my site on the free plan?

The product page says every 3 minutes. Paid plans list checks as fast as every 30 seconds.
