You use DynamoDB from the AWS console, the AWS CLI, or an AWS SDK. It suits developers who already build on AWS or want a key-value store without servers.

## What the free plan gives you

The DynamoDB free tier is enough for about 200 million requests a month, depending on item size. Each month, per Region and per payer account, you get 25 write capacity units, 25 read capacity units, and 25 GB of storage. You also get 25 replicated write units for global tables across two Regions, 2.5 million stream read requests, and 1 GB of data transfer out.

The allowance applies to provisioned capacity and the Standard table class. It is a recurring monthly allowance. It does not apply to on-demand tables.

New AWS accounts also get up to $200 in credits for up to 6 months.

## When you hit a limit

What happens depends on your AWS account plan. A Free account plan does not bill you. It ends after six months or when its credits run out, and then access is suspended. AWS keeps your data for 90 days, and you can upgrade to a Paid plan in that time.

On a Paid account plan, usage beyond the free allowance is billed at standard rates after credits. Optional project limits from $20 can pause resources; this is not a default DynamoDB service cap. The FAQ now calls expiry suspension, while some older billing pages still say closure.

## Gotchas

- On-demand capacity is not part of the free allowance. The CLI example in the getting-started guide creates a table in pay-per-request mode.
- The Free account plan suspends access when it ends, so the always-free allowance only lasts beyond six months on a Paid plan.
- Some actions upgrade a Free account plan to Paid automatically, such as joining AWS Organizations.

## Questions people ask

### Is DynamoDB free?

DynamoDB has a free tier of 25 GB of storage and 25 read and 25 write capacity units each month. It is enough for about 200 million requests a month on provisioned tables.

### Does the DynamoDB free tier expire?

The DynamoDB allowance itself has no end date. A new AWS Free account plan does end after six months, and then access is suspended with 90 days to upgrade and recover data.

### What happens when I go over the DynamoDB free tier?

On a Paid account plan you pay standard rates for the extra usage. On a Free account plan you are not billed, but the plan ends when its credits run out.
