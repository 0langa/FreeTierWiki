You build the form in your own HTML and CSS, then add the KwesForms script and endpoint. The docs say it is not a drag-and-drop service, and the script works with any front end or CMS.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page calls the free plan Free Forever. It is meant for smaller personal projects and is limited to 1 form with 100 submissions a month. You do not need a credit card to try the product.

The page does not list which features the free plan includes. Its feature table covers the four paid plans and names spam blocks, data retention, validation, file uploads, webhooks, Zapier, and CSV export. Check the dashboard before you rely on one of them.

The script is plain JavaScript, so it works with static site generators, CMS platforms, single-page apps, and page builders. Docs say the V2 script is 20KB.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 100 submissions in a month, or when you try to add a second form. It shows no overage fee, and the free plan has no card on file.

To go further you move to a paid plan. Bronze costs $19 a month for 5 forms and 1K submissions. Silver costs $39 a month for 20 forms and 5K submissions. Gold costs $99 a month for 100 forms and 10K submissions. Platinum costs $249 a month for 1000 forms and 100K submissions. All plans get a 14-day free trial.

## Gotchas

- KwesForms is for developers. You write the HTML and CSS yourself, and there is no drag-and-drop editor.
- Each form field needs a unique name attribute, or its value will not be sent.
- WordPress may strip custom attributes from forms in the page editor, so the docs suggest data-kw attributes, a template file, or a shortcode.

## Questions people ask

### Is KwesForms free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan allows 1 form with 100 submissions a month. Paid plans start at $19 a month.

### Is KwesForms a drag-and-drop form builder?

No. The FAQ says you build forms with your own HTML and CSS plus its form tools. Then you point the form at its backend.

### Does KwesForms need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the 14-day trial needs no card, and the free plan is free forever.
