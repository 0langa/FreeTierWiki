Slack is chat for teams, organized into channels, with direct messages, huddles for audio and video calls, and an app directory. A developer team would pick it for everyday chat in the place where many of its tools already post: the pricing page lists apps such as Jira and Google Drive among the more than 2,600 in the Slack App Directory.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 USD and is described as free forever. Messages are saved and searchable for 90 days. You can connect up to 10 apps from the App Directory. Huddles, Slack's audio and video meetings with screen sharing, are limited to one-to-one calls, and Slack Connect is limited to one-to-one direct messages with people outside your company.

The help article on free plan limits adds that canvases are available in channels and DMs only, while user- and admin-created canvases are on paid plans. AI features such as conversation summaries are listed on the paid plans.

## When you hit a limit

History is the limit most teams notice. After 90 days, older messages and files are no longer accessible on the Free plan, and Slack says messages and files on the Free plan are only stored for one year; content more than a year old is permanently deleted.

The pricing page does not say what happens when you try to add an app past the limit of 10.

## Gotchas

- The one-year deletion is permanent, so export anything you need to keep before it ages out.
- Ten apps go fast if every service on your team wants its own integration; pick the ones that post the alerts you act on.
- Group huddles and group external channels with partners need a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Slack free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and is listed as free forever, with 90 days of message history and up to 10 apps.

### What happens to messages older than 90 days?

They are no longer accessible on the Free plan, and Slack permanently deletes Free plan messages and files once they are more than a year old.

### Can I connect Jira or other tools on the free plan?

You can install up to 10 apps from the Slack App Directory on the Free plan, so a few integrations fit; past that you need a paid plan.
