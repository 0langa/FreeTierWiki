You build forms in a visual workflow designer and share each one by a unique URL. The docs also list a React SDK, a JavaScript SDK, and a REST API for developers.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. You can build as many forms as you like in the dashboard, but only 5 can be live at one time. You can switch forms on and off yourself.

The plan allows 500 submissions a month. Feathery counts one submission per end user per form per month, so a person who re-submits the same form in the same month counts once. Questions, seats, pages, styles, and components are all listed as unlimited on the free plan. File uploads can be up to 10MB. The developer library is listed as unlimited too.

Many features sit in higher tiers. The pricing table lists branding removal, custom domains, single sign-on, and compliance options as separate feature rows.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 500 submissions in a month. It also does not say what happens if you try to turn on a sixth form. You can still build and test extra forms while they stay off.

No card is on file, so a bill cannot arrive from the free plan. Growth and Business plans use custom pricing, so you must request pricing before you upgrade. The site says you are billed right away when you upgrade.

## Gotchas

- Only 5 forms can be live at once. Extra forms stay off until you free a slot.
- A person who submits the same form twice in one month counts as one submission, not two.
- Paid plans show custom pricing only, so you cannot see a monthly price before you contact the team.

## Questions people ask

### Is Feathery free?

Yes, there is a free plan with 500 submissions a month and 5 live forms. It costs $0 and needs no credit card.

### Does Feathery need a credit card?

No. The pricing FAQ says a card is only needed once you upgrade to a paid plan.

### How many forms can I build on the Feathery free plan?

You can build unlimited forms. Only 5 can be live at once, and you choose which ones are on.
