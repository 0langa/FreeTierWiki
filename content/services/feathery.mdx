You build forms in a visual workflow designer and share each one by a unique URL. The docs also list a React SDK, a JavaScript SDK, and a REST API for developers.

## What the free plan gives you

**Current Free terms are unconfirmed.** Feathery’s pricing page leads with demo bookings and custom quotes. Its [Formstack comparison](https://www.feathery.io/compare/formstack) lists 500 monthly submissions and five forms, while its [Paperform comparison](https://www.feathery.io/compare/paperform) lists 100 monthly submissions and two forms. The current pricing page gives no Free terms that resolve the discrepancy.

The last verified Free offer included 500 submissions a month, five live forms, unlimited drafts, questions and pages, and a 10 MB file upload limit. The two comparison pages both describe a Free plan, but neither establishes which terms apply to new accounts today.

## When you hit a limit

Feathery’s older [account and billing FAQ](https://docs.feathery.io/platform/faqs/account-and-billing) says a repeat submission by the same person on the same form counts once per month. It also says Feathery would ask for a card and start billing when you chose a paid upgrade. Current pricing does not confirm those billing terms, what happens when you reach a quota, or whether a Free signup is still available.

## Gotchas

- Confirm the submissions and live-form limits that apply to your account before publishing a form.
- Free drafts and the file upload limit were documented under the previous offer. Check the current terms if either matters to your project.
- Current pricing directs prospective customers to request a custom quote.

## Questions people ask

### Is Feathery free?

Feathery still describes a Free plan on comparison pages, but they disagree on its limits. The current pricing page gives no Free plan details or confirmed signup terms.

### Does Feathery need a credit card?

The older account FAQ says a card is required only for a paid upgrade. The current pricing page does not specify the card requirement for new Free accounts.

### How many Feathery forms can I publish on Free?

The last verified offer allowed five live forms. One current comparison lists five forms and another lists two, so the limit for new accounts is unconfirmed.
