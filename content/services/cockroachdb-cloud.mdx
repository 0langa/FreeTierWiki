You manage clusters in the CockroachDB Cloud Console and connect with PostgreSQL-compatible drivers and clients. It suits teams that want to test distributed SQL without setting up servers.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free cluster. Every new Cloud organization gets a 30-day free trial with $400 in credit. The pricing page says you do not need a credit card to try the Standard edition. Standard is billed from $0.057 per vCPU-hour, and credits apply to your usage during the trial.

The Mission Critical edition requires a credit card because it runs on dedicated infrastructure. Cockroach Continuum pricing applies to new Cloud organizations created on or after September 15, 2026. The trial ends after 30 days, or once you use the $400 credit.

## When you hit a limit

When the trial ends, your clusters are paused. You then have a 30-day grace period to add a payment method. If you add one, the clusters resume on usage-based pricing. If you do not, they are deleted.

Billing follows what you provision and store, not how much you query. An idle cluster still costs money after the trial. Managed backups are kept for their retention period after you delete a cluster, and you pay for that storage until they expire.

## Gotchas

- Standard has no card requirement, but Mission Critical does.
- After the trial, you must add a payment method within 30 days or lose the clusters.
- You cannot change a cluster's edition in place. To move between Standard and Mission Critical, you create a new cluster and restore your data into it.

## Questions people ask

### Is CockroachDB Cloud free?

Not permanently. New Cloud organizations get a 30-day trial with $400 in credit, and no card is required for Standard.

### What happens after the CockroachDB trial ends?

Your clusters are paused. You have 30 days to add a payment method, and clusters resume on usage-based pricing. Without one, they are deleted.

### Do I pay for an idle CockroachDB cluster?

Yes. Billing follows what you provision and store, not how much you query. Scale down or delete the cluster to cut charges.
