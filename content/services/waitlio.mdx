You create a hosted waitlist page in the dashboard and share its link. Developers can also read waitlists and subscribers through a REST API.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs €0 a month. It allows 1 waitlist and 100 subscribers a month, and it includes API access. The pricing page marks webhooks, email templates, branding removal, and custom domains as not included.

The site says each waitlist gets a landing page with your logo, colors, and copy. Waitlio verifies signups automatically, and you can search, filter, tag, and export subscribers. Subscribers can see their position in the waitlist.

The API uses Bearer tokens that you create in your team settings. It limits requests to 60 a minute per token.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens after 100 subscribers in a month, or what happens if you try to create a second waitlist. It shows no overage fee, and the free plan has no card on file.

To get more room you move to a paid plan. Basic costs €9 a month, or €6 a month on yearly billing, and allows 2,500 subscribers a month and 5 waitlists. Pro costs €29 a month, or €19 on yearly billing, with unlimited subscribers and waitlists. Both paid plans include webhooks, email templates, branding removal, and custom domains.

## Gotchas

- The free plan allows only 1 waitlist and 100 subscribers a month, so a successful launch can fill it fast.
- Webhooks are not on the free plan, even though the API reference documents webhook endpoints.
- Free waitlists keep Waitlio branding and sit on a waitlio.com subdomain.

## Questions people ask

### Is Waitlio free?

Yes. The free plan costs €0 a month with 1 waitlist and 100 subscribers a month. Paid plans start at €9 a month.

### Does Waitlio have an API on the free plan?

Yes. The pricing page marks API access as included on the free plan. Requests are limited to 60 a minute per token.

### Does Waitlio verify email signups?

Yes. The site says it verifies signups automatically. Custom verification email templates are a paid feature.
