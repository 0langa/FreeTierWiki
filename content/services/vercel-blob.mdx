You create a Blob store in the Vercel dashboard or with the Vercel CLI, then upload and read files with the `@vercel/blob` SDK from server or client code. It suits teams whose app already runs on Vercel.

## What the free plan gives you

Vercel Blob is free for Hobby users within the usage limits. The Hobby plan includes 1GB of storage per month, the first 10 GB of data transfer, the first 10,000 simple operations, and the first 2,000 advanced operations.

Simple operations count when a blob is read by its URL and it is a cache miss, or when you call head(). Advanced operations count when you call put(), copy(), or list(), and when you create a store. Cache hits do not count, and del() is free.

The included usage on Hobby is shared across all Vercel services in your project. Dashboard actions such as browsing the store also count as advanced operations.

## When you hit a limit

Vercel emails you as you near the limits. You will not pay for any additional usage on Hobby. Instead, you cannot access Vercel Blob once the limits are exceeded, and you have to wait until 30 days have passed before using Blob again.

Pro and other paid plans bill by use. Storage costs $0.023 per GB, simple operations cost $0.400 per 1M, advanced operations cost $5.000 per 1M, and data transfer costs $0.050 per GB. Multipart uploads count as several advanced operations.

## Gotchas

- Hobby limits are shared with your other Vercel services in the project, so heavy builds or traffic can use up the same pool.
- The access mode, public or private, cannot be changed after you create the store. Pick it carefully.
- Blobs larger than 512 MB are not cached. Each access is a cache miss, which counts as a simple operation.

## Questions people ask

### Is Vercel Blob free?

Yes, on the Hobby plan within its limits. You get 1GB of storage, 10 GB of transfer, 10,000 simple operations, and 2,000 advanced operations.

### What happens when I exceed the Vercel Blob Hobby limits?

Vercel does not bill you. It blocks access to Blob, and you wait until 30 days have passed before you can use it again.

### Does Vercel Blob need a credit card?

The pricing page does not say whether the Hobby plan asks for a card. Vercel says you will not pay for any additional usage on Hobby.
