You create a Blob store in the Vercel dashboard or with the Vercel CLI, then upload and read files with the `@vercel/blob` SDK from server or client code. It suits apps already on Vercel. Connected Vercel projects use OIDC by default. Code hosted elsewhere can use a store’s read-write token.

## What the free plan gives you

Vercel Blob is free for Hobby users within the usage limits. Hobby is for personal or non-commercial use. The Hobby plan includes 1 GB-month of average storage, the first 10 GB of data transfer, the first 10,000 simple operations, and the first 2,000 advanced operations.

Simple operations count when a blob is read by its URL and it is a cache miss, or when you call head(). Advanced operations count when you call put(), copy(), or list(), and when you create a store. Cache hits do not count as simple operations, but still count as CDN requests. The del() operation is free.

The included usage on Hobby is shared across all Vercel services in your project. Dashboard actions such as browsing the store also count as advanced operations.

## When you hit a limit

Vercel emails you as you near the limits. You will not pay for any additional usage on Hobby. Instead, you cannot access Vercel Blob once the limits are exceeded, and you have to wait until 30 days have passed before using Blob again.

Paid storage and operation rates vary by region. The docs illustrate $0.023/GB-month, $0.40/million simple operations and $5/million advanced operations; the pricing table now lists Pro Blob transfer under Flat Rate CDN. Check your region and plan rather than treating the example’s $0.05/GB transfer as universal. Multipart uploads count as several advanced operations. Setting automatic expiry adds one advanced operation per started 60 days of TTL, up to 13 at the 730-day maximum; automatic deletion is free.

## Gotchas

- Blob reads also consume CDN requests and, on cache misses, Fast Origin Transfer. Those allowances are shared with other Vercel services. Server uploads consume Fast Data Transfer when the app receives the file; direct client uploads do not incur upload transfer charges. Server uploads through Hobby Functions are limited to a 4.5 MB request body; use client uploads for larger files. Private delivery also consumes transfer for the Function-to-browser response.
- The access mode, public or private, and the store region cannot be changed after you create the store. Pick them carefully.
- Blobs larger than 512 MB are not cached. Each access is a cache miss, which counts as a simple operation.

## Questions people ask

### Is Vercel Blob free?

Yes, on the Hobby plan within its limits. You get 1 GB of storage, 10 GB of transfer, 10,000 simple operations, and 2,000 advanced operations.

### What happens when I exceed the Vercel Blob Hobby limits?

Vercel does not bill you. It blocks access to Blob, and you wait until 30 days have passed before you can use it again.

### Does Vercel Blob need a credit card?

No. Vercel’s documented account setup uses email verification or a Git provider, and the Hobby plan does not bill for additional usage.
