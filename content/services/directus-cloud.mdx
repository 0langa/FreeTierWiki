Directus is a headless CMS and data platform. It connects to a SQL database, turns its tables into collections, and gives you an admin app called Directus Studio plus an API for your front end. Directus Cloud is the version Directus hosts for you. It suits developers who want a CMS on top of a database they control.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page offers a 14-day free trial of Directus Cloud on Core and Team ("No commitment"). Core lists 3 seats and 25 collections. Public pages do not say whether the trial needs a card. There is no ongoing free hosted plan.

The Core license costs $0 a month and is meant to evaluate Directus or power small, individual builds within its limits: 3 seats, as a hard limit, and 25 collections, where a collection maps to a database table. Self-hosting is available on every tier, but Directus Cloud is an optional add-on at $99 a month for Core and Team. The Open Innovation Grant gives organizations under $5M in annual revenue and fewer than 50 employees a free license for self-hosting, with Cloud hosting at $99 a month.

## When you hit a limit

The public pages do not say what happens when the 14-day trial ends, so check the card and charge terms in the trial setup before you start. Core hosting costs $99 a month, or $89 a month billed yearly. Review the cancellation and billing terms before starting if you only want to evaluate it. Core's 3 seats and 25 collections are limits on the software license; moving to a larger plan raises them. A free self-hosted license does not pay for hosted infrastructure.

## Gotchas

- Public pages do not state a card rule for the trial. A free software license only covers self-hosting, not ongoing Cloud hosting.
- SSO is not available on Core and cannot be added as an add-on.
- Core's 25 collections can run out in a project with many tables.

## Questions people ask

### Is Directus Cloud free?

Only for a 14-day trial. After that, Cloud hosting is $99 a month on Core and Team.

### Is Directus free to self-host?

Yes. Self-hosting is available on every tier, and the Core license costs $0 a month within its seat and collection limits.

### What counts as a collection?

A collection maps directly to a database table. Core includes 25 collections.
