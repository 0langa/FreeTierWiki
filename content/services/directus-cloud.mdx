Directus is a headless CMS and data platform. It connects to a SQL database, turns its tables into collections, and gives you an admin app called Directus Studio plus an API for your front end. Directus Cloud is the version Directus hosts for you. It suits developers who want a CMS on top of a database they control.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no free hosted plan. The pricing page no longer lists a free Directus Cloud trial, and Directus Cloud is an optional add-on at $99 a month for Core and Team.

The Core license costs $0 a month and is meant to evaluate Directus or power small, individual builds within its limits: 3 seats, as a hard limit, 25 collections, where a collection maps to a database table, and 5 flows. Self-hosting is available on every tier, and the homepage says you can self-host free with no credit card. The Open Innovation Grant gives organizations under $5M in annual revenue and fewer than 50 employees a free license for self-hosting, with Cloud hosting at $99 a month.

## When you hit a limit

Keeping a project on Directus Cloud means paying the $99 a month hosting add-on, or you self-host instead. The Cloud policies page says self-service Cloud projects can face throttling or overage charges past their technical limits. On Core, the 3-seat limit is described as a hard limit, and more seats or collections mean moving to Team, which is $499 a month with an annual commitment or $599 a month on a rolling monthly basis.

## Gotchas

- The free license is free only when self-hosted; hosting on Directus Cloud is always paid.
- SSO is not available on Core and cannot be added as an add-on.
- Core's 25 collections can run out in a project with many tables.

## Questions people ask

### Is Directus Cloud free?

No. The pricing page lists no free Cloud plan or trial. Cloud hosting is a $99 a month add-on on Core and Team.

### Is Directus free to self-host?

Yes. Self-hosting is available on every tier, and the Core license costs $0 a month within its seat and collection limits.

### What counts as a collection?

A collection maps directly to a database table. Core includes 25 collections.
