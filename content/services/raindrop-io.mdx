You use Raindrop.io through a browser extension, the web and desktop apps, or mobile apps for iOS and Android. A REST API for developers is documented separately. It suits anyone who saves a lot of links and wants them in one searchable library.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan has no time limit. You get unlimited bookmarks, unlimited collections, and unlimited devices. Collections work like folders, and tags work like labels. You can highlight text in saved pages, and the highlights sync across your devices.

Collaboration also works on the Free plan. New file uploads are limited to 100 MB per month. Pro costs extra and adds full-text search, web archive copies of pages, an AI assistant, reminders, automatic backups, and file uploads up to 10 GB per month.

## When you hit a limit

The unlimited parts of the Free plan have no cap, so the limit that matters is file uploads at 100 MB per month. The help pages do not say what happens when you reach it beyond that new uploads are limited.

If you cancel Pro, you keep access until the end of the billing period, then return to Free. Automatic backups stop, uploads drop to the Free limit, and web archive copies become inaccessible. They are permanently deleted after about a month. If a Pro payment fails, Raindrop.io retries up to 4 times within a week, then downgrades you to Free.

## Gotchas

- Full-text search, web archive copies, and the AI assistant are Pro features.
- Going back to Free from Pro can delete web archive copies after about a month.
- The Free file upload limit is per month, and Pro raises it to 10 GB per month.

## Questions people ask

### Is Raindrop.io free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit, with unlimited bookmarks, collections, and devices. File uploads are limited to 100 MB per month.

### Can I collaborate on Raindrop.io without paying?

Yes. The help pages say collaboration works on the Free plan. You can publish collections publicly or invite a team.

### What do I lose if my Raindrop.io Pro plan ends?

You return to the Free plan. Automatic backups stop, the upload limit drops to 100 MB per month, and web archive copies become inaccessible.
