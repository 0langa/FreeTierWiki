PlanetScale is a serverless, MySQL-compatible cloud database with branching workflows for schema changes. It ended its free Hobby plan in April 2024 — every plan is now paid, and databases still on the old Hobby plan had to move to a paid plan or were shut down. It's well-suited for teams that need managed MySQL without operational overhead and are ready to pay from the start.
