You use WorkDrive in the browser, with a desktop sync app and iOS and Android apps for the rest. The free plan is an individual account, separate from the paid team plans.

## What the free plan gives you

The individual plan offers 5 GB of storage and a 1 GB limit for file uploads. It includes integrated office editors, an unlimited number of file versions, and search that looks inside file contents. You get the desktop sync app and the mobile apps for offline access.

Files are protected with 256-bit AES encryption at rest and SSL/TLS in transit. You can share files by email and assign permissions. The pricing page presents the free plan only as secure cloud storage for individual use.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when your 5 GB fills up or when a file is over the 1 GB upload limit. Plan on the upload being refused and on paying to get more room.

The paid plans are built for teams: Starter, Team, and Business are billed per user per month with a minimum of 3 users. A team plan has bigger upload limits, but converting is permanent. Zoho gives a 15-day trial of Business features with no credit card.

## Gotchas

- The free upload limit is 1 GB, while the pricing page lists 10 GB per file on Starter, so large files will not fit on the free plan.
- You cannot hold an individual account and a team account with the same email address.
- Once your account is converted to a team plan, you cannot switch back to the individual plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho WorkDrive free?

Yes, for one person. The individual plan has 5 GB of storage and a 1 GB limit for file uploads.

### Does Zoho WorkDrive need a credit card?

The individual plan page does not mention a card. The pricing FAQ says you need no credit card to start the 15-day free trial.

### Can I move from the free plan to a team plan?

Yes, but it is one way. You buy a Starter, Team, or Business plan, your files move over, and you cannot go back to the individual plan later.
