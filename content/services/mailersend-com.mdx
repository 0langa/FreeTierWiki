You send mail through the REST API, an SDK, or the SMTP relay, and you design templates in the dashboard. It suits developers who want app emails to leave from their own domain.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for occasional email testing and sending. It includes 500 emails a month, the email API and SMTP relay, and the drag and drop email builder. Per account you get 1 template, 1 domain, 1 user plus 1 accountant, 1 API token, and 1 webhook.

The API quota on Free is 100 requests per day, and the pricing table shows 1 day of log retention. Every new account also gets a 14-day trial of the Professional plan. When the trial ends, MailerSend moves you to the Free plan and does not charge you.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan has hard limits. When you reach the daily API quota, requests fail until the quota resets at midnight UTC. When the account reaches its monthly sending limit, sending resumes at the start of the next billing period.

To raise the limits, you upgrade to the Hobby, Starter, or Professional plan. Hobby starts at $5.60 per month when billed yearly. MailerSend says it never charges you unless you choose to upgrade.

## Gotchas

- MailerSend asks for credit card details for the Free plan to prevent abuse. It says you are not charged for anything on the Free plan.
- You can subscribe to the Free plan only after your account is approved, then you enter your address and card on the Plan and billing page.
- The default API rate limit is 120 requests per minute on the email endpoints, so a burst of sends can return a 429 error.

## Questions people ask

### Is MailerSend free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan includes 500 emails a month and one domain, and it is meant for occasional testing and sending.

### Does MailerSend need a credit card for the free plan?

Yes. MailerSend asks for card details on the Free plan to prevent abuse, and says it does not charge you for the Free plan.

### What happens if I go over the MailerSend free limit?

Requests fail once you pass the daily API quota, which resets at midnight UTC. You upgrade to a paid plan to get higher limits.
