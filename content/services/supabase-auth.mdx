You use Supabase Auth through the Supabase client libraries or its REST API, and you manage providers and users in the Supabase dashboard. It suits developers who already build on Supabase or want auth next to Postgres.

## What the free plan gives you

Supabase Auth is part of a Supabase project. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 50,000 monthly active users. The pricing page lists total users as unlimited, and social OAuth sign-in and basic multi-factor authentication are included.

The Free plan comes with project limits. Free projects are paused after 1 week of inactivity, and you can have 2 active projects. The Pro plan includes 100,000 monthly active users and then charges $0.00325 per monthly active user. The pricing page marks SSO for the Supabase dashboard, automatic backups, and certifications such as SOC2 as not included in Free.

## When you hit a limit

Supabase's cost-control guide says you are not charged while using the Free Plan. It describes the Spend Cap, which is a Pro plan feature: with it on, further use of an item is disallowed after the quota is used up, until the next billing cycle. The guide does not describe exactly what happens on the Free plan when you pass a quota.

Auth also has rate limits on its endpoints, which protect against abuse. When you pass them, Supabase Auth returns a 429 error. Most limits use a token bucket with a capacity of 30 requests, so a short burst is fine but steady traffic above the rate is denied. Some limits can be changed in the project's Authentication settings.

## Gotchas

- A free project pauses after 1 week of inactivity.
- Only 2 active projects fit on the Free plan, so a dev, staging, and production setup needs a paid plan.
- Enterprise SSO (SAML) and phone-based advanced MFA need a paid plan, according to the pricing page.

## Questions people ask

### Is Supabase Auth free?

Yes. The Free plan includes up to 50,000 monthly active users. It costs $0, and you are not charged while on it.

### Does Supabase Auth pause?

The project does. Free projects are paused after 1 week of inactivity, with a limit of 2 active projects.

### Does Supabase Auth need a credit card?

The pricing page shows a Start for Free button and does not mention a card.
