You add a feedback button with a small script tag or a React component. Messages arrive by email and in a dashboard. It suits small websites and early products that want a quick way to hear from users.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan gives you 25 total feedback submissions. The site says there is no time limit and no credit card. The cap counts all submissions together, so it does not reset each month.

You get the feedback widget and the dashboard. The site describes the flow like this: collect issues, ideas, and compliments with a simple widget, receive them as email, and dive deeper in the dashboard. The pricing page lists 25 total submissions as the Free plan, and does not break down other features by plan.

The paid plan costs $19 per month. It adds unlimited feedback submissions and is aimed at production applications and websites of all sizes. A custom Enterprise offer adds whitelabeling, service-level agreements, and live chat support.

## When you hit a limit

After 25 submissions, the Free plan has reached its cap. The pricing page does not say what the widget does after that point. It only lists the $19 plan as the one with unlimited submissions, so plan to upgrade before you launch to a wide audience.

You are not billed on the Free plan. The paid plan is a separate choice. The cap does not renew, so a small site can use all 25 submissions in a few days.

## Gotchas

- The cap is 25 submissions in total, not per month. It does not reset.
- To reply to a customer, you attach their email address to the feedback entry.
- Whitelabeling, service-level agreements, and live chat support are listed only for Enterprise, so the Free and $19 plans do not include them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Feedback.fish free?

Yes, up to 25 total feedback submissions. The site says there is no credit card and no time limit.

### What happens when I reach 25 submissions on Feedback.fish?

The pricing page does not say. It lists the $19 per month plan as the one with unlimited submissions.

### How do I get Feedback.fish messages?

You receive them as email, and you can read them in the dashboard. You can also reply to a customer by attaching their email address.
