VPNDetection returns information about the network behind an IP address. Your application can use it as one signal when investigating traffic or explaining why a connection came from a known VPN or relay. The provider exposes both a basic public response and a richer set of fields.

## What the free plan gives you

The basic offer includes VPN, Tor, relay, bot and anycast flags along with country-level location and network details. The provider advertises these lookups as unlimited and says they require no key. To inspect the full response, create a free account and use the separate allowance of 100 lookups a month. The extra fields include more network and proxy classifications. The two response shapes matter when building a parser. A field that appears in paid or test data can be entirely absent from the public lookup. Record that as missing information and preserve the distinction in your application.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing card now lists 100 full-field trial lookups per month, replacing the previous daily offer. It does not specify the exact trial reset time. Once the trial is spent, requests still answer with Unlimited core fields and a trial_expired indicator. If most of your workload needs rich fields, compare the paid plans and their per-lookup overage rates before choosing one. Paid overage is off by default: exhausted accounts fall back to core fields. Enabling overage in Billing can retain all fields up to a chosen spending cap, or without a limit if the cap is left empty. A retry can repeat work unnecessarily, especially if the issue is that a particular field is outside the selected response.

## Gotchas

- Treat a missing field in the basic response as unavailable. It does not establish that the IP is clear of that network type. Public classification describes network infrastructure. Avoid treating it as proof of a person’s identity.
- Commercial use in your own product is allowed, but the pricing FAQ prohibits reselling or redistributing API answers or using them in a competing service. Paid batches can contain up to 1,000 addresses, but each address counts as a lookup. Paid overage is optional, not automatic.
- Free accounts also get Tor IP, Relay IP and Relay Provider datasets under CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution and share-alike requirements.

## Questions people ask

### Do I need an API key?

The basic lookup is public. Create a key when you want to inspect the additional data on the free account.

### How much full data can I try?

The free account can try the full response for 100 lookups per day.

### What does the basic response cover?

The provider lists VPN, Tor and relay flags along with location and ASN.
