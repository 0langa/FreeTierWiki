You install the Tailscale client on each device, sign in, and the devices see each other with a stable IP address and a MagicDNS name. It suits people who want private access to their own machines with little setup.

## What the free plan gives you

The Personal plan is free forever. It allows up to 6 users and unlimited user devices, such as computers and phones. It includes up to 50 tagged resources, such as exit nodes and servers, up to 3 ACL groups, and 1,000 minutes a month for short-lived ephemeral resources like CI runners.

Personal gives you nearly all of Tailscale's features. These include secure peer-to-peer connections, subnet routers and exit nodes, MagicDNS, ACLs, and Tailscale SSH. Support is basic. The plan has the basic user roles of Owner, Admin, and Member.

Tailscale says the Personal plan is for individuals using it at home and is only suitable for non-commercial use.

## When you hit a limit

You can add more tagged resources for $1 a month each, using the add-on on the pricing page. Short-lived workloads that need more ephemeral minutes mean contacting sales. The Premium plan includes 10,000 of those minutes a month.

More than 6 users means the Standard plan, at $8 per user per month. Standard also lifts the user cap and allows up to 10 ACL groups. The pages do not describe a hard stop at the free limits, so check the admin console before you add users or tagged servers.

## Gotchas

- Signing up with a custom domain email, such as @example.com, starts a 14-day Enterprise trial and makes the company the account owner. A public domain like @gmail.com gets the Personal plan.
- The Personal plan is not meant for commercial use, even if you are below the user limit.
- Tailscale connects devices only. You must run a service, such as SSH or a web server, on the device you connect to.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tailscale free?

Yes. The Personal plan is free forever for up to 6 users with unlimited user devices and 50 tagged resources. It is for non-commercial use.

### Can I use the Tailscale free plan for my business?

No. Tailscale says the Personal plan is only suitable for non-commercial use. Business teams should use a paid plan or the 14-day trial.

### Does Tailscale need a credit card?

The pricing page does not ask for one for the Personal plan. You sign in with an identity provider account.
