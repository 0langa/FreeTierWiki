You install the Tailscale client on each device, sign in, and the devices see each other with a stable IP address and a MagicDNS name. It suits people who want private access to their own machines with little setup.

## What the free plan gives you

The Personal plan is free forever. It allows up to 6 users and unlimited user devices, such as computers and phones. It includes up to 50 tagged resources, such as exit nodes and servers, up to 3 ACL groups, and 1,000 minutes a month for short-lived ephemeral resources like CI runners.

Personal gives you nearly all of Tailscale's features. These include secure peer-to-peer connections, subnet routers and exit nodes, MagicDNS, ACLs, and basic Tailscale SSH for up to 5 hosts. Support is basic. The plan has the basic user roles of Owner, Admin, and Member.

Tailscale says the Personal plan is for individuals using it at home and is only suitable for non-commercial use.

## When you hit a limit

The current pricing FAQ says hard limits and overages for ACL groups, multiple tailnets, tagged resources and ephemeral resources are not yet enforced. Tailscale plans to introduce enforcement with account visibility and email notices; do not treat today's behavior as permanent.

Extra tagged resources are listed at $1 each monthly, with larger ephemeral allowances handled through sales. Personal's published allowance remains six users. The reviewed sources do not specify what happens when a seventh Personal user attempts to join. Paid seat-based plans can automatically increase purchased seats and prorate charges when more users join, so their billing behavior should not be applied to free Personal accounts.

## Gotchas

- Signing up with a custom domain email, such as @example.com, starts a 14-day Enterprise trial. A public domain like @gmail.com gets the Personal plan.
- The Personal plan is not meant for commercial use, even if you are below the user limit.
- Tailscale connects devices only. You must run a service, such as SSH or a web server, on the device you connect to.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tailscale free?

Yes. The Personal plan is free forever for up to 6 users with unlimited user devices and 50 tagged resources. It is for non-commercial use.

### Can I use the Tailscale free plan for my business?

No. Tailscale says the Personal plan is only suitable for non-commercial use. Business teams should use a paid plan or the 14-day trial.

### Does Tailscale need a credit card?

Current Personal pricing and setup docs do not explicitly state the card requirement. Signing in through an identity provider is not proof of a billing policy.
