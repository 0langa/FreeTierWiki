You call it through a REST API or an SDK, and you can test it first in the Foundry portal. It suits developers who need to screen user posts or model output in a small app.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free tier is called F0. It includes 5,000 text records per month and 5,000 images per month. The counts reset each month. You need an Azure subscription and a Content Safety resource in a supported region.

The docs also set a query rate for the F0 tier. Most features allow 5 requests per second, and groundedness detection shows no free access at all. That is enough for a prototype or a small site. It is not enough for a busy app that checks every message.

The paid S0 tier has higher rate limits. The pricing page shows its per-1,000 prices as blank, so check the Azure pricing calculator for real numbers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page is clear. Usage stops when you reach the transaction limit on the Free tier, and customers cannot build up overages on the free tier. Your calls fail until the next month, or until you move to a paid tier.

Moving to S0 is a separate step that you take on purpose. You will not be moved up on your own, so a surprise bill from the free resource is not the risk. The risk is that your moderation calls stop at the worst moment.

## Gotchas

- The F0 rate limit is 5 requests per second, even before you reach the monthly count.
- Groundedness detection is a preview feature, and the docs say it works with English only.
- Microsoft retires older API versions 90 days after a new version ships, so pin a current version and watch the What's new page.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure AI Content Safety free?

Yes, the F0 tier is free. It includes 5,000 text records and 5,000 images per month.

### What happens when I go over the Content Safety free limit?

Usage stops. The pricing page says customers cannot build up overages on the free tier. You must upgrade to a paid tier on purpose to keep going.

### Does Azure AI Content Safety need a credit card?

Yes. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.
