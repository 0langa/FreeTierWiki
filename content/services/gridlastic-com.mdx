You control Gridlastic from a web dashboard, then point your existing Selenium tests at the grid hub it gives you. It suits QA engineers who have Selenium scripts and want hosted browsers without running nodes.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists the Free Plan at $0 per month with 5 free grid starts and 2 parallel grid nodes. Each grid start runs for at most 2 hours, because the free plan limits the time your Selenium Grid runs.

The hub server normally runs 24/7 on paid plans. On the free plan, the 2-hour limit applies to each start. You choose the Amazon region and node size from the dashboard. The pricing page says you do not need an Amazon account, since the AWS costs are part of the subscription. Free plan support is by email only.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan stops your grid after 2 hours of runtime per start. When you use up your 5 free starts, you need a paid plan or you wait for the monthly refresh. The pricing page does not say what happens to a grid that is still running at the 2-hour mark beyond that limit.

Paid plans use credits that refresh monthly. The page says extra credits on a paid plan cost 3 cents each, but this does not apply to the free plan, so no overage bill is described for free accounts.

## Gotchas

- Email support tickets are ranked by your plan, so free accounts wait behind paying ones.
- Phone support is for paid plans only.
- You must pass the platform, browser, and browser version for each test, because the auto scaling needs all three to launch a node.

## Questions people ask

### Is Gridlastic free?

There is a free plan at $0 per month. It gives you 5 free grid starts, 2 parallel nodes, and 2 hours per grid start.

### What happens when I go over the Gridlastic free limits?

Your grid stops at the 2-hour runtime limit. After the 5 free starts, you need a paid plan. The cheapest paid plan on the pricing page is $795 per month.

### Does Gridlastic need a credit card?

The pricing page does not ask for a card on the free plan. Paid subscription plans are billed monthly by credit card.
