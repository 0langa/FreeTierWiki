You connect a Git repository in the dashboard, and Render builds it and gives the service a public address. You can also deploy from the Render CLI. The Free instance types are for testing and hobby projects, and Render says not to use them for production.

## What the free plan gives you

Static sites are free to deploy. A Free web service has 512 MB of RAM and less than 1 CPU, and the workspace gets 750 Free instance hours per calendar month. Services that are spun down use no hours. The Hobby workspace includes 5 GB of bandwidth per month, then $0.15 per GB, and the plan page lists up to 25 services.

You can also create one free Postgres database per workspace, with 256 MB of RAM, 100 connections, and 1 GB of storage. One free Key Value instance is allowed too, and it keeps data in memory only.

## When you hit a limit

If you use all 750 instance hours, Render suspends your Free web services until the start of the next month. If you use all your outbound bandwidth, Render bills you for the extra when a payment method is on file. Without a payment method, it suspends all your Free services for the rest of the month.

Build pipeline minutes work in a similar way. Without a payment method, or at your spend limit, Render disables new builds for the month while existing services keep running. Render emails you when you approach a limit.

## Gotchas

- A Free web service spins down after 15 minutes without inbound traffic, and waking it takes about one minute. Changes to its local filesystem are lost when it spins down.
- A Free Postgres database expires 30 days after creation. You then have a 14-day grace period to upgrade, and after that Render deletes it and its data. Free databases have no backups.
- Free web services cannot send outbound traffic on ports 25, 465, or 587, which SMTP mail commonly uses.

## Questions people ask

### Is Render free?

Yes, within limits. Static sites are free, a Free web service gets 750 instance hours per month, and you can create one free Postgres database that expires after 30 days.

### Does the Render free tier sleep?

Yes. A Free web service spins down after 15 minutes without inbound traffic. It spins back up on the next request, which takes about a minute.

### What happens when I go over the Render free limits?

If you use all 750 instance hours, Render suspends your free web services until next month. If you use all your bandwidth, you are billed for the extra when a payment method is saved. Otherwise your free services are suspended.
