Render offers a free tier for hosting static sites, a small web service, and a 1 GB PostgreSQL database, making it ideal for rapid prototyping and small projects. The platform automates deployments from Git, provides managed SSL, and includes basic monitoring. Free web services sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity, and bandwidth is capped at 5 GB/month per workspace, but there's no risk of surprise overages without a card on file. It's best suited for students, indie developers, and startups needing simple, reliable hosting without infrastructure management.
