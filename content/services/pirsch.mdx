Pirsch is a web analytics service that runs without cookies. It presents itself as an alternative to Google Analytics, with events, conversion goals, and funnels. It suits developers and agencies who want simple, privacy-friendly stats for one or many sites.

## What the free plan gives you

Pirsch offers a free 30-day trial with no credit card required, rather than a permanent free plan. Paid plans start at $6 a month for 10,000 monthly page views.

The Standard plan covers 50 websites, unlimited members, unlimited data retention, events and conversion goals, and Google Analytics import. Plus, at $12 a month at that traffic level, adds unlimited websites, funnels, and priority support. You can download all your statistics as CSV files from the settings page, and an API lets you work with the data in your own tools. Payment methods are credit card, PayPal, or SEPA direct debit for euro billing.

## When you hit a limit

The monthly limit counts page views, events, and 10% of session extensions since the start of your billing cycle, and deleted domains count until it resets. If you reach the limit before the end of the billing period, your access is limited to the day you reached it.

Pirsch keeps collecting data for your sites for a further five days. To regain access to your statistics and avoid gaps, you must upgrade within those five days or wait until the usage limit resets. The page does not say what happens when the 30-day trial itself ends.

## Gotchas

- There is no free plan to fall back to after the 30 days.
- Custom events count toward the page view limit, so event-heavy sites need a bigger tier.
- If you miss the five-day window after hitting the limit, data collection can have gaps.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pirsch free?

Not permanently. It has a free 30-day trial with no credit card, and paid plans start at $6 a month.

### What happens when I reach my monthly page view limit?

Your dashboard access is limited to the day you hit the limit, and data keeps being collected for five more days while you decide to upgrade.

### Can I export my Pirsch data?

Yes. You can download your statistics as CSV files from the settings page, and there is an API.
