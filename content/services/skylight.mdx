Skylight is a performance monitoring service for Ruby and Rails apps, made by Tilde. It records traces of web requests and background jobs and shows where time goes, so you can find slow endpoints and queries. It suits Rails developers who want a focused profiler rather than a general monitoring platform.

## What the free plan gives you

Billing is based on monthly traces, meaning web requests and background jobs, across all of your apps. The pricing page says every account gets 100,000 free traces per month. The tier table lists the Free tier at 100,000 monthly requests with no base price.

Skylight is also free for open source apps through the Skylight for Open Source program, which the program page describes as free and public performance reporting for open source Ruby and Rails apps, with guidelines for which apps qualify. The pricing page offers a free month to try it with no credit card required.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page says Skylight automatically adjusts your tier based on the number of monthly traces your apps receive. The next tier, S, covers 1 million requests for $20 a month, with additional requests at $20 per million. The pricing page does not say whether tracing stops for an account with no payment details. The support docs say each month's invoice on your billing page shows how many requests you used.

## Gotchas

- The free 100,000 traces are shared across all your apps, not given per app.
- Background jobs count as traces, so a busy job queue uses the allowance as fast as web traffic.
- Open source program data is public by design.

## Questions people ask

### Is Skylight free?

Yes, up to 100,000 traces a month across all your apps, and free for qualifying open source apps.

### What counts as a trace?

Web requests and background jobs both count toward the monthly traces.

### Does trying Skylight need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says you can start a free month with no credit card required.
