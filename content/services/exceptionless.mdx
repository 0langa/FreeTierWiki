You sign up, create a project, and add an Exceptionless client for your platform. Errors, logs, and feature usage then show up in the web app. It suits small .NET and JavaScript projects, and you can also host it yourself.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and needs no credit card. It includes 1 project and 1 user, 3 days of event retention, and 3,000 events per month. It has no premium features.

Premium features are searching, error occurrence notifications, and web hooks with Slack and Zapier integrations. On the Free plan you only get daily summaries instead of live notifications. The Small plan costs $15 per month and raises the numbers to 5 projects, 10 users, 30 days of retention, and 15,000 events per month.

Exceptionless is open source, and the pricing page says you can host it yourself if you have the resources.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing FAQ says it will simply send you a notification if you go over your error limit. If you continue to exceed the limit, you will be asked to upgrade your plan, or your account will be closed.

So the risk is losing the free account, not a surprise charge. The Free plan needs no card. You can change your plan at any time and cancel before your next billing period.

## Gotchas

- The Free plan allows 1 user, so a team cannot share it.
- Searching, web hooks, Slack, and Zapier are paid features.
- Event data is kept for only 3 days on Free, while Large and Extra Large keep it for 180 days.

## Questions people ask

### Is Exceptionless free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes 1 project, 1 user, 3,000 events per month, and 3 days of event retention. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I go over the Exceptionless event limit?

Exceptionless sends you a notification. If you keep going over, you will be asked to upgrade or your account will be closed.

### Can I host Exceptionless myself?

Yes. The pricing page says you can host it yourself, and the docs list Docker and Kubernetes pages under Self Hosting.
