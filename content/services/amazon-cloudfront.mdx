CloudFront is the CDN inside AWS. You create a distribution in front of an origin, such as an S3 bucket, a load balancer, or any public URL, and CloudFront serves it from edge locations near your visitors. It suits developers who host on AWS and want caching, HTTPS, and DDoS protection in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

There are two ways to use CloudFront for free. The first is the flat-rate Free plan at $0/month per distribution. It includes 1M requests and 100GB of data transfer each month, 5GB of S3 storage, always-on DDoS protection, a TLS certificate, DNS, and serverless edge compute. You can have up to 3 Free plans per AWS account.

The second is pay-as-you-go pricing with the Always Free tier: 1 TB of data transfer out to the internet, 10,000,000 HTTP or HTTPS requests, 2,000,000 CloudFront Function invocations, and 2,000,000 KeyValueStore reads each month, with free SSL certificates. Origin fetches from AWS origins such as S3 are free.

## When you hit a limit

On the flat-rate Free plan you are not charged overages, however much you exceed the allowance. AWS sends notifications as you approach and pass it. Your first traffic spike up to 3x the allowance does not affect service that month. If you keep exceeding it without upgrading, traffic delivery might be adjusted, for example served from fewer edge locations.

On pay-as-you-go pricing nothing stops at the Always Free amounts. Data transfer and requests past them are billed at the listed rates per GB and per request.

## Gotchas

- A new AWS account on the Free plan closes after 6 months or when its credits run out. Upgrade to a Paid plan to keep using CloudFront after that.
- You cannot delete a distribution that is on a pricing plan until you cancel the plan. Free plans cancel immediately.
- Each distribution has its own plan, so a second site needs a second Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amazon CloudFront free?

Yes, within limits. The flat-rate Free plan costs $0/month with 1M requests and 100GB of data transfer, and pay-as-you-go includes an Always Free 1 TB of data transfer out each month.

### Will I be charged if my site goes viral on the Free plan?

No. Flat-rate plans have no overage charges, though sustained use far above the allowance can lead AWS to adjust delivery.

### Do I need a credit card for AWS?

AWS says a payment method is not required to sign up for most new customers. In some cases it may ask for one to verify your identity.
