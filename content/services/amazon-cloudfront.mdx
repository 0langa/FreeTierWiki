CloudFront is the CDN inside AWS. You create a distribution in front of an origin, such as an S3 bucket, a load balancer, or any public URL, and CloudFront serves it from edge locations near your visitors. It suits developers who host on AWS and want caching, HTTPS, and DDoS protection in one place.

## What the free plan gives you

CloudFront has two free routes. Flat-rate Free costs $0/month per distribution, including 1M requests, 100GB monthly transfer, a 5GB S3 Standard storage credit, DDoS protection, TLS, DNS, and serverless edge compute. The credit offsets S3 Standard storage in the payer account, including buckets that are not CloudFront origins. Eligible accounts can have 3 Free plans, each with one apex domain. Accounts using AWS Free Tier cannot subscribe to flat-rate plans.

Pay-as-you-go includes an Always Free allowance: 1 TB internet transfer, 10,000,000 HTTP/HTTPS requests, 2,000,000 Function invocations, and 2,000,000 KeyValueStore reads monthly, plus SSL certificates. Fetches from AWS origins such as S3 are free.

## When you hit a limit

Flat-rate Free has no overages. AWS sends usage notifications; the first spike up to 3x the allowance does not affect service that month. Repeated excess can reduce delivery coverage unless you upgrade.

Attach an eligible Route 53 zone to cover standard DNS costs: unlimited supported ALIAS queries and 1 million other monthly queries, including CNAME. After excess usage and notification, AWS can move DNS to pay-as-you-go while CloudFront continues.

Pay-as-you-go excess consumes Free-account credits or incurs Paid-account rates. Optional Paid project spend limits start at $20 and pause resources; they are separate from CloudFront allowances. Monitor the CloudFront plan and DNS separately because their excess-usage consequences differ.

## Gotchas

- The AWS account Free plan is separate from CloudFront Free. At 6 months or depleted credits the account is suspended; data is retained 90 days for a Paid upgrade before deletion.
- You cannot delete a distribution that is on a pricing plan until you cancel the plan. Free plans cancel immediately.
- Plans apply per distribution, not per website. Origin resources and services outside the plan can still have separate AWS charges.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amazon CloudFront free?

Yes, within limits. The flat-rate Free plan costs $0/month with 1M requests and 100GB of data transfer, and pay-as-you-go includes an Always Free 1 TB of data transfer out each month.

### Will I be charged if my site goes viral on the Free plan?

The CloudFront distribution has no flat-rate overage charges, though sustained excess can lead AWS to adjust delivery. An attached Route 53 zone has a separate DNS allowance and can move to paid usage after excess and notification.

### Do I need a credit card for AWS?

Most new customers can sign up for AWS without a payment method, though AWS may request one for identity verification. To use the flat-rate CloudFront plan, the AWS account must meet the eligibility rules above.
