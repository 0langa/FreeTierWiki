You use D1 from a Worker through a binding, from the Wrangler CLI, or over the REST API. It suits developers who already build on Cloudflare.

## What the free plan gives you

The Workers Free plan includes D1 with 5 million rows read per day and 100,000 rows written per day. Storage is 5 GB in total across all databases, and each database can hold up to 500 MB. You can create 10 databases per account. Time Travel, the point-in-time recovery feature, keeps 7 days. A free Worker can run 50 queries per invocation.

There are no charges for data transfer or throughput. Free limits reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Cloudflare says the Free plan will always let you prototype with D1.

## When you hit a limit

At the daily read or write limit, you cannot run queries. The D1 API returns errors telling your client the daily limits are exceeded. When you reach the storage limit, you must delete unused databases or clean up data before you can insert more.

Nothing is billed on the Free plan. Upgrading to the Workers Paid plan removes these limits, usually within minutes.

## Gotchas

- Indexes add a written row for each write that touches an indexed column, which counts against the daily write limit.
- Storage is the sum of all databases in your account, and tables and indexes both count.
- Each free database can hold up to 500 MB, so large datasets need the paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare D1 free?

Yes. D1 is included in the Workers Free plan, with 5 million rows read per day, 100,000 rows written per day, and 5 GB of storage in total.

### What happens when I go over the D1 free limits?

Queries fail with errors once you hit the daily read or write limit, until the reset at 00:00 UTC. At the storage limit, you must free up space before inserting new data.

### Will Cloudflare D1 always have a free plan?

Cloudflare says the Workers Free plan will always include the ability to prototype and experiment with D1 for free.
