You work in a web app. You make an inbox, feed it documents by email, upload, or API, and pick a parser. Then you export the data or pass it to other tools. No code is needed.

## What the free plan gives you

The free Sandbox plan gives you 30 credits per month and needs no credit card. It includes all four parser engines, unlimited team members, and 30-day data retention. Credits renew monthly and are shared across parsers. A page uses credits only the first time Parsio parses it.

The cost per page depends on the parser. The template-based parser and the OCR converter cost 1 credit per page. The GPT-powered parser costs 2 credits per page. The AI parser costs 3 credits per page. The pricing page lists 15 pages with the GPT-powered parser or 30 OCR pages as free examples. Post-processing, where you write Python to reformat fields, is available on every plan.

## When you hit a limit

When your credits run out, your account can still receive emails, but Parsio will not parse them until the next billing period. You can instead upgrade your plan or turn on early plan renewal to top up your credits at once. You can also re-process documents that Parsio parsed before. Unused credits expire at the end of each billing period.

## Gotchas

- The AI parser uses 3 credits per page, so 30 free credits cover only a few documents with it.
- Unused credits expire. They do not roll over to the next month.
- Documents stay for 30 days on the free plan. Paid plans keep them for 90 days or longer.

## Questions people ask

### Is Parsio free?

Yes. The Sandbox plan gives you 30 credits per month with all four parser engines and unlimited team members. No credit card is needed.

### What happens when I run out of Parsio credits?

Emails still arrive, but Parsio does not parse them until the next billing period. You can upgrade or renew early to get credits right away.

### How many pages can I parse for free on Parsio?

It depends on the parser. With 30 credits, the template-based parser or OCR gives about 30 pages. The GPT-powered parser gives 15 pages.
