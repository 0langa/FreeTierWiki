You work in the Amplitude web app and add its SDK to your site or app to send events. Free fits product teams and startups that want funnels and retention without paying per seat.

## What the free plan gives you

Free includes 2 million events monthly, unlimited seats, no time limit and no credit card. Amplitude explicitly distinguishes it from a trial. Events include views, clicks, signups and purchases; the Browser SDK supports autocapture and explicit tracking.

Separate allowances cover 10,000 session replays and 2,000 AI feedback records monthly. Feature flags are unlimited. One active feature experiment and one active web experiment are permitted, but experiment results are excluded. Guides and surveys have a one-item allowance. Organizations can save 10 charts, use 1 collaboration space and access 1 year of analytics data. Session-replay retention is 1 month. Plus also starts with 2 million free events but requires a credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The billing FAQ describes a block after three monthly overages and account deletion after six months of continued excess following the initial block. During a block, charts and dashboards are unavailable, while administrative compliance functions remain accessible. The FAQ links to its Free tracked-user plan guide for details and directs you to Plans & Billing for your organization's allowance. It does not specify how the block applies to the 2 million-event offer advertised on the pricing page.

Plus is a separately selected paid plan requiring a card. Its usage beyond the purchased allowance incurs fees at the same per-unit rate, despite the pricing page’s phrase “no overage penalties.” Check the applicable quota and rate before upgrading.

## Gotchas

- Session replay and AI feedback records each have a separate monthly cap on the Free plan. The 2 million event cap is not the only limit.
- Plus needs a credit card, even though the first 2 million events each month stay free there too.
- Startup help is a scholarship, not a plan. Companies with under $10M in funding and fewer than 20 employees can apply for one free year of Growth.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amplitude free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 2 million events per month with unlimited seats and no time limit. You do not need a credit card.

### Does Amplitude need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan. You only add a card when you upgrade to Plus.

### What counts as an event in Amplitude?

An event is a tracked action such as a click, page view, signup, or purchase. The Browser SDK can autocapture interactions or send your own events; one user can generate many.
