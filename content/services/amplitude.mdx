You work in the Amplitude web app and add its SDK to your site or app to send events. Free fits product teams and startups that want funnels and retention without paying per seat.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan covers 2 million events per month, with no time limit and no credit card. Amplitude says it is not a trial. Every plan has unlimited seats, so your whole team can log in without extra cost. An event is any action a user takes, such as a click, a page view, a signup, or a purchase.

The Free plan also includes several add-on products with their own caps. You get 10,000 session replays per month and 2,000 AI feedback records per month. Feature flags are unlimited. Experiments, guides, and surveys are included in a limited form. The pricing page lists a retention period of 1 month for session replays. Amplitude Plus is the next plan up. It also starts with 2 million free events, but it needs a credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe what Amplitude does with events past the 2 million cap on the Free plan. It says you add a card only when you upgrade to Plus and grow beyond the free limits. So the Free plan itself never bills you.

The page also says Plus has no overage penalties. Plus is a separate plan that you must choose. Watch your event count in the usage view and plan to upgrade before a busy month. If you track every click, a single busy page can use up events fast.

## Gotchas

- Session replay and AI feedback records each have a separate monthly cap on the Free plan. The 2 million event cap is not the only limit.
- Plus needs a credit card, even though the first 2 million events each month stay free there too.
- Startup help is a scholarship, not a plan. Companies with under $10M in funding and fewer than 20 employees can apply for one free year of Growth.

## Questions people ask

### Is Amplitude free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 2 million events per month with unlimited seats and no time limit. You do not need a credit card.

### Does Amplitude need a credit card?

Not for the Free plan. You only add a card when you upgrade to Plus.

### What counts as an event in Amplitude?

An event is any action a user takes in your product, like a button click, a page view, a signup, or a purchase. One user can create many events in a month.
