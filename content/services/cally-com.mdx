You use Cally.com in the browser or in the iOS and Android apps. Think of it as a poll for dates and places that a group answers, and a place to set the final choice.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called Basic and the pricing page says it is free forever. It gives you an overview of all current and future events, events shown in a calendar, free iOS and Android apps, an address book with groups, and notification settings. You can import up to 100 contacts into the address book.

Plus costs 3 euros per month or 29 euros per year, both excluding VAT. It removes ads, keeps events, lets you add documents, adds your own logo and CC email addresses, and raises the contact import to 1000.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not describe a cap on how many events you can create. The limits are on features: ads on the free plan, events that are not retained after they finish, and 100 contact imports. When you need more, you upgrade to Plus. You can cancel Plus in the Subscription section of your account and keep its benefits until the end of the paid term. The free plan asks for no card.

## Gotchas

- Free events are not kept after they finish, so do not rely on Basic as a record of past events.
- Ads appear on Basic. The page lists no ads as a Plus feature.
- The Enterprise plan has its own price and extra features such as a custom domain, so it is not a free option.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cally.com free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever, but it shows ads and does not keep events after they finish.

### How much does Cally.com Plus cost?

Plus costs 3 euros per month or 29 euros per year, excluding VAT, for one user. You can cancel it in your account.

### Does Cally.com work on phones?

Yes. The site offers free iOS and Android apps for picking a date.
