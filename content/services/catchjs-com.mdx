You add one script tag to your pages and read errors, performance numbers, and pageviews in the CatchJS dashboard. It suits frontend developers who want to know about browser errors on their own site.

## What the free plan gives you

CatchJS has no permanent free plan. Every paid plan starts with a free 14-day trial, and a card is needed to begin it. The Development plan costs $44 per month when paid annually, or $49 when paid monthly. It includes 1,000 logged errors per month and 1,000 log API calls per month, plus performance metrics for 100 URLs. Pageviews cover 10,000 URLs, and one domain is allowed, with unlimited subdomains of it.

Open source projects can get a free subscription. The site asks you to apply.

## When you hit a limit

The docs say there is no charge when a quota is reached. CatchJS ignores any further logged data and does not bill you for the extra use. Each quota is counted separately, so one full quota does not stop the others. Logging quotas reset at the start of each month, and screenshot quotas reset every day.

The real bill is the plan itself. The first charge happens when the 14-day trial ends, and you pay monthly from then on. You can cancel at any time in account settings. If you cancel during the trial, the card is not charged. Larger plans raise the limits: Production costs $79 per month annually and allows 100,000 logged errors.

## Gotchas

- The sign-up form asks for a credit or debit card, even though the card is not charged until the 14-day trial is over.
- Each plan covers 1 domain, with unlimited subdomains of it. Enterprise allows 25 domains.
- Cancel before the trial ends if you do not want to pay, because the first charge is automatic.

## Questions people ask

### Is CatchJS free?

Not permanently. Every plan has a free 14-day trial, and open source projects can apply for a free subscription.

### What happens when I pass the CatchJS error quota?

CatchJS ignores further logged data and does not charge you for the extra use.

### How many errors does the cheapest CatchJS plan allow?

The Development plan allows 1,000 logged errors per month for one domain. It costs $44 per month when paid annually.
