You build a workflow in the Azure portal designer by adding a trigger and then actions from a gallery of connectors. You can also use Visual Studio Code or the Azure CLI. It suits teams that want automation without writing custom code.

## What the free plan gives you

The Consumption plan runs workflows in multitenant Azure Logic Apps and bills per execution. The pricing page lists the first 4,000 actions as free and included. Microsoft's pricing docs say the free built-in operations are counted per Azure subscription, so every workflow in the subscription shares them.

Built-in operations include the HTTP and Request triggers, the HTTP and Response actions, loops, conditions, and data operations. The Standard plan, which runs on a hosting plan you pay for, includes unlimited free built-in operations, but you pay for the hosting plan itself. The pricing page text we could read shows its dollar rates only as placeholders, so this page gives no prices.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops your workflows at the free amount. Above it, the Consumption plan meters each execution and bills it, whether or not the workflow finishes or even starts. A polling trigger counts as one execution each time it checks, even when it finds no new data.

Managed connector operations are never part of the free built-in operations. Microsoft bills them at standard or enterprise connector rates from the first call. A frequent polling trigger on a managed connector can therefore produce a bill quickly.

## Gotchas

- The free amount covers built-in operations only. Managed connector operations are billed as soon as you use them.
- Some managed connector operations also exist as built-in ones. The built-in version uses the free amount, and above it billing follows the actions price, not the connector price.
- A Consumption logic app resource holds only one workflow. The Standard type holds many workflows but bills through a hosting plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Logic Apps free?

Partly. The Consumption plan lists the first 4,000 actions as free, and you pay for everything above that. Managed connector calls are billed from the start.

### Does Azure Logic Apps need a credit card?

Yes. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

### What happens when I go over the free actions in Logic Apps?

Nothing blocks the workflow. Azure bills each extra execution at the actions price, so watch your cost reports.
