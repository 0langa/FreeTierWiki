You open the page, pick a method, type the URL, and send. The request goes out from your browser, so it needs no install and no sign-up. It suits learners and developers who want to try a few calls.

## What the free plan gives you

The whole REST client is free and needs no registration. You can set the method, the URL, basic authentication, headers, and a request body. You can beautify an XML or JSON response.

The page keeps the last 50 requests in your browser history. You can also save favorites and collections of requests. Without an account these stay local in your browser. With a free account the site backs them up on the server side.

The site is part of a larger free toolbox with converters, formatters, and other developer tools. The page says advertising is its only income.

## When you hit a limit

The page lists no usage quota or price. The limits are in how the tool works.

The page offers two ways to send a request. Sending from your browser needs CORS enabled on the API you call. Sending from the ExtendsClass server could reach public APIs without CORS, but the site has deactivated that mode due to misuse. The page also says it is better to use a heavier client for advanced tests.

## Gotchas

- Your history, collections, and favorites live in your browser and can be deleted at any time, unless you create an account.
- Do not save sensitive data such as production passwords in the tool. The page warns about this.
- With the server-side mode off, you can only call APIs that allow your browser to reach them.

## Questions people ask

### Is ExtendsClass free?

Yes. The REST client is free and needs no registration. A free account is optional and backs up your collections on the server.

### Does ExtendsClass keep my request history?

It stores the last 50 requests locally in your browser. Without an account, the browser backup can be deleted at any time.

### Why does my ExtendsClass request fail with CORS?

Browser mode needs CORS enabled on your API. The server-side mode that avoided this is deactivated due to misuse.
