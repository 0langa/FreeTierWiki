You manage repositories in the Repsy dashboard and use your usual build tool to publish and install. Deploy tokens let a CI job reach one repository without your account login.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It includes 20 GB of shared storage and unlimited repositories, public or private. The plan lists 1 user per repository and a 99% SLA.

Repsy hosts nine package types: Maven, npm, PyPI, Docker, Cargo, Go, Helm, NuGet, and Ruby. Private repositories need a signed-in account or a deploy token. Public repositories can be downloaded by anyone.

The Pro plan costs $7.90 per month, excluding VAT. It raises storage to 200 GB and adds 2 users per repository, 2 webhooks per repository, one custom domain, and a 99.9% SLA.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page and the docs do not say what happens when your account fills its 20 GB of storage. The Dashboard shows your plan, available storage, and a usage chart, so you can watch it.

The pricing page says no credit card is required for Free. If you downgrade or cancel a paid plan, your data stays secure and reachable for 30 days. In that time you can upgrade again or export your data.

## Gotchas

- Storage is shared across all repositories in your account. It is not 20 GB per repository.
- Repsy supports only scoped npm packages, with names like `@foo/foo`. Plan your package names with a scope from the start.
- A deploy token is scoped to a single repository. Use one to give a build job access to that repository only.

## Questions people ask

### Is Repsy free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 20 GB of shared storage and unlimited repositories. No credit card is required.

### Can I make a private repository on the Repsy free plan?

Yes. Repositories are private by default. Private repositories need a signed-in account or a deploy token to download.

### What happens to my packages if I cancel a Repsy plan?

Your data remains secure and accessible for 30 days after downgrading or cancelling. During that time you can upgrade or export it.
