You upload videos in the Wistia dashboard and publish them with an embed code on your site. It suits marketers who want a clean player and basic numbers on who watched.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It includes 25 GB of storage, 200 GB of bandwidth, and 1 user. It has a customizable, ad-free player and basic video analytics. Wistia branding stays on the player.

The comparison table also lists the virtual recording studio, transcript-based editing, and AI highlights and social clips as included on every plan. The Business plan costs $79 per month billed annually. It adds 250 GB or more of storage, 3 or more users, 1 TB of bandwidth, removal of Wistia branding, collections and playlists, CTAs and forms, and advanced analytics.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the free 25 GB of storage or the 200 GB of bandwidth. It only says that on Business you can prepay for more storage or pay for usage beyond the plan.

The page says the Free plan does not include more than one user. Extra seats cost $25 each, but only on paid plans. A free account that outgrows its limits has to move to Business.

## Gotchas

- Everything in the account counts toward storage, including recordings, clips, and videos you create.
- The free player carries Wistia branding. Removing it needs the Business plan.
- The Free plan has no extra-user option, because the price per additional user is listed as not included.

## Questions people ask

### Is Wistia free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 25 GB of storage, 200 GB of bandwidth, and 1 user, with a customizable ad-free player. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I go over the Wistia free limits?

The pricing page does not say. For Business, it says you can prepay for more storage or pay for usage beyond the plan.

### Can I remove the Wistia branding on the free plan?

No. The comparison table lists branding removal as not included on Free. It starts with the Business plan.
