MailChannels sells two products: Outbound Filtering for hosting providers, and an Email API for platforms and developers sending transactional email. This entry covers the Email API, which has a free developer plan.

## What the free plan gives you

The Email API's Free plan is aimed at developers testing, prototyping, or running small projects. It sends up to 3,000 emails a month with no overages, at a rate of 100 emails a day. It includes REST and SMTP integration, up to 3 domains, 30-day transaction logs, and community support. Open and click tracking are on paid plans.

Until you add a payment method, the docs say you can only send to the addresses of verified users on your account; any other recipient is rejected with a 550 error.

Usage is counted per recipient: one message sent to 100 recipients counts as 100 sends. A send includes any attempted delivery, even a rejected one. Your allocation resets at the start of each billing cycle, and unused emails do not roll over.

## When you hit a limit

MailChannels pauses delivery when you reach the daily limit on the free plan, and further API calls to send fail. The docs say the 100-sends-a-day limit resets at 00:00 UTC each day. The FAQ adds that delivery resumes at the next billing cycle or after a prorated upgrade.

So the free plan stops rather than charging you. The FAQ says there is no tier between the free developer plan and the paid plan for 10,000 emails a month, and the pricing overview says the Email API is volume-based from $10 a month after the free amount.

## Gotchas

- The 100-email daily cap can interrupt a burst even with monthly allowance left, and it resets at 00:00 UTC each day.
- Each recipient counts as a send, so a message to a group uses several sends.
- Without a payment method, sending is limited to verified users on your account. Outbound Filtering is a separate product for hosting providers, starting at $99.99 a month.

## Questions people ask

### Does MailChannels have a free plan?

Yes. The Email API has a free developer plan that includes 100 emails per day, up to 3,000 a month.

### What happens when I go over the MailChannels free limit?

Delivery pauses and further send calls fail. The daily cap resets at 00:00 UTC each day, and the monthly count at the start of the billing cycle.

### Do unused free emails roll over?

No. The allocation resets at the start of each billing cycle.
