MailChannels sells two products: Outbound Filtering for hosting providers, and an Email API for platforms and developers sending transactional email. This entry covers the Email API, which has a free developer plan.

## What the free plan gives you

The Email API's Free plan is aimed at developers testing, prototyping, or running small projects. It sends up to 3,000 emails a month with no overages, at a rate of 100 emails a day. It includes REST and SMTP integration, up to 3 domains, 30-day transaction logs, and community support.

Usage is counted per recipient: one message sent to 100 recipients counts as 100 sends. A send includes any attempted delivery, even one that is deferred, rejected, or marked as spam by the receiving provider. Your allocation resets at the start of each billing cycle, and unused emails do not roll over.

## When you hit a limit

MailChannels pauses delivery when you exceed the plan limit. Its FAQ says delivery resumes next billing cycle or after a prorated upgrade; it does not separately explain daily-cap reset timing. The Free card requirement is also unpublished.

So the free plan stops rather than charging you. The FAQ says there is no tier between the free developer plan and the paid plan for 10,000 emails a month, and the pricing overview says the Email API is volume-based from $10 a month after the free amount.

## Gotchas

- The 100-email daily cap can interrupt a burst even with monthly allowance left; the FAQ does not specify daily recovery timing.
- Each recipient counts as a send, so a message to a group uses several sends.
- Outbound Filtering is a different product for hosting providers, starting at $99.99 a month; it is not the free Email API.

## Questions people ask

### Does MailChannels have a free plan?

Yes. The Email API has a free developer plan that includes 100 emails per day, up to 3,000 a month.

### What happens when I go over the MailChannels free limit?

Delivery pauses. The FAQ describes recovery next billing cycle or on upgrade, but does not separately specify recovery from the daily cap.

### Do unused free emails roll over?

No. The allocation resets at the start of each billing cycle.
