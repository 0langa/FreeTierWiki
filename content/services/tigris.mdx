You use Tigris through any S3 client, the Tigris SDK for TypeScript and Go, or the Tigris CLI. It spreads and moves your data on its own, so you do not set up replication or caching.

## What the free plan gives you

The free tier needs no card to start. Each month you get 5 GB of Standard storage, 10,000 Class A requests, and 100,000 Class B requests. Class A requests are writes and lists such as PUT, COPY, POST, and LIST. Class B requests are reads such as GET.

Egress is free on every tier. DELETE and CANCEL requests are free too, and Tigris does not charge for unauthorized requests to your buckets.

The allowance resets each month and applies before anything is metered. Storage is measured in GB per month, averaged from the daily peak over the billing period.

## When you hit a limit

Tigris does not stop you at the free amounts. Storage past 5 GB and requests past the free amounts are billed. Standard storage costs $0.02 per GB per month. Class A requests cost $0.005 per 1,000, and Class B requests cost $0.0005 per 1,000.

The pricing page lists cheaper tiers. Infrequent Access costs $0.01 per GB per month with a data retrieval fee of $0.01 per GB. Archive tiers cost $0.004 per GB per month. Objects in those tiers carry a minimum storage duration charge.

## Gotchas

- The free allowance is for the Standard tier. Moving data to a colder tier adds retrieval fees and a minimum storage duration.
- Request counts add up quickly for apps that list buckets or fetch many small files. The free amounts are small.
- Object notifications cost $0.01 per 1,000 events.

## Questions people ask

### Is Tigris free?

Within limits. Each month includes 5 GB of Standard storage, 10,000 Class A requests, and 100,000 Class B requests. Egress is always free.

### Does Tigris charge for egress?

No. Tigris lists egress as free on every storage tier.

### Does Tigris need a credit card?

Not to start. The pricing page says there is no card to start, and the free allowance applies before anything is metered.
