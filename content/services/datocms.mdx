You build models and edit content in the DatoCMS dashboard. Your site reads it through the GraphQL Content Delivery API, and scripts can write it through the REST Content Management API. The docs include guides for Next.js, Nuxt, SvelteKit, Astro, and React Router.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is free forever. The pricing page says it needs no credit card and comes with 2 editors and 300 records. It adds 10GB of traffic and 100k API calls each month. The limits table lists 3 projects, 100 models, 5 locales, and 200MB of file storage. API calls are split: 100k Content Delivery API calls and 25k Content Management API calls per month.

It also includes 120 minutes of video streaming per month and 3 days of edit history. Support is community-based. The Professional plan starts at 149 euros per month, billed annually, or 199 euros per month if you pay monthly.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan has hard limits. The pricing page warns that you cannot go over the allowed monthly limits. If you reach them, the service stops responding as expected. The pricing page says no overages are allowed on Free. It does not say how long the service stays stopped or when the count resets, so test your limits before launch.

Paid plans work differently. The page says Professional has soft limits that you can exceed with pay-as-you-go pricing. For example, extra bandwidth costs 29 euros for every 150GB. If you expect traffic spikes, a hard stop can matter more than a bill.

## Gotchas

- The records limit of 300 is small, so check how many records your content model needs before you start.
- The free plan keeps edit history for only 3 days, while Professional keeps 60 days.
- The Free plan has community-based support only.

## Questions people ask

### Is DatoCMS free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever, with 2 editors, 300 records, 10GB of traffic, and 100k API calls each month. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I go over the DatoCMS free limits?

The service stops responding as expected. The pricing page says you cannot go over the monthly limits on the Free plan.

### Does DatoCMS need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says easy setup, with no credit card required.
