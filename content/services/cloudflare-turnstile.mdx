You create a widget in the Cloudflare dashboard, add a small script to your page, and check the token on your server. The same steps work through the API or Terraform. It suits anyone who runs forms on a website.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan covers up to 20 widgets per account and all widget types: managed, non-interactive, and invisible. Challenge volume is unlimited, so you do not pay by request. Each widget can have 10 hostnames, and analytics look back 7 days.

Pre-clearance for single-page apps is included, and so is community support. The docs list the Free plan as suitable for personal sites, small and medium businesses, and most production applications.

Enterprise adds unlimited widgets, up to 200 hostnames per widget, any hostname widgets, ephemeral IDs, Offlabel to remove Cloudflare branding, and 30 days of analytics.

## When you hit a limit

The plans page says free users are limited to 20 widgets per account. Customers with Enterprise Bot Management and Enterprise Turnstile can have that limit raised, and the page tells you to contact your account team.

The page does not describe what happens if you try to create a widget beyond the limit. It lists no overage charge, because challenges are unlimited. If you upgrade to Enterprise, your existing widgets and settings are kept.

## Gotchas

- Validating the token on your server with Siteverify is mandatory. Without it, the setup is incomplete and your analytics show zeroes for token validation.
- Tokens expire after 300 seconds (5 minutes), and each token can be validated only once.
- Use a testing sitekey for development. Production secret keys reject the dummy tokens that testing keys produce.

## Questions people ask

### Is Cloudflare Turnstile free?

Yes. The Free plan gives you up to 20 widgets per account and unlimited challenges. The plans page says Turnstile works without other Cloudflare services.

### Does Cloudflare Turnstile need my site on Cloudflare?

No. The docs say you can use it on any website, whether or not the site is proxied through Cloudflare, and the widget and validation steps are self-contained.

### How long does a Turnstile token last?

A token expires after 300 seconds (5 minutes) and can be validated only once. Expired or used tokens must be replaced with a fresh challenge.
