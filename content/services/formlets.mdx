Formlets is a hosted form builder. You design a form in its editor, publish it, and share or embed it, and responses are stored in your account. It suits a person or small site that needs one dependable form rather than a whole forms workspace.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page says the Personal plan is free forever. It lets you publish 1 form with unlimited responses. That form can be multi-page and multi-column, and the plan lists 2000+ software integrations. Forms on the free plan show Formlets branding.

Paid plans raise the number of published forms to 10, 50, or 100 and add features the free plan lacks: no Formlets branding, custom confirmations to customers, PayPal and Stripe payments, conditional display of form fields, file upload space, a form URL on your own subdomain or domain, and GDPR compliance with a DPA.

## When you hit a limit

The free limit is the number of published forms, not responses. The pricing page does not say what happens if you try to publish a second form on the free plan. It does not mention a card either. The home page says "Unlimited submits & No signup/creditcard needed" for a free form.

If you leave a paid plan, the page says you can downgrade back to the free Personal plan and all your forms will be kept. New paid plans come with a full refund if you cancel in the first 30 days.

## Gotchas

- Only one form can be published at a time, so a second campaign means unpublishing the first or paying.
- Payments and conditional fields are paid features, which rules out order forms and branching questionnaires on the free plan.
- The free plan lists no file upload space, so plan on a paid tier for forms with attachments.

## Questions people ask

### Is Formlets free?

Yes. The Personal plan is free forever and publishes 1 form with unlimited responses.

### Do I lose my forms if I downgrade?

No. The pricing page says all your forms are kept when you downgrade back to the free Personal plan.

### Can I take payments with the free plan?

No. PayPal and Stripe payments are listed on the paid plans only.
