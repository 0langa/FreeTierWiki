You manage instances in the CloudAMQP console. Your apps connect over AMQP, and the broker also supports STOMP, MQTT, and HTTP. It suits developers who want a hosted RabbitMQ or LavinMQ without running servers.

## What the free plan gives you

CloudAMQP has two free shared plans, and you can use them for as long as you like without a credit card. Little Lemur runs a shared RabbitMQ broker. It allows 20 connections, 100 queues, 10,000 queued messages, and 1 M messages per month.

Loyal Lemming runs a shared LavinMQ broker. It allows 40 connections, 200 queues, 20,000 queued messages, and 2 M messages per month. Both give you a single virtual host. Shared plans have a channel limit of 200 and at most 200 consumers per channel.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say what happens when you use up the monthly message quota or the connection limit. Count-based limits such as queues and queued messages are listed as maximums. The free plan needs no billing details, so no bill can follow.

Two clean-up rules matter more. On Little Lemur, a queue that sits idle for 28 days can be removed. Free instances that have not published or consumed a single message for two months are deleted, after a warning email. To grow, you move to a paid shared plan or a dedicated broker.

## Gotchas

- The free plans are for development and small hobby projects. The docs describe dedicated plans as the ones built for production.
- A free instance you leave alone is deleted after two months without a message, so keep a test publish running if you want it to stay.
- Paid plans require a credit card to activate. The free plan needs no billing information.

## Questions people ask

### Is CloudAMQP free?

Yes. The free shared plans Little Lemur (RabbitMQ) and Loyal Lemming (LavinMQ) are free for as long as you like, and need no credit card.

### Does CloudAMQP delete free instances?

Yes, in one case. Free instances that have not published or consumed a single message during the latest two months are deleted after a warning email.

### What is the difference between Little Lemur and Loyal Lemming?

Little Lemur is a shared RabbitMQ broker with 20 connections and 1 M messages per month. Loyal Lemming is a shared LavinMQ broker with 40 connections and 2 M messages per month.
