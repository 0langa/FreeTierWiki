You use Zoho Billing in a web dashboard. You create plans, add customers, and connect a payment gateway. It suits software and service businesses that bill customers on a schedule.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. You get a 14-day free trial of Zoho Billing. The help pages say that when you create an organization, it starts on the Premium plan, and the trial lets you explore the different plans. The pages do not mention a card for the trial.

After the trial you pay. The Standard plan costs $50 per organization per month, or $39 a month billed annually. It suits one-time billing needs and includes quotes and invoices, with up to 3 users. The Premium plan costs $100 per organization per month, or $79 billed annually. It adds subscription billing, hosted payment pages, usage-based billing, and proration, with up to 10 users. Prices do not include local taxes.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens to your data when the 14 days end. You can subscribe to a paid plan during the trial or after it. Plans are monthly or yearly, and the site says there are no binding contracts.

Both paid plans allow up to 100,000 invoices per year, with annual revenue under $1M. For more, you contact the support team. Add-ons cost extra, such as $9 per extra user per month.

## Gotchas

- The trial is the only free path. Do not plan a free-forever setup on it.
- The Standard plan allows 3 users. Premium allows 10. More users cost an add-on.
- Premium is the plan assigned by default in the trial, so you may see features that Standard does not have.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Billing free?

Only for a trial. You get a 14-day free trial, then you pay for Standard at $50 a month or Premium at $100 a month.

### Does Zoho Billing have a free plan?

No. The pricing page shows Standard and Premium, both paid, and a 14-day free trial for each.

### Does Zoho Billing need a credit card for the trial?

The pricing and help pages do not say. The sign-up form asks for a company name, email address, and password.
