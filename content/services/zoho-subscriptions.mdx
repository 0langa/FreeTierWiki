You use Zoho Billing in a web dashboard. You create plans, add customers, and connect a payment gateway. It suits software and service businesses that bill customers on a schedule.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no permanent free plan. You get a 14-day free trial of Zoho Billing. The help pages say that when you create an organization, it starts on the Premium plan, and the trial lets you explore the different plans. The trial signup form asks for contact and account details without a credit card.

The current US edition lists paid Standard, Premium, and a custom Enterprise edition. Standard costs $50 per organization per month, or $39 a month billed annually. It suits one-time billing needs and includes quotes and invoices, with up to 3 users. The Premium plan costs $100 per organization per month, or $79 billed annually. It adds subscription billing, hosted payment pages, usage-based billing, and proration, with up to 10 users. Prices do not include local taxes.

## When you hit a limit

After 14 days, you need to subscribe to keep using Zoho Billing. You can choose a monthly or yearly plan during or after the trial. The pricing page gives no timeline for data retention when the trial expires.

US Standard and Premium include up to 100,000 invoices per year. The plan comparison says accounts move to Enterprise at the $1 million annual billed amount threshold. Add-ons cost extra, such as an extra user at $9 per month or $7.50 with annual billing.

## Gotchas

- The trial is the only free path. Do not plan a free-forever setup on it.
- The Standard plan allows 3 users. Premium allows 10. More users cost an add-on.
- Premium is the plan assigned by default in the trial, so you may see features that Standard does not have.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zoho Billing free?

Only for a trial. You get a 14-day free trial, then you pay for Standard at $50 a month or Premium at $100 a month.

### Does Zoho Billing have a free plan?

No. The pricing page shows paid Standard and Premium plans with 14-day trials, plus a custom Enterprise edition.

### Does Zoho Billing need a credit card for the trial?

The public trial signup form has no credit card field. The account setup guide lists the required details without a card step.
