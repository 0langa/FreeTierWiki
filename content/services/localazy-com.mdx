You manage translations in the Localazy web app. You get your strings in by file upload, the CLI, an SDK, or the API. It suits developers and product teams of any size.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan covers up to 200 source keys, with all essential localization features and unlimited seats and languages. A source key is one text segment in your source language, such as a button label or a short paragraph. Think of it as one row in a spreadsheet.

Translations into other languages are unlimited. A key translated into 10 languages still counts as one source key. Uploading content is included in every plan at no extra cost. The FAQ says the free plan has all core localization features, enough to localize on your own or with volunteers.

New accounts also get a 14-day trial of the Business plan, so you can try paid features first. The pricing page says no credit card is needed.

## When you hit a limit

The limit applies to source keys. When you reach it, you can keep using Localazy and all the features in your plan without restriction. You cannot publish your changes until you upgrade your plan.

You have two ways out. You can upgrade to a higher tier. You can also raise the source key limit for free in the Community section, where reviews, referrals, and similar help earn rewards. The pages show no surprise bill for the free plan.

## Gotchas

- Machine translation with Localazy AI uses credits. The pricing page says paid plans include free credits and extra credits start at $10.
- SSO is only on the Enterprise plan.
- The limit counts source keys, not words. A project with many short strings reaches 200 faster than you expect.

## Questions people ask

### Is Localazy free?

Yes. The free plan covers up to 200 source keys, with unlimited seats and languages. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I reach the Localazy source key limit?

You can keep working with all your plan features. You cannot publish changes until you upgrade, or until you raise the limit through the Community rewards.

### Does Localazy need a credit card?

No. The sign-up guide and the pricing page both say no credit card is required for the free plan or the 14-day trial.
