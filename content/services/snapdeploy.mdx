SnapDeploy runs a container from a repository or an image. You get a URL that someone can open to test the application. The free service fits intermittent browser traffic, such as a small demo that needs to be available occasionally and can wait for a sleeping container to start.

## What the free plan gives you

The plan allows four small containers and 100 container-hours per month across the account. The hours count while containers run. A small container has 512 MB of memory and a quarter of a vCPU. The service also limits deploy attempts, including failed ones, to ten per day and five per rolling 12 hours. Open the URL after deployment to check that the app is listening correctly. If you run multiple demos at once, monitor how much runtime remains for the account. Each running container can draw from the same balance even when the containers belong to different projects.

## When you hit a limit

Free containers sleep after roughly 15 idle minutes. Browser visits wake them, normally within 60 seconds; sleeping apps return HTTP 503. Cron, webhooks and CI runners receive a wake page and cannot wake containers. Monthly-hour exhaustion instead requires renewed allowance or paid capacity.

More than 100 requests/minute from one IP to a shared-domain app triggers HTTP 429 for ten minutes. A one-time $1 Sprint Pack provides 24 hours' Always-On for one Small container and unlimited account deploys; it adds no free hours and returns to Free rules afterward. Distinguish sleep, exhausted hours and rate limits when diagnosing availability.

## Gotchas

- A shared running-hour allowance is consumed faster by several concurrent apps.
- A browser visitor may see a wake page before the container responds. Builds target ARM64 and may run up to 60 minutes. Free image uploads are limited to 2 GB; confirm architecture and memory before deployment. Idle apps keep their stored image and settings, but after roughly 14 idle days the hosting slot may be released and the next wake can take under two minutes. Free deploy-on-push pauses after 15 days without signing in; reconnect GitHub to resume. A public-image deployment that never starts successfully is removed 24 hours after failure.
- Local files disappear on restart, redeploy and wake. Persistent database add-ons, custom domains and WebSockets are outside the regular Free tier. Outbound TCP ports above 27017, plus 3333 and 9000–9099, are blocked; a database assigned a random high port may therefore be unreachable.

## Questions people ask

### Do I need a payment card?

The Free plan accepts signup without one. Paid Always-On and other add-ons are separate purchases.

### Can I run a webhook receiver on Free?

A running container can receive requests, but a sleeping one depends on the documented wake behavior. Use always-on capacity when the caller must get an immediate response.

### Do failed deployments count?

Yes. The free limits page counts them in the deployment allowance.
