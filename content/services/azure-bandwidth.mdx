You do not sign up for bandwidth on its own. It is the network traffic bill for the Azure resources you run, and you manage it in the Azure portal. Azure sign-up asks for a phone number and a credit or debit card (not prepaid) to verify your identity. It may place a temporary one-dollar hold, and it does not charge you unless you move to pay-as-you-go.

## What the free plan gives you

Data coming into Azure is free. Data moving inside one availability zone is also free, and so is data from an Azure origin to Azure Front Door Standard or Premium.

For internet egress, the first 100 GB each month is free. This applies when traffic goes out over the Microsoft global network, and also when it uses the cheaper routing preference option. The first 100 GB is listed per month for every source continent, but the price after it depends on the continent.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops your traffic at the free amount. Azure keeps sending data and bills you for every extra gigabyte. The rate depends on the continent the data leaves from. For example, the next tier of premium egress from North America or Europe costs $0.087 per GB, and rates are higher from Asia and South America.

Moving data between two Azure regions is charged too, even when both ends are in Azure. Moving data between services in the same region is not charged. The pricing page lists no spending cap for egress, so use Cost Management to watch your usage.

## Gotchas

- The free amount covers outbound internet traffic only. Transfer between regions is billed from the first gigabyte.
- Traffic leaving Azure after the first tier is billed by source continent, and the rates are higher for Asia and South America.
- The Azure free account stops your services when its credit runs out or expires, unless you upgrade. After you upgrade, you pay only for usage above the free amounts.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure bandwidth free?

Partly. Inbound data is free, and the first 100 GB of internet egress each month is free. Outbound traffic above that is billed per gigabyte.

### What happens when I go over the free Azure egress?

Azure does not block traffic. It bills the extra gigabytes at the rate for the continent the data leaves from.

### Is data transfer between Azure regions free?

No. The pricing page says outbound transfer between two regions is charged at the normal rate, and inbound transfer is free. Transfer inside the same region is not charged.
