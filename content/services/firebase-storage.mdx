Cloud Storage for Firebase is object storage for app developers who need to store and serve user-generated content, such as photos or videos. Files live in Google Cloud Storage buckets, and the Firebase SDKs handle uploads and downloads from iOS, Android, web, Flutter, C++, and Unity apps, with Security Rules deciding who can access what.

## What the free plan gives you

Cloud Storage for Firebase now requires the pay-as-you-go Blaze plan, even for default buckets, but no-cost usage is still available on Blaze. For new *.firebasestorage.app buckets, the no-cost amounts are 5 GB-months stored, 100 GB downloaded per month, 5K upload operations per month, and 50K download operations per month. These quotas apply only to buckets in us-central1, us-west1, or us-east1, and follow the Always Free tier for Google Cloud Storage.

A legacy *.appspot.com default bucket keeps its earlier no-cost level: 5 GB stored, 1 GB/day downloaded, 20K uploads per day, and 50K downloads per day.

## When you hit a limit

Nothing stops your bucket at the no-cost amounts. On Blaze, usage past them is billed: new buckets at Cloud Storage pricing, and legacy *.appspot.com buckets at $0.026/GB stored and $0.12/GB downloaded. The legacy no-cost limits are enforced daily and refresh at midnight.

If your project is on the Spark plan, you have no access to any Cloud Storage buckets. The console redirects you to an upgrade page, and calls from your app return 402 or 403 errors.

## Gotchas

- Blaze needs a Cloud Billing account, and the Google Cloud Free Trial asks for a credit card or other payment method at sign-up.
- A bucket created outside us-central1, us-west1, or us-east1 has no no-cost quota.
- Downloads are counted per month for new buckets but per day for legacy buckets, so moving buckets changes how the limit works.

## Questions people ask

### Is Firebase Storage free on the Spark plan?

No. Cloud Storage for Firebase requires the Blaze plan, and Spark projects lose access to their buckets, but Blaze includes no-cost usage.

### How much storage is free on Blaze?

New buckets in us-central1, us-west1, or us-east1 get 5 GB-months stored, 100 GB downloaded, 5K uploads, and 50K downloads per month at no cost.

### Why does my app get 402 errors from Storage?

A 402 or 403 error means the project is on the Spark plan, which no longer has access to Cloud Storage. Upgrading to Blaze restores access.
