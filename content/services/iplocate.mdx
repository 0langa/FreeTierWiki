You get a free API key on the website, then send a GET request to `iplocate.io/api/lookup/` with an IP address and your key. There are also client libraries and an MCP server. It suits developers who want full IP data on a free key without a credit card.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan gives you 1,000 requests per day, free forever, with no credit card. The pricing page says there are no other limits, and that you get the same data, accuracy, and API as paid users. Free access includes geolocation, threat detection, company, and hosting data.

Paid use is optional. You can buy credits in bulk, where one request is one credit, or pay a fixed price for a monthly quota that starts from 100,000 requests a month. Custom plans with an SLA are available on request. The site also offers free downloadable databases for country and ASN data.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what the API returns after the 1,000 daily requests. It also lists no overage charge for the free plan. A free key has no payment method attached, so it should not produce a bill.

To get more room, buy credits or a monthly quota. The pricing page says all plans use the same API endpoints, so you do not need to change your code.

## Gotchas

- You need a free API key. The service is not anonymous.
- The free plan lists no SLA. The pricing page offers an SLA only in custom packages.
- The free databases cover country and ASN only. City-level data from the database products is paid.

## Questions people ask

### Is IPLocate free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 1,000 requests per day, forever, with no credit card, and the same data as paid plans.

### Does IPLocate need an API key?

Yes. You get a free API key when you sign up, and you pass it as the `apikey` parameter in your request.

### What happens when I go over the IPLocate free limit?

The pricing page does not say. It lists no overage charge for the free plan, and you can buy credits or a monthly quota for more requests.
