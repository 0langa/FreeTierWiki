You work with Comet ML from Python: install the SDK, log in, and add a few lines to your training script. Then you read and compare runs in the web dashboard. It suits one person who trains models and wants hosted tracking without running a server.

## What the free plan gives you

The free MLOps plan is for one person. It allows 1 platform user and 100GB of data storage. It covers the core experiment tracking features, and the page says every plan includes experiment tracking and the model registry. Support comes through the community, not by email.

The monthly limits fall under a "fair usage policy". The page does not list a hard number for runs or experiments on the free plan. The home page says you do not need a credit card to sign up. Verified academics (researchers, students, and educators) can get the full Pro plan for free.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 100GB on the free plan. It does show how Pro handles extra data: Pro includes 500GB, and more storage costs $3 per 100GB per month. Pro also bills extra training time at $1 per training hour, after 1500 hours. A training hour is 60 minutes of active training time between starting and stopping an experiment. Free-plan overage rules are not written down, so check the page before you plan a heavy workload.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has 1 platform user, so a team cannot share one free workspace.
- The free plan runs on a fair usage policy with no published run limit, so heavy use could be restricted.
- The pricing page now leads with Opik, the LLM tracing product. The MLOps free plan is a separate set of limits further down the page.

## Questions people ask

### Is Comet ML free?

Yes. The free MLOps plan includes experiment tracking and the model registry for 1 platform user with 100GB of storage. A paid Pro plan costs $19 per user per month.

### Does Comet ML need a credit card?

No. The Comet home page says you do not need a credit card to sign up for the free tier.

### Is Comet ML free for students?

Yes. Comet gives verified academics (researchers, students, and educators) the full Pro plan at no cost. You apply and verify your academic status first.
