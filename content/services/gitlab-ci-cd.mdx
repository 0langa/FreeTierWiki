You describe your pipeline in a `.gitlab-ci.yml` file and read the results under the Build menu, then Pipelines. GitLab.com supplies instance runners, or you can register your own. It suits small teams and solo developers on GitLab.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for personal projects and open-source work. It includes 5 users per top-level group, 400 compute minutes per month, source code management with CI/CD, 10 GiB of adjustable storage, and static application security testing.

Free namespaces get 400 compute minutes each month. A job's compute usage is its duration divided by 60, times a cost factor that depends on the runner type and project settings. Usage resets on the first day of each month. Trigger jobs do not use compute minutes.

You can also bring your own runners and storage. GitLab.com offers runners on Linux, Windows, and macOS. The Premium plan costs $29 per user per month, billed annually, and includes 10,000 compute minutes per month.

## When you hit a limit

On GitLab.com the compute quota is on by default for Free namespaces. You get notices as you near it, and GitLab applies enforcement measures once you exceed it.

You can purchase additional compute minutes. The docs say purchased minutes are used only after the monthly quota runs out, and they roll over if unused. Your own self-managed runners do not use the quota.

## Gotchas

- The shared runners need a credit or debit card on file. GitLab verifies it with a one-dollar authorization and makes no charge. A bring-your-own runner needs no card.
- GitLab says the card rule exists because of abuse of free compute minutes for crypto mining.
- The quota applies to the top-level group, not to each user.

## Questions people ask

### Is GitLab CI/CD free?

Yes, within limits. The Free plan includes 400 compute minutes per month and 5 users per top-level group.

### Does GitLab CI/CD need a credit card?

For the shared runners, yes. GitLab verifies the card with a one-dollar authorization and makes no charge. You need no card if you bring your own runner.

### What happens when I run out of GitLab compute minutes?

GitLab applies enforcement measures once you exceed the quota. You can purchase more compute minutes, or use your own runners.
