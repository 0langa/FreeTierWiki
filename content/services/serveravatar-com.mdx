You install Lite on your own server with a single command, then manage that server from its panel in a browser. It suits developers who rent cheap VPS machines and want a control panel without a monthly panel fee.

## What the free plan gives you

ServerAvatar Lite is free for unlimited servers and unlimited sites, and the Lite page says no registration is required. You run an install script as root on a fresh Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 server. It is a lite version of the main ServerAvatar system.

The Lite page lists Apache, Nginx, OLS, and MERN stacks, one-click installers for WordPress, n8n, and more than 10 other apps, isolated environments for each app, and a file manager. It also lists automatic SSL, firewall management, per-site PHP settings, and automatic security updates. The server itself is not included. You rent it from your own provider.

## When you hit a limit

The pages list no usage limits for Lite, and the pricing page does not say what happens at a limit. Lite promises unlimited servers and sites, so the real limit is the size of your own server.

The paid Reseller Panel works differently. It offers a free trial of 4 days, and the pricing page says no card info is required. After the trial, you pick a paid plan. Plan prices are monthly or yearly.

## Gotchas

- Lite runs on your server, so you maintain that server and your provider bills you for it.
- Each Lite install is a panel for one server. The central dashboard for many servers is a paid feature.
- The pricing page says ServerAvatar follows a bring-your-own-server model and works with any VPS or cloud provider that supports SSH access.

## Questions people ask

### Is ServerAvatar free?

Yes, in the Lite version. ServerAvatar Lite is free for unlimited servers and sites, and the Lite page says no registration is required. The hosted Reseller Panel is paid.

### Does ServerAvatar need a credit card?

Lite needs no account. For the Reseller Panel trial, the pricing page says no card info is required.

### Do I need my own server for ServerAvatar?

Yes. The FAQ says ServerAvatar uses a bring-your-own-server model, and you connect servers from providers such as DigitalOcean, AWS, Vultr, and Hetzner.
