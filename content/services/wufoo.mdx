You build forms with a drag-and-drop editor, then share them by link or embed code. The site also describes a REST API for reading your data.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 a month and builds basic forms with a limited number of responses. It allows 5 forms, 100 entries a month in total across all forms, and 1 user. Reports are unlimited.

Forms on the free plan can have up to 10 fields, according to the pricing card. The API allows 100 requests a day. Support is by email, on weekdays.

Every Wufoo plan includes SSL hosting, reports, a template gallery of 400+ templates, a theme designer, field validation, email and text notifications, and the REST API. File storage and file uploads are limits of paid plans. Free plans have no file storage.

## When you hit a limit

On the free plan, forms are deactivated once they pass the maximum number of entries allowed for the month. The limit counts entries across all forms, and it resets each month.

Paid plans work differently. If you exceed the monthly entry limit on a paid plan, Wufoo charges $0.05 per extra entry at the end of the billing period. The free plan has no card on file, so no bill can come from it. Starter costs $195 billed annually and allows 10 forms and 1,000 entries a month.

## Gotchas

- The 100 entries are a total across all 5 forms, not 100 for each form.
- A free form deactivates when you pass the limit, so visitors cannot submit until the month resets or you upgrade.
- Free accounts have no file storage, and uploaded files have a 25MB limit on paid plans.

## Questions people ask

### Is Wufoo free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 a month and includes 5 forms and 100 entries a month. Paid plans start at $195 billed annually.

### What happens when I go over the Wufoo entry limit?

On a free account, your forms are deactivated once they exceed the monthly entry limit. On paid accounts, Wufoo charges $0.05 for each extra entry.

### Does Wufoo allow file uploads on the free plan?

No. File storage is only allowed on paid accounts, and the file upload field uses that storage.
