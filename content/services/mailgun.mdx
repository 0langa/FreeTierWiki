Mailgun is a popular email delivery service offering APIs and SMTP relay for sending, receiving, and tracking transactional emails. The free plan is always free, allowing up to 100 emails per day with one sending domain, making it suitable for testing and early-stage projects. Mailgun is best for developers and teams needing reliable email delivery.
