You send mail with the REST API, an SDK, or SMTP, and you manage domains, keys, and logs in the dashboard. It fits developers who want one service to send and also receive app email.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and includes 100 emails per day. You get the RESTful email API and SMTP relay, one custom sending domain, 2 API keys, tracking, analytics, and webhooks.

Logs are kept for 1 day, and bounce classification is also limited to 1 day. You get 1 inbound route to receive email, and support is by ticket only. Test sends go through the sandbox domain until you verify your own domain. The first plan up, Basic, starts at $15 per month with 10,000 emails and no daily limit.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan counts 100 emails per day. The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass that number, such as whether sends are rejected or delayed. It also lists no overage price for the free plan.

Paid plans have no daily email limit and list monthly overage rates per 1,000 emails. Upgrade to Basic if you regularly need more than 100 emails a day.

## Gotchas

- New accounts start with a sandbox domain. You must verify each address as an authorized recipient before the sandbox sends to it.
- The free plan includes only 1 custom sending domain. The Basic plan also has 1, and the Foundation plan has 1,000.
- Log retention is just 1 day on the free plan, so debug a failed send quickly.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mailgun free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and includes 100 emails per day with one custom sending domain. The home page invites you to start for free and send up to 100 emails a day.

### How many emails can I send with the Mailgun free plan?

You can send 100 emails per day. Paid plans remove the daily limit and bill by monthly volume.

### Can I receive email with Mailgun for free?

Yes, in a small way. The free plan includes 1 inbound route, while the Basic plan lists 5.
