You send mail with the REST API, an SDK, or SMTP, and you manage domains, keys, and logs in the dashboard. It fits developers who want one service to send and also receive app email.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs $0 and includes 100 emails per day. You get the RESTful email API and SMTP relay, one custom sending domain, tracking, analytics, and webhooks. Pricing lists one API key, while the Free-plan help article lists two.

Logs are kept for 1 day, and bounce classification is also limited to 1 day. You get 1 inbound route to receive email, and support is by ticket only. The Free-plan and domain setup articles say you can use one custom domain without a card. The authorized-recipient article says you must add a card to unlock custom domains. Confirm the activation rule for your account. The first plan up, Basic, starts at $15 per month with 10,000 emails and no daily limit.

## When you hit a limit

The free plan counts 100 emails per day. Mailgun's integration guide says sending stops until the next day or until you upgrade. Pricing lists no overage charge on Free.

Paid plans have no daily email limit. If you exceed a paid plan's included monthly volume, Mailgun lists an overage rate per 1,000 emails. Upgrade to Basic if you regularly need more than 100 emails a day.

## Gotchas

- New accounts start with a sandbox domain. You can add up to 5 authorized recipients, and each must accept the verification invitation before the sandbox sends to it.
- Pricing lists one custom sending domain on Free and Basic, and 1,000 on Foundation. The Help Center conflicts on using the Free domain without adding a card.
- Log retention is just 1 day on the free plan, so debug a failed send quickly.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mailgun free?

Yes. The free plan costs $0 and includes 100 emails per day with one custom sending domain. The home page invites you to start for free and send up to 100 emails a day.

### How many emails can I send with the Mailgun free plan?

Free sending stops at 100 emails per day, with no overage charge. Paid plans remove the daily limit and bill by monthly volume.

### Can I receive email with Mailgun for free?

Yes, in a small way. The free plan includes 1 inbound route, while the Basic plan lists 5.
