You manage clusters in the Zilliz Cloud console or through its API, and you read and write data with the Milvus SDKs, such as `pymilvus`. It suits developers who want Milvus without running it themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is $0 and is a starting point for learning and personal projects. It includes 5 GB of storage, which the docs say is enough for 1M vectors of 768 dimensions. You also get 2.5M vCUs per month. A vCU measures the resources used by reads such as search and query, and by writes such as insert, upsert, and delete.

The Free cluster allows up to 5 collections, and each organization can have only one Free cluster. Support comes from the community through Slack. For more resources, the docs point you to a free trial of Serverless and Dedicated clusters.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use up the 2.5M vCUs or the 5 GB. For more capacity, you move to a Serverless or Dedicated cluster. Serverless starts from $0 a month on the Standard plan and charges for what you use.

The trial works differently from the Free cluster. A work email signup gets $100 in credits that expire after 30 days. Adding a payment method earns another $100. If the trial ends without a payment method, the organization is frozen and trial clusters go to the recycle bin. They are deleted for good after 30 days.

## Gotchas

- Only one Free cluster is allowed per organization. A second serving cluster must be Serverless or Dedicated.
- Save the cluster password when you create the cluster. The console shows it only once.
- The trial credits do not apply to the Free cluster. Treat them as a separate, short test of paid clusters.

## Questions people ask

### Is Zilliz Cloud free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 5 GB of storage, 2.5M vCUs per month, and up to 5 collections. Each organization can have one Free cluster.

### Does Zilliz Cloud need a credit card for the free plan?

No. The docs say no payment information is required to register. You add a payment method only to earn extra trial credits or to use paid clusters.

### What happens after the Zilliz Cloud free trial?

If you have no payment method, the organization is frozen and trial clusters are recycled. They are deleted permanently after 30 days unless you add a payment method.
