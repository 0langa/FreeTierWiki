You start from the command line, which sets up a Studio, the editing app that runs in the browser. Editors write content in the Studio, and your code reads it through the content API.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 forever. It includes up to 20 user seats with 2 permission roles, 2 datasets that are public only, unlimited content types and locales, and free Sanity Studio hosting.

Each month the plan covers 250,000 API requests and 1 million API CDN requests. It also gives you 100 GB of bandwidth, 100 GB of asset storage, and 10,000 documents. Images and file assets do not count toward the document limit. They count toward the asset quota instead.

## When you hit a limit

The Free plan has hard caps, and overages are not allowed. If you reach a quota for documents, assets, API requests, CDN requests, or bandwidth, Sanity blocks the related function until the quota resets or you upgrade.

At the document limit, you cannot create more documents, including new drafts. At the asset limit, you cannot upload new assets, but existing assets keep being served. Quotas follow calendar months in UTC. Only paid projects with a credit card are billed for overage.

## Gotchas

- Datasets are public by default, and the Free plan has no private datasets. Do not store secrets in content.
- The document quota counts every document in all of your datasets, including drafts.
- The Growth plan costs $15 per seat per month and adds private datasets, more roles, and pay-as-you-go use above the quotas.

## Questions people ask

### Is Sanity free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0 forever and includes up to 20 user seats, 10,000 documents, 100 GB of bandwidth, and 100 GB of assets. It needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Sanity free limits?

Sanity blocks the related function until the quota resets or you upgrade. Free projects have hard caps, so no overage charges happen.

### Does Sanity host the Studio for free?

Yes. The pricing table lists free Sanity Studio hosting as included on the Free plan.
