Web3Forms is a form backend. You keep your own HTML form, set its action to the Web3Forms API with an access key, and each submission is emailed to you. It suits static sites, landing pages, and front-end apps that need a working contact form without a server.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page calls the free plan Personal and says it is free forever. It includes unlimited forms, 250 monthly submissions, unlimited domains, advanced spam protection, hCaptcha integration, honeypot protection, and a custom redirect page.

The comparison table adds the details. The free plan has one email recipient and two linked email addresses, 30 days of submission history, export to CSV, and up to one active integration. File uploads, autoresponders, webhooks, reCaptcha, Cloudflare Turnstile, domain restriction, and team seats are on paid plans. The FAQ says you can use the free plan as long as you need, though Web3Forms may suspend accounts it finds abusing the service.

## When you hit a limit

Web3Forms sends a warning email at 90% of your monthly limit and a final warning at 100%. If you go past the limit, it stops accepting new submissions until the next month. You can upgrade to keep receiving submissions.

That makes the free plan a hard stop, not a bill. The risk is lost leads: a form that stops mid-month does not tell your visitors why.

## Gotchas

- Submission history on the free plan is 30 days, so export anything you need to keep.
- A submission that does not arrive late in the month may be the limit, not a bug; check your warning emails.
- File uploads and autoresponders are not on the free plan, so a form with an attachment field needs a paid plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is the Web3Forms free plan really free?

Yes. The FAQ says you can use the free plan as long as you need after creating a free account, though it may suspend accounts that abuse the service.

### What happens if I exceed 250 submissions in a month?

Web3Forms stops accepting new submissions until the next month, after warning you at 90% and 100% of the limit.

### Can I receive file uploads on the free plan?

No. File upload and attachments are listed on the paid plans only.
