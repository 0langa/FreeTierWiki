You manage Supersonic CDN from a dashboard you reach through your Namecheap account. It suits owners of small sites on Namecheap who want caching and basic protection without a separate CDN account.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan costs nothing and is listed as free forever. It includes basic DDoS protection, custom SSL upload, and a traffic limit of 50 GB per month. The paid Basic plan raises the limit to 250 GB, Medium to 500 GB, and Advanced to 1000 GB.

The pricing page calls these plans an early-access beta for EasyWP and Shared Hosting customers. It lists EasyWP subscriptions with Namecheap domains, third-party domains that use FreeDNS, and domains on Namecheap Shared Hosting nameservers. EasyWP customers on free domains are not eligible.

A newer setup article says the CDN also works with third-party hosting when the domain uses Basic DNS, FreeDNS, or PremiumDNS. The dashboard has Overview, Analytics, CDN Settings, and SSL tabs, and a quick action to clear the cache.

## When you hit a limit

The pages do not say what happens when you pass the monthly traffic limit on the free plan. They do not mention a charge, and the pricing page says there are no hidden roaming charges for traffic spikes.

You can move up to a paid plan from the Quick Actions menu in your Supersonic CDN account, and you can pay by credit card, PayPal, or account funds. Namecheap says you cannot downgrade after you choose a plan. Some features, such as the Status Codes analytics, are for paid plans only.

## Gotchas

- Subdomains and free EasyWP domains do not work with Supersonic CDN.
- Setup asks you to confirm that the CDN app may change your domain's DNS records, and the service can take up to 30 minutes to be fully ready.
- The old pricing page text says third-party domain owners would get access later, so check the current terms in your Namecheap account before you plan around it.

## Questions people ask

### Is Namecheap Supersonic CDN free?

Yes, there is a free plan with 50 GB of traffic per month, basic DDoS protection, and custom SSL upload. It is offered for eligible Namecheap domains.

### Who can use the free Supersonic CDN plan?

The pricing page names EasyWP subscriptions with Namecheap domains, FreeDNS domains, and Namecheap Shared Hosting domains. A later help article also allows third-party hosting that uses Namecheap DNS.

### What happens when I go over the Supersonic CDN traffic limit?

The pages do not say. You can upgrade to Basic, Medium, or Advanced from your Supersonic CDN account.
