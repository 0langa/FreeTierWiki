You manage flags and config values in the Abby dashboard and read them in your code through an SDK. Abby also offers devtools for debugging flags and a first-party command line tool that the site marks as beta.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is the one Abby calls Starter. It costs nothing and covers 1,000 events per month, 1 A/B test, and 3 feature flags or remote config variables. You also get 5 environments, so you can keep separate values for development, staging, and production.

The pricing page prices plans per project. It says to start with feature flags and remote config variables for free and to scale up later. Abby is open source, and the site says you can self-host it, so the free plan is not the only way to run it.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass 1,000 events in a month or use more than 3 flags. It does not describe a bill or a pause either. Plan for the free plan to stop at its caps and treat any extra use as something to ask Abby about.

The next paid plan is Startup at $12 per project per month. It raises the caps to 10,000 events, 10 A/B tests, and 30 flags or remote configs.

## Gotchas

- The free plan allows only 1 A/B test and 3 flags or remote configs, so a real product runs out of room fast.
- Plans are priced per project, not per seat.
- The pricing page does not say whether the free plan needs a credit card or what happens at the cap.

## Questions people ask

### Is Abby free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes 1,000 events per month, 1 A/B test, 3 feature flags or remote configs, and 5 environments. Paid plans start at $12 per project per month.

### Is Abby open source?

Yes. The site says Abby is open source and fully typed, with SDKs for React and Next.js, and it mentions an option to self-host.

### What happens when I go over the Abby free limits?

The pricing page does not say. The next plan up, Startup, raises the caps to 10,000 events and 30 flags or remote configs for $12 per project per month.
