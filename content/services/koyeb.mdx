You connect a GitHub repository or pick a container image, and Koyeb builds it with a Dockerfile or buildpacks on every push. This page covers the free Instance that every organization gets.

## What the free plan gives you

Each organization gets one free web Service. It runs on a free Instance with 512MB of RAM, 0.1 vCPU, and 2GB of SSD, in either Frankfurt or Washington, D.C. You also get one free Postgres database with 5 hours of active time and 1GB of storage. The free database runs in Washington, D.C., Frankfurt, or Singapore and scales to zero when idle.

The pricing FAQ still gives 100GB of outbound bandwidth per month for free and ten free custom domains with automatic SSL. Its main paid-plan table now lists 1TB, so the applicable allowance needs confirmation. Koyeb says the free Instance is for testing the platform and hobby projects, not for production apps.

## When you hit a limit

Koyeb says you will never be charged for the free web Service. When it gets no traffic for 1 hour, it scales down to zero. The next request wakes it up, with a cold start that typically takes 1 to 5 seconds.

The FAQ says excess bandwidth is not charged yet, with $0.04/GB planned after notice. The paid table instead shows 1TB included, then $0.02/GB EU/US or $0.04/GB Asia. Confirm which applies. The free database’s behavior after 5 active hours is unpublished. Any Instance beyond the free one is billed per second, and there is no spending limit yet.

## Gotchas

- A credit card is required. Koyeb checks it with a $29 hold and cancels the hold; your bank can take 7 to 21 days to release it.
- The FAQ says the plan selected at sign-up is Pro, and the pro-rated Pro amount is charged after the card check. Starter is the pay-as-you-go plan that charges only for usage beyond the free Instance; you switch to it in Usage and billing.
- Each organization can have only one free Instance. A second service, a worker, or a volume is paid usage.

## Questions people ask

### Is Koyeb free?

Yes, within limits. Each organization gets one free web Service with 512MB of RAM and 0.1 vCPU, and one free Postgres database. You still need a credit card to sign up.

### Does the Koyeb free Instance sleep?

Yes. It scales down to zero after 1 hour without traffic. The next request wakes it up, usually within 1 to 5 seconds.

### Why does Koyeb want my credit card for the free plan?

Koyeb says the card prevents fraud and abuse. It places a $29 hold to verify the card and cancels the hold right away.
