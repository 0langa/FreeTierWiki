You add Clerk with an SDK for your framework, which gives you prebuilt sign-in components and middleware, and you manage users in the Clerk Dashboard. The docs cover many stacks, from Next.js to Go.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free and needs no credit card. It includes 50,000 monthly retained users (MRU) per app. A user counts as retained when they come back 24 hours or more after they sign up, so people who only sign up and leave do not count.

You also get 100 monthly retained organizations (MRO) per app, and up to 3 dashboard seats. An organization is retained when it has at least 2 members and at least one of them is a retained user that month. You can try the Pro features in the development instance of your app. The Hobby plan does not include SSO connections; the Pro plan includes 1.

## When you hit a limit

Clerk requires an upgrade once you pass the limits. Above 50,000 retained users, you must move to the Pro plan. Above 100 retained organizations, you must move to the B2B Authentication add-on.

You get a one-month grace period, so your app keeps working while you decide. After you upgrade, each user or organization beyond the free allowance is added to your monthly bill, and the paid plans publish their overage rates.

## Gotchas

- Only retained users count toward the limit. A user who signs up and never returns within 24 hours does not use your allowance.
- The Hobby plan has no SSO connection. Enterprise SSO belongs to paid plans.
- You can scaffold Clerk with the `npx clerk@latest init` command, which the quickstart says works whether or not you are signed in, and writes the publishable and secret keys to `.env.local`.

## Questions people ask

### Is Clerk free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free, needs no credit card, and covers the first 50,000 monthly retained users and 100 monthly retained organizations per app.

### What happens when I exceed the Clerk free limit?

Clerk requires you to upgrade to a paid plan. You get a one-month grace period so your app keeps working in the meantime.

### Does Clerk need a credit card?

No. The pricing page states that no credit card is required for the Hobby plan.
