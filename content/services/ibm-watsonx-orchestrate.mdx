IBM watsonx Orchestrate is an agent management platform. You build AI agents, connect them to tools and workflows, and govern how they run from one control plane, including agents built elsewhere. The old watsonx Assistant product page now opens the watsonx Orchestrate page, so this is where IBM's assistant and chatbot work continues.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page lists a Trial plan: try and explore watsonx Orchestrate for a 30-day free period using trial access. The trial's listed features are agent building and orchestration, an intuitive chat interface, and evaluation and observability.

There is no free plan after the trial. Essentials starts at $530.00 USD a month and adds workflow and document processing, an agents and tools catalog, and custom tool creation. Standard starts at $6,360.00 USD a month with higher usage limits and ready-to-use domain agents, and Premium adds data isolation.
## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the 30-day trial ends, or what usage limits apply during it. It also does not say whether you need a card to start. Since there is no free plan to fall back to, plan to buy a paid plan or wind down your agents when the trial period is over.

Paid plans have usage limits too: Standard is described as Essentials with higher usage limits, higher document volumes, and more concurrency, so a busy agent may need the larger plan.

## Gotchas

- The jump from a free trial to $530 a month makes this a platform for organisations, not individuals.
- Thirty days is short for an agent project that needs integrations approved by IT; line up access before you start the trial.
- Plan names and limits change as IBM folds older products into Orchestrate, so check the pricing page when you decide.
