Mouseflow is a behavior analytics tool for websites. It records sessions and turns them into replays, heatmaps, conversion funnels, form analytics, and friction detection. It suits site owners and developers who want to see how visitors move through a few key pages.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 a month and is meant for spotting friction and drop-offs on limited traffic. It gives you 500 sessions per month, 1 website project, 1 conversion funnel, and 1 month of data retention, with unlimited user seats.

New accounts get a stronger start. The trial includes 14 days of full-feature access with no credit card required: 2,000 sessions, 5 website projects, 10 funnels, and 1 month of data retention. Mouseflow says all of this is also included in the first 14 days of the free plan. After that, you can upgrade or stay on the Free Forever plan with 500 sessions a month and limited features.

## When you hit a limit

Your account gets a refill of sessions each month, depending on your plan. When you run out, all tracking stops until your next refill date, unless you manually request a refill or upgrade in the dashboard.

That makes the free plan safe from surprise bills, but it also means visits after the limit are not recorded at all. The Essential plan costs 25 a month and gives 5,000 monthly sessions on 1 website project.

## Gotchas

- 500 sessions a month runs out fast on any site with regular traffic, and nothing is recorded after that.
- The trial's larger limits last only the first 14 days, so early numbers can overstate what the free plan holds.
- Data retention on the free plan is 1 month, so replays older than that are gone.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mouseflow free?

Yes. The Free Forever plan gives 500 sessions per month on 1 website project.

### What happens when I run out of sessions?

All tracking stops until your next monthly refill, unless you request a refill or upgrade.

### Do I need a credit card to try Mouseflow?

No. The 14-day trial is full-feature access with no credit card required.
