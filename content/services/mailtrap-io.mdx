You point your app's SMTP settings at a Mailtrap sandbox, or use the Sandbox API, and every email lands in the sandbox instead of a real inbox. It suits a single developer checking staging emails.

## What the free plan gives you

The free Email Sandbox plan includes 50 test emails per month for 1 user. You get 1 sandbox, and it holds 10 emails at a time. When it is full, Mailtrap cleans out the oldest messages first. You can send 1 email per 10 seconds into a sandbox, and the Sandbox API has unlimited calls.

The same free account also has an Email API/SMTP plan for real sending. It is sold separately and allows 4,000 emails per month and 150 emails per day. No credit card is needed for the free plans.

## When you hit a limit

If you send faster than the rate limit, Mailtrap rejects the message with a "too many emails per second" error. For the 50 monthly test emails, the pricing page does not say what happens at the cap. For the separate Email API/SMTP plan, the docs say you get a "Monthly messages limit reached" SMTP error, and you must upgrade or wait for the next period. The free plans ask for no card, so no bill appears.

## Gotchas

- Email Sandbox, Email API/SMTP, and Email Marketing are sold separately, each with its own free plan.
- The free sandbox has no dedicated email address and no forwarding. Those start on paid plans.
- The sandbox holds 10 emails, so a busy test run pushes out older messages fast.

## Questions people ask

### Is Mailtrap free?

Yes. The free Email Sandbox plan gives 50 test emails per month for 1 user. A separate free Email API/SMTP plan allows 4,000 emails per month.

### Does Mailtrap need a credit card?

No. The docs say the free plan needs no credit card.

### What happens when I go over the Mailtrap free limit?

For sandbox testing the pricing page does not say. On the Email API/SMTP plan, sending stops with a monthly limit error until you upgrade or the next period starts.
