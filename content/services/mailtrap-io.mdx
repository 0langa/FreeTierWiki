Mailtrap.io is a developer-focused tool for safely testing and debugging email sending without risk of spamming real users. Its free tier is ideal for individual developers needing to validate email flows in non-production environments, offering one sandbox with 50 test emails per month. Mailtrap is easy to integrate with most frameworks and languages, making it a popular choice for email testing during development.
