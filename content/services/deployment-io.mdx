You work in a web dashboard at app.deployment.io, or you drive it from a coding agent through its MCP server. It runs jobs on a runner in your AWS account. It suits small teams that want AI-written pull requests and deploys in their own cloud.

## What the free plan gives you

Deployment.io offers a free tier on Platform, the self-serve option for teams that operate the delivery system themselves. The platform page says the free tier is enough to see the whole loop: shared context, a session, a Task, and a deploy that waits for your approval.

The pricing page does not list the free limits. It says current paid plans and limits are in the app, so check them after you sign up. Managed delivery is a separate service. It is priced per engagement with a fixed-price proposal, and it has no free option.

The pricing page does not mention a credit card for the free sign-up. Static sites and web services deploy to your own AWS account.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach a free tier limit. Those limits show in the app.

The costs outside the free tier matter more. The runner lives in your AWS account, so AWS bills the compute it uses. The agents also call a model on your own vendor account, and the vendor bills that usage directly. The docs say Deployment.io does not resell tokens. Tasks stay blocked until you add a model provider.

## Gotchas

- You must install a runner in your AWS account with a CloudFormation stack before deploys and Tasks work. The docs say it takes about 5 minutes.
- Tasks run one at a time, in the order you create them. If a Task fails the build and tests, nothing is committed and no pull request appears.
- The pricing and home pages describe AI-driven managed delivery, so the product is not a basic deploy button.

## Questions people ask

### Is Deployment.io free?

Partly. The self-serve Platform has a free tier, and the limits are shown in the app. Managed delivery has no free option.

### Does Deployment.io need a credit card?

The pricing page does not mention a credit card for the free sign-up. You still pay AWS and your model vendor for what you use.

### Does Deployment.io deploy to my own cloud?

Yes. The runner installs in your AWS account, and the docs say the infrastructure runs in your own cloud account through a scoped role you can revoke.
