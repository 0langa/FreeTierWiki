PagerDuty is an incident response service: monitoring tools send it alerts, and it notifies whoever is on call and escalates when nobody acknowledges. The pricing page splits it into Incident Management plans for teams and larger PD Reliability Platform plans. It suits developers who want a well-known pager for one small team.

## What the free plan gives you

The Incident Management Free plan is described as "a single team trying on-call and incident response" and costs $0 per year. It includes up to 5 users, 1 on-call schedule, and 1 escalation policy, with up to 5 users per escalation level. Notifications include a basic chat experience and limited email, push, SMS, and phone notifications; the comparison table says Free plan accounts receive 100 free global SMS or phone notifications per month.

Monitoring and developer integrations such as Datadog, CloudWatch, Splunk, and SumoLogic are included, as are audio and video conference integrations and standard ticketing integrations like Jira Software and Zendesk. The service directory is included too.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free account uses its 100 SMS or phone notifications, whether more are blocked or billed, or explicitly establish ongoing Free card requirements. The user, schedule, and escalation policy limits are plan limits, so adding a second rotation or a sixth user means moving to Professional, listed at $21 per user per month billed annually or $25 monthly.

## Gotchas

- Signal Intelligence Events are a PD Reliability Platform allowance, and features grouped under them, such as alert suppression, are not included on Free. Monitoring integrations remain included, so do not read this as a blanket API prohibition.
- External Status Pages, AIOps features, and Runbook Automation are not included on Free. The pricing page lists them as add-ons purchased separately for Professional.
- Advanced ITSM integrations such as ServiceNow and Salesforce are not included on Free.

## Questions people ask

### Is PagerDuty free?

Yes, for a small team. The Free plan costs $0 per year for up to 5 users with 1 on-call schedule and 1 escalation policy.

### How many SMS and phone alerts does the Free plan include?

The comparison table lists 100 free global SMS or phone notifications per month for Free plan accounts.

### Does the Free plan include status pages?

No. The comparison table marks External Status Pages as not included on Free, and Status Pages are a separately purchased add-on for Professional.
