You connect from the DuckDB command line, the web interface, or a client library in Python, Java, or Node.js. It suits solo analysts and DuckDB fans who want a small hosted warehouse.

## What the free plan gives you

Lite's $0 platform fee includes 10 GB storage, 10 monthly compute-unit hours on Pulse, 3 internal active users and 2 service accounts. Free Account terms allow only internal business use, excluding third-party products.

Pulse suits bursty work, billing per query with a one-compute-unit-second minimum. Lite cannot use larger Standard/Jumbo instances; Business costs $250/organization/month plus usage.

Flights run Python jobs. Card-free Lite includes 30 daily compute minutes. A card unlocks scheduling and unlimited concurrency, with a one-hour maximum per Lite run. Paid Lite Flights share Ducklings' monthly compute allocation and bill excess. Treat these daily Flight and monthly query allowances separately.

## When you hit a limit

Terms cap Free storage/query volumes and require commercial upgrade for excess. Billing instead describes card-free Lite's organization-wide 20-minute daily CPU budget, continuously refilling over 24 hours: exhausted queries continue at quarter-core speed and may time out. The precise monthly cutoff remains unclear.

Adding a card removes daily throttling; billed Lite/Business have no spending cap. Regional excess costs $0.04–$0.044/GB/month and $0.60–$0.80/Pulse compute-unit-hour.

After the seven-day no-card trial, connections/Flights stop until an admin chooses Lite or Business; databases remain but no read-only/export mode is available. Billing says Lite downgrades preserve databases, while general terms warn of possible loss.

## Gotchas

- The same Lite name covers free and billed accounts. The Free Account terms restrict use to internal business purposes and prohibit powering third-party products or services.
- One day of backup history is a paid Lite feature. Business supports configurable snapshot retention; do not assume the free grant includes backups.
- AI features have their own cost. SQL Assistant and advanced AI functions are billed per call or per AI Unit, while FixIt is free.

## Questions people ask

### Is MotherDuck free?

Yes. Free Lite includes 10 GB of storage and 10 compute unit hours per month for internal use. The terms require a Commercial Account beyond the included volumes, while the billing guide describes throttling on card-free Lite without invoices; do not assume an exact monthly hard cutoff.

### Does MotherDuck need a credit card?

No for the Lite plan or the 7-day Free Trial. A credit card is needed to unlock Flight scheduling and unlimited concurrent runs on Lite.

### How do I connect to MotherDuck from DuckDB?

Install the DuckDB CLI, run duckdb, then run ATTACH 'md:'. DuckDB opens your browser to sign in, or you can use an access token.
