You connect from the DuckDB command line, the web interface, or a client library in Python, Java, or Node.js. It suits solo analysts and DuckDB fans who want a small hosted warehouse.

## What the free plan gives you

Lite has a $0 platform fee for individual users and small projects. It includes 10 GB of storage, 10 compute unit hours per month on Pulse instances, up to 3 internal active users, and 2 service accounts. The Fees Addendum restricts Free Accounts to internal business use; you cannot embed the free service in a product offered to third parties.

Pulse instances handle light, bursty work and are billed per query, with a minimum of one compute unit second. Lite is limited to Pulse. Bigger instance types such as Standard and Jumbo are on the Business plan, which costs $250 per org per month plus usage.

Flights run Python jobs on a schedule. Without a credit card, Lite includes 30 minutes of Flight compute per day at no charge. A credit card unlocks scheduling and unlimited concurrent runs.

## When you hit a limit

Free and paid Lite accounts have different billing terms. The Fees Addendum caps Free Accounts at included storage and query volumes; exceeding them requires upgrading to a Commercial Account. Commercial Lite usage beyond the grants is billed. Storage past 10 GB is billed at $0.04 to $0.044 per GB per month, and extra Pulse compute is $0.60 to $0.77 per compute unit hour. Both rates depend on your cloud region. The terms do not specify the exact error or read-access behavior at the free cap.

New users get a 7-day Free Trial without a credit card. At the end, you can stay on Lite with no card or move to Business.

## Gotchas

- The same Lite name covers free and billed accounts. The Free Account terms restrict use to internal business purposes and prohibit powering third-party products or services.
- One day of backup history is a paid Lite feature. Business supports configurable snapshot retention; do not assume the free grant includes backups.
- AI features have their own cost. SQL Assistant and advanced AI functions are billed per call or per AI Unit, while FixIt is free.

## Questions people ask

### Is MotherDuck free?

Yes. Free Lite includes 10 GB of storage and 10 compute unit hours per month for internal use. Going beyond the free volumes requires a Commercial Account, which bills extra usage.

### Does MotherDuck need a credit card?

No for the Lite plan or the 7-day Free Trial. A credit card is needed to unlock Flight scheduling and unlimited concurrent runs on Lite.

### How do I connect to MotherDuck from DuckDB?

Install the DuckDB CLI, run duckdb, then run ATTACH 'md:'. DuckDB opens your browser to sign in, or you can use an access token.
