Webhook Relay gives you public input endpoints that receive webhooks and forward them to one or more destinations. A destination can be a public URL or, with its agent running, a server on an internal network. It also offers bidirectional tunnels on paid plans. Individual developers and home automation users are its free plan's audience.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free Starter plan is $0.00 a month and is aimed at individual developers and home automation enthusiasts. It includes 150 webhooks per month, secure webhooks, and MCP access for managing buckets, destinations, functions, and replays from coding agents. The quota table lists 1 input and 0 bidirectional tunnels.

Each webhook delivery counts towards the monthly quota. The FAQ says an agent is needed only for internal destinations; proxying webhooks or fanning them out to public destinations works without one. The pricing page says you start on the free plan with no credit card required.

## When you hit a limit

The FAQ says that when you reach the webhooks limit on the free plan, your buckets are temporarily suspended from receiving webhooks, and you can upgrade to a paid plan to continue. Paid plans are treated differently: they keep working over the quota under a fair-use policy.

Basic is $8.99 a month with 5,000 webhooks, custom subdomains, 10 destinations, and 8 bidirectional tunnels, and extra webhooks at $5 per 1k. The FAQ also mentions a discounted Basic plan for hobbyists doing non-commercial work.

## Gotchas

- 150 webhooks a month is about five a day, so a chatty integration can use it up in hours.
- When the quota runs out, webhooks stop arriving until you upgrade, and the pricing page does not say whether missed webhooks can be recovered.
- Tunnels are not part of the free plan; the quota table lists 0 bidirectional tunnels.

## Questions people ask

### Is Webhook Relay free?

Yes. The Free Starter plan costs $0.00 a month and includes 150 webhooks a month with no credit card.

### What happens when I reach the free webhook limit?

Your buckets are temporarily suspended from receiving webhooks. You can upgrade to a paid plan to continue.

### Do I need to run an agent?

Only for internal destinations. Forwarding to public destinations works without an agent.
