Encore is a backend framework plus a cloud platform. You declare services, databases, Pub/Sub topics, cron jobs, caches, and buckets in your code, and Encore provisions and deploys them, either on its own Encore Cloud or in your AWS or GCP account. Its Free plan hosts development environments on Encore Cloud, which suits hobby backends and early prototypes.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan needs only an email. It includes 2 free dev environments on Encore Cloud under fair use, a service catalog and architecture diagrams, distributed tracing with 1M events a month, 7 days of log and trace retention, and secrets management.

The usage limits page says the Encore platform is free for development and limited-scale commercial projects without SLA requirements. Per application, it expects you to stay within 100,000 requests a day, 1 GB of database storage, 100,000 Pub/Sub messages a day, cron jobs at most once every hour, and 1 GB of object storage. The pricing FAQ calls the plan free forever for local development and small projects.

## When you hit a limit

Encore does not stop your app automatically. The usage limits page says that if your application reaches a usage limit, the team will reach out and work with you on a solution that does not cause undue disruption.

Encore also says it will never charge you for usage of the Encore platform unless expressly agreed in advance. For more, the Pro plan costs $49 per member a month plus usage and can be tried free for 14 days, no credit card required.

## Gotchas

- The platform comes without warranty and is not intended for large-scale business-critical use cases, so treat it as hobby or development hosting.
- Custom domains and preview environments per pull request are listed under Pro, not Free.
- Logs and traces are kept for 7 days on the Free plan, so export anything you need for longer.

## Questions people ask

### Is Encore Cloud free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 2 dev environments on Encore Cloud under fair use, and you sign up with an email.

### What happens if my app goes over the Encore Cloud limits?

Encore says it will not stop the app automatically; its team will reach out to find a solution.

### Can I run production apps on the free plan?

Encore allows limited-scale commercial projects that do not need an SLA, but says the platform is not intended for business-critical use.
