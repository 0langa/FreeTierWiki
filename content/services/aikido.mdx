You sign up with your Git account, pick repositories, and read the results in the Aikido web dashboard. It also has IDE plugins, a pre-commit secrets hook, and an API. It suits solo developers and small teams.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan costs $0 and is free forever, with 2 users included. The pricing page says no credit card is required. It covers 10 repos under fair-usage limits, 2 container images, 1 domain, and 1 cloud account.

The free features include dependency scanning, SAST and AI SAST, secrets detection, cloud misconfiguration checks, container scanning, IaC scanning, DAST, and IDE plugins. You also get 10 AI AutoFixes per month and 250k protected requests per month. Free repos are rescanned every 3 days.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you pass the repo, image, or request limits. It only calls the repo limit "fair-usage". The page lists no overage price for the Developer plan. To add more repos or users you move to a paid plan, which starts with Basic.

Features such as PR security review, Jira and Linear sync, and reports need a paid plan. Credits for AI pentests and Deep PR Reviews are listed from the Pro plan up.

## Gotchas

- The free plan includes only 2 users. A larger team has to pay.
- Free repos are rescanned every 3 days, not on every push.
- The pricing page FAQ says Aikido connects with read-only access and never changes your code. Fixes arrive as pull requests that you review and merge.

## Questions people ask

### Is Aikido free?

Yes. The Developer plan is free forever and includes 2 users, 10 repos, 2 container images, and 1 cloud account. No credit card is required.

### What happens when I go over the Aikido free limits?

The pricing page does not say. It describes the repo limit as fair-usage. For more repos or users you need a paid plan.

### Can I try Aikido without connecting my repository?

Yes. The pricing page FAQ says you can sign up with your Git account, give access to no repo, and pick the demo repo instead.
