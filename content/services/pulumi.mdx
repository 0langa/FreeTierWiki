You work from the Pulumi CLI, write a program in your own language, and run it against your cloud. Pulumi Cloud keeps the state, secrets, and update history for you. It suits solo developers and small projects.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and does not ask for a credit card. It includes unlimited projects, stacks, and environments, and unlimited updates with history. Up to 5 stack updates can run at the same time.

For secrets and configuration, you get 25 managed secrets, and plaintext config is free. You also get 10K API calls per month. Workflow minutes are capped at 500 per month. The Free plan has one admin. The pricing table also lists up to 10 custom policy packs.

Paid plans add more. Essentials adds unlimited users and secure collaboration with CI/CD. It starts at $40 per month and includes 40 credits, where 1 credit equals $1.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you go past a free limit, such as the 10K API calls or 500 workflow minutes. It shows a cap of 10K API calls per month for the Free plan, and it shows unlimited API calls only on paid plans. Treat these as hard caps.

Paid plans bill extra usage on demand from credits. Examples from the page: managed secrets cost $0.50 per month on Essentials, and extra workflow minutes cost $0.01 each. Because Free needs no card, nothing can be billed to you there. To stay safe, watch your usage in the Pulumi Cloud console.

## Gotchas

- The Pulumi CLI is free and open source. Only the hosted Pulumi Cloud backend has the plan limits above.
- You do not need a Pulumi Cloud account to install the CLI. You can also pick a self-managed state backend such as S3 or a local folder.
- Your cloud provider bills for the resources you create. Pulumi does not cover that cost.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pulumi free?

Yes. The CLI is free and open source, and the Free plan of Pulumi Cloud costs $0 with no credit card. It has unlimited projects and stacks and 500 workflow minutes per month.

### Does Pulumi need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the Free plan. You only add a card if you move to a paid plan.

### Can I use Pulumi without Pulumi Cloud?

Yes. The install docs say you can pick a self-managed state backend, such as S3, Azure Blob, GCS, or local files. You only sign in to Pulumi Cloud if you want the hosted backend.
