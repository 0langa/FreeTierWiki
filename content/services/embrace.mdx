You register your app in the Embrace dashboard, add an Embrace SDK to your app, and initialize it. Embrace then records user sessions with crashes, network requests, and performance data. It suits mobile and web teams who want to see what users actually experienced.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is made for small apps and for development or staging environments. It costs $0 and is billed by session use: it is free up to 1 million sessions per year. A session is captured when an app is used in a particular state, such as when it is opened in the foreground.

You get 5 free users, 3 days of events data retention, and up to 100GB of logs ingested per year. Error logs are free, while info, warning, and system logs are charged at paid rates on paid plans. Embrace supports Android, iOS, Web, React Native, Unity, and Flutter.

## When you hit a limit

Embrace is clear about this one. The FAQ says it ingests up to 1 million sessions per year and offers 5 free users. You cannot add more users or ingest more data after you reach either cap, unless you upgrade to a paid plan.

That makes the Free plan a hard cap, not a bill. The Pro plan costs $0.80 per 1,000 sessions with a minimum spend of $80 per month, and it allows up to 10 users.

## Gotchas

- Free retention for events data is only 3 days, while Enterprise lists 30 days.
- The free plan is meant for small apps and for staging, not for production apps with heavy traffic.
- The home page notes that Palo Alto Networks completed its acquisition of Embrace, so plan terms could change.

## Questions people ask

### Is Embrace free?

Yes. The Free plan is free up to 1 million sessions per year, with 5 users and 3 days of events data retention.

### What happens when I reach the Embrace free session cap?

You cannot ingest more data or add more users until you upgrade to a paid plan. The FAQ says this for both the session and user caps.

### Does Embrace need a credit card?

The pricing page does not mention a card for the Free plan. The Pro plan has a minimum spend of $80 per month.
