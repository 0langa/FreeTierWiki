You register your app in the Embrace dashboard, add an Embrace SDK to your app, and initialize it. Embrace then records user sessions with crashes, network requests, and performance data. It suits mobile and web teams who want to see what users actually experienced.

## What the free plan gives you

On November 23, 2026, pricing redirected to Embrace's homepage and Cortex XCOR. Current pages offer demos without confirming former Free allowances. The following terms are historical, not a current offer.

Earlier Free covered small/staging apps at $0 for one million annual sessions, five users, three-day event retention and 100GB annual log ingestion. Sessions captured app use in states such as foreground activity. Error logs were free; paid plans charged for info, warning and system logs. Supported SDKs included Android, iOS, Web, React Native, Unity and Flutter. A separate thirty-day Pro trial formerly allowed choosing paid Pro or Free afterward.

## When you hit a limit

The former FAQ capped ingestion at one million annual sessions and five users, requiring an upgrade for additional ingestion or users. Those hard-stop rules are not confirmed by current replacement pages.

Earlier Pro pricing was $0.80 per 1,000 sessions with an $80 monthly minimum and ten users. These prices are historical too. Confirm present eligibility, retention and billing directly before installing an SDK or relying on the former Free allowance; the current pricing redirect does not establish that the plan continues.

## Gotchas

- Earlier Free event retention was 3 days versus Enterprise's 30; neither figure is reconfirmed by the replacement pages.
- The former Free offer targeted small apps and staging; do not assume it remains available for a new app.
- The home page notes that Palo Alto Networks completed its acquisition of Embrace, and the former public plan table is no longer available.

## Questions people ask

### Is Embrace free?

Current Free availability is unconfirmed. The previously published offer allowed 1 million sessions per year, 5 users and 3 days of events retention.

### What happens when I reach the Embrace free session cap?

The former FAQ said ingestion and new users stopped at the caps unless upgraded. The current replacement pages do not confirm that policy.

### Does Embrace need a credit card?

Current Free card requirements and paid prices are unconfirmed after the pricing redirect.
