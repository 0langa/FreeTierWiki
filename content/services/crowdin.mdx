You work in the Crowdin web app, through repository integrations, or through the API. It suits teams that run a translation process, and open source maintainers who want volunteer translators.

## What the free plan gives you

Crowdin has no ordinary free plan that the pricing page could confirm. Two free routes exist. Open source projects can sign up for a free account, set up a project, and send a request for an Open Source License. A granted license includes an unlimited number of projects, strings, and members. Academic projects can ask for an Academic License on the same terms.

Every other project starts with a trial. Once you create your first project, a 14-day trial of the Team plan starts. The Enterprise trial lasts 30 days. The home page also mentions a free subscription plan with essential features, but the pricing tables did not load, so this entry cannot list its limits.

## When you hit a limit

Plans count hosted words. That is the words to translate, multiplied by the number of target languages. When you go over your hosted words quota, Crowdin sends an email that asks you to upgrade. You then have 10 more days.

If you do not upgrade, Crowdin suspends your project for translators. You can still open your account and project settings, and your data stays as it is until you update the subscription. You can see your use under Account Settings, then Billing, in the Plan Limits and Usage section.

## Gotchas

- Open source and academic licenses need an application and an approval. They are not automatic.
- The 14-day trial is of the Team plan. After it, a private or commercial project needs a paid subscription.
- A hosted word multiplies by every target language, so adding languages uses your quota faster.

## Questions people ask

### Is Crowdin free?

Crowdin is free for approved open source and academic projects. Other projects get a 14-day trial of the Team plan, then need a paid subscription.

### What happens when I go over my Crowdin plan quota?

Crowdin emails you and gives you 10 more days to upgrade. After that, it suspends the project for translators. Your account and data stay available.

### Does Crowdin need a credit card for the trial?

The pricing page does not say whether the trial needs a card. The sign-up flow starts with a free account.
