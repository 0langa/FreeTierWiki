You manage localization in the Crowdin web app, through repository integrations, or through the API and CLI. Source text, glossaries and screenshots provide context. It suits individual developers, teams with a translation workflow and open-source maintainers working with volunteers.

## What the free plan gives you

The regular Free plan includes up to 60,000 hosted words, unlimited public projects, one private project and one integration. Hosted words count source words multiplied by target languages across every project in the account. Unlimited collaborators and invitations to your own translators apply to public projects; the comparison does not include private-project translators or manager seats on Free.

Free includes translation memory, glossary, screenshots, quality checks and basic reports. Its translations are donated to Crowdin's translation memory. Donation applies despite the private-project allowance. Paid Pro makes donation optional and adds private-project collaboration.

Approved non-profit open-source and academic projects have a separate licensing route. The pricing FAQ says granted licenses include unlimited projects, strings and members. Approval requires applying. Creating a first project also starts a 14-day Team trial; the Enterprise Business trial is 30 days.

## When you hit a limit

Crowdin emails you when hosted words exceed your plan quota and gives you 10 more days to upgrade. Without an upgrade, the project is suspended for translators. Account and project settings remain accessible, and the data stays in place until the subscription is updated. Usage is shown under Account Settings, Billing, Plan Limits and Usage.

This is a storage allowance, not a monthly renewable word grant. Adding target languages consumes it faster. AI, machine translation and professional translation can use a separate prepaid managed balance; their inclusion as platform features does not promise unlimited free provider usage.

## Gotchas

- Free translations are donated to Crowdin's translation memory. Choose a suitable paid plan if you cannot accept that condition.
- Free allows one private project, but inviting your translators and unlimited collaboration are scoped to public projects.
- Open-source and academic licenses need approval. Public pricing does not confirm the payment-card requirement for ordinary Free registration.

## Questions people ask

### Is Crowdin free?

Yes. The regular Free plan includes 60,000 hosted words and one private project, with translation donation and public-only collaborator restrictions. Approved open-source projects have separate grants.

### What happens after the hosted-word quota is exceeded?

An email gives you 10 days to upgrade. Translator access is then suspended; account settings and existing data remain available.

### Can I keep translations private on Free?

Free translations are donated to Crowdin's translation memory. Pro makes donation optional and supports private-project collaboration.
