Internet.nl provides a simple, free way to test your website, mail server, or internet connection for compliance with modern internet standards like IPv6, DNSSEC, and HTTPS. It is best for individuals or small teams needing quick, manual checks; a batch API exists for bulk testing but requires applying for access rather than self-serve signup.
