You type a domain name or email address into the test form on the site and read the report. For many domains there is a batch API and a web dashboard, and you can also run your own copy because the project is open source. The service suits site owners and administrators who want a quick standards check.

## What the free plan gives you

There is no paid plan. Single tests of a website, an email domain, or your connection are free and need no account. The test looks at standards such as IPv6, DNSSEC, HTTPS, security headers, DMARC, DKIM, SPF, STARTTLS, DANE, and RPKI route authorisation.

The batch API and dashboard are also free, but only for approved users. The access page lists platform members, non-profits (including students with a school email), some Dutch community members, organizations in sectors of high criticality, and hosters in the Hall of Fame. Each organization gets at most 3 accounts.

## When you hit a limit

Single tests have no stated quota on the site. The batch terms ask users to follow fair use rules: at most 2 batch requests per week, with at most 5000 domain names in each.

The terms say the service runs on a best effort basis with no guarantees about performance or availability. The Dutch Internet Standards Platform can withdraw API access, for example after misuse. Nothing is billed, so there is no overage charge.

## Gotchas

- The batch service is a compliance test, not a monitoring tool, and it must not be used to test a single domain per request.
- Batch accounts unused for longer than 12 months are deleted, and so are accounts whose owner cannot be reached by email.
- If you reuse the test results on your own site, the terms ask for attribution to Internet.nl.

## Questions people ask

### Is Internet.nl free?

Yes. Testing a website, an email domain, or your connection is free and needs no account. The batch API and dashboard are free too, but only for approved users.

### Does Internet.nl have an API?

Yes, a batch API, but access is by application. If you do not qualify, the project's access page suggests running your own instance, because the code is open source.

### How many domains can I test with the Internet.nl batch API?

The terms ask for at most 2 batch requests per week, with at most 5000 domain names per request.
