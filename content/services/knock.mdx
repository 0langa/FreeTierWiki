You design workflows and templates in the Knock dashboard, then trigger them from your own backend with the REST API or an SDK. It suits developers who want product and engineering to share one notification system.

## What the free plan gives you

The Developer plan costs $0 per month. It includes up to 10,000 messages a month, counted as messages sent from workflows and broadcasts, and 500 guide active users. A guide active user is a user who sees at least one in-app guide in the month. The plan also includes 500 AI agent credits.

Workflows, broadcasts, delivery channels, team members, recipients, and environments are unlimited on this plan. You also get batching and delay steps, and sign-in with Google SSO. Email client previews start on the Starter plan.

## When you hit a limit

Knock says it does not hard cap your notifications. If you go over the free limit, it gets in touch with you about adding a billing method for next month. The pricing page does not say that sending stops.

Extra AI agent credits cost $.01 each. Paid plans bill extra messages and active users per unit. Messages that come from guide views do not count toward your message total.

## Gotchas

- A message means one successful send to one user on one channel, so one event sent to a user over two channels counts as two messages.
- The pricing page lists email client previews only from the Starter plan, which costs $250 per month.
- The trigger API limits the data payload to 10MB, and any single string longer than 1024 bytes is cut short in your logs.

## Questions people ask

### Is Knock free?

Yes. The Developer plan costs $0 per month and includes 10,000 messages and 500 guide active users. Workflows and channels are unlimited.

### What happens when I go over the Knock free limit?

Knock does not hard cap notifications. It contacts you about adding a billing method rather than turning sending off.

### How do I send a notification with Knock?

Create a workflow in the dashboard, then call the trigger API with the workflow key, the recipients, and a data object. You can make that call through the REST API or an SDK.
