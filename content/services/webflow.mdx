Webflow is a visual website builder: you design pages on a canvas that maps to HTML and CSS, manage content in its CMS, and host the site on Webflow. It suits designers and developers who want a hand-built marketing site without writing every line of front-end code.

## What the free plan gives you

Webflow's Starter site plan is free, and Webflow says you can try it for as long as you like. A Starter site publishes to a webflow.io domain and includes 2 static pages, limited Webflow CMS, 1 GB of bandwidth, and 50 lifetime form submissions.

Starter also includes Webflow AI, the MCP server, and Webflow Cloud app hosting, and every account gets a free Starter Workspace. Prices are per site. The Basic site plan costs $15 per month billed yearly and adds a custom domain, 300 static pages, 10 GB of bandwidth, and unlimited form submissions.

## When you hit a limit

The form documentation says Starter's 50 submissions are a lifetime allowance that does not reset. Once you reach it, new submissions stop being collected until you upgrade your Site plan. The pricing page lists 1 GB of bandwidth per month but does not explain how that limit is enforced on Starter. A paid Site plan unlocks more pages, bandwidth, and form submissions.

The Starter plan has no listed overage charges.

## Gotchas

- Starter sites live on a webflow.io domain; connecting your own domain needs a paid Site plan.
- Only 2 static pages are allowed, so even a small multi-page site outgrows Starter quickly.
- Site plans are priced per site, so each additional site you launch needs its own plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Webflow free?

Yes. The Starter site plan is free and includes a webflow.io domain, 2 static pages, limited CMS, 1 GB of bandwidth, and 50 lifetime form submissions.

### Can I use a custom domain on Webflow's free plan?

No. A custom domain starts with the Basic site plan.

### How long can I stay on Webflow's Starter plan?

As long as you like. Webflow describes the Starter plan as free to try for as long as you like.
