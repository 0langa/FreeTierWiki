You use Linkinize through a browser extension and a web app. It suits teams that keep sharing links in chats and want them in one tagged, searchable list.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per month and the pricing page calls it free forever for teams and small businesses. The home page says you get 4 free workspaces and 10 members. You tag, search, and share bookmarks with your team through the browser extension.

Every free user can also try the full Pro experience, including AI features, for 30 days with no commitment. The privacy note on the home page says your data stays encrypted, is never sold, and has no ads or trackers.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page lists 10 users on Free and does not say what happens when you invite more. The Pro plan costs $19.99 per month for up to 50 users, and Enterprise is a custom plan. Pro adds a 48 hour support response time, plugins, and insights and usage analytics. SAML SSO is available on Pro and Enterprise. The free plan asks for no card, and you can cancel the Pro trial any time before it ends.

## Gotchas

- Free is capped at 4 workspaces and 10 members, so a growing company outgrows it fast.
- SAML SSO, plugins, and usage analytics are paid features.
- The pricing page does not state a bookmark limit for the free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Linkinize free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per month for 10 users, with 4 workspaces. Pro adds more users and features at $19.99 per month.

### Does Linkinize have a free trial?

Yes. The Pro plan has a 30 day free trial, and every free user can try the full Pro experience for 30 days.

### Does Linkinize support SSO?

Yes, on paid plans. SAML-based SSO is available on Pro and Enterprise, not on the Free plan.
