You use Twist in the browser or its apps, and it organizes talk into channels and threads. It suits remote and distributed teams that want fewer interruptions than a fast chat tool gives.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs 0 euros per user per month. You can view the last month of comments and messages. It allows up to 5 integrations and up to 5 GB of file storage. Standard support is included.

You can invite up to 500 external guest accounts. A guest is a limited member who only sees the channels they are invited to. On the free plan, guests are free. The Unlimited plan costs 6 euros per user per month and gives full history, unlimited integrations, and unlimited file storage.

## When you hit a limit

The main limit is history. On the free plan, you cannot view or search comments and messages older than one month. The pricing FAQ says nothing is deleted. All your team's data stays on Twist's servers, and the history returns if you upgrade to Unlimited.

The same rule applies if you downgrade from Unlimited to Free. The data stays, but you cannot view or search the older items. The page lists no automatic charge on the free plan.

## Gotchas

- Guests on the free plan see only threads and messages less than one month old, like every member.
- On Unlimited, multi-channel guests are billed like team members. Single-channel guests are always free.
- To mix free and paid members, the FAQ suggests creating a separate free team.

## Questions people ask

### Is Twist free?

Yes. The Free plan costs 0 euros per user per month, with the last month of history, up to 5 integrations, and up to 5 GB of storage.

### What happens to old messages in Twist's free plan?

You cannot view or search messages older than one month. The pricing page says nothing is deleted, and upgrading restores access.

### Are guests free in Twist?

Yes on the free plan. You can invite up to 500 external guests, but they see only recent threads.
