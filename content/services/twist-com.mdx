Twist organizes team messages into channels and threads in its web and native apps. Doist will close Twist on March 1, 2027 and is moving teams to Todoist Comms. Workspace admins choose whether and when to migrate before the shutdown.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 per user per month. You can view the last month of comments and messages. It allows up to 5 integrations and up to 5 GB of file storage. Standard support is included.

Free allows up to 500 internal member accounts, separately from up to 500 external guest accounts. A guest is a limited member who only sees the channels they are invited to. On the free plan, guests are free. The Unlimited plan costs USD 6 per user per month with yearly billing, or USD 8 billed monthly and gives full history, unlimited integrations, and unlimited file storage.

## When you hit a limit

Free workspaces can view or search the most recent month of messages and comments. Twist keeps older history during normal operation, including after a downgrade, and upgrading restores access.

At shutdown, workspace access ends and Doist will delete data still in Twist on May 1, 2027. Admins can move channels, threads, messages, files, and people to Todoist Comms before then. Free workspaces move to the free Todoist Starter plan, which also shows one month of history. The migration is one-way.

## Gotchas

- Guests on the free plan see only threads and messages less than one month old, like every member.
- On Unlimited, multi-channel guests are billed like team members. Single-channel guests are always free.
- Bots, integrations, and scheduled threads need to be recreated after migrating.

## Questions people ask

### Is Twist free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 per user per month, with the last month of history, up to 5 integrations, and up to 5 GB of storage.

### What happens to old messages in Twist's free plan?

Free hides messages older than one month while the workspace is running. Upgrading restores access, and Doist will delete workspace data still in Twist on May 1, 2027.

### Are guests free in Twist?

Yes on the free plan. You can invite up to 500 external guests, but they see only recent threads.
