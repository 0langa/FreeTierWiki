You call an HTTP API with your key, use one of the official SDKs, or route traffic through its proxy mode. There is also a request builder in the dashboard. It suits developers who want a page fetched or parsed without running browsers.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan gives you 2,000 API credits every month, with a maximum of 2 concurrent connections. It needs no credit card. A request can take up to 30 seconds.

Credits depend on the options. A request without JavaScript rendering costs 1 credit, and the default with JavaScript rendering costs 5. Residential proxies cost 10 to 25 credits, and stealth proxies cost 50. AI extraction adds 5 credits. A Google search through the SERP endpoint costs 15 credits, and a structured data page costs 15 credits too, or 50 for Reddit.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when the free credits run out. It does say that credits come from a monthly plan, from pay-as-you-go top-ups, or from both. Pay-as-you-go credits are used only after your plan quota runs out.

You can buy credits with no subscription. One dollar buys 5,000 credits, the minimum top-up is $20, and the credits stay valid for 12 months. Paid plans start at $29 a month for 250,000 credits.

## Gotchas

- Failed requests are free. For the data endpoint, pages that come back parse_failed or not_found still count as successful and are charged.
- The free plan allows 2 concurrent connections, while the first paid plan allows 10.
- Refunds are possible within 7 days of a first purchase, minus the pay-as-you-go value of the credits you used.

## Questions people ask

### Is WebScraping.AI free?

Yes. A free account gets 2,000 API credits per month with a maximum of 2 concurrent connections. No credit card is required.

### How many pages can I scrape with the WebScraping.AI free tier?

It depends on the options. A request without JavaScript costs 1 credit, and the default with JavaScript rendering costs 5. That is about 400 JavaScript pages or up to 2,000 plain pages a month.

### Does WebScraping.AI charge for failed requests?

No. Failed requests are free, and you pay only for successful responses.
