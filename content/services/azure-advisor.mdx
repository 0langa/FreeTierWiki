You use Advisor in the Azure portal, where the Overview page shows recommendations and a score for each category. It also has a REST API, and you can query recommendations with Azure Resource Graph.

## What the free plan gives you

Azure Advisor costs nothing. The pricing page says you can access recommendations at no additional cost. Advisor is a digital cloud assistant that analyzes your resource configuration and usage data, then recommends fixes for cost, performance, reliability, and security.

The dashboard groups recommendations into five categories: reliability, security, performance, cost, and operational excellence. You can see recommendations if you are the Owner, Contributor, or Reader of a subscription, resource group, or resource. Each recommendation opens into details and a recommended action, and you can postpone or dismiss ones you do not want. Advisor shows a score for each category on its overview page.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page and the docs do not describe a quota or a paid tier for Advisor. There is no limit to hit on the Advisor service itself.

The cost sits in the resources you act on. If you follow a recommendation, such as changing a resource, you pay for the resource you change under its own pricing. Advisor also needs an Azure subscription, so you must have an Azure account to open it. New resources can take up to 24 hours to appear in recommendations, and an applied action can take up to a day to register.

## Gotchas

- Advisor only reviews resources in Azure subscriptions. It will not look at other clouds.
- New resources can take up to 24 hours to show up in recommendations.
- You need a role on the subscription, resource group, or resource: Owner, Contributor, or Reader.

## Questions people ask

### Is Azure Advisor free?

Yes. Microsoft says you can access Advisor recommendations at no additional cost.

### Do I need an Azure subscription to use Azure Advisor?

Yes. Advisor shows recommendations for the resources in your Azure subscriptions, so you need a subscription and a role on it.

### How long does Azure Advisor take to show recommendations?

It runs in the background to find new resources. It can take up to 24 hours to give recommendations on newly created resources.
