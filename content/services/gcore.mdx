You use the Gcore dashboard to add a domain as a CDN resource and point your DNS at Gcore. The docs also offer a REST API, SDKs, and Terraform. It suits site owners and media teams that want a global CDN with simple billing.

## What the free plan gives you

The CDN page says Gcore CDN is free to start with no credit card required, with paid plans for higher throughput, advanced security, and dedicated support. The page lists 210+ points of presence on six continents, and DDoS protection and a web application firewall on all plans. Free Let's Encrypt SSL and custom certificates are supported. You can create up to thirty CDN resources on one account.

The pricing table shows two CDN plans, pay as you go and Enterprise. The pay-as-you-go column lists 1 TB per month, though the table has no row labels, so treat that figure as the monthly traffic allowance and confirm it in your account.

## When you hit a limit

The billing docs say each plan has a monthly traffic limit and a request limit, and the traffic resets on the first day of each calendar month. When you pass either limit, extra charges apply. Gcore collects overage in portions of 50 euros or 60 dollars, which is the account credit limit, and the CDN keeps working while overage stays below it.

Once the overage reaches the credit limit, Gcore charges the linked payment instrument, described as a credit card added at signup. If payment fails again, the CDN stops delivering content until you recharge the account. So going over is a bill risk, not a hard stop.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page does not describe a separate free CDN plan, so confirm what your account includes before you rely on 1 TB.
- Traffic and request overage costs are combined, so a high request count can cost money even if traffic stays low.
- Plan changes take effect on the first day of the following month.

The CDN page says it is free to start, with no credit card required. The pricing table lists a pay-as-you-go plan with 1 TB per month.

### What happens when I exceed the Gcore CDN traffic limit?

Extra charges apply. Gcore collects overage in portions of 50 euros or 60 dollars, and the CDN stops delivering content if payment fails.

### Does Gcore CDN need a credit card?

The CDN page says no credit card is required to start. The billing docs say overage is charged to a payment instrument added at signup.
