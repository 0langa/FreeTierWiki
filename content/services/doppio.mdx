You call the Doppio REST API from your code with an API key. You send a URL, raw HTML, or a saved template, and you get back a PDF or a screenshot. Official quick starts cover Node.js, Python, PHP, Ruby, Rust, serverless, and a CLI.

## What the free plan gives you

The Basic plan is free. It allows up to 400 documents per month, 1 template, and community support. The pricing page says there is no time limit and no trial period. Every plan uses the full rendering engine, so free PDFs and screenshots come out the same as paid ones.

The default timeout is 30 seconds per render. Async renders, which allow longer timeouts, and presigned URL upload to your own storage are listed only on paid plans. The paid plans start at $16 per month for up to 4,000 documents and 5 templates.

## When you hit a limit

Doppio says that on plans other than Pro, renders pause until the next billing cycle unless you upgrade. On the free plan that means a hard stop and no bill. Only the Pro plan charges extra documents, at $0.0037 each beyond its 36,000 documents.

So you will not get a surprise charge on the free plan. A pause also means your app needs a plan for failed renders. Doppio also offers a usage monitoring guide, so you can track your count before the pause.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page says no credit card is required. The quickstart guide says Doppio asks for a card to prevent abuse. Expect either at sign-up.
- The quickstart notes that you must refresh your API key the first time you copy it.
- The free plan has 1 template. A second saved template needs the Premium plan with 5 templates.

## Questions people ask

### Is Doppio free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free with up to 400 documents per month and 1 template. The site says there is no time limit.

### Does Doppio need a credit card?

The pricing page says no credit card is required. The quickstart guide says Doppio asks for one to prevent abuse, so be ready for either.

### What happens when I go over the Doppio free quota?

Renders pause until the next billing cycle unless you upgrade. The free plan does not bill extra documents.
