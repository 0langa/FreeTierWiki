You set up a business and project in the Kinde dashboard, then connect your app with a Kinde SDK for your language. It suits SaaS builders who want sign-in, access control, and billing from one vendor.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for hobbyists and personal projects, and it needs no credit card. The pricing page lists no setup fees or monthly fees. It includes 10,500 monthly active users, attack protection, and 2 environments, production and development.

Limits on the Free plan are small for extras. You get 10 feature flags, 2 custom roles with 10 permissions, 1 integration webhook per environment, 1 billing webhook, 5 monthly active organizations and 5 custom properties. The Pro plan lifts the limits with uncapped M2M tokens, unlimited roles, webhooks, and flags. The pricing table lists removing Kinde branding as a paid-plan feature. Users on paid subscriptions that you bill through Kinde are not counted toward your monthly active users.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not specify what happens when Free passes 10,500 monthly active users. Pro starts at $25 USD/month plus extra-MAU usage, with the first published rate $0.0175 per MAU. Payment details are added when upgrading.

The account-plan guide says excess monthly active organizations are billed at the plan unit price, including a Free allowance of 5, but does not establish that Free excess price or payment transition. Confirm before assuming every Free feature is capped without billing. Kinde Billing separately charges 0.7% on customer transactions.

## Gotchas

- The pricing table lists removing Kinde branding as a paid-plan feature.
- Roles are limited to 2 custom roles and 10 permissions on Free, which a multi-role B2B app can outgrow.
- If you bill customers through Kinde, there is a 0.7% fee per transaction on the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Kinde free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing, covers up to 10,500 monthly active users, and needs no credit card.

### Does Kinde need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the Free plan.

### What are the Kinde free plan limits besides users?

Free has 10 feature flags, 2 custom roles, 10 permissions, 1 integration webhook per environment, 1 billing webhook, 5 monthly active organizations and 2 environments. It does not include removing Kinde branding.
