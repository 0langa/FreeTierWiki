You set up a business and project in the Kinde dashboard, then connect your app with a Kinde SDK for your language. It suits SaaS builders who want sign-in, access control, and billing from one vendor.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan is for hobbyists and personal projects, and it needs no credit card. The pricing page lists no setup fees or monthly fees. It includes 10,500 monthly active users, attack protection, and 2 environments, production and development.

Limits on the Free plan are small for extras. You get 10 feature flags, 2 custom roles with 10 permissions, 1 webhook, and 5 custom properties. The Pro plan lifts the limits with uncapped M2M tokens, unlimited roles, webhooks, and flags. The pricing table lists removing Kinde branding as a paid-plan feature. Users on paid subscriptions that you bill through Kinde are not counted toward your monthly active users.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when a Free plan passes 10,500 monthly active users. It lists the Pro plan as uncapped, with a price for each extra monthly active user. The first rate shown is 0.0175 dollars, and higher tiers show lower rates.

The page says you can upgrade when you want and cancel any time. If you bill your own customers through Kinde, a 0.7% fee per transaction applies on the Free plan. Check the dashboard usage before a launch, and ask Kinde support if you expect to pass the free limit.

## Gotchas

- The pricing table lists removing Kinde branding as a paid-plan feature.
- Roles are limited to 2 custom roles and 10 permissions on Free, which a multi-role B2B app can outgrow.
- If you bill customers through Kinde, there is a 0.7% fee per transaction on the Free plan.

## Questions people ask

### Is Kinde free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing, covers up to 10,500 monthly active users, and needs no credit card.

### Does Kinde need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says no credit card is required for the Free plan.

### What are the Kinde free plan limits besides users?

Free has 10 feature flags, 2 custom roles, 10 permissions, 1 webhook, and 2 environments. It does not include removing Kinde branding.
