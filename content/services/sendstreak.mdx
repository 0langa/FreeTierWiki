You call a REST API or use an SDK, and you manage templates and logs in a web dashboard. It suits developers who want control over mail delivery without building the extras themselves.

## What the free plan gives you

SendStreak has one plan with every feature. The first 100 emails each day are free, and that allowance never expires. Templates, routing, retries, logs, analytics, and automations are all on from day one, with no tiers or seats.

Pricing is excluding VAT. The pricing page says the service is EU-hosted and GDPR compliant.

SendStreak does not deliver the mail by itself. It runs on top of the backend you connect, such as AWS SES, Postmark, or your own SMTP host. That provider bills you directly at its own rates.

## When you hit a limit

There is no hard stop at 100 emails a day. Every email beyond the daily 100 costs 2 euros per 10,000, and the free 100 a day stays after you upgrade.

Billing uses whole packages of 10,000 emails, once per billing period. A started package counts as a whole one. For example, 97,000 billable emails are 10 packages, so the bill is 20 euros. The pricing page says there is no monthly fee and no minimum.

## Gotchas

- The pricing page says your email backend bills you separately, and the comparison on that page includes the AWS SES cost.
- The free allowance is a daily one. The estimate on the page assumes sending is spread across the month.
- All prices exclude VAT.

## Questions people ask

### Is SendStreak free?

Yes, up to 100 emails a day, forever. The free amount stays after you start paying for more.

### How much does SendStreak cost after the free emails?

It costs 2 euros per 10,000 emails, excluding VAT, with no monthly fee. Billing rounds up to whole packages of 10,000.

### Do I still pay my email provider with SendStreak?

Yes. SendStreak runs on top of the backend you connect, so AWS SES, Postmark, or your SMTP host bills you at its own rates.
