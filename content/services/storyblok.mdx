You work in the Storyblok app with a visual editor and a content API. Developers add the SDK for their framework and register components, and editors build pages from those components.

## What the free plan gives you

The Starter plan is free. It includes 1 space and 1 seat, and you can add a second seat for $15. It covers 100,000 API requests and 100 GB of traffic per month, and 2 locales.

The comparison table also lists 20,000 stories, 2,000 assets with a maximum asset size of 500 MB, 200 components, and 100 content folders. Content version history is kept for 1 day, and Starter has no uptime SLA. New accounts are offered a 45-day free trial of the Growth Plus plan, after which you continue on the plan you choose.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when Starter passes its API requests or traffic. The table shows the extra-request price as not available for Starter. Only the Growth plan lists a price per extra 1M API requests, at $10, and a price per extra locale.

Growth is the next plan up. It costs $99 per month, with 5 seats and 1M API requests included.

## Gotchas

- Starter allows up to 2 seats, so it works for one person or a pair, not a team.
- The 45-day trial is of the Growth Plus plan. After it ends, you continue on the plan of your choice, so check which plan your space is on.
- A space is tied to a server region. The docs say to use the region key that matches your space's location in the SDK setup.

## Questions people ask

### Is Storyblok free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes 1 space, 1 seat, 100,000 API requests, and 100 GB of traffic per month.

### What happens when I go over the Storyblok free limits?

The pricing page does not say. It lists no price for extra requests or traffic on Starter, and Growth is the next plan up.

### Does Storyblok work with Next.js?

Yes. The docs have a Next.js quickstart that uses the `@storyblok/react` package, and a CLI command that scaffolds a Next.js project.
