You use Nuclino in the browser or its apps, and the help center lists an API for connecting other tools. It suits small teams that want one clean place for documents and diagrams.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan lets your team create up to 50 items. An item is a document that your team can edit in real time, and the 50 counts across all workspaces. You can also make up to 3 canvases, which are whiteboards and diagrams inside Nuclino.

Free includes 2GB of file storage for the whole team. Version history goes back 30 days. Paid plans lift the item and canvas limits, and they add admin tools. The Business plan lists 20GB of storage per user.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you reach 50 items or 3 canvases. It only states the caps, so plan on needing to delete old items or move to a paid plan to add more. Nothing on the page suggests an automatic charge, and Free needs no payment details.

If you try a paid plan first, the trial lasts 14 days and needs no credit card. When it ends, you can upgrade or stay on Free, and you lose the benefits of the paid plan.

## Gotchas

- The 50 item limit counts every document across all your workspaces, not one workspace at a time.
- A user is anyone who joins your team, including guests and read-only members. Upgrading moves the whole team to the paid plan.
- Admin tools are a paid feature. The pricing page lists access restrictions, editing restrictions, and groups in its comparison table, but its text does not show which plans include them.

## Questions people ask

### Is Nuclino free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing and needs no card. It allows up to 50 items, up to 3 canvases, and 2GB of storage.

### What counts as an item in Nuclino?

An item is a document that your team can edit in real time. On the Free plan you can create up to 50, across all workspaces.

### Does Nuclino need a credit card for the trial?

No. The 14-day paid trial needs no credit card or other payment information.
