Crashlytics is the crash reporter in Firebase. You add its SDK to an Apple, Android, Flutter, or Unity app, and crashes from your users' devices show up in the Firebase console, grouped into issues. It suits app developers who want to know which crashes hurt the most users without paying for a monitoring service.

## What the free plan gives you

The Firebase pricing page lists Crashlytics as a no-cost product. It costs nothing on the no-cost Spark plan and on the pay-as-you-go Blaze plan, and the page lists no usage limits for it.

Google describes Crashlytics as a lightweight, realtime crash reporter. It groups crashes intelligently and highlights the circumstances that led up to them. You can see whether a crash affects many users, get alerts when an issue suddenly increases in severity, and find which lines of code are causing crashes. You do not need a billing account to use it.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page shows no paid tier and no usage cap for Crashlytics, so there is no bill to reach. Your app keeps sending reports as long as the SDK is installed and the Firebase project exists.

Costs can come from other Firebase products you pair it with. For example, if you use Cloud Functions to react to new issues, Cloud Functions is billed on the Blaze plan. AI assistance in Crashlytics is part of Gemini in Firebase and listed separately on the pricing page.

## Gotchas

- Crashlytics covers app platforms; it is not an error tracker for a server or a plain web frontend.
- Crashlytics groups events that share a failure point into one issue, so check the events inside an issue before assuming it is a single bug.
- Readable reports depend on your build's mapping information, such as dSYM files for Apple apps.

## Questions people ask

### Is Firebase Crashlytics free?

Yes. The Firebase pricing page lists Crashlytics as no-cost on both the Spark and Blaze plans.

### Which platforms does Crashlytics support?

Google lists Apple platforms, Android, Flutter, and Unity.

### Do I need a credit card for Crashlytics?

No billing account is needed, because Crashlytics works on the no-cost Spark plan.
