You instrument your app with the Langfuse SDK or an OpenAI wrapper, then explore traces, prompts, and scores in the web app. It suits developers who build LLM apps and want to inspect every call.

## What the free plan gives you

The Hobby plan is free and needs no credit card. It includes all platform features with limits: 50k units per month, 30 days of data access, 2 users, and community support through GitHub. It is described as good for hobby projects and proofs of concept.

A billable unit is any tracing data point you send, including the trace, its observations such as spans, events, and generations, and scores from evaluations. Records generated by Langfuse features, such as experiments or annotation queues, also count. Core costs $29 per month with 100k units included, 90 days of data access, and unlimited users.

## When you hit a limit

Hobby's 50k monthly units have no published excess-handling policy or overage tariff. Core's $8/100k excess rate does not establish Hobby billing. A trace, observation or score each counts as a unit, so requests can consume several.

Organization-shared Hobby rate buckets allow 1,000 tracing-ingestion requests/minute and 30 General API requests/minute. Dedicated endpoints differ; excess returns HTTP 429 with Retry-After. Windows begin at the bucket's first request, rather than rolling or following calendar boundaries. MCP shares General API capacity. Requests and responses each have a 5MB payload cap. Monitor units and request rates separately.

## Gotchas

- Units count observations and scores, not only traces, so one request can use several units.
- Data older than 30 days is not accessible on Hobby, but remains stored and becomes accessible after upgrading. Data retention that permanently deletes records is a separate opt-in paid feature.
- Hobby has 2 users, while Core has unlimited users.

## Questions people ask

### Is Langfuse free?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free with no credit card, and it includes 50k units per month, 30 days of data access, and 2 users. You can also self-host the open-source version.

### What counts as a unit in Langfuse?

A billable unit is any tracing data point: a trace, an observation such as a span, event, or generation, or a score.

### Is Langfuse open source?

Yes. The docs say Langfuse is open source and that you can use Langfuse Cloud or self-host it with the same instrumentation code.
