You send a push with one HTTP request, a webhook URL, a command line tool, a browser extension, or an MCP server. The docs show cURL, JavaScript, Python, PHP, and Go examples, and no SDK is needed. It suits one developer who wants alerts on a phone.

## What the free plan gives you

The Free plan costs $0 and needs no credit card. It allows 50 daily messages, 1 device, 1 application, 1 webhook, and 1 form. Webhook history is kept for 7 days. The counter resets every day.

Every push delivered to a device counts as one message. Sending the same notification to two devices counts as two. Priority levels run from silent to critical. On Free, critical alerts are delivered as high priority. Paid plans keep true critical alerts, which can bypass Do Not Disturb on Android.

## When you hit a limit

Once you reach the daily message limit, further notifications for that day are not delivered. The counter resets the next day. Nothing is billed, so there is no overage charge on the free plan.

If you keep hitting the limit, the pricing page suggests a plan with higher daily volume. The Starter plan costs $6 per month for 1,000 daily messages, 3 devices, and 5 applications.

## Gotchas

- Over-limit notifications are not delivered. An alert sent after you reach the daily limit does not reach your phone that day.
- You can have only 1 device on Free, so a second phone or tablet needs a paid plan.
- Support on Free responds within up to 96 hours, compared with up to 24 hours on Starter.

## Questions people ask

### Is Pocket Alert free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever with no credit card. It gives you 50 push notifications a day, 1 device, 1 application, and 1 webhook.

### What happens if I hit the daily limit on Pocket Alert?

Further notifications for that day are not delivered. The counter resets the next day, and you can upgrade for a higher daily volume.

### Does Pocket Alert work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The home page lists iOS and Android apps, and you receive the pushes on the app you install on your phone.
