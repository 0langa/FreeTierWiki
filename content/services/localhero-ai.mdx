You set it up with a CLI command and a GitHub Action, then review translations in the dashboard or in the pull request. It suits developers who keep translation files in a repository.

## What the free plan gives you

The pricing page offers a free plan for open source and side projects: 1 project and 250 translation credits per month. It names the plan "free, forever". The page also says every plan gets the same high-quality AI translations.

A credit translates one source key, up to 100 characters, into one target language. Ten keys in five languages use 50 credits. Longer keys use more credits. At 250 credits a month, the free plan fits a small app with few new strings each month.

New accounts can also try Localhero.ai free for 30 days with full access. The site says this trial needs no credit card.

## When you hit a limit

The pricing page does not say what happens when you use all 250 free credits. It does not mention a bill for the free plan either.

For paid plans, it sells a top-up pack of 500 credits for $19. Purchased credits do not expire and roll over between billing cycles. The top-up pack is available on any paid plan, so a free account has to upgrade first. The paid plans start at $29 per month for Starter, with 2 projects and 1250 credits a month.

## Gotchas

- The free plan has 1 project. Starter has 2 projects, Growth has 5, and Professional has 20.
- Credits count each key and each target language. Adding a language multiplies the cost of every new key.
- The GitHub Action and the GitHub App must be connected for the Export to GitHub and Sync to GitHub buttons to work.

## Questions people ask

### Is Localhero.ai free?

Yes, for small use. The free plan gives 1 project and 250 credits per month. There is also a 30-day trial with full access.

### Does Localhero.ai need a credit card?

No. The pricing page says the 30-day trial has full access and needs no credit card, and it shows a free sign-up for the free plan.

### What is a Localhero.ai credit?

A credit translates one source key, up to 100 characters, into one target language. Longer keys need more credits.
