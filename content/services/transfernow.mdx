You use TransferNow in a web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. An account is optional for the free service. It suits people who need a quick one-off send, not long-term storage.

## What the free plan gives you

The free service lets you send up to 5 GB per transfer, even without creating a user account. Files stay available for 7 days. You can send by email or create a share link, and you can add a password to a transfer for free.

TransferNow scans the files at the end of each transfer, before they become available for download. Recipients can preview and stream videos, audio, and PDF files before downloading.

A free account is not required, but TransferNow offers one for higher transfer capacity and extra features such as a download tracking dashboard.

## When you hit a limit

The free service lists 5 GB per transfer and files available for 7 days. The page does not say what happens to a larger transfer.

The paid plans raise the limits. The cheapest paid plan costs 3 euros a month for 10 GB per transfer, 100 GB of storage, and 30 days of availability. Premium costs 6 euros a month for 250 GB per transfer, 365 days of availability, and 1000 GB of storage. Team costs 18 euros a month for 500 GB per transfer.

## Gotchas

- Files are available for 7 days on the free service, so recipients must download them in time.
- Branding, such as a personal subdomain with your logo, is a paid account feature.
- Transfers are not storage. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot lose.

## Questions people ask

### Is TransferNow free?

Yes. You can send up to 5 GB per transfer for free, with 7 days of availability, and no account is needed.

### Does TransferNow need an account?

No. The free service works without one. An account gives higher transfer capacity and extra features.

### Can I add a password to a free TransferNow transfer?

Yes. TransferNow says you can add a password to your transfers for free.
