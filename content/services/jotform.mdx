Jotform is a hosted form builder. You build forms by dragging fields onto a page or by starting from a template, then share the form by link or embed it on a site. It also handles payment fields, widgets, and signed documents. It suits people who want a ready form with many templates rather than writing a form backend.

## What the free plan gives you

The free plan is called Starter. Jotform’s official support confirms that registering a free account does not require credit-card information. Jotform's plan table lists these limits for it: 5 forms, 100 monthly submissions, 100 MB of available space, 10,000 monthly form views, 500 total submission storage, 100 fields per form, 10 monthly payment submissions, 1 user, and 10 monthly signed documents.

The pricing FAQ says Starter includes all standard features, such as form templates, payment integrations, and widgets. HIPAA compliance features are excluded; they are on the Gold and Enterprise plans only. Forms and signed documents on Starter include Jotform branding, which a paid plan removes. Jotform has no trial of its paid plans, so the Starter plan is how you try the product.

## When you hit a limit

Jotform emails a warning at a usage limit. Its account-usage guide says monthly or upload-space exhaustion disables forms on the date in that email, showing Form over quota. Monthly counters reset with your usage cycle; deleting entries does not restore them. You can wait for reset or upgrade.

Total submission storage works differently: new responses replace the oldest when it is full. Download and purge old entries to preserve a copy and free storage; purging uploaded files can also recover upload space.

## Gotchas

- Total submission storage is a running total, not a monthly amount, so an old form that keeps collecting answers will eventually push the oldest responses out.
- Only a small number of payment submissions are free each month, so a busy order form outgrows Starter fast.
- When you downgrade from a paid plan, the lower limits apply immediately.

## Questions people ask

### Is Jotform free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and includes standard features, with limits such as 5 forms and 100 monthly submissions.

### What happens to my data if I go over the storage limit?

Jotform deletes the oldest form response to make room for new submissions once total submission storage is full. Download and purge old submissions to keep them.

### Can I remove the Jotform branding for free?

No. Forms built on the Starter plan include Jotform branding; you need a paid plan to remove it.
